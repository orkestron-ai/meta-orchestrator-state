## 0. How the money, essential-supply and waste layer works

These are three resource flows that could not look more different, the money that settles every exchange, the water and food that keep a body alive, and the waste that the material economy leaves behind, and one commitment binds them: **money is a medium and never value, an essential is delivered and never weaponised, and the harm of a flow is borne by the one who made it, never dumped on the weak**. The money rail settles the $ side of the two-layer system while the sovereign Ae vector stays on its own ledger, never netted or exchanged; potable water, staple food and the ability to transact are life-critical floors delivered upfront through the standing reserve; and the anti-value of a contaminant or a waste stream travels with its producer and is never externalised onto a neighbour, a downstream community, or the commons.

**Money and payments (MONEY, PAY, F2).** The F2 surface is partitioned so each rule has one owner: MONEY (F2a) owns the currency, base-money inertness, the holding, issuance, and the boundary with F8 credit; PAY (F2b) owns the payment object, the cash-like carriage, the payment-access floor, and the cutoff bar. Money is the $ rail medium and never Ae value: it can never be minted as, netted against, or laundered into sovereign standing (a decidable two-legs-cancel test), private minting of the rail is counterfeiting, and seigniorage returns to the commons rather than a private captor. The ability to transact is a delivered floor with a fee-free essential minimum and a no-identity bridge for the undocumented; the essential minimum is never frozen even under a lawful order, debanking to punish or coerce routes through the coercion gate, and payment privacy holds through the cash-like tier and an S5 cohort default with per-person access only by a targeted, time-bounded, loud-audited judicial contract.

**Water and food (SUPPLY, NOURISH, F4).** The F4 surface is partitioned so NOURISH (F4b) owns the delivered water and food capability floor and SUPPLY (F4a) owns the supply chain, safety, the water commons, and cornering. Potable water and adequate nutrition are life_critical floors delivered unconditionally to every person with no solvency test and a physical fallback when payment fails, non-priceable to the floor-holder while the honest utility recovers its attested cost from the reserve; a food desert or water-poverty is a live breach; a water or food cutoff to coerce is barred; the water source is a rent-bearing commons under an aggregate safe-yield cap; a contamination that can sicken or kill (acute or cumulative) is a harm and a concealed known contaminant carries the concealment perpetual flip; and when supply is physically below the aggregate floor an equal-per-capita rationed allocation is the lawful discharge, the shortfall a preparedness breach, never per-household coercion.

**Waste and integrity (WASTE, RINT, F5).** The generator bears the embodied anti-value and the disposal-contingent tail, a recovery event releases the tail only on proven downstream beneficial use (never at the recovery event, so a sham recycler cannot shed liability then dump), hazardous streams pre-fund the harm-capacity escrow with a standing catchment reserve and a capacity-continuity plan, and illegal dumping and waste-export to a weaker jurisdiction are caught while a genuine consented safe transfer is protected. The integrity surface maps every attack, money-as-value laundering, private minting, debanking, contamination-concealment, cornering, the water-grab, illegal dumping and sham recycling, to its closing invariant, its A18 class, and a natural-person bearer, with the money rail never netted against the anti-value rail.

Three commitments recur, matching the rest of the model:

1. **Money is a medium, never value.** The two-ledger firewall holds at the money layer: money is never minted as, netted against, or laundered into the sovereign Ae vector, and a private actor never captures or mints the rail.
2. **An essential is delivered, never weaponised.** The payment, water, and food floors are delivered upfront through the standing reserve, unconditional and never frozen, cut, or gated on a solvency read, with a physical fallback and a continuity-of-service successor when an operator fails.
3. **The maker of the harm bears it, and never the weak.** The embodied anti-value of a contaminant or a waste stream travels with its producer, is booked per axis and never netted against the money rail, and is never dumped on a downstream community, a poorer jurisdiction, or the commons.

The sections follow: MONEY currency, issuance, base-money inertness and holdings; PAY payments, settlement and the payment-access floor; SUPPLY water and food supply chains and safety; NOURISH the water and food capability floor; WASTE waste streams and circular flows; RINT resource-flow integrity and capture.

## MONEY currency, issuance, base-money inertness and holdings (F2a)

### Purpose

MONEY (F2a) models money as the settlement medium that rides the $ rail, and nothing more. It owns four things and only these: the currency (the state unit of account and legal settlement instrument), base-money inertness (the monetary instrument as a yield-free, term-free, credit-free claim of settlement finality), the holding (a person's or organization's position in that money, derived from the ledger), and issuance and retirement of base money on the rail. It also owns the F8 boundary: the exact line at which a denominated claim stops being inert base money and becomes credit, debt, or a security.

F2 is partitioned. MONEY is F2a and PAY is F2b. PAY OWNS the payment and settlement object, the cash-like carriage of small-value money, the delivered payment-access floor, the coercive-cutoff bar, the fraud-hold regime, and the outage and degraded tier. MONEY does not re-model any of these: it CITES PAY-I4 for the cash-like carriage, PAY-I8 for the payment-access floor, PAY-I10 for the coercive-cutoff bar, PAY-I18 for the fraud-hold, and PAY-I19 for the degraded and offline tier. Where a MONEY holding operation (a freeze, a clawback, a retirement) touches the delivered essential minimum, MONEY states the local bar and cites the owner.

MONEY is deliberately thin. The two-ledger system, the sovereign non-transferable Ae vector, the transferable $ rail, the cash-like sub-threshold tier, the never-stored authoritative balance, settlement finality and clawback, and the read and privacy machinery are all owned by the Registry-and-Ledger layer (R2, R2b, R2c). MONEY CITES that machinery and builds the money object on top of it. It does not re-model it. Likewise the coupling of value to money is owned by Value-Money-Coupling.md, credit, debt, and securities are owned by Property-and-Ownership.md (F8), and money-rent leakage decomposition is owned by the RENT rule.

The load-bearing distinction this section protects, on both flanks, is that money is a medium and not value. Honest saving, honest transacting, and a stable predictable unit of account are protected AS floors of ordinary economic life. Private minting of the rail, seigniorage capture, and value-laundering (converting money into sovereign Ae standing) are barred. Neither flank is allowed to swallow the other: the rules that stop capture must not debank the honest saver or subject ordinary transactions to mass financial surveillance, and the rules that protect the saver must not open a private mint or a laundering channel into the Ae ledger.

### Object model

`Currency`: the state monetary instrument and unit of account. Attributes: `code` (ISO 4217 where interoperating externally), `minor_unit`, `issuer` (the polity, singular and public), `status` (active, superseded, or retired). There is exactly one active domestic settlement currency at a time on the $ rail; foreign currencies are held as F8-class claims, not as domestic base money.

`MonetaryInstrument`: a denominated unit of base money on the $ rail. Attributes: `currency`, `denomination_class` (above-threshold identified class, or the sub-threshold cash-like tier per R2-I4, whose carriage is owned by PAY per PAY-I4), `form` (account-position or bearer-token). A monetary instrument is base money: an unconditional claim of settlement finality on the rail, carrying no yield and no counterparty credit. Anything that carries yield, term, or counterparty credit is an F8 instrument, not a MONEY monetary instrument.

`Holding`: a party's position in money at a point in logical time. Attributes: `holder_ref` (above threshold) or `null` (sub-threshold cash-like, per R2-I4 which structurally omits stable counterparty identifiers, carried by PAY per PAY-I4), `currency`, and a position that is DERIVED from the R2 posting history, never an authoritatively stored balance (R2-I5). A holding is a view over settled postings, not a stored number the state keeps.

`Issuance`: a governed public act that creates or retires base money on the rail. Attributes: `authorizing_act`, `volume`, `direction` (issue or retire), `seigniorage_destination`. Issuance is a public power, never a private one.

Payment and settlement carriage between holdings is not a MONEY object: it is owned by PAY (F2b), cite PAY-I4. MONEY defines the holding a payment moves and the currency it is denominated in; it does not model the payment, its finality, or its clawback (R2b), nor any coercive-cutoff question (cite PAY-I10, A18-I4).

### Invariants

**MONEY-I1 (money is the $ rail medium, not value).** Money SHALL be modelled exclusively as the settlement medium on the $ rail defined by R2-I1 (the exactly-two-ledgers rule) and R2-I4 (the $ rail and its tiers). Money SHALL NOT carry, encode, or represent any per-axis Ae value. Predicate: any object whose semantics require reading or writing the Ae ledger is not a MONEY object and MUST be rejected by the MONEY type gate. Purpose: keep the medium and the value substrate structurally separate at the object level, so that neither the saver nor the operator can treat a money balance as a claim on sovereign standing. Enforcement hook: MONEY object validation refuses any monetary instrument, holding, or issuance that references an Ae axis.

**MONEY-I2 (no netting of Ae against the rail).** No MONEY operation SHALL net, offset, convert, or reconcile a $ rail position against an Ae vector position, in either direction, at any grain (cite R2-I6 and the RINT non-netting rule). Predicate: for every MONEY posting, the set of ledgers touched is a subset of {$ rail}; touching the Ae ledger is a type error. Purpose: the Ae vector is booked per axis and never netted across axes and never against money; MONEY must not become the place where that firewall is quietly breached. Both flanks: this protects the honest holder (their money is never silently converted into or out of sovereign standing) and the polity (no operator can buy Ae standing with money).

**MONEY-I3 (no money-as-value laundering, decidable test).** An operation SHALL be barred as money-as-value laundering if and only if any of the following holds: (a) a $-rail leg and an Ae-axis leg purport to net or cancel against each other; (b) a money creation cites an Ae quantity as its backing; or (c) a holder's Ae posting is settled by a $-rail transfer (cite R2-I6 and the RINT non-netting rule). Purpose: close the value-laundering channel at the money layer with a decidable test, not a probabilistic "as a function of money inflow" heuristic. Enforcement hook: any operation matching a limb of this predicate routes to A6 as an attempted Ae-laundering event; a designed or concealed such path is a concealment-class harm under A18 with the perpetual flip, and is escalated per A18-I4 where coercive.

**MONEY-I4 (currency as the state unit of account).** The polity SHALL maintain exactly one active domestic settlement currency as the unit of account and legal settlement instrument on the $ rail. The currency's issuer SHALL be the polity, singular and public. Purpose: a single stable unit of account is a public good and a floor of ordinary economic planning; fragmenting it into private units is how a private rail is smuggled in. Both flanks: honest actors get a predictable stable unit (protected), and no private actor may declare a competing domestic base-money unit (barred). Foreign currencies and multi-currency accounting MAY be permitted, but foreign-currency positions are F8-class claims, not domestic base money.

**MONEY-I5 (issuance is a governed public act, never a private mint).** Creation or retirement of base money on the $ rail SHALL occur only through a governed `Issuance` act with a named authorizing public act and a public volume. No private party SHALL mint, create, or retire base money on the rail. Predicate: a base-money supply change is valid only if it carries a valid `authorizing_act` traceable to the monetary authority under Governance-Mechanics; any other supply change is a forgery. Purpose: the power to mint the settlement rail is the power to tax silently and to capture the commons; it must be public, bounded, and accountable. Enforcement hook: unauthorized issuance is treated as counterfeiting of the rail, a non-priceable criminal-class matter routed to A6, with a natural-person bearer required (no autonomous-agent path may leave this breach unbeared, per O1).

**MONEY-I6 (seigniorage is the $-rail proceeds of issuance, returned to the commons).** An issue-direction `Issuance` yields the $-rail proceeds of issuance (seigniorage), not created value. Seigniorage SHALL be booked to the commons-return destination and SHALL NOT accrue as private seigniorage capture (cite MONEY-I5, the private-mint bar). The destination rule is owned by the commons-return owner: cite the commons-return and citizen-dividend destination in Distributive-Justice-and-Capability-Floors.md (COMP-I17). Predicate: for every issue-direction `Issuance`, `seigniorage_destination` MUST resolve to that commons-return destination, never to a private holder's uplift. Purpose: the mint is a commons; the gain from expanding the medium belongs to the members, not to whoever sits closest to the mint. Both flanks: this protects members against a silent transfer to insiders, and it does not penalize the honest operator, who neither expects nor receives issuance gain. MONEY authors no dividend machinery and does not price money-rent here (that is RENT's, on holding, not on seigniorage).

**MONEY-I7 (the holding is a derived view; the balance is never stored).** A `Holding` position SHALL be derived from the R2 settled-posting history and SHALL NOT be an authoritatively stored balance (cite R2-I5). Predicate: no MONEY store persists a canonical balance field; any displayed position is a recomputation over postings, reproducible from the ledger. Purpose: an authoritatively stored balance is a single point of tampering, surveillance, and freeze; deriving it from postings keeps the ledger, not a mutable account row, as the source of truth. Both flanks: the honest holder's position is always reconstructible and cannot be silently rewritten, and the state gains no standing store of everyone's money to mine. A holding derived from R2 postings survives operator insolvency because the ledger, not the operator's book, is authoritative.

**MONEY-I8 (money-rent is unearned leakage, not a property of the instrument).** Pure return on merely holding money or a money-like claim (money-rent) SHALL be treated as unearned leakage priced on the Ae overlay, and SHALL NEVER be modelled as an intrinsic yield attribute of a MONEY monetary instrument (cite the RENT money-rent decomposition and F8). Predicate: a MONEY `MonetaryInstrument` with a non-zero yield attribute is malformed; yield-bearing claims are F8 objects whose yield is decomposed by RENT (money-rent leakage versus protected return on creation). Purpose: keep base money inert so that the leakage rule has a clean target and cannot be evaded by dressing a rentier claim as base money. Both flanks: honest saving of inert base money is fully protected and carries no charge; only the pure rent on holding is priced, and genuine return on creation is protected by RENT.

**MONEY-I9 (base money is inert: no yield, no term, no counterparty credit).** A MONEY `MonetaryInstrument` SHALL carry no yield, no maturity term, and no embedded counterparty credit. Any denominated claim that carries yield, term, or counterparty credit is an F8 instrument and SHALL be modelled there (cite F8). Predicate: presence of any of {yield, term, counterparty_credit} routes the object out of MONEY into F8. Purpose: draw the F2 to F8 line at exactly the point where a claim stops being a medium and becomes credit, so that the credit, debt, and securities regime and its backing rule apply. Enforcement hook: the MONEY type gate rejects yield-bearing, term-bearing, or credit-bearing objects with an F8 redirection.

**MONEY-I10 (cash-like carriage of small-value money).** Owned by PAY: cite PAY-I4. MONEY does not re-model the cash-like sub-threshold tier or its privacy shape; it only carries the R2-I4 `denomination_class` attribute on its objects.

**MONEY-I11 (delivered basic payment-access floor).** Owned by PAY: cite PAY-I8. MONEY does not re-model the delivered payment-access floor or its COMP-I17 own-account delivery.

**MONEY-I12 (no coercive or punitive payment cutoff; essential minimum non-freezable).** The bar on punitive, suppressive, or coercive payment cutoff and debanking is owned by PAY: cite PAY-I10 (routing through A18-I4 with floor_breach_severity an input). MONEY does not re-model that bar. MONEY-local rule on its own holding object: no freeze, clawback, or retirement of a MONEY holding, even under a valid targeted A6 contract, SHALL reduce a natural person's holding below the delivered essential payment minimum (cite PAY-I8). That minimum SHALL be non-freezable and non-severable while a contest under A18-I34 runs, and a freeze whose reach touches it re-enters A18-I4 as coercion. Both flanks: this preserves the genuine narrow case (a targeted A6-authorized freeze on a specific above-minimum holding for a specific adjudicated cause) while making the essential minimum untouchable.

**MONEY-I13 (private money-like claims that replicate money without backing are caught).** A private claim that replicates the settlement function of base money without being either MONEY base money or a properly backed F8 instrument (an unbacked money substitute, a synthetic money-like derivative, a private scrip functioning as a rail) SHALL be caught and reclassified. Predicate: an instrument functions as money if it is used for general settlement and is redeemable-on-demand at par with no disclosed backing; such an instrument is either brought under F8 backing rules (cite F8) or barred as a private mint under MONEY-I5. Purpose: the private-mint bar cannot be evaded by issuing a money-substitute that is nominally not currency; substance governs. Both flanks: genuine backed instruments and honest closed-loop vouchers with disclosed terms are protected under F8, while unbacked general-settlement substitutes that would replicate the rail are barred.

**MONEY-I14 (wealth as settled claims, not dominion over others).** Money SHALL be a medium of settlement and a store of the holder's own settled claims only. The size of a holding alone SHALL NEVER be a breach. Accumulation that converts into leverage corrupting price discovery or foreclosing others' essential-access floors is governed by the cornering and concentration regime: cite COMP-I11 cornering and the concentration ceiling measured across the O1 acting-in-concert graph, with an essential-access-floor breach as the aggravator. Predicate: a breach requires the affirmative COMP-I11 price-discovery-corruption showing across the O1 concert graph, never inventory or balance size on its own. Purpose: separate wealth-as-settled-claims (legitimate) from wealth-as-dominion-over-others (barred), the exact hinge between honest saving and capture. The channel by which money might buy Ae standing is closed separately by MONEY-I3 and R2-I6, not here. Both flanks: the honest large saver keeps their settled claims intact and unpenalized for size alone, while accumulation that converts into leverage over others' floors is charged and, where coercive, escalated under A18-I4.

**MONEY-I15 (MONEY owns the medium, never the value or the commons it carries).** MONEY SHALL model only the money object, its currency, its inertness, its holding, and its issuance; it SHALL NOT own, re-model, or arrogate the value substrate (R2), the value-money coupling (Value-Money-Coupling.md), the Ae ledger, the payment and settlement object or cash-like carriage (PAY), the instruments regime (F8), or any commons (water, land, subsurface, spectrum) that a payment may pay for. Predicate: a MONEY clause that restates or overrides any cited invariant is malformed and defers to the owning spec. Purpose: a flow never owns the content or the commons it carries; keeping MONEY thin prevents the money layer from silently absorbing the value, payment, and commons regimes. Enforcement hook: conformance review rejects MONEY additions that duplicate or contradict R2, PAY, F8, RENT, or the commons specs.

### Protocols

**P-MONEY-issue.** An `Issuance` (issue direction) SHALL: (1) validate a live `authorizing_act` from the monetary authority (MONEY-I5); (2) create the base-money volume as inert instruments (MONEY-I9); (3) book the $-rail seigniorage proceeds to the commons-return destination (MONEY-I6); (4) emit an R2 posting; (5) log the act publicly; (6) record an S4 audit event capturing the actor, the input (`authorizing_act`, `volume`), the output (created volume, `seigniorage_destination`), the cited invariant (MONEY-I5, MONEY-I6), and a timestamp. Any step failure aborts the issuance; a supply change without step (1) is counterfeiting under MONEY-I5.

**P-MONEY-retire.** An `Issuance` (retire direction) SHALL remove base-money volume by settled R2 postings against a public retirement account under a live authorizing act, with the withdrawal publicly logged, and SHALL record an S4 audit event capturing the actor, the input (`authorizing_act`, `volume`), the output (retired volume, target account), the cited invariant (MONEY-I5), and a timestamp. Retirement SHALL NOT selectively target a person's holding as a disguised freeze; a targeted freeze is a P-MONEY-freeze matter under A6, not a retirement. No retirement SHALL reduce a natural person's holding below the delivered essential payment minimum (cite PAY-I8, MONEY-I12).

**P-MONEY-hold-view.** A `Holding` position SHALL be produced only by recomputation over R2 postings (MONEY-I7). No protocol persists a canonical balance. A read of an above-threshold holder's position by any party other than the holder requires a targeted A6 contract with S2 or S3 access and S4 audit; there is no bulk read of holdings. The state senses positions only at S5 cohort or corridor grain.

**P-MONEY-freeze (narrow, contestable, floor-preserving).** A targeted freeze or clawback of a specific holding SHALL occur only under a specific A6-authorized cause with R2b clawback semantics, SHALL be per-person and reasoned, and SHALL be suspensive-contestable under A18-I34. It SHALL NOT be applied as punishment, suppression, or bulk action (cite PAY-I10). It SHALL NOT reduce a natural person's holding below the delivered essential payment minimum (cite PAY-I8, MONEY-I12): that minimum is non-freezable and non-severable while a contest under A18-I34 runs, and a freeze whose reach touches it re-enters A18-I4 as coercion. Every freeze or clawback SHALL record an S4 audit event capturing the actor, the input (the A6 cause reference, the target holding), the output (amount frozen or clawed back, the preserved minimum), the cited invariant (PAY-I8, PAY-I10, MONEY-I12), and a timestamp. Failing any of these routes the action to A18-I4 as a coercion event with floor_breach_severity an input.

### Lifecycle and edge cases

- Currency supersession: an active currency moves to superseded then retired; open holdings redenominate by settled R2 postings at the published rate. Redenomination SHALL NOT be used to expropriate holders selectively; a selective redenomination is a coercive-cutoff matter (cite PAY-I10, A18-I4) and, where it touches the essential minimum, a MONEY-I12 breach.
- Sub-threshold aggregation (structuring): the anti-structuring defense is the R2-I4 threshold PLUS a per-instrument velocity or aggregation cap enforced at the instrument (token) level, not at the holder level, which bounds splitting without linking holders. A genuine structuring case is reached only through a targeted, time-bounded, loud-audited A6 contract seeded by a specific out-of-band predicate (a fraud report, a predicate offense), never by standing cohort mining of small payments. Absent such a predicate, non-detection is the accepted price of the privacy floor, not a compliance gap to close by surveillance. Detection senses only at S5 cohort or corridor grain. The cash-like carriage this rides on is owned by PAY (cite PAY-I4).
- Lost or destroyed bearer instruments: the loss falls on the holder as with physical cash; the polity SHALL NOT reconstruct a bearer position from surveillance it is barred from holding.
- Foreign currency and cross-border settlement: foreign-currency positions are F8-class claims; cross-border settlement interoperates via ISO 20022 messaging but does not create domestic base money outside MONEY-I5.
- Negative or synthetic positions: a holding cannot go negative in base money (that is credit, an F8 object); an apparent negative money position is a mis-typed F8 instrument and redirects under MONEY-I9.
- Autonomous-agent transactor: an agent MAY operate a holding, but no autonomous-agent path may leave a floor breach or a criminal-class money harm (counterfeiting, laundering, coercive cutoff) without a natural-person bearer (cite O1).

## PAY payments, settlement and the payment-access floor (F2b)

### Purpose

PAY is the F2b partition of money. It OWNS the payment and settlement object on the $ rail, the cash-like carriage below threshold, the payment-access floor, the coercive-cutoff bar, the fraud-hold regime, and the outage or degraded tier. MONEY (F2a) OWNS currency, issuance, base-money inertness, the holding object, and the F8 boundary, and cites PAY-I4, PAY-I8, PAY-I10, PAY-I18 and PAY-I19 by ID rather than restating them. PAY governs money as the medium of the transferable settlement rail, and nothing more: it models the payment object, the settlement act on the $ rail, the rail operator, and the floor of access to transact.

PAY does not re-model the two-ledger value substrate. The Ae ledger of sovereign, non-transferable, per-axis value and the thin $ settlement rail are owned by R2 (R2-I1), the cash-like sub-threshold tier by R2-I4, the never-stored derived balance by R2-I5, the bar on minting or netting Ae between holders by R2-I6, finality, escrow and clawback by R2b, and the read and privacy machinery by R2c. Money here is the $ rail medium, never Ae value, and a payment rail never owns the value, the identity, or the commons it carries. A $ recovery books on the money rail and the residual per-axis harm books on V4; the two are never equated or netted (R2-I6, and the RINT non-netting booking rule). The coupling of value to money, instant prevention plus retrospective clawback, and the guard against the charge becoming a capture weapon, are owned by Value-Money-Coupling.md and cited, not restated. Credit, debt, securities and the money-rent leakage rule are F8 and RENT and are cited, not re-modeled.

Two commitments carry the section. First, a basic ability to transact, hold, receive and send money is a delivered capability floor, delivered upfront on the person's own-account basis through the COMP-I17 standing reserve, not a declared eligibility, and the essential minimum is never frozen, cut, fee-charged, or gated on a per-household solvency read. Second, both flanks hold under every rule: the payer, the unbanked, the debanked and the surveilled are protected, and at the same time the honest rail operator earns a benchmark return, recovers its attested cost of the floor, runs genuine fraud controls, and a real financial crime is reachable by targeted judicial contract, while mass financial surveillance is never opened.

### Object model

- **payment**: a typed transfer instruction on the $ rail carrying a payer account, a payee account, an amount in the settlement unit, a currency, a purpose reference where one exists, a tier class per R2-I4, and a submission timestamp. A payment is an instruction until it settles; it is not itself value and never carries an Ae leg.
- **settlement_posting**: the R2 posting on the $ rail that discharges a payment, sequenced under the payer account's sole sequencer, final per its R2b tier class. Balance is derived from postings per R2-I5 and never stored authoritatively.
- **money_holding**: a derived $ rail balance over an account's sealed checkpoint plus folded legs (R2-I5). PAY defines no second balance store.
- **basic_payment_capability**: the low-barrier account object that discharges the payment-access floor: able to hold, receive and send the essential minimum, openable under a provisional or minimal identity, with no minimum balance and no bundled precondition, uncapped and non-exhaustible at the essential-minimum tier, and fee-free to the floor-holder.
- **bridge_account**: a minimal-assurance provisional account keyed to a biometric or attested-witness enrolment token rather than a state ID, sufficient to bind the COMP-I17 own-account draw and receive top-ups, carrying the cash-like privacy shape below threshold, non-transferable, and upgrading to a full basic_payment_capability on later identity resolution. The delivery mechanism of last resort so PAY-I8 has an operable path for the truly undocumented.
- **offline_credential**: a signed, monotonically-decrementing spending credential pre-authored to a single floor-holder with a bounded offline ceiling, so that offline spend is detectable for double-spend at true-up when settlement returns.
- **rail_operator**: the entity operating a payment or settlement rail (clearing, interchange, messaging, or final settlement), typically a natural monopoly and an essential facility.
- **payment_identifier**: the routable handle (account or alias) a subscriber uses to be paid, portable per PAY-I7.
- **access_action**: any refusal, freeze, throttle, hold, closure, or de-preferencing of a party's payment access, each a first-class logged event testable under the PAY-I10 two-part gate and PAY-I12.
- **fraud_hold**: a bounded, evidence-based hold on a specific suspected transaction or, at population scope, on a specific attested fraud signature, released automatically at a published window absent an A6 escalation (PAY-I12, PAY-I21).
- **investigation_contract**: a targeted, necessity-found, time-bounded A6 judicial access contract authorizing per-person read or freeze of payment data, the only lawful route to person-grain financial access (PAY-I14).
- **continuity_successor**: a pre-designated successor operator or the RES receiver-of-last-resort that carries the essential payment minimum without interruption on attested operator insolvency or exit (PAY-I26).

Enforcement: an instruction that carries or implies an Ae transfer is void ab initio and routes to A18 as attempted Ae laundering under R2-I6 and the RINT non-netting rule.

- **PAY-I25 (anti-structuring is bounded at the instrument and senses only at cohort grain).** The cash-like tier's anti-structuring defense SHALL be the R2-I4 threshold plus a per-instrument velocity or aggregation cap enforced at the instrument (token) level, not the holder, bounding splitting without linking holders; detection SHALL sense only at S5 cohort or corridor grain, and a per-person read SHALL be reached only through a targeted, time-bounded, loud-audited A6 investigation_contract seeded by a specific out-of-band predicate (a fraud report, a predicate offense), never by standing cohort mining of small payments, and bulk re-identification of the cash-like tier is barred. Purpose: bound structuring at the token so the privacy floor is not the price of an open laundering channel, while never converting the tier into a surveillance feed. Predicate: the velocity cap is applied at the instrument; absent an out-of-band predicate and an A6 contract, non-detection of a structured pattern is the accepted price of the privacy floor, not a compliance gap to close by surveillance. Enforcement: exceeding the per-instrument cap throttles or flags the instrument, not the holder; a standing cohort-mining or re-identification attempt over the cash-like tier is over_collection under R2c and void (MONEY cites this for the shared rule).

- **PAY-I26 (continuity of the essential payment minimum on operator insolvency or exit).** On attested operator insolvency or exit from an essential payment function, the essential payment minimum SHALL continue without interruption via a pre-designated continuity_successor or the RES receiver-of-last-resort, funded from the C1-I16 harm-capacity escrow and the COMP-I17 reserve. A holding derived from R2 postings survives operator insolvency because the ledger, not the operator's book, is authoritative (R2-I5). Purpose: an operator failing cannot strand a person's floor to transact or their settled holding. Predicate: the successor-designation actor is the RES floor_steward, the handover is a first-class S4 handover audit event, and the person's derived holding is reconstructed from surviving R2 roots regardless of the failed operator's records. Enforcement: an interruption of the essential minimum on operator exit is a live breach under PAY-I8 routing to A18; a successor that fails to carry the floor bears the A18-I4 ladder, and no clawback against a person's derived holding follows the operator's insolvency.

### Protocols

Every protocol below that performs a freeze, hold, floor delivery, or continuity handover SHALL emit an S4 audit event recording the actor, the input, the output, the cited invariant, and the timestamp (C30).

- **PAY-P1 (submit and settle a payment).** Validate the instruction (payer authority via S1, currency, tier per R2-I4), route through the operator subject to PAY-I6 carriage, run any PAY-I12 fraud check, then append the settlement_posting under R2b finality (PAY-I2). Fast-tier legs MAY self-execute escrowed pending a gated leg and unwind automatically if the gate refuses (R1 tier-split execution).

- **PAY-P2 (open the payment-access floor).** On any natural person lacking a working basic_payment_capability, the COMP-I17 standing reserve delivers one on the person's own-account basis under the PAY-I9 minimal-identity path, or through the PAY-I24 bridge_account where the person is undocumented, answering eligibility by S6 predicate where possible, with no minimum balance, no bundled precondition, and no fee to the floor-holder (PAY-I23). Passing the delivery deadline (SLA) is a live breach compelling continued delivery, not a suspension (PAY-I8); a person still without a working capability opens an A18 floor-breach case. S4-logged.

- **PAY-P3 (contest an access_action or a hold).** Any refusal, freeze, hold, or closure is logged with a reasoned ground and tested against the PAY-I10 two-part gate; it is contestable per A18-I34; the essential minimum is restored first and is non-severable while the contest runs (PAY-I20); the operator litigates its money-recovery after (PAY-I10, PAY-I11); floor-critical business outflows are carved under PAY-I22; a coercive purpose or effect escalates on the A18-I4 ladder. Each freeze, hold, and release emits an S4 audit event.

- **PAY-P4 (targeted investigation access).** An A6 case issues an investigation_contract naming the subject or a narrow predicate, finding necessity and setting expiry; the operator serves exactly the scoped legs, S4-logged to a loud audit; any person-grain freeze under the contract still preserves the essential minimum (PAY-I20); the contract lapses on expiry and any bulk or standing request is refused (PAY-I14, PAY-I25).

- **PAY-P5 (declare and reconcile a scoped fraud hold).** An operator declares a PAY-I12 transaction hold or a PAY-I21 signature-keyed mass hold, states its evidentiary basis and its published window, serves the essential minimum throughout (PAY-I19, PAY-I20), releases automatically at the window absent an A6 escalation, and admits per-account contest under A18-I34. Declaration, each acceptance under the degraded tier, and each release emit an S4 audit event.

- **PAY-P6 (continuity handover on operator insolvency or exit).** On attested operator insolvency or exit, the RES floor_steward activates the pre-designated continuity_successor or receiver-of-last-resort, funded from C1-I16 escrow and COMP-I17, reconstructs each person's derived holding from surviving R2 roots (R2-I5), and carries the essential minimum without interruption (PAY-I26). The handover is a first-class S4 audit event.

### Lifecycle and edge cases

- **The unbanked, stateless, or no-fixed-address person.** Not an eligibility question: PAY-I8 and PAY-P2 deliver the basic capability upfront under provisional identity, and the PAY-I24 bridge_account enrols the truly undocumented; exclusion is a breach to be cured, not a status to be verified.
- **Debanking for viewpoint or lawful conduct.** Tested by the PAY-I10 two-part gate on purpose or effect, routed through A18-I4; the essential minimum is never cut, and a lawful business gets a reasoned, contestable ground under PAY-I11.
- **A freeze reaching the essential minimum.** Inoperative to that extent under PAY-I20, even under a valid A6 contract; a good-faith reach is cured by restoration (RINT-I19), a deliberate reach bears the A18-I4 ladder.
- **Genuine fraud in flight.** The operator's PAY-I12 hold is narrow, evidenced, floor-preserving, and auto-releases on the published window absent an A6 escalation; a live scam or a breached instrument range is met by the PAY-I21 signature-keyed mass hold, keyed to the signature and never to identity.
- **A business freeze that would starve its workers.** Floor-critical payroll and floor-good procurement remain settleable under PAY-I22 while the above-floor dispute is contested; only discretionary business activity is restricted.
- **A real money-laundering or terrorist-financing case.** Reached by the targeted PAY-I14 contract, seeded by an out-of-band predicate, never by bulk cohort mining of the cash-like tier (PAY-I25); the ordinary payer is not surveilled.
- **Cross-border remittance under pressure.** Corridor priced within C1-I7, not blocked to coerce (PAY-I16); a genuine sanctions bar operates on its stated ground with contest.
- **Rail outage.** The degraded tier of PAY-I19 keeps water and food purchasable on a single pre-authored, monotonically-decrementing offline_credential, trues up append-only with double-spend detectable, and any essential-minimum shortfall is absorbed by COMP-I17, never clawed back below the floor.
- **Operator insolvency or exit.** The essential minimum continues via the pre-designated successor or receiver-of-last-resort under PAY-I26; the derived holding survives because the R2 ledger, not the operator's book, is authoritative.
- **Attempted Ae laundering via payments.** An instruction implying an Ae leg fails closed at append and routes to A18 under R2-I6 and the RINT non-netting rule (PAY-I1).

## SUPPLY water and food supply chains and safety (F4a)

### Purpose

F4a models the physical chains that deliver potable water and staple food from source, through treatment and processing, to the point of use. The cargo is not ordinary freight: potable water and staple food are RES-I1 life_critical delivered floors. SUPPLY therefore rides the F3 carriage machinery for its routing, custody, and liability, but adds a life-safety harm surface and a commons-stewardship regime that ordinary carriage does not carry.

F4 is partitioned into two owners. SUPPLY (F4a) OWNS the supply chain, food and water safety, the water commons, cornering, and traceability. NOURISH (F4b) OWNS the delivered water and food capability-floor state, its COMP-I17 own-account delivery, the floor_steward routing, affordability, non-priceability, and the coercive supply-cutoff bar. A single floor_delivery_state has one obligated deliverer, NOURISH; SUPPLY does not own or run the floor. Concretely SUPPLY owns and only owns: (1) the supply_chain object that composes source, treatment, and distribution over F3 legs; (2) the water SOURCE as a P2-I7 rent-bearing commons instance; (3) the food and water SAFETY surface as an A18 life-safety harm class; (4) the TRACEABILITY and RECALL machinery that makes contamination containment decidable and bounded; (5) the anti-cornering rule on life_critical staples and water. SUPPLY does not re-model the F3 route, leg, or custody chain (it composes them); it does not re-model K8 production (food processing is K8, cited); it does not re-model the R2 two-ledger AE/$ system, the F8 instruments, the K8-I10 waste-to-product rule, the P2-I7 water-commons grant, or the A18-I4 coercion gate. It does not define the floor; it CITES NOURISH-I1/I3 for the floor and supplies the physical-carriage and safety attestation input to it. A flow never owns the water commons or the food content it carries; it owns the record of carriage and the safety attestation, nothing more.

Both flanks are load-bearing throughout. The water-drinker and the eater SHALL receive a safe supply, delivered not merely certified, and SHALL be protected from contamination and from an essential-supply interruption used to coerce. The honest producer, farmer, treatment operator, and carrier SHALL be protected from a fabricated-contamination claim used to extort, foreclose, or seize market share, by a decidable contamination-and-recall standard that a false accuser cannot weaponize and that never opens the door to mass surveillance of who eats or drinks what.

### Object model

- **supply_chain**: an ordered composition `source -> treatment -> distribution` where each hop is one or more F3 legs with an F3 custody chain. Carries `commodity_class` (potable_water | staple_food | non_staple_food | ingredient), `criticality` (defaults life_critical for potable_water and staple_food per RES-I1), `lot_graph` (the provenance DAG), and `safety_state`.
- **source**: for water, a commons instance (aquifer, surface body, catchment, desalination intake) held under a P2-I7 extraction grant; for food, a farm, fishery, or import origin. Carries `extraction_grant_ref` (P2-I7), `abstraction_meter`, `escrow_ref` (C1-I16 harm-capacity escrow), `aggregate_safe_yield_cap` (held by the P2 commons steward, SUPPLY-I19), `downstream_dependents`.
- **treatment**: a potabilization or food-safety processing node (filtration, disinfection, pasteurization, sterilization). Carries `process_spec`, `assurance_attestation`, `hazard_barriers`. Food processing that transforms mass is a K8 production node and books mass balance and embodied anti-value on K8, not here.
- **distribution**: the delivery network to the point of use (reticulated main, standpipe, retailer, ration point, delivered floor drop). Carries `connection_ref` (CONN), `carriage_attestation_state` (the SUPPLY-I2 input to the NOURISH floor state).
- **lot**: the smallest recall-decidable unit, with `epcis_events` (commissioning, aggregation, transformation, transport, observation), `parent_lots`, `child_lots`.
- **safety_event**: a `{contamination | adulteration | pathogen | spoilage | mislabel}` finding, with `hazard_class` (acute sicken-or-kill and cumulative/chronic pathways), `evidence_ref`, `confidence_class` (calibrated at A10), `knowledge_state` (unknown | suspected | known), `disclosure_state`.
- **recall**: a decidable containment action bounded to a lot set, with `trigger`, `reach_set`, `holdback_release_condition`, `audit_ref` (S4).

### Invariants

**SUPPLY-I1 (supply chain composition on F3).** A supply_chain for potable water or staple food SHALL be represented as a composition of F3 legs with a continuous F3 custody chain from source to point of use, and SHALL carry a `criticality` field defaulting to RES-I1 life_critical for potable_water and staple_food. Purpose: reuse the carriage, custody, and F3-I7 joint-carrier-liability machinery rather than forking it, while flagging that the cargo triggers the delivered-floor and life-safety regimes. Predicate: no potable_water or staple_food consignment is admitted to distribution without an unbroken custody chain whose terminal leg resolves to a point of use. Hook: a custody-chain gap on a life_critical commodity is a per-leg F3 defect and blocks the SUPPLY-I2 carriage attestation until closed.

**SUPPLY-I2 (physical carriage and safety attestation input to the NOURISH floor).** The delivered water and food floor state is owned by NOURISH (NOURISH-I1/I3); SUPPLY SHALL supply only the physical-carriage-and-safety attestation that is an INPUT to that state, and SHALL NOT define a parallel floor or open a separate enforcement route. The attestation SHALL bind to the physical fact of a safe, actually-received supply at a reachable point of use, not to possession of a certificate: a certificate with no delivered safe supply, a food desert, or a water-poor settlement is a false attestation feeding a NOURISH floor breach, not compliance. The delivery obligation for a mandated uneconomic reach routes to the RES floor_steward with a duty of means funded through the COMP-I17 reserve; an operator mandated to deliver below cost SHALL be compensated at attested cost (C1-I7), and no private operator SHALL be conscripted to serve below cost, the remedy being reserve-funded reach. Affordability is not SUPPLY's concern: inability to pay never gates the water floor (NOURISH-I12), and the food affordability floor is NOURISH-I4; SUPPLY keeps only the physical-delivery and safety breach as its own. Predicate: `carriage_attestation_state` = satisfied only when carriage is unbroken (SUPPLY-I1), hazard barriers are evidenced (SUPPLY-I5/I11), and a reachable safe point of use exists; a false or paper attestation is a live SUPPLY breach. Hook: a physical-delivery or safety failure feeds the NOURISH floor state; where uneconomic reach is the cause, the RES floor_steward funds the reserve-funded reach; SUPPLY does not itself run the floor enforcement route.

**SUPPLY-I3 (water source is a stewarded commons: cite P2-I7 and C1-I16).** A water source is held only under the P2-I7 rent-bearing, time-bounded, revocable extraction grant, whose rent basis, revocation trigger, and concentration ceiling across the O1 acting-in-concert graph are owned by P2-I7 and not restated here; the source-restoration financial assurance is the C1-I16 harm-capacity escrow, not a parallel bond. SUPPLY keeps only the F4-specific rule: no abstraction node SHALL enter distribution without a live P2-I7 grant reference and a live C1-I16 escrow reference. Predicate: an abstraction node lacking either a live `extraction_grant_ref` (P2-I7) or a live `escrow_ref` (C1-I16) is barred from distribution. Hook: absent grant or escrow, the abstraction node is suspended and the commons steward recovers restoration from the C1-I16 escrow.

**SUPPLY-I4 (over-abstraction and downstream depletion are caught).** Abstraction that depletes the commons below its stewarded recharge or safe-yield envelope, or that measurably deprives a downstream user or a D4 habitat/ecology floor of water, SHALL be caught as an externality on the AE overlay and, above the A18-I4 threshold, feed a floor breach. Purpose: prevent an upstream grantee from draining the resource or a downstream community. Predicate: metered abstraction over a window exceeds the grant envelope OR a downstream dependent's delivered floor drops below RES-I1 attributable to the abstraction. Hook: the P2-I7 revocation trigger fires; restoration is funded from the C1-I16 escrow (SUPPLY-I3); deliberate depletion of a downstream user's life_critical supply to coerce or foreclose feeds the NOURISH floor state and routes through A18-I4 as an essential-access breach and is an aggravating factor.

**SUPPLY-I5 (food and water safety is an A18 life-safety harm surface, acute and cumulative).** A contamination, adulteration, or pathogen in potable water or food that can sicken or kill SHALL be an A18 harm on the life-safety surface, not a mere quality defect, and the hazard_class SHALL cover BOTH acute sicken-or-kill pathways at the delivered dose AND cumulative or chronic pathways that harm on repeated delivered dose over time (for example lead, arsenic, PFAS, nitrate). Purpose: put physical harm to eaters and drinkers on the coercion-and-harm rail whether the harm lands in one dose or accumulates. Predicate: hazard_class resolves to a credible sicken-or-kill pathway at the delivered dose OR a credible cumulative-harm pathway on repeated delivered dose. Hook: a confirmed hazard triggers the SUPPLY-I9 recall and books the harm to the responsible K8 producer or treatment operator with its embodied anti-value; concealment of a KNOWN cumulative-hazard contaminant is a SUPPLY-I6 concealment-class harm carrying the perpetual flip NOW, failing closed to concealment treatment pending A10 dose-and-latency calibration rather than exempt until calibrated; the non-priceable criminal class applies where the conduct crosses the A18 criminal threshold.

**SUPPLY-I6 (known or decidably-suspected concealed contamination is a concealment-class harm with the perpetual flip).** A contamination, adulteration, or pathogen that is KNOWN or suspected-above-threshold to a producer, treatment operator, or carrier and is concealed, or whose disclosure is suppressed, SHALL be an A18 concealment-class harm carrying the perpetual flip, mirroring the built-environment safety-defect-concealment pattern. Purpose: the gravamen is concealment of a life-safety fact, which does not expire. Predicate: the concealment duty attaches when the operator holds specific evidence, a positive internal test, a validated complaint cluster, or an exceedance reading, meeting a published confidence_class for the hazard (calibration routed to A10), NOT on unsubstantiated worry; AND `disclosure_state` = withheld while the affected lot remains reachable by a consumer. Safe harbor: prompt disclosure of a newly-formed above-threshold suspicion discharges the duty and bears no concealment harm. Hook: perpetual liability attaches to the concealing natural persons via the O1 veil rule; no autonomous-agent path may leave a concealed life-safety hazard without a natural-person bearer; sham reclassification of contaminated output to evade recall is externality concealment under A18 and, if routed as waste-to-product, under K8-I10.

**SUPPLY-I7 (decidable contamination standard, both flanks).** A contamination finding SHALL be admissible against an operator only when it states a hazard_class, an evidence_ref traceable to a sampled lot, a confidence_class, and a dose pathway, and SHALL be contestable under A18-I34 suspensive contest with adjudication at A6. Purpose: protect the eater and drinker by making real contamination actionable, AND protect the honest producer from a fabricated or unfalsifiable claim used to extort or foreclose. Predicate: a finding lacking a lot-traceable evidence_ref or a stated dose pathway is inadmissible as a basis for recall or seizure. Hook: a fabricated contamination claim advanced with knowledge of its falsity or reckless disregard of the truth AND coupled to an extortive or foreclosing gain is itself an A18 harm (coercion or fraud) against the accused operator and is priced accordingly (SUPPLY-I17); a good-faith precautionary hold on genuine suspicion is protected under the good-faith-alarm safe harbor and does not become a fabrication claim.

**SUPPLY-I8 (traceability and provenance).** Every life_critical lot SHALL carry an EPCIS-shaped custody record sufficient to resolve, from any downstream observation, the upstream source, treatment node, and sibling lots within a bounded number of hops. Purpose: make recall decidable and bounded, and make provenance claims (origin, organic, treatment) auditable rather than asserted. Predicate: for any lot L, the lot_graph resolves parent_lots and child_lots to closure within the declared bound. Hook: a life_critical lot admitted to distribution without a resolvable lot_graph is a per-lot SUPPLY-I1 defect; the traceability record is subject-owned per SUPPLY-I14 and is not a licence for consumer-level surveillance.

**SUPPLY-I9 (decidable, bounded recall).** A recall SHALL compute a reach_set that is the minimal lot closure containing the contaminated lot and every lot commingled or derived from it, and no more. Purpose: contain harm to the eater and drinker while sparing the honest producer an over-broad, market-destroying dragnet. Predicate: reach_set = transitive closure over commingling and transformation edges from the confirmed lot(s); lots provably outside the closure are excluded. Hook: over-broad recall beyond the decidable closure, used to damage a competitor or supplier, is an A18 harm against affected operators (SUPPLY-I17); under-scoped recall that leaves a contaminated lot reachable is a SUPPLY-I5 breach and, if the gap is known and concealed, a SUPPLY-I6 concealment harm.

**SUPPLY-I10 (food processing is K8 production).** Food processing that transforms mass SHALL be a K8 production node and SHALL book its mass balance, embodied anti-value, and use-phase on K8; SUPPLY SHALL NOT re-model production. Purpose: keep one mass-balance authority and avoid double-modelling. Predicate: any treatment node with a `commodity_class` change or yield loss registers a K8 transformation event. Hook: an output reclassified from a processing residue or condemned lot into a saleable product carries the K8-I10 waste-to-product embodied anti-value and disposal-contingent tail; sham beneficial use forfeits the holdback and is A18 concealment.

**SUPPLY-I11 (cold chain and spoilage as a joint safety and waste surface).** A required cold chain or shelf-life control SHALL be a hazard_barrier whose breach is both a SUPPLY-I5 safety event (spoilage or pathogen growth) and a WASTE event (the spoiled lot is anti-value to book), and SHALL be evidenced by continuous custody-linked monitoring on the F3 leg. Purpose: bind temperature and time excursions to both the harm surface and the waste ledger so a spoiled life_critical lot cannot be quietly re-sold or dumped. Predicate: a monitored excursion beyond the process_spec envelope flips the lot's safety_state to at_risk and, if unrecoverable, to condemned. Hook: a condemned lot books its embodied anti-value on WASTE and its disposal-contingent tail per K8-I10; re-sale of a condemned life_critical lot is a SUPPLY-I5 harm and, if the condemnation is known and hidden, a SUPPLY-I6 concealment harm; a genuine downgrade to a safe lower-grade use (for example, animal feed within its own safety envelope) is protected beneficial use, not dumping.

**SUPPLY-I12 (operators under Agro/Health oversight and the O3 labor floor).** SUPPLY source, treatment, processing, and distribution operators SHALL be registered under the Agro/Health oversight body and SHALL meet the O3 labor floor for their supply, farm, fishery, and treatment workers. Purpose: put life_critical operators under competent safety oversight and prevent the safe-supply mandate from being financed by unsafe or sub-floor labor. Predicate: an operator without a live Agro/Health registration, or with a labor arrangement below O3, is non-conforming. Hook: non-conformance suspends the operator's SUPPLY-I2 carriage attestation; the GOV level-capture guard applies so that operators cannot capture their own oversight body; O3 breaches route to the labor floor authority, not to a safety waiver.

**SUPPLY-I13 (import safety and border checks).** Imported potable water, food, and ingredients SHALL cross a border safety check that verifies the SUPPLY-I8 provenance record and samples per a risk-based, published protocol before admission to domestic distribution. Purpose: extend the eater-and-drinker safety floor to imports, AND give the honest importer a predictable, non-arbitrary standard that cannot be used as a disguised trade weapon. Predicate: an import lot is admitted only with a resolvable provenance record and a passing or waived-by-risk-tier check; a check failure yields a stated hazard_class and evidence_ref (SUPPLY-I7). Hook: an arbitrary or pretextual border hold with no SUPPLY-I7 evidence, used to protect a domestic incumbent or extort an importer, is an A18 harm (SUPPLY-I17) and a COMP anti-abuse breach; a genuine risk-based hold on stated evidence is protected.

**SUPPLY-I14 (supply-chain data sovereignty).** Supply-chain, provenance, and consumption records SHALL follow the F3-I18 logistics data-sovereignty pattern: the owner owns the record, cross-reads are S3 projections under S2 contracts and S4-logged, the state senses at S5 cohort or corridor grain, and a per-consignment or per-person read (a targeted recall, an AML lookup, a fraud probe) needs consent or a targeted A6 judicial contract. Purpose: make recall and safety work without building a map of who eats and drinks what. Predicate: no bulk per-person consumption or per-consignment read occurs absent consent or an A6 contract; network-scale movement aggregates are a data commons at S5 grain. Hook: a bulk read of consumption or per-consignment data outside S5 cohort grain and without an A6 contract is an S-layer breach and, if used to profile or coerce consumers, an A18 harm; a network-scale scarcity or contamination-spread aggregate at cohort grain is permitted for resilience sensing.

**SUPPLY-I15 (essential-supply interruption is coercion unless it passes the PAY gate).** An interruption of potable water or staple food supply SHALL be tested against the single decidable two-part coercive-cutoff gate owned by PAY (PAY-I10): the interruption is coercion, by PURPOSE OR by EFFECT, and routes through A18-I4 with floor_breach_severity as an input, UNLESS it carries a lawful, evidenced, contestable predicate (a settlement-finality, adjudicated-non-payment, or safety ground), is time-bounded, remains contestable under A18-I34, and is floor-preserving. Purpose: stop essential supply from becoming a punishment lever while leaving a genuine safety suspension lawful. Predicate: an interruption is coercion if it lacks the lawful evidenced predicate, OR persists past its published window without an A6 escalation, OR is shown keyed to the party's identity, viewpoint, or association; a mere STATED basis of non-payment or safety does NOT exempt a pretext, since a coercer never states a coercive basis. Water carve-out: the water floor quantum is carved out entirely, it is delivered without payment (NOURISH-I12), so the water floor is NEVER conditioned on payment and a payment-driven water cutoff can never reach the floor quantum. Hook: coercive interruption routes to A18-I4; a lawful safety-driven suspension (a contaminated main, a condemned lot) is not a cutoff and SHALL restore the floor by alternative delivery through the NOURISH floor_steward; a good-faith-but-wrong suspension is cured by restoration (RINT-I19), not automatically classed as coercion.

**SUPPLY-I16 (anti-cornering on life_critical staples and water, decidable).** A cornering finding on potable water, a staple food, or a chokepoint treatment or distribution asset SHALL require an affirmative COMP-I11 price-discovery-corruption showing PLUS evidence of intent to extort or coordinated withholding across the O1 acting-in-concert graph; inventory level alone SHALL NEVER be sufficient. Purpose: prevent starving or gouging a captive population on a life_critical essential, AND protect ordinary inventory and genuine resilience stockpiling from being mislabeled cornering, while letting a genuine attested-cost natural monopoly recover its costs. Predicate: the interim default, pending the A10 carrying-window benchmark, fails toward protecting ordinary inventory; but DURING a measured essential-access floor breach in a staple-food or water market, accumulation beyond a conservative published per-staple carrying window is presumptively cornering with the essential-access-floor breach as the aggravator, rebuttable by the holder showing genuine RES resilience or operational need. Hook: cornering routes to COMP enforcement per COMP-I11 and C2-I5 with the floor breach as an aggravator; a water or distribution natural monopoly is priced under the C1-I7 attested-cost corridor with the C1-I16 ex-ante-peak harm-capacity escrow, and over-recovery outside the corridor is clawed back through C1.

**SUPPLY-I17 (fabricated-contamination and foreclosure protection).** A contamination or recall claim, a border hold, or a safety condemnation used as a pretext to extort a producer, foreclose a supplier, or seize a rival's market SHALL be an A18 harm against the targeted operator, contestable under A18-I34 and adjudicated at A6, with the false accuser bearing the harm. Purpose: complete the honest-operator flank so that the powerful safety machinery of SUPPLY cannot itself be weaponized. Predicate: a claim shown under SUPPLY-I7 to lack a lot-traceable evidence_ref or dose pathway, advanced with KNOWLEDGE of its falsity OR reckless disregard of the truth (never mere negligence), AND coupled to an extortive or foreclosing gain. Hook: the fabricated claim is priced as coercion or fraud; a good-faith erroneous precautionary hold, later cleared, is protected under the good-faith-alarm safe harbor and bears no harm, so that operators are not deterred from raising genuine safety alarms.

**SUPPLY-I18 (continuity of essential supply on operator insolvency or exit).** On attested operator insolvency or exit from an essential water or food supply function, the physical supply of the life_critical stream SHALL continue without interruption via a pre-designated successor operator or the RES receiver-of-last-resort / floor_steward, funded from the C1-I16 escrow and the COMP-I17 reserve. Purpose: an operator's failure SHALL NOT drop the eater's or drinker's floor. Predicate: no essential supply function ceases carriage until a successor or the RES receiver has assumed the SUPPLY-I1 custody chain. Hook: the successor-designation actor is the Agro/Health oversight body, with the P2 register for water sources; the handover writes an S4 handover audit event; the floor state itself is owned and enforced by NOURISH, SUPPLY supplying only the continued physical carriage and safety attestation.

**SUPPLY-I19 (shared-source aggregate safe-yield cap and pro-rata apportionment).** A shared water source SHALL carry a single stewarded aggregate safe-yield cap held by the P2 commons steward, and the sum of the individual P2-I7 grant envelopes drawing on it SHALL be no greater than that cap. Purpose: prevent a commons drawdown that no single grantee's meter would reveal. Predicate: as aggregate metered draw approaches the cap, the steward SHALL pro-rata reduce all grants (revocable per P2-I7) BEFORE any downstream floor breach; the decidable attribution test is cap-versus-sum, not a per-grantee causation test. Hook: the collective-breach bearer is the steward's failure to enforce the aggregate cap, not any single grantee; breach of the cap routes to the steward under P2-I7 and, where a downstream life_critical floor drops, feeds the NOURISH floor state and A18-I4 as an essential-access breach.

**SUPPLY-I20 (physical-impossibility triage under true scarcity).** When attested available supply of a life_critical stream is below the aggregate floor even after the COMP-I17 reserve draw (drought, aquifer depletion, mass staple recall), the RES emergency regime SHALL apply an equal-per-capita rationed allocation of the available quantum, weighted by life-stage and medical need. Purpose: a genuine physical shortfall is triaged fairly, not resolved by ability to pay or by hidden priority. Predicate: each person's share = (attested available quantum divided by the weighted population) times that person's life-stage-and-medical-need weight, normalized so the weighted shares sum to the attested available quantum; this rationed allocation is the DISCHARGE of the floor for the attested shortfall duration, NOT a per-household breach. Hook: the shortfall books as a preparedness breach against the reserve-sizing owner (RES), never as per-household coercion; concealment of available supply to defeat the ration, or diversion of the rationed quantum to a paying buyer, is an A18 harm and, where the shortfall was known and hidden, a SUPPLY-I6 concealment harm.

### Lifecycle and edge cases

## NOURISH the water and food capability floor (F4b)

### Purpose

F4b governs the capability floor for potable water and adequate nutrition: the guarantee that every natural person actually holds, in the present tense, enough safe water for life and health and a nutritionally adequate diet, delivered upfront on the person's own-account basis rather than promised on paper. It is a delivery surface, not a pricing surface. The market for water and food clears on the $ rail like any other market: honest farmers, water utilities, wholesalers and retailers discover price and earn it, and the state prices only the residual externality, never the transaction price (this section cites C2-I1 and MVAL-I1, it does not re-model them). What F4b adds is the floor underneath that market: where the cleared price, the physical reach, or a deliberate cutoff puts safe water or adequate food out of a floor-holder's grasp, the polity closes the gap on the person's own account through the COMP-I17 standing reserve, never by commanding, capping, or suppressing the producer's price.

The section is engineered against two symmetric failures. The first is the floor written down but not received: the unbanked household, the food desert, the water-poor settlement, the drought cohort, the family whose water was shut off to punish a missed bill. Each of these is a live breach the moment it is measured, remediable now, not a budget line for next cycle. The second is the floor used as an instrument of control or as a pretext to seize the market: cutoff as coercion, ration-monopoly crowding out the honest retailer, "food security" invoked to administer prices or requisition standing capacity, or mass surveillance of who eats and drinks what. F4b protects the water-poor and the food-insecure AND the honest producer and retailer who is entitled to their market price and to a clear line between a genuine shortage from a real shortfall and a manufactured one.

Ownership split (C2): NOURISH (F4b) OWNS the delivered water and food capability-floor state, its COMP-I17 own-account delivery, the floor_steward routing, affordability and non-priceability, and the coercive supply-cutoff bar; a single floor_delivery_state has one obligated deliverer, and that deliverer is the F4b floor_steward. SUPPLY (F4a) owns the supply chain, food and water safety, the water commons, cornering-as-market-integrity, and traceability, and cites NOURISH-I1 and NOURISH-I3 for the floor rather than authoring a parallel floor definition or a separate enforcement route. F4b owns only the potable-water and nutrition floor and its delivery. It does not own the two-ledger system (R2), the money instrument (the MONEY / F2a surface), the basic payment floor (the PAY / F2b surface), production mass balance and the waste-to-product rule (K8, K8-I10), carriage and safety of the supply chain (F4a, the SUPPLY surface), the reserve and resilience machinery (RES), the water commons and extraction grant (P2-I7), or the coercion gate (A18-I4). It cites each and re-models none.

### Object model

- `floor_holder`: every natural person, the bearer of the F4b floor, carrying it on their own-account basis (COMP-I17). Citizenship is not a precondition for the subsistence bottom (this tracks the flows-layer dignity bottom, RES-I14).
- `water_floor_quantum`: the per-person quantity of potable water sufficient for life and health, sourced from the floor-magnitude owner (Distributive-Justice-and-Capability-Floors.md, which owns the magnitudes and theta), carrying a potability standard, not merely a volume.
- `nutrition_floor`: a nutritionally adequate diet for the person, defined on nutritional adequacy (macro and micronutrient sufficiency for the person's life stage and condition), not on a bare calorie count.
- `access_predicate`: the decidable reach test, whether the floor-holder can obtain the water_floor_quantum and nutrition_floor of adequate quality within a bounded budget of money AND travel time from their settlement of residence.
- `affordability_gap`: the shortfall between the $-rail cleared price of the floor quantum and the price reachable by the floor-holder, the quantity the COMP-I17 top-up funds on the person's own account (for food and for above-floor or non-reticulated water); for the reticulated potable-water floor quantum the same gap is closed as a direct cost-recovery leg to the utility, not as a household top-up (NOURISH-I12).
- `floor_steward`: the named, A12-mandated obligated deliverer for a water or food floor cohort and settlement, the RES-I25 role bound here, the single party to whom a floor miss routes and the successor-of-last-resort designee under continuity (NOURISH-I21).
- `essential_supply_relation`: a standing supply of floor-quantum water or food to a floor-holder household, the object a cutoff acts on.
- `strategic_reserve`: staple-food and potable-water reserve stock, owned and sized by the RES reserve machinery (RES-I6), cited here, not authored here.

### Invariants

- NOURISH-I1 (potable water and adequate nutrition are delivered life_critical floors). Potable water sufficient for life and health, and a nutritionally adequate diet, are RES-I1 life_critical delivered capability floors, held by every floor_holder on the own-account standing-reserve basis (COMP-I17), delivered upfront and never merely declared. Purpose: fix water and food at the highest criticality tier so no plan, price, or cutoff may discharge them by payment or by paperwork. Predicate: for every floor_holder, the water_floor_quantum and the nutrition_floor are physically received at adequate quality in the current period. Enforcement hook: a shortfall books a capability-floor breach against the floor_steward and routes through A18-I4 with floor_breach_severity as the max_rung input (matching NOURISH-I6, formula not restated here); the magnitudes and theta are owned by Distributive-Justice-and-Capability-Floors.md and cited, not set here.
- NOURISH-I2 (delivered, not declared). A water or nutrition floor recorded as owed but not received is a live breach in the present tense, not a compliance state and not a deferral. Purpose: bar the polity from treating a written entitlement as satisfaction of the floor. Predicate: the unbanked person unable to hold or spend the top-up, the food-desert household failing the access_predicate, and the water-poor settlement below the water_floor_quantum each register as an open breach the moment measured, regardless of any ledger entry to the contrary. Enforcement hook: measured at S5 cohort grain per NOURISH-I15; an open breach opens a remediation obligation with a deadline on the floor_steward (NOURISH-P1).
- NOURISH-I3 (food desert and water poverty are measured, remediable breaches). A settlement or person who cannot reach the floor quantum of adequate-quality water or food within the bounded money-and-time budget of the access_predicate is in a live floor breach, mirroring the mobility transit-desert rule. Purpose: make physical unreachability, not just unaffordability, a breach. Predicate: access_predicate fails for the cohort or person. Enforcement hook: routes to the RES-I25 floor_steward for present-tense remediation, coordinated with the SUPPLY carriage-and-reach surface (F4a) and the PLAN settlement-reach surface; remediation is by extending real reach (a supply point, a delivery leg, a water connection), never by declaring the desert closed on paper. Both flanks: the honest retailer is not conscripted to serve an unprofitable point at a loss, the steward's duty-of-means (RES-I25) funds the reach through the standing reserve and ordinary procurement, and a mandated uneconomic delivery is compensated at attested cost (SUPPLY-I2, C1-I7).
- NOURISH-I4 (affordability is closed on the person's account, never by commanding price). Where the $-rail cleared price of the floor quantum exceeds the floor-holder's reach, the affordability_gap for food and for above-floor or non-reticulated water is topped up as delivered quantity funded from the COMP-I17 standing reserve onto the floor-holder's own account, and never by the state setting, capping, flooring, or suppressing the producer's or retailer's price (C2-I1, MVAL-I1). The reticulated potable-water floor quantum is not funded by a household top-up but by the direct utility cost-recovery leg of NOURISH-I12. Purpose: protect the floor-holder AND the honest seller's market price simultaneously. Predicate: top-up magnitude equals the affordability_gap at the current cleared price. Enforcement hook: the top-up settles on the $ rail as the money medium (the MONEY / F2a surface, R2-I1), never netted against or exchanged for any Ae posting (R2-I6, the two-ledger rule); a price administered to deliver the floor is an ultra vires act routing to the acting organ, and a seller cornered or curtailed by administered price has the A18-I34 suspensive contest.
- NOURISH-I5 (quality is part of the floor, not an extra). The floor is potable water and a nutritionally adequate diet, not any water and any calories: unsafe water or a nutritionally deficient food ration delivered in full volume is a floor breach, not floor delivery. Purpose: bar discharge of the floor with contaminated or empty-calorie supply. Predicate: delivered water meets the published potability standard and delivered food meets the nutritional-adequacy standard for the recipient. Enforcement hook: a quality miss books as a floor miss under NOURISH-I2 and, where a known contamination or deficiency was concealed, escalates under NOURISH-I14.
- NOURISH-I6 (cutoff of an essential is coercion, decided by the two-part gate). Cutting off, throttling, or degrading a floor-holder's essential water or food supply to punish, coerce, exclude, or extract compliance is barred; NOURISH owns this supply-cutoff bar and applies the single decidable two-part coercion gate consistently, in the shape PAY owns and this section cites (PAY-I10). Purpose: stop the essential-supply relation from becoming a lever against the person while leaving lawful, floor-preserving interruption available. Predicate (part one, presumptively lawful): an interruption of an essential_supply_relation cites a settlement-finality, fraud, safety, or adjudicated-non-payment predicate, is time-bounded, is contestable under A18-I34, and preserves the floor quantum. Predicate (part two, coercion): the interruption is coercion, by purpose or effect, only if it lacks such a lawful predicate, OR persists past the published window without an A6 escalation, OR is shown keyed to the party's identity, viewpoint, or association; a stated benign basis does not cure an interruption whose effect breaches the floor, and a pretext is caught. Enforcement hook: a part-two interruption routes through A18-I4 with floor_breach_severity as an input (formula not restated); a good-faith-but-wrong interruption is cured by restoration, not automatically classed as coercion. Both flanks preserved by NOURISH-I7.
- NOURISH-I7 (the essential minimum is never cut, unconditionally, the above-floor arrear is handled on the rail). The subsistence minimum of water and food stays connected UNCONDITIONALLY to EVERY floor_holder, with no solvency test and no per-household hardship classification; only above-floor consumption is subject to ordinary collection. Purpose: keep the floor a bare-status guarantee and delete any gate that would require the state to read who is poor before delivering it. Predicate: the below-floor quantum is delivered to every floor_holder regardless of payment; above-floor arrears are collected through ordinary $-rail instruments and credit machinery (the MONEY / F2a and Property-and-Ownership.md / F8 surfaces), initiated by the supplier on its own account relationship (the supplier already knows its own counterparty, so no state per-person read is required), which may restrict above-floor supply but never the floor quantum. Enforcement hook: any restriction that reaches the floor quantum re-enters NOURISH-I6; the supplier's above-floor claim is a normal $-rail receivable, never written off by the floor and never a basis for a state solvency read of the household.
- NOURISH-I8 (no hoarding or cornering of an essential, decidably). Accumulating or withholding staple food or potable water to manufacture scarcity and extort price is caught by the cornering rule (COMP-I11, C2-I5), with breach of the essential-access floor as an explicit aggravating factor. Purpose: stop artificial-scarcity extortion on the one class of goods where scarcity kills, without treating ordinary inventory as an offense. Predicate: a cornering finding requires an affirmative COMP-I11 price-discovery-corruption showing PLUS evidence of intent to extort or coordinated withholding across the O1 acting-in-concert graph; inventory level alone is never sufficient, and the interim default, pending the A10 carrying-window benchmark, fails toward protecting ordinary inventory. During a measured essential-access floor breach in a staple-food or water market, accumulation beyond a conservative published per-staple carrying window is presumptively cornering with the essential-access aggravator, rebuttable by the holder showing genuine RES resilience or operational need. Enforcement hook: routes through C2-I5 and the A18 ladder; the acting-in-concert graph (O1) catches coordinated withholding across nominally separate holders. Both flanks preserved by NOURISH-I9.
- NOURISH-I9 (genuine scarcity is not an offense). A real shortage arising from a genuine shortfall, a drought, a crop failure, a corridor loss, is not cornering, and the honest producer or retailer earns the market-cleared price even when that price is high because the good is genuinely scarce. Purpose: protect the honest seller and preserve the scarcity price signal that pulls supply toward the shortage. Predicate: the price movement tracks a real supply-demand shift with no withholding-to-extort and no price-discovery corruption (the COMP-I11 test); inventory held for prudent operational or reserve reasons is not withholding. Enforcement hook: the scarcity-versus-manufacture determination is made on the C2-I5 / COMP-I11 evidentiary test, not on price level alone; a wrongful cornering finding against an honest seller opens the A18-I34 suspensive contest with the good-faith-precautionary safe harbor (SUPPLY-I17).
- NOURISH-I10 (the life_critical floor is never dischargeable by payment). Neither a market price, a rationing rule, nor a reserve-release schedule may discharge the water or nutrition floor by money in place of quantity: where scarcity pricing would price a below-floor cohort out, the remedy is delivered floor quantity from the reserve plus rationed allocation (RES-I5), never an administered price and never a cash substitute that leaves the household without physical supply. Purpose: keep the life_critical tier a quantity guarantee, immune to being bought out. Predicate: a below-floor cohort exposed to a floor-pricing-out event receives delivered quantum, not a price cap and not a payment in lieu of supply. Enforcement hook: binds RES-I5 and the COMP-I17 reserve funding; a cash-only response to a physical-supply floor miss is itself a floor miss.
- NOURISH-I11 (food security, strategic reserves, and drought or famine resilience). Staple-food and potable-water security under scarcity, drought, and famine is governed by the RES resilience and reserve regime (RES-I1 tiering, RES-I3 pre-authored regimes, RES-I4 floors-first ladders, RES-I6 reserve machinery, RES-I7 release discipline), which F4b binds by citation and does not re-author. Purpose: give the water and food floor a pre-funded resilience substrate without duplicating or diverging from the flows-layer reserve machinery. Predicate: staple food and potable water carry sized, dispersed, standing-funded reserves per RES-I6, released only on pre-declared triggers per RES-I7. Enforcement hook: an off-trigger or price-management release of a food or water reserve is caught by RES-I7; reserve sizing shortfalls for a life_critical class are a preparedness breach (RES-I16). Both flanks: reserve stock rotates through the ordinary market before expiry (RES-I28), so the reserve serves floors without becoming a standing thumb on the honest seller's market.
- NOURISH-I12 (the right to water is non-priceable at the floor, the utility is fully compensated). The reticulated potable-water floor quantum is delivered non-priceable, which means the FLOOR-HOLDER is never charged for it, NOT that the operator is uncompensated. No organ, utility, or extraction-grant holder may condition the floor quantum on a transaction price to the floor-holder; and the utility recovers its full attested cost of the floor quantum (C1-I7) DIRECTLY from the COMP-I17 standing reserve on the person's own-account basis, settled on the $ rail, never zeroed against the operator and never routed through a household top-up. Purpose: place the life-and-health minimum of water beyond pricing to the person while keeping a live cost-recovery leg to the operator, and leave above-floor water a normal $-rail good. Predicate: the water_floor_quantum is delivered to every floor_holder unconditioned on payment; the operator's attested floor-quantum cost is settled from the reserve on the $ rail; consumption above the floor clears on the $ rail at the utility's attested-cost corridor price (C1-I7). Enforcement hook: a price attached to the floor quantum charged to the floor-holder is an ultra vires act and, if used to punish or exclude, coercion under NOURISH-I6; a floor quantum delivered with no cost-recovery leg to the operator is a breach of the operator flank, not compliance, and books against the funding organ; the utility's legitimate cost recovery for above-floor supply runs through C1-I7, unaffected.
- NOURISH-I13 (water is a stewarded commons the flow carries, never owns). A water supplier holds an extraction grant, not the water: extraction is under the P2-I7 rent-bearing, time-bounded, revocable grant, never freehold, with the concentration ceiling applied across the O1 acting-in-concert graph, and the F4b flow never owns the water commons it carries (the commons and its safety are owned by the SUPPLY / F4a surface and by Property-Rent-and-Commons.md, cited here). Purpose: prevent enclosure of the water commons and prevent an extraction grant from being leveraged into a floor-breaching chokehold. Predicate: extraction runs under a live P2-I7 grant; grant concentration across the concert graph stays under the ceiling. Enforcement hook: leveraging an extraction grant to withhold floor-quantum water re-enters NOURISH-I8 and NOURISH-I6; grant terms, rent, and revocation are owned by Property-Rent-and-Commons.md and cited, not set here.
- NOURISH-I14 (concealed water or food safety defect is a concealment-class harm). A known potable-water contamination or food-safety defect that is concealed is an A18 concealment-class harm carrying the perpetual flip, mirroring the structural safety-defect-concealment pattern (Buildings-Infrastructure-and-Settlement.md), and a waste or non-food input reclassified into the food or water stream carries the K8-I10 waste-to-product embodied anti-value and disposal-contingent tail. Purpose: make hiding a contamination as grave as the buildings surface makes hiding a structural defect, and close the relabeling laundering path into food and water. Predicate: a known defect, contamination, or adulteration is not disclosed to the recipient cohort and the responsible floor_steward or regulator, OR a stream is reclassified from waste to food or potable water. Enforcement hook: concealment routes to the A18 concealment class with the perpetual flip; a sham food or water reclassification is externality concealment under A18 and carries the K8-I10 tail back to the originating producer.
- NOURISH-I15 (who is food-insecure or water-poor is subject-owned, sensed at cohort grain). Data about who receives a floor top-up, who fails the access_predicate, and whose supply is at risk is subject-owned and default-deny: the state senses at S5 cohort or settlement grain, and a per-person read (to place a top-up, target a recall, or investigate a specific cutoff) needs consent or a targeted A6 judicial contract, never bulk surveillance of consumption, mirroring the F3-I18 logistics data-sovereignty pattern. Purpose: deliver the floor without turning it into a register of who is poor or what each household eats and drinks. Predicate: floor-miss and affordability measurement runs on S5 cohort aggregates; per-consignment or per-person reads carry consent or an A6 contract, every read S4-logged. Enforcement hook: a per-person read outside its S2 or A6 basis, or any collection beyond the S5 cohort grain the delivery needs, is an over-collection and default-deny breach under R2c and the S2/S3/S4 access-and-audit machinery, booking anti-value regardless of outcome; a network-scale food or water movement aggregate is a data commons, not a per-household record.
- NOURISH-I16 (the floor is delivered onto the person's own account, not through a state supply monopoly). The affordability top-up and floor delivery flow onto the floor-holder's own account through the basic payment capability (the PAY floor), leaving the person to obtain water and food from the ordinary market of their choice; the state does not displace the honest retailer or utility with a standing ration-monopoly except inside a live RES emergency regime. Purpose: close the floor without crowding out the honest supplier or capturing the food and water market. Predicate: default delivery is own-account demand into the private market; a state-run distribution monopoly exists only under a live RES declaration and winds down with it (RES-I26). Enforcement hook: a persistent state supply monopoly outside a declared emergency is a market-capture breach under the C2 and COMP discipline; leaving the food-insecure to the unaided market is the NOURISH-I2 breach on the other flank. Where the payment capability is absent, frozen, or unreachable, delivery does not stall but takes the payment-independent physical path (NOURISH-I19).
- NOURISH-I17 (the floor is unconditioned, never weaponized). Delivery of the water or nutrition floor is never conditioned on political compliance, conduct, association, or surrender of identity or data beyond the minimum the delivery mechanism requires, mirroring the O5 anti-weaponization rule and the CONN floor. Purpose: stop the floor from becoming a lever of behavioral control or a data-extraction pretext. Predicate: floor delivery predicate is bare floor-holder status; no compliance, endorsement, or excess-disclosure condition attaches. Enforcement hook: a condition attached to floor delivery is coercion routing through A18-I4; the sub-threshold cash-like tier (R2-I4) lets the smallest floor transactions carry no stable counterparty identifier, so basic subsistence spending is not a surveillance trail.
- NOURISH-I18 (staple food is non-priceable at the floor). Symmetric with water (NOURISH-I12): the staple-food floor quantum is never conditioned on payment for any floor_holder, the affordability gap is always closed on the person's own account (NOURISH-I4) so the household never faces an unaffordable floor price, and above-floor food clears normally on the $ rail. Purpose: guarantee that no floor-holder is priced out of the staple-food floor, while keeping the honest seller paid and the above-floor market intact. Predicate: for every floor_holder the staple-food floor quantum is received without a floor-price charge to the person; the seller is paid at the cleared price through the own-account top-up delivered from the COMP-I17 reserve on the $ rail. Enforcement hook: a floor-quantum food charge that the reserve does not close for a floor-holder is a NOURISH-I2 delivered-not-declared breach; an above-floor food arrear is an ordinary $-rail receivable handled per NOURISH-I7, never reaching the floor quantum.
- NOURISH-I19 (payment-independent physical delivery of the floor). Whenever a floor-holder's payment capability is absent, frozen, or unreachable, the RES-I1 water and food floor quantum is delivered by a direct reserve or ration path under the RES regime, so the life_critical floor is never gated on the PAY floor being live. Purpose: guarantee physical receipt even when the own-account rail cannot carry the top-up, closing the unbanked, frozen-account, and outage gap. Predicate: on an attested absence, freeze, or unreachability of the floor-holder's payment capability, direct reserve or ration delivery of the floor quantum is triggered without waiting for the payment path to be restored. Enforcement hook: a floor miss excused by a dead payment path is itself a floor breach under NOURISH-I1 and NOURISH-I2, routing to the floor_steward; the physical path stands down when the own-account rail is restored (coordinated with the PAY continuity tier).
- NOURISH-I20 (physical-impossibility triage, the rationed floor is the discharge). When attested available supply of a life_critical water or food stream is below the aggregate floor after the reserve draw (drought, aquifer depletion, mass staple recall), the RES emergency regime applies an equal-per-capita rationed allocation of the available quantum, weighted for life stage and medical need; this rationed floor is the DISCHARGE, not a per-household breach, for the duration of the attested shortfall. Purpose: keep the floor decidable and non-punitive when the physical good genuinely does not exist, while still booking the systemic failure where it belongs. Predicate: allocation per person equals the available quantum after reserve draw divided across the covered population by equal-per-capita share, adjusted only by the published life-stage and medical-need weights, so the allocation is decidable at audit; every covered person receives their weighted share. Enforcement hook: the shortfall books as a preparedness breach against the reserve-sizing owner (RES-I16), not as per-household coercion; a cutoff dressed as triage without an attested aggregate shortfall is coercion re-entering NOURISH-I6, and an unequal or identity-keyed allocation of the available quantum is a floor breach.
- NOURISH-I21 (continuity of the essential water and food service). On attested operator insolvency or exit from an essential water or food function, the essential minimum continues without interruption via a pre-designated successor or the RES receiver-of-last-resort / floor_steward, funded from the C1-I16 harm-capacity escrow and the COMP-I17 standing reserve. Purpose: ensure the floor-holder's water or food floor never blinks when the operator behind it fails or walks away. Predicate: a live successor-designation names the successor operator or the floor_steward as receiver of last resort ahead of any exit; on the trigger, delivery of the floor quantum continues uninterrupted while handover completes; a floor-holder's own-account standing derived from R2 postings survives operator insolvency because the ledger, not the operator's book, is authoritative (R2-I5). Enforcement hook: the successor-designation actor is the floor_steward (RES-I25); the handover is recorded as an S4 audit event; a lapse in pre-designation that interrupts the floor is a preparedness breach against the steward, and an interruption reaching the floor quantum re-enters NOURISH-I6.

### Protocols

- NOURISH-P1 (measure and close a food-desert or water-poverty breach): (1) evaluate the access_predicate for each settlement and cohort at S5 grain (NOURISH-I3, NOURISH-I15). (2) For each failing cohort, open a live breach against the named floor_steward with a remediation deadline (NOURISH-I2, RES-I25). (3) The steward discharges a duty of means: extend physical reach through the SUPPLY carriage surface (a supply point, a delivery leg, a water connection), compensating any mandated uneconomic delivery at attested cost (SUPPLY-I2, C1-I7), and, where reach is impossible in period, deliver interim floor quantum from the RES reserve (RES-I6), including by the payment-independent physical path where the own-account rail is dead (NOURISH-I19). (4) Coordinate a durable fix with the PLAN settlement-reach surface. (5) Verify closure by re-running the access_predicate; publish the miss and closure at S5 grain (NOURISH-I15, RES-I17 disclosure shape). (6) Record an S4 audit event for each remediation action, capturing actor, input, output, the cited invariant, and timestamp.
- NOURISH-P2 (close an affordability gap): (1) observe the $-rail cleared price of the floor quantum, unaltered (C2-I1). (2) Compute the affordability_gap for the floor-holder against the reach budget (NOURISH-I4). (3) For food and for above-floor or non-reticulated water, fund the gap as delivered quantity from the COMP-I17 standing reserve onto the floor-holder's own account via the PAY payment floor; for the reticulated potable-water floor quantum, settle the utility's full attested floor-quantum cost (C1-I7) directly from the reserve on the operator's cost-recovery leg, not as a household top-up (NOURISH-I12). (4) Settle on the $ rail as the money medium (R2-I1), never netted against any Ae posting (R2-I6); never touch the producer's price. (5) Where physical supply, not price, is the barrier, escalate to NOURISH-P1, not a cash payment in lieu (NOURISH-I10). (6) Record an S4 audit event for each own-account draw and each operator cost-recovery settlement, capturing actor, input, output, the cited invariant, and timestamp.
- NOURISH-P3 (handle non-payment without cutting the floor): (1) keep the floor quantum connected UNCONDITIONALLY to every floor_holder, with no solvency test and no hardship classification (NOURISH-I7). (2) For any above-floor arrear, the supplier restricts only above-floor supply and pursues the arrears through ordinary $-rail instruments on its own counterparty relationship (the MONEY / F2a and F8 surfaces), with no state per-person read of the household. (3) Refuse at source any proposed action reaching the floor quantum and, if it is punitive or lacks a lawful predicate under the two-part gate, route it as coercion (NOURISH-I6, A18-I4). (4) Log the supplier's above-floor receivable as a normal claim, never extinguished by the floor. (5) Record an S4 audit event for any interruption applied and any coercion routing, capturing actor, input, output, the cited invariant, and timestamp.
- NOURISH-P4 (adjudicate scarcity versus manufactured scarcity): (1) on a price spike or supply withdrawal in a staple-food or water market, run the C2-I5 and COMP-I11 price-discovery-corruption test (NOURISH-I8, NOURISH-I9). (2) Require an affirmative corruption showing PLUS intent-to-extort or coordinated-withholding evidence across the O1 concert graph; inventory level alone is never sufficient, and outside a measured essential-access breach the default protects ordinary inventory (NOURISH-I8). (3) During a measured essential-access floor breach, treat accumulation beyond the conservative published per-staple carrying window as presumptive cornering with the aggravator, rebuttable by attested resilience or operational need. (4) On a manufacturing finding, apply the cornering offense with the essential-access aggravator up the A18 ladder. (5) On a genuine-scarcity finding, take no price action, let the market clear (C2-I4), and open the A18-I34 suspensive contest for any honest seller wrongly accused, with the SUPPLY-I17 good-faith safe harbor. (6) Record an S4 audit event for the determination and any coercion or wrongful-accusation routing, capturing actor, input, output, the cited invariant, and timestamp.
- NOURISH-P5 (drought or famine resilience, by citation): F4b invokes the RES protocols, RES-P1 to declare and operate the incident, RES-P2 for the food and water reserve lifecycle, RES-P5 for close-out, and does not author its own emergency protocol; the F4b-specific inputs are the water and nutrition criticality tiers (NOURISH-I1 into RES-I1), the floor-quantum ladders authored floors-first (RES-I4), and the physical-impossibility triage discharge (NOURISH-I20).

### Lifecycle and edge cases

- Newborn, new arrival, and displaced person: the floor attaches on presence for the subsistence bottom (RES-I14 dignity bottom, citizen or not); NOURISH-P1 places interim reserve delivery before own-account enrollment completes, and the payment-independent physical path (NOURISH-I19) covers the person whose payment capability is not yet live, so the unbanked or just-arrived person is not left below the floor while paperwork settles.
- Contaminated delivery meeting volume: fails NOURISH-I5 as a floor miss; if the contamination was known and concealed, escalates under NOURISH-I14 with the perpetual flip, not merely a re-delivery.
- Household with an above-floor arrear during a genuine local scarcity: above-floor supply may be restricted and arrears pursued on the rail (NOURISH-P3) on the supplier's own counterparty relationship, the floor quantum stays connected unconditionally (NOURISH-I7), and the scarcity itself is adjudicated separately (NOURISH-P4).
- Off-calendar reserve sale during a price spike with no declaration: a per se off-trigger release under RES-I7, booked against the releasing organ; the F4b floor is unaffected because it delivers quantity, not a market price.
- Water utility as natural monopoly raising the floor-quantum price to the person: barred at the floor (NOURISH-I12), while the utility still recovers its full attested floor-quantum cost directly from the reserve; the above-floor tariff runs through the C1-I7 attested-cost corridor with the C1-I16 harm-capacity escrow, not through F4b.
- Physical shortfall below the aggregate floor after reserve draw: equal-per-capita weighted rationing is the discharge, not a per-household breach (NOURISH-I20), while the sizing shortfall books as a preparedness breach against RES (RES-I16).
- Extraction-grant holder threatening to withhold to renegotiate the grant: re-enters NOURISH-I8 and NOURISH-I13; the grant is revocable (P2-I7), and withholding floor-quantum water is coercion (NOURISH-I6), not a bargaining position.
- Essential water or food operator becoming insolvent or exiting: the pre-designated successor or the RES floor_steward continues the floor without interruption, funded from the C1-I16 escrow and the COMP-I17 reserve, with an S4 handover audit event (NOURISH-I21); the floor-holder's own-account standing survives on the R2 ledger (R2-I5).
- Autonomous-agent operator in the water or food chain leaving a floor breach: no autonomous path discharges the breach without a natural-person bearer (Organizations-Contracts-and-Employment.md), the floor_steward and the operator's veil-pierced principals carry it.

## WASTE waste streams and circular flows (F5)

### Purpose

F5 models the waste flow: the objects and rules by which a waste_stream is generated, tracked, treated, recovered, or disposed of, and by which its anti-value is kept attached to the party that produced it rather than pushed onto a neighbour, a downstream community, a receiving jurisdiction, or the commons. Waste is anti-value. The cardinal move of this section is that anti-value does not evaporate at the point of generation and does not evaporate at the point of recovery either: it is a durable, traceable liability that the generator bears until it is genuinely neutralized, or genuinely recovered into new value and that recovery is proven by an attested downstream beneficial use, or safely and consentingly transferred to a processor who assumes it under contract.

F5 does not re-model production mass balance or embodied anti-value: K8 owns those, and this section cites them. F5 does not re-model the waste-to-product anti-laundering rule: K8-I10 owns it, and this section cites it and adds only the F5-specific stream tracking. F5 does not re-model emissions accounting: F6 owns the lifecycle ledger, and this section books onto it by citation. F5 does not re-model the value ledgers, the $ rail, the Ae overlay, or the cash-like tier (R2-I1, R2-I4, R2-I6): a disposal fee settles on the $ rail as a money medium and is never an Ae posting, the money rail and the anti-value rail are never netted against each other, and a flow never owns the commons it carries. F5 protects both flanks throughout: the community, the receiving jurisdiction, the habitat, and the commons are shielded from dumping, and the honest collector, processor, remanufacturer, and legitimate exporter keep their earned fee and are not treated as dumpers, with the wrongly-accused actor contesting under A18-I34.

### Object model

- waste_stream: the base object. Fields SHALL include a stable stream_id, the originating generator_id (a natural-person or O1 firm bearer, never only an autonomous agent), the material class and hazard class, a mass and composition record inherited by reference from the K8 mass-balance record, an embodied anti-value quantum (by reference to K8, not recomputed here), a custody pointer to the current holder, a held-anti-value register recording any quantum held in contingency pending a beneficial-use event (WASTE-I5), and a disposition state (generated / in-custody / in-treatment / recovered-contingent / recovered-released / disposed / breached).
- treatment: a processing event applied to a waste_stream by a processor. Fields SHALL include processor_id (natural-person bearer), input stream_id(s), the treatment class (sort, neutralize, incinerate, compost, digest, stabilize, landfill-prepare), the output stream_id(s) and any recovered-material output, the emissions record booked on F6, the residual anti-value remaining after treatment, and the S4 audit reference for the event (WASTE-I9, WASTE-P-Treatment).
- recovery: a closing-the-loop event that converts a waste_stream, in whole or part, into a recovered_material that re-enters supply as an M3 good. Fields SHALL include the input stream_id, the recovered mass and grade, the value genuinely recovered (booked on the $ rail as a money medium), the anti-value quantum moved into the held or contingent state at the recovery event, the pointer to the attested genuine-beneficial-use event that releases that held quantum (WASTE-I5), and the S4 audit reference for the event.
- disposal: a terminal placement event (landfill cell, deep storage, controlled destruction). Fields SHALL include the disposal site_id, the emplaced mass and hazard class, the C1-I16 harm-capacity escrow reference where the hazard class requires it, the sink capacity state and the pre-authored continuity-plan reference where the sink is an essential-service sink (WASTE-I21), the perpetual remediation-liability pointer to a natural-person bearer, and the S4 audit reference for the event.
- transfer: a change of custody of a waste_stream between parties or across a jurisdictional boundary, carrying a legitimacy record (consent of the receiver, safety attestation, processing capability of the receiver) that distinguishes a legitimate transfer from dumping, plus any below-cost price flag raised for verification (WASTE-I11).

### Invariants

WASTE-I1 (waste is generator-borne anti-value). Every waste_stream SHALL carry, from the instant of generation, an anti-value liability attributed to its generator_id, taken by reference from the K8 embodied-anti-value and mass-balance record and never recomputed or discounted at the F5 boundary. Purpose: extended producer responsibility, so the cost of a stream is not silently externalized. Predicate: a waste_stream with a null or unattributed generator_id, or with an anti-value quantum lower than its K8-referenced embodied anti-value, is non-conformant. Enforcement hook: registration of the stream is refused and the K8 production record is flagged for embodied-anti-value concealment.

WASTE-I2 (anti-value is not externalized onto neighbours or the commons). The anti-value and the physical disposal cost of a waste_stream SHALL travel with the generator until the stream is genuinely neutralized, genuinely recovered with the recovered quantum released on beneficial use (WASTE-I5), or transferred under a conformant transfer (WASTE-I11, WASTE-I12). Neither SHALL be discharged by placing the stream where a neighbour, a downstream community, a receiving jurisdiction, or the commons absorbs it uncompensated. Purpose: block the core externalization. Predicate: a disposition whose realized cost-bearer differs from the generator, absent a conformant transfer or a released genuine recovery, is a breach. Enforcement hook: the uncovered cost books back to the generator with the perpetual remediation-liability pointer, and the placement is assessed as an A18 harm where a floor or the habitat floor is breached, routed through A18-I4.

WASTE-I3 (F5 stream tracking under the waste-to-product rule). Where an output is reclassified from waste to product, F5 SHALL maintain the stream-level tracking that K8-I10 presupposes: a persistent link from the reclassified output back to the originating waste_stream and its generator, the embodied anti-value carried forward, and the disposal-contingent tail as defined by K8-I10, released only on genuine beneficial use and forfeited on sham use or downstream dumping. F5 CITES K8-I10 for the substantive rule and adds only this tracking obligation. Purpose: make the K8-I10 tail operable at stream grain without re-modelling it. Predicate: a reclassified output whose F5 record lacks a back-link to the originating stream, or whose tail is marked released without an attested beneficial-use event, is non-conformant. Enforcement hook: the reclassification is treated as unmade, the output reverts to waste_stream status bearing full anti-value, and a sham reclassification routes to A18 as externality concealment per K8-I10.

WASTE-I4 (custody chain and joint liability). Every waste_stream SHALL carry an unbroken custody chain from generator through each collector, carrier, and processor to terminal disposition, mirroring the F3 custody machinery. A gap in custody SHALL fail closed: the last attested custodian and the generator remain jointly liable for the stream until custody is re-established. Purpose: no stream disappears between hands. Predicate: any interval on the stream timeline with no attested custodian is a custody gap. Enforcement hook: joint anti-value liability (paralleling F3-I7) attaches to the last custodian and the generator, and an unexplained gap coincident with a downstream contamination is evidence of dumping under WASTE-I10.

WASTE-I5 (genuine recovery holds anti-value in contingency until beneficial use; sham recovery is concealment). A recovery event SHALL book the value genuinely recovered on the $ rail as a money medium, in proportion to the recovered mass at its attested grade re-entering as an M3 good, and SHALL NOT reduce the generator's outstanding anti-value at the recovery event. The recovered anti-value quantum SHALL instead move into a held or contingent state and SHALL be released from the generator's outstanding anti-value only on an attested genuine-beneficial-use event downstream, per K8-I10 and RINT-I22; on sham use or downstream dumping the held quantum SHALL be forfeited back to the generator with the concealment charge. Separately, a recovery claimed but not physically realized, or booked at a value exceeding attested-recovered-mass-times-grade, or lacking a corresponding M3 good entering supply, SHALL be void and treated as concealment under A18. Purpose: reward real circularity only when it is proven by real use, and punish paper circularity. Predicate: recovered-value booked exceeds attested recovered-mass-times-grade; or a held quantum marked released with no attested beneficial-use event; or a recovered_material claim has no corresponding M3 good entering supply. Enforcement hook: the excess booking is reversed on the $ rail, the held quantum is restored to the generator's outstanding anti-value, and the false claim routes to A18 concealment class with the perpetual flip.

WASTE-I6 (recovered material re-enters as a good, not as laundering). A recovered_material that re-enters supply SHALL enter as an M3 good carrying its true residual anti-value and, where applicable, the K8-I10 disposal-contingent tail; recovery SHALL NOT be used to strip a stream of its anti-value by relabelling. Purpose: keep the loop honest at re-entry. Predicate: a recovered_material entering M3 with anti-value lower than the residual left by its recovery event. Enforcement hook: the difference books back to the originating generator, and repeated relabelling is aggregated as a K8-I10 sham-use pattern.

WASTE-I7 (hazardous waste is a harm-capacity object requiring escrow). A waste_stream in a hazardous class SHALL be treated as a concentrated ex-ante-peak harm-capacity object and SHALL be bound to a C1-I16 harm-capacity escrow sized to its worst-case remediation cost before it may be accumulated, treated at scale, or emplaced. Purpose: ensure the money to clean up exists before the harm can be realized, not after. Predicate: a hazardous stream held or emplaced above a de-minimis threshold with no live C1-I16 escrow of sufficient magnitude. Enforcement hook: accumulation or emplacement is barred until the escrow is posted, and operating without it is an A18 harm with the concealment flip where the shortfall was hidden.

WASTE-I8 (run-to-failure and deferred-remediation book to a natural-person bearer). A treatment or disposal facility operated so as to defer or offload its remediation cost, including running a landfill or treatment site to failure and abandoning the cleanup, SHALL have the deferred-remediation externality booked to the operator firm and pinned to a named natural-person bearer via the O1 veil rule; no autonomous-agent or shell-operator path SHALL leave the remediation liability without a natural-person bearer. Purpose: close the walk-away exit. Predicate: a facility whose posted escrow plus solvent operator assets fall below its accruing remediation liability, or an operator entity dissolving with an open remediation tail. Enforcement hook: the C1-I16 escrow is drawn, the veil is pierced to the natural-person bearer for the residual, and deliberate run-to-failure with concealed condition routes to A18 concealment class.

WASTE-I9 (waste and treatment emissions book on F6). All emissions from generation, storage, transport, treatment, recovery, and disposal of a waste_stream SHALL be recorded on the F6 lifecycle ledger against the generator and the operating custodian as F6 assigns; F5 SHALL NOT maintain a parallel or netting emissions account. Purpose: single source of emissions truth, no double-count and no escape. Predicate: a treatment or disposal event with an emissions profile absent from F6. Enforcement hook: the event is non-conformant until its F6 posting exists, and a suppressed posting routes to A18 concealment.

WASTE-I10 (no illegal or midnight dumping). Placement of any waste_stream outside a permitted disposition, including unpermitted emplacement, discharge to land, water, or air outside an F6-booked pathway, and abandonment, SHALL be an A18 harm. Where the dumping breaches an essential-access floor, a D4 habitat floor, or targets an identifiable weaker community, the floor breach and the environmental-injustice character SHALL be aggravating factors, and A18-I4 SHALL set max_rung accordingly with floor_breach_severity as an input. Purpose: the hard prohibition. Predicate: a stream reaching terminal placement with no conformant disposal or transfer record; a custody gap (WASTE-I4) closed by disappearance. Enforcement hook: full anti-value plus remediation cost books to the generator and any complicit custodian with the perpetual flip, routed through A18-I4, and a wrongly-accused custodian contests under A18-I34.

WASTE-I11 (no waste-export to a weaker jurisdiction or community; both flanks). A transfer of a waste_stream to another community or across a jurisdictional boundary SHALL be barred where its purpose or effect is to evade the disposal cost by placing the stream where a poorer or weaker receiver bears it, including a receiver lacking the attested capability to process the hazard class safely. Symmetrically, a transfer SHALL be permitted where the receiver genuinely consents, is genuinely capable of safe processing, the safety attestation holds, and the anti-value and any K8-I10 tail travel with the stream. Purpose: stop environmental-injustice export without strangling legitimate cross-border processing trade. Predicate for breach: receiver consent absent or coerced, OR receiver processing-capability attestation for the hazard class absent, OR the safety attestation absent or false. A transfer price below the honest at-origin disposal cost SHALL NOT by itself be a breach: it is a non-dispositive flag that SHALL trigger verification of the positive conditions (genuine consent, attested capability, safety attestation), and is a breach only where that verification fails. Where the below-cost benchmark is used as a flag, the at-origin cost SHALL be measured against a C1-I7 attested-cost standard, excluding a documented genuine-capacity efficiency delta so a genuinely cheaper capable processor is not condemned by price alone. Enforcement hook: an evasive export whose positive conditions fail is an A18 harm and an environmental-injustice harm on both flanks (the receiving community and the commons), the stream's liability snaps back to the origin generator with a natural-person bearer, A18-I4 gates the response, and a wrongly-accused legitimate exporter contests under A18-I34.

WASTE-I12 (legitimate transfer is distinguished from dumping). A transfer meeting WASTE-I11's positive conditions, with a complete legitimacy record (consent, capability, safety attestation, anti-value carried), SHALL be recognized as a lawful commercial transfer and the receiving processor SHALL earn its fee on the $ rail as a money medium; such a transfer SHALL NOT be reclassified as dumping absent evidence that a condition was false. Purpose: protect the honest-processor flank and the genuine circular-economy trade. Predicate: legitimacy record complete and attestations verifiable. Enforcement hook: the transfer clears; a later-falsified attestation reopens liability against the attestor under A18 concealment (contestable under A18-I34), not against a good-faith receiver.

WASTE-I13 (habitat floor breach is caught). A waste_stream disposition that breaches the D4 habitat and ecology floor SHALL be non-conformant regardless of permit status, and the breach SHALL be remediated at the generator's and operating custodian's cost. Purpose: the ecological backstop that a paper permit cannot override. Predicate: a monitored ecological indicator crossing the D4 floor attributable to the stream. Enforcement hook: disposition is halted, remediation is compelled, and a concealed known breach carries the A18 concealment flip; F5 CITES D4 for the floor and does not re-model it.

WASTE-I14 (the honest collector and processor earn their fee). A conformant collection, treatment, recovery, or disposal service SHALL be able to charge and settle a fee on the $ rail as a money medium (never an Ae posting, never netted against the anti-value rail), and providing waste service SHALL NOT by itself impute anti-value to the provider; anti-value stays with the generator except the residual a processor contractually assumes. Purpose: keep waste handling a viable honest business, protecting the operator flank. Predicate: a fee posting matched to a conformant service event. Enforcement hook: fees clear on the $ rail; an attempt to route waste-service anti-value onto a good-faith processor is corrected to the generator under WASTE-I1.

WASTE-I15 (essential household waste collection is an unconditional floor, not a coercion lever). Basic waste collection for a natural person's household SHALL be treated as an essential service and SHALL stay connected UNCONDITIONALLY for every household, with no solvency test and no per-household hardship classification; the essential collection floor SHALL never be withheld, cut, or gated on ability to pay, and a person's non-payment SHALL never be answered by dumping the person's uncollected waste back on them. Only ABOVE-floor or commercial waste-collection arrears SHALL trigger ordinary $ rail collection, initiated by the supplier's own account relationship (the supplier already knows its own counterparty; no state per-person read is needed). Purpose: protect the resident flank without turning the essential floor into a free-rider hole, since ordinary above-floor debt remedies remain. Predicate: an essential-collection cutoff conditioned on payment or hardship class; or a cutoff whose purpose or effect is punitive or coercive rather than a genuine time-bounded safety necessity. Enforcement hook: the coercion determination is owned by the PAY coercive-cutoff gate (PAY-I10) and routes through A18-I4 with floor_breach_severity as an input; a bona fide safety suspension that is time-bounded, floor-preserving, and carries a restoration path, contestable under A18-I34, is not a coercive cutoff.

WASTE-I16 (waste records are subject-owned and sensed at cohort grain). Waste_stream, treatment, and transfer records SHALL follow the F3-I18 data-sovereignty pattern: the record owner owns the record, cross-reads are S3 projections under S2 contracts and S4-logged, the state senses at S5 cohort or corridor grain, a per-consignment read needs consent or a targeted A6 judicial contract, and a network-scale movement aggregate is a data commons. Purpose: enable regulator visibility into flows and dumping patterns without mass surveillance of who threw out what. Predicate: a per-stream state read lacking consent or an A6 contract. Enforcement hook: the read is refused at S1; bulk per-person waste surveillance is barred, and pattern detection operates on S5 cohorts. F5 CITES F3-I18 and does not re-model it.

WASTE-I17 (no autonomous-agent path escapes a natural-person bearer). No autonomous-agent or automated operating path in generation, treatment, recovery, transfer, or disposal SHALL terminate a waste_stream's anti-value, remediation, or dumping liability without a natural-person bearer, per the O1 veil and no-orphan-harm rule. Purpose: prevent laundering liability through automation or shell operators. Predicate: an open remediation or dumping liability whose bearer resolves only to an agent or a dissolved entity. Enforcement hook: the O1 veil is pierced to the responsible natural person; failing that, the liability escrow (WASTE-I7, C1-I16) is drawn and the sponsoring principals bear the residual.

WASTE-I18 (orphan and legacy streams fail closed to a funded reserve). Where a generator is insolvent, dissolved, or unidentifiable, an orphan or legacy waste_stream SHALL fail closed: its remediation SHALL draw first on any C1-I16 escrow, then on the last solvent custodian in the WASTE-I4 chain, then on the standing catchment remediation reserve maintained under WASTE-I19, and the search for a natural-person bearer SHALL persist under the concealment perpetual flip rather than lapsing. Purpose: no stream becomes the commons' silent burden by attrition, while an honest late-arriving custodian is not made to bear a harm it did not cause beyond its custody share. Predicate: a stream with remediation due and no solvent attributed generator. Enforcement hook: the escrow-then-custodian-then-reserve waterfall executes, and any concealed original generator, once found, bears the reserve's outlay with the flip; a wrongly-attributed custodian contests under A18-I34.

WASTE-I19 (the catchment remediation reserve is standing-funded and fails closed on shortfall). The catchment remediation reserve that backstops the WASTE-I18 waterfall SHALL be a standing reserve maintained at or above a D4-sized remediation floor for each catchment, funded by a levy on hazardous-class generators in proportion to their emplaced hazard, so the community is never the silent residual bearer. Where a catchment's reserve falls below its sized floor, new hazardous emplacement in that catchment SHALL be barred (fail closed) until the reserve is restored above the floor. Purpose: guarantee that the money to clean up orphaned and legacy hazard exists before more hazard is added. Predicate: a catchment reserve below its D4-sized floor with a pending or proposed new hazardous emplacement; or a hazardous-class generator emplacing without a live levy contribution. Enforcement hook: the emplacement permit is refused at registration until the reserve is restored, and a concealed emplacement made while the reserve is below floor routes to A18 concealment class with the perpetual flip. F5 CITES D4 for the remediation-floor sizing basis and does not re-model it.

WASTE-I20 (continuity of essential waste collection on operator insolvency). On attested insolvency or exit of a waste-collection operator from an essential function, essential household waste collection (WASTE-I15) SHALL continue without interruption via a pre-designated successor operator or the RES receiver-of-last-resort / floor_steward, funded from the C1-I16 escrow and the COMP-I17 standing reserve. A stream's custody, liability, and disposition records survive operator insolvency because the F5 record, not the operator's book, is authoritative. Purpose: an operator failing is never a reason a household floor is cut. Predicate: an essential collection lapsing on operator insolvency with no successor engaged. Enforcement hook: the RES floor_steward is engaged as receiver of last resort, the successor-designation authority under the RES regime records the handover as an S4 audit event, and a lapse used as a coercive cutoff routes through the PAY coercive-cutoff gate (PAY-I10) and A18-I4.

WASTE-I21 (essential disposal-sink capacity continuity; unprovisioned capacity is a preparedness breach, not a licence to dump). An essential-service disposal or treatment sink approaching its permitted capacity SHALL trigger a pre-authored RES continuity plan: a successor cell, interim safe storage under the C1-I16 escrow, or emergency treatment routing, so a life_critical waste stream continues to a lawful disposition. Purpose: keep a life_critical waste stream lawfully disposable when its sink fills. Predicate: an essential-service sink within a published margin of its capacity with no pre-authored, funded continuity plan on file. Enforcement hook: failure to pre-provision successor capacity is a preparedness breach booked against the RES sink-provisioning owner; it SHALL NOT be a licence to dump, and any unlawful placement caused by unprovisioned capacity remains a WASTE-I10 dumping harm routed through A18-I4, with a wrongly-accused operator contesting under A18-I34.

### Protocols

Generation and registration. On production of a stream, the generator SHALL register a waste_stream referencing the K8 mass-balance and embodied-anti-value record (WASTE-I1), assign a hazard class, and, if hazardous above the de-minimis threshold, post or reference the C1-I16 escrow before accumulation (WASTE-I7) and, for a catchment below its sized reserve floor, be refused new hazardous emplacement (WASTE-I19). Registration fails closed on a missing generator_id or under-stated anti-value.

Custody and carriage. Each hand-off SHALL append an attested custody event (WASTE-I4); carriage rides the F3 machinery and books emissions on F6 (WASTE-I9). A custody gap triggers joint liability.

Treatment. A treatment event SHALL record inputs, outputs, residual anti-value, and F6 emissions (WASTE-I9), and SHALL write an S4 audit event recording the actor (processor_id / natural-person bearer), the input stream_id(s), the output stream_id(s) and residual anti-value, the cited invariant, and the timestamp.

Recovery. A recovery event SHALL book genuine recovered value on the $ rail, move the recovered anti-value quantum into the held or contingent state rather than reducing the generator's outstanding anti-value, and re-enter recovered_material as an M3 good carrying residual anti-value and any K8-I10 tail (WASTE-I5, WASTE-I6); the held quantum is released only on an attested downstream genuine-beneficial-use event (K8-I10, RINT-I22) and forfeited to the generator on sham use or downstream dumping. Recovery claims are validated against attested output mass and grade. The event SHALL write an S4 audit event recording the actor, the input stream_id, the recovered mass and grade and booked value, the held quantum and its beneficial-use release pointer, the cited invariant, and the timestamp.

Transfer. A transfer SHALL assemble the legitimacy record (receiver consent, capability attestation for the hazard class, safety attestation, anti-value carriage) and be checked against WASTE-I11; a below-cost price is recorded as a non-dispositive flag that triggers verification of the positive conditions, not an automatic breach. A complete record clears as a lawful transfer under WASTE-I12; an incomplete or evasive one is blocked and routed to A18, with the accused contesting under A18-I34.

Disposal and closure. Terminal disposal SHALL record site, emplaced mass, hazard class, escrow reference, the sink capacity state and continuity-plan reference where the sink is essential (WASTE-I21), and the perpetual remediation pointer to a natural-person bearer (WASTE-I8, WASTE-I17). Site closure does not release the remediation tail; monitoring against the D4 floor continues (WASTE-I13). The event SHALL write an S4 audit event recording the actor, the emplaced mass and hazard class, the escrow and continuity references, the cited invariant, and the timestamp.

Continuity handover. On attested operator insolvency or exit, essential collection continues via the pre-designated successor or the RES receiver-of-last-resort (WASTE-I20), and an essential sink nearing capacity engages its pre-authored RES continuity plan (WASTE-I21); each handover SHALL write an S4 audit event recording the successor-designation actor, the streams handed over, the funding source (C1-I16 escrow, COMP-I17 reserve), the cited invariant, and the timestamp.

### Lifecycle and edge cases

- Reclassification churn: a stream repeatedly flipped waste-to-product-to-waste is tracked across flips (WASTE-I3) and an oscillation used to shed anti-value is a K8-I10 sham pattern.
- Recovery without downstream use: a recovery booked but never proven by a downstream beneficial-use event leaves the anti-value in the held or contingent state; the quantum is not released, and a sham or dumped downstream use forfeits it back to the generator with the concealment charge (WASTE-I5).
- Mixed streams: a co-mingled hazardous and ordinary stream inherits the highest hazard class for escrow purposes (WASTE-I7) until attested separation.
- Cross-border legitimate processing: distinguished from export-dumping strictly on the WASTE-I11 positive conditions, not on destination wealth or price alone; a rich receiver with no capability is as barred as a poor one, and a genuinely cheaper capable processor with a documented efficiency delta is not condemned by a below-cost flag.
- Force-majeure spill: a spill during a genuine emergency is not automatically A18 dumping, but the anti-value and remediation still book to the responsible custodian; concealing a spill converts it to A18 concealment class.
- Insolvent generator mid-tail: handled by the WASTE-I18 waterfall against the WASTE-I19 funded reserve; the honest downstream custodian bears only its custody share.
- Operator insolvency: essential household collection does not stop; it continues via successor or RES receiver-of-last-resort funded from C1-I16 and COMP-I17 (WASTE-I20), and the authoritative record is the F5 ledger, not the failed operator's book.
- Essential sink nearing capacity: the pre-authored RES continuity plan is engaged (WASTE-I21); an operator that let capacity run out with no plan bears a preparedness breach, and any resulting unlawful placement is still WASTE-I10 dumping.

## RINT resource-flow integrity and capture surface (F2, F4, F5)

### Purpose

This section is the integrity and anti-capture index for the resource flows: money (F2, partitioned into MONEY (F2a) and PAY (F2b)), water and food (F4, partitioned into SUPPLY (F4a) and NOURISH (F4b)), and waste (F5, WASTE). It is a mapping layer, not an authoring layer. It enumerates the attack and capture surface of these flows and binds each attack to the closing invariant that already owns it, to its A18 class, and to a natural-person bearer. It does not re-model the value substrate, the two-ledger system, the cash-like sub-threshold tier, instruments, the coupling of value to money, the waste-to-product rule, the sensing regime, the water commons, or the coercion gate. Those are cited by published invariant ID and are binding here as written.

RINT authors only two normative rules of its own, both genuinely cross-cutting and stated in full below: the per-axis anti-value booking rule that never nets against the $ rail (RINT-I16), and the decidable money-as-value laundering test (RINT-I1), R2-I6 shaped. Every other RINT-I is a one-line mapping that CITES the owning MONEY, PAY, SUPPLY, NOURISH, or WASTE invariant plus A18-I4 where a coercive cutoff is in play, and does NOT restate the rule.

Two design commitments govern the whole section. First, money is the $ rail medium, not value: every enforcement hook books anti-value on the AE overlay per axis and never nets that booking against the $ rail (R2-I1, R2-I6); a $ recovery settles or claws back on its own rail under R2b, while the residual per-axis harm books on the AE overlay, and the two are never equated. Second, every mapping protects both flanks. The holder, eater, floor-holder, and community are protected from capture, coercion, and floor breach; the honest saver, operator, producer, processor, and the genuine narrow emergency are protected from over-broad suspicion, from mass financial surveillance, and from a false contamination, hoarding, or dumping claim wielded to extort or foreclose. A mapping that closes one flank while opening the other is not compliant with this section.

### Object model

- Attack mapping entry: a tuple of an attack class, the owning closing invariant ID that shuts it, the A18 class it carries (non-priceable criminal, concealment plus perpetual flip, or coercion under A18-I4), and the natural-person bearer of the harm. RINT owns the index of these entries, not the closing rules they point to.
- Money-as-value laundering attempt: an operation whose two legs sit on different ledgers (one $ rail leg, one AE-axis leg) and purport to net or cancel, or a $ mint citing an Ae quantity as its backing, or a holder's Ae posting settled by a $ rail transfer. This is the decidable predicate of the RINT-I1 original test, R2-I6 shaped.
- Anti-value booking: a per-axis Ae posting on the AE overlay recording the externality or capture harm of a resource-flow act, never netted across axes and never netted against the $ rail (R2-I1, R2-I6). This is the RINT-I16 original rule.
- Capture claim: an assertion that a resource-flow act is a capture, contamination, hoard, corner, or dump; itself an object that can be true, false, or weaponized, and therefore itself subject to RINT-I17.
- Sensing projection: an S5 cohort or corridor-grain read of flow data, or a targeted per-subject A6 read, never a bulk cross-subject read.

### Invariants

#### Money flow (F2: MONEY, PAY)

RINT-I1 (money-as-value laundering is void, cardinal breach). RINT ORIGINAL. Any operation that treats the $ rail as if it carried sovereign value SHALL be void on its face and SHALL NOT settle. The decidable predicate is the one in the object model: a two-legged posting whose legs sit on different ledgers and purport to net or cancel, or a $ mint citing an Ae quantity as backing, or a holder's Ae posting settled by a $ rail transfer (cite R2-I6 and the two-ledger rule; money is the $ rail medium, not value, per R2-I1). A18 class: non-priceable criminal. Natural-person bearer: the person whose per-axis Ae value the launderer attempts to monetize, and the community on the monetary-integrity axis. Enforcement hook: the attempted netting is reversed, the $ leg is clawed back under R2b, and the laundering books as anti-value on the corrupted axis, never netted down by the money that moved (RINT-I16). Both flanks: an honest holder who merely holds money and separately bears an ordinary money-rent leakage (RINT-I21) is not in breach; the breach is the netting or the exchange, not the coexistence.

RINT-I2 (rail integrity: counterfeiting, private minting, debasement). Counterfeiting, private minting, or debasement of the $ rail SHALL be closed under the MONEY currency and issuance invariants and the MONEY-I5 private-mint bar. A18 class: non-priceable criminal on the monetary-integrity axis. Natural-person bearer: every holder of the rail. Mapping only, see MONEY. Both flanks: a lawful F8-registered instrument issuer issues a claim on the rail (F8), never a substitute rail unit, and bears no charge.

RINT-I3 (payment-cutoff-as-coercion). Cutting off, freezing, or debanking a natural person's basic payment capability, or an operator's rail access, to punish, suppress, coerce, or retaliate SHALL route through the single decidable coercion-cutoff gate owned by PAY (cite that gate and A18-I4, with floor_breach_severity an input). A18 class: coercion under A18-I4. Natural-person bearer: the debanked or cut-off person. Mapping only, see PAY for the gate and for the payment-access floor and its COMP-I17 delivery; RINT does not restate the two-part test.

RINT-I4 (payment monitoring stays at cohort grain). Bulk, standing, or cross-subject mass transaction monitoring SHALL be barred; monitoring senses only at S5 cohort or corridor grain, and any per-person read requires a targeted A6 contract (cite R2c, S5, and the MONEY/PAY cash-like anti-structuring rule). A18 class: per severity on the privacy axis. Natural-person bearer: the honest saver and honest operator. Mapping only. Both flanks: a genuine, narrow AML or fraud inquiry proceeds on a per-person A6 contract, and the R2-I4 cash-like sub-threshold tier remains structurally unidentified by design, not by evasion.

RINT-I5 (no private capture of the money or payment rail). Controlling capture of the money or payment rail by a private actor or O1 acting-in-concert group, and any unbacked money-like claim circulating as a rail substitute, SHALL be closed under the MONEY-I5 private-mint bar and the COMP-I11 cornering rule with the concentration ceiling across the O1 concert graph (cite COMP-I11; essential-access breach an aggravator). A18 class: non-priceable criminal on the monetary-integrity axis. Natural-person bearer: every economy participant subject to the choke point. Mapping only, see MONEY and F8. Both flanks: a fully backed, F8-registered payment instrument or clearing service that does not capture the rail bears no charge.

Both flanks: lawful RES resilience buffering, seasonal storage, and genuine strategic reserve are not hoarding, per the owning rule.

#### Cross-cutting integrity

RINT-I16 (per-axis anti-value booking, never netted against the $ rail). RINT ORIGINAL. Every enforcement hook in this section SHALL book anti-value on the AE overlay per axis and SHALL NOT net that booking against any $ rail-event, fee, saving, or revenue produced by the captured act (cite R2-I1, R2-I6). This is the load-bearing RINT contribution: it prevents a capture from buying down its own charge, and it holds the money rail and the anti-value rail structurally separate. Decidable predicate: any enforcement posting that reduces an axis anti-value by an amount settled on the $ rail is void. Enforcement hook: the netting is reversed and the underlying anti-value restored to full; the $ recovery, if any, settles on its own rail under R2b. Both flanks: the honest actor's lawful $ rail revenue is untouched by this rule; only the attempt to net a charge against it is barred.

RINT-I17 (a false capture claim used to extort or foreclose is itself an offense). Asserting a contamination, adulteration, hoarding, cornering, water-grab, dumping, or sham-recovery claim to extort, foreclose, or capture an honest saver, operator, producer, or processor SHALL itself be an offense under the SUPPLY-I17 false-claim standard: knowledge of falsity or reckless disregard for the truth, coupled to an extortive or foreclosing gain, never mere negligence, with a good-faith-precautionary-alarm safe harbor (cite SUPPLY-I17). A18 class: on the market-integrity axis, escalating to the concealment or coercion class where the false claim hides a fact or is used to cut off an essential (A18-I4). Natural-person bearer: the wrongly accused honest actor. The wrongly-accused flank is restored through the A18-I34 suspensive contest and A6 (RINT-I19). Both flanks: a good-faith precautionary alarm that later proves wrong, raised through A6 and not weaponized, bears no offense charge; the target is the knowing or recklessly false claim wielded as leverage, not honest error or a genuine contest.

RINT-I18 (fail-closed calibration and detection-signal proportionality). Every detection signal in this section SHALL be calibrated by A10 before it can escalate, SHALL fail closed to the least-intrusive projection until calibrated, and SHALL be proportionate to the criticality tier of the stream (cite A10, S5, RES-I1). A18 class: on the privacy or due-process axis for an executed uncalibrated escalation. Natural-person bearer: both the community awaiting timely detection on a life_critical stream and the honest actor shielded from an unproven flag. Mapping only; RINT does not re-model the calibration machinery. Both flanks: the fail-closed default is conservative on intrusion, not on safety response.

RINT-I19 (suspensive contest and restoration for the wrongly flagged). An honest saver, operator, producer, or processor flagged, frozen, or charged under this section SHALL have a suspensive contest under A18-I34 and, on prevailing, SHALL be restored, including reversal of any $ rail freeze under R2b, re-credit of any wrongly booked anti-value, and repair of reputational harm (cite A18-I34, R2b, A6). Natural-person bearer: the wrongly flagged actor. Mapping only; this is the shared wrongly-accused flank for every attack above. Both flanks: irreversible steps are stayed during contest for non-life-critical matters, while a life_critical physical safety hold stands with the liability charge stayed pending adjudication.

RINT-I20 (essential-access floor breach is a standing aggravator). Wherever a capture, coercion, hoard, corner, dumping, or concealment act in this section also breaches a delivered essential-access floor (potable water, staple food, or basic payment), that breach SHALL be a standing aggravating factor, entering A18-I4 as floor_breach_severity where coercive and raising the per-axis anti-value elsewhere (cite A18-I4; do not restate the max_rung formula). Decidable predicate: the act targets a RES-I1 life_critical stream or a delivered payment floor and reduces a natural person's received floor below the delivered magnitude. Natural-person bearer: the floor-holder whose received floor was reduced. Enforcement hook: the aggravator is applied per axis and never netted against the $ rail (RINT-I16). Both flanks: a standard-tier or deferrable-tier matter is not inflated by this aggravator.

RINT-I21 (money-rent leakage is not a capture charge, and vice versa). The ordinary money-rent leakage on holding, priced on the AE overlay while return on creation is protected (cite F8 and the RENT money-rent rule), SHALL remain distinct from the capture and concealment charges of this section, and neither SHALL be booked as, or netted against, the other (RINT-I16, R2-I6). Decidable predicate: a posting that relabels a RINT capture charge as ordinary rent, or ordinary rent as a RINT charge, is void. Natural-person bearer: the honest holder protected from a capture accusation for merely holding. Both flanks: the honest holder pays only the money-rent leakage on holding; the genuine capturer cannot launder the graver charge into ordinary rent.

RINT-I22 (waste-to-product boundary integrity at the flow edge). At the F5 to K8 boundary, a stream reclassified from waste to product SHALL cross only with its embodied anti-value and its disposal-contingent tail intact, and F5 SHALL NOT release, discount, or absorb that tail; release occurs only in K8 on attested genuine beneficial use (cite K8-I10). ### Protocols

- Anti-capture booking protocol. On a confirmed capture, contamination, hoard, corner, water-grab, dumping, or concealment: (1) identify the owning closing invariant and the corrupted axis or axes; (2) book anti-value per axis on the AE overlay (RINT-I16); (3) settle or claw back the $ leg on its own rail under R2b; (4) apply the RINT-I20 essential-access aggravator where a floor was breached; (5) attach the owner's concealment perpetual flip (RINT-I6, RINT-I15) or route the coercion (RINT-I3, RINT-I10) through the owning coercion-cutoff gate and A18-I4 as applicable; (6) never net (2) against (3); (7) emit an S4 audit event recording actor, input, output, the cited owning invariant, and timestamp.
- Sensing escalation protocol. A detection signal senses first at S5 cohort or corridor grain; escalation to a per-person read requires a targeted A6 contract and an A10-calibrated basis (RINT-I4, RINT-I18); every read is S4-logged; a network-scale movement aggregate is a data commons under F3-I18.
- Contest and restoration protocol. A flag or charge triggers the RINT-I19 suspensive contest under A18-I34; irreversible steps are stayed except a life_critical physical safety hold, which stands while the liability charge is stayed; on prevailing, the actor is restored across rail, anti-value, and reputation, with an S4 audit event on the restoration.
- False-claim protocol. A capture claim shown false to the SUPPLY-I17 standard and wielded for extortion or foreclosure is turned back under RINT-I17 onto the asserter, with restoration of the target under RINT-I19, and an S4 audit event.

### Lifecycle and edge cases

- Genuine emergency drawdown. A narrow, evidenced emergency (a contamination shutoff, a fraud freeze, an emergency waste diversion) is lawful under the owning invariant when it satisfies the owner's coercion-cutoff gate: time-bounded, promptly contestable (RINT-I19), floor-preserving, and restorable; it does not become hoarding, debanking, or dumping by virtue of the emergency (RINT-I7, RINT-I3, RINT-I11).
- Good-faith error. A disclosed, promptly corrected false positive (a mistaken contamination alert, an over-conservative grade) is not concealment or fraud; disclosure is the safe harbor at the owner (RINT-I6, RINT-I8, RINT-I15), and a good-faith-but-wrong action is cured by restoration under RINT-I19, not classed as an offense.
- Cash-like tier. The R2-I4 sub-threshold cash-like tier remains structurally unidentified by design; RINT-I4 does not reach into it to synthesize identifiers, and its privacy is not evasion.
- Cross-boundary movement. A consented, capacity-genuine, true-cost cross-boundary waste or water movement under S2 contracts and F3-I18 is lawful; only a movement selected to escape remediation or oversight onto the weak is barred (RINT-I13).
- Concurrent charges. Where an act triggers several owning invariants (for example a concealed hazardous dump that also breaches a floor), each axis is booked separately and never netted (RINT-I16), and the gravest applicable A18 class governs escalation.
