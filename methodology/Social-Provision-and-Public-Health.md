## 0. How the social-provision and public-health layer works

This is where the state meets the person at their most exposed: when they are owed a floor, when they need care, when they are ill, and when a contagion makes the collective reach for the individual body. The layer's first commitment is that **the floor is unconditional and delivered, and the body is inviolable**: a capability floor is delivered upfront through the standing reserve on the person's own-account basis and is never conditioned, clawed back below itself, weaponised, or gated by a participation condition; and no public-health purpose, however grave, ever authorises forcing an intervention or a sample onto a body over a competent refusal. MOS has no taxation, so provision is floor delivery, not redistribution by levy; and every coercive public-health measure runs through the one canonical coercion threshold, never a standing police power.

**The benefit and its delivery (PROV, B13).** Social provision is the administration layer around the COMP-I17 standing reserve: it models the benefit, entitlement, delivery and take-up objects and cites the floor magnitudes rather than re-pricing them. A floor written down but not received is a live breach, not compliance, so the take-up gap fails toward breach and the default is proactive delivery, not a claim the person must discover; the capability floor is unconditional (theta and behaviour never gate it, only a non-floor top-up may carry conditions), delivered on the person's own account and never cut to punish or coerce, while a non-floor benefit keeps legitimate room for conditions and a solvent free-rider is not handed an open door.

**Care and administration (CARE, ELIG, B13).** Care is a delivered service governed by the recipient's dignity and autonomy, supported not substituted, with a quality-of-care floor, a care worker on the labor floor, unpaid family care booked but never discharging the state's own obligation, and a capture surface (neglect, isolation, looting, over-medication) closed with a named natural-person bearer, a guardian-inaccessible welfare contact, and an affirmative inspection for the recipient who cannot self-signal. Eligibility is a decidable governed act where administrative burden is not allowed to become silent denial (the state bears the burden and delivers what it can already see is owed), overpayment recovery is fault-graded and never breaches the floor, no floor is denied by an opaque algorithm without a human bearer and appeal, and a privacy-preserving check stops a duplicate draw without a surveillance join.

**Population health and measures (EPI, MEAS, B14).** Epidemiology senses the population at cohort grain, never a standing person-grain read: case and contact data stay subject-owned and reachable only through the bounded HACC gates, an epidemiological figure is an indicator not a truth, and immunization coverage is a commons indicator, not a person registry. A public-health measure is coercion: it routes through the A18-I4 threshold (fail-closed until calibrated), is proportionate, least-restrictive, time-boxed and sunset, contestable and non-discriminatory, delivers the constrained person's floor so compliance is never destitution, and stops absolutely at the inviolable bodily-integrity floor, an immunization requirement conditioning at most a non-floor privilege and never forcing the needle or gating a floor. The integrity surface closes benefit fraud on both flanks, benefit-as-coercion, surveillance and function creep, the eugenics and forced-treatment surface, the manufactured emergency, and robo-denial at scale, with the money rail never netted against the anti-value rail.

Three commitments recur, matching the rest of the model:

1. **The floor is unconditional and delivered.** Every capability floor is delivered upfront through COMP-I17 on the person's own account, is never conditioned or gated by theta, is maintained in full during a contest, and is delivered to the constrained person so no measure and no process ever makes compliance mean destitution.
2. **The body is inviolable and coercion runs one gate.** No measure, rung, or emergency touches the bodily-integrity floor, save the single B10-I11 imminent-harm rescue of a person who has not competently refused; every coercive measure routes through A18-I4 and its suspensive contest, never a standing public-health police power.
3. **Both flanks, everywhere.** The claimant, the care recipient, the patient and the surveilled are protected against non-delivery, neglect, coercion and tracking; and the honest administrator, the genuine emergency, the honest care provider and the solvent non-claimant are protected against fraud, false accusation, and paralysis.

The sections follow: PROV social provision and the benefit; CARE social care services; ELIG eligibility, entitlement and administration; EPI population health and epidemiology; MEAS public-health measures and the coercion threshold; HINT social-provision and public-health integrity and capture.

## PROV social provision and the benefit: delivery of the floor (B13)

### Purpose

B13 is the administration and delivery layer that sits around the COMP-I17 standing reserve. It does not create, size, or re-price any floor: the floor magnitudes, the Nussbaum-capability crosswalk, and the theta participation condition are owned by Distributive-Justice-and-Capability-Floors.md, and the upfront own-account delivery mechanism is owned by COMP-I17. This layer models the objects the state actually operates on the delivery side, the benefit, the entitlement, the delivery, and the take_up_record, and cites the care-service by reference from CARE. Its job is the set of rules that turn a declared floor into a delivered one.

The core commitment: a floor that is written down but not received is not compliance, it is a live breach. MOS has no taxation and no redistribution-by-levy, so social provision is not a transfer program that a claimant must find, qualify into, and defend; it is floor delivery on the person's own-account basis, computed and routed, never commanded as a market price and never handed down as discretionary largesse that can be withdrawn to punish or coerce. B13 protects both flanks throughout: no genuine claimant is left below the floor for want of navigating a process, and provision is not a patronage lever nor an open door to a solvent free-rider. B13 opens no new person-grain health read power, no new coercion gate, and no new floor-delivery mechanism.

### Object model

- **benefit**: a typed provision stream. Exactly one of three classes: `floor_benefit` (an instrument of a Distributive-Justice capability floor, delivered via COMP-I17), `top_up` (a non-floor supplement above the floor), or `contributory` (an insurance or contribution-linked benefit). The class is a hard attribute, not a label, and governs which conditionality rules apply. This benefit-class attribute {floor_benefit, top_up, contributory} is the single attribute that ELIG's eligibility predicate reads (cite ELIG), so the delivery side and the eligibility side agree on one classification.
- **entitlement**: the resolved link between a natural person and a benefit at a point in time, carrying `basis` (the floor citation or the contribution record), `grain` (cohort-derived or person-predicate), `status`, and `owed_quantum_ref` (a citation to the Distributive-Justice magnitude, never a number stated here). The `status` enum is the single reconciled set {active, bridged, redetermining, closed} shared by reference with ELIG's Determination object; B13 does not maintain a second, divergent status vocabulary.
- **delivery**: an append-only record that a quantum was actually received on the person's own-account basis, with timestamp, channel (`cash` or `in_kind`), and, for in-kind, the protective ground that justified the departure from cash.
- **take_up_record**: the reconciliation object between what is owed (entitlement) and what was received (delivery). Its residual is the take-up gap.
- **care_service (owned by CARE)**: a delivered service (home care, disability support, personal assistance, reachable essential care). B13 does not model its fields or standards; it cites CARE's `care_service` by reference and treats a care floor as a floor delivery in kind under PROV-I14.
- **escrow_earmark**: a delivery-side constraint that routes an in-kind or ring-fenced quantum to protect the intended beneficiary against capture (fabricated-dependent risk, guardianship-capture).

ELIG's determination, recovery, appeal, and provisional-identity objects, and CARE's care_relationship and care_institution objects, are owned in those sections and cited here by reference, never restated.

### Invariants

- PROV-I1: The benefit, entitlement, and delivery are three separated objects and administration cites the floor, it does not re-model it. A B13 benefit SHALL carry a `basis` that is either a published Distributive-Justice floor citation (for `floor_benefit`) or a contribution record (for `top_up`/`contributory`), and SHALL NOT restate, re-derive, or locally override any floor magnitude, the capability crosswalk, or theta. Purpose: the administration layer can never silently move the floor by adjusting a form. Predicate: a `floor_benefit` whose delivered quantum diverges from the cited COMP-I17/Distributive-Justice magnitude is a defect, not a discretionary variation. Enforcement: the divergence is void on the delivery side and routed as a floor breach; the citation, not the administrator, controls.
- PROV-I2: A floor is delivered, not declared, and the take-up gap fails toward breach. Where a natural person holds an owed floor and no matching `delivery` exists, the residual take_up_record SHALL be booked as an active breach against the administering organ, not as claimant non-compliance. Absent a published delivery interval the interval SHALL default to immediate, so an owed floor with no matching delivery is a live breach on the administrator from the moment the entitlement resolves, and it remains live until a published per-function delivery interval supersedes this conservative default. Purpose: forecloses the historic failure mode where a benefit exists on paper but the eligible person never receives it, and denies the administrator a stall by simply publishing no interval. Predicate: entitlement.status active and owed_quantum_ref cited, with no delivery within the published delivery interval or, absent one, at the moment of resolution. Enforcement: V4 anti-value booked to the administrator per axis (never netted against the $ rail), surfaced at S5 cohort grain, and remediated through COMP-I17 delivery; the breach clears only on a delivery record or under the PROV-I20 informed-declination carve-out, never on a re-declaration.
- PROV-I3: Where a floor is owed, the default SHALL be proactive delivery or auto-enrollment, not a claimant-discovered claim. An administering organ SHALL enroll and deliver a `floor_benefit` from the facts it already lawfully holds at cohort grain plus an S6 eligibility predicate (ELIG), and MAY require a person-initiated claim only where the floor genuinely cannot be resolved without a fact the person alone can supply, and even then SHALL deliver a provisional bridge pending that fact. Purpose: a person owed a floor is not made to find, decode, and navigate a process to receive what is already theirs. Predicate: a `floor_benefit` whose sole delivery path is a claimant-initiated application, where the eligibility facts were resolvable proactively, is a defect. Enforcement: the claim-only gate is void as the default; the omission books to the administrator as a PROV-I2 gap.
- PROV-I4: The capability floor is unconditional, and theta is out of scope for the floor. A `floor_benefit` SHALL NOT be conditioned on behaviour, work-search, activity, drug-testing, sobriety, cooperation, the theta participation condition, or any compliance test, and SHALL NOT be reduced, suspended, or sanctioned as a penalty. Any workfare-style or conduct-conditioned arrangement that operates on the floor itself is void as to the floor. Purpose: the floor secures a capability threshold that no conduct forfeits (capability sufficientarianism, Distributive-Justice); a conditioned floor is not a floor. Predicate: a delivery below the cited floor magnitude attributable to a behavioural condition, a theta shortfall, or a sanction. Enforcement: the conditioned reduction is void and restored via COMP-I17; a scheme that structurally conditions the floor is a floor-waiver-void case under O5 and, where it coerces, an A18 harm. Theta is confined to above-floor and contributory entitlements per PROV-I5 and PROV-I21 and never reaches the floor.
- PROV-I5: A non-floor top-up or a contributory benefit MAY carry conditions, including theta, bounded by the floor. A `top_up` or `contributory` benefit MAY attach reasonable conditions (activity requirements, contribution history, means tests, program rules, the theta participation condition), provided that no condition, sanction, or exhaustion of a conditioned benefit reduces the person's total delivered provision below the cited capability floor. Purpose: both flanks. The state keeps legitimate room to run incentive-bearing, participation-linked, and insurance benefits, and the honest administrator is not forced to hand unconditional extras to a solvent non-claimant, while the floor stays untouched. Predicate: a conditioned reduction, theta shortfall included, whose net effect drops the person below the floor magnitude. Enforcement: the reduction is capped at the floor; the below-floor remainder is delivered as a `floor_benefit` via COMP-I17 regardless of the top-up condition or the theta metric (PROV-I21).
- PROV-I6: Benefit is own-account and cannot be weaponized. A delivered benefit SHALL be held on the person's own-account basis and SHALL NOT be cut, withheld, delayed, or threatened in order to punish, coerce, extract a waiver or concession, or secure political or administrative compliance. Purpose: provision is a delivered floor, not a patronage instrument or a lever of control, mirroring the CONN-I6/CONN-I7 disconnection floor and the O5 anti-weaponization rule. Predicate: a reduction or threat of reduction of a `floor_benefit` tied to any demand other than the benefit's own lawful eligibility facts. Enforcement: the reduction is void; using it to extract a concession is an A18 harm with a natural-person bearer resolved per O1, criminal-class where it targets a rights-holder and concealment-class where the coercion is hidden, and any waiver so extracted is void per O5.
- PROV-I7: Cash on the own-account basis is the default channel; in-kind is permitted only where it protects the person. A `floor_benefit` SHALL default to own-account cash, and MAY be delivered in kind or under an escrow_earmark only on a stated protective ground, for example escrowed in-kind delivery where a fabricated-dependent or diversion risk is present per FAM, or where a service is the floor good itself (CARE). In-kind SHALL NOT be used to control, moralize about, or restrict the ordinary autonomy of a solvent, capable adult. Purpose: both flanks. The person keeps agency over the delivered floor, and a genuinely at-risk beneficiary (a fabricated dependent, a captured ward) is protected from diversion. Predicate: an in-kind or earmarked delivery with no recorded protective ground is a defect defaulting back to cash. Enforcement: an unjustified in-kind restriction is void and redelivered as cash; a justified earmark is recorded on the delivery object and reviewable.
- PROV-I8: No benefit-trap and no cliff: withdrawal tapers so provision never punishes work or honesty. Where a benefit reduces as the person's own resources rise, the reduction SHALL taper such that the marginal effective withdrawal rate stays below a published cap and total resources are monotone non-decreasing in earned value across the taper. A benefit SHALL NOT withdraw in a discontinuous cliff that leaves an earning or honest-reporting person worse off than concealment. Purpose: both flanks. The claimant is never made poorer by working or by telling the truth, and the state is not committed to a flat unconditional maximum to everyone regardless of means. Predicate: a schedule where an increment of earned value produces a net decrease in total resources, or a marginal withdrawal rate above the published cap. Enforcement: the schedule is a defect; the taper is recomputed to restore monotonicity, and the honest reporter is held harmless against the concealer.
- PROV-I9: The floor-benefit is never collateral and never waivable. A `floor_benefit` entitlement and its delivered quantum SHALL NOT be pledged, assigned, attached, set off, garnished, or contracted away, and any waiver of the floor is void. Purpose: the delivered floor stays outside the security and outside private ordering, mirroring the capability-floor-never-collateral rule (U1-I5) and enforcing O5 floor-waiver-void. Predicate: an instrument that collateralizes, assigns, or waives a `floor_benefit`. Enforcement: the instrument is void as to the floor per O5; a lawful $ claim against the person survives intact only against resources above the floor, never against the floor delivery itself.
- PROV-I10: Entitlement is resolved by predicate at cohort grain, and the person-grain gate is B10-I2. Eligibility for a `floor_benefit` SHALL be determined through an ELIG S6 predicate over the minimum necessary facts, revealing only the yes/no protection status and never the underlying means, diagnosis, or income, with sensing defaulting to S5 cohort grain. B13 SHALL NOT read the personal record at person grain except through the B10-I2 person-grain gate (the person's consent or an A6 judicial contract); it opens no new person-grain read power of its own. HACC-I10 is referenced only where a communicable-disease emergency is actually in play, and it remains owned elsewhere. Purpose: a person is not forced to expose their circumstances to receive a floor, and provision cannot become a backdoor surveillance surface. Predicate: an entitlement determination that resolves a cohort to an individual, or reads person-grain facts outside the B10-I2 gate (or, only in a live communicable-disease emergency, the HACC-I10 gate). Enforcement: the read is refused; a resolution outside the S2/S3 access-contract path with S4 audit is a security breach, and no benefit process may cite it.
- PROV-I11: The solvent-free-rider guard is need-indexed and fails toward delivery. A `floor_benefit` is a floor on realized capability, so a person already above the floor on their own resources draws no floor top-up, but where the guard cannot resolve means cleanly and quickly, it SHALL fail toward delivering the floor and reconciling afterward, not toward withholding it. Purpose: both flanks. The state does not hand a floor supplement to someone demonstrably above the floor, yet an uncertain or delayed means check never leaves a genuinely eligible person below the floor while administration deliberates. Predicate: a delivery withheld solely pending an unresolved means determination. Enforcement: provisional COMP-I17 delivery proceeds, any established overpayment is recovered under PROV-I12 and ELIG-I13, and the withholding-pending-check is itself a PROV-I2 gap.
- PROV-I12: Overpayment recovery never breaches the ongoing floor and never nets against an Ae axis; fault-grading is owned by ELIG-I13. Recovery of a benefit overpayment SHALL be fault-graded under ELIG-I13, cited and not restated here, SHALL NOT be netted against any Ae axis (a $ recovery books on the money rail and any residual harm books V4 per axis, never across axes or against the money rail), and SHALL NOT be effected by reducing the person's ongoing delivered floor below the cited magnitude. Purpose: both flanks. The administrator keeps whatever enforceable claim ELIG-I13 grades as recoverable, and the person's live floor is not cannibalized to satisfy a past debt, mirroring CONN-I8. Predicate: a recovery action that reduces the current-period delivery below the floor, that books the debt against an Ae axis, or that asserts a recoverability grade conflicting with ELIG-I13. Enforcement: the below-floor portion of the recovery is void; where ELIG-I13 grades an amount recoverable, it is collected only against above-floor resources on a taper consistent with PROV-I8, and an agency-error or good-faith grade under ELIG-I13 controls what may be pursued at all.
- PROV-I13: No administrative measure may cross the dignity and bodily-integrity floor, and any non-bodily coercive step routes through A18-I4. No benefit condition, sanction, recovery, verification, or in-kind restriction SHALL breach the inviolable dignity and bodily-integrity floor that sits outside the value calculus. Any bodily-penetrating or forced-treatment step (compelled treatment, forced injection, forced sampling by venipuncture, swab, or biometric taking over a competent refusal) is non-priceable criminal-class OUTSIDE the A18-I4 calculus and SHALL NOT be assessed within it at all; it is barred on the inviolable side and cited to MEAS-I7. Only a non-bodily administrative step that is coercive in substance (compelled disclosure beyond a predicate, compelled relocation, compelled activity backed by loss) SHALL be assessed as coercion under A18-I4 rather than as ordinary administration. Purpose: provision administration cannot become a soft channel for coercion the enforcement ladder would otherwise gate, and it can never launder a bodily breach through a pricing calculus. Predicate: a bodily-penetrating or forced-treatment step, which is void and criminal-class per MEAS-I7; or a non-bodily administrative step whose non-compliance penalty functions as a hard enforcement rung. Enforcement: a bodily step is void and criminal-class outside A18-I4 (MEAS-I7); a non-bodily coercive step is void until it passes A18-I4 and fails closed while the coercion threshold function is uncalibrated; the dignity floor is non-touchable by any measure or emergency.
- PROV-I14: Delivered care services are floor deliveries and their labour is a delivered wage floor. A `care_service` (owned by CARE) that instruments a capability floor (essential home care, disability support, personal assistance, reachable essential care) SHALL be treated as a floor delivery in kind under this layer, subject to the same take-up-gap, unconditionality, and non-weaponization rules as a cash `floor_benefit`, its paid care workers SHALL receive the O3 delivered labour floor, and unpaid household care that substitutes for it SHALL be booked as delivered value per FAM. Purpose: both flanks. The person receives the care they are owed rather than a cash line they cannot convert into care, the care worker is not floored below the O3 wage, and the household that provides care unpaid is not treated as providing nothing. Predicate: a care floor delivered subject to a behavioural condition, or a care worker paid below the O3 floor, or unpaid household care booked at zero. Enforcement: the condition is void per PROV-I4, the wage shortfall routes to O3, and the FAM booking is corrected. The care-service standard and its objects are owned by CARE and cited here, not restated.
- PROV-I15: Benefits, entitlements, and deliveries are append-only records of account, corrected by new event. Every benefit, entitlement, and delivery, and every change to their state, SHALL be an append-only entry in the A12-mandated benefit register (R1), a mutable current view being a non-authoritative fold over appended entries, and corrections SHALL be new events, never silent edits. Purpose: delivery is court-provable, the take-up gap is auditable, and good-faith reliance on the projection is protected (R1-I28). Predicate: a benefit state asserted outside the register, or a retroactive edit that overwrites a prior delivery record. Enforcement: the unregistered state is non-authoritative; a correction that is not a new appended event is void.
- PROV-I16: An entitlement SHALL NOT silently lapse and redetermination SHALL NOT open a floor hole. A `floor_benefit` entitlement SHALL NOT expire, pause, or terminate by mere passage of time, form non-return, or scheduled review, and a periodic redetermination SHALL deliver a continuing bridge at the cited floor while it runs, so no gap in delivery opens below the floor during administration. Purpose: both flanks. The person is not dropped below the floor by a clerical lapse or a review cycle, while the administrator retains a live redetermination path to correct genuine ineligibility going forward. Predicate: a delivery interruption whose cause is a lapsed form, an unreturned notice, or a pending review rather than an affirmatively resolved ineligibility. Enforcement: the interruption is a PROV-I2 gap; the bridge continues under COMP-I17 until an affirmative, contestable ineligibility finding closes the entitlement.
- PROV-I17: The floor is reachable without registration status and for the undocumented. Delivery of an essential-care and subsistence floor SHALL be reachable by every natural person physically present, including the stateless, undocumented, and no-fixed-address, and SHALL NOT be gated on citizenship, residence, or registration status, citing the HACC-I11 essential-care-and-registration floor and treating an unmet reachable floor (an R4-absence) as a monitored breach. Purpose: both flanks. No person falls below the essential floor for lack of papers, while the wider contributory and top-up benefits legitimately remain status-linked. Predicate: an essential floor refused solely on registration or documentation status. Enforcement: the refusal is void as to the essential floor; the R4-absence surfaces as a monitored breach and routes to remediation, without opening any new person-grain read.
- PROV-I18: A benefit delivered to a person under supported decision-making is not a guardian's to capture. Where a beneficiary has graded or supported capacity (CAP), the benefit SHALL be delivered to and for that person under supported-not-substituted decision-making, never routed to a guardian as a silent default, and where a capture risk is present the delivery SHALL carry an escrow_earmark to the beneficiary's own use. Purpose: both flanks. A ward's floor reaches the ward and cannot be looted or leveraged by a guardian (guardianship-capture, Persons-Life-Events-and-Property.md), while a genuinely supporting guardian can still administer delivery for the person's benefit. Predicate: a benefit re-routed to a guardian's account absent an affirmative, recorded supported-decision basis, or diverted from the ward's use. Enforcement: the diversion is caught as guardianship-capture and reversed; persistent looting is an A18 harm with a natural-person bearer.
- PROV-I19: An adverse benefit decision gets suspensive contest on the money, but the full floor keeps flowing during the contest. A reduction, refusal, closure, or recovery SHALL be contestable with A18-I34 suspensive effect on the disputed $ amount, and where a `floor_benefit` is in dispute the FULL cited floor magnitude, not merely an essential minimum, SHALL continue to be delivered through COMP-I17 while the contest runs, never suspended by the monetary dispute; only the disputed above-floor amount waits on A18-I34. Purpose: both flanks. The person is not driven below the floor while contesting an error, mirroring the CONN-I6 lifeline that a monetary contest never suspends, and the administrator's above-floor recovery or reduction is preserved for resolution. Predicate: a floor delivery halted or reduced below the full cited magnitude pending contest, or a recovery enforced against the floor before A6 resolves. Enforcement: full-floor delivery is restored immediately; the disputed above-floor amount waits on A18-I34 and, where needed, an A6 finding. ELIG-I11 and MEAS-I13 cite this single rule.
- PROV-I20: The take-up gap is monitored at cohort grain and a persistent gap books to the administrator, not the person, and an operating floor with no reconciliation interval is itself a breach. Aggregate take-up SHALL be measured as an S5 cohort-grain statistic and reconciled against the population owed a floor, and a persistent or structural gap SHALL be booked as V4 anti-value to the administering organ (a delivery failure, not a claimant fault). An operating `floor_benefit` with no published reconciliation interval is itself a floor-administration breach against the state, and until a reconciliation interval is published the residual books immediately rather than waiting. The informed-declination carve-out SHALL clear the breach ONLY on an affirmative, register-recorded (R1), contestable, non-coerced refusal event by a capable adult, and SHALL NEVER be inferred from non-response, silence, or mere non-take-up; a floor drawn by no one because no one is owed it is likewise not a breach. Purpose: both flanks. The state is held to actually delivering the floor rather than merely publishing it or hiding behind an unpublished interval, and it is not charged with a phantom breach for a floor a capable person affirmatively declined or that no eligible person exists to receive. Predicate: an owed-versus-delivered residual persisting beyond the published reconciliation interval, or immediately where none is published, with no register-recorded informed-declination event. Enforcement: the residual books to the administrator per axis and drives a proactive-delivery (PROV-I3) remediation; the cohort statistic never resolves to an individual (S5).
- PROV-I21: The theta participation condition never gates the capability floor. The theta participation condition owned by Distributive-Justice-and-Capability-Floors.md is a V5-frame, above-floor, contributory participation condition ONLY, and SHALL NEVER condition, gate, reduce, suspend, or delay delivery of a capability floor under COMP-I17. Any delivery below the cited floor magnitude attributed to non-participation, low participation, or any theta shortfall is void and SHALL be restored via COMP-I17. Purpose: theta calibrates above-floor and contributory participation, never the unconditional floor; a floor conditioned on participation is not a floor (Distributive-Justice, capability sufficientarianism). Predicate: a `floor_benefit` delivery reduced, withheld, or delayed by reference to theta or any participation metric. Enforcement: the reduction is void and restored via COMP-I17; theta is confined to `top_up` and `contributory` entitlements per PROV-I5 and is expressly out of scope for the floor per PROV-I4.

### Protocols

- **Proactive enrollment (default path)**: cohort-derived facts plus an ELIG S6 predicate resolve an entitlement, COMP-I17 delivers the floor on the own-account basis, and a delivery record closes the take_up_record. No claimant action is required (PROV-I3, PROV-I10).
- **Claim-assisted path (exception)**: used only where a person-supplied fact is genuinely necessary. A provisional bridge delivers immediately under PROV-I11/PROV-I16 while the fact is gathered; the person is never below the floor during collection.
- **Means reconciliation**: the solvent-free-rider guard runs after delivery, not before it (PROV-I11); any overpayment is fault-graded under ELIG-I13 and, only to the extent ELIG-I13 grades it recoverable, collected on the $ rail against above-floor resources on a PROV-I8 taper without ever breaching the ongoing floor (PROV-I12).
- **Redetermination**: periodic review runs against a continuing bridge (PROV-I16); closure requires an affirmative, contestable ineligibility finding, not a lapse.
- **Adverse-decision contest**: reductions, refusals, closures, and recoveries route to A18-I34 suspensive contest on the money; the full cited floor continues throughout (PROV-I19).
- **Coercion check**: a bodily-penetrating or forced-treatment step is barred outright as non-priceable criminal-class outside the A18-I4 calculus (MEAS-I7); only a non-bodily administrative step that is coercive in substance is gated through A18-I4 and fails closed while uncalibrated (PROV-I13).

### Lifecycle and edge cases

- **The person who never claims**: an owed floor with no delivery is a PROV-I2 breach on the administrator from the moment the entitlement resolves, remediated by proactive delivery, not excused by non-application.
- **The solvent non-claimant**: a person above the floor draws no floor top-up (PROV-I11); the guard nonetheless fails toward delivery and reconciles afterward so uncertainty never denies a genuine claimant.
- **The honest reporter versus the concealer**: the PROV-I8 taper guarantees the person who reports rising income is never left worse off than one who conceals it.
- **The person who affirmatively declines**: a floor is not counted as a breach only where a capable adult files an affirmative, register-recorded, contestable, non-coerced refusal event; silence, non-response, and non-take-up never evidence declination (PROV-I20).
- **The captured ward**: benefit reaches the ward under CAP supported decision-making with an escrow_earmark where capture risk exists; diversion is caught as guardianship-capture (PROV-I18).
- **The undocumented person**: the essential floor is reachable regardless of papers (PROV-I17); an unmet reachable floor is a monitored R4-absence breach.
- **The fabricated dependent**: escrowed in-kind delivery per FAM protects the intended beneficiary against a fabricated or diverting intermediary (PROV-I7).
- **The overpayment**: fault-graded under ELIG-I13 and recovered only to that extent, on the $ rail against above-floor resources, never by cutting the live floor and never netted against an Ae axis (PROV-I12).
- **The sanction attempt**: a behavioural, workfare, or theta condition on the floor is void (PROV-I4, PROV-I21); the same condition, theta included, on a top-up is permitted only so far as the floor stays whole (PROV-I5).
- **The review-cycle lapse**: no floor hole opens during redetermination; a bridge continues until an affirmative closure (PROV-I16).
- **The bodily-integrity line**: no administrative step may compel treatment or take a sample over a competent refusal; such steps are barred criminal-class outside the pricing calculus (PROV-I13, MEAS-I7), not routed through A18-I4.
- **The public-health emergency**: B13 opens no person-grain read; person-grain benefit administration runs through the B10-I2 gate (consent or an A6 judicial contract), and only where a communicable-disease emergency is actually in play does the HACC-I10 notify-only gate apply, owned elsewhere (PROV-I10); any coercive measure is handled by MEAS/A18, not administration.

The informed-declination boundary is resolved: PROV-I20 requires an affirmative, register-recorded, contestable, non-coerced refusal event by a capable adult, never an inference from non-response, so it cannot become a coercive disclosure demand.

## CARE social care services: the care relationship and the care institution (B13)

### Purpose

This section models B13 social care services (childcare, elder care, disability care, social work) as a DELIVERED service standing on top of the COMP-I17 standing reserve. It gives the care_service object, the care_relationship object, and the care_institution object their normative shape, so that a capability floor booked at the person's own-account basis actually arrives at a competent, dignified standard and cannot be captured, hollowed out, or converted into a lever of control over a dependent person.

This layer is administration and delivery. It CITES the floor magnitudes and the theta participation condition owned by Distributive-Justice-and-Capability-Floors.md, the delivery mechanism and the Benefit / Entitlement objects owned by PROV / COMP-I17, the eligibility objects owned by ELIG, the capacity and guardianship-capture discipline owned by CAP in Persons-Life-Events-and-Property.md, the personal-health sovereignty owned by B10 and the essential-care floor owned by HACC-I11, the coercion threshold owned by A18-I4, and the delivered labor floor owned by O3. It does not re-model any of them and opens no new person-grain health read power, no new coercion gate, and no new floor-delivery mechanism.

Both flanks are load-bearing throughout: the care recipient and the care worker are protected against a capturing, neglecting, or extractive institution, AND the honest solvent provider and the honest administrator are protected against a fabricated neglect claim used to extort, defund, or seize a book of dependents. Continuity of the delivered floor is stated as a bounded, monitored obligation, not asserted as gapless.

### Object model

CARE OWNS care_service, care_relationship, and care_institution (together with their sub-objects care_plan, standard_of_care, and welfare_contact_channel). It CITES PROV's Benefit and Entitlement objects and ELIG's eligibility objects (Determination, ProvisionalIdentity) by reference and does not restate their fields (per C13).

- **care_service**: a CARE-owned delivered service instance with fields {service_id, service_class (childcare | elder_care | disability_care | social_work | mixed), recipient_ref, provider_ref, accountable_officer_ref, entitlement_ref (cites the PROV Entitlement of benefit-class floor_benefit that this care draws on, delivered on the COMP-I17 own-account basis), care_plan_ref, standard_of_care_ref, start_event, status}. A care_service is a delivered-value object on the slow floor rail, not a money-rail transaction, and never a redistribution-by-levy transfer.
- **care_relationship**: the CARE-owned standing relation {recipient_ref, worker_or_kin_ref, relationship_class (paid_worker | family_unpaid | volunteer | statutory_social_work), consent_state, supported_decision_ref (nullable, cites CAP), self_signal_capacity (assessed and recorded at admission and at each review cadence per CARE-I12), review_cadence}. A care_relationship is the unit at which dignity, consent, and continuity are evaluated.
- **care_institution**: a CARE-owned organization-typed provider {org_ref (cites O1), accountable_officer_ref (named natural person), capacity, occupancy, register_ref, welfare_contact_channel_ref, restraint_log_ref, exit_bond_ref}. An institution MAY hold many care_service instances.
- **care_plan**: the recipient-specific plan of delivered acts and outcomes, versioned, correction-by-new-event, held in an A12-mandated append-only register (R1/R2). The care_plan references clinical facts by cohort-safe pointer only and never embeds the personal health record (cites B10-I1, HACC-I7).
- **standard_of_care**: the published, versioned floor standard for a service_class (staffing ratio, competency, safeguarding, nutrition, mobility, social-contact minima). The standard is the deliverable, distinct from the funding.
- **welfare_contact_channel**: a guardian-inaccessible and provider-inaccessible reach-back path from recipient to a state or independent safeguarding endpoint, with a liveness expectation.
- **neglect_signal** and **capture_signal**: booked anti-value events (V4) on the harm axis, per axis, never netted against the money rail or against the provider's solvency.

### Invariants

**CARE-I1 (care is a delivered floor, not a funded declaration).** A care_service SHALL be counted as delivered only when the standard_of_care is actually met at the recipient, measured at the recipient and not at the point of funding. Purpose: a funded placement that does not deliver competent care is a take-up-gap floor breach under COMP-I17, not compliance. Predicate: `funded(service) AND NOT met(standard_of_care, recipient) => floor_breach`. Enforcement hook: the breach books to the entitlement owner (the PROV floor benefit) as an undelivered floor, and to the provider as a delivery default; it does not reduce the recipient's floor.

**CARE-I2 (recipient consent governs the care relationship).** Entry into, continuation of, and material change to a care_relationship SHALL be governed by the recipient's consent, expressed directly or through supported decision-making per CAP. Purpose: care is done with a person, not to a person. Predicate: `change(care_relationship) requires valid_consent(recipient) OR supported_consent(recipient, CAP)`. Enforcement hook: an unconsented material change is void and books a capture_signal.

**CARE-I3 (supported not substituted decision-making).** Where capacity is graded or fluctuating, the care_relationship SHALL default to supported decision-making per CAP, and SHALL NOT silently substitute a guardian's or institution's will for the recipient's. Purpose: dependency is not a forfeiture of the will. Predicate: any decision recorded as substituted SHALL carry an explicit CAP authorization event and a least-restrictive justification; absence of that event on a substituted decision is a capture_signal. Both flanks: this equally protects the honest guardian who holds a genuine CAP authorization, whose recorded authorization is a complete answer to a substitution challenge. Note: no CAP process, supported or substituted, ever authorizes a bodily intervention over a person's refusal (cite MEAS-I7, CARE-I4).

**CARE-I4 (forced care is barred except the single B10-I11 rescue).** No care act SHALL be imposed on a competently refusing recipient. Consistent with MEAS-I7, the ONLY exception is the single B10-I11 imminent-harm presumed-consent rescue of a person who has NOT competently refused; forced intervention over a competent refusal is non-priceable criminal-class outside the A18-I4 calculus (cite A18). Any coercive containment (locking in, restraint, confinement) is a coercive measure that routes through A18-I4 and never through this layer. Purpose: close the gap where "care" becomes unaccountable detention. Predicate: `forced_act AND NOT B10-I11_rescue => criminal-class`; `coercive_containment => A18-I4 routing, not a care-provider power`. Enforcement hook: no standing care-provider power to detain exists; the dignity and bodily-integrity floor sits outside the calculus and no placement, plan, or emergency may touch it (cite A18, MEAS-I7).

**CARE-I5 (quality-of-care floor is a delivered standard).** Each service_class SHALL have a published, versioned standard_of_care, and delivery SHALL be evaluated against it at the recipient grain via cohort-safe outcome sensing (cites S5). Purpose: make "good enough" decidable and auditable rather than discretionary. Enforcement hook: FAIL-CLOSED, an unpublished standard for an operating service_class is itself a floor-administration breach against the state, not a licence for any standard.

**CARE-I6 (neglect books V4 anti-value with teeth).** A shortfall below the standard_of_care that reaches the recipient SHALL book as V4 anti-value on the harm axis, per axis, attributed to the accountable_officer as bearer (subject to the CARE-I19 fundability-defect reallocation) and never netted against the provider's revenue, occupancy, or other-axis performance. The reaching-the-recipient harm SHALL be evidenced at cohort-grain, consented-grain, OR A6-contracted person-grain safeguarding-inspection grade (CARE-I12, CARE-I15). Purpose: a well-run balance sheet cannot buy out a neglected person. Predicate: `harm_to_recipient => V4_booking(axis=care_harm, bearer=accountable_officer OR CARE-I19 fundability_bearer)`. Both flanks: the booking requires a reached-the-recipient harm, not a mere paperwork defect (see CARE-I15), so an honest provider with a clerical lapse and no harmed recipient does not acquire an anti-value debit.

**CARE-I7 (the care worker is a floor-holding worker).** Every paid care worker SHALL hold the O3 delivered labor floor in full; care work SHALL NOT be structured to route around O3 through misclassification, unpaid on-call, or agency layering. Purpose: an underpaid, overstretched care workforce is the upstream cause of neglect, so the worker floor and the recipient floor are protected together. Enforcement hook: an O3 breach in a care_institution is also a leading indicator flagged into the CARE-I13 capture surface; the O5 floor-waiver-void rule applies to any contract term purporting to waive it.

**CARE-I8 (unpaid family care is delivered value but never discharges the state floor).** Unpaid household and kin care SHALL be booked as delivered value per FAM and SHALL be supported, but SHALL NOT discharge, reduce, or offset the recipient's own-account floor entitlement under COMP-I17, and SHALL NOT be counted as the state's delivery of the care floor. The recipient's COMP-I17 entitlement to a chosen paid care_service remains whole regardless of available kin care. Purpose: recognize and support kin care without conscripting kin, trapping the recipient, or letting the mere availability of family become a reason to withhold the delivered floor. Both flanks: the family carer's delivered value is recognized and supported; the recipient retains the CARE-I11 choice to move to a paid provider (COMP-I17) without that move being scored as the family's failure or treated as a new draw the state may refuse.

**CARE-I9 (named natural-person accountable officer).** Every care_institution and every care_service SHALL name a natural-person accountable_officer who is the bearer for CARE-I6 anti-value and CARE-I13 capture findings, subject to the CARE-I19 fundability-defect reallocation. Purpose: no autonomous-agent, shell, or corporate-veil path SHALL leave a neglect or capture harm without a natural-person bearer (cites O1 veil-piercing and the no-floor-breach-without-a-bearer rule in Organizations-Contracts-and-Employment.md). Predicate: `care_service WITHOUT live accountable_officer_ref => operating_without_bearer` and is a suspend-to-safe condition, not a silent continuation.

**CARE-I10 (care-institution capture predicate).** An institution that neglects, loots the recipient's property or entitlement, isolates the recipient from outside contact, over-medicates, or over-restrains a dependent recipient SHALL trip the care-institution capture predicate, mirroring the CAP guardianship-capture predicate. Purpose: institutional capture is the same wrong as guardian capture and gets the same teeth. Predicate: `looting OR isolation OR chemical_or_physical_over_restraint OR entitlement_diversion => capture_signal(bearer=accountable_officer)`. Enforcement hook: capture routes to A6 for a finding; looting of the recipient's floor entitlement is criminal-class (a rights violation) and is never priced.

**CARE-I11 (choice and portability of provider).** A recipient SHALL be able to change care provider and SHALL NOT be locked to a captor provider by contract, by control of the entitlement, by control of records, or by control of the physical exit. Purpose: exit is the recipient's first defence against capture. Predicate: the entitlement_ref follows the recipient (own-account basis, COMP-I17), the care_plan is portable on request (correction-by-new-event, register-held), and any exit penalty that exceeds the CARE-I14 exit_bond is void per O5. Both flanks: the honest provider keeps a bounded, published notice period and a genuine cost-recovery exit_bond; it simply cannot weaponize them to trap a recipient.

**CARE-I12 (guardian-inaccessible welfare-contact channel; cohort alarm, A6-gated individuation).** Every recipient in a care_relationship SHALL have a welfare_contact_channel that the guardian, the institution, and the accountable_officer cannot read, block, or sever, reaching a state or independent safeguarding endpoint. The channel SHALL carry a liveness expectation such that a signal-capable recipient's unexplained silence past the review_cadence raises a cohort-grain safeguarding alarm. The cohort layer only raises the alarm and opens no new standing person-grain read. The individual welfare check is explicitly person-grain and SHALL run ONLY through an A6 judicial contract that is scoped, S4-audited, and time-bound (cite B10-I2). The single fast person-grain path without a prior A6 contract is the B10-I11 imminent-harm presumed-consent rescue, available only where the alarm evidences imminent harm to a person who has NOT competently refused, and it SHALL carry a mandatory post-hoc A6 review. For a recipient assessed at admission and at each review cadence as unable to reliably self-signal, safeguarding SHALL NOT rely on the passive silence alarm; the state SHALL instead conduct an affirmative periodic guardian-and-provider-inaccessible in-person welfare inspection under the same A6-gated person-grain safeguarding authority. Purpose: an isolated recipient cannot ask for help, so absence of contact must be a signal, but individuation must not become a standing surveillance read of every dependent person, and the recipient who cannot invoke the channel must not be left unwatched. Predicate: `no_liveness(channel, signal_capable recipient) beyond cadence => cohort_alarm`; `cohort_alarm => A6_contracted_welfare_check OR (imminent_harm => B10-I11_rescue WITH post_hoc A6 review)`; `NOT self_signal_capable(recipient) => affirmative A6-gated in-person inspection at cadence`. Enforcement hook: no new person-grain health read power is opened here (cite B10-I2, B10-I11); individuation is A6-gated and S4-audited; HACC-I10 is not a listed path because this is not a communicable-disease matter. Both flanks: a benign explanation (recipient travelling, competent recipient opting out of contact) clears the alarm on record, so an honest provider is not punished for a recipient's own choice; the affirmative inspection for a non-signaler protects the recipient who cannot invoke the channel while remaining scoped, gated, and audited so it cannot be used as a pretextual sweep.

**CARE-I13 (restraint-for-convenience is a capture signal).** Physical or chemical restraint, confinement, or sedation applied for staffing convenience, punishment, or cost rather than for the recipient's own imminent-safety need SHALL be treated as a capture signal, not as care. Purpose: convenience restraint is the most common quiet form of institutional capture. Predicate: every restraint event SHALL be logged in restraint_log with a contemporaneous least-restrictive justification tied to the recipient's own safety; a restraint without that justification, or a pattern correlating with low staffing (an O3 / CARE-I7 stress indicator) rather than with recipient risk, books a capture_signal. Both flanks: a genuine, logged, proportionate, time-limited, consented-or-A6-authorized safety restraint under the recipient's own imminent-harm case is protected and is not a capture signal; the log is the honest clinician's defence.

**CARE-I14 (continuity of care; state as monitored provider of last resort).** A provider insolvency, licence loss, or voluntary exit SHALL NOT strand a recipient. Last-resort provision is floor-delivery-in-kind whose FUNDING routes through the COMP-I17 own-account standing reserve (cite PROV-I14); the operational service standard of that provision is CARE's standard_of_care, and state operation does not re-define the COMP-I17 compute-and-route mechanism. On any provider_exit event the state SHALL secure a competent successor provider or direct provision within a published maximum interval; an unfilled interval is a monitored breach routed to remediation, not a gapless guarantee. Purpose: the recipient's floor is unconditional and does not blink when a business fails, and the guarantee is honestly bounded rather than asserted as instantaneous. Predicate: `provider_exit(service) => successor_or_direct_provision(recipient) WITHIN published_max_interval; unfilled_interval => monitored_breach routed to remediation`. Enforcement hook: providers of dependent care post a proportionate exit_bond funding orderly transition; the bond funds handover, it does not gate the recipient's floor, and last-resort provision is compute-and-route delivery, never a lever withdrawn to coerce a recipient or a provider.

**CARE-I15 (both-flanks neglect claim: fabricated-claim guard).** A neglect or capture finding SHALL rest on reached-the-recipient evidence at cohort-grain, consented-grain, OR A6-contracted person-grain safeguarding-inspection grade (CARE-I12), and SHALL survive A18-I34 suspensive contest before any hard enforcement rung attaches. Purpose: protect the honest solvent provider and the honest administrator from a fabricated neglect claim used to extort, defund, seize a book of dependents, or capture the institution's level (cites the GOV level-capture guard). Predicate: `neglect_finding requires evidence_grade(reached_recipient) AND passes(A18-I34)`; a claim that fails this is itself booked, and a knowingly fabricated claim is concealment-class with the perpetual flip. Enforcement hook: the coercion rung is set only by A18-I4 on the published function and never by the claimant's assertion; FAIL-CLOSED until A10 calibrates.

**CARE-I16 (essential care and registration floor for the unreachable recipient).** No person SHALL be excluded from B13 essential social care for want of status, documents, guardianship, or a provider willing to take them, and the essential-care and registration floor of HACC-I11 SHALL be honoured for the stateless, undocumented, or provider-abandoned recipient, with R4-absence a monitored breach. Purpose: the hardest-to-place recipient is exactly the capture and abandonment target. Enforcement hook: provider-of-last-resort (CARE-I14) plus HACC-I11 essential-care floor; the state carries the residual.

**CARE-I17 (no bundling of care with a rights waiver).** Access to a care_service SHALL NOT be conditioned on the recipient waiving a floor, a vote, a consent right, control of property, or the CARE-I12 welfare channel. Purpose: the dependency relationship is the classic site of coerced waiver. Predicate: any care contract term extracting such a waiver is void per O5 floor-waiver-void and books a capture_signal against the accountable_officer. Both flanks: ordinary, disclosed, priced service terms (fees within the money rail, house rules proportionate to communal safety) remain valid; only floor-waiver bundling is void.

**CARE-I18 (over-medication and entitlement diversion are per-axis harms).** Over-medication is booked on the care-harm axis as bodily-integrity anti-value (V4) and entitlement diversion (skimming the recipient's floor entitlement, pension, or property) is booked as looting on the property axis, each per axis, never netted against each other or against the provider's money-rail solvency, and diversion of a floor entitlement is criminal-class and never priced. Purpose: keep the two capture channels, the body and the purse, separately visible so neither hides behind the other. Enforcement hook: A6 finding, bearer is the accountable_officer, restitution of diverted entitlement to the recipient in full and outside any priced settlement.

**CARE-I19 (bearer allocation for a fundability defect).** Where a neglect harm is attributable to a funded standard set below the resources the published standard_of_care requires (a fundability defect), the CARE-I6 V4 booking SHALL route to the funding and standard-setting organ's named bearer, not to the front-line accountable_officer. The accountable_officer is DISCHARGED of that harm on showing a good-faith, contemporaneously recorded fundability shortfall (a documented record that adequate resources to meet the standard were requested and not funded), and bears ONLY harms within their control given adequate funding. Purpose: do not scapegoat the front-line officer for a defunding decision made above them, and do not let the funding organ hide behind the front-line name. Predicate: `neglect_harm AND funded_standard < published_standard_of_care AND recorded_good_faith_shortfall(officer) => V4_booking(bearer=funding_and_standard_setting_organ)`; `else => V4_booking(bearer=accountable_officer)`. Both flanks: the honest officer who flagged the shortfall is protected; the funding organ that set the standard below its own published requirement carries the harm it caused; an officer who fabricates a shortfall or who caused a controllable harm despite adequate funding is not discharged.

### Protocols

- **Admission protocol.** Verify entitlement_ref (PROV floor benefit, COMP-I17 own-account), obtain recipient consent or CAP supported consent (CARE-I2, CARE-I3), assess and record self_signal_capacity (CARE-I12), name the accountable_officer (CARE-I9), open the care_plan register entry and the welfare_contact_channel (CARE-I12), publish the applicable standard_of_care version (CARE-I5), post the exit_bond for dependent care (CARE-I14). No admission on a waiver bundle (CARE-I17).
- **Continuous safeguarding protocol.** Cohort-safe outcome sensing against standard_of_care (CARE-I5, S5); welfare-channel liveness checks raising a cohort-grain alarm (CARE-I12); restraint-log review for convenience patterns and O3 staffing correlation (CARE-I13, CARE-I7). Person-grain escalation ONLY via a scoped, S4-audited, time-bound A6 judicial contract, or the B10-I11 imminent-harm rescue with mandatory post-hoc A6 review; affirmative A6-gated in-person inspection for recipients assessed as unable to self-signal (CARE-I12). Never a new standing read power, and never via HACC-I10 (this is not a communicable-disease matter).
- **Capture-finding protocol.** A capture_signal or neglect_signal opens an A6 matter; the provider gets A18-I34 suspensive contest (CARE-I15) before any hard rung; the rung, if any, is set by A18-I4 on the published function; bearer is allocated per CARE-I6 and the CARE-I19 fundability-defect split; criminal-class harms (looting, entitlement diversion, bodily-integrity violations) are never priced.
- **Exit and continuity protocol.** On provider_exit, trigger successor placement or direct state provision within the published maximum interval (CARE-I14), port the care_plan (CARE-I11), draw the exit_bond for handover only, keep the recipient's floor untouched; an unfilled interval books a monitored breach routed to remediation.

### Lifecycle and edge cases

- **Fluctuating capacity.** Track consent_state, supported_decision_ref, and self_signal_capacity over the review_cadence; a downgrade in capacity SHALL widen support (CAP), never silently license substitution (CARE-I3), and SHALL re-trigger the CARE-I12 self-signal assessment.
- **Recipient refuses beneficial care.** Competent refusal governs (CARE-I4); the refusal is recorded, the floor remains available and re-offerable, and non-take-up is monitored as a possible floor-breach signal under COMP-I17 but is never coerced.
- **Recipient who cannot reliably self-signal.** Safeguarding SHALL NOT rest on the passive silence alarm; the state runs the affirmative, guardian-and-provider-inaccessible, A6-gated periodic in-person welfare inspection (CARE-I12), scoped and S4-audited so it protects the recipient without becoming a pretextual sweep.
- **Family carer burnout or conflict of interest.** FAM value recognized and supported (CARE-I8), and the recipient's own-account floor and CARE-I11 choice of a paid provider remain whole regardless of available kin care; if the family relationship shows CARE-I10 capture indicators (isolation, diversion, over-restraint), the same predicate applies to kin as to institutions, and the CARE-I12 channel is the recipient's independent reach-out.
- **Provider sudden collapse.** State provision activates within the published maximum interval (CARE-I14); an unfilled interval is a monitored breach routed to remediation, and no recipient waits on the insolvency estate for the delivered floor.
- **Neglect traced to a defunded standard.** Where a good-faith recorded fundability shortfall explains the harm, the V4 booking routes to the funding and standard-setting organ's bearer, not the front-line officer (CARE-I19); a fabricated shortfall or a controllable harm despite adequate funding does not discharge the officer.
- **Malicious or competitor-driven neglect report.** CARE-I15 evidence grade and A18-I34 contest protect the honest provider; a knowingly false report flips to concealment-class.
- **Recipient with no reachable next of kin, stateless or undocumented.** CARE-I16 plus HACC-I11 carry the floor; R4-absence is a monitored breach, not an exclusion.

## ELIG eligibility, entitlement determination and administration (B13)

### Purpose

This layer models the administration that stands between a person and a capability floor: the act that decides whether a benefit, entitlement or care-service is owed to a given subject, and the delivery machinery around that decision. It does NOT model floor magnitudes, the Nussbaum crosswalk or the theta participation condition (those belong to Distributive-Justice-and-Capability-Floors.md, and theta is a V5-frame participation condition that NEVER gates the floor), and it does NOT re-model the delivery reserve (COMP-I17 owns compute-and-route own-account delivery). ELIG OWNS the eligibility-decision surfaces: `EligibilityPredicate`, `Determination`, `AppealCase`, `RecoveryAction` and `ProvisionalIdentity`. It CITES the PROV-owned `Benefit` and `Entitlement` and the CARE-owned `care_service` by reference rather than restating their fields, and it reads PROV's benefit-class attribute {floor_benefit, top_up, contributory} as the classifier its predicates key on.

Both flanks are load-bearing throughout. A determination that denies the genuine claimant is a floor breach; a determination that cannot detect the ineligible or the fraudulent turns floor delivery into an unpriceable leak that the honest administrator cannot defend. Neither flank may be sacrificed to purchase the other. The design principle is that eligibility is a decidable question answered by the state against data the state largely already holds, not an obstacle course the claimant must survive.

### Object model

ELIG-owned objects (fully specified here):

- `EligibilityPredicate`: a published, versioned, decidable rule mapping a subject's booked facts to {owed, not_owed, indeterminate}. Fields: predicate_id, version, benefit_ref (citation into the PROV `Benefit`), benefit_class_read (reads PROV's {floor_benefit, top_up, contributory} attribute), input_facts[] (each a named register field or S5 cohort statistic), decision_procedure, indeterminate_handling, published_at. `indeterminate` is a real third value and routes to proactive provisional delivery, not to denial.
- `Determination`: the governed decision act. Fields: determination_id, subject_ref, predicate_ref+version, input_facts_snapshot, outcome, reason_statement, bearer (natural-person or role accountable for the decision), automated_flag, human_review_state, issued_at.
- `AppealCase`: an A6-routed contest. Fields: appeal_id, determination_id, subject_ref, filed_at, maintenance_state (full cited floor delivered pending appeal for floor-level claims per ELIG-I11), a6_contract_ref.
- `RecoveryAction`: an overpayment or error-recovery event. Fields: recovery_id, entitlement_ref (citation into the PROV `Entitlement`), cause (agency_error, good_faith, established_fraud), amount, above_floor_headroom_at_recovery, floor_touch boolean (SHALL be false to execute).
- `ProvisionalIdentity`: a reachability handle for a subject lacking documentary identity. Fields: prov_id, essential_care_ref (HACC-I11), assurance_level, upgrade_path, single_delivery_check_ref (the ELIG-I19 cross-frame check state).

Cited objects (owned elsewhere, referenced by ID, fields NOT restated here):

- `Benefit` and `Entitlement`: PROV-owned. ELIG cites benefit_id, benefit_class {floor_benefit, top_up, contributory}, floor_ref and the single reconciled `Entitlement` status enum {provisional, active, suspended, closed}; ELIG issues `Determination`s that open and change entitlements but does not restate their delivery-side fields or floor guarantees.
- `care_service`: CARE-owned (with its O3 worker-wage-floor binding). ELIG cites the care_service and its determination linkage; it does not re-specify the service object.

All ELIG decision objects are held in append-only R1/R2 registers (A12); correction is by new event, never by silent overwrite.

### Invariants

ELIG-I1 (determination is a governed decidable act). Every floor-linked `Benefit` (benefit_class = floor_benefit) SHALL have a published, versioned `EligibilityPredicate` whose output is one of {owed, not_owed, indeterminate} and which is computable from named input facts. Purpose: eligibility is a decidable question with a stated procedure, not administrator discretion. Predicate: no `Determination` may be issued against an unpublished or unversioned predicate; a benefit with no published predicate FAILS-CLOSED to proactive delivery of the floor, not to denial. Enforcement hook: an issued determination citing a missing predicate_version is void and re-opens the entitlement.

ELIG-I2 (both-flanks calibration of the predicate). An `EligibilityPredicate` SHALL be published with its two error rates named and bounded: the false-deny rate (genuine claimant classified not_owed) and the false-grant rate (ineligible classified owed). Purpose: protect the claimant against wrongful denial AND the honest administrator and solvent non-claimant against leakage. Predicate: neither error rate may be traded to zero at the cost of driving the other above its published bound; a predicate revision that lowers false-grant by raising false-deny above bound is void. Enforcement hook: the published rates are the standard against which A6 measures a systemic-denial finding.

ELIG-I3 (state bears the burden of ineligibility). The STATE, not the claimant, SHALL bear the burden of establishing that a floor benefit is not owed. Purpose: a floor is presumed owed to a natural person and withdrawn only on shown ground. Predicate: where input facts are `indeterminate`, the outcome resolves toward provisional delivery, not denial; the claimant is never required to prove a negative (that they do not hold disqualifying means or status). Enforcement hook: a determination whose reason_statement rests on the claimant's failure to disprove ineligibility is void under ELIG-I1.

ELIG-I4 (proactive delivery of the visibly-owed floor). Where the state already holds, in its registers or at S5 cohort grain resolved to the person by a lawful gate, facts sufficient to show a floor benefit is owed, it SHALL deliver that floor proactively through COMP-I17 without requiring a claim. Purpose: close the take-up gap; a benefit that is owed and unclaimed because the person did not know to ask is declared-not-delivered. Predicate: for each floor `Benefit` the administrator SHALL maintain a coverage figure (delivered / eligible-per-register); a persistent gap is a monitored breach. Enforcement hook: the coverage gap is booked as a V4 anti-value floor breach per axis, not netted against fiscal saving.

ELIG-I5 (administrative-burden-as-denial is barred, fail-closed protective). A floor SHALL NOT be gated behind paperwork, documentary demands, in-person appearances, digital-only channels or process friction that the intended population cannot reasonably navigate. Purpose: burden is a denial mechanism that disproportionately excludes the poorest and least-capable, exactly the floor's beneficiaries. Predicate: a requirement is barred where (a) it demands a document the claimant population provably cannot obtain, or (c) it measurably suppresses take-up below the ELIG-I4 coverage figure; and (b) until a navigability bound is published for a target cohort, any requirement beyond supplying a single fact the state does not already hold (per ELIG-I6) is PRESUMED unnavigable for that cohort and is barred as burden. The administrator bears the burden of showing a demand is navigable; the claimant does not bear the burden of showing it is not. Enforcement hook: a floor gated behind a barred requirement is declared-not-delivered and books as a floor breach; the requirement is struck on A6 finding, and until the navigability bound is published the fail-closed presumption governs.

ELIG-I6 (minimal and necessary demand). Any information demanded of a claimant SHALL be the minimum necessary to run the published predicate, and SHALL NOT be demanded where the state already holds it. Purpose: protect the claimant from redundant burden and surveillance-by-form, and protect the administrator from holding data it cannot justify. Predicate: each demanded field maps to a named predicate input the state does not already hold; unmapped or duplicative demands are barred. Enforcement hook: struck as burden under ELIG-I5.

ELIG-I7 (reasoned determination with a named bearer). Every `Determination` reducing, denying, suspending or closing a floor entitlement SHALL carry a written reason_statement in plain language and a named bearer (natural person or accountable role) answerable for it. Purpose: an unreasoned denial cannot be contested and hides error and malice; the honest administrator is also protected by a clear record of the ground relied on. Predicate: no adverse floor determination issues without reason_statement and bearer populated. Enforcement hook: a determination missing either is void and the entitlement is maintained.

ELIG-I8 (automated-decision guard). No adverse floor `Determination` (denial, reduction, suspension) SHALL be issued on a purely-automated basis without (a) a stated reason per ELIG-I7, (b) a human-reviewable bearer able to explain and reverse it, and (c) an available appeal per ELIG-I11. Purpose: prevent silent algorithmic exclusion from the floor. Predicate: automated_flag set AND human_review_state unavailable on an adverse floor outcome is a void state. Enforcement hook: the determination does not take effect until human_review_state is reachable.

ELIG-I9 (no opaque risk-score as sole ground). An opaque or non-auditable algorithmic risk-score SHALL NOT be the sole basis of a floor denial, reduction or fraud referral. Purpose: an unexplained score is not a reason and cannot be contested; both the claimant and the honest administrator need an inspectable ground. Predicate: any adverse floor determination or per-person investigation referral SHALL cite at least one named, auditable input fact independent of the risk-score; a determination whose reason_statement reduces to "the model flagged this subject" is void. Enforcement hook: void under ELIG-I7 and ELIG-I9; the score may inform triage but never decide the floor.

ELIG-I10 (indeterminate resolves to provisional delivery). Where a determination is `indeterminate`, the administrator SHALL open a provisional `Entitlement` and deliver the floor while the fact gap is resolved. Purpose: uncertainty SHALL NOT fall on the claimant as a delay-denial. Predicate: provisional status has a published resolution clock; on expiry it converts to active, not to closure, absent a positive not_owed finding. Enforcement hook: silent lapse of a provisional entitlement is a floor breach.

ELIG-I11 (appeal to A6 with the full floor maintained pending). Every adverse floor `Determination` SHALL be appealable to an A6 adjudicator, and for a floor-level claim the benefit SHALL continue to be delivered pending the appeal outcome per the full-floor-during-contest rule owned by PROV-I19. Purpose: no person is dropped below the floor while contesting a possibly-wrong denial; the suspensive-contest principle (A18-I34) is honored on the delivery side. Predicate: filing an `AppealCase` on a floor entitlement sets maintenance_state = maintained (delivering the FULL cited floor magnitude per PROV-I19, not merely an essential minimum) until A6 disposes; only the disputed above-floor amount waits on A18-I34. Enforcement hook: interruption or partial delivery of a floor benefit during a live floor-level appeal is void and self-reversing. Counter-flank: maintenance pending appeal does not bar later ELIG-I13 recovery of genuinely-not-owed amounts from above-floor means if the appeal fails.

ELIG-I12 (error and overpayment recovery shall not breach the floor). No `RecoveryAction` SHALL push or hold a person below the capability floor. Purpose: clawback that creates the very destitution the floor exists to prevent is self-defeating harm. Predicate: a recovery executes only against above-floor headroom booked at the time of recovery (above_floor_headroom_at_recovery > 0 and floor_touch = false); any amount that would touch the floor is not extracted, even where a debt stands under ELIG-I13. Enforcement hook: a recovery breaching this books as a V4 floor breach and is reversed.

ELIG-I13 (fault-graded recovery, single owner of the overpayment fault-grading rule). Recovery SHALL be graded by cause; this invariant is the SINGLE OWNER of the overpayment fault-grading rule and PROV-I12 cites it. Purpose: allocate the cost of error to the party who caused it, protecting the good-faith recipient and preserving the administrator's claim against genuine fraud. Predicate: (a) an agency-error overpayment SHALL NOT be recovered from the recipient at all; (b) a good-faith overpayment (recipient neither caused nor could reasonably detect it) is recoverable ONLY from above-floor headroom per ELIG-I12, and any residue beyond current above-floor headroom is written off, not carried as a standing debt; (c) an established-fraud overpayment (an A6 finding of fault) is FULLY recoverable on the $ money rail as an enduring debt for the whole sum, pursued against all above-floor means and not written down to current headroom, still subject to the ELIG-I12 floor (no single extraction pushes the person below the capability floor) and to the inviolable dignity floor, with any coercive enforcement rung routed through A18-I4. Enforcement hook: a recovery mis-classified as good-faith or fraud without the required A6 finding is void; cause is set by evidence, not by administrative convenience. Rail discipline: a $ recovery books on the money rail; any residual per-axis harm books V4 per axis and is never netted across axes or against the $ rail.

ELIG-I14 (fraud detection at cohort grain). Systemic fraud and error detection SHALL run at S5 cohort grain and SHALL NOT constitute standing per-person surveillance of claimants. Purpose: a claimant does not forfeit privacy by being poor; the administrator retains a real detection capability without a claimant panopticon. Predicate: population-level anomaly detection operates on S5/S6 cohort and predicate outputs; it never resolves a named person by default. Enforcement hook: a claimant-monitoring pipeline operating at standing person grain is a security breach under S1/S5.

ELIG-I15 (per-person investigation needs individualized suspicion and an A6 contract). Escalation from cohort-grain detection to per-person investigation of a specific claimant SHALL require individualized, stated suspicion and an A6 judicial access contract; a cohort flag alone is not suspicion. Purpose: protect the individual claimant from being singled out on a statistical shadow, while giving the honest investigator a lawful, auditable path against a genuine bad actor. Predicate: person-grain claimant investigation opens only on an A6 contract citing named ground independent of a bare risk-score (ELIG-I9); the contract is S4-audited, scoped and time-bound. Enforcement hook: person-grain claimant data pulled without the contract is unlawful and inadmissible; any health-record touch routes through the B10/HACC gates, never through a new ELIG read power. Counter-flank: individualized suspicion, once shown, does support a full lawful investigation, disciplined recovery and A18-I4 referral.

ELIG-I16 (identity and reachability for the undocumented claimant). Absence of documentary identity SHALL NOT bar access to the essential-care floor or to registration; the administrator SHALL open a `ProvisionalIdentity` and deliver reachable essential care. Purpose: the stateless, undocumented and unregistered person is exactly who a floor must reach; documentary-status demands must not become a burden-denial for them. Predicate: essential care and registration are delivered under HACC-I11; a provisional identity carries a published upgrade path to full registration and is never a lever to withdraw already-delivered care. Enforcement hook: R4-absence (unreachable essential care) is a monitored breach per HACC-I11; a benefit conditioned on prior documentary status the person cannot obtain is barred under ELIG-I5.

ELIG-I17 (recovery and investigation never touch the dignity floor). No `RecoveryAction`, fraud investigation or eligibility measure SHALL reach the inviolable dignity and bodily-integrity floor, and no eligibility measure imposes coercion outside A18-I4. Purpose: administration of a benefit is not a licence for coercion; the floor's own machinery SHALL NOT become an enforcement backdoor. Predicate: any coercive step arising from an eligibility or recovery matter (compelled disclosure beyond the predicate, capability-restriction, standing-loss) routes through A18-I4 with its confidence-class and floor-breach-severity inputs; the dignity and bodily-integrity floor sits OUTSIDE the calculus and is never assessable, and no forced sampling or forced bodily step is ever an eligibility or recovery measure. Enforcement hook: a coercive eligibility measure taken without an A18-I4 route is void; ELIG opens no standing coercive power of its own.

ELIG-I18 (no floor-waiver, no autonomous-agent escape). An eligibility term, consent form or service contract SHALL NOT waive a claimant's floor, and no automated or delegated administration path may leave a floor breach or criminal-class harm without a natural-person bearer. Purpose: prevent the floor being contracted away or laundered through an agent. Predicate: a clause purporting to waive floor entitlement is void per O5 floor-waiver-void; every adverse floor determination, automated or delegated, resolves to a named bearer per ELIG-I7 and ELIG-I8, with acting-in-concert delegation caught by O1. Enforcement hook: an unbeared adverse floor outcome is void; the accountable role holder inherits bearer status.

ELIG-I19 (cross-frame single-delivery guard for the own-account floor). A floor_benefit delivered on the own-account basis SHALL carry a privacy-preserving cross-frame single-delivery check: an S6 zero-knowledge "already-delivered-elsewhere" predicate, NOT a person-linkable surveillance join, that detects whether the same natural person's floor is already being delivered before a new `ProvisionalIdentity` or a second frame draws it. Purpose: hold the honest administrator's both-flanks position, a genuine person is fixed a single whole floor while a duplicate draw against the reserve is caught, without opening a claimant-linkage surveillance capability. Predicate: the check runs at S6 zero-knowledge grain and returns {single, duplicate, indeterminate}; where indeterminate, delivery proceeds (fail toward delivery) and reconciliation runs afterward; a confirmed duplicate is a HINT-I1 fraud with restitution against the bearer on the $ money rail, while the genuine person's single floor is fixed whole and never disturbed by the recovery. Enforcement hook: a duplicate confirmed after delivery books restitution on the $ rail against the bearer (V4 residual per axis where applicable), never against the genuine person's floor; a delivery blocked on a merely-indeterminate check is a floor breach. The check opens no new person-grain read power and no new floor-delivery mechanism: it rides the COMP-I17 own-account reserve and the S6 zero-knowledge predicate.

### Protocols

- Proactive-delivery sweep. On each `Benefit`'s review_cadence, the administrator runs the published predicate over register facts and S5 cohorts, computes the ELIG-I4 coverage figure, opens entitlements for the visibly-owed, and books any gap as a monitored V4 breach. Claims are an entry point, never the only one.
- Claim intake. A claim collects only ELIG-I6 minimal facts, runs the ELIG-I1 predicate, and issues a `Determination` with reason and bearer (ELIG-I7). `indeterminate` opens a provisional entitlement (ELIG-I10). Adverse automated outcomes hold until human-reviewable (ELIG-I8). Any demand beyond a single held-elsewhere fact is presumed unnavigable and barred until a cohort navigability bound is published (ELIG-I5(b)).
- Own-account draw. Before a new `ProvisionalIdentity` or a second frame draws the floor on the own-account basis, the ELIG-I19 S6 zero-knowledge single-delivery check runs; {single} or {indeterminate} proceeds to delivery, {duplicate} fixes the genuine single floor whole and opens a HINT-I1 restitution against the bearer on the $ rail.
- Appeal. Filing routes to A6, sets maintenance_state = maintained delivering the full cited floor per PROV-I19 for floor claims (ELIG-I11), and disposes by new register event; a successful appeal reverses and re-delivers, a failed one may proceed to ELIG-I13 recovery from above-floor means only.
- Recovery. Classify cause (ELIG-I13, the single owner of the fault-grading rule); agency-error is not recovered from the recipient; good-faith recovers only against above-floor headroom with residue written off (ELIG-I12/I13); established-fraud is fully recoverable on the $ rail as an enduring debt, still floor-bounded (ELIG-I12); coercive enforcement steps route through A18-I4 (ELIG-I17).
- Fraud pipeline. Cohort-grain anomaly detection (ELIG-I14) produces triage signals; escalation to a named claimant requires individualized suspicion and an A6 contract (ELIG-I15); a risk-score never decides alone (ELIG-I9).

### Lifecycle and edge cases

- Predicate revaluation. A predicate version change re-runs against open entitlements; a person owed under the old version is not retroactively closed without a fresh reasoned determination and appeal right. Honest revaluation (D2 discipline) is not a silent mass-denial.
- Provisional-to-active. Provisional entitlements convert on the resolution clock absent a positive not_owed finding (ELIG-I10); they never lapse to closure by timeout.
- Cross-frame provisional. A person provisional in one frame and registered in another is reconciled by the ELIG-I19 S6 zero-knowledge single-delivery check, which fixes a single whole floor and catches a duplicate draw without a surveillance join across frames; where the check is indeterminate the floor still delivers and reconciliation follows.
- Capacity and guardianship. Where CAP capacity is graded or a supported-decision arrangement exists, the determination is delivered to the supported subject, never silently to a substitute; guardianship-capture that diverts a benefit from the ward is caught (Persons-Life-Events-and-Property.md). No CAP process authorizes any bodily step or floor override.
- Death and closure. Closure of an entitlement is a register event with reason and bearer; an over-recovery against an estate still respects surviving dependants' floors.
- Emergency. A declared X3 communicable-disease emergency does not open a new claimant-surveillance power; any person-grain health touch remains bound to HACC-I10 notify-only, dual-controlled, hard-sunset, A6-ratified.

## EPI population health, surveillance and epidemiology (B14)

### Purpose

B14 is the population layer of the health system. It models health at the grain of cohorts, populations and commons: the state of a population's health, the detection and description of outbreaks, immunization coverage as a collective-protection good, and the epidemiological indicators computed over these. It exists so that the population is genuinely protected: real surveillance catches outbreaks and floor gaps early enough to act, and the collective good of herd immunity is measured honestly.

B14 owns nothing about any individual person's health. Personal health, including any individual immunization status, is subject-owned and lives in B10 (B10-I1 subject sovereignty), reachable only through the HACC gates. The access surface for any health data is HACC. The coercion decision for any measure is A18-I4. The floor magnitudes and delivery are COMP-I17 and Distributive-Justice-and-Capability-Floors.md. This layer CITES all of these and re-models none of them. B14 opens NO new person-grain read power, NO new coercion gate, and NO new floor-delivery mechanism.

Population health and herd immunity are booked as a commons collective-protection good in the value substrate, not as a V5 participation good; the theta participation condition NEVER conditions this good and never gates any floor delivered under it (COMP-I17). Both flanks are load-bearing throughout: the population SHALL be protected by surveillance that actually works AND no individual may be identified, tracked, listed, or coerced by the surveillance apparatus outside the bounded HACC gates.

### Object model

- `population_health`: a cohort-grain state object describing the health of a defined population or cohort over a period. Fields: cohort key (an S5 k-anonymous cohort descriptor, never a person set), period, indicator bundle, provenance chain, evidence pin. Holds NO person records and NO person identifiers.
- `outbreak`: a detected or hypothesized elevation in a syndrome or case rate above an expected baseline for a cohort. Fields: cohort key, agent or syndrome descriptor, baseline model, signal, detection method, uncertainty, status (candidate, confirmed, closed), and, when person-grain action is required, a reference to the governing HACC-I10 gate instance. The outbreak object does not contain cases as persons.
- `case_series` / `contact_set`: population-layer aggregates only. The underlying case and contact records are B10 subject-owned and are NEVER copied into B14. B14 holds counts, rates and gate references, never the person-grain records.
- `notifiable_disease`: an entry on the versioned, published list of conditions whose person-grain reporting is permitted, each entry naming the HACC-I10 gate class it rides and its sunset discipline.
- `immunization_coverage`: a cohort-grain indicator of the fraction of a cohort protected, plus a modeled collective-protection (herd-immunity) figure. Holds no roster of the immunized or the refusing, and no per-person immunization status.
- `epi_indicator`: any epidemiological figure (incidence, prevalence, R-effective, coverage, excess signal). Every instance is an INDICATOR, carrying pinned evidence, stated method, and stated uncertainty per D2.
- `zoonotic_env_signal`: a one-health signal at the D4 habitat/ecology seam (animal reservoirs, vectors, environmental exposure), cohort-grain and referenced to D4, not a new person-grain sensor.

### Invariants

**EPI-I1 (population layer, no person record).** B14 SHALL hold population-grain and cohort-grain objects only and SHALL NOT hold, mirror, or cache any person-grain health record, including any per-person immunization status; the owner of any personal health datum is always the person (B10-I1). Predicate: every B14 object resolves to a cohort key of size at least k (S5) or to a HACC-I10 gate reference, never to a person identifier. Enforcement: any B14 object that resolves to a natural person outside a live gate reference is void on write (S1 controller rejects) and is a concealment-class event if concealed. Purpose: keep the population layer structurally incapable of becoming a person registry or an immunization roster, while still describing the population.

**EPI-I2 (cohort-grain surveillance default).** Population-health surveillance SHALL operate at S5 cohort grain by default (HACC-I1 cohort-grain default), with k-anonymity enforced on every output; the state SHALL NOT hold a standing person-grain health read (B10-I1, B10-I2). Predicate: for every published or acted-on surveillance output, cohort size at least k and no linkage key permitting person resolution. Enforcement: S5 sensing rejects sub-k emission except via EPI-I10; S4 logs. Both flanks: the honest surveiller retains full cohort-grain sensing (protection is not weakened), and no person is exposed by default.

**EPI-I3 (outbreak object computed at cohort grain or distributed).** An `outbreak` SHALL be detected and described from data computed at cohort grain, or via distributed secondary use that never centralizes person records (HACC-I7 secondary use distributed-only). Predicate: the outbreak's signal derives from (a) cohort-grain counts, or (b) a distributed computation whose only egress is an aggregate. Enforcement: a detection pipeline that centralizes person-grain case data outside a gate is void and fails closed. Purpose: outbreaks are found without building a person-grain surveillance store.

**EPI-I4 (syndromic and case-based both cohort-resolved).** Both syndromic surveillance (symptom or indicator patterns) and case-based surveillance (confirmed diagnoses) SHALL resolve only to cohorts in B14; neither SHALL resolve a person identity outside the HACC-I10 gate. Predicate: no B14 surveillance query returns a person set. Enforcement: query planner denies person-resolving predicates absent a live gate; the S6 zero-knowledge predicate MAY answer threshold questions ("is cohort rate above baseline") without exposing members. **EPI-I5 (case and contact data is subject-owned; tracing only via the PHE gate).** Case data and contact data are B10 subject-owned; person-grain contact tracing SHALL operate ONLY through the HACC-I10 notify-only public-health-emergency path, bound to a declared X3 communicable-disease emergency, dual-controlled, per-organ quota, hard-sunset, loudest S4, with mandatory A6 post-hoc ratification. B14 SHALL NOT open any general person-grain read for tracing. Predicate: any person-grain contact notification carries a live, in-quota, un-sunset HACC-I10 gate reference; else it does not issue. Enforcement: fail-closed at the notify service; an out-of-gate notification is a criminal-class rights-violation (never priced). Both flanks: a genuine declared emergency gets a working notify-only tracing path, and outside it no person is ever contact-traced.

**EPI-I6 (notifiable-disease reporting rides the same gate, and the list is published).** Notifiable-disease reporting that resolves to a person SHALL ride the HACC-I10 gate; the notifiable list SHALL be a versioned, published register entry (A12, R1 append-only) naming each condition's gate class and sunset. Predicate: no condition is notifiable at person grain unless it is on the published list AND a matching gate is live. Enforcement: silent addition of a condition, or reporting a condition not on the published list, is void and concealment-class. Purpose: mandatory reporting cannot be quietly broadened into standing surveillance; the honest reporter has a clear, published mandate.

**EPI-I7 (gate discipline is cited, not re-created; fail-closed).** B14 SHALL cite the HACC-I10 gate controls (dual control, per-organ quota, hard-sunset, loudest S4, A6 ratification) and SHALL add no read power, quota, or path of its own. Predicate: every person-grain B14 action maps to exactly one HACC-I10 gate instance and adds nothing to it. Enforcement: absent a calibrated, published gate, B14 person-grain functions FAIL CLOSED (no read on an unpublished or lapsed gate). Purpose: one gate, one discipline, no shadow surveillance authority accreting in the population layer.

**EPI-I8 (indicator not truth).** Every `epi_indicator` SHALL be booked as an INDICATOR under the D2 evidence discipline: pinned evidence, stated method, stated uncertainty, and honest revaluation as evidence matures. Predicate: an indicator lacking a pinned evidence reference, a named method, or an uncertainty statement SHALL NOT be published or acted on. Enforcement: publication gate rejects unpinned figures; a superseded figure is corrected by a new append-only event (R1/R2), never silently overwritten. Purpose: no case count is mistaken for ground truth; the honest analyst and the affected public both see the same caveats.

**EPI-I9 (no coercion on a bare figure).** No case count, rate, coverage figure, or any single `epi_indicator` SHALL by itself be a basis for a coercive measure; any coercive public-health measure (quarantine, isolation, mandate, closure) is coercion and SHALL route through A18-I4 (confidence class, cross-frame reach, floor-breach severity, fail-closed until A10 calibrates). Predicate: a proposed measure references A18-I4 output, not an epi figure, as its coercion basis. Enforcement: a measure whose sole cited basis is an indicator is void; the dignity and bodily-integrity floor sits outside the calculus and no figure or emergency may touch it. Both flanks: a genuine emergency can still act, but only through A18-I4 with an A6 finding for any hard rung; the individual is never coerced on a number alone.

**EPI-I10 (sub-k floor sensing preserved, not pooled away).** For the smallest cohorts below the k threshold, a below-floor health signal SHALL surface via the B10-I7 noised-count or sealed monitor-only flag mechanism, and SHALL NOT be mean-pooled into a larger cohort that erases it. The noise budget and the k threshold are set by the B10-I7 and S5 sub-k discipline (the owner), which B14 consumes and does not re-create or restate. Predicate: for any cohort below k, the pipeline emits either the B10-I7 differentially-noised count or a B10-I7 sealed flag routed to the floor-monitor, never a plain suppression and never absorption into a super-cohort. Enforcement: an aggregation that makes a below-floor small-cohort signal disappear is a monitored breach and routes to COMP-I17 floor review. Purpose: the smallest and most vulnerable cohorts (where floor breaches concentrate) remain visible for floor delivery, without exposing their members.

**EPI-I11 (immunization coverage is a cohort commons indicator; individual status stays subject-owned).** `immunization_coverage` and herd immunity SHALL be modeled as a cohort-grain collective-protection (commons) good. The individual immunization decision and the individual immunization status are B10 subject-owned and reachable only through the HACC gates; neither B14 nor MEAS holds or measures individual immunization status at person grain. Predicate: coverage objects carry a cohort key and a modeled protection figure only, with no per-person immunization status and no reference to a person-grain immunization measure. Enforcement: any per-person immunization roster or per-person immunization-status read in B14 is void (EPI-I1); MEAS is referenced only for the coercive `immunization_requirement` measure (MEAS-I8), which is a measure over a person, not a store of that person's status. Purpose: the population's collective protection is measured and defended as a commons, while the individual choice and status stay subject-owned behind the gates.

**EPI-I12 (no registry of refusers, no targeting by coverage gap).** A coverage indicator SHALL NOT name, list, or target any individual by immunization status; a coverage gap SHALL NOT itself be a coercion basis (EPI-I9). Predicate: no B14 output enumerates non-immunized persons; no measure cites a coverage figure as its coercion basis. Enforcement: construction or use of a refuser list is criminal-class if it violates B10-I1, else void and concealment-class. Both flanks: the state may raise coverage through floor-delivered access (COMP-I17) and honest information, and no person is coerced or listed for a private health choice.

**EPI-I13 (zoonotic and environmental health as a D4 seam).** One-health surveillance (zoonotic reservoirs, vectors, environmental exposure) SHALL be booked at the D4 habitat/ecology floor seam and referenced to Missions-Science-and-Ecology.md, not implemented as a new person-grain sensor. Predicate: every `zoonotic_env_signal` carries a D4 reference and a cohort or habitat key, never a person key. Enforcement: a zoonotic pipeline that resolves persons routes to EPI-I5 (gate) or is void. Purpose: environmental and animal-origin health risks are surveilled through the ecology floor, closing the one-health gap without a new surveillance power over people.

**EPI-I14 (booking: commons value and residual externality, never netted, never levied).** Population health SHALL be booked as a commons collective-protection good in the value substrate (a commons value, NOT a V5 participation good; the theta participation condition never conditions it and never gates any floor delivered under it). Outbreak harm and floor gaps SHALL be booked as V4 anti-value per axis with teeth, never netted across axes and never netted against the $ money rail; the state SHALL price only the residual externality, never the price itself, and SHALL fund no provision by levy (MOS has no taxation). Predicate: each Ae entry is per-axis; a $ recovery books on the money rail and the residual per-axis harm books as V4; floor gaps route to COMP-I17 own-account delivery, not to a tax-funded transfer. Enforcement: a netted or cross-rail booking is void; a levy-funded provision is out of frame. Purpose: the accounting keeps harm honest and floors delivered, not redistributed by tax, and the commons good is never mistaken for a participation-conditioned entitlement.

**EPI-I15 (provenance pinned or suppressed).** Every population-health figure SHALL carry a provenance chain showing it derives only from consented, distributed-secondary-use (HACC-I7), or live-gate (HACC-I10) sources. Predicate: a figure whose provenance cannot be shown to be one of these three fails closed and is not published. Enforcement: publication gate rejects unprovenanced figures; provenance is append-only (R1). Purpose: a laundered person-grain leak cannot re-enter the system disguised as an aggregate; the honest analyst's clean figures pass freely.

**EPI-I16 (re-identification prohibition).** Population outputs SHALL NOT be constructible, alone or in combination, to re-identify an individual; the S5/S6 predicate discipline governs release. Predicate: a differencing or linkage test over released outputs cannot isolate a person. Enforcement: adversarial re-identification of a B14 output is a concealment-class event with a perpetual flip, and the offending release is retracted by new event. Both flanks: legitimate combined analysis remains available at cohort grain, and mosaic re-identification is caught and punished.

Enforcement: over-broad resolution or a lapsed sunset fails closed; wrongful X3 use triggers X3 compensation and A6 review. Both flanks: a genuine surge in a real emergency gets more capacity through the one gate's own loud, ratified, sunset-bound process, and the quota can never be quietly defeated by overflow.

- **Cohort-grain sensing.** All standing surveillance queries run against S5 cohort views. Threshold questions ("is cohort X above its outbreak baseline") SHOULD be answered by the S6 zero-knowledge predicate where possible, exposing no members (EPI-I2, EPI-I4).

- **Person-grain notify (tracing / notifiable reporting).** Runs only inside a HACC-I10 gate instance: dual control, per-organ quota, hard-sunset, loudest S4, A6 post-hoc ratification, all owned by HACC-I10. B14 supplies the population reason and consumes the gate; it adds no control of its own (EPI-I7). Fail-closed on any missing control. When legitimate notify demand exceeds the per-organ quota, capacity is raised only through the EPI-I19 fresh A6-ratified quota adjustment, never by silent overflow; the excess is held at cohort grain until the adjustment ratifies.

Hard rungs require an A6 finding; the measure carries A18-I34 suspensive contest. The dignity and bodily-integrity floor is untouchable.
- **Floor-gap surfacing.** Below-floor small-cohort signals surface via the B10-I7 noised count or sealed monitor-only flag (EPI-I10) and route to COMP-I17 own-account delivery, never to a coercive or means-tested path.

### Lifecycle and edge cases

- **Gate lapse mid-outbreak.** When a HACC-I10 gate sunsets while an outbreak is active, all person-grain functions fail closed instantly; the outbreak reverts to cohort-grain management until a fresh gate is calibrated and A6-ratified. No grace-period person read.
- **Notify surge mid-emergency.** When in-emergency notify demand exceeds the per-organ quota, the excess is held and managed at cohort grain and the organ requests a fresh A6-ratified, loudest-S4, hard-sunset quota adjustment through HACC-I10 (EPI-I19); no notification issues above quota until the adjustment ratifies.
- **Take-up gap in immunization.** Low coverage is a floor-access signal, not a compliance failure of individuals: it routes to COMP-I17 access delivery (a declared-but-not-taken benefit is a floor breach, not the person's fault), never to EPI-I12 targeting.
- **Small stigmatized cohort.** A below-k cohort with a stigmatizing condition surfaces only as a B10-I7 sealed monitor-only flag to the floor-monitor (EPI-I10), never as a released figure, avoiding both erasure and exposure.
- **Revaluation.** When an indicator is revised (better evidence, corrected method), the old figure is superseded by an append-only correction event (EPI-I8); prior measures resting on the old figure are re-examined under A18-I4 and A18-I34.
- **Cross-border / zoonotic spillover.** A D4 zoonotic signal crossing into human cohorts escalates through EPI-I13 to cohort-grain human surveillance, and to person grain only via EPI-I5.
- **Adversarial re-identification attempt.** Detected mosaic re-identification retracts the release and triggers the EPI-I16 concealment flip; the underlying cohort analysis is re-issued at safe grain.

## MEAS public-health measures and the coercion threshold (B14)

### Purpose

This section models the public-health MEASURE: the class of state acts that constrain a natural person's or organization's conduct in the name of collective protection (quarantine, isolation, immunization requirement, movement or gathering restriction, premises closure, mandatory screening). Population health and herd immunity are a commons good; the state may act to protect that commons. But every such act that constrains a person is coercion, and coercion in MOS has exactly one gate. This layer therefore does two things and only two things. It gives the measure a typed, registered, contestable object with a proportionality, time-box, sunset, non-discrimination and compensation discipline. And it binds every coercive edge of that object to the single coercion threshold A18-I4 and to the existing X3 emergency and HACC access surface, opening no new coercion gate, no new person-grain health read power, and no new floor-delivery mechanism. The state has no standing public-health police power. This layer holds no per-person immunization status and measures no health attribute at person grain: individual immunization status is B10 subject-owned (EPI-I11) and is referenced here only for the coercive `immunization_requirement` measure. Both flanks are load-bearing throughout: the public SHALL be protected from a genuine contagion by real, proportionate, sunset-bound measures, AND no person SHALL be subjected to a disproportionate, indefinite, discriminatory, or bodily-inviolable-breaching measure.

### Object model

A `PublicHealthMeasure` is an append-only record (A12-mandated register, R1/R2 holder-of-record, correction-by-new-event) with at least the following fields.

- `measure_id`, `version`, `issuing_organ`, `issue_event`.
- `measure_type` from the canonical closed set: `quarantine` (constraint on a person exposed but not confirmed), `isolation` (constraint on a person confirmed or presumed infectious), `immunization_requirement`, `movement_restriction`, `gathering_restriction`, `premises_closure`, `mandatory_screening`. No open-ended residual type; a new type is a versioned amendment to this set, not an administrator's improvisation.
- `hazard_ref`: the X3 communicable-disease hazard or the D4 environmental-health hazard the measure answers.
- `evidence_pin`: the pinned D2 epidemiological indicator(s) grounding the measure, with confidence class.
- `scope`: the cohort or setting constrained, expressed at S5 cohort grain by default. This layer holds no per-person immunization status or other person-grain health attribute in the record; person-grain resolution stays gated by MEAS-I11.
- `coercion_class`: `advisory` (non-binding), or `coercive` (binding, routes through A18-I4). An `immunization_requirement` that conditions access on immunization status is `coercive` (MEAS-I8).
- `rung`: for a coercive measure, the enforcement-ladder rung, which SHALL be whatever A18-I4 returns for the individuated confidence_class of the specific person (MEAS-I3), never a locally free-set value.
- `least_restrictive_justification`: the record of why no less-restrictive option is effective.
- `time_box`: issue instant, hard expiry instant, and `sunset_default = expire`.
- `compensation_ref`: the COMP-I17 delivery binding covering any person driven below the capability floor by the measure (MEAS-I13) and floor-level business loss (MEAS-I14) for the duration.
- `contest_state`: the A18-I34 suspensive-contest status.
- `audit_ref`: the S4 audit channel; loudest S4 when the measure is X3-emergency-borne.

An `advisory` measure (guidance, recommendation, published risk signal) is not coercion and does not route through A18-I4; it also may not masquerade: an advisory that is enforced in fact is a coercive measure mis-booked, caught by MEAS-I2.

### Invariants

**MEAS-I1 (typed registered measure).** Every public-health measure SHALL be a `PublicHealthMeasure` record in the A12-mandated register before it binds any person, carrying a `measure_type` from the canonical set, an `evidence_pin`, a `scope`, and a `time_box`. Purpose: no unwritten, unregistered, or retroactive measure. Predicate: a constraint enforced against a person with no prior registered `measure_id` is void and is itself an A18 harm by the enforcing organ. Both flanks: the public gets a fast, auditable record of what protects them; the constrained person gets a named, dated, contestable act rather than a discretionary order.

**MEAS-I2 (single coercion gate, no police power).** Any measure with `coercion_class = coercive` (quarantine, isolation, immunization requirement, movement or gathering restriction, premises closure, mandatory screening) SHALL route through A18-I4 and SHALL carry an A18-I4-derived `rung`. Purpose: the state has NO standing public-health police power that bypasses the coercion threshold. Predicate: a coercive measure whose `rung` is not the output of the published A18-I4 function is void; because A18-I4 is fail-closed until A10 calibrates, no hard rung SHALL issue on an unpublished or uncalibrated function. Both flanks: a genuine outbreak is met by lawful graded coercion up the one canonical ladder (annotation, priced-debit, capability-restriction, standing-loss, custodial-restriction); no organ invents a parallel health-police ladder that skips the finding and the calculus.

**MEAS-I3 (the rung is A18-I4's output, not a second locus).** A coercive measure's `rung` SHALL be whatever A18-I4 returns for the individuated confidence_class of the specific person; MEAS opens no second locus for setting a rung. Cohort or statistical membership alone feeds a low confidence_class per A18-I4, fail-closed until A10 calibrates. Purpose: the rung has one owner and one calculus; population-level probability is an A18-I4 input, not an independent basis for coercion. Predicate (proportionality as an A18-I4 input, not a second gate): absent an individuated confirmed-infectious basis (a positive screen or an A6 finding), a `quarantine` of an exposed-but-unconfirmed person SHALL NOT exceed a movement or contact restriction, and custodial confinement (`isolation`) requires the individuated confirmed-infectious basis that raises confidence_class enough for the custodial rung. Both flanks: the public is still protected because cohort evidence fully grounds cohort-scoped measures (setting closures, gathering limits) and the lower rungs A18-I4 returns for a low confidence_class; the individual is not detained or stripped on a base rate alone.

**MEAS-I4 (least-restrictive effective option).** A coercive measure SHALL be the least-restrictive option that is effective against the pinned hazard, and SHALL record `least_restrictive_justification`. Purpose: proportionality is decidable, not rhetorical. Predicate: if a less-restrictive measure of the same or greater expected effectiveness exists on the record, the more-restrictive measure fails and SHALL be replaced. Ordering hint: advisory before conditional before coercive; setting-scoped before person-scoped; movement-limited before confined. Both flanks: the public keeps the effective floor of protection (least-restrictive is not least-effective); the constrained person carries no burden beyond what the hazard requires.

**MEAS-I5 (time-box and affirmative sunset).** Every coercive measure SHALL carry a hard `time_box` with `sunset_default = expire`, and SHALL lapse automatically at expiry unless an issuing organ files affirmative re-justification against a fresh `evidence_pin` before expiry. Purpose: no indefinite measure by inertia; extension is an act, not a non-act. Predicate: at `expiry`, absent a filed and registered re-justification, `contest_state` and enforcement both terminate; an enforcement action after lapse is an A18 harm. Both flanks: a persisting hazard is met by an explicit, evidenced extension; a receded hazard cannot leave a stale measure standing because someone forgot to lift it.

**MEAS-I6 (individual suspensive contest defers to A18-I34).** Every coercive measure SHALL be individually contestable under A18-I34, and the contest's suspensive effect SHALL be exactly what A18-I34 provides; MEAS adds no local exception. Purpose: due process reaches the person through the one owning gate, not a bespoke health-law carve-out. Any confirmed-infectious continuation-pending-review is a feature of A18-I34/A6, not created here; where A18-I34 lacks such a feature, MEAS raises it as an interface request to Offense-and-Enforcement rather than minting a carve-out. Predicate: until A6/A10 pin a maximum review interval, no continuation carve-out is available and the contest is fully suspensive; an `isolation` or any custodial measure continued over a filed A18-I34 contest SHALL lapse to the least-restrictive alternative unless an A6 review is actually held within the published default maximum interval, fail-closed. Both flanks: the public is protected because a genuine A18-I34/A6 continuation feature (if it exists in that owning layer) still governs a real isolation; the wrongly-constrained person is not held past the published review interval on an unreviewed hold.

**MEAS-I7 (inviolable bodily-integrity floor).** The dignity and bodily-integrity floor is the inviolable floor of A18 (cite the A18 non-priceable criminal class and the A18 inviolable dignity/bodily-integrity floor) and sits OUTSIDE the A18-I4 calculus. No measure, rung, or emergency SHALL effect a forced injection, forced medical treatment, forced sterilization, or ANY bodily penetration or sample (blood draw, swab, biometric sampling) taken over a competent person's refusal or otherwise without that person's own valid consent; such an act is a non-priceable criminal-class A18 harm attributed to the acting natural person(s), with NO exception and never priced or justified by outbreak severity. Purpose: the floor is untouchable and admits no public-health override. The single narrow exception is the B10-I11 imminent-harm presumed-consent rescue of a person who has NOT competently refused, owned by B10 as a rescue and not a floor override. Neither supported nor substituted decision-making, nor guardian consent, nor any CAP process EVER authorizes a bodily intervention over a person's refusal or as a floor override. MEAS-I7, CARE-I4 and HINT-I13 name the same single B10-I11 exception and cite A18 for the criminal class. Both flanks: the public-health objective is achievable without ever crossing the force-to-the-body line; the person's bodily autonomy is absolute save for the one B10-owned imminent-harm rescue of someone who has not refused.

**MEAS-I8 (immunization requirement: coercive, fail-closed, never a floor gate).** Conditioning access on immunization status is COERCIVE and SHALL route through A18-I4 with an A18-I4-derived `rung`, fail-closed so that no requirement issues until A10 calibrates, in addition to being proportionate (MEAS-I4), time-boxed (MEAS-I5), non-discriminatory (MEAS-I12) and individually contestable under A18-I34 (MEAS-I6). It SHALL NOT force administration and never crosses the MEAS-I7 line. The high-risk-congregate-setting predicate is fail-closed pending A10 calibration: no setting-conditioning issues absent a published, A10-calibrated predicate distinguishing a genuine congregate high-risk setting from a de-facto floor gate. Per-se bar: where a setting is the sole reachable channel to ANY capability-floor good (shelter, essential care, subsistence, registration, education-to-floor, livelihood or employment, or any setting a person cannot avoid without falling below a floor), an immunization condition on entry is VOID unless an equivalent unconditioned floor-access alternative is delivered. In a floor setting (school, essential care, hospital, transit, workplace) the collective-protection commons SHALL be defended by NON-exclusionary means: cohort measures, alternative floor-compliant access, and delivered voluntary uptake, never by denying the floor. Both flanks: the free-rider who would endanger others in a genuine high-risk setting is addressed by an A18-I4-graded, contestable condition on that setting once the predicate is calibrated, not by assault; the person who declines keeps their body, their floors, and every essential service intact, with a floor-access alternative wherever the setting is unavoidable.

**MEAS-I9 (commons delivered without touching the bodily floor).** The collective-protection commons SHALL be pursued only through cohort-scoped measures, setting conditions (MEAS-I8), and voluntary uptake, never through any act reaching the MEAS-I7 line. Purpose: name the commons good and bound its reach. Predicate: a measure justified by "herd immunity" or "commons protection" that would force administration or gate a floor fails MEAS-I7 or MEAS-I8 and is void regardless of the commons benefit claimed. Both flanks: the commons is a real good the state may protect vigorously at the setting and cohort grain; it is never a licence to override an individual body.

**MEAS-I10 (declared emergency is the X3 narrow-and-loud path).** A measure invoking urgency beyond the ordinary registered path SHALL be bound to a declared X3 communicable-disease emergency and SHALL inherit the HACC-I10 discipline in full: dual-controlled, per-organ quota, hard-sunset, loudest S4 audit, and mandatory A6 post-hoc ratification, with compensation when the emergency power is later found wrongly used. Purpose: emergency speed is bought only at the price of maximal visibility and after-the-fact review. Predicate: an emergency-borne measure without a live X3 declaration, without dual control, or without a scheduled A6 ratification is void; failed post-hoc ratification triggers unwind and compensation. Both flanks: a fast-moving genuine outbreak gets a real fast path; that path is loud, sunsetted, quota-bounded, reviewed, and compensated so it cannot ossify into standing police power.

**MEAS-I11 (no new person-grain health read power).** No measure SHALL open, imply, or rely on any person-grain read of a subject's health record beyond what B10 and HACC already grant. Surveillance grounding a measure SHALL be S5 cohort-grain by default; person-grain resolution SHALL come only via B10-I2 consent, an A6 judicial contract, or the HACC-I10 notify-only PHE gate. Purpose: measures do not become a backdoor sensing power. Predicate: a measure whose scope or enforcement requires reading a named person's health record without one of the three existing gates is void; secondary use stays HACC-I7 distributed-only. Both flanks: public-health authorities retain full cohort-grain situational awareness (S5, S6 predicate checks); the person's health record stays subject-owned (B10-I1) and is never re-opened by this layer.

**MEAS-I12 (non-discrimination).** A measure SHALL NOT target, or disparately burden, a protected group, national origin, migration status, or any cohort defined other than by actual exposure or actual hazard. Purpose: public health is not a vector for animus. Predicate: a measure whose scope correlates with a protected characteristic beyond what the pinned hazard's actual distribution requires fails; disparate targeting is an A18 harm attributed to the issuing organ, and applies with equal force to the stateless and undocumented, who retain HACC-I11 essential care and registration. Both flanks: where a hazard genuinely concentrates in a setting or cohort the measure MAY scope there on the evidence; it may never dress prejudice as epidemiology.

**MEAS-I13 (floor delivered under any coercive measure).** ANY natural person driven below the capability floor by ANY coercive measure_type (`quarantine`, `isolation`, `movement_restriction`, `gathering_restriction`, or any other) SHALL receive upfront COMP-I17 own-account delivery of the capability floor and essential needs for the measure's duration, with a live `compensation_ref` as a precondition of the measure's enforceability; this delivers the floor so compliance never means destitution, cited not re-modelled. The full-floor-during-contest rule of PROV-I19 applies while any dispute runs. Purpose: compliance SHALL NOT be self-impoverishment; a floor withdrawn to coerce compliance is itself a breach. Predicate: a coercive measure with no live `compensation_ref` is a floor breach and the measure's enforceability is suspended until delivery is in place; a take-up gap in that delivery is a breach, not compliance. Scope note: above-floor income loss from a lawful, non-wrongful measure is NOT compensated; the word "compensation" is reserved for the wrongful X3-use case per MEAS-I10, and floor-level business loss and the O3 worker floor are addressed under MEAS-I14. Both flanks: the public gains genuine compliance because staying home does not mean going hungry; the person is not forced to choose between the measure and subsistence, and the state does not socialize ordinary above-floor loss.

**MEAS-I14 (compensation for floor-level business loss).** A `premises_closure` or trading restriction that drives a lawful organization below its floor SHALL have that floor-level loss addressed via the existing COMP-I17 and O3 floors (the O3 delivered wage floor for its care and other workers is not waivable, O5 floor-waiver-void). Purpose: a closed business's workers and its floor solvency are not collateral. Predicate: a closure with no addressed floor-level loss path is incomplete and its extension under MEAS-I5 SHALL account for cumulative floor damage. Both flanks: the state may close a genuinely dangerous premises without hesitation; it may not use closure to floor-breach an operator or strand its workers, and above-floor commercial loss is not socialized (MOS has no taxation, no redistribution-by-levy), only the floor is protected.

**MEAS-I15 (mandatory screening compels presentation, never a forced sample).** A `mandatory_screening` measure is coercive (routes through MEAS-I2) and conditions or consequences a test; it SHALL NOT be executed by physically taking a sample from a non-consenting person. Purpose: screening compels presentation to a test, not a surrender of the body or of the record. Predicate: any forcible venipuncture, swab, or other bodily penetration to obtain a sample is a non-priceable criminal-class breach of the MEAS-I7 bodily floor, outside the A18-I4 calculus, attributed to the acting natural persons; non-compliance SHALL be met by measure lapse or a non-bodily A18-I4-derived rung, never by physically obtaining the sample. Any result SHALL deposit into the subject-owned record under B10, reporting to the state only at S5 cohort grain; a person-grain result reaches the state only through the three existing gates (MEAS-I11), and a positive screen MAY individuate confidence for a downstream isolation under MEAS-I3 through the subject's own record and gate, not a bulk feed. Both flanks: the state learns the cohort prevalence it needs to act and may lawfully consequence non-presentation up the ladder; the individual keeps their body and ownership of their own result.

**MEAS-I16 (escalation and capacity).** A measure applied to a person of reduced or absent CAP capacity SHALL follow the supported-not-substituted CAP process, SHALL NOT be a silent guardianship default, and SHALL NOT permit a guardian to authorize anything the person themselves could not be forced into, above all nothing reaching MEAS-I7. Purpose: the ward is protected both from the hazard and from guardianship-capture. Predicate: a guardian "consent" to forced treatment, forced injection, forced sampling, or sterilization of a ward is void (MEAS-I7 criminal class) and is caught by the Persons-Life-Events guardianship-capture guard. Both flanks: a person who genuinely cannot decide still receives protective care through supported decision-making; no one is force-treated or force-sampled by relabeling them incapable.

**MEAS-I17 (evidence discipline and honest revaluation).** A measure's `evidence_pin` SHALL treat every epidemiological figure as a D2 INDICATOR, not truth, and an honest revaluation of that indicator (the hazard receding, the figure revised down, the measure shown ineffective) SHALL trigger MEAS-I4 replacement or MEAS-I5 lapse. Purpose: measures track the evidence, not the sunk cost of having issued them. Predicate: an organ that continues a coercive measure after its pinned indicator has been revalued below the threshold that justified it, without re-justifying against fresh evidence, commits a floor-adjacent breach and the measure is contestable on that ground alone. Both flanks: a strengthening hazard justifies escalation on the record; a fading one cannot sustain a measure by momentum.

**MEAS-I18 (enforcement stays on the one ladder, bearer named).** Non-compliance with a lawful measure SHALL be handled only through the A18-I4 canonical enforcement ladder, never through extra-ladder punishment, collective penalty, or a bodily-floor breach; and deliberate acts that weaponize contagion (intentional mass infection, willful concealment endangering others) SHALL be attributed to a bearing natural person, with concealment routed through the A18 CONCEALMENT class with its perpetual flip and no autonomous-agent or organizational veil leaving such harm without a bearer (O1 acting-in-concert, single veil-piercing rule). Purpose: even the wrongdoer is coerced only up the one lawful ladder, and the worst harms always find a human bearer. Predicate: a penalty for non-compliance that is not an A18-I4-derived rung is void; a deliberate-infection harm with no named natural-person bearer is an unresolved criminal-class breach. Both flanks: the public is protected against both the ordinary non-complier (graded, proportionate rungs) and the deliberate spreader (criminal-class attribution); the non-complier is never met with force to the body or with punishment off the ladder.

**MEAS-I19 (competent advance refusal survives incapacity).** A competent refusal of a specified bodily intervention SHALL SURVIVE the refuser's later loss of capacity and SHALL bar the B10-I11 rescue carve-out, any CAP substitution, and any guardian consent as to that intervention. Purpose: the one narrow B10-I11 rescue reaches only a person who has never competently refused the act, not a person who refused it while competent and later lost capacity. Predicate: the B10-I11 presumed-consent rescue applies only where no competent refusal of that act is on record; a recorded competent refusal makes any subsequent administration of that intervention a MEAS-I7 criminal-class breach attributed to the acting natural persons, regardless of later incapacity, guardian appointment, or CAP process. Both flanks: a person who never decided is still reachable by the imminent-harm rescue; a person who decided while competent keeps that decision intact through incapacity, and no guardianship route reopens it.

### Protocols

- **Issue protocol.** Organ pins D2 evidence with confidence class; selects the least-restrictive effective `measure_type` (MEAS-I4); if coercive, takes `rung` from A18-I4 for the individuated confidence_class (fail-closed if uncalibrated, MEAS-I3); sets `time_box` with `sunset_default = expire`; binds `compensation_ref` via COMP-I17 (MEAS-I13/-I14) BEFORE the measure binds any person; registers the record (MEAS-I1); opens the S4 audit channel (loudest if X3-borne).
- **Emergency issue protocol.** Requires a live X3 declaration; dual control; per-organ quota check; hard-sunset; loudest S4; schedules the mandatory A6 post-hoc ratification (MEAS-I10). On failed ratification: unwind, correction-by-new-event, compensation.
- **Contest protocol.** Person or organization files A18-I34; suspensive effect is exactly what A18-I34 provides (MEAS-I6); a custodial measure continued over the contest lapses to the least-restrictive alternative unless an A6 review is held within the published default maximum interval, fail-closed.
- **Sunset/extend protocol.** At `expiry`: default lapse; extension only on a filed, registered re-justification against fresh `evidence_pin` (MEAS-I5, MEAS-I17). Lapse terminates enforcement and compensation-duration together.
- **Screening result protocol.** Presentation is compelled, a sample is never forced (MEAS-I15); result to subject-owned B10 record; state reporting at S5 cohort grain; person-grain downstream use only via B10-I2 / A6 / HACC-I10 (MEAS-I11, MEAS-I15).

### Lifecycle and edge cases

- **Advisory hardening into enforcement.** An advisory in fact enforced is re-classed coercive and SHALL satisfy MEAS-I2 retroactively or is an A18 harm (MEAS-I1).
- **Measure outliving its hazard.** Handled by MEAS-I5 auto-lapse plus MEAS-I17 revaluation; no organ can keep a measure alive by non-action.
- **Cross-frame reach.** A measure whose scope reaches across value frames feeds `cross_frame_reach` into A18-I4; this raises the calculus's scrutiny, it does not create a new gate.
- **Stateless / undocumented person under a measure.** Retains HACC-I11 essential care and registration and MEAS-I13 floor delivery; a measure that strands them is a monitored R4-absence breach and a MEAS-I12 violation.
- **Free-rider in a genuinely high-risk congregate setting.** Addressed only by MEAS-I8 setting-conditioning once its predicate is A10-calibrated (fail-closed until then), never by force (MEAS-I7).
- **Floor setting reached only through one channel.** An immunization condition on entry is void unless an equivalent unconditioned floor-access alternative is delivered; the commons is defended by non-exclusionary means (MEAS-I8).
- **Advance refusal then incapacity.** A competent refusal on record bars the B10-I11 rescue, CAP substitution, and guardian consent for that intervention (MEAS-I19); the rescue reaches only a person with no such refusal on record.
- **Guardianship route to force.** Blocked by MEAS-I16 and MEAS-I19; guardian consent cannot cross the MEAS-I7 line.
- **Surge past the HACC-I10 quota.** When legitimate person-grain notify demand exceeds the HACC-I10 quota during a live X3 emergency, the only lawful response is a fresh A6-ratified, loudest-S4, hard-sunset quota adjustment owned by HACC-I10, never a silent overflow; excess pending adjustment is managed at S5 cohort grain (consistent with the EPI-side surge rule).
- **Business above-floor loss.** Not compensated (no levy, no redistribution); only floor-level loss and O3 worker floors are protected (MEAS-I14). Above-floor income loss to a person from a lawful, non-wrongful measure is likewise not compensated (MEAS-I13).

## HINT social-provision and public-health integrity and capture surface

### Purpose

HINT is the integrity and anti-capture surface for the B13 social-provision administration layer and the B14 public-health layer. It does not deliver floors (COMP-I17 delivers unconditionally upfront on the person's own-account basis), set floor magnitudes (Distributive-Justice-and-Capability-Floors.md owns them and the theta participation condition), open any person-grain health read power (HACC-I10 is the only public-health-emergency read path and HACC-I11 the essential-care floor), or invent a coercion gate (A18-I4 is the single threshold). HINT enumerates the ways provision and public health can be corrupted, defrauded, weaponized, or captured, and binds each attack to a closing invariant. Where a rule is owned elsewhere HINT CITES the owning invariant by ID and does not re-mint it; HINT reserves its own IDs for genuinely new anti-capture machinery (the false-positive-hold and restitution split, purpose-binding, and function-creep detection). Every closing invariant protects both flanks: the claimant, patient, ward, and public on one side, and the honest administrator, the genuine emergency, and the solvent non-claimant on the other. Every anti-value booking here is per axis on the V4 rail and is never netted across axes and never netted against the money ($) rail; restitution of a wrongly-moved subsidy is a separate $-rail correction with a named natural-person bearer.

### Object model

- `provision_integrity_case`: an opened matter over a benefit, entitlement, delivery, or care-service object, carrying a `detection_signal`, an `accused_bearer` (always a natural person once a bearer attaches), a `flank` marker (over-claim side or over-suspicion side), and an A18 class annotation.
- `false_positive_hold`: a SUSPENSION token placed on a facially fraudulent draw pending a fast re-test, distinct from a `benefit_termination` (which cuts delivery). A hold pauses only the contested increment, never the household's other floors.
- `restitution_ledger_entry`: a $-rail correction that recovers a wrongly-drawn COMP-I17 subsidy from a natural-person bearer; it is booked on the money rail and carries no V4 charge by itself.
- `purpose_binding`: the declared, enumerated purpose stamped on every benefit-administration or public-health sensing artifact, against which any downstream read is checked.
- `sunset_token`: the hard expiry and destruction commitment on emergency or contact-trace data, inherited from the HACC-I10 hard-sunset and the S5 cohort floor.
- `phe_declaration`: a declared X3 communicable-disease public-health emergency, carrying its independent-evidence basis, its per-organ quota, its loudest-S4 audit handle, its auto-sunset horizon, and, for each re-declaration, an independently collected and independently sunset data scope with no carry-over of prior person-linked artifacts.
- `robo_denial_finding`: a systemic floor-breach record against a deploying organ where an automated eligibility system denied floors to a cohort.

### Invariants

- **HINT-I1** (benefit fraud, over-claim edge): A draw of a COMP-I17 subsidy by a person or organization not entitled to it is an A18 matter with two separated bookings: the wrongly-drawn amount is recovered as a `restitution_ledger_entry` on the $ rail from a named natural-person bearer, and the deception itself books `benefit_fraud` anti-value on its V4 axis, never netted against the recovered money. Detection signal: an entitlement predicate that fails on re-evaluation against the person's own-account record. Purpose: fraud is made unprofitable without letting the money recovery stand in for, or launder away, the harm charge.

- **HINT-I2** (fraud coercion-rung discipline): A benefit-fraud finding SHALL route its coercive rung, if any, through A18-I4; restitution and annotation are the default response, and no capability-restriction or standing-loss rung attaches without an A6 finding. Purpose: the solvent debtor who repays is not escalated as if a criminal, and honest overpayment recovered without deception carries no V4 charge at all.

- **HINT-I3** (false-fraud accusation edge): A knowingly-false fraud accusation, or a reckless one advanced without the entitlement predicate actually failing, books `false_accusation` anti-value on its V4 axis against the accuser as a natural-person bearer. Detection signal: an accusation whose asserted predicate-failure is not reproducible on the record. Purpose: the accusation power cannot be turned into a costless weapon against a genuine claimant, and the accuser bears the harm they manufactured.

- **HINT-I4** (suspend, do not cut, mid-dispute): A facially fraudulent draw SHALL be met with a `false_positive_hold` on the contested increment plus a fast re-test, NOT a `benefit_termination` of the household's floors. The non-contested floors continue delivering under COMP-I17 for the duration. A cut that precedes the re-test is itself a floor breach booked against the administering organ. Purpose: a real household is never starved on suspicion, while the genuine-fraud increment is frozen so the fraudster does not keep drawing during the dispute.

- **HINT-I5** (algorithmic false-positive parity): An automated or scored fraud flag receives the identical HINT-I4 hold-and-re-test treatment as a human accusation, and the re-test SHALL be a substantive re-evaluation of the person's own-account entitlement, not a re-run of the same model. A score alone SHALL NOT suspend, and SHALL NEVER terminate. Purpose: model error does not acquire more cutting power than a human accuser, and the deploying organ cannot hide behind the score.

- **HINT-I6** (fast re-test as a right, and its abuse): The claimant holds a right to the fast re-test with a bounded horizon and a default-in-favor-of-continuation rule if the horizon lapses. Symmetrically, a claimant who games the re-test to prolong a draw already shown fraudulent on a completed re-test falls back under HINT-I1. Purpose: neither an administrator can run out the clock on a real household, nor a fraudster can weaponize the re-test as indefinite delay.

- **HINT-I7** (benefit-as-coercion prohibition): Cutting, withholding, downgrading, or conditioning a person's COMP-I17 floor to punish dissent, lawful behaviour, association, or group membership is a prohibited coercive use, mirroring the disconnection-as-coercion rule. It books `coercive_provision_withdrawal` anti-value on its V4 axis and, being a floor breach used as leverage, routes any rung through A18-I4. Detection signal: a delivery change whose stated or inferred basis is a behaviour, viewpoint, or protected trait rather than an entitlement-predicate change. Purpose: floors are computed and routed, never discretionary largesse withdrawable to coerce; the honest administrator correcting a true entitlement change is untouched because the basis there is the predicate, not the person's conduct.

- **HINT-I8** (conditionality boundary, theta out of scope for the floor): Theta, a V5-frame, above-floor, contributory participation condition, NEVER conditions capability-floor delivery; the floor is delivered unconditionally upfront via COMP-I17 on the person's own-account basis, and any delivery at or below the cited floor magnitude attributed to non-participation is void and restored via COMP-I17, per PROV-I4/PROV-I5 (theta expressly out-of-scope for the floor) and Distributive-Justice-and-Capability-Floors.md (owner of the floor magnitudes and the theta condition).
