## 0. Privacy as a graduated, warrant-gated boundary (resolving D1)
The owner's insight: real states may lawfully intrude (surveillance, search, even intimate) **when
the threat level justifies it**, and the boundary moves by law/vote. Axiacracy adopts this — with a
decisive improvement: **the intruder is itself watched.**
- **Intrusion is graduated and warranted, proportional to threat:** cohort-aggregate (always
  allowed) → cohort de-anonymization (low bar) → individual (judicial authorization) → intimate/deep
  (highest bar, strong justification, time-limited, contestable).
- **Every intrusion is a logged value flow on the verifiable log** — auditable, contestable, and
  **abuse is a chargeable anti-value** against the intruding organ (unlike present states, where
  surveillance is secret).
- **The thresholds move by law/vote; the principle is Charter-level** (proportionality + warrant +
  logging + sunset + contestability). Emergency organs may *temporarily* raise them under strict
  due process (§8).

## 1. The organizing principle
Every organ of the state is a **value transformer whose job is to ensure value flows** — and is
therefore **measured by the same value-flow accounting it applies to everyone.** Its **critic and
validator is the citizen who receives the value** (or who validates its production). Concretely:
an organ's output is a value flow whose **Realized value is attested by the recipient citizens**;
the **claimed-vs-attested gap is the organ's performance/leakage**; and this attestation drives the
organ's standing, autonomy, funding, and continuation. **The state grades itself by its own
ledger.**

### 1.1 Human or AI — the position goes to whoever delivers more attested value
Every role in **every branch** — a party, a minister, a municipal executive, a judge, even a
**citizen** — may be held by a **human or an AI agent** (MOS exists partly to explore this). The
choice is **not ideological:** because a position's KPI is **citizen-attested value delivered**, the
role goes to whoever — human or AI — delivers more of it. Since attestation captures **received
quality, not just throughput**, **humans may win exactly where quality / judgment / trust /
legitimacy outweigh speed and scale**, while AI wins where tireless speed, scale, and consistency
dominate. A few roles are **constitutionally human-reserved** for legitimacy/accountability (apex
constitutional adjudication; constituent power; and — per D4 — the human liability chain for
governance). Whether a **citizen** may be an AI agent is itself a constitutional choice of each
polity (and a core MOS experiment).

## 2. The three branches (+ municipalities)
| Branch | Who | What it does | How citizens drive it |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Legislative** | the **demos** (weight-voting + parties + floor-setting) + a deliberative body for discrete laws | sets *values* (weights `w(t)`), *floors*, and the specific coercion-ladder **thresholds** (as versioned law) | vote weights; set floors; citizen initiative; ratify via sortition juries |
| **Executive** | the **ministries** (Sub-Agents), each owning an axis/domain, + **municipalities** (territorial) | sense → diagnose imbalance → generate strategic demand → graduated response; allocate financing | attest value received; announce needs; recall low-standing organs |
| **Judicial** | courts + constitutional review + the **Equity/Ombudsman** guardian | adjudicate contested assessments; police the inter/intra-frame boundary and **warrants**; constitutional review | appeal any assessment/coercion; challenge a warrant |

**Subsidiarity:** value is balanced at the lowest competent level — **municipalities** hold local
corridors/floors within national ones and are attested by their local citizens; only cross-region
imbalance escalates to national ministries.

## 3. Ministries in detail (the executive)
Each ministry is a permanent **Position** filled by validated, hot-swappable AI-agent services with
**PositionMemory**, backed by a named human liability chain for *governance* (not per-output, per
D4). Each:
1. **owns a value dimension** (Economic-Balance, Cognitive-Health, Demography, Social-Stability,
   Ecology, Science, Infrastructure, Education, Epistemic-Integrity, …) or a cross-cutting mandate
   (Equity guardian, Defense, Emergency);
2. **continuously senses** its dimension and **diagnoses imbalance** against the voted weights +
   floors;
3. **generates strategic demand** — proposes missions, incentives, and **financing requests** to
   correct imbalance;
4. **is scored by a KPI = attested value delivered to citizens, net of anti-value, keeping cohorts
   above floor, without breaking participation conditions θ.** Its **TrackRecord/standing is built
   from citizen attestation** → drives its autonomy, its budget share, and whether its officeholder
   is replaced.
A ministry that claims value citizens don't attest accrues **flow resistance** and loses standing,
funding, and autonomy — the same mechanism applied to any transformer.

## 4. The fiscal reframe — a democratically-chosen revenue mix
The doctrine does **not** dogmatically fix a single tax base. It fixes only **constraints** —
*don't let money buy governance power; keep floors funded; price externalities* — and leaves the
**revenue mix as a per-polity democratic dial.** (This answers the objection that rent alone may be
too small: a state with no resources or public property can fund itself from the other channels.)

**Revenue channels (the polity votes the mix):**
- **Commons rent** — unearned rent (land/location, natural resources, spectrum, and crucially
  **AI-compute/data/network-effects**) → the **Commons Fund.** The Georgist move (fund the state from
  *what nobody created*); primary **where it is abundant.**
- **Pigouvian externality & excise charges** — anti-value charges on harmful flows; an
  **excise / "sin tax" is just an externality charge**; corrective *and* revenue.
- **Concentration charges + escrow/clawback recoveries** (earmarked to the harmed).
- **A voted contribution on earned value** — an income/production contribution **is allowed if
  citizens vote it**; it is *not* forbidden. For **rent-poor states it may be the main base.** It is
  reconciled with "own the earned" by framing it as **payment for the *commons inputs* you consumed
  while earning** (security, infrastructure, the knowledge commons) — you still pay for the *unearned*
  inputs, measured via income as a proxy.
- **Incentivized donation** — companies/citizens may **donate value to the state**, which **raises
  their civic standing / value-to-the-state** in exchange for **cheap-to-provide benefits**
  (recognition, priority access, honours). A positive-sum flow: pay the donor **in their own frame**
  (inter-frame routing), channelling the **generosity multiplier** into public finance.

*(Honest flag: whether any given mix funds a full state is empirical — a first-order thing for the
simulation to test. The point is the mix is **chosen and plural**, not fixed.)*

**Expenditure (how funds flow as financing along value flows):**
- **The citizen dividend** — the rent-funded material floor guarantee (everyone's baseline
  availability, §Distributive-Justice).
- **Strategic financing** — the Commons Fund is allocated to **close measured imbalances**, directed
  by ministries to where value is under-produced.

## 5. The budget cycle (the governing loop applied to public finance)
```
Rent + externality + concentration + clawback  →  Commons Fund
        →  allocate by  ( measured_imbalance × voted_weight × floor_urgency )
        →  release financing in TRANCHES, each gated on citizen-attested delivery
        →  re-sense; under-delivering programs lose the next tranche
```
- **Allocation follows value flows + weights mechanically.** A ministry's financing share rises with
  its domain's `imbalance × w(t)`; **floors override weights** (a below-floor cohort is financed
  regardless of how the majority weighted that axis).
- **Money follows *realized* value, not promises.** Financing is released in tranches on **citizen
  attestation of delivered value** (reusing settlement-on-verification, BR14). A program that
  doesn't deliver attested value is defunded. No backroom appropriations — every allocation is on the
  verifiable log and replayable.

## 6. How a citizen's vote becomes budget (the translation)
When citizens vote up an axis (say, cognitive health), the imbalance term for that axis is scaled by
the higher weight → the Cognitive-Health ministry's financing requests win a larger share of the
Commons Fund — **automatically and transparently, through the imbalance formula, not through
appropriations politics.** This is the mechanical bridge from *"citizens set priorities"* to *"money
moves."*

## 7. The citizen-accountability loop — six concrete channels
The owner's principle ("the organs' critic is the citizens") is mechanized as **six continuous
channels** (not a periodic election):
1. **Weight-vote** — tilt which domains get financed.
2. **Need-announcement** — create bottom-up demand a ministry must serve.
3. **Value-attestation** — validate (or deny) the value a ministry/program claims to have delivered;
   this *is* the ministry's performance score.
4. **Revaluation-petition** — challenge past ministry-celebrated value later shown harmful.
5. **Recall / court-challenge** — remove a low-standing organ or contest a specific assessment/coercion.
6. **Delegation** — hand any of the above to a party (revocably, topic-scoped) if you don't want to
   micromanage.
**Anti-gaming (from the round-2 fixes):** ministries **cannot self-attest** their value
(independence); citizen attestation is **staked + collusion-checked**; and **no governance signal may
be moved by transferable money** (a ministry cannot buy attestation or bias a governance market).

## 8. Defense & emergency organs (new pillars, sketched)
- **Defense** is a ministry too, but Charter-bound: graduated, accountable, logged. It is the domain
  where **threat-proportional intrusion** (§0) and command-style action are legitimate — but only
  under warrant/emergency due process, never as the peacetime default.
- **Emergency / state-of-exception:** a supermajority-triggered mode that may **temporarily raise
  intrusion/coercion thresholds and shift bounded domains to command**, with a **strict sunset** and
  **mandatory post-emergency review** (every emergency act is logged and re-judged afterward). This
  answers "the homeostatic loop has no hull" without making the exception permanent (the Weimar Art.
  48 failure mode).
