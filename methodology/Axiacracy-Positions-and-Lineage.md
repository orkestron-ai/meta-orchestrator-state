# Axiacracy — Intellectual Positions & Lineage

*A companion to [`Axiacracy-The-System.md`](./Axiacracy-The-System.md). Where the systemic
description states **what** Axiacracy is, this register states **where it stands in relation to
the thinkers and schools that came before it** — what it adopts, what it adapts, and where it
**consciously diverges and offers its own solution** — with citations, so a reader can jump
straight to "Axiacracy vs capitalism," "vs socialism," "vs Smith," etc.*

## How to read the stance

| Stance | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **ADOPTED** | Became a foundational commitment of Axiacracy's philosophy. |
| **ADAPTED** | The concept was taken but generalized/modified to fit. |
| **ANCESTOR** | The author independently argues something Axiacracy holds — a vindication, not a borrowing. |
| **DIVERGED** | Conscious disagreement; Axiacracy rejects the thesis and offers its own solution. |
| **OPEN** | Engaged but not yet resolved / awaiting a deeper read. |

## Entries

### Classical political economy

**Adam Smith — *An Inquiry into the Nature and Causes of the Wealth of Nations* (1776).**
*Deep line-by-line analysis: [`research/Adam-Smith-critique-of-Axiacracy.md`](../research/Adam-Smith-critique-of-Axiacracy.md).*
- **ANCESTOR** on: value is multidimensional and price is blind to use-value (the water–diamond paradox);
  **unearned rent** is real, separable, and the best tax base ("reap where they never sowed"; ground-rent
  "owes its existence to the good government of the sovereign"); property in one's **earned labour** is
  "the most sacred and inviolable"; the division of labour **degrades the worker's mind** ("mental
  mutilation") and the state must protect that non-monetary value; money is a costly **wheel** worth
  economizing; credit **mobilizes dead stock**, it does not create capital.
- **ADOPTED as a constraint** (the decisive one): the **knowledge problem** — a state that directs capital
  "assume[s] an authority which could safely be trusted … to no council or senate whatever." Axiacracy
  therefore **renounces the "fourth duty"** of directing value and governs by *correct-and-release*
  (System §6). It also adopts Smith's four maxims (certainty > equality), his warning that **capture is
  the default** ("conspiracy against the public"), and his verdict that **state-owned funds are a poor
  base** → the Commons Fund is a rent-*taxer*, not an asset-*operator*.
- **ADAPTED**: money is demoted in *civic power* but kept as the *numéraire/settlement layer*; the capital/
  credit engine is rebuilt on Smith's own dead-stock + patient-capital logic; public debt is un-deferred
  and disciplined per his Book V warning against debasement.
- **Net:** Smith is Axiacracy's most important single interlocutor — simultaneously its philosophical
  ancestor and the disciplinarian that forced it to see richly but steer humbly.

### Markets, liberalism, capitalism

**Capitalism / market liberalism (classical & neoclassical).**
- **ADOPTED**: the market clears allocation; the **profit signal** is the distributed, self-correcting
  mechanism that reallocates capital — Axiacracy does not override it (System §11).
- **DIVERGED**: capitalism treats *money/exchange-value as the measure of value* and leaves externalities
  and concentration unpriced. Axiacracy prices the **externality residual** (multidimensional Pigou),
  captures **unearned rent**, and separates civic power from wealth — while keeping the market for what it
  does well. *Reference:* Smith (1776); the neoclassical price-theory tradition.

**"Axiocracy" as market value-discovery — David Mc (Substack "Axio," 2026).**
- **DIVERGED (near-namesake, opposite thesis).** His *axiocracy* = "governance by value discovery" where
  value means *demonstrated market exchange-value* and the state is minimal. Axiacracy holds value is
  **multidimensional and perspectival**; his market-only model is **the special case of Axiacracy with only
  the economic axis and only the market loop active.** The `-a-` spelling (ἀξία, *value*) deliberately
  differentiates. *Reference:* axio.fyi, "Axiocracy: Governance by Value Discovery."

### Socialism, Marx

**Socialism / central planning.**
- **DIVERGED**: socialism collectivizes even *earned* value and the *means of production*, and directs the
  economy centrally. Axiacracy collectivizes **only the unearned rent**, protects earned property as
  Smith's "most sacred," and refuses central direction (the knowledge problem). It is neither capitalism
  nor socialism but a **third position: private ownership of the earned, commons ownership of the unearned**.

**Karl Marx — *Capital* (1867). [OPEN — deep read queued.]**
- **Preliminary partial ANCESTOR**: Marx's analysis of **capital concentration** and of **alienation**
  (labour losing meaning) prefigures Axiacracy's anti-concentration ceiling and its **meaning/cognitive
  axis** — the article's "loss of meaning" is an alienation claim.
- **Preliminary DIVERGED**: Axiacracy rejects the **labour theory of value** (value is multidimensional and
  perspectival, realized at consumption, not embodied labour), and rejects the abolition of markets and
  private property. *To be confirmed by a line-by-line read of Capital Vol. I.*

### Land & rent

**Henry George — *Progress and Poverty* (1879); the land-value tax.**
- **ADOPTED & GENERALIZED.** Rent from land/nature belongs to all; tax the *unearned* rent, not
  production. Axiacracy makes this a core pillar and **generalizes it from land to AI-compute, data and
  network-effects** ("the new land"), with Smith's improvement-exclusion so only *unimproved* rent is
  captured. *Reference:* George (1879); [Georgism](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Georgism).

### Democracy & the constitutional tradition

**Liberal democracy.**
- **ADOPTED**: legitimacy by consent, inviolable rights, separation of powers, and — its crown jewel —
  **peaceful transfer of power** (in Axiacracy: loyalty runs to the Charter, not the officeholder).
- **DIVERGED**: the unit of decision is *headcount*, so minorities are structurally outvoted. Axiacracy's
  governing signal is **imbalance measured per cohort, independent of headcount**, with **non-votable
  per-cohort floors** — protecting minorities structurally rather than by the majority's goodwill (System
  §7, §14).

**Hans Kelsen — the hierarchy of norms; *jus cogens*.**
- **ADOPTED**: the **Charter** is the *Grundnorm* / meta-constitution above every polity's constitution and
  codes (conform-or-void). *Reference:* Kelsen, *Pure Theory of Law* (1934).

**Carl Schmitt — the state of exception.**
- **DIVERGED (engaged).** Schmitt: "sovereign is he who decides on the exception." Axiacracy accepts that a
  polity needs an emergency mode, but **bounds it** — supermajority trigger, strict sunset, mandatory
  post-hoc review — precisely to prevent the **Weimar Article 48** slide into permanent exception. *Reference:*
  Schmitt, *Political Theology* (1922).

### Justice & wellbeing

**Amartya Sen & Martha Nussbaum — the capability approach.**
- **ADOPTED**: floors are defined in **capability-space** (the real freedom to achieve central functionings),
  as the **availability of material means**, not resources or audited feeling — and require *equity*
  (unequal inputs) not equality. *Reference:* Sen, *Development as Freedom* (1999); Nussbaum, *Women and
  Human Development* (2000); [SEP: capability approach](https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/capability-approach/).

**John Rawls — *A Theory of Justice* (1971). [ADAPTED.]**
- Rawls's **difference principle** (inequalities must benefit the worst-off) and **veil of ignorance** are
  close kin to Axiacracy's floors. Axiacracy **adapts**: it is **capability-based** (what people can *do*),
  **multidimensional**, **contribution-responsive above the floor**, and **ceiling-bounded** — where Rawls is
  primary-goods-based. *A fuller read is queued to sharpen the relation.*

### Commons & institutions

**Elinor Ostrom — *Governing the Commons* (1990); polycentricity.**
- **ADOPTED**: Ostrom showed commons can be governed without either privatization or central authority, via
  **polycentric, overlapping self-governance**. This is Axiacracy's **federation of value-frames** and its
  **"no apex"** result — the state is one frame among many, not the owner of all value. *Reference:* Ostrom
  (1990).

### Cybernetics & mechanism design

**Stafford Beer — the Viable System Model; Project Cybersyn (1971–73).**
- **ADAPTED / ANCESTOR**: Axiacracy is cybernetic feedback governance — sense → diagnose → correct → re-sense
  — Beer's lineage. But it **adds the multidimensional value-vector Beer lacked**, and it **heeds Cybersyn's
  failure**: the knowledge problem means the loop must *correct*, not *direct*. *Reference:* Beer, *Brain of
  the Firm* (1972); Medina, *Cybernetic Revolutionaries* (2011).

**Robin Hanson — futarchy ("vote on values, bet on beliefs").**
- **ADAPTED**: Axiacracy already "votes on values (weights)"; it **adds a belief-aggregation / prediction-
  market layer for *measurement*** (the per-axis settling mechanism, System §5c) — but, to close the
  money-buys-confidence attack, such markets must be **staked in non-transferable Æ, never money.** *Reference:*
  Hanson, [*Shall We Vote on Values, But Bet on Beliefs?*](https://mason.gmu.edu/~rhanson/futarchy.pdf) (2000/2013).

**Kate Raworth — doughnut economics.**
- **ADOPTED-as-validation**: her *social foundation + ecological ceiling* is almost exactly Axiacracy's
  floors + ceilings; Axiacracy **generalizes** it from static thresholds to a **dynamic, weighted, multi-axis
  balance**. *Reference:* Raworth, *Doughnut Economics* (2017).

### Foundational value theory (the parent framework)

**Value Management Theory (VMT) / Æilus — Victor Bolshakov.**
- **ADOPTED as the substrate.** The **value system {A,F,G,R,T}**, the four value-forms
  (Potential/Planned/Realized/Retrospective), **anti-value as an independent category**, flow resistance,
  leakage, and the **participation condition θ** are the ontology on which the whole Æ-vector and its
  governance are built. *Reference:* [value-management.science](https://value-management.science) ·
  [aeilus.tech](https://aeilus.tech).
