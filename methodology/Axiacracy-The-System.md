## Preface — what this is

Axiacracy (from ἀξία, *value*, + -κρατία, *rule*) is a proposed **form of government for the age of
artificial intelligence**: a state that governs by **seeing and rebalancing the multidimensional
flows of value across a society**, rather than by taxing labour and redistributing money.

It is offered as a *system* — a mutually-consistent set of institutions covering the constitution,
the economy, the law, the fiscal base, and the apparatus of state — not a slogan. The reader should
judge it as one judges a machine: does each part reinforce the others, and can the whole survive
contact with self-interest, scarcity, war, and time? The design has been stress-tested against the
major rival orders and against Adam Smith's *Wealth of Nations*; the discipline those tests imposed
is built into what follows, not appended to it.

Two commitments run through the whole system and should be held in mind:

1. **See richly; steer humbly.** Axiacracy *measures* value across many dimensions — the revolution
   is to stop being blind to what GDP cannot see — but it *compels* only narrowly, in proportion to
   how well a thing can be measured and how far its harm crosses between people. It corrects what
   markets miss; it does not direct what markets already do well.
2. **Value is plural and belongs to no one frame — including the state's.** There is no single
   number for "civilizational value." The state is one participant with a bounded mandate, not the
   owner of all value.

# PART I — FOUNDATIONS (why a new form is needed)

## 1. The rupture

For two centuries every state rested on one assumption: **human labour is the basis of economic
value.** Taxation, welfare, labour law, education, even national identity were built on it. As
artificial intelligence automates cognitive and physical labour, output can keep rising while human
participation in production falls. The industrial loop — *labour → income → consumption → tax →
redistribution* — begins to break: labour income falls, consumption weakens, the tax base erodes,
and value concentrates around the owners of AI infrastructure, compute, models and data.

Two dangers dominate the transition, and neither is visible to the instruments a modern state uses:

- **Concentration.** Whoever owns computation, models, data, logistics and robotics can capture a
  runaway share of value, beyond any historical precedent.
- **Loss of meaning.** If humans become economically optional, the deepest risk is not unemployment
  but the collapse of purpose, status and belonging that work once provided. Adam Smith already saw
  the seed of this in 1776: the division of labour, left to itself, renders the common worker "as
  stupid and ignorant as it is possible for a human creature to become" — a "mental mutilation" the
  state has a duty to counteract. AI industrialises that mutilation.

GDP sees neither. A platform can add trillions in market value while degrading attention, dissolving
professions, weakening communities, and eroding the shared knowledge commons. **Financial success is
not civilizational success.** A governance instrument that reads only money is, in the AI era, flying
blind.

## 2. The reframe: society as a graph of value transformers

Axiacracy stops seeing society as *taxpayers, employees, corporations, consumers*. It sees a **graph
of value transformers** — citizens, families, businesses, AI systems, institutions, cities,
infrastructure, agents — each continuously **consuming, transforming, creating value, or generating
anti-value**. Governance becomes the **coordination and rebalancing of the value flows** between these
transformers, measured across many dimensions.

This is not a metaphor to be governed by literally in every particular. It is a *lens* that makes
visible what the money-lens hides — and, as Part II insists, the state may act on that lens only where
it can measure honestly and where markets genuinely fail.

## 3. What Axiacracy is — and is not

- **It is** a doctrine for governing by *seeing* multidimensional value and *rebalancing* its flows,
  with money demoted from sovereign to instrument, coercion disciplined by measurement, and the state
  funded from unearned rent and priced externalities rather than from taxing earned labour.
- **It is not** central planning. Markets keep clearing allocation; the state prices only what markets
  miss. Smith's warning is honoured, not defied: the statesman who would "direct private people in
  what manner they ought to employ their capitals" assumes "an authority which could safely be trusted
  … to no council or senate whatever." Axiacracy therefore renounces the ambition to *direct* value
  and keeps to *correcting* it (Part III).
- **It is not** a single imposed value score, nor social credit: the Æ-vector is the state's *own*
  weighted lens, openly political and revisable; sensing is privacy-bounded; there is no secret
  behavioural score used to punish.
- **It is not** money abolition: money survives as one axis and — crucially — as the settlement and
  measurement layer that lets any allocation clear at all.
- **It is not** a finished blueprint. It is a research programme with an explicit test bench
  (Part VI), and an honest list of open problems (Part VII).

Its lineage is deliberately mixed: it takes multidimensional value and the anti-concentration ethos
from the critics of GDP; the primacy of markets, the sanctity of *earned* property, the taxing of
*unearned* rent, and the duty to protect the worker's mind from Adam Smith; per-cohort protection from
the constitutional tradition; and cybernetic feedback from Stafford Beer — while adding the one thing
none of them had: a disciplined, measurement-bounded way to see and rebalance value beyond money.

# PART II — THE VALUE SUBSTRATE AND ITS MEASUREMENT (the language, and its limits)

## 4. The substrate (V1–V5)

Five foundational constructs are the vocabulary of the whole system.

- **V1 — Value Transformer.** The universal node: any entity modelled as consuming, transforming, and
  creating value, or generating anti-value. A parent, a firm, a ministry, a city, an agent are all
  transformers.
- **V2 — Multidimensional Value (the Æ-vector).** Value is a vector across ten dimensions —
  *economic, educational, scientific, demographic, cognitive, infrastructural, social-stability,
  ecological, meaning, epistemic* — with a parallel **anti-value** vector. GDP is one axis of ten.
  The axes are a *shared vocabulary* (partial commensurability), not a claim that they are mutually
  reducible; the **weights** across them (§7) are an open political choice, not a metaphysical exchange
  rate.
- **V3 — Value Flow.** The edges: transfers/transformations of value between transformers, with a
  lifecycle that is load-bearing — **Planned** (the producer's claim) → **Realized** (the recipient's
  experienced value) → **Retrospective** (a later re-scoring when new evidence arrives). The gap
  between Planned and Realized is *flow resistance*; persistent net outflow is *leakage*.
- **V4 — Anti-Value.** Harm as a first-class, independent category (not "negative value"): a single
  flow can carry both real value and real anti-value at once. Classified by **scope** (edge vs
  systemic), **detection lag** (immediate → generational), and **intent/kind** (rights-violation,
  malicious, self-directed distress, negligent).
- **V5 — Value System / Frame.** Following Value Management Theory, each citizen, family, business —
  and **the state itself** — is a *value system* with its own criteria. Value exists only within a
  frame; the polity is a **federation of frames**, not a hierarchy with one at the top. There is no
  "value-in-general," and the state is **one frame with a bounded mandate**, never the owner of all
  value. Each actor's **participation condition θ** must hold, or the system is unsustainable — a
  first-class alarm on the state, not a fault of the actor.

## 5. Measurement — the load-bearing discipline

The whole system rises or falls on one question: *can value be measured honestly enough to act on?*
Adam Smith is the standing warning here. He judged **labour** the true measure of value and then spent
a hundred and fifty pages failing to measure the real value of **corn** across centuries — "I do not
pretend that any very certain conclusion can be drawn." He showed that the plausible official metric
(the balance of trade) had "no certain criterion" and became a vacuum filled by "the private interest
of particular traders." And he ranked, among the maxims of taxation, **certainty above equality**: "a
very considerable degree of inequality … is not near so great an evil as a very small degree of
uncertainty."

A ten-axis metric is therefore not a richer instrument by default — it is *ten* simultaneous
measurement problems, most of them *less* observable than Smith's single labour axis and with no
"higgling of the market" to force a number into being. Axiacracy answers this not by pretending
measurement is easy, but by making its limits **constitutional**:

**(a) Sensing is separated from coercion.** *Seeing* value across many dimensions is observation — the
founding revolution, and no exercise of power. *Compelling* — charging, enforcing, redirecting,
restricting — is where legitimacy, gaming and capture bite, and it is tightly bounded. The founding
idea lives entirely in the sensing layer and survives whole; only coercion is disciplined. (Sensing is
itself privacy-bounded: cohort-aggregate by default, individual detail only by consent or a
proportionate, logged warrant.)

**(b) Coercion is graded by measurement-confidence × inter-frame scope.** The state may compel in
proportion to how confidently a thing can be measured and how far its harm crosses between frames.
High-confidence, objective, inter-frame harm, or a breach of a material floor → coercion is permitted.
Low-confidence, subjective, or intra-frame matters → **soft power only**: publish the signal, offer
incentives, illuminate — never compel. *The state may see meaning; it may never compel on it.*

**(c) Every axis earns its coercive weight, or has none.** An axis may carry coercive weight only if
it has: a **falsifiable, real (not nominal) definition** with a **published error bar**; a **market-
like settling mechanism** (a market, a prediction market, or contested sortition-based attestation)
that forces a number into existence rather than a ministry computing one; **out-of-sample validation**
against an independent welfare outcome; and **separation of the sensor from the beneficiary** (whoever
defines or operates an axis's metric must gain nothing on that axis). An axis without a settling
mechanism carries **zero coercive weight** and lives purely in the sensing layer.

**(d) Certainty is bought at the price of some equality — deliberately.** Following Smith's maxim, the
rules by which value is measured are published as **slow-to-change fundamental law**; guardian
discretion is stripped from anything that determines an *individual's* obligation. The full ten-axis
vector is used for **collective steering**; an *individual's* liabilities and dividends are assessed on
a few **simple, certain, hard-to-conceal, unshiftable bases** (chiefly rent and luxury consumption —
§12), so no citizen faces the arbitrary, ever-reweighted personal assessment that Smith condemned as
the French *taille*. Measurement invasive enough to score everyone on ten axes must therefore never be
the basis of personal liability — only of policy.

This discipline is the spine of the system. It converts "governance by value" from an assertion into a
bounded, auditable, self-limiting practice: **measure widely, but act only where the measure can be
defended.**

# PART III — THE GOVERNING PRINCIPLE (see richly, steer humbly)

## 6. Correct, do not direct — the renounced "fourth duty"

Smith reduced the legitimate state to three duties — **defence, justice, and the public works no
individual would provide** — and *discharged* the sovereign from a fourth: "superintending the industry
of private people, and … directing it towards the employments most suitable to the interest of the
society," for which "no human wisdom or knowledge could ever be sufficient." Axiacracy accepts this
verdict. Its externality-pricing, public-goods, rent-taxation and human-capability core maps onto
Smith's second and third duties and is legitimate. Its temptation — to *steer* capital toward
high-scoring axes — is the self-appointed fourth duty, and it is renounced.

Concretely, Axiacracy governs by **correct-and-release**, not by direction:

- The Æ-price of a genuine externality is added **on top of** market prices, with an explicit
  **ceiling**, and then **profit-seeking re-clears allocation freely.** The state prices what the
  market missed; it does not substitute an administered value-score for the profit signal, because —
  as Smith showed — the deviation of profit from its natural rate *is itself* the distributed,
  real-time, self-correcting signal that reallocates capital "without any intervention of law."
  Decoupling profit from value does not add a signal; it jams the only one the economy has.
- **Subsidiarity of steering.** The Æ-vector may *measure and publish* an imbalance; the default remedy
  is to change **general rules** available to all (prices, rights, information), never to direct a
  named actor's capital into a named use. Any targeted intervention must clear a **local-knowledge
  burden of proof**: the state must show it holds information the dispersed actors provably lack (a
  real externality invisible at the point of decision). Otherwise steering is presumed folly and is
  disallowed.
- **Corrective instruments only.** The state may price a genuine externality residual and remove a
  pre-existing distortion; it may **not** deploy *promotional* instruments (bounties, "missions" that
  pull capital toward an axis for its own sake). Every intervention must pass a **net-diversion test**:
  the value gained on an axis must exceed the value lost on the axes it drains — including the trap
  that over-steering a favoured axis starves the broad productive base that feeds it.

## 7. The governing loop

The state runs a continuous cybernetic loop: **sense (richly) → diagnose imbalance → correct
(graduated) → re-sense.** The imbalance signal, per axis *a*, cohort *c*, region *r*:

```
imbalance =  w_a · distance(current_Æ, target_band)   ← steering, weight-scaled
           + below_floor_penalty                       ← LARGE; NOT weight-scaled
           + variance_penalty(across cohorts)          ← equity; NOT weight-scaled
```

Two terms are *not* scaled by the voted weights: a cohort **below its floor** is a large signal
regardless of how the majority weighted that axis, and **cross-cohort inequity** is itself a flagged
harm. The **weights `w(t)`** are the state frame's democratically-set priorities (§8), openly political
and change-rate-limited (hysteresis, to prevent policy whiplash). The response is **graduated by
confidence × inter-frame scope** (§5b) and, where coercion is warranted, applied as an **escalation
ladder** — annotation → charge → access/standing restriction → loss of liberty — whose specific
thresholds are set per polity, its principle (proportionality, only on defensible measurement)
universal. The state steers on **settled** value and trends; it treats **provisional** value cautiously.

## 8. Who sets the weights — and how capture is resisted

Citizens set the value **weights** (what the polity currently prioritizes) and the **floors** — directly
or through revocable, topic-scoped delegation to **parties** — by a quadratic, reputation-influenced-
but-capped vote. This is where Smith's darkest lesson applies, and it is designed into the constitution
rather than wished away. "People of the same trade seldom meet together … but the conversation ends in
a conspiracy against the public"; the three economic orders are **not** symmetrically aligned with the
public good, and the best-organized order's interest wears the public's name. Ten axes are ten
legitimizing vocabularies for ten lobbies. Axiacracy therefore treats **capture as the default**:

- **Proposer ≠ scorer.** No ministry may both propose an intervention and score its own delivery;
  scoring goes to a body that gains nothing on the axis.
- **Hard sunsets, no auto-renewal.** Every weight, axis, mission and ministry expires and must be
  affirmatively re-justified against current conditions — the burden on continuation, not abolition
  (Smith's master-pathology: institutions outlive the reasons that made them reasonable).
- **Published beneficiary registers.** For every priced externality and every intervention, the
  concentrated interests that gain are public, so capture is visible.
- **Adversarial, sortition-based attestation**, not open voting (open attestation is trivially
  astroturfed by the organized few); **alignment-weighted input**, with the wealth of the profit-order
  firewalled from weight-setting by the non-transferable civic ledger (§10), and the aligned-but-
  voiceless structurally amplified.
- **The measurement layer is independently governed.** Whoever writes the value-attribution schemas is
  a hidden sovereign unless that layer is itself constitutionalized: an independent Standards authority,
  changes ratified by sortition juries, calibrated, adversarially audited, versioned and appealable.

## 9. Epistemic humility

Smith's most profound observation is that commerce's most important effect — the order and liberty it
brought — was "**by far the most important of all their effects**" and yet "**the least observed**,"
produced by people with "not the least intention to serve the public." A state that scores value will,
by construction, optimize the axes it can currently *name and measure*, and be blind to the
unnameable second-order goods that turn out to matter most. Axiacracy builds in structural humility:

- an **unobserved-effects reserve** — a permanently unallocated band of steering weight standing for
  "value we cannot yet name," plus a deliberately **unsteered zone** where emergent dynamics can arise
  before being scored, and **ex-post positive-externality auditing** that back-credits value the metric
  missed;
- **concentration/fragility caps** — a limit on how much of total capital any intervention may pull
  into one axis, and market **variety** as an explicit competing objective, so the state does not
  create the fragile, over-swollen sectors it must then protect.

# PART IV — THE ECONOMY (value, money, property, capital)

## 10. Two layers of money

Axiacracy separates two things ordinary money fuses:

- **The sovereign layer — a non-transferable Æ standing ledger.** A record of what an actor has
  contributed and received across the axes — like a credit history, not a coin. It cannot be bought,
  sold, gifted or inherited. This is what keeps **civic power separate from wealth** (anti-plutocracy),
  and it is the tool that lets the system firewall the profit-order's wealth out of weight-setting.
- **The convenience layer — money as the settlement and measurement layer.** Money is demoted **in
  civic power** but retained as the **numéraire and clearing technology**. This is a correction the
  classical critique forces: money's genius, Smith showed, is *divisibility* — it alone "can be divided
  into any number of parts" and so defeats barter. A "thin rail" carrying only one axis would re-create
  the barter deadlock *between axes* (how does an educational gain settle against an ecological loss?).
  So all Pigouvian charges and the citizen dividend are **priced and settled in money**, precisely
  because it is the only medium that divides without loss.
- **The settlement unit is inviolable.** Because money is demoted, the temptation to inflate away
  obligations must be closed by rule: redenomination or the inflating-away of debts is a **supermajority
  constitutional act**. Smith's deepest fiscal warning is that states escape their debts by debasing the
  unit — "an injustice of treacherous fraud" that enriches "the idle and profuse debtor at the expense
  of the industrious and frugal creditor." A state that treats money as a policy dial must bolt that
  lever shut.

## 11. Markets and the externality overlay

The **market clears allocation** (revealed value; Hayek and Smith respected): citizens direct their
earned entitlement to the providers they choose, and that choice is the reward — the state does not
decide which business deserves value. The state prices only the **externality residual** — the harm or
benefit on the *other* axes that the market price omitted — as a **multidimensional Pigou** overlay,
capped, and correct-and-release (§6). **Profit is thereby decoupled from *civilizational value* only in
the narrow, Pigouvian sense** — a profitable-but-harmful actor pays for its measured harm and loses
standing — **not** in the sense of overriding the profit signal that coordinates allocation. To prevent
the corrective charge from corrupting into a revenue addiction, **Pigouvian revenue is hypothecated away
from the ministries' operating budgets** (to the Commons Fund / remediation), so the state never
acquires a fiscal interest in the harm continuing.

## 12. Property, rent and the commons

The principle: **you own what you create; you do not own what nature or society created.** This is
neither capitalism (which lets rent be captured privately) nor socialism (which collectivizes even
earned value). It is, precisely, Smith's own position — and it is operationalized with his rigour, not
by vague "signatures":

- **Rent is detected by a cause-vs-effect test.** Smith: "high or low wages and profit are the *causes*
  of high or low price; high or low rent is the *effect* of it." A value flow is **rent** to the extent
  that it is an *effect* of scarcity rather than a *cause* of supply — i.e., its withdrawal would not
  reduce supply. Before any commons claim attaches, four deductions are made (also Smith's): **interest
  on genuine improvement capital, the wages of superintendence, a risk/insurance premium, and the
  owner's own labour.** The commons captures only the **unimproved locational/scarcity rent**, assessed
  independently of what the holder builds, non-retroactively, with **100% of the return to improvement
  left to the improver** — the pure land-value-tax property Smith endorses, and the opposite of the
  produce-share (tithe/metayer) levy he showed "is found to be a very great hindrance to improvement."
- **AI-compute, data and network-effects are the new land.** Their value comes largely from humanity's
  collective data, public science and network effects — not solely from the owner. Their **unearned
  rent is commons-owed**, exactly as ground-rent "is altogether owing to the good government of the
  sovereign." This funds the citizen dividend and the civilizational reinvestment the founding thesis
  calls for.
- **The Commons Fund is a rent-*taxer*, not an asset-*operator*.** This is the sharpest correction the
  classical critique forces. Smith examined the "state's own funds" model and rejected it: crown lands
  yield "not the fourth part" of their private-management potential and "cost more to the society than
  perhaps any other equal revenue." So Axiacracy **leaves land, compute and data in decentralized
  productive hands and taxes away only the unearned rent** — flipping its weakest classical position
  (state ownership) into its strongest (rent taxation). And because **capital is "a citizen of the
  world"** that flees a vexatious levy, the Fund is weighted toward **immobile bases** (land, physical
  resources, spectrum, local network position); mobile compute/data rents are pursued only moderately
  and by international coordination, or via their immobile complements (data-centre land and power).

## 13. The capital and credit engine

A value-flow state that demotes money must still answer the oldest economic question: *where does next
year's capital come from?* The classical critique showed this to be the design's weakest module, and
supplied the repair. Smith: "**parsimony, and not industry, is the immediate cause of the increase of
capital**," and saving *is* investment. The engine has four parts:

- **A savings vessel.** A non-transferable civic ledger plus a *thin* money rail would sever the
  motive to save (consumption keeps its motive; abstention loses it). So money's functions are
  **split**: thin for *circulation*, but a **stockable, compounding store-of-value** is restored for
  *savings*, firewalled from the civic Æ ledger. Civic power stays separate from wealth; the saving
  *motive* — Smith's womb-to-grave "desire of bettering our condition" — is preserved.
- **Patient capital.** Long-horizon assets (infrastructure, research, energy) return "after a period of
  many years … too distant to suit the conveniency of a bank," and require patient funders paid a
  time-return. So Axiacracy **licenses a duration-tolerant, equity-like instrument** — a state-recognized
  share in an asset's *future realized value* — with an at-risk return over years. The rate may be
  *constrained*; it may **not** be zeroed, or fixed capital never forms.
- **Credit as the mobilization of dead stock.** Smith's own credit theory is almost verbatim
  Axiacracy's: credit "render[s] a greater part of that capital active and productive," turning idle
  reserves into working stock — it does *not* create capital. Adopt it as the official rule: **credit
  only against real, near-term realizable value, only up to the amount of otherwise-idle resource**,
  with **maturity-matching** (short/callable credit for short self-liquidating uses; long assets to
  patient capital) and a **redemption test** — so the state never repeats the Ayr-Bank detonation of
  funding slow assets with callable money. **Rate by decree is rejected** (it backfires and funnels
  capital to "prodigals and projectors"); instead the credit rate floats near the return on productive
  stock and the **Æ standing ledger is the borrower-quality signal** — ration by demonstrated creation,
  not administered price.
- **Security of the return-to-waiting.** Because value-weights are re-priceable, a naive design would
  make the return on any sunk improvement politically variable — reviving Smith's short-lease/metayer
  disincentive. So Axiacracy grants a **"value-weight lease"**: the weights and prices applying to a
  *specific* sunk improvement are locked for a long, constitutionally-fixed horizon from the date of
  investment. Steering may change the weights for *new* activity; it may never retroactively reprice
  capital already committed. Paired with a **productive/reproductive axis** and a watched
  **capital/revenue ratio** ("wherever capital predominates, industry prevails; wherever revenue,
  idleness"), this keeps a value-state from legislating a Versailles — rich in honoured activity, poor
  in reproducing capital.

## 14. Distributive justice

The stance: **capability sufficientarianism + a bounded contribution-responsive band + an
anti-concentration ceiling + a generosity premium** — which is exactly the corridor of §7, now
grounded. Floors guarantee a **threshold of central capabilities** (Sen/Nussbaum), defined as the
**availability of material means to function**, *not* an audit of whether a person *feels* fulfilled —
the state guarantees you *can* be healthy, educated and connected; it does not measure your inner state.
Above the floor, inequality is permitted (contribution-responsive) up to the ceiling; donated,
un-consumed value books a **generosity premium**, so the gradient points to giving, not hoarding. Two
classical refinements are built in: floors keep **competition and skin-in-the-game inside them**
(co-payment, provider choice, "emulation"), lest a fully-salaried monopoly deliverable rot like Smith's
endowed professors who "have given up altogether even the pretence of teaching"; and floors are
designed for **dispersed independence** — freeing the citizen *from* any single patron, including the
state — the "maintained without him" condition Smith identifies as the source of the improving yeoman's
effort, not the servile dependence of the fed retainer.

## 15. Inter-frame value routing

Knowing an actor's own frame (what *they* most value) lets the state **pay them in their own currency**
— a scientist a hard problem and recognition, a parent time — which is positive-sum and cheaper than
generic cash; it lets the state **monitor θ** to see when a valuable actor is about to disengage; and it
lets the state act as a **matchmaker**, composing value flows across frames that money-markets miss.
This runs over a **privacy-preserving frame disclosure** (a scoped summary, never the raw ledger,
cohort-aggregate by default, opt-in for individuals) and under a bright line: the state may **serve** a
frame but never **rewrite** it — the difference between incentive and propaganda is transparency,
consent, and service of the actor's own goals.

# PART V — THE CONSTITUTION AND THE STATE (law, powers, fiscal survivability)

## 16. The Charter and the hierarchy of norms

At the top sits the **Charter** — a **meta-constitution** with the standing of *jus cogens*. Any polity
adopting Axiacracy instantiates it, and no subordinate norm — a national constitution, a code, a
regulation, a ruling — may contradict it; a conflicting norm is **void**. The Charter carries an
**unamendable eternity core** (the value-balancing mandate, inviolable rights and floors,
anti-concentration, transparency and contestability) so the doctrine's own amendment process cannot be
used to abolish it — reconciled with popular sovereignty by a defined **constituent power** (a human
body may replace the Charter by deep supermajority). The hierarchy: *Charter → state constitution →
legal codes → regulations → rulings*, each conforming to the one above, with **constitutional review**
as a first-class function. **The Charter is methodology (universal); the specific thresholds and ladders
are implementation (per polity).**

## 17. Rights, crime and justice as rebalancing

Inviolable rights — life, bodily integrity, liberty, consent, exit — are **not priceable, votable or
tradeable**. Harm is handled **by kind**: a rights-violation (murder, violence) is not priced but tried
under criminal law; malicious/predatory harm is charged and clawed back; **distress harm (addiction) is
met with care, not punishment**; negligence is priced. **Incapacitation is justified not as retribution
but by sustainability** — a person whose continued participation makes *others'* participation
conditions fail threatens everyone's value systems.

The **judiciary is a rebalancing operator, not merely a punisher.** Every event is a change of state in
the value flows; a harmful event that crosses a threshold is measured (magnitude per axis, the affected
set read from the flow record, intent, and — for **systemic** harm — population-attributable causation
across many contributors), adjudicated with the accused's right to contest under the **norms in force at
the time of the act** (non-retroactivity: evidence updates, norms do not), and answered by a
**commensurate response = restoration + deterrence** — making every harmed party whole on every damaged
axis (cohort-wide by computation, so victims need not each sue), plus a proportional, escalating
deterrent. Justice is given a **stable, constitutionally-fixed endowment** and **irremovable judges**,
because — Smith — a judiciary whose pay depends on the executive's "good will" cannot be independent, and
funding it from a volatile source is "improper … for an institution which ought to last for ever."

## 18. The apparatus — value-flows, not org-charts

Axiacracy describes **value flows**, grouped by direction; **ministries and municipalities are the organs
*bound* to those flows**, not doctrine primitives. The three branches are **functions over the flows**:
the **legislative** = the demos setting *values* (weights, floors); the **executive** = organs that
sense, finance and rebalance across the **flow-domains** (the axes plus cross-cutting domains); the
**judicial** = the rebalancing of harmful flows. Three cross-cutting **guardians** stand outside the line
organs: **Equity/Ombudsman** (distributional fairness), **Standards & Measurement** (governs the
accounting rules — the hidden sovereign — by sortition-ratified change), and **Audit & Integrity**
(red-teams the measurement layer). Any role — a party, a minister, a judge, even a *citizen* — may be
held by a **human or an AI agent**; the choice is not ideological but decided by **citizen-attested value
delivered**, so humans win where quality, judgment and legitimacy outweigh speed, and AI where scale and
consistency dominate. Because a delegated agent — like Smith's chartered company, "the managers rather of
other people's money than of their own" — will optimize its own metric, agents are held under **revocable
time-limited charters**, their objective is **never coupled to a resource they control**, and their
delivery is **adversarially, sampled-attested**, not self-reported. A **named human liability chain**
stands behind algorithmic-office decisions.

## 19. The three duties and the fiscal base

Axiacracy funds and organizes the state around Smith's three duties — and pays for them by a
**democratically-chosen revenue mix** whose constraints are fixed (no money buys governance power; floors
stay funded; externalities are priced) while the mix is a per-polity choice:

- **Defence — the first duty.** It grows *more* costly with prosperity, is non-excludable, and needs a
  three-to-fourfold surge in war — so it **cannot** be left to a votable contribution, to donations, or to
  a volatile Commons-Fund yield. Axiacracy **ring-fences an existential-security floor** that the vote may
  raise but never lower, funded from the broadest inelastic base, and backs it with an emergency-finance
  facility (below).
- **Justice — the second duty.** Stable endowment, irremovable judges (§17).
- **Public works and institutions — the third duty.** User-fees where the benefit is particular (a toll
  *is* a Pigouvian charge — Smith invented the intuition), general revenue where it is general, and
  **fiscal federalism** so local value-flows are funded and governed locally, lest centralized (AI-)
  ministries build monuments while the cross-roads rot.

**Revenue** = **unearned rent** (the Commons Fund, §12) + **Pigouvian/excise charges** (hypothecated away
from operating budgets, §11) + a **voted contribution on earned value** (allowed if citizens choose it —
the main base for rent-poor states — framed as payment for the *commons inputs* consumed while earning) +
**incentivized donations** (marginal only). The design honours Smith's **four maxims** — proportion to
ability, **certainty** (individual liability on simple, certain, unshiftable bases, §5d), convenience, and
economy in collection (ledger-native settlement slashes the "odious inquisition" of tax-gatherers) —
**never taxes necessaries**, and **models tax incidence** (burden travels through prices regardless of
which axis is charged) before claiming the dividend is progressive, concentrating charges on the "sticky"
bases (rent, ground-rent, luxuries, monopoly gains) that do not shift.

**Public debt is not deferred.** Smith's Book V verdict is that unmanaged funding "has gradually enfeebled
every state which has adopted it," and that peace-time parsimony is the only prevention. So debt discipline
is **constitutionalized before launch, not improvised under wartime duress**: a rules-based, bounded
borrowing framework with an explicit **war-contingency facility**; an **un-raidable sinking discipline**; a
**peace-time reserve** in the Commons Fund; the **inviolable settlement unit** (§10); and a preference for
short, self-liquidating, purpose-tied, *value-linked* instruments over perpetual funding, so debt does not
manufacture the very passive rentier class the doctrine exists to abolish.

## 20. Membership, external relations, and emergency

- **Citizenship rests on unique, verifiable identity** — the anti-Sybil bedrock without which voting,
  attestation and delegation are all gameable. Membership is a value-participation relationship; entry is
  by birth or **naturalization** (a throttle, not a mint). **AI-agent personhood is granted in tiers**
  (tool → delegate → autonomous participant → full civic person) by constitutional choice, gated by unique
  identity and naturalization so no sponsor can mint a voting swarm; standing is non-transferable, so no
  one inherits civic power. **Exit is inviolable — but cannot be used to escape an outstanding liability**,
  which closes the capital-flight loophole. Inviolable rights are **universal**, held by anyone in the
  jurisdiction, member or not.
- **External relations and defence** are first-class organs, not deferrals: diplomacy, treaties,
  foreign-frame recognition, trade, and a Charter-bound defence establishment whose **loyalty runs to the
  Charter, not to any officeholder** — the structural guarantee of peaceful succession.
- **Emergency / state of exception** is a supermajority-triggered mode that may temporarily raise
  intrusion and coercion thresholds and shift bounded domains to command — with a **strict sunset** and
  **mandatory post-hoc review** (every emergency act logged and re-judged afterward), so the homeostatic
  loop has a hull without the exception becoming permanent.

# PART VI — THE WHOLE AS A SYSTEM

## 21. The value-flow map (VSS)

The state is, in the end, **one circulating value system**: value enters from the external boundary
(resources, trade, diaspora remittances, foreign investment) and from citizen contribution; unearned rent
pools in the Commons Fund; the three state functions set priorities, correct externalities and rebalance
harms; value returns to citizens as dividend, delivered services and restoration; and citizens steer the
whole through votes, needs, attestation and recognition — while anti-value is priced back onto its source.
This is expressed formally as a **Value-Stream Schema** (52 nodes, 97 flows, value and anti-value elements)
in the canonical VSS format, so the entire circulation can be inspected, simulated, and — because each
organ node can expand into its own sub-VSS — examined at any depth.

## 22. Failure modes the system designs against

A serious form of government must name how it dies. Axiacracy's self-diagnosed pathologies, each with its
built-in counter: **measurement tyranny / metric-gaming** (→ confidence-gated coercion, adversarial
measurement, retrospective revaluation, sensor≠beneficiary); **capture by organized interest** (→ the
presumption-of-capture regime, §8); **the hidden sovereign of the accounting layer** (→ constitutionalized
Standards guardian); **capital decay** (→ the savings/patient-capital engine, §13); **fiscal ruin by debt
and debasement** (→ constitutionalized debt discipline + inviolable unit, §19); **the endowed-monopoly rot
of fully-funded provision** (→ competition inside floors, §14); **institutional ossification** (→
sunset-and-rejustify, §8); **blindness to the "least observed" good** (→ the unobserved-effects reserve,
§9); and **the mercantilist trap of directing capital** (→ correct-and-release and the renounced fourth
duty, §6).

## 23. Where it stands among rival orders

Axiacracy is not eclecticism; it takes a definite position. Against **capitalism**, it prices the
externalities and the concentration that markets ignore, and taxes unearned rent — but it *keeps the
market and the profit-signal* for allocation. Against **socialism**, it collectivizes only the *unearned*
(rent), never the earned or the means of production, and it refuses central direction. Against **majority
democracy**, it protects minorities structurally (per-cohort non-votable floors, headcount-independent
imbalance) rather than by hoping the majority is kind. Against **technocracy**, it binds the expert:
influence is earned by calibration, reach is not purchasable, the state annotates rather than censors, and
every assessment is contestable. It generalizes **Georgism** from land to all rent (including AI/data); it
grounds its floors in the **capability approach**; it echoes **Beer's** cybernetic feedback but adds the
value-vector he lacked; and it accepts **Smith's** discipline — measure widely, steer humbly, tax the
unearned, protect the mind, and keep to the three duties. Its distinctive claim is the disciplined middle
none of them occupied: *a way to see and rebalance value beyond money without becoming the value-directing
statesman.*

## 24. Implementation and the test bench

Axiacracy the *doctrine* is deployment-agnostic; it fixes only the invariants above. **MOS** — the
Meta-Orchestrator State — is one *realization*: an executable simulation populated by AI-agent citizens on
the Orkestron stack, and, beyond it, a playable online world in which anyone can enter and live as a
citizen. The simulation's scientific purpose is not to *prove* the doctrine but to **map where it stays
stable and where it gets gamed** under noisy, adversarial, contested measurement — the players are the
adversarial pressure on the sensors. A real polity would keep the doctrine and replace everything in the
realization (AI citizens → humans, the sim-clock → real time, the stack → real civic infrastructure).

# PART VII — HONEST STATUS AND OPEN PROBLEMS

This is a mature doctrine, disciplined by its rivals and by the classical critique — and it is a
deliberately *unfinished specification*, because its hardest choices are exactly what the simulation
exists to test rather than to assume. The genuinely open problems, stated plainly:

1. **The formal, executable specification** — the exact imbalance mathematics, the event schemas, cohort
   definition and anti-fragmentation rules, hysteresis parameters, and a worked tick — remains to be
   written; it is the bridge to both the simulation and the game.
2. **Measurement remains the load-bearing bet.** The confidence-gated design makes the *dependence* on
   measurement honest and bounded, but whether enough axes can earn a defensible settling mechanism is an
   empirical question the simulation must answer, not the doctrine.
3. **The inter-frame / intra-frame boundary** that gates coercion is itself contestable and expandable; it
   needs a bright-line, judicially-policed test, or it becomes the battlefield.
4. **The capital/credit and public-debt modules**, though now designed (§§13, 19), are the least-tested and
   need adversarial stress-testing before any real deployment.
5. **The scale ceiling of trust.** Voluntary, participatory, self-assessed mechanisms held, historically,
   only in small high-trust polities; Axiacracy likely must **federate** — trust-dependent participation
   local, compelled certain charges for the large-scale duties.

The doctrine's wager is simple to state: that a state which **sees value in ten dimensions but compels only
where it can measure honestly, corrects rather than directs, taxes the unearned rather than the earned, and
keeps to the three duties** can hold a civilization in balance through the AI transition where a
money-blind state cannot — and that the way to find out is to build it, break it in simulation, and let
people live in it.
