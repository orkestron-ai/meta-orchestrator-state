## 0. How the organizations layer works

This is where persons meet institutions and institutions meet each other. An organization is one V1 value transformer with exactly one controlling structure resolving through its officers to natural-person beneficial owners (the C1 firm, unchanged), existing constitutively in the commercial register. The layer's first commitment is that **control is determinate and personhood is instrumental**: a shell chain or opaque control structure is a concealment surface, not a privacy choice; limited liability is a default for ordinary risk, not a shield for fraud; and an organization holds no dignity floor, so the non-priceable criminal class never attaches to the entity as a substitute for the culpable humans behind it, including where the harm runs through an autonomous agent the organization deployed.

**Organizations (O1).** The organization is a constitutive register object; its mutable state is a non-authoritative fold over appended R1 entries, the register the single holder of record. The beneficial-owner and concert graph is itself a first-class register maintained by the registrar and read by a sealed evaluator, screened at every gated event against its public-faith projection with a forfeitable stake (the judicial asset-discovery instrument reserved for post-judgment tracing). Concentration-summing and personal identification are two guarantees, never conflated; veil-piercing has one canonical rule (concealment, fraud, domination or undercapitalization against ex-ante peak harm capacity), and honest bonded groups are never pierced.

**Contracts (O5).** A contract is where the market clears: price is what the parties agree on the $ rail, the state prices only the externality residual and never the price itself. But a contract cannot contract around a floor: any waiver of a capability floor, the consent floor, a non-priceable right, the suspensive-contest guarantee or the whistleblower guarantee is void ab initio, and total-life-capture bundling is void and criminal, never reformed into a lawful-looking term. Machine-readable terms carry an authoritative human-readable master that governs on divergence; self-executing contract code is the instrument, a bug is a breach not a fait accompli, with a real reversal affordance required before irreversible settlement.

**Employment (O3, LAB).** The sharpest rights surface: the worker is a natural person holding the full floors, so labor is never an ordinary commodity. The relation is declaratory, its floors binding from the fact of work regardless of registration, so non-registration sheds nothing. The wage clears on the market but never below the delivered capability floor, which is delivered upfront through the standing reserve (never by commanding or suppressing the price), with a below-threshold recapture so the subsidy is not a public gift to wage-suppressing employers. Frictional monopsony is charged, misclassification and the trainee/volunteer relabel are tested by substance with the relation floor-attached pending finding, total-life capture is caught de facto as well as structured, displacement funds a delivered floor upfront, and unpaid household care settles rather than staying invisible; while genuine flexible work, hard-but-fair bargains and merit selection are protected.

**Governance and integrity (GOV, OINT).** Officers owe decidable fiduciary duties (a care breach is an uninformed or grossly negligent process, never a bad outcome from an informed decision), with a named complainant path against a breaching controller, an independent fair-value standard for compulsory minority buyouts, and AI delegation that never launders accountability away from a human bearer. The anti-capture layer carries a level-capture guard so a single steward cannot uniformly relax every threshold in incumbents' favor, an objective test for the front-versus-private-association line, and revolving-door detection independent of self-declaration; while honest business judgment, legitimate group structures and genuine anonymous association are protected.

Three commitments recur, matching the rest of the model:

1. **One controller, one register, one rule.** Control resolves to a determinate concert graph; the register is the single holder of record with the organization a fold over it; veil-piercing, the wage floor, the floor-waiver-void rule, unconscionability and the concert substrate are each stated once and cited, not duplicated.
2. **Floors bind from the fact, delivered not declared.** Employment floors attach from the work relationship regardless of registration or label; the wage and displacement floors are delivered upfront through the standing reserve on the worker's own-account basis, with recapture from the employer that induced them.
3. **Both flanks, everywhere.** The worker, the minority member, the adhesion counterparty and the whistleblower are protected against exploitation, squeeze-out, abusive terms and reprisal; and the honest employer, the genuine contractor, the legitimate group, the informed hard bargain and honest business judgment are protected against over-reach.

The sections follow: O1 organizations; O5 commercial contracts and agreements; O3 employment and membership; GOV organizational governance and control; LAB labor in the value doctrine; OINT organizational integrity and capture.

## Organizations (O1 core)

An organization is a first-class constitutive object: it exists as a legal person only from the moment a constitutive-effect entry is appended to the commercial register (R1 base_effect = constitutive), never from a mere declaration, a letterhead, or a name in use. Before that entry there is no O1 person that can own, contract, sue, or be bound; a purported act "by" an unregistered organization binds the natural persons who performed it. This is the organizations-layer application of the registry pattern: personality is created and mastered by the R1 commercial register, mutated only by appended R1 register events, and extinguished only by a register dissolution or succession event. The organization record (current legal_form, officer set and mandate scope, control-graph state, floor markers) is a non-authoritative fold over those appended R1 entries, never a competing stored copy (R2-I5, materializations are caches with no legal weight); R2 is the value ledger, not the master of personality. Every organization carries one register identity, exactly one declared legal form from a closed enumeration (company, cooperative, non-profit, community, association, institution, partnership, state organ per A3), and a constitutive charter (an N1 pinned document, provenance-tracked per D2) stating its purpose, its organ structure, and its officer mandates. Its legal capacity is instrumental and bounded by that purpose: an act outside purpose is ultra vires and reaches the controllers who directed it, not a limitless corporate will.

The control doctrine is the seam where this layer binds the economy spec. An organization is one V1 value transformer (C1) with EXACTLY ONE controlling structure that resolves, through its officers, to natural-person beneficial owners (C1-I1). Co-control is modeled as governance stakes under a single controller of record, never as two direct controllers of the object (S1, PROP-I1), except for genuine concert-rebutted co-control (a deadlocked joint venture proven independent), which takes an explicit joint-and-several co-control regime rather than a phantom single controller (O1-I19). Unattested control links resolve UPWARD into the concentration-summed high-scrutiny class, never into a void (C1-I5). Concentration-summing and beneficial-owner identification are two distinct guarantees: an unresolved link is always summed upward for concentration, but the natural person is claimed as identified only where the terminal node is within register or asset-discovery reach, and an off-register terminal controller is barred from ordinary limited liability rather than treated as identified (O1-I6). The beneficial-owner and concert graph is itself a first-class append-only R1 register maintained by the registrar and read by the S5 sealed evaluator (O1-I18); at every gated formation, acquisition, or transfer of control the registrar screens against that register's R1 public-faith projection (R1-I28) backed by a forfeitable stake, with the R1-I39 judicial asset-discovery instrument reserved for post-judgment tracing, and concert-screens acting-in-concert parties (kinship, shared financing, option or loan-back arrangements, and the COMP-I9 coordination mechanisms of information exchange, shared or rival-ingesting algorithms, common decision infrastructure, and non-equity governance levers) as presumptively one controller with the burden on them to prove independence; screening is continuous, not only at gated events. A shell chain, an interposed nominee, or an opaque control structure is a concealment surface, not a lawful privacy choice: ultimate-beneficial-owner detail is owner-gated for ordinary reads (S default-deny, cohort-grain state sensing at S5), but it is always resolvable through the A6 judicial_access_contract path and A18 tracing. Officers and organs are distinct binding mechanisms: an organ decision (a resolution of the members' body, board, or council under the charter decision_rule) and an officer act (an individual exercise of a delegated mandate) are separate K2 acts, and an officer binds the organization only within a live S1 delegation bound to the office, with S4 recording both the office and the person, the officer set and mandate scope being the R1 public-faith projection on which counterparties rely (O1-I8). The members (shareholders, cooperative members, association members) hold the governance stake that lives here in O1, the right to compose organs, vote, and appoint or remove officers, while the membership relation itself is O3. And legal personality has hard limits: an organization may own, contract, sue, and be liable, but personhood is a legal construct, so it holds NO A9 dignity floor and no Charter Art. 4 inviolable right, its rights are instrumental and bounded by purpose, and the criminal in-kind, non-priceable class never attaches to the entity as a substitute for the culpable natural persons (C1-I14), including where the harm arises through an autonomous agent the organization deployed, whose culpable bearer is the accountable officer and deployer (GOV-I15, C8).

Limited liability is a default allocation of ordinary business risk, not a shield for concealment, and O1-I12 is the single canonical veil-piercing rule: the veil is pierced (A18-I45) only on concealment, fraud, domination, or undercapitalization relative to attached harm capacity, and an honest, disclosed, adequately bonded group is never pierced. Bare thin capitalization is not a standalone trigger; the wrong is operating a high-harm-capacity domain without the required ex-ante bond or license, and the undercapitalization predicate is capital or escrow below the C1-I16 harm-capacity escrow floor sized to ex-ante peak harm capacity, with a domination and instrumentality prong as an independent basis and post-attachment thinning a presumptive fraudulent conveyance. A dissolved organization's obligations do not vanish with the register tombstone: they follow the successor on merger or division as a PROP-I5 subject-succession (R1-I11 governs register-steward continuity, not subject-entity merger), or run against the estate in the PROP-I12 insolvency waterfall with capability floors, A18 restoration primacy, and location-rent arrears (PROP-I12 step 2 / RENT-I10) protected ahead of ordinary unsecured claims, and a concealed or intentional liability is perpetually clawback-eligible and pierces to the beneficial owner (C1-I14, A18-I49), so an organization cannot dissolve to shed it. A parent-subsidiary group and any acting-in-concert control chain is ONE economic actor for concentration and systemic purposes: the consolidated concert graph is summed across the group for the Art. 10 ceiling, the COMP-I13 demand-side monopsony ceiling, and the COMP-I16 systemic-centrality test, with post-gate activation of a coordination arrangement a new gated event and the sealed evaluator's over-ceiling boolean contestable by selective disclosure to the charged party (O1-I16). Throughout, the frame holds: the market clears and the state prices only the residual, Ae is booked per axis and never netted against the $ rail, unearned rent is charged on an attested-cost benchmark, capability floors are delivered, and every charge against an organization is a reversible provisional booking with suspensive effect on contest.

### Invariants

- **O1-I1 (constitutive existence).** An organization SHALL acquire legal personality only on a constitutive-effect R1 entry in the commercial register (base_effect = constitutive), never by declaration. Before the entry no O1 person exists to own, contract, sue, or be bound, and acts purportedly taken for it bind the acting natural persons. The R1 commercial register masters personality; personality is mutated only by appended R1 register events and extinguished only by a register dissolution or succession event. R2 is the value ledger and does NOT master personality; lifecycle events are R1 entries, and the organization record is a non-authoritative fold over those appended R1 entries (R2-I5, materializations are caches with no legal weight).

- **O1-I2 (identity and legal form).** Every organization SHALL carry one register identity and exactly one declared legal_form from a closed polity-wide enumeration (company, cooperative, non-profit, community, association, institution, partnership, state organ per A3). The current legal_form, officer set and mandate scope, control-graph state, and floor markers are a non-authoritative fold over the organization's appended R1 entries, the R1 commercial register being the single holder of record (R2-I5); no stored organization row competes with the register of record. The form fixes the default governance-stake model and liability regime. Where the form is operated in ACTUAL mismatch with its conduct (a conduct signal, not the mere choice of form) to escape a rent overlay, a concentration charge, or a capability-floor duty, that mismatch is S7 audit_evasion and an A18 offense, with the burden then on the organization to justify the form against its conduct; an honest organization choosing a form for ordinary liability, tax, or organizational reasons carries no justification burden absent such a mismatch.

- **O1-I3 (constitutive charter, bounded purpose).** Every organization SHALL carry a constitutive charter (an N1 pinned document, amendments provenance-tracked per D2) stating purpose, organ structure, and officer mandates. Legal capacity is instrumental and bounded by the stated purpose: an act outside purpose is ultra vires and reaches the controllers who directed it. Charter amendments are new R2 events, never silent edits; governing bylaws implemented as software are N4 objects and do not override the charter of record.

- **O1-I4 (single controlling structure, the C1 firm rule).** An organization is one V1 value transformer with EXACTLY ONE controlling structure resolving through its officers to natural-person beneficial owners (C1-I1). Co-control SHALL be modeled as governance stakes under a single controller of record (S1, PROP-I1), never as two direct controllers of the object. Control links that cannot be attested SHALL resolve upward into the concentration-summed high-scrutiny class, never into a void (C1-I5). The one exception is genuine concert-rebutted co-control (a deadlocked joint venture that has discharged the O1-I5 independence burden): it is not forced into a phantom single-controller fiction but takes the joint-and-several co-control-of-record regime of O1-I19.

- **O1-I5 (determinate control graph, concert-resolved).** Beneficial ownership and control SHALL resolve to a determinate, positively verified graph, never accepted on self-declaration (C1-I5). At every gated formation, acquisition, or transfer of control the REGISTRAR (not an A6 court) SHALL screen the graph against the R1 public-faith projection of the beneficial-owner and concert register (the R1-I28 minimum-projection pattern, O1-I18) backed by a forfeitable stake; the R1-I39 judicial asset-discovery instrument is reserved for post-judgment tracing and is NOT the gated-screening surface. Acting-in-concert parties are presumptively one controller, with the burden on the parties to prove genuine independence. The concert presumption reaches not only kinship, prior coordination, shared financing, and option or loan-back arrangements but also the COMP-I9 coordination mechanisms (information exchange, shared or rival-ingesting algorithms, common decision infrastructure, and non-equity governance levers), so anything summed for collusion is at least screened for control-concert under the same burden-shifted rebuttal. Screening is continuous or periodic, not only at gated events; unverifiable links resolve upward to the concentration-summed high-scrutiny class per O1-I6.

- **O1-I6 (shell chain is a concealment surface).** An opaque control structure, a shell chain, or an interposed nominee that breaks the control graph is a concealment surface, not a lawful privacy choice. Two distinct guarantees apply and SHALL NOT be conflated. First, for concentration: an unattested or unresolved control link ALWAYS resolves upward into the concentration-summed high-scrutiny class, never into a void (C1-I5). Second, for identification: the natural-person ultimate beneficial owner is claimed as resolved only where the terminal node is within register or asset-discovery reach; ultimate-beneficial-owner detail is then owner-gated for ordinary reads (S default-deny, S5 cohort-grain state sensing) but always resolvable through the A6 judicial_access_contract path and A18 tracing, and a false or omitted beneficial-owner declaration is an A18 concealment offense with perpetual clawback and beneficial-owner tracing (A18-I45). Where the terminal controller is off-register and beyond asset-discovery reach, the entity SHALL NOT pretend the UBO is identified and SHALL be barred from the ordinary limited-liability regime: a resident, forfeitable, fully-liable controller of record, or a bond covering the maximum criminal-class restitution for the entity's harm capacity, is required as a condition of formation or continued registration.

- **O1-I7 (organ decision versus officer act).** An organ decision (a members'-body, board, or council resolution under the charter decision_rule and quorum) and an officer act (an individual exercise of a delegated mandate) are distinct binding K2 acts. An officer binds the organization only within the scope of a live S1 delegation bound to the office (A11/O2), and the S4 audit trail SHALL record both the office and the natural person. An act beyond mandate scope, or an organ decision taken without the charter quorum, does not bind the organization and reaches the actor personally.

- **O1-I8 (who may bind, mandate scope per A14).** The set of officers who may bind the organization and the scope of each mandate SHALL be the R1 public-faith projection (R1-I28) on which a counterparty may rely, a fold over the appended R1 register entries (R2-I5), not an independently stored roster. A regulated activity requiring an A14 permit binds the organization only where the permit is held and unexpired; acting without it is ultra vires and an A14/A18 matter. Apparent-authority reliance is protected only to the register-surfaced mandate, never beyond it.

- **O1-I9 (members and the governance stake).** The members (company shareholders, cooperative members, association members) hold the governance stake: the right to compose organs, vote under the charter decision_rule, and appoint or remove officers. This governance stake lives in O1; the membership relation (admission, exit, member obligations) is O3. A governance stake is itself a property object with a single controller (PROP-I2) and SHALL be concentration-aggregated to the declared beneficial owner (C1-I5).

- **O1-I10 (legal personality and its limits).** An organization MAY own, contract, sue, and be liable as a legal person, but personhood is a legal construct, not a natural person. An organization SHALL hold no A9 dignity floor and no Charter Art. 4 inviolable right; its rights are instrumental and bounded by its purpose (O1-I3). The non-priceable criminal in-kind class SHALL NEVER attach to the entity as a substitute for the culpable natural persons (C1-I14): the organization bears standing_loss, priced debits, clawback, and dissolution, while the criminal ladder pierces to the culpable officers and owners (A18-I45). Where a criminal-class harm or floor breach arises through an autonomous agent the organization deployed, the culpable natural-person bearer is the accountable officer and deployer per GOV-I15 and C1-I14: the delegation grade floors at the maximum harm grade of the delegated domain, a named human personally underwrites the agent's floor-compliance envelope, no autonomous-agent path may leave such harm without a natural-person bearer, and where no other bearer exists the deployer holds it in kind (C8).

- **O1-I11 (dual-ledger bearer, never netted).** As a C1 firm the organization carries the C1/FVAL two-ledger position and the MVAL overlay unchanged, by reference and without in-layer restatement: the two never-netted ledgers and no cross-axis buy-back into a floor (C1-I2, C1-I4), the rent charge measured on the independently attested cost counterfactual (C1-I7, MVAL-I7), positive-spillover credits under the additionality and attestation discipline (FVAL-I7), the uniform published overlay carrying no per-firm verdict (MVAL-I5), and every same-tick anti-value booking a reversible provisional debit or freeze with suspensive effect on contest (C1-I10, A18-I34).

- **O1-I12 (limited liability is a default; the single canonical veil rule).** Limited liability is a default allocation of ordinary business risk, not an entitlement, and O1-I12 is the single canonical veil-piercing invariant for the layer (OINT-I3 reduces to a reference to this rule plus its adversarial narrowing gloss). The veil SHALL be pierced (A18-I45) ONLY on concealment, fraud, domination, or undercapitalization relative to attached harm capacity; honest limited liability and a genuine group are never pierced. (a) Bare undercapitalization is NOT a standalone, intent-free trigger: operating a high-harm-capacity domain WITHOUT the required ex-ante bond or license is the wrong, while an honestly bonded, disclosed, thin-capital venture that simply fails is protected under OINT-I11, with victims' floors delivered from the COMP-I17 standing reserve, not from the owner's person. (b) A domination and instrumentality prong is an independent piercing basis: no independent business substance, use as a mere conduit, commingling of assets, or disregard of separateness pierces even where formalities and disclosure were observed. (c) The single mechanical undercapitalization predicate is capital or escrow below the C1-I16 harm-capacity escrow floor for the attached harm capacity, sized to ex-ante PEAK plausible harm capacity, not the contemporaneous figure; there is no separate standalone undercapitalization ratio (any distinct group ratio is defined only as a multiple of the C1-I16 floor). A distribution thinning the entity below its latent-harm capacity is a presumptive fraudulent conveyance under A18-I45 even before a specific harm attaches. On piercing, restoration and clawback reach the parent and the natural-person beneficial owner personally, and the criminal class attaches to the culpable persons, never to the entity as a buy-out (C1-I14).

- **O1-I13 (organizational domination and the theta trigger).** An organization's combined provision of employment plus two or more essentials (housing, credit, essential supply) to a dependent cohort with no reachable substitute is total-life capture in substance, structured OR de facto, regardless of contractual bundling, and is the non-priceable A9/A18 criminal-class offense that cannot be bought out (C1-I15). The exit-foreclosure-in-substance definition, its burden-shifted reachable-exit rebuttal, and its mechanical opening trigger are owned by O3 (C13) and bind here by citation. A single organization's cross-market capture of one dependent cohort aggregates as a concentration or theta trigger independent of per-market share, so organizational scale confers no immunity, and the theta cross-market aggregation escalates to the C1-I15 criminal class on proven de facto foreclosure. The participation-condition theta on a served or dependent cohort is otherwise a diagnostic, not a freestanding charge (C1-I12); paired with an independently measured V4 harm it prices the residual, and an Art. 6 cohort-floor breach routes to cohort restitution plus the licensed hard ladder (A18-I44).

- **O1-I14 (lifecycle as register events).** Formation, charter amendment, merger, division (de-merger, spin-off, carve-out), conversion of form, dissolution, and insolvency wind-down are all constitutive R1 events, sequenced append-only and traced as X1 occurrences, each surfacing the control-graph and floor-marker deltas to relying parties. A merger or division is a subject-succession event: rights, obligations, contracts, permits, and every cross-register dependent (property collateral_refs, O5 agreements, A14 permits) SHALL map atomically to the successor as one PROP-I5 multi_leg_commit, and if any dependent cannot be re-mapped the event fails closed. R1-I11 governs register-steward continuity, not subject-entity succession, and is NOT the citation for this re-map (reconciled with OINT-I4).

- **O1-I15 (obligations survive dissolution).** A dissolved organization's obligations and liabilities SHALL NOT vanish with the register tombstone. They follow the successor on merger or division as a PROP-I5 subject-succession (not R1-I11, which governs register-steward continuity), or run against the estate in the PROP-I12 insolvency waterfall with capability floors and A18 restoration primacy protected first. A concealed or intentional liability is perpetually clawback-eligible and pierces to the beneficial owner (C1-I14, A18-I49), so an organization cannot dissolve to shed it; standing_loss and clawback follow the beneficial owner regardless of the entity's death.

- **O1-I16 (groups: one economic actor for concentration).** A parent-subsidiary group and any acting-in-concert control chain SHALL be treated as ONE economic actor for concentration and systemic purposes. The consolidated concert graph (O1-I18, C1-I5) is summed across the group for the Charter Art. 10 concentration ceiling, the COMP-I13 demand-side and monopsony ceiling, and the COMP-I16 systemic-centrality test; intra-group fragmentation to stay sub-threshold is presumptively summed with the burden on the group and is an aggravating A18 basis. Post-gate activation of a coordination arrangement among previously cleared sub-threshold holders is itself a new gated control event that re-tests this aggregation, and failure to declare newly-formed concert is an A18 concealment offense with the perpetual flip (A18-I49). Consolidation runs through the S5 sealed evaluator, which emits only over-ceiling booleans and charge totals, never the underlying holdings; but the boolean is contestable: the charged party SHALL receive selective disclosure of the specific concert links and aggregation grounding its OWN charge (third-party holdings stay sealed), sufficient to exercise A18-I34 contest, and an independent audit path for the evaluator's summing logic SHALL make a mis-sum discoverable without exposing the sealed inputs.

- **O1-I17 (wind-down floors and essential continuity).** An organization's insolvency wind-down SHALL run the PROP-I12 waterfall (essential housing and goods at the capability floor carved out ahead of all claims; then A18 restoration primacy; then accrued location-rent arrears, which ride with the asset ahead of ordinary unsecured claims and never fall into the pro-rata pool, PROP-I12 step 2 / RENT-I10; then security interests by perfected rank; then unsecured claims pro rata; then residual) and SHALL NOT push any dependent natural person (worker, occupier) below the A9 dignity floor or a delivered capability floor; a displacement cohort's delivered floor binds beyond the organization's death (C1-P4, K8-I11), funded from the standing commons reserve so delivery is decoupled from same-period recovery (COMP-I17). A systemic organization in an essential class above the systemic-cascade threshold (GOV-I13, COMP-I16) SHALL maintain the pre-committed, ring-fenced, transferable essential-function unit that continues the function through resolution; a smaller essential provider below that threshold owes only a proportionate continuity plan (O3-I11, GOV-I13), not the full ring-fenced transferable unit.

- **O1-I18 (beneficial-owner and concert register).** The concert and beneficial-owner graph SHALL be a first-class append-only R1 instance (a beneficial-owner and concert register) maintained by the commercial registrar, materialized as an R2-style fold that is authoritative ONLY as the latest sealed fold plus the legs appended since (R2-I5), revalued by appended edges at each gated formation, acquisition, or transfer of control and reconciled at the segment-seal cadence (that cadence a D-layer defaults-annex parameter under DVAL). The S5 sealed evaluator is the named reader of this register for group consolidation (O1-I16). Gated-event screening reads the register's R1 public-faith projection (R1-I28), never the R1-I39 judicial instrument; the register masters the control-graph state that O1-I2 folds and O1-I8 surfaces.

- **O1-I19 (concert-rebutted co-control and deadlock).** Where two or more unrelated controllers hold genuine, concert-rebutted co-control (a deadlocked joint venture that has discharged the O1-I5 independence burden), the layer SHALL NOT collapse them into one controller nor accept a hollow nominee. The co-controllers are jointly and severally liable as controllers regardless of which is designated controller of record; a controller of record with less resourcing or harm-capacity coverage than the venture demands, or a judgment-proof nominee, is barred from the role; and the closed legal_form enumeration (O1-I2) SHALL carry an explicit deadlock or co-control form so the case takes a real form rather than a forced single-controller fiction. The concentration of each co-controller aggregates to that co-controller under O1-I16, never to a phantom.

### Protocols

- **O1-P1 (formation and constitutive registration).** (1) Validate the charter: purpose, one legal_form from the closed enumeration, organ structure, officer mandates (O1-I2, O1-I3). (2) The registrar (not an A6 court) positively screens the beneficial-owner graph against the R1 public-faith projection of the beneficial-owner and concert register (R1-I28, O1-I18), requires a forfeitable stake, and concert-screens including the COMP-I9 coordination mechanisms (O1-I5); the R1-I39 judicial instrument is not used here. Unverifiable links default to the high-scrutiny concentration-summed class, and an off-register terminal controller triggers the O1-I6 bar from ordinary limited liability. (3) Append the constitutive R1 entry (base_effect = constitutive) creating personality, and surface the mandate scope and control-graph minimum in the public-faith projection. A formation presenting an opaque control chain or failing owner verification fails closed or registers only under the high-scrutiny class with the O1-I6 resident-controller or bond condition.

- **O1-P2 (officer act and organ decision).** (1) Resolve the actor's live S1 delegation bound to the office and its mandate scope (O1-I7, O1-I8); for a regulated act confirm the unexpired A14 permit. (2) For an organ decision, verify the charter decision_rule and quorum. (3) Bind the organization only within scope, recording both office and natural person to S4; an out-of-scope act or an inquorate decision reaches the actor personally and does not bind the organization.

- **O1-P3 (control-graph resolution and concert screening).** (1) At every gated formation, acquisition, or transfer of control, and on a continuous or periodic re-screen between gated events, the registrar re-verifies the beneficial-owner graph against the O1-I18 register projection (R1-I28) and concert-screens for acting-in-concert parties including the COMP-I9 coordination mechanisms (O1-I5); post-gate activation of a coordination arrangement among cleared sub-threshold holders is treated as a new gated control event (O1-I16). (2) Consolidate the group under the S5 sealed evaluator and test the Art. 10, COMP-I13, and COMP-I16 ceilings (O1-I16), returning only over-ceiling booleans and charge totals, with selective disclosure to the charged party of the specific links and aggregation grounding its own charge (O1-I16). (3) On an opaque chain, a false or undeclared-concert declaration, or a capacity mismatch, open A18 tracing through the A6 judicial_access_contract (O1-I6, A18-I45, A18-I11).

- **O1-P4 (veil piercing).** (1) On a concealment, fraud, domination, or undercapitalization signal (a subsidiary capitalized or escrowed below the C1-I16 harm-capacity escrow floor for its ex-ante peak harm capacity, operation of a high-harm domain without the required ex-ante bond or license, a domination or mere-conduit pattern with no independent business substance, or a post-attachment value or harm-capacity transfer), test the form against actual conduct with the burden on the organization (O1-I12); bare honest thin capitalization with the required bond and full disclosure is not a trigger. ## Commercial contracts and agreements (O5 core)

A commercial contract is the instrument by which two or more parties bind themselves to an exchange, and in this model it is a governed first-class object on the N1 pinned-document discipline, not a private paper the polity can neither see nor reason about. Its parties resolve through the S1 control graph (an organization contracts only through its authorized officers, and is itself one V1 value transformer with one controller answering for it per O1 and C1-I1); the object carries subject matter, the obligations of each side, the consideration, a performance status folded from its X1 event stream, and a term. Formation is not a mood but a pair of K2 acts, an offer and its acceptance, whose meeting is an X1 event the register timestamps: the moment a binding obligation comes into existence. A drafted term sheet confers none of the effect of a formed contract (N1-I6), and a purported contract made without capacity or without the S1 authority for the party bound is void ab initio (S1-I4).

A contract is where the market clears. The consideration is a bargained-for exchange in any lawful form, a promise, a forbearance, goods or a service; where a leg is priced it is denominated, cleared and settled on the $ rail, and no organ SHALL set, cap or fix that price (MVAL-I1, C2-I3). No Ae credit is ever the consideration a party pays or receives (FVAL-I3), the two rails never netting. What the state does price is the externality residual the transaction leaves, through the uniform published overlay applied identically to every flow and carrying no per-firm verdict (MVAL-I5). The consideration is the parties' business; the residual is the polity's, and the parties cannot bargain the residual away. From this the hard limit follows: a contract cannot contract around a floor or a mandatory rule. A clause purporting to waive a delivered capability floor, to strip the consent floor the privacy machinery guarantees, to waive the suspensive-contest or whistleblower guarantee, or to disclaim an anti-value liability the flow generates is void ab initio, severed from the instrument while the lawful remainder stands.

The section protects the honest bargain as fiercely as it strikes the abusive one. A hard, even punishing, deal between informed equals with a genuine exit is a market working and is enforced as written; only power-abuse terms are reached. Total-life capture and lock-in (C1-I15), a contract structured to foreclose a party's exit by bundling their livelihood, housing, debt and essential supply, an adhesion term that captures a floor, and any non-priceable-right waiver are VOID, never reformed, and route in kind to the A18 criminal class that cannot be bought out; only a priced unconscionable penalty is void or judicially reformed. A cross-market capture of one dependent cohort aggregates as a concentration or theta trigger no matter how small each market is, and the concentration charge attaches to unrebutted pricing power, never to size. Good faith binds formation; active misrepresentation, a misleading half-truth, or concealment of a material fact routes to the A18 concealment class, whose perpetual-limitation flip and clawback attach only on the hard-rung gate (A18-I49), an honest non-intentional non-disclosure carrying prospective correction and restitution instead; caveat emptor survives only inside that bound. A dispute clause is honored for a garden-variety commercial dispute, but it can never oust the judicial path for a rights matter or a mandatory floor, and its arbitrability and kind classification are reserved to A6. And where the contract's performance is governing software, the code is the instrument: its integrity is the contract's integrity, a bug is a breach to be remedied and not a fait accompli, and a real reversal affordance is a registration precondition.

### Invariants

- **O5-I1 (contract as a first-class pinned instrument).** A commercial contract SHALL be a governed first-class O-layer object on the N1 pinned-document discipline, carrying its parties (each resolved through the S1 control graph, S1-I1), its subject matter, the obligations of each side, the consideration, a performance_status that is a non-authoritative fold over its append-only X1 event stream (never an independently stored field, per the R2-I5 caches-have-no-legal-weight discipline), and a term. Its authoritative content SHALL be mastered in exactly one holder of record and referenced by immutable content digest, never a competing second copy (N1-I2); every consumer and every downstream instrument pins that digest (N1-I16). A drafted or negotiated term sheet confers none of the effect of a formed contract and attracts no reliance (N1-I6).
- **O5-I2 (formation is two K2 acts meeting in an X1 event).** A binding obligation SHALL come into existence only on the meeting of an offer and an acceptance, each a K2 act attributed to a party with capacity and authority, their meeting recorded as an X1 formation event with an independently witnessed received_at fixing the moment of binding (N1-I6). Formation SHALL require a definite offer, an acceptance on its terms, bargained-for consideration in any lawful form (a promise, a forbearance, an in-kind or reciprocal exchange), and the capacity of every natural-person party and the authority of every officer acting for an organization (S1-I4). Any monetary consideration is $-denominated and no Ae is ever consideration (O5-I5); a $-rail movement is NOT a precondition of formation, so an enforceable gratuitous undertaking and a barter or in-kind bargain form and bind on their own consideration. A purported contract formed without capacity or without the S1 authority for the party bound is void ab initio and attracts no reliance protection at any tier (S1-I4, N1-I6). A minor's or an incapacitated person's contract is VOIDABLE at the protected party's election, not void and not pending a prior finding, with necessaries binding and capacity a contestable element only when raised; the A18 protected-class discipline governs an exploitative bargain.
- **O5-I3 (parties resolve through the control graph; organizations contract through officers).** Every party SHALL resolve to exactly one S1 controller, and an organization SHALL bind itself only through an officer acting under a valid S1 delegation for the transaction class, the organization being one V1 value transformer with one controller answering for it (O1, C1-I1). Where a party is an affiliate or an acting-in-concert group, the counterparties and any concentration test SHALL concert-sum them with the burden on the group to prove independence (C1-I5). Consideration priced between affiliates SHALL be re-stated at arm's length before it enters any residual or rent base (C1-I7 benchmark, K8-I3 pattern).
- **O5-I4 (registration and effect class).** A contract whose effect the law makes registrable SHALL draw that effect from its R1 register entry under the entry's A12 effect class, declaratory, probative or constitutive (R1-I25, R1-I26, R1-I27), the registered instrument being R1/R5 evidence of the entry and never a source of truth competing with the register (N1-I10). A constitutive class (for example a transfer of a registered asset) takes effect only on the register entry; an unregistered agreement is a first-class private record binding its parties from formation for whatever its content and attestations support, but SHALL NOT acquire an official effect it was never registered for (N1-I11). The employment and membership relation is not a commercial contract of this section: it is declaratory and binds from the fact of the work relationship regardless of registration, and O3/LAB own it (see Interfaces).
- **O5-I5 (the market clears the price on the $ rail).** The consideration of a contract SHALL be whatever the parties agree; where it is priced it is denominated, cleared and settled $-only (C2-I3), and no organ SHALL set, cap or fix a transaction price (MVAL-I1). A non-priced bargain (barter, in-kind or reciprocal consideration, an enforceable gratuitous undertaking) is a valid consideration and does not require a $ movement to form; any priced leg of it still settles $-only. Ae SHALL NEVER be the consideration a party pays or receives, is booked-not-transferred per axis, and SHALL NOT net against the $ price (FVAL-I3, MVAL-I9). A contract term that would draw consideration from, or purport to transfer, the Ae ledger fails closed.
- **O5-I6 (the state prices the residual, not the exchange).** The externality residual a transaction leaves SHALL be priced by the uniform, published, per-flow-identical overlay, which attaches after clearing and carries no per-firm verdict (MVAL-I1, MVAL-I5); anti-capture rests on uniformity plus the steward-mastered parameters (MVAL-I6), never on a per-contract judgment. The residual charge books on the no-double-charge embodied-anti-value ledger (MVAL-I2, K8-I4). A term purporting to extinguish, cap or reallocate the residual charge away from the party that generated it is void as to the residual (the commissioner of directed production stays jointly and severally liable, K8-I13); the parties MAY allocate the residual's cost between themselves on the $ rail but SHALL NOT reduce what the polity is owed.
- **O5-I7 (no contracting around a floor or a mandatory rule; the canonical floor-waiver-void rule).** This is the single canonical floor-waiver-void rule of the layer: a contract clause is void ab initio, and severed while the lawful remainder stands, to the extent it purports to waive or degrade ANY delivered capability floor (COMP-I17, the civic-layer floors including B10), the consent floor the privacy and knowledge machinery guarantees (S2-I8 consent basis, N3-I19 consent-withdrawal, and the B10/HACC consent primacy for a person-subject), ANY non-priceable Charter right (A18-I3, non-priceable, no buy-out), the A18-I34 suspensive-contest guarantee, or the whistleblower guarantee, or to disclaim an anti-value liability the flow generates (MVAL, K8). No sequence of drafting, incorporation by reference, or choice-of-law recital SHALL revive a term this invariant voids. OINT-I8 cites this rule rather than re-enumerate a divergent subset.
- **O5-I8 (performance status and breach are tracked as X1 events).** The performance_status of each obligation SHALL be a fold over the contract's append-only X1 event stream (R2-I5), not an independently stored field, and every material performance, tender, default, cure and repudiation SHALL be recorded as an append-only X1 event on the contract object (R2). The performance-event materiality bound is a D-layer annex parameter under DVAL. Each obligor SHALL post its own tender and performance events and the counterparty SHALL post its default and repudiation claims, so a breach by omission surfaces as an absent expected event plus the counterparty's claim. A breach is an X1 event, not a bare assertion; it acquires consequence only on the counterparty's recorded claim and, where contested, on an A6 adjudication.
- **O5-I9 (remedies: damages on the $ rail, specific performance, suspensive contest).** The remedy for breach SHALL be restorative first: damages computed from the flow record and settled on the $ rail, or specific performance where damages cannot make the injured party whole, at the least-restrictive level proportionate to the breach. A disputed breach or penalty charge SHALL follow the suspensive-contest discipline: a pre-adjudication charge against a party is a reversible, capped provisional freeze, not a forfeiture, and on contest its accrual freezes until an A6 finding, with automatic restoration-with-interest if the charge is not sustained (A18-I34, A18-I6). A liquidated sum is enforceable if it was a genuine pre-estimate of loss or is proportionate to a legitimate interest in performance, judged at formation; it is an unconscionable penalty subject to O5-I10 only where it is grossly disproportionate to that interest, and the breaching party SHALL NOT defeat a genuine pre-estimate by pointing to a smaller ex-post actual loss. This liquidated-damages test is unified across O5-I9, O5-I10 and OINT-I9.
- **O5-I10 (unconscionability, total-life-capture and lock-in, both flanks).** A priced-class term SHALL be void or judicially reformed where it is unconscionable: a penalty grossly disproportionate to a legitimate interest in performance judged at formation (O5-I9), or an oppressive priced adhesion term. But total-life capture and lock-in (a structure that forecloses a party's exit by bundling employment, debt, housing and essential supply, C1-I15), any adhesion term that captures a capability floor, and any non-priceable-right-waiving term are VOID, never reformed, and route in kind to the A18 criminal class that cannot be bought out (C1-I15, C1-I14, A18-I3, A18-I50); judicial reformation is reserved for the priced class only, because reforming a non-priceable capture would price and cure the very offense the criminal class forbids. A single actor's cross-market capture of one dependent cohort SHALL aggregate as a concentration or theta trigger independent of per-market share (C1-I15), and a take-it-or-leave-it term imposed from a dominant demand position SHALL be tested against the monopsony ceiling (COMP-I13). The mechanical opening trigger for the total-life-capture inquiry and its worker-facing form are owned by O3 (the total-life-capture floor as it bears on a worker) and by C1-I15; final adjudication stays with A6. Both flanks bind: a hard bargain between informed equals with a genuine exit is protected and enforced as written, and the concentration charge attaches to unrebutted pricing-power abuse, never to a party's mere size or to a tough but fair price (C1-I6, COMP-I7).
- **O5-I11 (standard-form and adhesion contracts; machine-readable terms).** A mass-consumer or other adhesion contract offered on non-negotiated terms SHALL carry the fairness overlay: its terms are construed against the drafter, a floor-capturing or exit-foreclosing boilerplate term is void per O5-I7 and O5-I10, and a surprising or oppressive term buried in boilerplate does not bind absent fair notice. Where the terms are published machine-readable, they bind ONLY if carried by an authoritative human-readable master (N1-I2 one-holder-of-record), pinned by digest, that GOVERNS on any divergence and is construed against the drafter; a machine-readable clause with no faithful human-readable counterpart gives no fair notice and does not bind. The terms bind through the LIC licence machinery and the N4 governing-software discipline of the knowledge spec, and a licence term SHALL NEVER forbid the citation, contest or evidentiary use of the instrument (N1-I18). OINT-I9 aligns to this rule with the same surprising-or-oppressive-term-without-fair-notice catch-all and construe-against-drafter rule, and the hardening layer is never weaker than this O5 floor.
- **O5-I12 (good faith and disclosure; caveat emptor bounded).** Formation SHALL be in good faith. Both an active misrepresentation that induces the contract (fraud in the inducement) and a misleading half-truth, not only a pure omission of a duty-bound fact, are record falsification routed to the A18 concealment class, the disclosure-duty categories being illustrative rather than exhaustive of what counts as material formation fraud (N1-I5 pattern). The perpetual-limitation flip and the concealment clawback attach ONLY on the A18-I49 hard-rung gate (intentional, high-confidence, objective, corroborated); an honest, non-intentional non-disclosure carries prospective correction and restitution, not the perpetual, no-limitation class. Caveat emptor survives only within this bound: it does not license active misrepresentation, a misleading half-truth, or the concealment of a material defect, a floor breach, or a fact the disclosing party alone could know and the counterparty could not reasonably discover. An honest, openly disclosed hard term is never an offense.
- **O5-I13 (dispute resolution; the non-ousting limit).** A contract MAY nominate its forum, arbitration or an A6 court, and that clause SHALL be honored for a garden-variety commercial dispute (A6). A dispute clause SHALL NOT oust the judicial path for a rights matter, a mandatory-floor question, or a criminal-class offense. The effective-remedy and forum-neutrality test extends to ANY adhesion party, not only a consumer or a party below the participation condition theta: the arbitral forum SHALL be neutral and not drafter-controlled, and a clause binding an adhesion party to a captured, remedy-foreclosing, or distant-seat forum is void as to those matters, which route to A6 (A18-I3, A18-I44). The threshold arbitrability and kind classification is reserved to A6 (A18-I50): an arbitrator SHALL NOT conclusively self-classify a matter out of the rights, floor or criminal class. The externality residual and any capability-floor breach are never arbitrable and are never settled by a private award (MVAL-I5).
- **O5-I14 (self-executing contracts: the code is the instrument).** Where a contract's performance is N4 governing software, that software SHALL be the instrument: it registers as a governed N4 instance under the N4-I2 append-only release discipline, pinned by digest, its source owner-gated per O5-I16, and its integrity is the contract's integrity. It enrols on the strictest N4-I6/N4-I7/N4-I8 tier (commons publication, independent-rebuilder quorum) ONLY where its output is relied on to gate a strictest-tier state system, as GOV-I14 provides; the blanket strictest-tier-and-transitive-closure mandate does not apply to an ordinary private self-executing contract that gates no state system. A divergence between the code's behavior and the parties' agreed terms is a breach to be remedied on the ordinary ladder (O5-I9), NOT a settled or irreversible outcome: a bug, an exploit, or an undisclosed behavior is not a fait accompli. Reversibility is made real: a registration precondition is an A6-invokable reversal affordance, a pre-committed escrow-hold or dispute window before irreversible settlement, a registered hold key held under S1/A6 reach, or on-rail (not external-immutable-chain) settlement, so the A18-I34 restoration-with-interest path has an asset to reverse; reliance on a self-executing contract whose settlement is technically irreversible before the contest window closes is barred. A change to the governing code is a new version openly superseding the old (N1-I4); a silent edit is concealment (A18-I49).
- **O5-I15 (assignment, novation and succession; no laundering through a shell; the non-assigning party protected).** Assignment, novation or succession of a contract SHALL re-map atomically to the successor controller as one multi_leg_commit that fails closed if any dependent leg cannot be re-mapped (PROP-I5), on a subject-succession basis, the obligations passing intact and traceably; R1-I11 is NOT the basis, as it governs register-steward continuity rather than subject succession (reconcile with OINT-I4). A novation of a party's obligations, or an assignment that materially changes the counterparty's position, SHALL require that party's consent, or, for an adhesion or consumer contract, SHALL preserve every floor, forum and remedy the original conferred, so assignment cannot be used to move the weaker party to a worse counterparty or forum. A transfer of value or of the harm capacity the contract carries out of the conforming S1 graph after an obligation or a residual liability attached is presumptive fraudulent conveyance, with the burden on the transferor and personal standing-loss on proof (A18-I45); the criminal-class consequence does not dissolve with a shell and attaches to the natural-person beneficial owner (A18-I45, C1-I1).
- **O5-I16 (owner-gated reads of the instrument).** Reading a contract's content SHALL be owner-gated like every read in the Dimension: default-deny, only through an S2 access contract producing an S3 projection, each read appended as an owner-enumerable access_event, and additionally subject-enumerable where a party or subject is a natural person (S2-I8, S4-I1). Registration under R1 makes the existence and effect-class of an entry provable without exposing the instrument's private content; there is no research, official or emergency basis for raw access to the contract store (S2-I2).

## Employment and membership (O3 core)

Employment is the relation where a natural person and an organization meet, and it is the sharpest rights surface in the whole organizations layer. The organization is a C1 firm: one V1 value transformer with one controller acting through officers, whose existence is constitutively recorded in the commercial register (R1, R1-I27). The worker is a natural person carrying the full A9 dignity floor and the Art. 6 wellbeing capability floors, and is a value transformer in their own right. The employment relation itself rides an O5 commercial contract that binds its parties from formation; its R1 registration is a DECLARATORY entry (R1-I26), at most probative and never constitutive, recording a relation that exists independently of registration (LAB-I14). The worker is never a mere counterparty: the capability floors and the Charter Art. 12 participation condition theta bind the relation from the fact of the work relationship whatever the contract says and whether or not it is registered; non-registration never sheds a floor. No clause waives a floor; a clause purporting to waive one is void ab initio. This is the load-bearing asymmetry the section is built to hold, and it is defended on both flanks with equal weight, because both a labor market that strips the worker's floors and a regime that criminalizes legitimate, freely-chosen work are catastrophic.

The labor doctrine is that labor is not an ordinary commodity, because the seller is a person with floors. The wage clears on the market like any price on the $ rail (C2-I1 clears, the state never sets the price), but the clearing wage SHALL NOT leave the worker below the delivered capability floor for their cohort. That delivered wage-and-capability floor is owned by LAB (LAB-I7/LAB-P3): where the market clears below the floor the shortfall is delivered, not faked, from the standing commons reserve (COMP-I17) as an income top-up bound person-grain to the identified worker on the worker's own-account basis, exactly as the civic layer delivers a floor rather than declaring it (the B10 and HACC pattern of a funded, reachable floor, never a nominal one). Suppressing or capping the measured price to disguise a floor breach is itself a violation: the price stays honest so the residual can be seen and delivered. The worker books created value in Ae for the realized, citizen-directed contribution of their labor, to their own non-transferable Ae account (never converted to a price, Art. 13); the wage is the separate $ settlement, and the two ledgers never net (C1-I2). Symmetrically, workplace harm, injury, unsafe conditions, overwork past a health bound, and dignity violations book anti-value against the employer on the relevant V4 and floor axes (K8, MVAL), and no credit on any other axis can buy a dignity or safety breach back (C1-I4).

Participation through work is a route, never a coercion. Theta, the worse-off diagnostic, senses a worker cohort pushed below the participation floor, but it is a diagnostic (C1-I12), routing to overlay recalibration, A7 incentives, or a delivered top-up, not a freestanding charge, unless it coincides with a measured floor breach or proven de facto exit-foreclosure. The hard limit is coerced labor: debt peonage, bonded labor, and total-life capture are the C1-I15 non-priceable A9 and A18 criminal-class offense, with no buy-out (A18-I50), attaching to the culpable natural persons (A18-I45). Total-life capture is exit-foreclosure in substance, structured or de facto: one controller's provision of employment plus two or more essentials (housing, credit, essential supply) to a dependent cohort with no reachable substitute is the offense regardless of any bundling clause, with the burden on the controller to prove a reachable exit. Collective organization is the worker's legitimate answer to employer monopsony: unions and worker collectives are O1 organizations, collective bargaining is protected countervailing power (COMP-I13), and the strike is a protected act, not an O5 breach. Membership beyond employment (cooperatives, associations, professional bodies) carries the member's voice and exit rights on the same terms, and a membership that captures exit is the same lock-in offense. Throughout, the worker's data is owner-gated per the S cluster and the employer's monitoring is bounded, never total.

## Organizational governance and control (GOV)

An organization is one V1 value transformer with one controller acting through officers, existing constitutively in the commercial register (R1-I27, O1). Governance is the machinery that allocates power inside that single controller: the constitutive charter distributes decision rights among the members (who hold ownership), the governing organ (the board or its equivalent), and the officers (who hold control and act as the office). MOS keeps ownership and control distinct. Ownership is the membership or equity claim; control is the S1 graph that resolves the organization's acts to natural-person beneficial owners (C1-I1). The market clears the organization's outputs and the state prices only the residual, so governance is not a price the state sets but a structure the charter declares and the register records, always subordinate to the polity Charter and its A12 mandates. A charter is an N1 record (N1-I1), never a private law that can defeat a floor or a duty the polity Charter withholds.

Officers and the members of the governing organ owe duties of loyalty and care to the organization, and where the charter or a mandate so provides, to the members and to the polity. A breach of care is bad process, an uninformed or grossly negligent decision, not a bad outcome from an honest informed decision, which the business-judgment safe harbor protects (OINT-I11). A breach is an offense against the organization routed to the A18 ladder: an honest, disclosed breach carries suspensive contest and prospective correction with restitution but no anti-value (A18-I34, S1-I14), while self-dealing and concealed diversion are concealment-class carrying the A18-I49 perpetual flip and clawback. Most hidden diversion surfaces as unearned capture (rent) toward a beneficiary, so a related-party dealing is re-priced at arm's length on the attested-benchmark discipline (C1-I7, FVAL-I5, MVAL-I7) symmetrically across too-dear purchases and too-cheap sales and cannot be laundered through padded cost; but a corporate-opportunity diversion that leaves no ledgered flow is still reached, on the organization's counterfactual loss, and a unique-asset transfer requires an independent valuation. Mere beneficial ownership triggers clawback of proceeds regardless of culpability, but the non-priceable criminal class attaches only to culpable natural persons on individualized evidence (C1-I14), and no autonomous-agent delegation may leave a floor breach or criminal-class harm without a natural-person bearer (GOV-I15).

An organization's account is its two-ledger position (C1 firm ledgers): profit_$ on the $ rail and per-axis net_Ae on the Ae vector, booked side by side and never netted or inter-converted (C1-I2, FVAL-I1). Disclosure duties scale with size and systemic centrality (COMP): a small organization's governance stays owner-gated and default-deny, sensed by the state only at S5 cohort grain and reachable per-person only through an S2 judicial-access contract on an A6 case, while an above-threshold or systemically central organization carries a heightened duty whose governing decisions and positively verified beneficial-owner graph are reachable through the judicial path (C1-I5, C1-I12, COMP-I14). Minority members and affected stakeholders are protected: a governance or control transaction structured to strip minority value or to dump anti-value is void or charged, never licensed, and a compulsory minority acquisition rests on an independent fair-value standard (GOV-I9). Both flanks are guarded: the honest, efficient, well-governed organization keeps suspensive contest and privacy, and only oppression, concealment, and un-bondable cascade are pursued.

## Labor in the value doctrine (LAB)

This section binds the value doctrine onto the O3 employment and membership relation: how the employment relation books value honestly. An employment relation is an O3 relation between an O1 firm-controller acting through officers under S1 delegation and a natural-person worker who is themselves a V1 value transformer. The relation and its floors bind from the FACT of the work relationship: the O3 status is a declaratory R1 entry recording a fact that exists independently of registration, at most probative and never constitutive, so no floor is shed by not registering (R1-I26, C1). The worker's labor is created value, booked per axis to the worker's own Ae account on the R2 ledger through a V3 recognition posting, AND an input the firm transforms in its K8 production run. The wage is the $ price that clears the labor market, never the measure of the labor's value. There are two rails, not three: the $ rail carries both the firm's profit_$ and the wage, which legitimately interact (the wage is a $ cost that reduces profit_$), and it never nets or inter-converts against the worker's per-axis net_Ae account (C1-I2, Charter Art. 13); the wage is never an Ae consideration, and the worker's Ae credit is booked-not-transferred (FVAL-I3, R2-I6). Labor is never bought in Ae and the firm can never net its own Ae position against a wage it pays.

Because the two rails are separate, a gap between the worker's created value (Ae) and the wage ($) is the normal, expected condition of the model, not evidence of theft. The doctrine does not equate value with wage, and it does not treat every created-value-minus-wage gap as exploitation: in a competitive labor market the market-clearing wage carries neither a charge nor an offense, the labor-side mirror of the protected free-competition band (C1-I7, COMP-I4). The state does not set, cap, or floor the wage (C2-I1, MVAL-I1); it prices only the residual the price signal cannot internalize. That residual takes three forms. The first is exploitation rent: the margin attributable to demonstrated monopsony power, wage held below the competitive labor price, the demand-side mirror of the C1-I7 pricing-power rent. Monopsony power is never share alone: it is charged where the employer clears the COMP-I13 monopsony threshold, is pivotal for a worker cohort, OR holds frictional monopsony power (elevated worker search and switching costs, low labor-supply elasticity to the firm), always benchmarked against an independently attested competitive labor price, never the employer's own numbers. The second is the displacement externality. No-poach and wage-fixing agreements are cartels, the demand-side mirror of product-market collusion, and route through COMP. The third is the below-floor subsidy incidence: an employer whose workers draw the delivered wage-floor top-up funds a recapture of its share of that subsidy, benchmarked against the same competitive labor price and charged independently of the COMP-I13 threshold, so the below-threshold below-floor employer cannot suppress the reservation wage onto the public purse. Both flanks are load-bearing: the competitive employer, the efficient winner, and the new entrant are protected from any charge, while the wage-suppressing monopsonist and the colluding employer group face both the overlay and the ladder.

The floors are delivered, not commanded, and they do not run through the wage. Where the cleared wage leaves a worker below the Charter Art. 6 capability floor or the A9 dignity floor, C5 computes the shortfall and draws a top-up from the COMP-I17 standing commons reserve sub-account, C6 routes it as a $-rail draw leg with a cause_ref to the floor computation, as a first-charge obligation replenished by recovered charges and decoupled from same-period recovery, never by ordering the wage up or suppressing the price signal: this is the civic-layer delivered-floor pattern applied to pay. Person-grain delivery binds to the worker on the worker's own account (S2-I18), not by state person-grain sensing, so it does not breach the cohort-only labor-sensing rule. Participation through work is one route to contribution, never a precondition of any floor, so the floors do not depend on employment and workfare that conditions a floor on labor is a coercion offense. Labor displaced by automation funds a delivered floor to the identified cohort, delivered upfront from the reserve on identification and decoupled from the suspensive-contest status of the employer's charge, binding past the end of employment (K8-I11, C12). A non-compete is a distinct clause, void unless it protects a genuine, proportionate, compensated interest and neither entrenches monopsony power nor forecloses exit, and aggravating a monopsony finding where it suppresses a cohort's outside options; it is stated separately from a debt, housing, and essential-supply bundle, whose combined provision to a dependent cohort with no reachable substitute is exit-foreclosure in substance, structured or de facto, and total-life capture (the trigger owned by O3). Unpaid care work is created value on the Ae ledger even where no $ clears, so the value map sees it, and genuine household care settles through a lightweight civic attestor rather than lapsing permanently provisional, distinguished from a synthetic inter-organization common-control transfer; it is never anti-value for being unpaid. Hours worked, headcount, and output-per-worker are $-side or operational contract terms and are never, by themselves, a value or a floor basis.

### Invariants

- LAB-I1 (labor is created value, the wage is a price): The worker's labor SHALL be booked as created value per axis to the worker's OWN Ae account through a V3 recognition posting (R2-P1), and simultaneously as a K8 C3 labor input the firm transforms. The wage is the $ price that clears, never the measure of the value. There are two rails, not three: the $ rail carries BOTH the firm's profit_$ and the wage, which legitimately interact (the wage is a $ cost that reduces profit_$), and the $ rail SHALL NOT net or inter-convert against the worker's per-axis net_Ae account (C1-I2, Charter Art. 13). The worker's Ae credit is booked-not-transferred and non-transferable across postings (FVAL-I3, R2-I6), and no wage leg SHALL fund or draw from any Ae account.

- LAB-I2 (the state does not set the wage): The labor market clears on the $ rail and the state SHALL NOT set, cap, or floor a wage (C2-I1, MVAL-I1). The state prices only the residual: exploitation rent where monopsony power suppresses the wage (LAB-I4), the displacement externality (LAB-I11), and the below-floor subsidy incidence it recaptures from the inducing employer (LAB-I18). The wage floor is delivered, never commanded (LAB-I7).

- LAB-I3 (the created-value-minus-wage gap is not per se exploitation): A gap between the worker's created value (Ae) and the wage ($) is the normal state of two non-netting rails and SHALL NOT, by itself, be an offense or a rent. In a competitive labor market the market-clearing wage carries neither an exploitation-rent charge nor an offense: this is the labor-side protected competitive band, the mirror of C1-I7 and COMP-I4. Exploitation rent attaches only per LAB-I4.

- LAB-I4 (exploitation rent is the monopsony wage-suppression margin): Exploitation rent is the margin attributable to demonstrated monopsony power, meaning the wage held BELOW the competitive labor price, the demand-side mirror of the C1-I7 pricing-power rent. Monopsony power is NOT share alone: it SHALL be found where the employer's concert-aggregated demand-side labor-market share exceeds the COMP-I13 monopsony threshold, OR the employer is pivotal for a worker cohort, OR the employer holds frictional monopsony power evidenced by elevated worker search and switching costs and low labor-supply elasticity to the firm. "Pivotal" is defined operationally as non-substitutable for a worker cohort within a reachable labor market: the cohort has no comparable alternative employer reachable without disproportionate cost. Where the independently attested C7 competitive labor price exceeds the paid wage under demonstrated frictional power, exploitation rent SHALL be charged REGARDLESS of the COMP-I13 share threshold (C11). Power is computed on the R1-verified beneficial-owner and acting-in-concert graph (C1-I5, COMP-I14). The counterfactual SHALL use an independently attested competitive labor-price basis (C7), never the employer's own wage or self-reported figures (C1-I7, COMP-I3, MVAL-I7), and the employer's own wage is excluded from its own benchmark above the threshold (mirror C1-I8, COMP-I2). Attested productivity and genuine ability-to-pay differences are credited as created value inside the counterfactual, never confiscated (mirror COMP-I6).

- LAB-I5 (labor cartels): No-poach, wage-fixing, and coordinated pay-benchmark agreements among employers are cartels, the demand-side mirror of product-market collusion, and route per COMP-I8 regardless of any proven agreement, including public wage signaling, employer information exchange, and deployment of shared or rival-ingesting pay-setting algorithms (COMP-I9). Deployment of a wage-setting algorithm producing sustained coordinated below-benchmark pay is an individualized act personally attributable to the deploying beneficial owner (A18-I45, COMP-I9). The concert graph aggregates colluding employers as a single demand-side position (C1-I5, COMP-I13).

- LAB-I6 (monopsony is charged on power, never on size): Being large, or the sole local employer, is NEVER an offense in itself; consequence attaches only to demonstrated wage-suppression power (share-based, pivotal, or frictional per LAB-I4) or exit-foreclosure (mirror COMP-I1, COMP-I7).
