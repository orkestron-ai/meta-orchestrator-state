## 0. How the people-and-society layer works

This is the layer of the living human. Every other model in the world eventually resolves to a natural person who holds floors, bears liability, and cannot be substituted away. The layer's first commitment is that **personhood is not a status the state grants and is not a status the register can revoke**: a human holds the full capability floors, the consent floor, and the dignity floor from the fact of being a living person, delivered up front through the standing reserve, whether or not any birth was ever recorded and whatever label anyone attaches. Registration declares the person; it never creates or un-creates them.

**The natural person (PER, B1).** The person is the single S1 controlling meta-object of their own namespaces (health, life events, their own acts and interactions), anchored by exactly one durable person-identifier that is distinct from every credential that proves it, so a lost or stolen credential is never a lost or stolen person. The capability, consent and dignity floors attach to the person and to nothing that can be substituted for them, and are delivered, not merely declared, on a deduplicated own-account basis while only the count-once civic entitlements wait on the anti-Sybil bijection. The person controls and can correct their own biography without rewriting history, and their post-mortem residue and its defence are provided for even when no successor survives.

**Life events and the civil register (CIV, B12).** The civil register is an R1 instance, the append-only holder of record for the legally-effective events that punctuate a life, with civil status computed only as a fold over appended entries and corrected only by a new event, never an edit. The person owns the events, the registrar holds the register, and the death event carries the highest evidentiary standard of all because a false or concealed death is catastrophic on both flanks; the death cascade fires only on the validated death, and a living human wrongly recorded dead is restored completely and can contest their own death without that act being read as impersonation. Un-coerced consent to marriage is verified privately at booking, prior name and gender entries are sealed self-descriptions, and access to one's own origin is a right, not a defeasible balance.

**Household and family (FAM, B3).** Marriage is a consensual relation between capable persons whose consent, exit right, and floors can never be waived, and the household is an economic unit whose joint holdings are single-controller titled objects held as severable share-claims, never co-control. Family-frame legal effects attach to a family actually lived, not to a bare signature obtained to capture a benefit; the exiting partner's floor is delivered on filing, unpaid care work is booked as delivered value rather than left invisible, and coercive structures (forced and child marriage, dowry capture, economic abuse, sham dissolution against creditors) are void or voidable while genuine diverse families and honest specialization are protected.

**Personal property (HOLD, B11).** The person-side view of ownership over movable, built and financial objects: an estate that is a re-derivable fold over the registers netting only the money rail, never anti-value; an essential-holdings and homestead floor kept whole by substitution through the standing reserve rather than by defeating a valid recorded creditor; and a death-to-succession handoff that tags the corpus and hands it to PROP-I5, which alone states the distribution waterfall. Total-life capture of a person's whole future or body is void and criminal, and a floor that gates an unrecoverable key is delivered by re-provisioning, not by a promise the key can be found.

**Capacity, guardianship and integrity (CAP, PINT).** Capacity is presumed, graded and domain-specific, found and never assumed; guardianship is the one canonical explicit, bounded, auditable S1 delegation (never a silent default), preferring supported over substituted decision-making, with a guardian-inaccessible welfare contact so an isolated ward's silence itself raises the alarm, a named natural-person bearer behind every institution, and restraint-for-convenience and manufactured incapacity treated as capture. The integrity surface closes identity theft, register forgery, ghost and nominee persons, guardianship and family-structure capture, and personal-data weaponization, on both flanks: it protects the genuine victim and denies the fraudster wearing a victim's mask.

Three commitments recur, matching the rest of the model:

1. **Personhood is prior to the register.** Floors bind from the fact of the living human, delivered up front; registration, label, status and even a recorded death never make a living person floorless, and the register administers status, never personhood.
2. **One controller, one record, cited once.** The person self-owns their namespaces; the register is the single holder of record with every view a fold over it; guardianship, the veil and nominee rule, the capacity presumption, the total-life-capture void and the floor-waiver void are each stated once in their owning spec and cited, not duplicated.
3. **Both flanks, everywhere.** The stateless person, the trapped spouse, the looted ward, the outed survivor, the wrongly-dead and the unpaid carer are protected against erasure, coercion, capture and reprisal; and the register, the honest creditor, the good-faith successor and the falsely-accused are protected against ghost claimants, sham transfers, forged records and abusive claims.

The sections follow: PER the natural person; CIV life events and the civil register; FAM household and family; HOLD personal property and the personal estate; CAP capacity, guardianship and the vulnerable person; PINT person-layer integrity and capture.

## PER the natural person: identity anchor, self-sovereignty and the life-cycle spine (B1)

### Purpose

This section models B1, the Citizen, as a LIVING NATURAL PERSON: a biological human being with a biography that runs from birth to death and a residue that persists after death. It is the person-side root of the people-and-society layer. It does NOT model citizenship status, membership, or AI-agent personhood: those are owned by Citizenship-Membership-and-Personhood.md and are cited here, never re-modelled. Where this spec says "person" it means the living human; where status matters it says "citizen/member" and defers.

The purpose is to fix, once and operationally, four things that everything else in the people layer depends on: (1) that the person is the single controlling meta-object of their own namespaces under S1; (2) that there is exactly one durable person-identifier per living human, anti-Sybil, but distinct from any credential that proves it; (3) that the capability floors, the consent floor, and the dignity floor attach to the person and to nothing that can be substituted for the person, and are delivered up front rather than merely declared; and (4) that the person controls their own biography and can correct or annotate their record without being able to rewrite history.

Both flanks are protected throughout. Every rule that shields a vulnerable person against erasure, capture, or silent guardianship is paired with a rule that shields the system against Sybil inflation, false personhood claims, ghost anchors, abusive erasure demands, and identity laundering.

### Object model

**PER-Person.** The root meta-object of a living human. It is a controlling meta-object in the S1 sense (Security-Ownership-and-Access.md), not a data record. It owns, as its subordinate namespaces: the person's B10 health namespace (owner = the person always, per Civic-Health-and-Democracy.md), the B12 life-events namespace, the person's K2 acts (their side of Actions-and-Events.md), and the person's X5 interaction side. The Person is the default sole controller of all of these.

**PER-Anchor.** The one durable person-identifier: a single stable meta-identifier that denotes THIS living human for the person's whole life and into the post-mortem residue. The Anchor is an opaque, non-semantic key. It is DISTINCT from any credential (DID document, biometric template, civil-register entry, national number, login). Credentials PROVE a binding to the Anchor; they are not the Anchor and are individually revocable and rotatable without changing the Anchor.

**PER-Record.** The biographical spine: a non-authoritative R1 FOLD over the append-only civil register and the B12 life-events register (birth, name, capacity determinations, guardianship grants and revocations, major status changes, death) plus the folds those events produce. The authoritative holder of record is the underlying append-only register (see Registry-and-Ledger.md and the A12 civil register); PER-Record is a current-state view over it and is never itself the authority. The term "append-only" is reserved for that underlying register, not for the fold.

**PER-CapabilityProfile.** The person-attributes to which the capability floors attach: the person's wellbeing and capability state as seen by Distributive-Justice-and-Capability-Floors.md. This layer holds the ATTACHMENT POINT (which floors are owed to this person and their V5-frame theta participation state); it does not hold the floor machinery, which stays in the distributive-justice spec and the COMP-I17 standing reserve.

**PER-DignityCore.** A non-priceable, non-transferable attribute of the Person that binds the A18 dignity floor (Offense-and-Enforcement.md) to this specific human.

**PER-CapacityState.** The presumed-full legal-capacity attribute, with a pointer to any graded exception adjudicated by CAP (the capacity/guardianship spec). This layer asserts the presumption and the delegation shape; CAP owns the gradation.

**PER-ContextHandle.** A pseudonymous facet: a context-scoped presentation of the person (a persona, a role-name, a pen-name) that binds to the Anchor underneath but need not reveal it to a relying party. A Person MAY hold many ContextHandles.

**PER-Residue.** The post-mortem object: what persists after death. It comprises the estate handoff (PROP-I5 in Property-and-Ownership.md), the biographical Record (now closed to new first-person acts but not sealed), the person's reputation and memory as protected interests, and still-owed obligations and still-owed liabilities that death does not extinguish. Where the deceased has no qualifying successors, standing to defend the residue's memory and reputation vests in a named public office of last resort (PER-I17).

### Invariants

**PER-I1 (Person is the root controlling meta-object).** Every living natural human SHALL be represented by exactly one PER-Person, and that PER-Person SHALL be the S1 controlling meta-object of the person's B10, B12, K2-own and X5-own namespaces. Purpose: self-sovereignty by default, so that no ministry, firm, or agent is the silent controller of a human's own life-data. Predicate: for each such namespace, `controller == the PER-Person` unless an explicit S1 delegation (PER-I9) is in force. Enforcement hook: any S1 access-contract grant over these namespaces that lacks a live delegation chain terminating at the PER-Person is void and is denied at the S2/S3 read boundary.

**PER-I2 (One anchor per living human, and only one).** There SHALL be exactly one live PER-Anchor per living natural human, and each live PER-Anchor SHALL denote exactly one living human. Purpose: anti-Sybil bedrock on the person side, protecting the vulnerable (no ghost double-registration used to strip someone of floors) and the system (no one person claiming many anchors to multiply votes, floors, or claims). Predicate: the map living-human to live-Anchor is a bijection. This layer does NOT define the proof-of-personhood mechanism that enforces the bijection: that mechanism is the anti-Sybil bedrock of Citizenship-Membership-and-Personhood.md, named here as the controlling dependency (PER-I20) and cited, not re-modelled. Predictable degradation: while that substrate returns a low-confidence result, the capability floors still deliver (PER-I6, PER-I15, PER-I20) and only the count-once civic entitlements are withheld; the layer does not assume a bijection oracle. Enforcement hook: minting a second live Anchor for a human already anchored is a registry-level rejection appended as a VOID event under S4; the concealment of a known duplicate is a CONCEALMENT-CLASS violation under A18 with the A18 perpetual flip.

**PER-I3 (Anchor is distinct from credential).** The PER-Anchor SHALL NOT be any single credential, and no credential SHALL be treated as the Anchor itself. Purpose: protect the person against being erased or locked out when a credential is lost, stolen, revoked, or breached (rotate the credential, keep the human), and protect the system against credential-theft becoming person-theft. Predicate: revoking or rotating any one credential leaves the Anchor and its bindings to other credentials intact. Enforcement hook: any process that would delete or merge a PER-Anchor as a side effect of a credential operation is rejected; Anchor life-cycle events are B12 events requiring their own authority.

**PER-I4 (Anchor stability across the whole spine).** A PER-Anchor SHALL persist unchanged from birth-registration through death and into the PER-Residue, and SHALL NOT be reassigned to any other human. Purpose: the biography, the estate, and still-owed obligations must all hang off one stable spine; reuse would let one human inherit another's liabilities or reputation. Predicate: `Anchor.assignedHuman` is write-once; post-mortem the Anchor transitions to residue-state, never to free-pool. Enforcement hook: reassignment attempts are void at R1; a fold that shows an Anchor pointing at two humans across time is a register-integrity alarm. Collapse of a duplicate Anchor is never a reassignment: it is the append operation of PER-I15/PER-I17, marking the duplicate `status:void-duplicate` and pointing it at the surviving live Anchor.

**PER-I5 (Birth links to the civil register).** Creation of a PER-Person and its Anchor SHALL be anchored to a birth event recorded in the A12 civil register, and the PER-Record's opening entry SHALL cite that register event; CIV-I3 is controlling for the register-side birth-recording standard. Purpose: one authoritative origin, so personhood cannot be fabricated outside the register (protecting the system) and cannot be denied to a truly-born human (protecting the person: a contested or missing birth registration triggers the PER-I15 provisional path, it does not leave the human anchorless). Enforcement hook: the FULL capability-floor set, the consent floor, and the dignity floor (PER-I7) attach to the living human immediately and independent of registration completeness, delivered via COMP-I17 (PER-I6, PER-I15, cross-cite FAM-I11); registration status conditions ONLY the count-once civic entitlements (the vote and the per-capita civic allocation), which wait on PER-I2 bijection confirmation. A PER-Person whose birth event is missing or contested is carried on a PER-I15 provisional anchor, never as a floorless human.

**PER-I6 (Capability floors attach to the person, delivered not declared).** The full capability floors and the consent floor SHALL attach to every PER-Person as person-attributes, and SHALL be delivered up front through the COMP-I17 standing reserve on the person's own-account basis, not merely declared on the Record. Purpose: floors that exist only as a Record annotation are floors on paper; the person layer must expose the ATTACHMENT so the distributive-justice machinery can deliver. Predicate: for each floor f in the floor set, `attached(PER-Person, f) == true` and the delivery obligation routes to COMP-I17. This layer does not re-model theta or the floor set: Distributive-Justice-and-Capability-Floors.md owns the floor magnitudes and theta as a V5-frame participation condition. Enforcement hook: a person shown as anchored-and-alive with an undelivered floor is a distributive-justice breach, surfaced against the standing reserve, not against this Record.

**PER-I7 (Dignity floor inheres in the person and is non-transferable).** The A18 dignity floor SHALL inhere in the PER-DignityCore of every living natural person and SHALL NOT be transferred, waived, sold, delegated, or attached to any entity as a substitute for the person. Purpose: dignity is not a tradable asset and cannot be laundered onto a firm to shield the culpable humans. Predicate: `transferable(PER-DignityCore) == false` and `bearer(dignity) is always a natural person`. Enforcement hook: any instrument purporting to move or waive the dignity floor is void ab initio under the O5 floor-waiver-void rule; criminal-class harms against the DignityCore are prohibited and never priced (A18), and no autonomous-agent path may leave such a harm without a natural-person bearer (PER-I19, PINT-I15).

**PER-I8 (Presumption of full legal capacity gates self-ownership).** Every PER-Person of the polity's age of majority SHALL be presumed to hold full legal capacity per CAP-I1..I5, and this layer adds only its section-local hook: the capacity presumption is what gates the PER-Person's S1 self-ownership of their B10, B12, K2-own and X5-own namespaces, so full capacity is the default controller-competence and any restriction is a specific, adjudicated, graded CAP exception with a stated scope on the Record. Purpose: protect autonomy against paternalistic default-stripping while protecting genuinely incapacitated persons by making restriction a visible adjudicated act, not a silent status. Enforcement hook: acting on an assumed incapacity with no cited CAP determination is an unauthorized act; the burden of proof is on the party asserting incapacity; the person retains suspensive-contest rights (A18-I34). CAP owns the gradation.

**PER-I9 (Guardianship is a bounded S1 delegation, and ambiguity resolves for the ward).** Any exercise of another party's control over a PER-Person's namespaces (for a minor or an incapacitated adult) SHALL be a guardianship of the CAP-I7 shape, and this layer adds only its section-local hook: the guardian's authority is a substitution of the S1 namespace-controller, effective only within the delegated scope, so that every guardian read or write over a PER-Person namespace is denied at S2/S3 when it falls outside that scope. Pending the shared machine-readable capacity-scope vocabulary (owned jointly with CAP, still open), any ambiguity in a delegation's scope SHALL resolve AGAINST the guardian and FOR the ward's retained control under the narrowest reading: a domain not unambiguously enumerated in the delegation is outside scope, and the ward's own act in that domain binds. Purpose: protect the ward against capture-by-guardian and silent scope-creep, and protect an honest guardian by giving them a defined, reviewable mandate. Enforcement hook: each guardian read/write is an S4 event; a guardianship that cannot produce its bounding grant is void; the ward's own future contest of guardian acts is non-waivable (A18-I34) and survives majority or capacity-restoration. On any conflict CAP-I7 governs.

**PER-I10 (Reads are projections, the state sees cohorts not persons).** Reads of a PER-Person's namespaces SHALL be projections under S2/S3 access contracts with default-deny, and state reads SHALL default to cohort-level under the S5 k-anonymity floor. Purpose: protect the person from panoptic state reads while protecting the state's legitimate need to govern populations. Predicate: a per-person state read is admissible only with the person's consent or a judicial S2/S3 access contract; otherwise the state sees only S5 cohorts. Enforcement hook: per-person state reads without consent or judicial contract are denied and logged as S4 events; over-threshold predicates (over-18, solvent, licensed) SHOULD be answered by S6 zero-knowledge proof rather than by disclosing the underlying attribute. This layer cites S2 to S6, it does not re-model them.

**PER-I11 (Pseudonymity and the right to multiple contexts).** A PER-Person SHALL be entitled to hold multiple PER-ContextHandles and to present different personas in different contexts, and no party SHALL compel linkage of a person's ContextHandles except under a judicial access contract or a floor-protecting necessity. Purpose: protect the person's contextual integrity (a pen-name, a support-group persona, a whistleblower identity) against forced correlation, while protecting against pseudonymity being used to escape liability or multiply Sybil identities. Predicate: ContextHandles bind privately to one Anchor; correlation across handles requires either the person's consent, a judicial S2/S3 contract, or an A18 harm/liability nexus. Enforcement hook: forced de-pseudonymization without one of those bases is an unauthorized read (S4 logged); conversely, using a ContextHandle to present as a distinct human for voting, floors, or claims violates PER-I2 and the anti-Sybil bedrock.

**PER-I12 (One-true-anchor governs, many faces are lawful).** The one-durable-anchor rule (PER-I2, PER-I4) SHALL govern for all anti-Sybil, floor-attachment, liability, and single-vote purposes, while the many-ContextHandles right (PER-I11) SHALL govern for presentation and privacy. Purpose: resolve the standing tension between "one true identity" and "many lawful faces" by scoping each to its own domain. Predicate: anything that counts a human once (vote-weight, per-capita civic allocation, floor entitlement, liability bearer) resolves to the Anchor; anything about how the human appears resolves to a ContextHandle. Enforcement hook: a mechanism that counts ContextHandles instead of Anchors for a count-once purpose is malformed and rejected at the mechanism boundary; a mechanism that forces Anchor disclosure for a mere presentation purpose is an over-collection denied under PER-I10.

**PER-I13 (Person controls their own biography).** A PER-Person SHALL be the controller of their own PER-Record and B12 life-events namespace, including who may read which biographical facts, subject to the mandatory-register facts that the A12 civil register requires to be held. Purpose: biography is self-owned, not owned by the recorder, while the state's minimal register of record still stands. Predicate: read access to non-mandatory biographical facts follows the person's S2/S3 grants; mandatory civil-register facts follow A12 and the R1-I28 public-faith projection. Enforcement hook: a reader of non-mandatory biography without a grant is denied; the person cannot suppress a mandatory register fact but CAN annotate it (PER-I14).

**PER-I14 (Right to correct and annotate, never to erase history).** A PER-Person SHALL have the right to append a correction or an annotation to any entry about them, and the underlying register SHALL record the correction as a NEW event, never as an edit or deletion of the prior entry. Purpose: protect the person against living under a false or stale record, while protecting third parties and the integrity of the append-only register against silent rewriting. Predicate: a correction produces `newEvent(corrects: priorEventId)`; the prior entry remains visible in the append-only register and the PER-Record fold reflects the latest. Enforcement hook: any attempt to mutate or delete a prior entry is rejected under the R1/R2 append-only rule; good-faith third parties who relied on the superseded public projection are protected by R1-I28. Abusive or knowingly-false "corrections" are themselves recordable acts and may found an A18 claim.

**PER-I15 (No-anchor and contested-anchor safety net, both flanks).** A living human who lacks an Anchor (unregistered birth, destroyed records, statelessness-at-birth) SHALL be issuable a PROVISIONAL anchor, and CIV-I3 is controlling for the register-side standard; cross-cite FAM-I11 for the dependent-side floor backstop. This invariant composes a delivery duty with a fraud gate:

- Positive-liveness gate: a provisional anchor SHALL be minted only on a POSITIVE liveness/embodiment predicate (a physical-presence attestation or a biometric-liveness attestation), NOT on mere non-duplication. A filing behind which no living human is shown to exist is not mintable.
- Immediate full delivery: on minting, the FULL capability-floor set, the consent floor, and the dignity floor SHALL be delivered to the living human immediately and unconditionally on the person's own-account basis via COMP-I17, deduplicated so that only the duplicate share is clawed back if the anchor is later collapsed.
- Count-once gate only: ONLY the count-once CIVIC entitlements, namely the vote and the per-capita civic allocation, SHALL be withheld pending PER-I2 bijection confirmation. The full capability floors are never withheld on this ground.
- Hard time-box: the provisional state SHALL carry a hard expiry. On expiry without bijection promotion, the count-once civic entitlements remain withheld and the provisional anchor still cannot author consents, WHILE the delivered capability floor continues on its own-account dedup basis regardless (a human does not lose their floors because a clock ran out).
- Consent bar: a provisional anchor SHALL NOT author any third-party-binding consent until PER-I2 bijection promotion.

Purpose: no living human falls entirely outside protection, and the humanitarian path is not a Sybil hole. A real human passes liveness and receives the floors at once; a ghost fails liveness, cannot author consents, and can never draw the count-once civic entitlements. Enforcement hook: provisional-to-live promotion runs the citizenship proof-of-personhood substrate check (PER-I20); a provisional anchor that duplicates a live human is COLLAPSED by appending a B12 event marking it `status:void-duplicate` pointing to the surviving live Anchor, never by delete or merge, so the prior entry stays visible in the append-only register and the fold reflects the void (this reconciles with PER-I3/PER-I4 and mirrors PER-I14). Knowingly filing a provisional anchor behind which no living human exists, not only a duplicate, is a CONCEALMENT-CLASS violation under A18 with the perpetual flip.

**PER-I16 (Death closes the first person, opens the residue, only on the CIV-valid death event).** Death SHALL close the PER-Person to new first-person K2 acts, transition the Anchor to residue-state, and open the PER-Residue, and SHALL NOT extinguish still-owed obligations or still-owed liabilities. This closure SHALL fire ONLY on the CIV-validated death event, cite CIV-I6/CIV-I7 for the corroboration and evidentiary standard, and SHALL NOT fire on a bare appended death assertion. Pending validation the Anchor SHALL stay live, first-person acts remain valid, and the capability floors continue to deliver via COMP-I17, and any interim disposition is reversible. Purpose: protect creditors, dependents, and victims (liability survives death) and protect the deceased and their successors against a still-live identity being used to act, while protecting the living human against a premature or fraudulent death fold stripping their capacity and floors. Predicate: post-validated-death, `canPerformFirstPersonAct(Anchor) == false`; estate handoff runs under PROP-I5; outstanding anti-value and debts settle against the estate. Post-mortem culpability follows the PINT post-mortem-culpability rule (cross-cited, not restated): criminal-class culpability of a sole culpable human is extinguished as to punishment while still-owed anti-value and restitution settle against the succession corpus ahead of heirs, and surviving concert-graph humans remain bearers. Enforcement hook: any new first-person act under a residue-state Anchor is void and is a fraud/impersonation act; a putatively-dead person may contest their own death fold through the standing liveness channel owned by CIV, which restores provisional first-person capacity; the estate-side of succession is owned by Property-and-Ownership.md and PROP-I5, cited here, not re-modelled.

**PER-I17 (Post-mortem residue: memory and reputation, protected but not weaponizable).** The PER-Residue SHALL carry the deceased's reputation and memory as protected interests with defined standing to defend them, and SHALL NOT confer on the residue any living-person right (vote, per-capita civic allocation, floor entitlement, consent capacity, dignity-core in the A18 criminal sense) that the deceased no longer holds. Standing to defend the residue's memory and reputation vests in the deceased's named successors under PROP-I5; where the deceased has NO qualifying successors, that standing SHALL vest in a named public office of last resort, so a person who dies without heirs is not left defamation-exposed and desecration-exposed. Purpose: protect the dead against defamation and desecration through their successors or the office of last resort, while protecting the living against a dead person's identity being used to accrue power or dodge the count-once rules. Predicate: residue interests are defensible by named successors, or failing successors by the office of last resort; residue holds no count-once civic entitlement. Enforcement hook: a mechanism crediting a residue with a live entitlement is rejected under PER-I16; harms to residue memory/reputation found standing claims but are adjudicated as harms to the living successors' (or the office's) protected interest, not as fresh dignity-core harms to the deceased; post-mortem culpability and the settlement of still-owed anti-value against the corpus follow the PINT post-mortem-culpability rule, cited not restated.

**PER-I18 (Emergency access is narrow and loud).** Access to a PER-Person's namespaces without a standing grant, in a genuine emergency (life-threatening B10 health event, safeguarding of a minor), SHALL be permitted only under the X3 narrow-and-loud path and SHALL generate an immediate high-visibility S4 event. Purpose: protect the person in extremis (a locked health record must not kill its owner) while protecting against "emergency" as a pretext for surveillance. Predicate: emergency access is scope-minimal, time-boxed, and post-hoc reviewable; the person or their guardian is notified. Enforcement hook: an emergency read outside the X3 envelope is an unauthorized read and, if concealed, a CONCEALMENT-CLASS violation under A18; the health-record specifics are owned by Civic-Health-and-Democracy.md, cited here.

**PER-I19 (No entity substituted for the culpable human, person-side hook).** For any floor breach, non-priceable right, or criminal-class harm arising against or from a PER-Person, the nominee and veil-piercing question is governed by PINT-I15, and this layer adds only its section-local hook: the culpable-human accountability and the dignity-floor bearer SHALL resolve through the PER-Anchor to at least one identified natural person, so no such event exists without a natural-person bearer reachable via the Anchor. Purpose: close the veil-piercing and nominee escape on the person side. Predicate: for each such event there exists at least one identified natural-person bearer via the Anchor. Enforcement hook: a natural person used as a nominee is an edge in the O1 acting-in-concert graph; the one canonical veil-piercing rule and the "no autonomous-agent path leaves a floor breach without a natural-person bearer" rule govern through PINT-I15, cited here, not re-modelled.

**PER-I20 (Proof-of-personhood substrate dependency and predictable degradation).** The PER-I2 bijection and the PER-I15 provisional-to-live promotion SHALL depend on the proof-of-personhood substrate owned by Citizenship-Membership-and-Personhood.md, named here as the enforcing dependency and NOT re-modelled. This layer SHALL degrade predictably rather than assume a bijection oracle: while the substrate returns a low-confidence result for a given human, the full capability floors, the consent floor, and the dignity floor SHALL continue to deliver via COMP-I17 (per PER-I6, PER-I15), and ONLY the count-once civic entitlements (the vote and the per-capita civic allocation) SHALL be withheld until the substrate returns sufficient confidence. Purpose: a proof-of-personhood outage, backlog, or ambiguity must never strip a living human of their floors; it may only defer the count-once civic entitlements that genuinely depend on uniqueness. Predicate: `floorDelivery` is independent of `substrateConfidence`; `countOnceEntitlement` requires `substrateConfidence == sufficient`. Enforcement hook: a mechanism that withholds a capability floor on the ground of low substrate confidence is malformed and rejected; a mechanism that grants a count-once civic entitlement on a low-confidence anchor violates PER-I2.

### Protocols

**Anchoring at birth.** Executing actor: the A12 civil registrar, or the named-owner automated evaluator for the register. Input: a civil-register birth event (CIV-I3 controlling). Output: an appended PER-Person creation entry and a minted PER-Anchor bound to the newly-registered human via the citizenship proof-of-personhood substrate (PER-I20). Audit: the mint is an S4 event. The dignity floor (PER-I7), the consent floor, and the FULL capability-floor set attach at the moment of live birth, independent of registration completeness (PER-I5, PER-I15), and the COMP-I17 delivery obligation is registered (PER-I6). A minor's namespaces open under a CAP-I7 guardianship (PER-I9). On a predicate failure (for example, a second live anchor for an already-anchored human, or a duplicate detected on promotion), the registrar or automated evaluator appends a VOID event readable under S4 rather than minting, and never deletes or merges.

**Provisional anchoring.** Executing actor: the A12 civil registrar (or the named-owner automated evaluator). Input: a filing for a human lacking an Anchor, plus a POSITIVE liveness/embodiment attestation (PER-I15). Output: a minted anchor carrying `status: provisional`, with the full capability floors, consent floor, and dignity floor delivered at once via COMP-I17 on a deduplicated own-account basis, and the count-once civic entitlements withheld pending bijection. Audit: mint, hard-expiry timestamp, and any collapse are S4 events. On promotion the substrate check runs (PER-I20); on a duplicate the anchor is collapsed by an appended `status:void-duplicate` event pointing at the surviving live Anchor (never delete/merge), and knowingly filing a ghost or a duplicate is a CONCEALMENT-CLASS A18 violation.

**Credential issuance and rotation.** A credential (DID document per W3C DID, a biometric-uniqueness token, a login) is issued as a proof-of-binding to the Anchor, never as the Anchor (PER-I3). Rotation, revocation, and re-issuance are credential-scoped events that leave the Anchor and its other bindings intact. Loss of all credentials triggers a re-binding protocol under the anti-Sybil bedrock, not a new Anchor.

**Capacity determination and guardianship.** Full capacity is presumed (PER-I8, CAP-I1..I5). A CAP proceeding may append a scoped, time-boxed capacity exception and a matching CAP-I7 guardianship whose namespace-controller substitution is bounded by scope (PER-I9); ambiguity in scope resolves for the ward. Restoration of capacity is itself a B12 event that revokes the delegation; the ward's retrospective contest rights survive (A18-I34, non-waivable).

**Biographical correction.** The person files a correction/annotation; the underlying register appends a new event citing the prior entry id (PER-I14); the PER-Record fold updates; superseded public projections remain protected for good-faith reliers under R1-I28. Contested corrections route to adjudication; knowingly-false corrections are recordable acts.

**Pseudonymous presentation.** The person derives a ContextHandle bound privately to the Anchor (PER-I11). Relying parties verify only the predicate they need, preferably by S6 zero-knowledge proof (over-18, solvent, licensed), without learning the Anchor. Correlation across handles requires consent, a judicial contract, or an A18 nexus.

**Death and succession handoff.** A CIV-validated death event (CIV-I6/CIV-I7) closes first-person acts, transitions the Anchor to residue-state, and triggers PROP-I5 succession on the estate side (PER-I16); pending validation the Anchor stays live and floors continue via COMP-I17. Still-owed liabilities and anti-value settle against the estate per the PINT post-mortem-culpability rule. The Record closes to first-person acts but remains open to successor annotations, or annotations by the office of last resort, defending memory and reputation (PER-I17).

### Lifecycle and edge cases

- **Unregistered or destroyed birth records:** a provisional anchor under PER-I15 delivers the full capability floors, consent floor, and dignity floor immediately on a positive-liveness attestation; only the count-once civic entitlements wait on bijection confirmation.
- **Ghost / no-human-behind-it filing:** fails the PER-I15 positive-liveness gate, so no anchor is minted; a filing knowingly made behind no living human is a CONCEALMENT-CLASS A18 violation with the perpetual flip.
- **Provisional state expiry:** on the hard time-box lapsing without bijection promotion, the delivered capability floor continues on its own-account dedup basis, but the anchor still cannot author consents and still draws no count-once civic entitlement.
- **Suspected duplicate anchors:** the later anchor is collapsed on discovery by an appended `status:void-duplicate` event pointing at the surviving live Anchor (append, never delete/merge, PER-I15/PER-I4); knowing concealment of the duplicate is a CONCEALMENT-CLASS A18 violation with the perpetual flip.
- **Identity theft / impersonation:** a credential is compromised, not the Anchor; rotate the credential (PER-I3), and acts by the impostor are void and found A18 claims by the true person, whose suspensive-contest right (A18-I34) is non-waivable.
- **Presumed dead who is alive (and the reverse):** the death fold fires only on the CIV-valid death event (PER-I16); a live human contests their own death fold through the CIV standing liveness channel, which restores provisional first-person capacity; a wrongful death entry is corrected by a new event (PER-I14), with R1-I28 protecting third parties who relied in good faith and PROP-I5 governing the estate unwinding.
- **Long-term incapacity vs temporary incapacity:** both are scoped CAP exceptions with CAP-I7 guardianships (PER-I9); temporary incapacity auto-expires; neither becomes a silent permanent default; scope ambiguity always resolves for the ward.
- **Whistleblower or protected persona under threat:** the person's ContextHandle stays uncorrelated (PER-I11) and the whistleblower guarantee is non-waivable; forced de-anonymization requires a judicial access contract and is loudly logged (PER-I10, S4).
- **Statelessness at birth:** anchoring (this layer) is decoupled from citizenship status (Citizenship spec); a stateless-at-birth human still receives an Anchor, the full capability floors, the consent floor, and the dignity floor; membership status is resolved separately.
- **Proof-of-personhood substrate degraded:** floors keep delivering via COMP-I17; only the vote and the per-capita civic allocation are deferred until confidence returns (PER-I20).
- **Post-mortem defamation:** founds a standing claim over PER-Residue memory/reputation (PER-I17), brought by named successors or, where there are none, by the named public office of last resort, adjudicated as a harm to their protected interest, not as a fresh dignity-core harm to the deceased.
- **Death of a sole culpable human:** criminal-class punishment is extinguished, but still-owed anti-value and restitution settle against the corpus ahead of heirs and surviving concert-graph humans remain bearers, per the PINT post-mortem-culpability rule (PER-I16/PER-I17 cross-cite).
- **Nominee human fronting for an entity:** the human is folded into the O1 acting-in-concert graph via PINT-I15 (PER-I19); TOTAL-LIFE-CAPTURE bundling of a person's floors, rights, and future acts is VOID and CRIMINAL under the O5 total-life-capture rule, never judicially reformed.

## CIV life events and civil status: the civil register (B12)

### Purpose

This section models B12, the civil register: the append-only holder of record for the legally-effective events that punctuate a natural person's life, being birth, death, marriage, divorce, adoption, legitimation, gender marker change, name change, and the derived civil-status transitions. It fixes who owns the events (the person), who holds the register (the civil registrar under an A12 mandate), how an event is booked (a K2 act that appends an X1 event and emits certificates as R1 pinned instruments / N1 documents), and how the death cascade fires without re-modelling succession, title, health, or citizenship, which live in their owning specs.

This layer is the single owner of the death-corroboration and evidentiary standard for the whole people-and-society layer. CIV-I6, CIV-I7, and CIV-I15 define when a death is VALID and what attestation it takes; every other section that consumes a death (PER residue-state closure, HOLD estate freeze, PINT succession-timed detection) SHALL cite those invariants by ID and condition its own action on the CIV-valid death event, never on a bare appended assertion.

The layer takes the PERSON-SIDE view. It defers title and PROP-I5 succession to Property-and-Ownership.md and Property-Rent-and-Commons.md; the relational and contractual substance of marriage to the family layer (FAM); the health record to Civic-Health-and-Democracy.md (B10); citizenship and membership status to Citizenship-Membership-and-Personhood.md; the register mechanics to Registry-and-Ledger.md (R1/R2); the guardianship shape to CAP-I7; access mechanics to Security-Ownership-and-Access.md (S1 through S6, X3); and the harm surface to Offense-and-Enforcement.md (A18).

Both flanks are protected throughout: every rule that shields a vulnerable person against erasure, coercion, or a false record is paired with a rule that shields the register and third parties against a false, fraudulent, or abusive claim. The civil register is a load-bearing anti-Sybil and anti-fraud instrument precisely because it is also a dignity instrument; neither purpose may be sacrificed to the other.

### Object model

- **B12 civil register**: an R1 INSTANCE. Append-only entry log; the register is the single holder of record; the queryable "civil status of person P today" is a non-authoritative FOLD over appended entries, never a stored mutable row (per R1). Kept by the civil registrar under an A12 keep-mandate.
- **Life event (CIV-event)**: a K2 act with legal effect. Booking it appends exactly one X1 event to the register. Subtypes: `birth`, `death`, `marriage`, `divorce`, `adoption`, `legitimation`, `gender-marker-change`, `name-change`, `annulment`, `correction`, and `recognition` (of a foreign event).
- **Person-record**: the B12 fold keyed to one natural person, opened by a `birth` event (or a late-registration event), whose controlling meta-object under S1 is the person themselves once of age and capacity, subject to bounded guardian delegation (CAP-I7) before then.
- **Civil status**: the derived fold-projection over a person-record: `single | married | divorced | widowed`, plus current legal name, current gender marker, current parentage links, and liveness (`living | deceased`).
- **Certificate (CIV-cert)**: a projection of one or more appended entries into an R1 pinned instrument / N1 document (birth certificate, marriage certificate, death certificate, extract of civil status). A certificate is a snapshot with public faith, not the holder of record.
- **Parentage link**: a person-side pointer from a person-record into FAM, asserting legal parent-child relation. B12 records the STATUS fact of parentage; FAM owns the relationship substance.
- **Correction event**: a `correction` subtype that supersedes an earlier entry in the fold without deleting it, under due process.
- **Safety-seal**: a seal category over prior gender-marker and name entries (and the old-to-new linkage) that removes them from every ordinary projection, disclosable only to the person or under a judicial access contract with cause (CIV-I23).
- **Liveness-assertion channel**: a standing channel, keyed to the person's S6 liveness predicate, by which a person whose record has folded to `deceased` may contest their own death and freeze the succession cascade (CIV-I8).

### Invariants

**CIV-I1 (Register is an R1 instance, holder of record).** The civil register SHALL be an R1 instance: append-only, with civil status computed only as a fold over appended entries. No process SHALL store a mutable "current civil status" as the authority; any cached status is a non-authoritative projection. *Purpose*: single source of truth, tamper-evidence, and a complete correction history. *Predicate*: `authoritative_status(P) == fold(entries(P))`. *Hook*: any write path that mutates a status field in place instead of appending fails R2 ledger validation and is void.

**CIV-I2 (Person owns the event, registrar holds the register).** For every life event, the controlling meta-object of the EVENT-as-it-pertains-to-a-person SHALL be that person (S1 self-ownership of their B12 namespace); the controlling meta-object of the REGISTER as an institution SHALL be the civil registrar. Neither owns the other's object. *Purpose*: separate the person's biographical self-ownership from the state's custodial duty, so the state cannot claim to own the person's life and the person cannot unilaterally rewrite the public register. *Hook*: a registrar read of a person's non-public event detail requires consent or a judicial access contract (S2/S3); a person's demand to alter the register routes to the correction protocol (CIV-I13), not to a direct write.

**CIV-I3 (Registration is declaratory, not constitutive, of the person).** A natural person SHALL hold the full capability floors and the consent floor from birth in fact, whether or not any birth event has been recorded. Registration declares the person; it does not create the person. This mirrors the O3 declaratory-employment principle. *Purpose*: an unregistered or never-registered human is never floorless, never a non-person, never outside COMP-I17 delivery. *Predicate*: `floors_held(P) := is_natural_person(P)`, independent of `exists(birth_event(P))`. *Hook*: any denial of a floor, benefit, or dignity protection conditioned solely on absence of a birth record is void and is itself a floor breach under the O5 floor-waiver-void rule. This invariant is cited as controlling by PER-I5/PER-I15.

**CIV-I4 (Late and delayed registration is a right, not a re-judgment of personhood; age-relied late registration is independently determined).** A person, or their guardian under a bounded CAP-I7 delegation, SHALL be able to register a birth or other past event after the fact through a delayed-registration protocol. The late registration SHALL record the true event date and the registration date as distinct fields, and SHALL NOT downgrade any floor, benefit, or status that accrued in the interval. Where a late birth registration is relied on to satisfy a marriage, adoption, or capacity age threshold, the recorded age SHALL be fixed at the highest attestation tier PLUS an INDEPENDENT age determination (CIV-I15), and SHALL NEVER rest on the assertion of a party, guardian, or family member who benefits from the recorded age; the FAM age-capacity default (FAM-I2) governs the protective presumption until such determination completes. *Purpose*: protect the vulnerable (undocumented, displaced, trafficked, or institutionally-erased persons) without letting the gap be weaponized against them, and without letting a self-serving late birth entry manufacture marital or capacity age. *Counter-flank*: a late registration carries a heightened evidentiary standard (CIV-I15) precisely because retroactive claims are the classic fraud vector, most acutely age fraud into a child marriage.

**CIV-I5 (Birth event opens the person-record and links parentage).** A `birth` event SHALL open exactly one person-record, stamp date and place, and assert parentage links into FAM. The birth event SHALL NOT by itself determine citizenship status (deferred to Citizenship-Membership-and-Personhood.md). *Purpose*: one canonical origin entry per person, anti-Sybil at the root, with citizenship kept a separate graded question. *Predicate*: `count(birth_event(P)) <= 1` in the authoritative fold; a second purported birth event for the same natural person is a duplicate-identity flag routed to S1/anti-Sybil, not a second person.

**CIV-I6 (One canonical liveness bit, death is the highest-integrity event, and this is THE death standard).** A person-record's liveness SHALL be `living` until exactly one VALID `death` event is appended, after which it is `deceased`. The death event SHALL carry the highest evidentiary and audit standard of any B12 event, because a false death (a living person recorded dead) and a concealed death (a dead person recorded living) are both catastrophic. CIV-I6 together with CIV-I7 and CIV-I15 is the SINGLE owner of the death-corroboration and evidentiary standard for the people-and-society layer: no other section defines what makes a death valid; PER-I16, HOLD-I11, and PINT-I7/PINT-I23 SHALL cite CIV-I6/CIV-I15 for the standard rather than positing their own. *Purpose*: liveness gates succession, guardianship, benefits, voting, and consent; corrupting it is high-value fraud on both flanks, so exactly one authority defines validity. *Hook*: a `death` event SHALL require the A12-mandated highest attestation set; a false or concealed death is a criminal-class harm under A18 (never merely priced), and concealment carries the A18 perpetual flip (the clock never runs out on a concealed death).

**CIV-I7 (Death cascade is atomic-with-teeth, and fires only on a VALID death event).** The death cascade SHALL fire only on appending a VALID `death` event under CIV-I6/CIV-I15, never on a bare appended assertion. On a valid `death` event, the register SHALL fire the cascade: (a) trigger PROP-I5 succession (death-to-estate handoff) in Property-Rent-and-Commons.md; (b) close or hand off the decedent's personal namespaces (B10 health per its own spec, B12 going read-only-plus-succession, K2 acts, X5 interaction side) per S1 succession-of-control rules; (c) flip any guardianships the decedent held or was subject to (CAP-I7 auto-termination); (d) resolve marital status of any surviving spouse to `widowed`. Until a death is CIV-valid, the person's Anchor stays live, first-person acts remain valid, floors continue via COMP-I17, and any provisional disposition is reversible. *Purpose*: a death has system-wide legal effect that must not be partially applied, and must not be applied at all on an uncorroborated claim. *Predicate*: cascade steps are booked as linked X1 events referencing the `death` event id. *Hook*: an incomplete cascade is a register-integrity fault escalated under R2; no namespace may silently persist under a dead controller (that is the S1 orphaned-control failure mode). PER-I16 (residue-state, first-person-act closure) and HOLD-I11 (estate freeze) CITE CIV-I6/CIV-I7 and condition closure/freeze on the CIV-valid death event.

**CIV-I8 (Erroneous death is reversible by new event, a live liveness predicate immediately contests it, and it restores the person completely.)** Where a `death` event is later shown erroneous (the person is living), the register SHALL append a `correction` reversing liveness to `living` and SHALL unwind the cascade: reverse or re-open succession under PROP-I5's own reversal path, restore namespaces to the person, and re-instate every floor and status. The person SHALL NOT bear the burden of the register's error. A person whose record has folded to `deceased` SHALL retain a standing liveness-assertion channel: a verified S6 liveness predicate presented by the putatively-dead person through that channel SHALL IMMEDIATELY (a) restore provisional first-person capacity (exempt from the PER-I16 void-as-impersonation rule, so a death-folded Anchor may use the channel to contest its own death) and (b) FREEZE all succession dispositions of the corpus, permitting no further transfer that could vest R1-I28 good-faith protection in a buyer, pending resolution. The burden of resolution SHALL rest on continuation of the death fold, not on the person asserting they are alive. *Purpose*: protect the living person wrongly declared dead (a severe dignity and property harm) and stop the corpus draining out from under them while the record is corrected. *Counter-flank*: the freeze and the final reversal follow the correction protocol's evidentiary and due-process standard so that "I am not actually dead" cannot be abused to launder a genuine death or defraud good-faith successors; a genuine death fold that survives contest reverts the freeze, and successors' reliance is protected under R1-I28 public faith. This invariant is cross-cited by PINT-I23.

**CIV-I9 (Marriage and divorce are STATUS events here; substance is FAM's).** B12 SHALL record marriage, divorce, and annulment as civil-status transitions only: the fact, the parties, the date, and the resulting status. The relational, financial, and contractual substance of the union SHALL live in FAM and its referenced contract objects (O5), not in B12. *Purpose*: keep the public status rail thin, portable, and privacy-minimal while the sensitive substance sits behind FAM's own access contracts. *Hook*: a query for "is P married" returns the status bit under S6-style predicate disclosure where possible; the identity of a spouse or the terms of the union require consent or a judicial access contract (S2/S3), never the bare public projection.

**CIV-I10 (Consent floor governs marriage, consent is verified privately at booking, coercion voids it, and there is a repudiation window.)** A `marriage` event SHALL be bookable only on the present, competent, un-coerced consent of every party, each holding the non-waivable consent floor. Before booking, each party's un-coerced consent SHALL be confirmed INDIVIDUALLY and PRIVATELY, out of the presence of the other party, any family member, and any guardian, by the attesting official; no such confirmation, no booking. A marriage recorded without such consent (forced marriage, marriage of a person below capacity, marriage by fraud as to identity) is void ab initio, not merely voidable, and any waiver of the consent floor purporting to authorize it is void under the O5 floor-waiver-void rule. There SHALL further be a non-waivable repudiation window, running from booking, during which either party MAY void the marriage ab initio on a bare assertion of coercion with NO proof burden on the repudiating party; the burden of establishing validity SHALL rest on the party asserting the marriage is valid. Repudiation SHALL be routable through a guardian-inaccessible, whistleblower-guaranteed channel (mirroring FAM-I3 and the PER-I11 whistleblower persona). *Purpose*: the register must never launder a coerced union into a faith-bearing public fact, and a coerced party isolated at the ceremony must have a private moment and a later escape hatch. *Counter-flank*: outside the repudiation window a bare later allegation of coercion does not by itself un-record a marriage; it opens a correction/annulment proceeding (CIV-I13) with due process, protecting a genuine spouse against an abusive unilateral erasure.

**CIV-I11 (Divorce cannot be a total-life-capture bundle or a floor waiver.)** No divorce or separation instrument recorded through B12 SHALL bundle away a capability floor, the consent floor, a non-priceable right, the suspensive-contest guarantee, or the whistleblower guarantee of either party or of any child. Such a bundle is void, and where it constitutes total-life-capture it is void and criminal under the O5 total-life-capture-void-and-criminal rule (person-side operational test owned by HOLD-I7), never judicially reformed. Any floor waiver in such an instrument is void under the O5 floor-waiver-void rule. *Purpose*: block the divorce instrument from being used to strip a weaker party or a child of floors. *Hook*: the B12 booking validator rejects any divorce entry whose referenced O5 terms fail the HOLD-I7 total-life-capture test or the O5 floor-waiver-void check; the void is surfaced as an appended VOID event under S4, not silently dropped.

**CIV-I12 (Name and gender marker are self-owned self-descriptions.)** A competent adult SHALL be able to append a `name-change` or `gender-marker-change` event over their own B12 namespace as an exercise of S1 self-ownership, without third-party permission and without proof of any underlying medical fact (which lives in B10, if anywhere, and is not required here). *Purpose*: dignity and self-description are person-owned; the register records the self-declared legal identity going forward. *Counter-flank*: the prior entries remain in the append-only history (never deleted per R1) but are SEALED under CIV-I23, so the change cannot be used to escape a pre-existing liability, judgment, or identity linkage while still not being exposed to parties the person is protected from; anti-Sybil and post-judgment tracing (R1-I39) see through the change UNDER SEAL (available to the register/court), and a third party who relied in good faith on a prior public name is protected under R1-I28 EXCEPT where CIV-I23 bars that reliance in favor of a party the person is protected from.

**CIV-I13 (Correction is a new event under due process, never an edit.)** A wrongly recorded event SHALL be corrected only by appending a `correction` event that supersedes the erroneous entry in the fold, under a due-process standard proportionate to the stakes (highest for liveness and parentage). No entry SHALL ever be edited or deleted (per R1). *Purpose*: fix real errors while preserving a complete, tamper-evident history of what was recorded and when. *Predicate*: `fold` prefers the latest non-superseded entry; the superseded entry stays queryable in history. *Hook*: a correction to a high-integrity field (liveness, parentage, birth date) requires the elevated attestation set (CIV-I15) and is itself contestable under the A18-I34 suspensive-contest guarantee.

**CIV-I14 (Certificate is a public-faith projection, not the record.)** A CIV-cert SHALL be a pinned R1 instrument projecting the state of the fold at issue-time, carrying the issue timestamp and the entry ids it projects. A third party who relies in good faith on a validly-issued certificate SHALL be protected under R1-I28 public faith even if the underlying record is later corrected. *Purpose*: certificates must be safely relyable in commerce and border control without freezing the register. *Counter-flank*: reliance protection extends only to good-faith reliance; a party who knew or colluded in a falsity (for example a forged or bribed certificate) gets no public-faith shield, a certificate SHALL NOT project a CIV-I23-sealed prior name or gender marker, and the certificate's projected-entry ids let any verifier detect staleness against the live fold.

**CIV-I15 (Evidentiary standard scales with cascade blast radius, and this is THE evidentiary standard.)** The evidentiary and attestation standard for booking an event SHALL scale with the legal effect it triggers: highest for `death` (fires PROP-I5 and namespace handoff), high for `birth`, `parentage`, and `adoption` (creates persons and lifelong links), and standard for `name-change` and `gender-marker-change` (self-description). A late birth registration relied on to satisfy a marriage, adoption, or capacity age threshold SHALL additionally require an INDEPENDENT age determination (CIV-I4). CIV-I15 with CIV-I6/CIV-I7 is the single owner of the death evidentiary standard; other sections cite it. *Purpose*: put friction where fraud is most damaging and keep it low where the change is a self-owned self-description. *Hook*: the booking validator selects the required attestation set by subtype; an under-attested high-blast event is rejected as an appended VOID event under S4, not provisionally accepted.

**CIV-I16 (Guardianship over a minor's or incapacitated person's B12 namespace cites CAP-I7.)** Where a person cannot yet or can no longer control their own B12 namespace, control over that namespace SHALL pass only under CAP-I7 (explicit, bounded, auditable S1 delegation, never a silent default, scope- and time-bounded, cannot waive floors, the consent floor, or non-priceable rights, auto-terminating at majority, restoration, or the death cascade). This invariant adds only the section-local hook: the guardian's register-write-on-behalf. *Purpose*: enable protection of minors and incapacitated persons without converting guardianship into ownership of the person, under one canonical guardianship shape. *Hook*: the guardian MAY append events on the person's behalf as a register-write-on-behalf; every such write is an S4 event resolving to the named guardian (or, for an institutional guardian, the CAP-I20 named accountable officer); on conflict CAP-I7 governs.

**CIV-I17 (Adoption and legitimation re-write parentage forward; origin access is a non-waivable person-side right.)** An `adoption` or `legitimation` event SHALL append new parentage links into FAM and update the civil-status fold going forward, without deleting the original birth entry from the append-only history. Access by the person to their OWN origin, being the FACT of adoption or donor-conception and the medically-relevant genetic origin, SHALL be a NON-WAIVABLE person-side right vesting at majority: the origin-seeker is entitled to it as of right, and a parent's or donor's privacy interest governs only the manner, timing, and non-contact conditions, NEVER whether disclosure occurs. The identity-and-contact question SHALL be routed separately from the fact-of-origin and health core, and the S2/S3 access contract calibrates the FORM of disclosure, never its EXISTENCE. *Purpose*: give the adopted or legitimated person a coherent forward legal identity while guaranteeing the biographical and medical truth of origin to the person it is about. *Counter-flank*: the parties' privacy is honored in the manner and non-contact conditions and in third-party disclosure limits, so recognizing the person's right does not expose a donor or parent to unwanted contact; but privacy can never be used to deny the person the fact or the health-relevant genetic core. Cross-cited by FAM-I14.

**CIV-I18 (Register sees cohorts by default; per-person reads are consented or judicial.)** Analytic and administrative access to B12 SHALL default to cohort projections under the S5 k-anonymity floor; per-person reads SHALL require the person's consent, a judicial access contract, or a narrow-and-loud X3 emergency access. Every read SHALL be logged as an S4 event. *Purpose*: the civil register is a mass-surveillance risk if read per-person by default; it must serve statistics without exposing individuals. *Hook*: a query resolving to fewer than k persons is refused or coarsened; an emergency X3 read is time-boxed, notified, and after-the-fact reviewable.

**CIV-I19 (Prove-without-revealing for civil-status predicates.)** Where a relying party needs only a civil-status predicate (is-over-18, is-not-deceased, is-not-currently-married, is-legal-parent-of), the system SHALL be able to answer with an S6 zero-knowledge predicate rather than disclosing the underlying entries or full certificate. *Purpose*: minimize disclosure in the common case (marriage licensing, benefits eligibility, border checks) while still binding to the authoritative fold. *Counter-flank*: the predicate is cryptographically bound to the live register, so a false "not currently married" answer is detectable, protecting against bigamy and identity fraud without over-disclosing to every counterparty.

**CIV-I20 (Cross-border events: recognition is a new domestic event, with named provenance validation, and fraud and floor breach are the limits.)** A life event validly registered in another jurisdiction SHALL be recognizable by appending a domestic `recognition` event that records the foreign event and its source, rather than by importing the foreign register as authority. For a foreign entry the A12-mandated civil registrar SHALL be the attesting A12 actor for the domestic `recognition` event; the foreign authoring official is NOT a domestic author, so the registrar substitutes domestic provenance by requiring an apostille or the applicable treaty-channel authentication PLUS corroborating evidence proportionate to the event's blast radius (CIV-I15). CIV-I22 authorship-provenance validation SHALL apply to the `recognition` entry: its accountable author is the domestic registrar acting under mandate, bound to the validated apostille/treaty provenance of the foreign source; an entry whose foreign provenance cannot be authenticated is refused, not provisionally accepted, so no unvalidated forgery is imported. Recognition SHALL be refused only for a bounded, stated set of reasons: unauthenticable or proven-fraudulent provenance, a floor breach, a consent-floor violation (for example a recognized-abroad forced or child marriage), or a criminal-class harm under A18. *Purpose*: make foreign life events portable and relyable while keeping a narrow, non-abusive public-policy backstop and a decidable provenance test. *Counter-flank*: refusal is enumerated and contestable under A18-I34, so recognition cannot be denied for arbitrary or discriminatory reasons on one flank, and a coerced, fraudulent, or unauthenticable foreign event cannot be laundered into the domestic register on the other.

**CIV-I21 (No event, and no correction, may erase a person or their floors.)** No B12 operation, including death, correction, adoption, name or gender change, annulment, or non-recognition, SHALL have the effect of rendering a living natural person a non-person, stripping their capability or consent floors, or removing them from COMP-I17 delivery. *Purpose*: the register administers status, never personhood; personhood is not a status the register can revoke. *Hook*: any pipeline whose net effect is a floorless living human is void ab initio and is a criminal-class outcome with a natural-person bearer (no autonomous-agent or procedural path discharges it), consistent with the no-floor-breach-without-a-bearer rule.

**CIV-I22 (Every B12 event is an X1 event with a natural-person or accountable-registrar author.)** Every appended life event SHALL carry an author and an S4 audit trail resolving to an accountable natural person (the declarant, the attesting official, or a named guardian) or to the registrar acting under mandate. No life event SHALL be authored by an anonymous or purely-automated actor with no traceable human bearer. *Purpose*: the register's integrity depends on accountability for every entry; anti-Sybil and anti-fraud both require a who. *Hook*: an entry lacking a resolvable accountable author is rejected at booking as an appended VOID event under S4; a concealed or falsified authorship is itself an A18 concealment-class violation with the perpetual flip. For a `recognition` entry the accountable author is the domestic registrar bound to validated foreign provenance (CIV-I20).

**CIV-I23 (Prior gender-marker and name entries are sealed self-descriptions.)** The prior gender-marker and name entries superseded by a `gender-marker-change` or `name-change` (CIV-I12), and the old-to-new linkage between them, SHALL be SEALED: disclosable only to the person themselves or under a judicial access contract with stated cause, and NEVER via a certificate projection (CIV-I14), a family, caregiving, or employer access scope, or an R1-I28 public-faith reliance claim asserted by a party the person is protected from. A safety-seal category (parallel to the PER-I11 whistleblower persona) SHALL seal the old-to-new linkage for a person at risk (for example a trans person or a domestic-violence survivor); re-correlation of the old identity to the new SHALL occur only by judicial access contract with prior notice to the protected person, and an abusive re-correlation attempt carries abuse-of-process exposure. Anti-Sybil and post-judgment (R1-I39) tracing SHALL still operate UNDER SEAL, available to the register and the court but not to private reliers. *Purpose*: prevent the append-only history from becoming an outing or a locating instrument against the very person whose dignity the self-description right serves, while keeping the register anti-Sybil-sound for the state. *Counter-flank*: the seal does not defeat legitimate court or register tracing of liability across the change (that continues under seal), and the judicial-contract-with-notice path lets a party with genuine cause reach the linkage without handing it to a private adversary, so neither the protected person nor a good-faith legitimate claimant is sacrificed.

### Protocols

Each protocol below names the executing actor, the input record, the output event, and the S4 audit entry. Where a predicate fails, the named actor appends a VOID event readable under S4 rather than silently dropping or provisionally accepting the act.

**P-CIV-birth (open person-record).**
- *Actor*: the A12-mandated civil registrar.
- *Input*: a `birth` K2 act submitted by the declarant (parent, guardian under CAP-I7, or self in late registration).
- *Steps*: registrar evaluates CIV-I5 uniqueness and CIV-I15 birth-tier attestation; on pass, appends the `birth` X1 event, opens the person-record with self-ownership vested (guardian-delegated until majority per CIV-I16), asserts FAM parentage links, and emits the birth CIV-cert as an R1 pinned instrument; citizenship is evaluated separately by its own spec.
- *Output*: `birth` X1 event, person-record, birth CIV-cert; on failure, a VOID event stating the failed predicate.
- *S4 audit*: booking entry resolving to the declarant and the registrar.

**P-CIV-death (append and cascade).**
- *Actor*: the civil registrar, on submission by the attesting official.
- *Input*: a `death` K2 act carrying the highest attestation set (CIV-I6/CIV-I15).
- *Steps*: registrar validates the highest attestation set; only on a VALID death event appends the `death` X1 event and fires the atomic cascade (CIV-I7): PROP-I5 succession, namespace close/handoff, guardianship flips (CAP-I7), surviving-spouse status to `widowed`; cascade steps are linked X1 events under R2 integrity checks; an incomplete cascade escalates under R2 rather than silently completing.
- *Output*: `death` X1 event, linked cascade X1 events, death CIV-cert; on failed attestation, a VOID event (no cascade fires).
- *S4 audit*: booking entry resolving to the attesting official and the registrar; each cascade step logged with a reference to the `death` event id.

**P-CIV-marriage / P-CIV-divorce (status transition).**
- *Actor*: the attesting official (consent confirmation and booking), with the registrar recording the entry.
- *Input*: the `marriage` or `divorce` K2 act from the parties, plus any referenced O5 terms.
- *Steps*: the attesting official confirms each party's un-coerced consent individually and privately (CIV-I10) and, for marriage, opens the repudiation window; the registrar runs the HOLD-I7 total-life-capture test and the O5 floor-waiver-void check on referenced terms (CIV-I11) and an S6 not-currently-married predicate on each party (CIV-I19); on pass, appends the status event and updates the fold; substance is booked in FAM, not B12.
- *Output*: `marriage`/`divorce` X1 event and certificate; on failed consent confirmation or failed floor check, a VOID event and no booking.
- *S4 audit*: private-confirmation attestation and booking entry resolving to the attesting official and each party; a within-window repudiation is itself an appended `annulment`/void event routed through the guardian-inaccessible channel.

**P-CIV-correction (fix by new event).**
- *Actor*: the adjudicator or court (A6) for high-integrity fields (liveness, parentage, birth date); the registrar for standard fields.
- *Input*: a `correction` K2 act with supporting evidence from the contestant.
- *Steps*: due-process review proportionate to field stakes (CIV-I13/CIV-I15); on pass, appends a `correction` X1 event superseding, never deleting, the erroneous entry; if a high-integrity field, re-runs any dependent cascade (for example the CIV-I8 death reversal, including the liveness-freeze handling) and re-issues affected certificates, whose old copies remain projections of the superseded state and lose public faith only against parties on notice.
- *Output*: `correction` X1 event, re-issued certificates; on failure, a VOID event stating the unmet due-process standard.
- *S4 audit*: review-and-append entry resolving to the adjudicator or registrar and the contestant.

**P-CIV-recognition (cross-border).**
- *Actor*: the civil registrar as the attesting A12 actor (CIV-I20).
- *Input*: a foreign event presented by the holder, with apostille or treaty-channel authentication and corroborating evidence.
- *Steps*: registrar validates foreign provenance under CIV-I20/CIV-I22 and checks the enumerated refusal set; on pass, appends a domestic `recognition` X1 event recording source, authenticated provenance, and foreign entry; the recognized event then participates in the fold as a domestic status fact.
- *Output*: `recognition` X1 event; on unauthenticable or refused provenance, a VOID event stating the refusal reason (contestable under A18-I34).
- *S4 audit*: provenance-validation and booking entry resolving to the registrar and the presenting holder.

**P-CIV-late-registration.**
- *Actor*: the civil registrar; the adjudicator or court (A6) for an age-relied determination.
- *Input*: a past event submitted by the applicant for first-time recording.
- *Steps*: registrar applies the heightened evidentiary standard (CIV-I4/CIV-I15); where the record is relied on for a marriage, adoption, or capacity age threshold, an INDEPENDENT age determination (never on a benefiting party's assertion) is obtained; appends the event with distinct event-date and registration-date fields; no retroactive downgrade of any accrued floor, benefit, or status.
- *Output*: the appended past event with dual dates and, where applicable, an independent age determination; on failure, a VOID event.
- *S4 audit*: booking entry resolving to the applicant, the registrar, and any independent age assessor.

### Lifecycle and edge cases

- **Never-registered living person.** Holds all floors and COMP-I17 delivery in full (CIV-I3, controlling for PER-I5/PER-I15); may register at any time (CIV-I4); absence of a record is never a status downgrade (CIV-I21).
- **Concealed death.** Treated as an A18 concealment-class violation with the perpetual flip (CIV-I6); the succession and namespace handoff that were suppressed are the measure of harm, with natural-person bearers (CIV-I22).
- **Living person wrongly recorded dead.** A verified S6 liveness predicate through the standing liveness-assertion channel immediately restores provisional first-person capacity and freezes all corpus dispositions, burden on continuation of the death fold (CIV-I8); full reversal restores every floor and status with no burden on the person, balanced against R1-I28 protection of good-faith successors on any pre-freeze transfer.
- **Bigamy / duplicate identity.** Blocked at booking by the S6 not-currently-married predicate (CIV-I19) and by the single-birth-event anti-Sybil check (CIV-I5), routed to S1/anti-Sybil rather than silently accepted.
- **Coerced marriage caught at or after the ceremony.** The private individual consent confirmation blocks booking where a party cannot freely confirm (CIV-I10); a party who could not speak up in the moment voids the marriage ab initio within the repudiation window on a bare assertion, through the guardian-inaccessible channel, with the burden on the party asserting validity (CIV-I10).
- **Age fraud into a child marriage via late registration.** A self-serving late birth entry cannot manufacture marital age; the highest attestation tier plus an independent age determination governs, and until it completes the FAM protective age presumption (FAM-I2) treats the party as below capacity (CIV-I4/CIV-I15).
- **Guardian overreach.** A guardian action is bounded by CAP-I7 (cited by CIV-I16) and cannot waive a ward's floor; every register-write-on-behalf is an S4 event resolving to the named guardian or CAP-I20 accountable officer.
- **Post-judgment tracing across a name or gender change.** The change never breaks liability linkage in the append-only history, but the prior entries and old-to-new linkage are sealed (CIV-I23); the R1-I39 asset-discovery instrument (post-judgment only) and register/court tracing operate UNDER SEAL, while a private relier the person is protected from gets neither the linkage nor an R1-I28 shield against the seal (CIV-I12/CIV-I23).
- **Trans person or DV survivor at risk of outing/locating.** The safety-seal category seals the old-to-new linkage; re-correlation only by judicial contract with prior notice to the protected person and abuse-of-process exposure (CIV-I23).
- **Adopted or donor-conceived person seeking origin.** The fact of origin and the medically-relevant genetic core are a non-waivable right vesting at majority; the access contract calibrates only the manner, timing, and non-contact conditions, and identity-contact is routed separately (CIV-I17).
- **Foreign event of doubtful provenance.** Recognized only on authenticated apostille/treaty provenance plus corroborating evidence proportionate to blast radius; unauthenticable provenance is refused, not provisionally accepted (CIV-I20/CIV-I22).

## FAM household and family: the relational substrate (B3)

### Purpose

B3 models the household and the family as the relational substrate that B12 marriage and divorce life events presuppose. B12 records the events; FAM defines the standing relations those events create, mutate, and dissolve: the partnership contract, the shared household economy (the household RELATION, not the estate object), the care relationship over dependents, and the filiation graph linking persons across generations.

This section takes the PERSON-SIDE view. It does not re-model title, estate, or succession mechanics (Property-and-Ownership.md, Property-Rent-and-Commons.md, PROP-I5), the estate object or the person-side share-claim ledger (Household-and-Estate.md, B11), citizenship status (Citizenship-Membership-and-Personhood.md), the health record (Civic-Health-and-Democracy.md, B10), the harm surface (Offense-and-Enforcement.md, A18), the floor machinery (Distributive-Justice-and-Capability-Floors.md, COMP-I17), the guardianship and capacity shape (CAP-I7, CAP-I1..I5), the person-side nominee pointer (PINT-I15), or the veil-piercing and total-life-capture rules (O5, O1). It binds to them and cites them by ID.

The design holds two duties in tension on every point, and protects both flanks by construction: it SHALL protect the vulnerable person inside the family (the coerced spouse, the neglected child, the captured elder) AND it SHALL protect the honest household against false claims and against state intrusion (the diverse family that owes the state no explanation, the spouse falsely accused, the parent wrongly stripped of a child).

### Object model

- **B3.Partnership**: a consensual relational contract between two or more capable natural persons, establishing a household frame (V5). Attributes: parties, formation event (CIV/B12), consent records, exit terms, B3.Household linkage, dissolution event if any. A partnership is a relation, never a meta-object that owns its members (S1: every meta-object has exactly one controlling meta-object, and a person's controlling meta-object is always the person).
- **B3.Household**: the household RELATION, a person-side economic frame over which members MAY conduct a shared economy. B3.Household is NOT the estate object and holds no title, no floors, and no control over any asset. A jointly-held asset is a single P2 title object with exactly one controlling meta-object, owned by Property-and-Ownership.md; each member's interest in it is a severable share-claim recorded in that member's B11 entry (Household-and-Estate.md), and FAM asserts no co-control of the object. FAM books only the person-side relation, the non-pooling rule, and the intra-household distribution ledger.
- **B3.Care**: a directed care relationship from one or more named care-bearers to a dependent (minor, incapacitated adult, elder with reduced capacity). Attributes: bearer(s), dependent, delegated control scope (CAP-I7), the deliverable care duty, review cadence.
- **B3.Filiation**: the parentage graph. Edges are parent-of relations with a stated basis (biological, adoptive, assisted-reproduction, or ripened social/functional per FAM-I13). Each edge is a register event (R1, append-only) linked to a CIV birth or adoption event.
- **B3.Dependent**: a natural person whose capability floors are, in whole or part, delivered through or supplemented by a care relationship, without ceasing to be own-account under COMP-I17.

### Invariants

**FAM-I1 (partnership is consent between capable persons).** A B3.Partnership SHALL be valid only if every party gives affirmative, contemporaneous, revocable consent and holds legal capacity at formation. Purpose: the family is founded on agreement, not status conferred on a person from outside. Predicate: `valid(P) := forall x in parties(P): consent_affirmative(x) AND capacity(x) AND not coerced(x)`. Enforcement hook: a formation event failing the predicate is registered as VOID ab initio (no fold recognizes it), and the defect is a new corrective register event, never an edit (R1).

**FAM-I2 (capacity and minimum-age gate; child marriage void; protective default for the undocumented).** A party below the polity's marital-capacity age, or lacking decisional capacity for this act, SHALL NOT be a valid party, and any partnership purporting to include such a party is VOID. Where no reliable independently-attested birth record exists, the party SHALL be treated as below marital-capacity age (marriage void) UNLESS age is established by an INDEPENDENT age assessment; age SHALL NEVER be established by the assertion of the party, a guardian, or a family who benefits from the marriage. Purpose: protect the child and the incapacitated from being married, and prevent the missing-document loophole from being turned against the child. Both flanks: the gate voids child marriage regardless of parental consent, custom, or a foreign ceremony recognized elsewhere; AND it SHALL NOT be used to retroactively invalidate a marriage between adults who both held capacity, nor to strip an of-age person of their chosen partnership. Enforcement hook: capacity is checked against the CIV birth-event age (an S6 over-age predicate suffices, no full birth-record disclosure) and any adjudicated incapacity status (FAM-I22); a late birth registration relied on to satisfy the age threshold requires the highest attestation tier and independent age determination (CIV-I4/CIV-I15).

**FAM-I3 (consent floor non-waivable; verified at booking; guarded repudiation window; forced marriage is criminal).** The consent floor SHALL NOT be waived in family formation by any party, guardian, family, or contract term. Each party's un-coerced consent SHALL be confirmed at booking individually and privately, out of the presence of the other party, any family member, and any guardian, and a non-waivable repudiation window SHALL follow, exactly as CIV-I10 delivers it; FAM does not restate that machinery and defers to CIV-I10 for the pre-booking check and the guardian-inaccessible, whistleblower-guaranteed repudiation channel. Forced marriage (formation without a party's genuine consent) is VOID ab initio and is a non-priceable CRIMINAL-class harm (A18), never priced, never reformed. Purpose: no one is contracted into a family against their will. Both flanks: the rule voids coerced unions and books culpability on the human coercers; AND it SHALL distinguish coercion from culturally-mediated but genuinely consented formation (an introduced or arranged match where the party freely consents is valid), so that consent, not the courtship pathway, is the decidable test. Enforcement hook: the coerced party's contest is suspensive and non-waivable (A18-I34).

**FAM-I4 (exit right is non-waivable, delivers the exiting person's floor immediately, and does not extinguish obligations).** Every partner SHALL retain a unilateral right to exit the partnership (dissolution). Any term purporting to bar, penalize into practical impossibility, or condition exit on a third party's permission is VOID. Filing dissolution SHALL trigger IMMEDIATE delivery of the exiting person's capability floor and homestead floor via COMP-I17 on that person's own-account basis, prior to and independent of the FAM-I16 estate settlement, so that no partner is trapped by the fear of leaving floorless; the FAM-I11 upfront backstop extends to any adult exiting a partnership, not only to dependents. Purpose: no person is trapped in a family relation for want of the means to leave it. Both flanks: exit can never be barred and the exiting person's floor is delivered up front rather than after a contested settlement; AND exit is renunciation of the relation, not of accrued liabilities, so it does not extinguish the joint-estate settlement or the continuing care obligations (FAM-I16), consistent with exit-cannot-escape-liability in Citizenship-Membership-and-Personhood.md, and the immediate floor delivery is COMP-I17 delivery, not a pre-judgment award of the other partner's share. Enforcement hook: a dissolution filing is self-effecting on the relation and triggers the settlement protocol; it cannot be blocked by the other party.

## HOLD personal property and the personal estate (B11)

### Purpose

This section models B11, the person-side view of what a natural person holds, acquires, disposes of, encumbers, and passes on at death. It is the biography of ownership as lived by a living human, not the mechanics of title. Title, movable-object identity, building identity, financial-instrument identity, rent, commons stewardship, and the death-to-estate handoff are owned by Property-and-Ownership.md (P2 / M3 / U1 / F8) and Property-Rent-and-Commons.md (PROP-I5 succession); this section references those and does not re-model them. On any conflict between a HOLD succession statement and PROP-I5, PROP-I5 governs.

B11 exists to do three things at once, and each pulls against the next. First, it gives the person a single coherent view of their own material standing, the personal estate, so that self-ownership under S1 is legible to the person and to authorized readers. Second, it hard-wires the essential-holdings / homestead floor so that no creditor, no counterparty, and no state process can strip a person below the delivered capability floor by reaching their property. Third, it keeps the register, not the estate view, as the holder of record, so that the person-side aggregate can never become a second, forgeable source of truth about who owns what.

Both flanks are load-bearing throughout. This section protects the vulnerable person from eviction from their tools, their dwelling, or their means of subsistence, and from being tricked into pledging their whole future. It equally protects the honest counterparty and the legitimate post-judgment creditor from a debtor who hides assets, mislabels luxury as essential, or launders a transfer through a household or a nominee. Every invariant below is written to hold both flanks.

### Object model

- **B11 holding**: a person-side relation asserting that a given natural person is the S1 controlling meta-object, or a bounded delegate, of a specific P2 title, M3 movable, U1 building interest, or F8 financial instrument. A holding is a projection of the authoritative register entry, never a substitute for it.
- **Personal estate**: an aggregate, balance-sheet-shaped FOLD over the person's current holdings. On the monetary rail it nets $-rail claims and encumbrances against $-rail holdings only; V4 anti-value and Ae-axis entries are carried per axis alongside the monetary figure and are never folded into it. It is a non-authoritative current-state view in the R1 sense, computed on demand, and it carries a provenance stamp naming every register it folds.
- **Essential-holdings set (homestead floor)**: the subset of a person's holdings marked protected: a dwelling interest sufficient for shelter, tools and instruments of the person's trade, and the personal-property minimum needed to keep the delivered capability floor whole. Distinguished at object level from luxury holdings.
- **Luxury holding**: any holding above the essential-holdings set. Fully reachable by lawful creditor process subject to the invariants below.
- **Encumbrance**: a person-side event recording a lien, pledge, security interest, or personal guaranty burdening a holding or the person's future flows. Every encumbrance names the holding or flow, the beneficiary, the secured amount or cap, and its priority basis.
- **Personal guaranty / pledge of future**: a promise that reaches beyond a specific holding to the person's future income, labor, or body. Bounded by the person-side total-life-capture test (HOLD-I7).
- **Joint or household share-claim**: a co-holder's severable person-side SHARE-CLAIM against a single P2 title object that has exactly one controlling meta-object per S1. B11 owns the person-side share-claims; the underlying single-controller rule is P2's. Reads and folds scoped to a FAM household frame respect that frame's V5 participation condition theta (HOLD-I17), which governs frame participation only and is never a floor magnitude.
- **Succession corpus**: the personal estate as a re-derivable fold pinned at the CIV-valid death event, tagged and handed to PROP-I5 as the input to succession. B11 produces this corpus and its tags; it does not run succession.
- **Digital / intangible personal asset**: N-cluster objects (documents, data, software, keys, accounts, credential-bearing artifacts) held by the person; referenced to Documents-Data-and-Software.md, treated here only as B11 holdings for estate, floor, and succession purposes.

### Invariants

**HOLD-I1 (estate is a fold, not a source of truth; nets only the $ rail).** The personal estate SHALL be computed as a fold over authoritative register entries and SHALL carry a provenance stamp listing every register folded and the fold timestamp. The fold SHALL net $-rail financial claims and encumbrances against $-rail holdings only; V4 and Ae-axis entries SHALL be carried per axis alongside the monetary figure and SHALL NEVER be netted into it. Predicate: an estate view that reduces any Ae axis into the $ balance is malformed, and an estate view lacking a complete provenance stamp is non-authoritative and inadmissible as proof of ownership. Enforcement hook: any reader relying on an estate view for a title question SHALL be redirected to the register of record; a mismatch between fold and register resolves in favor of the register (register supremacy) and raises an R2 reconciliation event.

**HOLD-I2 (person is the S1 owner of the estate view).** The controlling meta-object of a person's own personal-estate namespace SHALL be that person, per S1, and SHALL default to no one else. Purpose: self-ownership of one's own material biography. Enforcement hook: any B11 estate read by a party other than the person requires consent or a judicial access contract (S2/S3), is logged as an event (S4), and defaults to deny.

**HOLD-I3 (holding events are append-only and mirror the register).** Acquisition, disposal, and encumbrance SHALL be recorded as new B11 events that reference the underlying P2 / M3 / U1 / F8 register event, never as edits to a prior holding. Purpose: corrections-as-new-events (R1). Predicate: a B11 event with no matching underlying register event is provisional and SHALL NOT count toward the essential-holdings floor or the succession corpus until reconciled. Both flanks: protects the honest holder's record integrity and denies a debtor a forgeable side-ledger.

**HOLD-I4 (essential-holdings floor is kept whole by substitution, not by defeating a valid encumbrance).** The essential-holdings set SHALL NOT be seized, foreclosed, forcibly sold, or encumbered where doing so would drop the person below the delivered capability floor named in Distributive-Justice-and-Capability-Floors.md. Actor and basis: the post-execution capability projection SHALL be computed by the automated capability-floor evaluator owned by Distributive-Justice-and-Capability-Floors.md, with its essential-holdings basket and floor threshold bound to that document's concrete parameters, on referral from the registrar or the A6 adjudicator; on a projected drop below the floor the evaluator appends a BLOCK/VOID event readable under S4. Where the projection cannot be computed, the seizure SHALL be provisionally blocked pending A6 adjudication (protecting the person). Delivery rule: a seizure or pledge that would breach the floor SHALL NOT void a prior valid recorded encumbrance the person granted over that holding (protecting R1-I28 reliance and closing the pledge-then-shield attack); instead the floor is kept whole by COMP-I17 delivering a substitute (rehousing or reserve payout) on the person's own-account basis. A pledge that passed the HOLD-I7 guard is enforceable against the pledged asset; the floor is then delivered by the reserve, never by commanding or suppressing a market price.

**HOLD-I5 (essential vs luxury is a decidable, contestable classification).** Each holding SHALL be classified essential or luxury by a stated predicate tied to the person's actual dwelling need, actual trade, and the capability-floor basket, and the classification SHALL be contestable by both the person and any affected creditor. Purpose: protect the vulnerable person from over-narrow floors AND protect creditors from a debtor gold-plating "essential." Predicate: essential status requires demonstrated function (occupied dwelling, exercised trade, floor-basket item), not mere label; value above the functional minimum spills into the luxury tranche, and no classification may leave the person below the delivered capability floor. Enforcement hook: a contested classification is adjudicated by Courts-and-Adjudication.md (A6); pending adjudication the holding is provisionally protected but frozen against disposal, and the freeze is logged under S4.

**HOLD-I6 (luxury holdings are reachable by lawful process only).** Luxury holdings SHALL be reachable to satisfy a lawful, adjudicated obligation, and SHALL NOT be reachable by self-help, by pre-judgment seizure, or by any process that bypasses adjudication. Purpose: creditor reach with due process on both flanks. Enforcement hook: control transfer of a luxury holding to a creditor requires an adjudicated order referencing the obligation; absent it, the transfer is void and reversible.

**HOLD-I7 (person-side total-life-capture test; single owner).** This invariant is the single person-side operational test for the O5 total-life-capture-void-and-criminal rule, which it CITES for the void-and-criminal consequence; FAM-I5, PINT-I10, and CIV-I11 CITE this test rather than restating it. Definition: an instrument T bundles a protected element X, written bundles(T, X), when its enforcement, reversal, clawback, forfeiture, or penalty is conditioned on the person exercising or retaining X, or its effect is to transfer, waive, or render practically unexercisable X, where X ranges over a capability floor, the exit right, own-account control, and undifferentiated future labor. Decidable test: a transfer reaching undifferentiated future flows is capture unless it carries a stated cap AND a stated term AND a preserved essential-holdings / exit / own-account floor; an instrument that carries a cap and a term but still bundles the exit right (bundles(T, exit right)) is still capture. Consequence: an instrument meeting the capture test is VOID under O5 and, where structured to capture a life, CRIMINAL under O5. Both flanks: a bounded, capped, term-limited personal guaranty over luxury assets that bundles no protected element remains valid, so honest credit is not extinguished.

**HOLD-I8 (body and non-priceable core are never estate objects).** The person's body, organs, dignity, consent, and any A18 non-priceable right SHALL NOT appear as a B11 holding, collateral, or estate line item. Purpose: the dignity floor attaches to the person and is never priced (A18). Enforcement hook: any B11 event asserting such an object SHALL be rejected at ingestion and flagged to Offense-and-Enforcement.md; concealing such an assertion triggers the A18 concealment-class perpetual flip.

**HOLD-I9 (encumbrance must be recorded, capped, and priority-stamped).** Every encumbrance SHALL name the burdened holding or flow, a secured cap, a beneficiary in the O1 beneficial-owner graph, and a priority basis, and SHALL be recorded before it binds third parties. Purpose: prevent secret liens and over-collateralization. Predicate: an unrecorded or uncapped encumbrance does not bind good-faith third parties who relied on the public record (R1-I28 public-faith projection). Enforcement hook: priority disputes resolve on recorded order; a beneficiary hidden behind a nominee is resolved under PINT-I15 (HOLD-I19).

**HOLD-I10 (the estate does not out-run the floor for the person's own dependents).** Where a holding also secures the delivered floor of the person's minors or dependents under a bounded S1 guardianship delegation (CAP-I7), that floor SHALL be protected on the same delivered-floor basis as the holder's own. Purpose: a creditor cannot reach through a parent to strip a child's floor. Both flanks: the protection is bounded to the dependents' actual floor basket and does not shield luxury value parked in a dependent's name; such parking is a concealment event resolved under HOLD-I19.

**HOLD-I11 (death pins, it does not freeze-forever, the succession corpus).** The estate freeze and handoff SHALL fire only on the CIV-validated death event (CITE CIV-I6/CIV-I7); pending validation the Anchor stays live, first-person acts remain valid, floors continue via COMP-I17, and dispositions are reversible. On the valid death event no new first-person B11 acquisition or disposal by the deceased is possible; post-death B11 events purporting to act as the deceased are void, and control passes to the succession process, not to any self-asserting party. The succession corpus is a re-derivable fold PINNED at the death-fold timestamp, recomputed from the append-only register whenever a correction-by-new-event about a corpus holding is appended (R1-I39 nominee discovery, wrongful-inclusion correction); HOLD-I1 register supremacy governs any fold-versus-register mismatch. PROP-I5 consumes the current fold, not a one-time snapshot.

**HOLD-I12 (obligation and holding tags are handed to PROP-I5; PROP-I5 governs).** HOLD does not model succession ordering. HOLD SHALL tag each recorded obligation of the corpus with its recorded priority basis, and each corpus holding as essential/floor-bearing, dependent-shelter, or luxury, and hand those tags to PROP-I5. The creditor-priority ladder, the heir-liability cap (heirs not personally liable beyond the corpus except where they are themselves in the acting-in-concert graph of a fraud), and the debts-before-distribution ordering are stated ONCE in PROP-I5 and are cited here by ID. Both flanks: the tags protect creditors' recorded priority AND protect heirs from unbounded personal liability, and the concert-graph exception denies a fraudster a clean pass-through to a colluding heir. On any conflict between a HOLD succession statement and PROP-I5, PROP-I5 governs.

**HOLD-I13 (dependent-shelter tag, subordinate to prior recorded encumbrances).** Where a co-resident dependent's delivered capability floor rests on a dwelling in the corpus, HOLD SHALL tag that dwelling as dependent-shelter for PROP-I5, subject to these limits: the co-resident dependency SHALL be corroborated (PINT-I12/I20) and SHALL pre-date the liability asserted against the dwelling, and the tag SHALL be subordinate to any prior valid recorded encumbrance. Precedence for a dwelling that is both secured and sheltering: the dependent shelter floor is carved out and kept whole by COMP-I17 substitution (rehousing or reserve payout where the dwelling must be sold), and only surplus above the prior recorded encumbrances and the shelter floor is reachable in recorded creditor priority. The carve-out does NOT bind a pre-existing secured lien; instead the COMP-I17 reserve indemnifies the sheltered dependent by substitution while the lien-holder is paid in recorded priority. Purpose: a death does not evict a surviving dependent below the delivered capability floor, and reliance on a validly recorded lien is not defeated. PROP-I5 governs the ordering on conflict.

**HOLD-I14 (asset discovery is post-judgment only).** Per-holding discovery of a person's assets against their will SHALL use the R1-I39 judicial asset-discovery instrument and SHALL be reserved for post-judgment tracing only. Purpose: no pre-judgment fishing through a person's estate. Enforcement hook: any asset-discovery read absent a judgment SHALL be denied at the S2/S3 gate, logged (S4), and the requester exposed to abuse-of-process review. Both flanks: a real judgment creditor can trace hidden assets; a person is not strip-searched on suspicion.

**HOLD-I15 (state sees cohorts, not per-person estates).** Absent consent or a judicial access contract, the state SHALL read personal-estate data only as k-anonymous cohorts (S5), never as an identified per-person holding. Purpose: default-deny surveillance limit. Predicate: over-solvency or over-threshold facts SHOULD be answered by an S6 zero-knowledge predicate (solvent, above-floor, not-above-luxury-cap) rather than by disclosing the estate. Enforcement hook: emergency per-person access is narrow and loud (X3) and is itself an event.

**HOLD-I16 (joint holdings are severable share-claims against one titled object; shared floor is capped and contribution-traced).** A jointly-held asset is a single P2 title object with exactly one controlling meta-object per S1 (owned by Property-and-Ownership.md); each co-holder's B11 entry is a severable SHARE-CLAIM against that object, never co-control of the object, and the underlying control rule is deferred to P2. A creditor of one co-holder SHALL reach only that co-holder's severable share above the shared essential floor, and SHALL NOT reach an innocent co-holder's floor. The shared essential floor SHALL be capped at the sum of each co-holder's individual functional-minimum (HOLD-I5), so it cannot be inflated by pooling. On creditor contest the debtor-co-holder's contribution history into the joint holding SHALL be traced: value the debtor moved in while a liability was incipient is presumptively reachable and treated under the PINT-I15 nominee rule unless genuine consideration is shown. Both flanks: denies a debtor the trick of parking value in a co-holder's share AND denies a creditor the seizure of an innocent co-holder's floor.

**HOLD-I17 (household frame theta governs household-scoped estate reads).** Reads and folds scoped to the FAM household frame SHALL respect that frame's V5 participation condition theta, and a household-scoped estate view SHALL NOT leak a co-holder's separate (non-household) holdings. Purpose: the family frame is a distinct value frame, not a licence to see everything a relative owns; theta here is a frame participation condition only, never a floor magnitude. Enforcement hook: separate holdings require the holder's own consent or a judicial access contract, exactly as any other person-scoped read.

**HOLD-I18 (digital and intangible personal assets are full estate objects; lost-key floors are re-provisioned).** Digital and intangible personal assets (N-cluster: documents, data, keys, software licences, accounts) SHALL be first-class B11 holdings for floor, encumbrance, and succession purposes, referencing Documents-Data-and-Software.md for their object mechanics. A credential or key that gates access to an essential holding inherits that holding's floor protection. When such a key is unrecoverable, the person's capability floor SHALL be kept whole by COMP-I17 re-provisioning the underlying dwelling or tool access (compute-and-route), NOT by any claim that the key itself is recoverable. A register-recoverable custodial key SHALL be distinguished from a non-recoverable self-custody key: a custodial key is recovered through the register or the narrow-and-loud X3 emergency path; a self-custody key is not reconstructed, and the floor is re-provisioned instead. Both flanks: a person does not lose dwelling access because a key was lost; a debtor cannot park reachable value behind a "just a password" claim.

**HOLD-I19 (no nominee laundering of person-side holdings).** A holding placed in another natural person's name to defeat a floor, a creditor, or a disclosure SHALL be treated as held by the true controller. This CITES PINT-I15 for the person-side nominee and veil rule (the O1 acting-in-concert graph and the one canonical veil-piercing rule) and does not restate it. Section-local hook: a person-side nominee chain in B11 is resolved identically to any other holding chain, and the concealment is an A18 concealment-class event with a perpetual flip.

**HOLD-I20 (no B11 instrument waives a floor).** No B11 pledge, guaranty, household agreement, or estate instrument SHALL waive a floor protected by the O5 floor-waiver-void rule, which this invariant CITES (as CAP-I14, PER-I7, and FAM-I3 do); any such waiver is void ab initio. The only addition made here: the essential-holdings / homestead floor counts as a capability floor for that rule. No new waiver-void authority is created in B11. Both flanks: honest, floor-preserving instruments remain fully enforceable, so credit and household arrangements are not chilled.

**HOLD-I21 (residual corpus with no qualifying heir routes to a named default taker).** Where the CIV-valid death event resolves to no qualifying heir, HOLD SHALL tag the residual corpus, after debts and the dependent-shelter and essential floors, for routing by PROP-I5 to the named default taker (the state reserve or commons named in PROP-I5). HOLD produces the tag; PROP-I5 performs the routing and governs on conflict. Standing to defend the deceased's residue memory and reputation vests in the named public office of last resort per PER-I17 (cited, not modeled here).

**HOLD-I22 (commorientes: computable order of death).** Where two or more persons hold reciprocal corpora and the order of their deaths cannot be established, the succession fold SHALL apply a stated commorientes presumption so that reciprocal corpora are computable without circular dependency: HOLD binds to the PROP-side commorientes rule where one exists, and otherwise applies the default that neither is presumed to have survived the other, each corpus computed as if the other predeceased for the purpose of that other's reciprocal gift. The presumption actually applied SHALL be recorded as an S4 audit event. PROP-I5 governs on conflict.

### Protocols

Each protocol names its executing actor, input, output event, and S4 audit entry.

**Acquisition.** A person acquires a holding when a P2 / M3 / U1 / F8 register event vests control in them under S1; the registrar appends the mirroring B11 holding event (HOLD-I3), logged under S4, and, if the holding passes the HOLD-I5 function test, may mark it essential. No B11 acquisition is authoritative ahead of its underlying register event.

**Disposal.** A person disposes by a register control-transfer event; the registrar appends the B11 mirror event under S4. Disposal of an essential holding that would drop the person below the delivered capability floor is blocked at the S1 control-transfer gate: the Distributive-Justice capability-floor evaluator computes the post-execution projection and, on a projected breach or an uncomputable projection, appends a BLOCK event (HOLD-I4) unless a replacement holding keeps the floor whole. Disposal of a luxury holding is free, subject to any recorded encumbrance priority (HOLD-I9).

**Encumbrance and pledge.** The registrar records an encumbrance event with cap, beneficiary, and priority (HOLD-I9), tested against the person-side total-life-capture test (HOLD-I7) and the essential-holdings floor (HOLD-I4); it binds third parties only once recorded. A personal guaranty is admitted only if capped, termed, and floor-preserving; an instrument failing HOLD-I7 is appended VOID under S4.

**Creditor satisfaction.** On an A6-adjudicated obligation, the creditor reaches luxury holdings and severable joint share-claims in recorded priority (HOLD-I6, HOLD-I16), never the essential-holdings floor (HOLD-I4, delivered instead by COMP-I17), and traces hidden assets only post-judgment via R1-I39 (HOLD-I14). Contribution into a joint holding while a liability was incipient is traced under HOLD-I16 and the PINT-I15 nominee rule.

**Death handoff.** On the CIV-valid death event (HOLD-I11) the registrar folds and pins the estate at the death-fold timestamp, stamps it, and tags its holdings and obligations (essential/floor-bearing, dependent-shelter, luxury; priority basis) for PROP-I5, logging the handoff under S4. PROP-I5 settles debts before distribution, applies the heir-liability cap, and distributes in recorded priority (HOLD-I12); dependent-shelter floors are carved out and delivered by COMP-I17 subordinate to prior recorded encumbrances (HOLD-I13); a residual with no qualifying heir routes to the named default taker (HOLD-I21); reciprocal-death order follows the recorded commorientes presumption (HOLD-I22). PROP-I5 governs on any conflict.

### Lifecycle and edge cases

- **Minor or incapacitated holder.** Holdings are controlled through an explicit, bounded, auditable S1 guardianship delegation per CAP-I7, never a silent default; the ward's essential-holdings floor is protected on the delivered-floor basis (HOLD-I10) and a guardian's dealing that breaches it is void and reviewable.
- **Contested essential vs luxury.** Provisional protection with a disposal freeze pending A6 adjudication (HOLD-I5), logged under S4; neither flank can act unilaterally during the freeze.
- **Insolvency.** The luxury tranche is marshalled to creditors in recorded priority; the essential-holdings floor is not carved out of a validly encumbered asset by voiding the encumbrance but is delivered whole by COMP-I17 substitution (HOLD-I4), so insolvency never crosses the delivered capability floor while a good-faith creditor's recorded lien is honored.
- **Sudden death with hidden or nominee assets.** The pinned corpus is recomputed as post-judgment R1-I39 tracing appends corrections into nominee chains (HOLD-I11, HOLD-I14, HOLD-I19); heirs colluding in concealment lose the beyond-corpus liability shield under PROP-I5 (HOLD-I12).
- **No qualifying heir.** After debts and the dependent-shelter and essential floors, the residual corpus is tagged for routing to the named default taker via PROP-I5 (HOLD-I21); residue memory and reputation are defended by the PER-I17 office of last resort.
- **Simultaneous or indeterminate-order death.** The commorientes presumption is applied and recorded as an S4 audit event so reciprocal corpora are computable without circular dependency (HOLD-I22).
- **Digital asset locked by a lost key.** A key gating an essential holding inherits floor protection; when the key is unrecoverable the floor is kept whole by COMP-I17 re-provisioning the underlying access, not by asserting the key is recoverable (HOLD-I18); a register-recoverable custodial key runs through the register or the narrow-and-loud X3 path, never a silent state key-grab.
- **Household dissolution.** Severance splits the joint holding by recorded share-claim against the single titled object (HOLD-I16); each departing member keeps their own essential floor capped at their individual functional minimum; separate holdings are never leaked by the household frame (HOLD-I17).

## CAP capacity, guardianship and the vulnerable person

### Purpose

This section models legal and decisional capacity of the living natural person, the finding of incapacity, and guardianship as a bounded delegation. It exists to hold two goods in tension without collapsing either: the vulnerable person SHALL be genuinely protected (from a world that can exploit their incapacity, and from a guardian who can loot or isolate them), and the presumptively capable person SHALL be protected against being declared incapable so that another party can seize control of their acts, their consent, or a HOLD estate. Capacity is the gate on self-ownership: S1 says a person self-owns the controlling meta-object of their own namespaces, and capacity determines when, and only to what extent, that control may be delegated to another human. This section takes the person-side view of that delegation. It does not re-model title, succession (PROP-I5), the health record (B10), citizenship status, or the fiduciary machinery of firms (O1); it binds to them. CAP-I7 (with CAP-C4) is the single canonical statement of guardianship shape for the whole layer; other sections cite it and add only their local hook.

### Object model

- CAP-C1 capacity-holder: the natural person whose capacity is in question. Always a living human; capacity is never asserted of an entity.
- CAP-C2 decision-domain: a typed class of decisions over which capacity is assessed separately (for example: routine consumption, contracting above a threshold, medical consent under B10, disposition of a HOLD estate asset, marriage and family-frame acts under the family theta, votes and civic acts). Capacity is a vector indexed by domain, never a scalar.
- CAP-C3 capacity-finding: an entry appended to the R1 civil register (the A12-mandated register) recording, for a named capacity-holder and a named decision-domain, a status of SUPPORTED or INCAPABLE only, with a stated basis, an evidentiary record, an issuing authority, and a mandatory review date. A domain that carries no live finding is PRESUMED-CAPABLE by CAP-I1 and is never recorded as a stored status. A finding is never an edit of a prior finding; corrections, reversals, and continuations are new entries folded per R1.
- CAP-C4 guardianship-delegation: an S1 delegation from the capacity-holder (the controlling meta-object) to a named guardian, scoped to an enumerated set of decision-domains, for a bounded term, with a fiduciary standard attached. The delegation is the object; the guardian's authority is exactly its scope and nothing outside it.
- CAP-C5 guardian: the natural person or, only where CAP-I15 permits, the supervised institutional guardian holding a CAP-C4 delegation. A guardian is a fiduciary, not an owner of the ward. An institutional guardian carries a named accountable officer under CAP-I20.
- CAP-C6 support-arrangement: a lighter object than guardianship, recording named supporters who assist a SUPPORTED capacity-holder to form and express their own decision, holding no authority to decide in the person's place.
- CAP-C7 will-and-preferences record: the ward's known values, prior expressed wishes, and standing instructions, appended by the ward while capable and referenced as the primary decision standard under CAP-I9.

### Invariants

- CAP-I1 (presumption of capacity). Every natural person who has reached the age of majority SHALL be presumed to hold full capacity in every decision-domain. Purpose: make capability the default and incapacity the exception that must be proven. Predicate: absent a CAP-C3 finding of SUPPORTED or INCAPABLE for a given (person, domain), status is PRESUMED-CAPABLE and the person's own acts bind; PRESUMED-CAPABLE is a computed default, not a stored status. Enforcement hook: any act, contract, or consent refused or overridden on a bare assertion of incapacity, with no registered finding, is voidable at the person's instance and the refuser bears the harm under V4.

- CAP-I2 (incapacity must be found, not assumed, by an independent assessor). Incapacity in a decision-domain SHALL exist only by a CAP-C3 finding on a stated evidentiary basis by a competent authority, never by inference from age (above majority), diagnosis, disability, unpopular choice, or unusual preference. The assessing authority SHALL be independent in a structural sense: it SHALL have no edge in the O1 acting-in-concert or beneficial-owner graph to any person who inherits from, is paid from, or gains control from the finding, and it SHALL be selected by the CAP-I11 public office and never by the petitioner. Any finding that reduces a domain to INCAPABLE SHALL rest on an independent B10 health determination appended by the assessor's own controller directly to the register, never routed through the putative guardian's control of the ward's B10 namespace (B10 owner is always the person). A finding of SUPPORTED carries a lighter, explicitly stated bar: a stated functional-support basis, without the full B10 incapacity determination. Purpose: block the slide from difference to incapacity and keep the assessor's hand off the estate that gains. Predicate: a finding whose basis is only a diagnosis label, or only that the person decided against the assessor's judgment of their interest, or that issues from a conflicted or petitioner-selected assessor, is void. Both flanks: a genuine functional deficit in understanding, retaining, weighing, or communicating a decision IS a valid basis; the mere presence of a condition is not.

- CAP-I3 (capacity is graded and domain-specific; ambiguity resolves for the ward). A capacity-finding SHALL name the decision-domains it covers and SHALL NOT extend to any domain not named. Purpose: a person who cannot manage a complex estate may still choose where to live, whom to see, and what care to consent to. Predicate: authority claimed by a guardian in a domain absent from the CAP-C4 scope is ultra vires and its acts are void. Interim scope rule: pending a shared capacity-scope vocabulary, any ambiguity in a delegation's scope SHALL resolve for the ward's retained control (narrowest reading); a domain not unambiguously enumerated in CAP-C4 is outside scope and the ward's own act in that domain binds. Enforcement hook: the register rejects a finding that states a global incapacity without domain enumeration.

- CAP-I4 (capacity is time-bounded and presumptively temporary). Every CAP-C3 finding SHALL carry a review date, and no finding is permanent. Purpose: incapacity from a treatable, developmental, or episodic cause must not harden into a lifetime status by inertia. Predicate: a finding past its review date without a completed CAP-I12 review lapses to PRESUMED-CAPABLE for that domain; this lapse is a deterministic R1 fold computed from the finding's own appended review-date field (an expiring entry), not a silent edit of the register, and any positive continuation of the finding past that date is itself a new appended event bearing the completed review. Both flanks: a stable, degenerative, permanent condition MAY carry a long review interval, but never no review.

- CAP-I5 (narrowest intervention that works). Where support suffices, guardianship SHALL NOT be imposed; where guardianship over one domain suffices, it SHALL NOT extend to others; where a time-bounded order suffices, an indefinite one SHALL NOT issue. Purpose: guardianship is a scalpel, not a seizure of the person. Predicate: a guardianship order is defective, and reviewable on that ground, if a less restrictive arrangement adequate to the found deficit existed and was not adopted, with reasons recorded. Enforcement hook: the issuing authority SHALL record on the CAP-C4 object why each less-restrictive option was insufficient.

- CAP-I6 (supported before substituted). The system SHALL prefer supported decision-making (CAP-C6, the person decides with help) over substituted decision-making (the guardian decides), and a substituted decision is legitimate only in a domain where support has been tried and found insufficient, or where the incapacity is total in that domain (for example CAP-I13 unconsciousness). Purpose: help the person decide before deciding for them. Predicate: a substituted decision in a SUPPORTED-eligible domain, taken without a recorded attempt at supported decision-making, is a fiduciary breach under CAP-I10.

- CAP-I7 (guardianship is an explicit, bounded, auditable S1 delegation, never a silent default). Guardianship SHALL exist only as a CAP-C4 object registered under S1, with named guardian, enumerated domains, bounded term, and fiduciary standard; no relationship (parent, spouse, child, carer, institution) SHALL confer decisional authority over an adult by status alone; and no CAP-C4 delegation SHALL waive, suspend, or transfer a capability floor, the consent floor, or a non-priceable right (CAP-I14). A CAP-C4 delegation auto-terminates and folds closed at the ward's majority (for a minor default guardianship under CAP-I18), on restoration of capacity in the covered domains (CAP-I12), and at the ward's death (CAP-L4), never continuing by inertia past any of these. Purpose: no one acquires control of a person by merely standing near them, and control ends the instant its basis ends. Predicate: authority asserted over an adult with no registered CAP-C4 delegation is void and, if it moves the person's assets or consent, is treated under CAP-I17. Both flanks: for minors, CAP-I18 supplies a registered default guardianship precisely so it is explicit rather than silent. This invariant is the single canonical statement of guardianship shape for the layer; PER-I9, FAM-I9, CIV-I16, and PINT-I13 cite it by ID and add only their section-local hook, and CAP owns the capacity gradation; on conflict CAP-I7 governs.

- CAP-I8 (every guardian act is an event; the ward's reads are protected). Every act a guardian takes in the ward's name SHALL be an S4 event on the ward's own-account basis, readable by the ward and by the CAP-I11 complainant path, subject to S2/S3 access contracts and the S5 cohort-not-person default for third parties. Purpose: guardianship without an audit trail is capture waiting to happen. Enforcement hook: an unlogged guardian act is presumed adverse to the ward in any CAP-I10 or CAP-I17 proceeding.

- CAP-I9 (the will-and-preferences standard). A guardian SHALL decide first by the ward's known will and preferences (CAP-C7), and only where those cannot be ascertained by the ward's best interests construed as the ward would, not by the guardian's convenience, the guardian's values, or a third party's benefit. Where no capable-era CAP-C7 record exists (for example a congenitally never-capacitated ward), the guardian SHALL center the ward's presently-expressed preferences and communications, however non-standard, reached through documented supported decision-making (CAP-I6), rather than pure best-interests substitution; a failure to attempt to ascertain the ward's present will is a CAP-I10 breach. Purpose: substituted decisions are the ward's decisions made through another hand, not the guardian's decisions about the ward. Predicate: a decision contrary to a clear, capable-era CAP-C7 instruction, taken without a recorded protective necessity, is a breach. Both flanks: a known or presently-expressed preference that would inflict a non-priceable A18 harm on the ward or a third party does not bind the guardian to execute it.

- CAP-I10 (fiduciary duty with a decidable breach predicate). The guardian SHALL owe the ward a fiduciary duty of loyalty and care mirroring the O1/GOV fiduciary structure; that structure's breach test and remedies are referenced, not restated. Purpose: reuse one canonical fiduciary standard rather than invent a weaker one for the vulnerable. Predicate (breach is found where any hold): (a) the guardian took a benefit from the ward's estate not authorized by the delegation or a court; (b) the guardian acted outside the CAP-C4 scope; (c) the guardian substituted where CAP-I6 required support; (d) the guardian decided against the ward's clear CAP-C7 will, or failed to attempt to ascertain a present will where no CAP-C7 record exists, without recorded protective necessity; (e) the guardian failed the CAP-I8 logging duty. Enforcement hook: breach voids the tainted act, restores the ward, and books the guardian's gain and the ward's loss under V4 per axis.

- CAP-I11 (named complainant path). Standing to challenge a capacity-finding, a guardianship order, or a guardian act SHALL be held by: the ward always and non-waivably; any person the ward names; a defined class of close relations; and a public guardian-of-last-resort office that any person or the register itself MAY alert. Purpose: an isolated ward cannot enforce their own rights, so the path must not depend on the ward's unaided initiative. Both flanks: to deter harassment of good-faith guardians, a complainant found to have brought a knowingly false incapacity or breach claim bears the guardian's and ward's harm under V4, and repeat abusive claims MAY be gated, but the ward's own standing is never gated.

- CAP-I12 (restoration is a live, reversible path, not a ratchet). A capacity-finding and a guardianship order SHALL be reviewable on the ward's request at any time, on the mandatory review date, and on a material change of circumstance; the burden on review sits on continuation of the restriction, not on the ward to re-prove capability. Purpose: guardianship must be as easy to leave as the person's recovered capacity allows. Predicate: on review, absent affirmative evidence that the found deficit persists in the named domain, the finding reverts to PRESUMED-CAPABLE. Enforcement hook: a guardian who obstructs, delays, or conceals a review triggers CAP-I17 and the CAP-I11 office MAY suspend the delegation pending review.

- CAP-I13 (temporary and total incapacity: narrow and loud). Where a person is transiently and totally unable to decide (unconsciousness, acute crisis) and no CAP-C4 guardian and no CAP-C7 standing instruction covers the moment, a decision necessary and non-deferrable to prevent serious harm MAY be taken by the narrowest emergency path, and SHALL be logged loudly under X3 and reversed to the person's control the instant capacity returns. Purpose: neither abandon the unconscious person nor let emergency become a standing seizure. Predicate: an emergency decision that was deferrable to the person's imminent return of capacity, or that exceeded preventing serious harm, is void and booked as harm. Binding: medical emergencies route through B10 health consent and emergency rules; this invariant covers the non-health decisional gap only.

- CAP-I14 (no capacity finding waives a floor or a non-priceable right). A finding of incapacity SHALL NOT waive, suspend, or transfer any capability floor, the consent floor, a non-priceable A18 right, the suspensive-contest guarantee (A18-I34), or the whistleblower guarantee; the O5 floor-waiver-void rule governs and is referenced. Purpose: incapacity narrows who exercises a decision, never whether the person still holds their floors. Predicate: a guardianship instrument purporting to waive the ward's floors or dignity core is void ab initio as to those terms. Both flanks: the guardian MAY exercise floor-delivered goods on the ward's behalf within scope (route the COMP-I17 own-account delivery), but MAY not extinguish them.

- CAP-I15 (guardian eligibility, conflict screening, and continuing independent supervision). A guardian SHALL be screened for conflict of interest against the ward's estate and against the O1 acting-in-concert graph, and a person or entity that stands to inherit, be paid from, or otherwise gain from the ward's estate SHALL NOT hold unsupervised authority over that same estate without a recorded independent check. The independent check SHALL mean a recurring review by a party with no payment or inheritance nexus to the ward, selected by the CAP-I11 office and never by the petitioner or the guardian; it SHALL be ongoing and proportional to the volume and value of estate-touching acts, tied to the CAP-I17 capture predicate and the CAP-I12 review cadence, with each estate-touching act logged under CAP-I8 and periodically re-checked by the CAP-I11 office. "Supervised" means this recurring independent review, not a one-shot appointment check. A guardian who is the ward's residential provider or payee SHALL NOT hold both the contacts-and-communications domain and the estate domain unless an independent reviewer co-signs every estate-touching and contact-restricting act. For any standing psychoactive medication, sedation, or physical or chemical restraint consent, an independent medical review by a clinician with no nexus to the guardian or institution SHALL be obtained and logged under CAP-I8 (see CAP-I17 limb (e)); unreviewed standing restraint is presumptively adverse. Purpose: keep the hand that guards separate from the hand that gains, and make supervision continuous rather than nominal. Predicate: an undisclosed conflict is itself a CAP-I10 breach and, if concealed, flips under the A18 concealment class. Both flanks: a family carer who is also a natural heir is not thereby disqualified; the good-faith carer is expressly protected, but their estate-touching acts get the recurring independent check and the CAP-I8 log.

- CAP-I16 (manufactured incapacity is a non-priceable harm). Procuring, fabricating, or exaggerating a capacity-finding to obtain control of a person, their consent, their acts, or a HOLD estate SHALL be a criminal-class A18 harm, prohibited and never priced, borne by the natural persons who acted in concert (O1 concert graph; a person used as a nominee guardian is part of that graph, and an institutional guardian's CAP-I20 accountable officer is the human bearer). Purpose: the most dangerous attack on the vulnerable is the false finding itself, and it must sit above pricing. This invariant is the single owner of the manufactured/fabricated/exaggerated-incapacity criminal-class characterization; PINT cites it by ID for that characterization and adds only its detection signal. Enforcement hook: the tainted finding and every act under it are void; the S1 delegation is revoked; gains are clawed back and booked under V4; concealment of the manufacture carries the perpetual A18 concealment-class flip.

- CAP-I17 (guardianship-capture predicate: looting, isolation, obstruction, restraint). A rebuttable finding of guardianship-capture SHALL arise where a guardian, singly or in pattern: (a) moves ward assets to the guardian's or a concert party's benefit outside authorized scope; (b) isolates the ward by obstructing contacts, communications, the CAP-I11 complainant path, or the CAP-I21 welfare contact; (c) obstructs or delays a CAP-I12 review; (d) fails the CAP-I8 logging duty on estate-touching acts; or (e) administers or consents to medication, sedation, or physical or chemical restraint beyond the ward's CAP-C7 will and preferences or documented therapeutic necessity (restraint for control or convenience). Purpose: give isolation, looting, and chemical or physical control a decidable trigger that a third party or the register can raise without the ward's unaided complaint. Enforcement hook: on the finding, the CAP-I11 office SHALL suspend the delegation, install an interim guardian or restore the ward, freeze the traced assets, and route restitution; unreviewed standing restraint under limb (e) is presumptively adverse; the R1-I39 post-judgment asset-discovery instrument is available only after judgment. Both flanks: a guardian who rebuts each limb with logged, in-scope, ward-interested reasons is cleared, and the good-faith protective guardian who restricts a genuinely harmful contact for the ward's safety, on record, does not meet the isolation limb, nor does a restraint carrying the CAP-I15 independent medical review and documented therapeutic necessity meet limb (e).

- CAP-I18 (the minor and evolving capacity). A minor SHALL hold an explicit, registered default guardianship (CAP-C4) that contracts as the minor's capacity evolves, not a binary switch at majority. Purpose: children are neither property nor miniature adults; their self-ownership grows. Predicate: for a domain with a defined age threshold, authority passes to the minor at that threshold by operation of the register; below it, a demonstrated domain-specific capacity (for example a mature-minor medical consent under B10) SHALL be recognized and SHALL narrow the guardian's authority in that domain. Both flanks: age thresholds protect the child from decisions they cannot yet weigh AND protect the maturing minor from a guardian overriding a decision the minor is demonstrably capable of; the guardian's authority over the minor is itself a CAP-I10 fiduciary duty and is subject to CAP-I16 and CAP-I17.

- CAP-I19 (self-authored future arrangements). A capable adult MAY appoint, in advance and on the register, a future guardian and standing CAP-C7 instructions to govern a later incapacity. A self-authored arrangement SHALL take precedence over an authority-imposed guardian only as to the identity of the guardian for the named domains where it is valid and current, and never over scope, the CAP-I5 least-restrictive requirement, the CAP-I12 review cadence, or the CAP-I14 floor-non-waiver. At activation (when incapacity actually arises), the named guardian SHALL pass the CAP-I15 conflict screen broadened to ANY control gain and not only inheritance, AND a CAP-I5 least-restrictive check, and SHALL remain subject to CAP-I17 capture and CAP-I11 challenge. Purpose: let the person choose their own future decision-maker before the need arises, without letting that choice defeat the protective floors. Both flanks: a self-authored arrangement does not bind if the named guardian is captured or conflicted under CAP-I15/I17, and it cannot waive the CAP-I14 floors.

- CAP-I20 (institutional guardian: a named accountable natural person). Where a CAP-C4 delegation is held by an institutional guardian (CAP-C5), the institution SHALL name in the S4 log, for every scope and every estate-touching act, the natural-person accountable officer who authored it. That officer is the culpable-human bearer for any CAP breach (CAP-I10), manufacture (CAP-I16), or capture (CAP-I17) arising from the act, is an edge in the O1 acting-in-concert graph, and is conflict-screened under CAP-I15 identically to a natural-person guardian. Purpose: an institution cannot dilute culpability into an org-chart; every act traces to a person who answers for it. Predicate: an institutional guardian act with no named accountable officer in the S4 log is unlogged for CAP-I8 purposes and presumed adverse to the ward.

- CAP-I21 (guardian-proof welfare contact: isolation detected by absence). Every ward under a CAP-C4 delegation SHALL receive a mandatory, periodic, private, and un-mediated welfare contact with the CAP-I11 guardian-of-last-resort office on a channel the guardian cannot intercept, attend, or attenuate. Purpose: an isolated ward cannot raise a complaint, so the check-in must reach the ward independently of the guardian, and the ABSENCE of an expected check-in is itself a CAP-I17 isolation signal that the office SHALL act on. Both flanks: a guardian who facilitates rather than obstructs the contact meets no isolation limb; a guardian who blocks, monitors, or attends the contact triggers CAP-I17.

### Protocols

- CAP-P1 finding a capacity limit. Actor: the competent capacity-assessing authority, an independent assessor selected by the CAP-I11 public office (never the petitioner) per CAP-I2, with any resulting CAP-C4 delegation issued by an adjudicator (court A6). Input: a concern raised in a named decision-domain, plus the assessor's evidentiary record and, for any INCAPABLE reduction, the independent B10 health determination appended through the assessor's own controller. Steps: (1) presume capacity (CAP-I1); (2) the independent assessor assesses the specific functional deficit in that domain against CAP-I2, trying support first (CAP-I6), and for any INCAPABLE reduction obtains the independent B10 health determination, with SUPPORTED requiring only the lighter functional-support basis; (3) if support is insufficient, the authority issues a CAP-C3 finding of SUPPORTED or INCAPABLE with domain enumeration (CAP-I3), review date (CAP-I4), and the recorded reasons the less-restrictive option failed (CAP-I5); (4) the registrar appends the finding to the R1 civil register as the output event; (5) if substituted authority is needed, the adjudicator opens a CAP-C4 delegation of narrowest scope with a CAP-I15-screened guardian. Output and audit: the finding and the delegation are each an S4 audit event readable by the ward and the CAP-I11 path; on a failed predicate (for example a conflicted or petitioner-selected assessor, a missing domain enumeration, or a missing B10 determination for an INCAPABLE reduction) the registrar appends a VOID event readable under S4 rather than recording the finding.

- CAP-P2 exercising guardian authority. Actor: the guardian (for an institutional guardian, the CAP-I20 accountable officer). Each act: confirm the domain is in scope (CAP-I3), decide by CAP-C7 will-and-preferences, then best-interests-as-the-ward, or by presently-expressed will where no capable-era record exists (CAP-I9), log the act as an S4 event on the ward's own-account basis (CAP-I8), route any floor-delivered good through COMP-I17 without waiving it (CAP-I14); for any standing psychoactive or restraint consent, obtain the CAP-I15 independent medical review before the act. Output and audit: each act is the logged S4 event; an unlogged act is presumed adverse (CAP-I8).

- CAP-P3 challenge and restoration. Actor: any CAP-I11 complainant or the ward, before the reviewing authority. Any complainant, or the ward, requests review; continuation bears the burden (CAP-I12); on insufficient evidence of persisting deficit, the register folds the status back to PRESUMED-CAPABLE and closes or narrows the CAP-C4 delegation; obstruction routes to CAP-P4. Output and audit: the review outcome is an appended R1 event and an S4 audit entry.

- CAP-P4 capture response. Actor: the CAP-I11 guardian-of-last-resort office. On a CAP-I17 limb (including a missing CAP-I21 welfare check-in) or a CAP-I16 manufactured-incapacity signal: the office suspends the delegation, installs interim protection or restores the ward, freezes traced assets, books gains and losses under V4 per axis, and refers criminal-class conduct to A18; concealment invokes the perpetual A18 flip. Output and audit: each step is an S4 audit event.

### Lifecycle and edge cases

- CAP-L1 birth to majority: registered default guardianship (CAP-I18) that contracts by age thresholds and demonstrated domain capacity, auto-terminating to full self-ownership at majority absent a live adult finding (CAP-I7).
- CAP-L2 sudden total incapacity: CAP-I13 emergency path, narrow and X3-loud, reverting on return of capacity; health decisions route to B10.
- CAP-L3 fluctuating capacity: a person capable on some days and not others is presumed capable and supported (CAP-I6) in lucid windows; a standing guardianship SHALL define which domains it covers only in the non-lucid state and SHALL NOT displace lucid-window decisions.
- CAP-L4 death: capacity and guardianship auto-terminate at death (CAP-I7); the estate handoff is PROP-I5 succession, not a guardian power; a guardian has no post-mortem authority over the estate by virtue of the guardianship.
- CAP-L5 guardian incapacity, death, resignation, or removal: the delegation lapses to the CAP-I11 office pending a successor; no gap confers status-based authority on a relation (CAP-I7).
- CAP-L6 disputed cross-frame acts: where a decision sits in the family frame (marriage, dependants) versus the citizen frame, the applicable frame's participation condition theta governs which acts a guardian may take at all; a guardian never acquires frame-participation the ward does not hold.
- CAP-L7 the recovered ward: on restoration, prior in-scope guardian acts stand as valid when taken (public-faith reliance under R1-I28 protects good-faith third parties who relied on the then-current finding), but tainted or ultra vires acts are unwound under CAP-I10/I17.

## PINT person-layer integrity and capture surface

### Purpose

This section is the integrity and anti-capture surface for the natural person as a living human. It mirrors the OINT section of Organizations-Contracts-and-Employment.md, but where OINT defends the firm-and-contract graph, PINT defends the person-side objects: the person's civil status, their self-owned namespaces (B10 health, B12 life events, K2 acts, X5 interaction side), their HOLD estate, and their capacity and consent. The threat model is capture: any maneuver by which a controlling relationship over a person, or over the value that person holds and is owed, is acquired, forged, or exercised by anyone other than that person acting freely, or is fabricated by a claimant to extract value from the honest.

PINT enumerates the attack surface and closes each attack with a numbered invariant. Every invariant protects both flanks: it protects the vulnerable person against capture, and it protects the honest counterpart, creditor, family member, or fiduciary against false, abusive, or opportunistic claims. PINT does not re-model identity (Citizenship-Membership-and-Personhood.md), the health record (Civic-Health-and-Democracy.md), the civil-register corroboration standards (the CIV section of this spec), the guardianship and capacity shape (the CAP section of this spec), title or succession mechanics (Property-and-Ownership.md, Property-Rent-and-Commons.md, PROP-I5), or the veil and total-life-capture rules (Organizations-Contracts-and-Employment.md). It takes the person-side view and binds to those by citation.

PINT-I1 (framing). Every PINT invariant SHALL be read as protecting two classes at once: the person who is the target of capture, and the counterpart who is the target of a false or abusive person-side claim. No detection signal in this section SHALL by itself constitute proof of wrongdoing; each signal SHALL open a bounded, auditable inquiry under an access contract (S2/S3), never a standing suspicion, a status penalty, or a floor suspension. Purpose: forbid the anti-capture machinery from itself becoming an instrument of capture over the honest person.

### Object model

The objects PINT reasons over, all owned and defined elsewhere and referenced here:

- Person self-ownership root: the controlling meta-object of the person's own namespaces (S1, one controller per meta-object).
- Civil-register entries: birth, death, marriage, divorce, guardianship, name, and capacity events, appended to the civil register mandated by A12, held of record under R1/R2 (append-only, correction-by-new-event); the death-corroboration standard over these entries is owned by CIV-I6/CIV-I7/CIV-I15.
- HOLD estate: the person-side view of what the person owns and is owed (P2 title, M3 movable, U1 building, F8 instrument), referenced not re-modeled.
- Capacity state: a non-authoritative fold over appended capacity and guardianship events; guardianship is an explicit bounded auditable S1 delegation whose shape is owned by CAP-I7.
- Family relationships: marriage, dependency, and household edges as V5 frame memberships with a participation condition theta (the family frame), never as ownership of one person by another.
- Concert linkage: the edge from a natural person to the O1 acting-in-concert and beneficial-owner graph when that person is used as a nominee or conduit (PINT-I15).
- Sole-control attestation: a periodic person-authenticated liveness-plus-sole-control predicate over the self-ownership root (PINT-I25), whose absence is a capture signal.
- Detection signals and their grain: decidable predicates over appended events (S4 read events, register folds) that open inquiry, defined per invariant below, computed cohort-first under S5 and descended to per-person grain only on the PINT-I26 ladder.

PINT-I2 (personhood singularity). Each living natural person SHALL correspond to exactly one self-ownership root and exactly one active civil-personhood record, bound to the anti-Sybil identity bedrock of Citizenship-Membership-and-Personhood.md. Detection signal: two or more active personhood records resolving to the same biometric or identity bedrock, or a personhood record with no bedrock binding. Enforcement: the duplicate is a candidate ghost or Sybil under PINT-I8; adjudication SHALL determine which record, if any, is the true person before any HOLD or status consequence. Both flanks: a genuine person SHALL NOT lose status because a duplicate was forged in their name, and a forger SHALL NOT gain a second acting identity.

### Invariants: identity theft and the stolen-life attack

PINT-I3 (self-ownership is non-transferable by impersonation).
