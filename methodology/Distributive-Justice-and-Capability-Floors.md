> Every order has a theory of *what it guarantees and what inequality it permits*. Axiacracy's
> was implicit. Made explicit: **capability sufficientarianism + a bounded, contribution-
> responsive band of inequality + an anti-concentration ceiling + a generosity premium.**
> This *is* the corridor structure — now normatively grounded.

## 1. The stance

- **A guaranteed floor** — everyone is secured a **threshold of central capabilities**
  (non-negotiable, per cohort). *(the "just" part)*
- **A contribution-responsive band above the floor** — differential access follows earned
  value (draw-rights); inequality is permitted **above the floor** only so far as it neither
  pushes anyone **below floor** nor breaches the **ceiling**. *(the "incentive" part)*
- **An anti-concentration ceiling** — runaway accumulation self-charges. *(the "anti-BigTech" part)*
- **A generosity premium** — donating received value up-levels standing, softening the top.

**The distributive shape = the corridor (floor / target_band / ceiling).** The capability
approach grounds the **floor**; contribution grounds **position in the band**;
anti-concentration grounds the **ceiling**.

## 2. Capability-space, not resource-space (Sen)

Wellbeing is **capabilities** — real freedoms to achieve valued *functionings* — **not
resources or utility**. Two people with equal resources can have unequal capabilities (a
disabled person needs more input for the same mobility). Therefore:

- **Floors are defined on *achieved capability*, not on inputs.** Guaranteeing the floor may
  require **unequal resources** — **equity of outcome-capability, not equality of input.**
- This is exactly **Realized Æ**: realized value = the actual state-change/functioning
  achieved (A6), not nominal resources. A **capability floor = a floor on realized Æ** in the
  wellbeing-relevant axes, adjusted per the actor's **conversion factors**.
- This grounds the **per-cohort floors + Equity/Ombudsman guardian** we already have; and it
  gives **inter-frame routing** a job: knowing an actor's conversion factors (part of its
  frame) lets the state deliver the *right* input to reach the floor efficiently.

## 3. Nussbaum's ten central capabilities → crosswalk to our axes + rights

Nussbaum: institutions must secure a **threshold** of ten central capabilities. They map
cleanly onto Axiacracy — and tell us **which axes carry a capability floor**:

| Central capability | Axiacracy home |
|---|---|
| life; bodily health; bodily integrity | **inviolable rights (A9)** + demographic/health floors |
| senses, imagination, thought; practical reason | **cognitive + epistemic + educational** axes |
| emotions; affiliation | **social_stability + meaning** |
| play | **meaning / culture** |
| other species (nature) | **ecological** axis |
| control over one's environment (political + material) | **political voice** (voting/delegation) + **economic** floor |

So the wellbeing-relevant axes (**meaning, cognitive, social_stability, demographic/health,
economic**) carry a **capability floor**; purely-steering axes need not. This directly grounds
**GATE-2** (which axes P0 instruments) and **P-2** (floor levels).

## 4. Democratic specification + entrenchment (Nussbaum)

Nussbaum: the list and threshold should be **democratically debated and constitutionally
entrenched.** Axiacracy: the **axes and the existence of floors are Charter-level
(entrenched, Art. 5–6)**; the **exact threshold levels are set by the weight-voting /
constitutional process (democratic).** Consistent with "floors constitutional, their levels
ratified."

## 5. Positioning vs rival theories of justice

- **vs Egalitarianism** — we permit inequality **above** the floor (incentive); we are
  **sufficientarian**, not equalizing.
- **vs Meritocracy** — bounded: a floor no merit can revoke, a ceiling no merit can breach.
- **vs Rawls** (difference principle, primary goods) — close, but we are **capability-based**
  (what people can *do*), **multidimensional**, and add the band + ceiling + generosity.
- **vs Utilitarianism** — floors are **lexically prior**; we never trade the below-floor for
  aggregate gains.

**Name:** *capability sufficientarianism with bounded contribution-responsive inequality.*

## 6. What this grounds for P0
- Floors are no longer arbitrary numbers: they are **capability thresholds** on the
  wellbeing axes, measured on **realized (achieved) value** adjusted for **conversion
  factors** (P0 may approximate, but the principle is fixed).
- Confirms the **P0-active axis set** (GATE-2) as the capability-bearing axes.
