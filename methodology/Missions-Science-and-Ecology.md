## 0. How the civilization layer works

This is where the model carries the polity's slowest and most contested value: aims that outlive governments, knowledge whose worth is unknowable at proof time, and ecosystems that regenerate on horizons no mandate spans. The layer's first commitment is that **slow value is booked honestly in both directions**: neither inflated into propaganda by premature certainty, nor starved invisible by refusing to book what has not yet matured. Provisional credits carry declared confidence tiers from one unified vocabulary, are revalued as evidence matures, and honest revaluation is never failure.

**Missions (D1).** A civilizational mission is a chartered, registered parcel of strategic demand: it pulls production toward an under-served value dimension by paying for verified outcomes on the $ rail, and never commands a producer, sets a price or exceeds the monopsony ceiling. Its objective, kill criteria and series-critical activities are declared at birth on evidence independent of the proposer; money follows verified milestones through independently appointed verifiers; and the death discipline runs one machinery with two branches: honest disclosed lag opens review with continuation as the default, everything else freezes on a hard clock whose lapse defaults to kill. Killing is itself a screened act, because stopping can cross irreversibility thresholds exactly as acting can.

**Science (D2).** Knowledge production is value creation on the same first-class machinery as the economy: research claims with pinned evidence, an append-only lifecycle, replication credited comparably to novelty under a positive independence test, and the epistemic pipeline guarded end to end: claims become the polity's operating parameters only through separated parameter stewards, retraction propagates over the transitive closure of the claim graph, fabrication re-opens downstream bookings while honest error never does, and the anti-Goodhart rule holds: no citation metric is ever a funding or charging basis.

**Ecology (D4).** The ecology axis is made operational through condition accounts: stocks and condition of real referents, distinct from the flows spec's fluxes, measured on steward-owned sampling designs that make mis-siting checkable. Ecosystem services and restoration book as created value under additionality and mandate-set durability; biodiversity and habitat floors are commons floors discharged only by restored measured condition, with escalation gated on attribution so an exogenous drought cannot ratchet a region into a permanent veto.

**Long horizons (HOR).** The binding paradox is resolved: a polity binds future budgets to floors and endowment principals, never future polities' discretion. Endowments are shielded on both the draw side and the asset side, with the payout rule inside the shield; floors are protected against re-parameterization by a non-regression rule with a future-cohort advocate; irreversibility screens fire on acting AND on stopping; precaution is a bounded protocol that must eventually decide; and preservation is a funded standing obligation with named holders of record, drills, and a consent terminus so century archives neither leak nor rot sealed forever.

**Value and integrity (DVAL, INT).** One civilization parameter steward masters one D-layer defaults annex under the published partition with the flows steward; the knowledge and ecology axes book per-axis with no cross-axis netting into floors and no Ae/$ conversion ever; and the capture surface is closed symmetrically: pork meets open-tender flags with teeth, citation cartels meet independence-discounted load, greenwash meets decaying attested baselines, endowment raids meet custodian separation, while the honest actor keeps the two-class contest discipline, finality for honest bookings, celebrated negative results and protected honest lag.

Three commitments recur, matching the rest of the model:

1. **One machinery per question.** One tier vocabulary, one defaults annex, one parameter-steward partition, one contest discipline, one kill and re-authorization machinery, one mission register, one inter-floor priority order: every seam the reviewers found duplicated is now owned once and bound by citation.
2. **The future is a party.** Floors are never discounted and never quietly re-parameterized downward; stopping is screened like acting; series and archives survive their missions; and each generation re-authorizes discretion while floors and principals persist.
3. **Truth has a guarded pipeline.** From pinned evidence through independent replication to steward-adopted parameters, no actor writes its own results into the numbers that price its work, and the honest scientist, steward and contractor are safer under this layer than outside it.

The sections follow: D1 civilizational missions; D2 science and knowledge; D4 ecology as value; HOR long-horizon governance; DVAL civilization in the value doctrine; INT integrity and capture.

## Civilizational missions (D1 core)

A civilizational mission is the polity's instrument for aims no market actor can carry alone at the horizon required: demographic renewal, a science program, a space capability, ecological restoration, an infrastructure program. The market doctrine fixes the mission's entire economic shape before it begins. The market clears and the state prices only the residual; the state's other lawful economic presence is strategic demand, and a mission is exactly that: a large, chartered, registered parcel of strategic demand that PULLS production toward an under-served value dimension by paying for verified outcomes on the $ rail, and never commands a producer, sets a price, licenses a monopoly, or nationalizes a sector to get them. A mission is therefore a first-class object with a charter, not a budget heading: its objective, the cohort it serves, its horizon, its budget envelope, its series-critical activities, and its success and kill criteria are declared at birth, and the whole portfolio lives in one public register, so "what is this state trying to do, at what cost, with what results so far" is a query against the register, never lore.

The life discipline follows from the birth discipline. A mission is chosen by the governance layer through the published decision machinery, born from a measured per-axis imbalance or floor urgency, never from an organ's ambition, and never from the proposer's own uncorroborated evidence. It is funded as strategic demand per C2-I9 through competitive instruments (milestone contracts, prizes, advance market commitments) that clear through venues where feasible, capped by the demand-side concentration ceiling of COMP-I13 so a mission never becomes a monopsonist that sets price and quantity by weight of purse. It executes through ordinary K5 projects and ordinary C1 contractors under ordinary market and A14 authorization rules: mission status confers no procurement privilege beyond the registered envelope, no exemption from the uniform overlay, and no sensing privilege beyond the owner-gated S-cluster rules. Money follows verified delivery: milestones are proven under the single DVAL tier discipline that prefers measured over modeled over default evidence, tranches release only against verified milestones and only behind every published first charge on the commons_rent_fund, and costs book on the $ rail while created value books per axis on the Ae ledgers under the DVAL lag and revaluation discipline, the two rails never netting.

The death discipline is what keeps the instrument honest, and both flanks are catastrophic here. A polity that cannot hold a decade-horizon or century-horizon aim forfeits exactly the class of value civilization is made of; a mission apparatus without kill discipline becomes the planning state through the back door: zombie programs, captured envelopes, command creep dressed as urgency. So kill criteria are as mandatory as objectives, and the layer runs exactly one kill machinery with two branches: honest, fully disclosed lag opens review with continuation as the default inside the horizon, while every other fired criterion freezes new commitments on a hard clock whose lapse defaults to kill. A mission operating past its horizon, or past a sustained kill, without re-authorization is a zombie whose every commitment is unauthorized; continuation requires re-authorization through the same machinery that birthed it, never inertia, and a legitimate mission never dies of gridlock: inside the horizon a cycle that decides nothing books the lapse against the organ that owed the decision. Killing is itself a screened act, because stopping can cross irreversibility thresholds exactly as acting can: no kill, wind-down or disposal above the criticality flag executes without a screen verdict, and series-critical activities keep funding until transferred or formally closed. A failed mission pays its epistemic debt in full: its learnings, negative results included, book as N-layer knowledge, its assets dispose under the property spec, and no successor mission is ever a rename of a corpse.

### Invariants

- D1-I1 (charter at birth): A civilizational mission SHALL exist only as a registered first-class object carrying, at birth: an objective stated as a falsifiable outcome, the served cohort defined per the S5 cohort rules, a horizon, a $-rail budget envelope, a milestone schedule, success criteria, kill criteria, its declared series-critical activities per D1-I19, and the imbalance citation of D1-I3 carrying a declared evidence tier per D1-I11, produced or attested by an organ structurally separate from the proposer. A charter missing any field SHALL NOT register, a birth resting on the proposer's sole uncorroborated evidence fails registration closed, and expenditure under a mission heading with no Active registered charter fails closed at the settlement layer.
- D1-I2 (falsifiable success and kill criteria): Success and kill criteria SHALL be machine-checkable predicates or independently attestable findings over registered evidence, declared at birth and changed only by formal charter amendment. Every kill set SHALL include at minimum: milestone non-delivery beyond the review window (routed per the D1-I16 branches, so an honestly lagged, fully disclosed shortfall opens review rather than firing a fault), envelope exhaustion, and obsolescence of the objective (the outcome achieved by others or the need dissolved). A criterion of the shape "we will know it when we see it" registers as no criterion and blocks registration.
- D1-I3 (selection through the published machinery): Missions SHALL be chosen by the governance layer through the published decision machinery: the charter cites the measured per-axis imbalance or floor urgency it corrects under the D1-I1 tier and independence discipline, allocation follows the published function of imbalance, voted weight and floor urgency, and the birth decision is a logged register event with full change provenance. No organ SHALL birth, extend, or enlarge a mission by discretion outside the machinery.
- D1-I4 (strategic demand on the $ rail): All mission funding SHALL route as strategic demand on the $ rail per C2-I9: $-denominated milestone contracts, prizes, advance market commitments, procurement bids and incentive payments that clear like any order, through C2 venues wherever a venue exists for the subject. Ae is never consideration in any mission instrument; any retained Ae credit is strictly a booked-not-transferred credit to the serving firm's own account per the MVAL-I11 discipline.
- D1-I5 (monopsony ceiling, no emergency shortcut): Each mission's share of any sector's demand field SHALL be capped per COMP-I13 and C2-I9; demand above the ceiling SHALL be split across independent buyers or tendered so the market still discovers supply and price. Missions hold no emergency exceedance: mission demand is never emergency demand, and the RES modulation path is closed to it.
- D1-I6 (never command): A mission confers no authority to direct any producer's output, compel participation, set or cap a price, or requisition capacity. Command over production exists only inside RES pre-authored declared regimes and never under a mission charter; a mission instruction that purports to command a producer is ultra vires and routes to A18 against the issuing steward.
- D1-I7 (floors outrank missions): Mission envelopes draw from the commons_rent_fund only behind every published first charge on that fund, named: the COMP-I17 essential-floor coverage ratios, the RENT-I13 citizen-dividend floor, accrued FVAL-I8 subsidy obligations, and the preservation-class coverage ratios (the HOR-I12 classes typed as HOR-I4 standing obligations). A mission SHALL NOT draw down floor funding, and a below-floor cohort's delivered-floor claim outranks every mission tranche in every period, ordered per the single D-layer inter-floor priority rule published by HOR on the RES-I23 pattern.
- D1-I8 (the portfolio register, single master): The mission portfolio register is the single master of mission state: an R1 instance under an A12 mandate with a public projection carrying the canonical schema for every mission: the charter, the envelope, commitments against it, contractors with their verification predicates, benchmark references, the sunset date, milestone verdicts with their evidence tiers, booked outcomes per axis, amendments, and close-out records; INT-I1 cites this schema and restates nothing. The register is append-only with corrections as new events, and carries an epistemic-revaluation event class: a milestone verdict revalued on a D2 evidence-basis change adjusts unreleased tranches without re-charging honest contractors. For any mission whose horizon exceeds one mandate cycle, D1-P1 auto-emits the HOR-I1 typed commitment entry as a derived projection by reference, and divergence between the projection and this register is a conformance defect against the register steward. Beneficial owners are never force-published: the public envelope carries the contractor's firm identity, the award, the benchmark reference and an S6-style predicate attesting that the beneficial-owner graph was verified per C1-I5 and passed the concert screen; the resolved graph stays sealed with the verifying steward, reachable per case only through the S2 judicial path. A mission absent from the register does not exist as a spending authority.
- D1-I9 (envelope as hard ceiling, no procurement privilege): Mission execution buys under ordinary market rules and ordinary A14 authorization; the registered envelope is a hard ceiling and commitments beyond it fail closed at settlement. An envelope raise is a charter amendment through the D1-I3 machinery, never an administrative top-up, and a mission confers no priority, exemption, or fast lane in any A14 or procurement process beyond being a buyer with a registered budget.
- D1-I10 (ordinary contractors, ordinary discipline): Execution SHALL run through K5 projects and C1 contractors carrying their full published discipline unchanged: rent benchmarked per C1-I7 against independently attested cost, externalities measured and charged on the running ledger per K8-I4, the overlay uniform per MVAL-I5 with no mission exemption. Mission work is never a shield: a contractor's anti-value books exactly as any other run's, and a mission SHALL NOT indemnify it.
- D1-I11 (milestone evidence tiers): Every milestone verdict SHALL declare exactly one evidence tier from the single D-layer set {measured, modeled, default} mastered by DVAL on the F6-I2 pattern; the single conservative-side rule and the tier dominance rule (a booking's tier capped by the weakest evidence tier in its supporting chain, a cross-layer mapping never raising the conservatism class) are stated in DVAL and bind here by citation, never restated. "Attested" is a qualifier only, meaning independently attested under the D1-I12 separation, never a party's self-attestation. Every tier claim SHALL declare its evidence producer and that producer's interest position relative to the tranche. Measured tier requires instrument or registry provenance per the F6-I4 pattern from an instrument or laboratory outside the beneficial-owner graphs of both the contractor and the mission steward, with C1-I5 concert aggregation; modeled tier requires a published versioned model registered as a D2 claim at or above the published grade or independently attested per C1-I7, in neither case authored, funded or controlled by the contractor or the mission steward. Evidence failing these tests demotes to the default tier at the conservative bound. The default tier is the conservative adverse default of the F6-I2 shape, never a party's own attestation: it releases nothing above the published default-tier floor (annex entry per D1-I17), and a party's self-attestation alone releases zero tranche value. A verdict lacking a declared tier is void.
- D1-I12 (independent verification, gated tranches): Milestone verification SHALL be performed by a verifier structurally separated from both the mission steward and the contractor; the steward SHALL NOT self-attest delivery. Verifier selection, rotation, fixed escrowed fees, staking, booked track record and the three-role split follow the INT-I16 verifier-economics discipline; the mission never appoints or pays its own verifier. Tranches release only against verified milestones: an undelivered milestone's tranche remains unreleased until verification, and a failed milestone routes per the D1-I16 branches, so an honestly lagged, fully disclosed shortfall opens review without freezing commitments or firing a fault, series-critical activities continuing per D1-I19. Contests over amounts accrued to verified work follow the single D4-I18 two-class split, which binds here by citation: honest-error and honest-lag bookings carry full suspensive effect, concealment-class charges accrue into escrow during contest with collection deferred, restored with interest if not sustained. Future tranches are never property, and their withholding is never a charge requiring adjudication.
- D1-I13 (two rails, never netted): Mission costs book on the $ rail against the envelope; created value books per axis on the Ae ledgers under the DVAL lag and revaluation discipline: booked when delivered and attested, revaluable when later evidence moves it, never netted across axes and never netted against $ cost. A mission is never "profitable" or "loss-making": it is delivered or not per axis, and spent or not in $, on two lines that never meet.
- D1-I14 (additionality of mission value): Mission-attributed created value SHALL pass the FVAL-I7 additionality discipline: no credit for baseline activity, and no credit for value the market already revealed and credited to the contractors the mission paid. Spillover claims follow MVAL-I12 (multi-source, revaluable, burden on the claimant), and a contractor's genuine additional spillovers earn the matching $ subsidy per FVAL-I8 through the ordinary route, never through the mission envelope. A materially overstated mission value claim is an A18 concealment-class offense against the steward.
- D1-I15 (cohort truth, owner-gated): Mission success reads from delivered outcomes to the charter cohort, sensed at S5 cohort grain through owner-gated reads. A mission acquires no person-grain read, no bulk read, and no sensing privilege beyond the ordinary S-cluster rules, and the claimed-versus-attested delivery gap books against the mission steward's standing.
- D1-I16 (sunset, one kill machinery, one re-authorization machinery, the zombie rule): A mission SHALL end at its charter horizon or on a sustained kill decision, and the layer runs exactly one kill machinery with two branches. Branch (a), honest lag: an honestly lagged, fully disclosed milestone shortfall freezes nothing and fires no fault; it opens the INT-I7 human review with continuation as the default inside the charter horizon, and termination requires an affirmative decision citing a fired charter criterion plus a recorded irreversibility screen verdict per D1-I20. Branch (b), every other fired kill criterion (envelope exhaustion, obsolescence, a sustained concealment finding): new commitments freeze at the next tranche boundary while accrued verified work settles, and review opens on the hard annex deadline, whose lapse defaults to kill. Series-critical activities continue to fund per D1-I19 through either branch. Re-authorization runs exactly one machinery: always through the D1-I3 decision machinery on fresh evidence, with review inputs horizon-neutral per the DVAL convention, which binds here by citation: progress reads against the registered milestone schedule and maturity horizon, never the current-period creditable quantity alone, and the DVAL-I3 lower bound is barred as a review or ranking metric. Every Nth renewal (N an annex entry) escalates to a higher or broader organ with a published continued-justification finding against the charter's own criteria, never a necessity finding, on the RES-I22 Nth-renewal shape; the escalation caps at the terminal organ named in the annex entry, where subsequent renewals repeat at constant altitude. Inside a declared charter horizon, continuation is the default: a mandate cycle that produces no decision books the lapse as a named, timestamped decision event against the enumerating organ, contestable at A6, and silence never triggers wind-down inside horizon. Past horizon, silence extends nothing: new commitments fail closed while already-authorized milestone delivery completes. A zombie is a mission operating past its horizon, or past a sustained kill, without re-authorization: its commitments are unauthorized, fail closed at settlement, and book anti-value against the steward. INT-I5, INT-I6, INT-I7 and HOR-P2 bind this machinery by citation.
- D1-I17 (defaults annex entries, contributed not hosted): Every D1 window, fraction and threshold SHALL live as named entries contributed to the single D-layer defaults annex mastered by the civilization parameter steward per DVAL-I15; D1 hosts no annex and names no parameter organ of its own. Each entry carries a conservative activation default so no value is ever null, revisable only with published change provenance under the annex discipline.
- D1-I18 (failure honesty): A killed, failed, or expired mission SHALL close out honestly: its learnings, negative results included, book as N-layer knowledge objects with a public projection; its assets and IP dispose under the property spec, never by silent transfer to a successor or to the steward; its unspent envelope returns to the commons_rent_fund; and the close-out record is a permanent public register entry. No close-out disposal of a registered capability above the annex criticality flag executes without the recorded irreversibility screen verdict of D1-I20, and close-out fails closed without one. A successor mission SHALL cite the predecessor's close-out record and is a new birth through D1-I3, never a rename; silent roll-over of assets, envelope, or claims into a successor without close-out is an A18 concealment-class offense.
- D1-I19 (series-critical continuity): Every charter SHALL declare at birth which of its activities are series-critical: cohort studies, collections, monitoring networks, calibration chains, and any activity whose interruption destroys the series rather than pausing it. Series-critical activities are funded first-charge on the remaining envelope through any review, kill or wind-down, until either transferred to the D2-I8 commons steward or formally closed with the loss booked as an irreversibility event on the HOR-I9 pattern. Interrupting a series-critical activity without that transfer or closure books against the deciding organ, never the performers, and the interruption enters the D1-I18 close-out record.
- D1-I20 (the irreversibility screen gates both ends): The HOR irreversibility screen (HOR-I9, HOR-P3) binds this section by citation and gates it at both ends: D1-P1 registration, every D1-P2 award above the annex screening threshold, and every kill, non-re-authorization, wind-down and close-out disposal of a registered capability above the annex criticality flag SHALL carry a recorded screen verdict, and D1-I18 and D1-P5 fail closed without one. ## Science and knowledge (D2 core)

Knowledge production is value creation, and D2 gives it the same first-class machinery the economy already has. The unit is the research claim: an N-layer object carrying a statement, a bounded domain of validity, a versioned method reference, an evidence basis of pinned references, quantified uncertainty and a full provenance chain, with exactly one S1 controller. Evidence is referenced, never copied (composition Rule 2), and every reference is pinned to an immutable version identifier or content digest, so the polity holds one claim-evidence graph rather than a thousand drifting reprints, and every change of epistemic state (asserted, replicated, contested, superseded, retracted, resolution_unavailable) is an append-only event on the Semantic Timeline, never a silent edit. A claim's confidence is a derived, recomputable quantity read off its evidence graph by a versioned function under a separated steward, not an editorial verdict: no committee, no ministry and no laureate holds a write on truth; only evidence events move the grade. This is what makes science governable without being governed: the state can see, fund and consume the graph while owning none of its content.

The economics follow the market doctrine unchanged. The polity expresses its research priorities as strategic demand on the $ rail (grants, prizes, programs that clear like any order and pull, never command), it funds the knowledge-commons infrastructure as a delivered Art. 6 floor while program funding stays strategic demand, and it books the researcher's civilizational contribution in Ae on the epistemic axis, per axis, never netted across axes and never netted against $. Two corrections to the legacy incentive landscape are load-bearing. First, replication is first-class created value: the independent replication of a load-bearing claim books value comparable to a novel result of the same weight, because the polity's exposure is to the claims its parameters rest on, and the act that independently raises or destroys confidence in such a claim is exactly as valuable as the act that asserted it. The novelty-only incentive is dead by construction, and independence is a positive test, never a presumption. Second, validated science is the paradigm positive spillover, rewarded through exactly one door: research the polity bought is rewarded by its purchase price, and the matching $ subsidy attaches only to the demonstrably additional increment beyond what the contract purchased, so the researcher whose result quietly powers a sector is paid on the rail without ever being paid twice for the same result.

Both flanks of integrity are catastrophic and both are engineered. Fabrication, falsification and plagiarism are concealment-class offenses: they poison the commons and every operating parameter downstream, they carry the full clawback of the concealment class against the fabricator, and they re-open what honest beneficiaries booked on the poisoned basis as unjust enrichment, never as offense. But the chilling flank matters equally: honest error, failed replication, contested findings and good-faith self-retraction are the system working, never offenses, and every integrity charge runs under the single layer-wide contest split, escrow accrual for concealment-class charges and suspensive effect for honest-error bookings, so an accusation is never an unadjudicated career execution and never a fraud float either. Finally, the epistemic pipeline is one-way and stewarded: validated claims become the operating constants of the polity (F6 default factors, C7 appraisal methods, B14 epidemiology models) only through the owning parameter steward's published change discipline, never by direct write, so no laboratory, funder or minister can silently rewrite the numbers the state runs on, and every constant remains traceable through pinned citations to the evidence graph beneath it.

### Invariants

Evidence is referenced, never copied (composition Rule 2), and every evidence reference SHALL be pinned to an immutable version identifier or content digest, datasets and observation acts included; a snapshot used in analysis is marked as a snapshot with source and as-of time (Rule 3). An unpinned reference is unresolvable by construction, and a claim whose evidence basis cannot be resolved by pinned reference SHALL NOT enter any validated status or any parameter pipeline. Any change to referenced evidence is an X1 event on the referenced object that propagates an evidence-basis-changed event to every citing claim, with mandatory recompute and republication of the affected grades; where pinned evidence is withdrawn or becomes unresolvable, the claim transitions to resolution_unavailable per D2-I2.

## Ecology as value (D4 core)

The ecological axis of the Ae vector is the one axis whose referents exist whether or not anyone books them: a river, a soil horizon, a fish population, a wetland, an aquifer, a forest stand. D4 makes that axis operational by giving each of those referents an account of its own. Every ecological asset is a P-cluster object (P3 waters, P5 deposits, P6 flora, P7 fauna populations, P8 ecosystems, P10 soils) carrying exactly one condition account: stock quantities and condition indices, per period, at a declared measurement tier with declared uncertainty, owned or stewarded through S1 and appended on the R2 pattern as append-only entries with witnessed checkpoints and corrections as new entries. The condition account is a state record, and this is the line that keeps the layer coherent: the F6 inventory masters flux, the rate of matter and energy released by an owner into a commons medium, while the D4 condition account masters state, what the receiving asset actually is. A flux is a flow INTO an asset and never an entry in its condition account; a condition index is never an entry in an emission inventory. The two cite each other by reference, so a pressure and its effect are joinable without either becoming a copy of the other, and the mastering table of the flows layer extends unchanged: meter facts master raw quantity, the inventory masters flux, the condition account masters state, and the K8 running externality ledger masters the charge.

From that account the doctrine follows without new machinery. Maintained or improved condition is created value: the steward or owner who performed the maintenance books an ecological-axis Ae credit under the FVAL-I7 additionality discipline against a no-stewardship counterfactual built on an independently attested basis (C1-I7, FVAL-I5, MVAL-I7), and draws the matching $ Pigovian subsidy from the commons_rent_fund on the same rail and timescale that harmful production is charged (FVAL-I8, MVAL-I12). The Ae credit stays non-convertible and never enters a clearing path (FVAL-I3, C2-I3, Charter Art. 13). Restoration is the same thing performed against a degraded baseline, and it binds the F6 removal machinery in full: verified post hoc and never projected, additional against an attested baseline, durability floors set by mandate rather than by the claimant, monitored for the whole credited horizon, reversal liability pre-funded in the latent-harm escrow class, and above all booked only in the account of the asset physically restored and only to the party that restored it. No instrument transfers ecological credit between assets, accounts or parties, at any price, on any rail. The offset market is replaced by the overlay exactly as it is for flux: degraders pay, restorers are paid, and no private instrument nets the two. Symmetrically, no credit of any kind buys back a floor: an ecological credit never discharges a capability-floor breach on another axis, and a credit on another axis never discharges an ecological floor breach or depletion beyond a regeneration bound (C1-I4, MVAL-I10, F6-I9).

Floors here are delivered, not priced. Habitat extent, population viability, water and soil condition are commons floors measured as the condition an asset actually holds, never as a territorial average and never as a payment. Where a floor breach persists, the escalation is the airshed pattern of F6-I21 lifted to habitat and catchment grain, and it is bounded on both flanks: it opens and persists only while the published attribution models assign the breach, above a declared confidence, to the implicated regional pressure set; attested exogenous or transboundary causation suspends the schedule at its current step; the overlay strength dial rises on a published uniform schedule with no per-firm verdict but under a published annex ceiling; mandatory mitigation review opens over the attributed source set with organization-level consequences only; and the escalation discharges on restored measured condition, or, where restoration is attested physically infeasible on the duty-of-means standard, through a mandated adaptation decision rather than a ratchet without exit. A source population cannot collectively buy a habitat, and an exogenous collapse is never charged to a region that did not cause it. On the other side of the same account sits extraction: a K10 operation is an ordinary K8-discipline run that books depletion anti-value at the run, priced only by the uniform published overlay, and the condition account is the evidence of that depletion, with the regeneration bound as a hard limit that a quota may never be issued above. Both flanks are held throughout. ## Long-horizon governance (HOR)

The shared problem of missions (D1), science (D2) and ecology (D4) is time: a civilizational mission outlives the government that authorized it, a research program outlives its funders, and an ecosystem regenerates on a horizon no single mandate spans. HOR fixes the instrument set through which a polity commits across generations without mortgaging them. Exactly three instruments exist: the endowment (capital whose principal, defined in real terms, is shielded together with its payout rule, where raiding the principal is a distinct offense class), the standing obligation (the COMP-I17 standing-reserve pattern extended to multi-decade horizons), and the milestone-gated tranche (never a blank multi-decade cheque). Every instrument and every preservation class carries an obsolescence and deaccession route, so no dead hand survives its purpose. HOR hosts no defaults annex of its own: every HOR window, fraction, cadence, ceiling and threshold is a named entry in the single D-layer defaults annex mastered by the civilization parameter steward (DVAL-I15), per HOR-I24. Every other doctrine survives the change of scale unchanged: the Ae vector is booked per axis and never netted across axes or against $, the market clears and the state prices only the residual, strategic demand pulls on the $ rail and never commands (C2-I9), every read is owner-gated, and every booking carries the single two-class contest discipline stated in D4-I18.

The binding paradox is resolved by splitting what persists from what is re-chosen. A polity MAY bind future budgets to capability and commons floors and to endowment principals; it SHALL NOT bind future polities' policy choices beyond them. Each generation re-authorizes every discretionary mission on fresh evidence through the single machinery of D1-I16; floors and shielded principals persist without a vote, amendable only through the constitutional path with the Charter's unamendable core untouched, and the persistence cuts both ways: a floor may not be entrenched upward by relabeling, and it may not be regressed downward by re-parameterization (HOR-I21). Discount discipline follows the same split: capability and commons floors are never discounted away, a future cohort's floor weighs exactly as the present cohort's, and discounting applies only to discretionary $ streams, with the discount parameter held under the MVAL-I6 anti-capture discipline by the civilization parameter steward (DVAL-I15), an organ structurally separated from every budget it makes affordable. This is the distributive-justice doctrine applied to time: floors are lexically prior, and the below-floor future is never traded for aggregate present gains.

Two failure modes are equally catastrophic on this surface and the section is engineered against both. A raider polity strips endowments, defers floors past mandate boundaries to orphan them, books irreversible harm as a payable line, or simply terminates: the cheapest raid is a kill, so the irreversibility screen fires on stopping, defunding and disposal exactly as on acting (HOR-I9, HOR-P3). Against the raider stand the real-terms principal shield with its shielded payout rule, the custody and related-party discipline of HOR-I18, the no-deferral-laundering rule and the precaution protocol. A paralyzed polity turns precaution into a standing veto and preservation into a locked room; against it, precaution is BOUNDED doctrine with evidence-tiered triggers, deadlines, a decision-forcing tail and a deemed decision at the end of it, indefinite deferral is itself a decision that books accountability, every instrument and preservation class carries an obsolescence route, and preservation duties are verified by drills, not by attestation. Knowledge and asset preservation (archives, seed banks, reference standards, the model of the polity itself) are funded standing obligations, never unfunded immortal duties: the polity's model of itself is an N6 object, physical collections are P and M cluster referents under R5 attested custody carrying N-layer catalogue records, and every class record sits on the R2 append-only pattern. The stewards of century-scale institutions carry pre-settled succession on the R1-I11 register succession pattern with S8 cascade revocation, so no duty ever depends on the survival of the officeholder who currently performs it.

Raiding an endowment is a DISTINCT offense class: concealment-grade on the A18 ladder with the A18-I49 perpetual flip, clawback piercing to natural persons per A18-I45, and personal attribution to whoever directed or recklessly enabled the raid, officeholder or steward alike. - HOR-I5 (milestone-gated tranches, no blank cheques): No discretionary mission SHALL be funded as an unconditional multi-decade cheque. Sunk cost is never an authorization: continuation past a failed milestone requires a fresh decision event, not momentum.
- HOR-I6 (the binding rule): A polity MAY bind future budgets to capability and commons floors and to endowment principals; it SHALL NOT bind future polities' policy choices beyond them. Discretionary missions, tranche schedules and steward mandates are re-authorized each generation per HOR-P2; floors and shielded principals persist without re-authorization, amendable only through the constitutional path with the Charter's unamendable core untouched. ## Civilization in the value doctrine (DVAL)

The D layer creates the polity's slowest value. A civilizational mission pays off in decades, a theorem's applications are unknowable at proof time, a restored watershed is proven only by thirty years of hydrology. DVAL fixes how that value is booked without corrupting the ledger in either direction, because both flanks are catastrophic. A polity that books mission hopes as settled created value turns the Ae ledger into propaganda: the dashboard shows a flourishing civilization while the underlying outcomes never materialize, and every downstream corridor, mandate and standing decision is poisoned by the inflated figure. A polity that refuses to book anything until certainty starves science, missions and restoration of standing and reward for a generation, which is its own form of ledger dishonesty: real created value existed and was never credited. The resolution is the measurement stance the flows layer already proved: outcomes book immediately as PROVISIONAL created value at a declared confidence tier with quantified uncertainty, are credited conservatively, and are revalued on a published cadence as evidence matures. Premature certainty is a booking offense. Honest revaluation, up or down, never is.

The doctrine's spine holds unchanged at civilizational scale. Value books on the Ae vector per axis: the knowledge axis for D2 outcomes, the ecology axis for D4 outcomes, the declared target axes for D1 missions. Axes never net against each other into floors (C1-I4, MVAL-I10), and Ae is never converted to a price (Charter Art. 13, MVAL-I9). The state is a large customer with civilizational preferences, never a planner: missions and science programs pull demand on the $ rail under C2-I9 and MVAL-I11, buy outcomes at venue-cleared prices where a market exists and at C1-I7 benchmarked prices where none does, and sit under the COMP-I13 monopsony ceiling; demand above that ceiling is always split across independent buyers or tendered, because missions hold no exceedance and the RES emergency modulation path is closed to them. The state never sets the price of knowledge or restoration, and mission status confers no exemption from the overlay: a mission that generates anti-value pays for it like any actor.

Civilization also inherits, rather than reinvents, the crediting discipline the economy layer built for firms. Every science and ecology credit passes the FVAL-I7 additionality test under the self-originated cap, and the double-pay trap killed in F6 stays killed here: one measured increment earns exactly one $ reward, the cleared purchase price, or the avoided overlay charge (F6-I12), or the matching FVAL-I8 subsidy, never two of them. Finally, the anti-Goodhart stance: there is no single index of civilization. The dashboard publishes per-axis positions at S5 cohort grain and never collapses them into one number, on the same non-collapse rule that keeps the Trust Vector from becoming a social score.

## Integrity and capture (INT)

The D layer moves value on the state's behalf where no market clears to discipline it. Missions spend strategic demand toward outcomes no buyer reveals a price for, science supplies the evidence that prices everything else, and ecology certifies the condition of commons whose only witness is measurement. Each surface is therefore a capture magnet with a distinct signature: mission capture (pork, missions run as disguised transfers to connected contractors, and empire-building, organs growing missions to grow themselves), science capture (grant monoculture, citation cartels, p-hacking and publication bias as statistical concealment, replication suppression), and ecology capture (paper parks, fake restoration, baseline manufacturing, steward self-dealing). One pipeline unites them: laundering interested evidence into the parameters that price the launderer's own work, which converts a measurement defect into a standing subsidy. INT is the adversarial surface of the whole D cluster: it exists to close these channels without inventing new machinery, by composing the published procurement, measurement-integrity, parameter and offense disciplines the economy and flows layers already carry.

Both flanks are catastrophic here and INT holds them with equal weight. Under-enforcement lets a connected contractor eat the mission budget, a cartel eat the evidence base, and a self-certifying steward eat the commons. Over-enforcement is just as fatal in kind: kill criteria that execute automatically against honestly lagged long-horizon work destroy exactly the missions the layer exists to carry, accusation machinery without suspensive contest chills the honest scientist into publishing only what is safe, and a monitoring regime that treats every steward as a presumptive fraud strangles stewardship itself. The doctrine frame is unchanged and INT adds nothing to it: the Ae vector is booked per axis and never netted across axes or against $, the market clears and the state prices only the residual through the uniform published overlay (MVAL-I5), strategic demand pulls on the $ rail and never commands, capability and commons floors are delivered in kind and funded from the standing reserve (COMP-I17), reads are owner-gated with the state sensing at S5 cohort grain, and every adverse booking is provisional and contestable under the single two-class contest discipline stated in D4-I18.

INT's own objects are deliberately thin: the public mission envelope, the sealed capture flag, the integrity screen, the separation duties, and the verification economy that pays for all of them honestly. Everything with teeth is a citation: the C1-I7 benchmarked procurement and C1-I5 concert screening, the D1-I16 kill and re-authorization machinery, the D4-I18 two-class contest discipline, the F6 tamper and cross-check machinery extended from flux to condition, the MVAL-I6 parameter discipline with its structurally separated steward, and the A18 ladder with its concealment class, perpetual flip and honest-actor finality.
