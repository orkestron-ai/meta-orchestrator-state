> **The common pattern (true of every organ below).** Each organ (1) **owns a value domain**;
> (2) **senses** its domain and **diagnoses imbalance** against the voted weights `w(t)` + floors;
> (3) **generates strategic demand** — proposes missions, incentives, and **financing** to close the
> imbalance; (4) is **scored by a KPI = citizen-attested value delivered**, net of anti-value, keeping
> cohorts above floor, without breaking participation conditions θ; and (5) is filled by **whoever
> (human or AI) delivers more attested value** (§1.1 of the apparatus doc). Its budget share rises with
> `imbalance × weight × floor-urgency`, released in tranches on attested delivery.

## 1. The ministries (executive) — a working catalogue

Real states run ~15–20 departments; here each maps to an **axis or cross-cutting domain**, and each
has a specific *value mechanic*.

| Ministry | Owns (axis / domain) | Core function | How it works with value |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Commons Fund & Fiscal** | the treasury | manages the Commons Fund, the revenue mix, allocation & tranches | routes financing by `imbalance × weight × floor`; releases tranches only on **citizen-attested delivery**; publishes every allocation to the verifiable log |
| **Economic Balance** | economic | markets, competition, concentration | prices the **externality residual** over a market it does not run; applies the **concentration charge**; keeps the economic axis in-band without breaching floors |
| **Compute & Data Commons** | infrastructural / economic (the "new land") | governs AI-compute, models, data, network-effects | measures & collects the **rent** on the new land (observable signatures) → Commons Fund; guards against compute/data concentration |
| **Infrastructure** | infrastructural | energy, logistics, transport, housing stock, resilience | finances infrastructure missions where the resilience signal drops; attested by users' realized service value |
| **Demography & Family** | demographic | households, care, births, age-structure | senses family θ **at the boundary only** (privacy); generates *support* to lower family flow-resistance — never intrudes inside the home |
| **Health** | demographic / cognitive (bodily) | physical & mental health provision | guarantees the **material health floor** (availability); financed to close below-floor cohorts first |
| **Cognitive Health** | cognitive | attention, deep-work capacity, anti-dependency | watches long-lag cognitive anti-value (content dependency) via **aggregate cohort outcomes**; reshapes the demand field with incentives, coerces only on measured inter-frame harm |
| **Education & Human Development** | educational | learning paths, skills, mentorship | scored by learners' **attested** competency gain (not enrolment counts); funds where the educational floor is unmet |
| **Science** | scientific | research institutions, discovery | funds research missions by scientific-imbalance; guards against fraud/irreproducibility (epistemic link) |
| **Epistemic Integrity** | epistemic | health of the shared knowledge commons | runs the **Brier-calibrated expert registry**; guarantees non-purchasable reach; **annotates, never censors** |
| **Culture & Meaning** | meaning | culture, identity, belonging | **senses** meaning in aggregate; may **only** illuminate + incentivize (never coerce on meaning); protects the meaning floor's *availability* |
| **Social Stability & Civic Order** | social_stability | cohesion, conflict resolution, policing | policing acts **under warrant** (graduated intrusion, §0 apparatus); every enforcement act is a logged flow, abuse is chargeable anti-value |
| **Ecology** | ecological | ecosystems, emissions, resources | prices ecological externalities with an **exponential escalator on cumulative degradation** (anti "pollute-as-line-item") |
| **Contribution & Livelihood** | economic / meaning (post-labour) | contribution opportunities, the citizen dividend | administers the **rent-funded dividend** (material floor) + matches citizens to value-creating contribution (post-"jobs") |
| **External Relations** | cross-cutting (new pillar) | diplomacy, treaties, foreign-frame recognition, trade | negotiates inter-polity value flows; recognizes other frames at the boundary |
| **Defense** | cross-cutting (new pillar) | external/digital security | Charter-bound, logged; the domain where **threat-proportional intrusion & command action** are legitimate — only under warrant/emergency, never as default |
| **Emergency / Civil Protection** | cross-cutting (new pillar) | crisis, disaster, pandemic | runs the **state-of-exception** (supermajority trigger, strict sunset, mandatory post-hoc review) |

**Three cross-cutting guardians** (structurally independent of the line ministries):
- **Equity / Ombudsman** — owns *distributional fairness itself*; watches cross-cohort variance and
  below-floor cohorts; the counter-majoritarian organ.
- **Standards & Measurement Authority** — governs the **accounting-rule / value-attribution schemas
  and confidence thresholds** (the "hidden sovereign", B1): every schema change is **sortition-jury
  ratified**, calibrated, adversarially audited, versioned, and appealable. *No line ministry may
  change the rules by which it is judged.*
- **Audit & Integrity** — red-teams the measurement layer continuously, hunts gaming/collusion,
  runs the anomaly detection.

## 2. The legislative branch — how laws and priorities are made

Two modes, both continuous:

**(a) Continuous steering — the demos.** Citizens (and delegated **parties**, which may themselves be
AI agents) set the **weights `w(t)`** and the **floor levels** — always-on, change-rate-limited
(hysteresis), not sessional. This is how priorities move.

**(b) Deliberative law-making — a chamber.** A body that drafts and **ratifies discrete law**: the
specific **coercion-ladder thresholds**, rights specifications, the **revenue mix**, and constitutional
amendments. Composition is a per-polity choice (elected · sortition · hybrid; human · AI). Changes to
the **measurement/accounting schemas** are ratified by **sortition juries** (via the Standards
Authority), so the un-voted coder cannot become sovereign.

**How it works with value:** **a law is itself a value transformer.** Its **realized value** (did it
improve the balance it targeted?) is measured and attested after enactment; a law that produced
anti-value accrues flow-resistance and is revised — **evidence-based legislation with retrospective
scoring**, judged always by **contemporaneous norms** (non-retroactivity). **Parties** are scored on
**representation-fidelity** (did their votes match what their delegators wanted?) **plus** the realized
value of what they passed; low-fidelity or low-value parties lose delegated weight.

## 3. The judicial branch — how disputes are resolved

**Tiers:** first-instance review (independent reviewer, may be AI — speed/consistency) → appeal →
**constitutional court** (apex; **human-reserved** for legitimacy) → constituent level.

**Functions:** adjudicate **contested value / anti-value assessments**; police the **inter-frame /
intra-frame boundary** (the bright-line test that gates coercion, D1); **review warrants** (authorize
graduated intrusion); **constitutional review** (law vs Charter → void if in conflict); try
**rights-violations** (the criminal ladder).

**Due-process spec (first-class, not slogans):** defined **burden of proof** per harm class; **standing**
rules (who may bring a case; anti-frivolous thresholds); **enforcement pauses during appeal** for
reversible coercion; **remedies** (reversal, restitution, remediation routing); **class actions** for
systemic harm. Reviewer independent of the assessed (BR2); bounded rework → escalate (BR4).

**How it works with value:** **a ruling is a value flow** whose realized justice is attested by the
parties + community; the judge's **calibration/TrackRecord** is built from whether rulings held up
(non-retroactivity respected). **AI judges** serve lower tiers where speed/consistency dominate;
**humans are reserved** where interpretation, mercy, and legitimacy outweigh speed — and at the apex
and rights level. Abuse of judicial power is itself a chargeable anti-value.

## 4. A typical municipality — the state in miniature (subsidiarity)

A municipality is a **local value-balancer**; most citizen-attestation happens here, because proximity
gives higher-quality feedback.

**Structure (mirrors the national in miniature):**
- **Local demos** — residents weight-vote *local* priorities **within the national corridors**;
- **Local executive** — a municipal council / Sub-Agent (human or AI) running the local
  sense→diagnose→finance→respond loop;
- **Local court** — first-instance dispute resolution;
- **Local corridors/floors** nested inside national ones; a **local Commons sub-fund** (local rent +
  national transfers).

**How it works with value & money:**
- senses **local** value flows, closes **local** imbalances, financed by **local rent + national
  equity transfers** (so rent-poor regions are not starved — inter-region redistribution runs on the
  same imbalance × floor logic);
- **attested by local residents** (their attestation is the municipality's score);
- **escalates only cross-region imbalance** upward (subsidiarity) — a problem is handled at the lowest
  competent level, and only what a municipality cannot balance goes national.

## 5. Why the whole apparatus coheres
Every organ — legislative, executive, judicial, national, municipal, human or AI — **reads the same
value signal** (the voted weights + floors + the Æ dashboard), is **financed by the same rule**
(`imbalance × weight × floor`, tranche-released on attestation), and is **scored by the same critic**
(the citizens who receive its value). There is no separate "politics" layer: **priorities, money, and
accountability are one transparent value-flow loop**, and each citizen touches it through the six
channels (weight-vote, need, attestation, revaluation-petition, recall, delegation).
