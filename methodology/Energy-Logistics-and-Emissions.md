## 0. How the flows layer works

This is where the model touches physics. Energy, goods and emissions are the continuous flows a polity lives on, and the layer's first commitment is that **metering is sensing**: every dispatched joule, every custody transfer, every tonne of flux is already an append-only ledger event in the register of its owner, so the Value Flow Map of the state reads physical reality through the same owner-gated ledgers as everything else, with no parallel surveillance channel. The mastering chain is stated once and holds everywhere: the metering-point register masters raw quantity, the F6 inventory masters flux and cites meter facts, the K8 running externality ledger masters the charge and cites the inventory line, and everything else is a projection by reference.

**Energy (F1).** Every energy quantity is a typed flow object; generation is ordinary K8 production whose flux lands on the running externality ledger, netting the fuel's pre-charged use-phase component through an explicit ledger join so one tonne of harm is charged exactly once, at the border and at the burner included. Wholesale scheduling clears through C2 batch auctions; inside the delivery interval the balancing steward (the network operator of record, acting under an A14 delegation) activates pre-contracted reserves in published merit order, settled ex post at the interval imbalance price, so real physics and venue doctrine finally meet. The delivered energy floor is the delivery side of the published essential-class floor: a lifeline quantum for every household, funded from the standing reserve, with the subsistence and life-safety bottom absolutely non-severable.

**Goods movement (F3).** A consignment moves leg by leg, each leg one carrier and one custody holder, each transfer a booked act on the timeline; embodied anti-value rides the goods as a projection by reference, never a mergeable copy. Every carrier operating period closes as a mobile source act, so deadheads and repositioning burn nothing invisibly. A de-minimis consignment class keeps parcel-scale traffic at manifest grain without opening a leak, and re-imported goods credit their prior domestic settlement by ledger reference.

**Emissions (F6).** Flux is measured in declared tiers with a ratchet that makes non-measurement irrational: above a published source size, measurement is a duty; on tamper or void, the substituted rate is the worst evidenced, not the class default. Charges flow through threads capped at the measured total, preserving downstream catch-up while forbidding double charges. Removals earn credit only against mandate-set durability floors, monitored for the full credited horizon. Where a diffuse airshed holds a cohort below the clean-air floor, escalation runs at airshed grain against the source set, and discharge is measured only by restored ambient exposure: the floor cannot be bought.

**Networks (NET).** The layer's doctrinal knot, that the state never sets a price but a natural monopoly has no competitive price, is untied with the economy spec's own machinery: mandatory non-discriminatory access, a tariff corridor anchored to the independently attested cost benchmark plus a benchmark-cleared capital return, everything above it charged as pricing-power rent, congestion priced once by auction with revenue segregated from the operator, mandated duties per se prudent and recoverable, and informational separation with zero analytic privilege. Both flanks hold: the operator cannot inflate the corridor, and the regulator cannot confiscate honestly committed capital.

**Resilience (RES).** An emergency compresses the doctrine, it never suspends it. Regimes are pre-authored and drilled; declarations are evidence-pinned, independently registered, judicially ratified and ratcheted on renewal; relay-speed protective action is pre-authorized and regularized after the fact; curtailment serves floors first through published tranche ladders the operator can actually execute; reserves are sized to hazard classes, positioned for corridor loss, rotated on a public calendar; requisition pays interim compensation; and when the metering substrate itself is down, a degraded-evidence regime keeps delivery, settlement and floor verification alive on witnessed attestation until telemetry returns and everything trues up append-only.

Three commitments recur, matching the rest of the model:

1. **One quantity, one master, one charge.** Raw quantity, flux and charge each have exactly one mastering register; every downstream figure cites its master; charge threads never exceed the measured total; and no congestion, residual or tonne is priced by two mechanisms at once.
2. **Floors are delivered in kind, first.** The energy floor, the essential-goods reach and the clean-air floor are delivered quantities with named stewards, funded from the standing reserve, served first under curtailment, verified even when sensing is down, and never severable at the subsistence bottom.
3. **Both flanks under stress.** The honest generator, carrier, network investor and storage trader keep the protected band, suspensive contest, prudence finality and interim compensation; the tamperer, withholder, corridor-inflater and emergency-power abuser meet ratchets, escrow-accruing defaults, per se rules and the offense ladder.

The sections follow: F1 energy; F3 goods movement and logistics; F6 emission and environmental flux; NET network infrastructure commons; NVAL flows in the value doctrine; RES resilience, disruption and emergency.

## Energy (F1 core)

Energy is the polity's most continuous value flow and its most tempting surveillance instrument, and F1 governs both facts at once. Every quantity of energy that moves is a typed flow object: a carrier (electricity, gas, heat, hydrogen, liquid or solid fuel) with a quantity, an interval, an injection and a withdrawal point, and parties resolved through S1. Generation is not a special sector: a generation asset is a K8 production site, its runs book created value and anti-value exactly as any production run does (K8-I1), its combustion and lifecycle flux lands on the K8 running externality ledger with the no-double-charge rule (K8-I4), netting the fuel's pre-charged use-phase component through the F6 fuel-input join (charter C1), and imported energy meets border externality-parity (K8-I6, C2-I10). The physical law that generation equals consumption plus losses plus storage delta is not managed as an opinion; it is appended each interval as a witnessed ledger fact on the R2 pattern, derivable and court-provable, never a stored authoritative figure (R2-I1, R2-I13). Metering is the sensing substrate that makes all of this true, and every meter reading is an append-only ledger entry owned by the metered party, readable by anyone else only through the S-cluster gate: the supplier sees its billing legs, the state sees cohorts, and nobody sees a household's minute-grain life pattern without consent or a judicial contract (R2-I26, R2-I27, R2-I28).

The market doctrine applies without exception: the market clears, the state prices only the residual. Wholesale energy scheduling and trading clear through C2 frequent sealed batch auctions (C2-I4) on the $ rail only (C2-I3); F1 defines energy products and never reinvents venue mechanics, and inside the delivery interval the balancing steward activates pre-contracted reserves in published merit order as venue execution of cleared positions, never as dispatch (F1-I19). The state does not dispatch plants, cap prices, or bless technologies; the uniform published overlay prices each run's externality residual (MVAL-I5), and strategic demand for adequacy, reserves and the floor routes as $ orders under the monopsony ceiling with the single-buyer carve-out guards (C2-I9, F1-I21). Storage arbitrage, buying energy when it is abundant and selling it when it is scarce, is legitimate created value, the temporal analogue of transport, and sits inside the protected free-competition band (C1-I7); only demonstrated pivotality or physical withholding converts a storage position into rent or manipulation. Congestion rent on scarce network capacity, by contrast, is unearned by whoever pockets it; its pricing and routing are owned once, by NET-I8 (F1-I7). Prosumers are producers: self-consumption behind the meter is private life, exported energy is a market flow with the same externality discipline as any run, scaled to its size and concert-aggregated against fragmentation (F1-P4).

Both flanks are delivered. The delivered energy floor is a capability floor in the civic-layer pattern (HACC-I11), the delivery side of the published essential-class floor (M3-I11): a lifeline quantum of delivered energy for every household, including the unregistered and the provisionally identified, funded as a first-charge obligation from the essential-floor standing reserve (COMP-I17), delivered at the market price the state pays like any buyer, and never faked by distorting the price signal. Load shedding under scarcity never touches the subsistence and life-safety bottom, which RES owns and makes absolutely non-severable (RES-I14, A18-I57); any severance of the floor quantum above subsistence exists only inside RES-I4 pre-authored regimes (F1-I11). On the other flank, the honest generator, the efficient storage operator, the aggregator and the new entrant face the overlay and the venue rules, never coercion: every same-tick booking against an energy actor is provisional, suspensive on contest for honest-error classes and escrow-accruing for tamper defaults (A18-I34, A18-I6, F1-I18), and no F1 rule prices, caps or commands a lawful transaction.

### Invariants

- F1-I1: Every energy flow SHALL be a typed flow object carrying a carrier from the closed enumeration {electricity, gas, heat, hydrogen, liquid_fuel, solid_fuel}, a quantity in carrier-native units, a delivery interval, injection and withdrawal service points (U5) on an identified network (U3), and parties resolved through S1. Carrier conversion (generation, power-to-gas, power-to-heat, refining) is a K8 production act that closes one typed flow and opens another; it SHALL NOT occur as a ledger relabeling, so no quantity changes carrier without a booked run.
- F1-I2: Every generation asset is a K8 production site and every generation run is a K8 production run: it books created value and anti-value per K8-I1, its F6 emission flux, depletion and waste land on the K8 running externality ledger at the run as first-class anti-value (K8-I2), and its externality escrow is sized per K8-I12. Fuel-input embodied anti-value is already charged upstream: at any combustion or use act the measured flux charge is reduced by the pre-charged use-phase component carried on the consumed fuel via the F6 running-ledger join (charter C1), and border parity paid on imported fuel is upstream credit at the domestic burner. The residual is priced only by the uniform published overlay (MVAL-I5); the state SHALL NOT dispatch generation by fiat, set a generation price, or grant any per-technology exemption from the overlay. Imported energy, as carrier or as embodied input, is charged at border externality-parity per K8-I6 and C2-I10, subject to the fungible-carrier mix rule (F1-I17) and the transit rule (F1-I22).
- F1-I3: For every balancing interval of every network, the balancing steward (identity per F1-I21) SHALL append the interval balance fact (metered generation, metered consumption, measured or modeled losses, storage delta, interchange) as an append-only ledger entry on the R2 pattern: witnessed checkpoints, no stored authoritative balance, corrections only as later entries (R2-I1, R2-I13). The balance fact is computed at aggregate grain only (charter C30, F1-P1); raw quantity is mastered by the metering-point register and every downstream figure cites it by reference per the NVAL mastering table (charter C50). A residual imbalance term above the published metering-error band (a parameter of the flows parameter steward, charter C53) is a defect that opens an investigation entry, never a silently plugged number. When the metering substrate itself is down under a declared incident, the RES degraded-evidence regime supplies the temporary witnessed-attestation tier (charter C33), and entries estimated under it true up as append-only corrections in the mandated reconciliation window.
- F1-I4: Every meter reading is an append-only ledger entry in the register of its metering point, and the metered party is its owner per S1. Reads are default-deny and flow only as S2 projections: the supplier, the network operator and the balancing steward in its named role (F1-I21) receive exactly the legs their contract or A14 authorization requires for billing and balancing (the counterparty shape, per the R2-I26 discipline); any per-point leg to the steward flows only under a registered standing S2 contract class bound to the balancing purpose at the coarsest sufficient grain, every read S4-logged (charter C30). Governance sensing consumes only S5 cohort aggregates (R2-I27, R2-I28); any person-grain read of a consumption profile beyond the billing projection SHALL require the owner's consent_record or an S2 judicial access contract through an A6 case. Sub-interval household profiles are a catalogued re-identification hazard: eligibility and lifeline questions SHALL be answered by S6 predicates where a predicate suffices, and demanding a raw profile where a predicate suffices is over_collection. Raw readings compact on the F1-I23 retention schedule.
- F1-I5: Meters are attested M8 devices: calibration and sealing are R5 attestations under an A14 authorization, and every reading carries its device attestation chain. Flow at an unmetered, broken or tampered point defaults against the party controlling that metering point at a conservative estimate taken in the direction adverse to that party per affected quantity: high for withdrawal, consumption and flux, zero for injection or export credit and any quantity that would pay the party, with the burden of rebuttal on that party (charter C14); estimation parameters are owned by the flows parameter steward (charter C53). Continued flow at a point with a void meter requires restoration of attested metering within the published window of the defaults annex (charter C54), else the point is refused as a market leg, floor deliveries excepted (charter C8). Deliberate tampering, bypass or falsified telemetry is an A18 concealment-class offense, and a tampered reading is void as evidence against any counterparty. Where a declaration attributes meter or instrument compromise to an external attack, the RES victim-class carve-out applies (charter C35): affected points demote to a neutral estimated tier, the tamper presumption is suspended for the attested victim class, and the fleet re-attestation protocol governs recovery; individual concealment inside the window remains chargeable on specific evidence.
- F1-I6: Wholesale energy scheduling and trading SHALL clear through C2 venues under C2 mechanics unchanged: frequent sealed uniform-price batch auctions (C2-I4), $-only settlement (C2-I3), sealed venue surveillance (C2-I6), suspensive provisional bookings (C2-I11). F1 defines the product grid (carrier, interval, location or zone, firmness) and nothing else; F1 SHALL NOT operate a parallel matching mechanism for scheduling or trading, and any bilateral over-the-counter energy contract above the de-minimis class SHALL be reported to the venue tape for benchmark integrity. Venue exclusivity is scoped to scheduling and trading, explicitly not to real-time physical activation inside the delivery interval, which is governed by F1-I19 (charter C48).
- F1-I7: Congestion rent is unearned. Network-capacity scarcity pricing and congestion-revenue routing have exactly one owner, NET-I8, which F1 binds by citation and never duplicates (charter C17); a constraint priced implicitly by zonal energy clearing SHALL NOT also be auctioned, one mechanism per constraint per interval (NET rule). Two energy facts remain here: physical or contractual withholding of generation, storage or transport capacity to widen a spread is manipulation per C2-I5 and cornering doctrine (COMP-I11); and spreads earned above cleared capacity cost by non-withholding traders sit in the protected free-competition band (C1-I7), the locational analogue of F1-I12 storage arbitrage (charter C25).
- F1-I8: A retail supply contract is a commercial contract whose terms SHALL be published in a comparable machine-readable form: energy price, network charge and overlay pass-through separately lined. Switching supplier SHALL be low-friction with no exit foreclosure; bundling energy with debt, housing or employment so as to foreclose exit routes to the C1-I15 total-life-capture class. A retail supplier above the MVAL-I8 market-power threshold faces the C1-I7 rent decomposition on its retail margin against independently attested cost (C7), never its self-reported cost.
- F1-I9: The delivered energy floor is a Charter Art. 6 capability floor: a published lifeline quantum of delivered energy per household per interval, differentiated only by objective need class (climate zone, household size, attested medical dependency via an S6 predicate that reveals no diagnosis). It is a DELIVERED floor in the civic pattern (HACC-I11): delivery is the discharge condition, not budget allocation. It reaches every household including unregistered persons under a provisional identity, and non-payment, debt, or contract termination SHALL NOT reduce a household below the floor quantum. F1-I9 is the single owner of the household energy floor across the layer (NVAL-I10 ownership map, charter C29): it is the delivery side of the published essential-class floor machinery (M3-I11), not new machinery; F3 covers settlement-level logistics reach for goods classes only and cross-references F1 for energy carriers, and NET keeps only the no-disconnection duty citing F1-I10.
- F1-I10: The floor is funded, never price-faked. Floor energy is bought at the market price by the floor steward (named in the RES register, charter C40) as $ strategic demand on the state's account clearing like any order (C2-I9), under the single-buyer carve-out and guards of F1-I21 (charter C21), funded first-charge from the essential-floor standing reserve that recovered charges replenish (COMP-I17), decoupled from same-period recovery volume. Exactly one COMP-I17 first-charge draw exists per household energy floor; NET's no-disconnection duty cites this rule and opens no second draw (charter C29). The floor SHALL NOT be delivered by capping the market price, obligating suppliers to sell below cost, or netting the cost against any Ae quantity (C2-I3, Charter Art. 13).
- F1-I11: Load shedding executes only through the published, machine-checkable curtailment ladder, and the ladder is the compliance standard (charter C32). Floor-quantum household delivery and attested life-safety loads (B10 medical dependency, sensed by predicate) are the last tranche and restored first; discretionary classes shed first. The bottom layer is owned by RES and bound here by citation: the A9 subsistence quantum and attested life-safety loads are absolutely non-severable (RES-I14, A18-I57), and any severance of the Art. 6 floor quantum above subsistence exists only inside RES-I4 pre-authored regimes; F1 authors no severance rule of its own (charter C28). RES-I4 is restated as a design duty on the ladder plus a best-feasible-granularity duty: where per-point control exists, floor quanta are protected per point via S6 predicates; where only feeder-level control exists, the plan documents the limit; cohort-grain post-hoc audit at S5 grain is the breach test. The priority-flag set is held by the OPERATOR under a purpose-bound S2 contract limited to curtailment execution and restoration, S4-logged, zero-analytic-privilege, pruned on cadence, and its compromise is a B10-grade breach (charter C32). Every shedding act is a logged event bound to a declared X3 scarcity condition, except pre-authorized automatic protective actions per the RES rule (charter C34): they execute against published technical triggers without a live declaration, log as K2 acts at execution, and force a declaration-or-stand-down decision within the fixed short window; an automatic action outside its published trigger envelope books anti-value against the operator. Shedding that deviates from the published ladder, or that lands disparately on a cohort, is a floor breach sensed at S5 cohort grain and routed to A18; a commanded curtailment of a cleared position settles per F1-I20.
- F1-I12: Storage arbitrage is created value, not rent. The margin earned by buying, storing and reselling energy across intervals or locations through the venue is protected within the free-competition band (C1-I7, FVAL-I6) and SHALL NOT attract a rent charge absent demonstrated pricing power under the MVAL-I8 threshold test (a pivotal storage position in an islanded pocket is the paradigm case). Storage losses are ordinary conversion losses booked in the flow object; storage sited to relieve a constraint earns the cleared price, never a regulated premium.
- F1-I13: Flexibility is a market product. Demand response, curtailable load and dispatchable flexibility clear through C2 as attested deliverables. The counterfactual baseline against which delivered flexibility is measured SHALL be independently benchmarked (C7 discipline, mirroring C1-I7), never self-declared; baseline inflation is concealment-class (A18). Participation is consensual: conditioning an essential service on flexibility enrollment carries the coercion presumption of the S2 prohibited-demand schedule, and an aggregator is a C1 firm carrying the full C1 discipline over its pooled positions, whose pooled exports book at run level per the concert-aggregation rule (F1-P4, charter C10).
- F1-I14: Imbalance is priced uniformly, not punished. A party's deviation from its cleared position settles at the interval imbalance price (F1-I19), identically for all parties; no per-firm penalty, forecast-accuracy license, or administrative fine attaches to honest forecast error, and a deviation under a logged shedding act settles per F1-I20 and is excluded from C2-I5 screening (charter C49). Reserve and balancing capacity are procured by the balancing steward as $ strategic demand on the state's account through C2 (C2-I9) under the single-buyer carve-out of F1-I21 (charter C21), and gaming the imbalance settlement by deliberate mis-nomination is manipulation per C2-I5.
- F1-I15: The network and balancing operator is a stewarded essential-function entity: systemic by construction, it carries the COMP-I16 duties (independently sized bond, resolution plan, a ring-fenced transferable essential-function unit that keeps the grid balanced through any resolution) and, where no feasible bond covers the cascade, the hard structural cap. The operator SHALL NOT hold a trading position on any venue it balances beyond a published, sealed balancing envelope, and its operational read of flow data confers zero analytic privilege in both its operator and its balancing-steward roles (the R2-I27 rule applied to grid telemetry, extended to the steward role by name per charter C21).
- F1-I16: The polity maintains a strategic energy reserve, and F1 defines only the energy product grid (fuel stocks, storage capacity, contracted capability, by carrier, tier and firmness) and the energy-specific adequacy inputs to sizing. The reserve lifecycle is owned by RES and bound here by citation (RES-I6, RES-I7, charter C31): sizing anchored to the hazard-class catalog with parameters under the MVAL-I6 discipline, funding per COMP-I17, declared triggers, release, replenishment, rotation and dispersion. Release MAY flow into a declared regime's allocation under a live declaration; all above-floor volume releases through the venue at the clearing price, and the reserve SHALL NOT be cycled to manage or target a price level, which would be state price-setting in violation of the residual doctrine (MVAL-I1).
- F1-I17: Origin and greenness are attestations, not vibes. A certificate of origin is an R5 attestation bound to a specific metered generation interval, single-use, retired on claim, with the embodied anti-value of the certified energy carried per K8-I4 and MVAL-I2 so a green claim and an externality charge can never double-count or double-launder the same quantity. For a fungible grid carrier, the attestation SHALL use the exporting system's measured average or published marginal mix per interval, verified against the interconnector physical schedule; contractual allocation below the physical mix is barred, and a bare certificate unbacked by the physical mix takes the default-high rule (charter C7); transit status is governed by F1-I22. An overstated, double-claimed or unbacked origin claim is A18 concealment-class with clawback, mirroring FVAL-I7 spillover discipline.
- F1-I18: Every same-tick booking F1 generates against a party is a reversible provisional entry that splits by class (charter C8). Honest-error bookings (imbalance charges, disputed measured figures) carry full SUSPENSIVE effect on contest per A18-I34: accrual suspends on contest and restores with interest if not sustained. Tamper and unmetered-flow defaults accrue into escrow during contest: collection is deferred, accrual never suspends, and the escrow restores with interest if the finding is not sustained, mirroring F6-P5. No forfeiture accrues before an A6 finding or the close of an uncontested window (defaults annex, charter C54). Physical delivery obligations, including the floor and the curtailment ladder, are never suspended by any monetary contest; a regime-commanded curtailment settles per F1-I20.
- F1-I19: Continuous balancing (charter C48). C2 batch auctions govern scheduling and trading; inside the delivery interval the balancing steward activates pre-contracted reserve products (procured per F1-I21) in published merit order. Activation is venue execution of a cleared position, not dispatch and not a parallel matching mechanism (F1-I6). Activations and deviations settle ex post at the interval imbalance price, derived from the last batch clearing price by a published formula carried in the F1 product grid; the maximum batch cadence for the shortest balancing product is a published product-grid parameter.
- F1-I20: Commanded curtailment settles (charter C49). A regime-commanded curtailment of a cleared position settles the shortfall at the interval imbalance price (F1-I19) with the declaring organ as counterparty, funded per COMP-I17. Each settlement SHALL bind one-to-one to a logged shedding act (F1-I11), and deviations under a logged shedding act are excluded from C2-I5 manipulation screening.
- F1-I21: Single-buyer system functions and the balancing steward (charter C21). Balancing, system reserves and floor procurement are physically single-buyer: they carve out of the C2-I9 and COMP-I13 ceilings with substitute guards in place of fictitious splitting: an independent procurement agent or mandatory published tenders, product-level unbundling of reserve classes, and corridor treatment of cost pass-through. The balancing steward IS the network operator of record acting under an A14 authorization as delegated agent of the adequacy organ; its reserve procurement clears as C2-I9 strategic demand on the state's account, and the F1-I4 balancing-leg grant and the R2-I27 zero-analytic-privilege rule extend to the steward role by name.
- F1-I22: Grid transit parity (charter C7). Grid transit is defined by netted physical interchange per balancing interval on the F1-I3 balance-fact pattern: only the net physically wheeled quantity earns transit status, and nominated transit above net physical interchange books as import at border externality-parity (K8-I6, C2-I10).
- F1-I23: Retention and compaction (charter C52). Raw sub-interval readings retain for the published window of the defaults annex (activation default 13 months, charter C54), then compact to billing-interval aggregates under a witnessed checkpoint hash that preserves court-provability. Charging, balancing and seam reconciliation SHALL read only aggregates after the window, joining at the aggregate key.

### Protocols

- F1-P1 (interval close and balance fact): 1. Assemble the interval quantities as operator-side aggregates or by distributed secure aggregation (the S5-I12 pattern): person-grain readings never leave the owner's register (F1-I4), and any per-point leg the steward needs flows only under a registered standing S2 contract class bound to the balancing purpose at the coarsest sufficient grain, every read S4-logged (charter C30). 2. Compute the balance fact (generation, consumption, losses, storage delta, interchange) and append it with witnessed checkpoints per F1-I3. 3. Settle each party's deviation at the interval imbalance price (F1-I14, F1-I19), booking charges per the F1-I18 split; commanded-curtailment shortfalls settle per F1-I20. 4. If the residual term exceeds the metering-error band, open an investigation entry and default unexplained flow against the controlling metering parties, jointly and severally where several, per F1-I5 and the F3-I6 pattern (charter C14). 5. Emit only S5 cohort aggregates to governance sensing; after the F1-I23 window, reconciliation reads only compacted aggregates.
- F1-P2 (wholesale clearing and residual carry): 1. Define the product (carrier, interval, zone, firmness) in the F1 product grid. 2. Hand the book to the C2 venue: sealed batch collection, uniform-price clearing, reveal (C2-I4, C2-P2). 3. Carry each cleared quantity's embodied anti-value on the flow object and charge only measured residual minus already-charged upstream on the K8 running ledger (K8-I4, MVAL-I2), including the fuel-input use-phase pre-charge joined per the F6 fuel-input join (charter C1); apply border parity to imports (K8-I6) with the F1-I17 mix rule and the F1-I22 transit rule. 4. The locational spread on constrained capacity is congestion revenue under NET-I8, the single owner of its pricing and routing; F1 routes nothing (charter C17). 5. Retire any origin attestation claimed against the delivery per F1-I17.
- F1-P3 (floor delivery and scarcity shedding): 1. Register each household's lifeline quantum and need class, using S6 predicates for medical dependency and provisional identity where R4 anchoring is absent (F1-I9); the operator holds the predicate-derived priority flags under the purpose-bound S2 contract of F1-I11 (charter C32). 2. Procure floor energy as $ strategic demand through C2 under the F1-I21 carve-out guards and fund it first-charge from the essential-floor standing reserve (F1-I10, COMP-I17). 3. In normal operation, meter floor delivery and treat the floor obligation as discharged only on delivered energy, booked per household interval. 4. On a declared scarcity event under the RES declaration pattern, execute the published curtailment ladder with floor and life-safety tranches last and restored first (F1-I11); pre-authorized automatic protective actions run per the RES rule (charter C34); log every shedding act against the declaration and settle commanded curtailments per F1-I20. 5. After the event, run the S5 cohort audit for disparate shedding and route any floor breach to A18.
- F1-P4 (prosumer and flexibility onboarding): 1. Register the service point and its attested meter (F1-I5); behind-the-meter self-consumption remains unread at person grain (F1-I4). 2. Classify the party with the de-minimis export threshold concert-aggregated per C1-I5: a common beneficial owner, common operator or common aggregator counts as one party, and an aggregator's pooled exports book at run level (F1-I13); below the aggregated threshold, sector-default externality figures apply, split by attested generation technology with a fuel-purchase input proxy at cohort grain so a fuel-buying party cannot book the solar-blended default (charter C10); above it, book exports as K8 runs with run-level externality accounting (F1-I2). 3. For flexibility offers, fix the independently benchmarked baseline before the first cleared offer (F1-I13). 4. Clear exports and flexibility through C2 like any order; settle on the $ rail. 5. Audit delivered flexibility against the baseline; route baseline inflation to A18 concealment.
- F1-P5 (strategic reserve cycle): The reserve lifecycle (sizing, procurement, custody, release, replenishment, rotation) is owned by RES and runs on RES machinery (RES-I6, RES-I7, charter C31, charter C55); F1 supplies only the energy product grid and adequacy inputs per F1-I16.

### Interfaces

## Goods movement and logistics (F3 core)

Movement is the economy's circulatory system, and F3 refuses to treat it as a new substance. A consignment is the flow object: a declared, bounded set of M3 tradable goods in motion between states of rest, moved leg by leg, each leg one carrier and one custody holder, each leg a K2 act realized over X1 occurrences on the Semantic Timeline. Movement that carries no consignment still burns fuel, so every vehicle operating period closes as a source act of its own. F3 adds no new value semantics. The good's provenance chain (M3-I4) and its position on the embodied anti-value running ledger (K8-I4) simply keep accumulating while the good moves: the transport residual joins the same ledger under the same no-double-charge rule, the fuel's pre-charged use-phase component (K8-I14) joins at the burner so a tonne of transport combustion is charged exactly once, and transport emissions follow the goods across every border per the parity doctrine (K8-I6). The market clears freight and the state never sets a rate; the state prices only the residual the movement generates and guarantees only the floor.

Three property boundaries hold the section together. Custody is never ownership: a carrier holds and cares, it never gains disposal authority, and the custody chain is an append-only R2-grade record in which every transfer is a booked, signed act and every dispute routes through the A18 machinery with suspensive contest. Infrastructure is U1-class property: a port is a chokepoint before it is an asset, so terminals, hubs and warehouses sit under the concentration ceiling, the essential-class open-access duty, and the ring-fenced continuity discipline of COMP-I16. Data about movement belongs to whoever the data is about: the shipper owns the consignment record, the carrier owns fleet and telemetry data, the facility controller owns throughput data, and every cross read is an S2-contracted S3 projection, S4-logged, with the state sensing at S5 cohort and corridor grain only.

The floor is a delivery, not a price. The polity does not run the trucks; it publishes a delivered-flow floor for essential goods per settlement, satisfies it through the market where the market serves, and has its named floor steward procure the unserved remainder as $-rail strategic demand funded from the COMP-I17 standing reserve, so delivery never depends on same-period charge recovery and never becomes a freight price control; the energy floor itself lives in F1, and F3 only carries the fuel. Both flanks are catastrophic on this surface: a leaky custody chain launders provenance and residual, while an over-instrumented one turns every carrier into a state sensor. The invariants below hold both.

### Invariants

- F3-I1 (consignment as flow object): A consignment SHALL be the unit F3 governs: a declared, bounded set of M3 goods (serialized references or fungible quantities per holding key) bound for movement, with exactly one shipper of record as its S1 controller. The consignment references its goods and never copies them (composition Rule 2), and it references each good's provenance chain (M3-I4) and the good's position on the K8-I4 running ledger as a projection by reference keyed to that ledger position, never a mergeable copy, per the flows-layer mastering rule recorded in NVAL. A fungible quantity that cannot resolve a lawful-acquisition custody attestation SHALL NOT enter a consignment as clean stock (M3-I4, M3-I5).
- F3-I2 (leg grammar): A movement SHALL decompose into legs, each with exactly one carrier and exactly one custody holder for its duration. Each leg is a K2 act realized over X1 occurrences (departure, waypoints, handover, arrival, condition events) appended to the Semantic Timeline per K2-I1; a doing that spans two carriers or two custody holders is two legs under the K2-I3 boundary discipline, and deliberate fragmentation or fusion of legs to obscure attribution or pricing is an aggravating A18 basis, never an escape.
- F3-I3 (custody chain as append-only record): The custody chain SHALL be an append-only R2-grade record over the consignment: every custody state change is a booked event, corrections are new events, and no entry is ever rewritten or deleted. A gap or unresolvable link in the chain is a provenance defect that shifts the burden of lawful custody to the current holder (M3-I4) and excludes the affected quantity from clean commingling until cured.
- F3-I4 (transfer as dual-attested act): A custody transfer SHALL be booked as a signed K2 act (K2-I5) attested by both transferor and transferee, or by the transferor plus an authorized witness where the transferee's attestation is technically unavailable, with the transferee's confirmation appended within the confirmation window of the flows-layer defaults annex. Until the transfer is attested, custody, care duties and liability remain with the last attested holder. A disputed transfer is carried contested and routes to A18 with suspensive contest per A18-I34 and the uncontested-window rule of A18-I6.
- F3-I5 (custody is not title): Custody SHALL convey possession and care duties only, never S1 control. A custody holder cannot transfer title, pledge, or encumber the goods it holds; any such filing fails the M3-I7 encumbrance-authority check and fails closed. A carrier's or warehouse operator's lien is lawful only as an F8/PROP security interest perfected on the priority ladder with the controller's second-channel consent, never as an implied custody right.
- F3-I6 (loss and damage attribution): Loss, damage or condition breach SHALL be booked as an X1 effect-occurrence with a cause-ref locating it in a specific leg, and liability attaches to that leg's custody holder as a care-duty breach. Where the occurrence interval cannot be located, liability SHALL default to the holder or holders of the unattested or unmonitored interval, jointly where several, so an evidentiary gap is borne by whoever failed to attest, never by the shipper.
- F3-I7 (subcontracted carriage): A carrier that subcontracts a leg SHALL split it into sub-legs, each with its own attested custody holder, and SHALL remain jointly and severally liable for the sub-legs it contracted and profits from, mirroring the K8-I13 commissioning-firm rule; the subcontractor's inadequate escrow does not cap recovery against the contracting carrier.
- F3-I8 (carriers as market actors): A carrier is a C1 firm and its market conduct is governed by C2 and COMP with no modal carve-out: the state SHALL NOT set, cap or floor a freight rate (C2-I1); freight clearing MAY run through C2-I4 sealed batch auctions per corridor and time window; carrier rent is benchmarked only against independently attested cost per C1-I7 with the free-competition band protected; and capacity withholding or corridor cornering that corrupts price discovery is a C2-I5 and COMP-I11 offense, aggravated where it breaches an essential-access floor.
- F3-I9 (infrastructure as U1 property): Terminals, hubs, warehouses, ports and transfer facilities are U1-class property objects, each with a single S1 controller, subject to the concentration ceiling with acting-in-concert aggregation (U1-I7). A facility in an essential logistics class SHALL carry an open-access duty on published, non-discriminatory terms, and a systemically central facility SHALL maintain a pre-committed, ring-fenced, transferable essential-function unit per COMP-I16 so movement continues through the operator's failure without the operator's cooperation.
- F3-I10 (warehouse attestation backing): A warehouse or terminal custody attestation is the witnessed M2 backing that M3-I5 requires of every fungible holding credit, and the attesting operator is liable for the stock it attests. An over-attested or unbacked attestation fails closed at append and opens an S7 audit_evasion inquiry; attested stock reconciles to physical stock at witnessed checkpoints per M3-I5.
- F3-I11 (transport residual follows the goods): Each leg's anti-value (F6 emissions, plus measured noise and spill harm) SHALL be measured at the leg, booked as V4 against the carrier as controller of the movement act, priced by the uniform published MVAL overlay (never a per-carrier verdict, MVAL-I5), and appended as charge lines on the goods' K8-I4 running ledger through the consignment's projection by reference, so the total charged over goods plus movement never exceeds the measured total and transport emissions ride the goods instead of vanishing between M3 transactions. The embodied anti-value of the fuel consumed on a leg counts as already charged upstream: the measured combustion charge SHALL be reduced by the pre-charged use-phase component carried on the consumed fuel for the same substance and quantity, via the F6 running-ledger join keyed to the fuel's residual_ledger (K8-I14), and border parity paid on imported fuel is upstream credit at the domestic burner. Congestion is not an F3 harm: on a classified corridor capacity scarcity is priced solely by the NET-I8 mechanism, and on an unclassified corridor a published factor held by the flows parameter steward MAY price congestion harm, declared mutually exclusive with NET pricing per corridor per interval, so exactly one mechanism prices any given constraint.
- F3-I12 (border crossing and parity): A consignment crossing an A16 border SHALL settle import parity per K8-I6 and C2-I10 with the transport legs' embodied residual included in the parity base. A foreign origin or transport charge is a credit only against a verified equivalent charge net of origin-side rebates, exemptions and matched subsidies, never a waiver; a re-imported good whose residual_ledger shows a prior domestic settlement (K8-I5 reversion or completed running-ledger charges) SHALL receive that settlement as a full parity credit, resolved by ledger reference rather than foreign attestation. Customs inspection SHALL consume the consignment's declared projection (a statutory-gate read under S2 discipline), never a raw namespace dump. A transit-only consignment moves under sealed-transit status and books no import charge unless it exits transit into the polity; a broken seal converts the transit to a contested import pending A18 review.
- F3-I13 (dangerous goods): Every consignment SHALL declare the M1 hazard class of its contents, and misdeclaration or concealment of a hazard class is an A18 concealment-class offense. Hazard classes carry binding routing, mode, segregation and packaging constraints (population-proximity, tunnel and infrastructure limits) verified before leg start, and the K8-I7 gated-class discipline applies: a realized Art. 4 life or bodily-integrity breach routes to the criminal, in-kind, non-priceable ladder (A18-I2, A18-I3), never to a payable residual.
- F3-I14 (cold chain and condition evidence): A condition-sensitive consignment SHALL carry a declared condition envelope (temperature, humidity, shock, seal integrity) and an evidence duty for it. Sensor readings are X1 occurrences appended to the sensing device owner's register, owner-gated per the S cluster; condition compliance SHOULD be proven as an S6 zero-knowledge predicate (continuously within envelope) rather than by moving raw telemetry (S6-I1). A gated-class good (M4 food, M5 medicine) whose condition evidence is broken or breached SHALL lose market eligibility per C2-I7 until re-conformance or recall.
- F3-I15 (recall reverse logistics): A K8-I9 recall SHALL execute as an X1 chain over the custody record: every custody holder in the affected chain is enumerable to the run_controller through a purpose-bound S3 projection limited to recall execution, and the recall's movement cost and residual attach to the run_controller regardless of who currently holds the good.
- F3-I16 (delivered-flow floor): For essential goods classes (food and medicine at minimum), every settlement SHALL carry a published per-cohort delivered-flow floor: a quantity per period that actually arrives, not a nominal access right. This floor covers settlement-level logistics reach for goods classes only; for fuel and other energy carriers the floor itself (definition, quantum, discharge and funding) is owned by F1-I9 and F1-I10, F3 provides only the physical movement of the carrier as goods, and no second COMP-I17 draw arises here for the household energy floor. Where the market serves the settlement, the floor is satisfied by observed delivered flow measured at S5 cohort grain; where it does not, the floor steward (the A12-mandated organ named per essential class and settlement cohort in the RES register) SHALL procure delivery as $-rail strategic demand through the single-buyer carve-out and substitute guards defined in F1 (independent procurement agent or mandatory published tenders on the C2-I9 discipline), funded from the COMP-I17 standing reserve as a first-charge obligation subject to the RES inter-floor priority order and exhaustion protocol. The floor SHALL NOT be delivered by setting or capping any freight or goods price.
- F3-I17 (floor breach routing): A settlement measurably below its delivered-flow floor beyond the window published in the flows-layer defaults annex is an Art. 6 cohort-floor breach routing via the cohort_floor severity band (A18-I44) against the floor steward as obligated deliverer, and the obligation is discharged only by delivery of the flow, never by payment alone, mirroring the K8-I11 delivered-floor discipline. Carriers carry only their contracted leg liability per F3-I6, never the floor obligation. During physical impossibility the steward carries a duty of means (activate pre-authored reroute regimes, draw reserves, tender alternate modes), and impossibility suspends the breach clock only while that duty is demonstrably discharged.
- F3-I18 (logistics data sovereignty): The shipper owns the consignment record, the carrier owns its fleet, route and telemetry data, and the facility controller owns its throughput data; cross reads occur only as S3 projections under S2 access contracts and every read is S4-logged. A carrier's operational projection of a consignment is minimized to what carriage requires (hazard class, mass and handling data, condition envelope, next custody party), and the shipper's projection of the carrier is minimized to leg status and condition evidence, never fleet internals. The state senses logistics at S5 cohort and corridor grain; a per-consignment or per-vehicle state read requires the owner's consent or a judicial S2 contract through an A6 case. A network-scale aggregate of movement data crossing the M3-I14 threshold is stewarded as a data commons, never held as carrier freehold.
- F3-I19 (de-minimis consignment class): A consignment below published value and mass thresholds (held by the flows parameter steward with conservative activation defaults) MAY move in a de-minimis class in which custody attests at manifest grain (carrier batch, not parcel), leg residual books at vehicle-period grain through the F3-I20 source act, and loss attribution defaults to the manifest holder. Gated-class goods (M4, M5) are excluded from the class regardless of size, and splitting a consignment to fit under the thresholds is the F3-I2 fragmentation offense, never an escape.
- F3-I20 (mobile source acts): Every vehicle operating period of a C1 carrier SHALL close as a per-vehicle or per-fleet-period source act covering all movement in the period, including deadheads, repositioning, empty running and shuttles, tiered per F6-I2 with a fuel-acquired input-proxy default (fuel purchased minus attested stock delta books as combusted, at the default-high tier), booked as V4 against the asset controller and priced by the uniform MVAL overlay. Leg residuals already booked under F3-I11 for the same period are cited lines within the same F6 charge thread, so the cumulative charge never exceeds the overlay price of the period's measured total. Personal and passenger movement is not an F3 flow object and has no separate movement section: its combustion residual is captured entirely by the fuel lifecycle pre-charge (K8-I14) and the F6 fuel-input join, and this coverage is stated here so no movement class dangles without a capture trigger.

### Protocols

- F3-P1 (consignment formation):
  1. The shipper composes the consignment referencing M3 goods, verifying each serialized reference and each fungible quantity's lawful-acquisition attestation (M3-I4, M3-I5); unattested quantities are refused. A consignment within the F3-I19 de-minimis class forms and attests at manifest grain.
  2. Declare the M1 hazard class (F3-I13), the condition envelope where condition-sensitive (F3-I14), and the goods' running-ledger positions as references (K8-I4, F3-I1).
  3. For gated-class contents verify a valid, unexpired conformance_release and no open recall (C2-I7), else fail closed.
  4. Contract carriage per leg, bilaterally or through a C2-I4 batch auction; record the leg plan and check it against the hazard routing constraints.
  5. Open the custody chain with the shipper or its agent as first attested holder.
- F3-P2 (leg execution and custody transfer):
  1. At leg start verify the carrier's identity (R4 key), the routing constraints, and the condition duty.
  2. Append departure, waypoint and condition occurrences to the timeline as the leg runs, as owner-gated evidence per F3-I14.
  3. At handover book the dual-attested custody act (F3-I4); absent the transferee's attestation, custody and liability remain with the transferor.
  4. At leg close measure the leg residual, reduce it by the fuel's pre-charged use-phase component via the F6 running-ledger join (F3-I11), book it against the carrier as a reversible provisional debit with suspensive contest (A18-I34, A18-I6), and append the charge lines to the goods' running ledger through the consignment reference (F3-I11).
  5. On final delivery close the chain with the consignee's attested receipt; the consignment retires and the reference closes (the goods' ledger position was never copied, F3-I1).
- F3-P3 (border crossing):
  1. Present the declared customs projection: contents, hazard class, embodied anti-value attestation, and origin charges claimed as credits.
  2. Compute the parity charge per K8-I6 on the goods plus transport residual, crediting only verified equivalent origin charges net of origin-side rebates, exemptions and matched subsidies, and crediting a prior domestic settlement on the residual_ledger in full by ledger reference (F3-I12); a parity charge paid on imported fuel registers as upstream credit against the domestic combustion charge through the F6 join (F3-I11).
  3. For transit, seal the consignment and book sealed-transit status; verify seal integrity at exit; a broken seal converts the transit to a contested import pending A18 review.
  4. Book the crossing as an X1 occurrence in the custody chain and release the consignment to the next leg.
- F3-P4 (incident, loss and recall):
  1. Book the loss, damage or condition-breach occurrence with its cause-ref and leg location (F3-I6).
  2. Where the interval is unattested, attribute liability to the interval's holders by default, with the contest route open.
  3. Settle care-duty liability against the responsible holder's escrow as a provisional debit (A18-I34); a realized Art. 4 breach routes to the criminal ladder per F3-I13.
  4. On recall, resolve the custody chain to the affected holders through the purpose-bound projection and execute the reverse chain (F3-I15), with cost and residual on the run_controller.
- F3-P5 (floor assurance):
  1. The floor steward monitors delivered flow per settlement and essential goods class at S5 cohort grain against the published floor (F3-I16).
  2. On a projected shortfall, procure the gap as $-rail strategic demand through the F1 single-buyer carve-out and its substitute guards (mandatory published tenders on the C2-I9 discipline), drawing the COMP-I17 standing reserve subject to the RES inter-floor priority order.
  3. On a measured breach beyond the defaults-annex window, route per A18-I44 against the floor steward as obligated deliverer and continue procurement until delivered flow is restored; discharge only on delivery, with the duty of means governing impossibility (F3-I17).
  4. Refer recurring shortfalls to NET and RES as a corridor-capacity and reserve-positioning signal, never as a price intervention.

## Emission and environmental flux (F6 core)

Every release of matter or energy into a commons medium (air, water, soil, and governed radiative or acoustic load) is a first-class flow object: a quantified, owner-attributed, time-stamped flux from a named source into a named medium. Sources are acts the rest of the model already records: a K8 production run, an F1 generation or combustion act, an F3 movement or vehicle operating period, a K9 or K10 land-use or extraction operation. F6 gives those acts their environmental shadow as data: what left the pipe, when, measured how, with what declared uncertainty, owned by whom. Flux is strictly distinct from ambient state: flux is a rate attributable to an owner; concentration in a medium is commons state stewarded under the P cluster, and the two never share one record.

F6 is the measurement and flux layer, never the charging layer. The running externality ledger of the economy spec (K8-I4, MVAL-I2) is where a residual becomes money; F6 is where the residual becomes a fact. The seam between them is conservation-keyed: every flux fact anchors exactly one charge thread, every ledger line in the thread cites the fact it draws on, and the money summed over the thread never exceeds the overlay price of the measured quantity, so one tonne of harm is charged at most once across the whole supply chain while every lawful downstream catch-up stays inside that cap. The same conservation runs vertically through the fuel chain: a use-phase component pre-charged at fuel production (K8-I14) is never charged again at the burner, because every combustion charge joins the consumed fuel's own ledger and nets the pre-charge (F6-I19). An unbooked tonne is an escape defect, never a free release: where the owner kept no verifiable inventory line it books at the default-high tier, and where an honest line went uncharged the organ, not the owner, wears the defect. Because every charge reads from a measurement, the integrity of measurement is the integrity of the entire pricing chain: tampering with an instrument is not an environmental peccadillo, it is concealment aimed at the ledger, and it carries the same consequences as falsifying the ledger itself.

The doctrine prices the residual and protects created value, and F6 applies that symmetrically to the environment. Emission anti-value is measured here and charged once through the uniform overlay. Verified removal is created value: credited on the ecology axis and paid the matching Pigovian subsidy on the $ rail (FVAL-I8). Measured abatement is also created value, but its $ reward is the avoided overlay charge itself: it earns the ecology-axis Ae credit under the FVAL-I7 cap and never a second $ payment, so no flows organ ever sets a second price on flux. Neither direction launders the other. A removal never becomes a transferable indulgence: it books where it physically happened, to the owner who performed it, and no credit of any kind buys back a floor-axis violation (C1-I4). Ambient quality carries delivered capability floors: the clean-air floor is what each cohort actually breathes, sensed per cohort with the same sub-k discipline the civic layer uses for health floors, never a territorial average.

### Invariants

- F6-I1 (first-class flux object). Every release into a commons medium SHALL exist as an F6 flux object carrying source reference, owner (resolved through S1), substance, medium, quantity, period, measurement tier and declared uncertainty, bound to exactly one source act and appended as an X1 event to the owner's R3 register. The closed source-act set is: a K8 run, an F1 generation or combustion act, an F3 consignment movement or vehicle-period source act (the F3 mobile-source rule, charter C11), and a K9/K10 land-use or extraction operation. Deadheads, repositioning and fleet shuttles close through the F3 vehicle-period act; personal and passenger movement closes no F6 source act, its combustion flux being charged upstream through the fuel lifecycle pre-charge (K8-I14) and netted at any downstream business burn by the F6-I19 join. For owners below the F6-I22 threshold this duty is discharged upstream per F6-I22. A release with no identifiable source act SHALL book to the controller of the site or asset it issued from; an unowned release does not exist as a lawful state.
- F6-I2 (measurement tier ladder and the one commanded instrument). Every flux record SHALL declare exactly one tier from the closed set {measured, modeled, default_factor}, with quantified uncertainty. Above a published source-size threshold per source class (an F6-I23 parameter), measured tier is a duty; this is the single place the state commands instrumentation. Default factors SHALL be set conservatively high per substance and source class, meaning above the observed class maximum, never a class mean plus margin, and modeled tiers SHALL use published, versioned models (F6-I23). The burden of establishing a quantity lower than the applicable default rests on the owner and is discharged only through a higher tier, the F6-I17 input-proxy reconciliation runs as the mandatory detector over sources sitting long on default tiers, and the F6-I20 ratchet closes the reversion direction; an unmeasured release never outperforms a measured one.
- F6-I3 (conservative uncertainty into the charge). The chargeable quantity handed across the seam SHALL be the central estimate for measured-tier flux and the upper confidence bound for modeled and default tiers, so improving measurement is always privately rational; beyond the F6-I2 source-size duty the state commands no instrumentation. A tier improvement applies prospectively; it SHALL NOT retroactively re-charge honestly booked prior periods (the finality pattern of FVAL-I12).
- F6-I4 (measurement facts with instrument provenance). A measured-tier fact SHALL be an X1 event carrying instrument identity (M8 device reference), a current calibration attestation (R5), the operating party, and chain of custody. A measurement lacking logged provenance or valid calibration is void as a measured-tier fact, and the affected period substitutes the F6-I20 ratchet rate, so voiding one's own adverse measurement never pays; the log-and-attribute discipline mirrors A18-I16.
- F6-I5 (per-owner append-only inventory on the R2 pattern). Every owner with attributable flux above the F6-I22 threshold SHALL hold an emission inventory as an owner-held register carrying the R2 pattern: append-only, witnessed checkpoints, corrections as new entries referencing the corrected entry, never edits or deletions (R2-I1 and R2-I13 cited as pattern, not as membership; the polity's two value ledgers remain the only R2 ledgers). Reads are gated by the S cluster per F6-I18. Under the flows-layer mastering table (charter C50) the inventory masters flux and cites meter facts, and the K8 running ledger masters the charge and cites the inventory line. The inventory is the single reading surface for the charging layer; no organ SHALL charge from any other source of flux data.
- F6-I6 (the seam: one fact, one charge thread, quantity conservation, no escape). Each inventory line SHALL anchor exactly one charge thread on the running externality ledger (K8-I4, MVAL-I2) for its (source, substance, period). A thread MAY hold multiple charge lines, including lawful downstream K8-I4 catch-up lines; every line in the thread SHALL cite the F6 record identifier; and the cumulative $ charged over the thread SHALL NOT exceed the overlay price of the line's chargeable quantity net of the F6-I19 fuel-input join. Only the excess above that cap is void, never a catch-up within it. F6 books no $ charge and the charging layer re-measures nothing. Negative-flux removal lines are seam-exempt: they anchor no charge thread, book zero at the seam, and are compensated only through the FVAL-I8 and MVAL-I12 route (F6-I11). At the close of a reconciliation window (flows-layer defaults annex, charter C54), a valid inventory line with no citing thread is charged at exactly its declared tier and quantity with the defect logged solely against the charging organ; only where no verifiable inventory line exists does the escape book to the source controller at the default-high tier. No residual is charged beyond its measured total; none escapes.
- F6-I7 (flux is not concentration). Ambient concentration in air, water and soil is commons state under P-cluster stewardship and SHALL NEVER appear as an entry in any owner's inventory. Attribution from ambient state back to sources SHALL pass through dispersion or transport models with declared uncertainty; such statistical apportionment MAY ground cohort restitution and organization-level standing consequences and SHALL NEVER alone supply the basis for a hard rung against an individual natural person (A18-I9).
- F6-I8 (delivered ambient floors, routed by kind). Per-cohort ambient quality floors (the clean-air floor and its water and soil analogues) are Charter Art. 6 capability floors measured as the delivered exposure of each cohort, never as a territorial average. Floor sensing SHALL NOT mean-pool a below-floor cohort into a balanced ancestor; a sub-k breach SHALL surface through a differential-privacy-noised below-floor count or a sealed monitor-only flag per the B10-I7 pattern. An ambient floor breach is presumptively non-priceable (C1-I11, MVAL-I13): the presumption bars pay-to-continue and never selects the criminal class. Routing follows the K8-I7 split: realized Art. 4 harm, attributable within the A18-I9 limits, routes to the in-kind non-priceable ladder (A18-I2, A18-I3); an ambient Art. 6 floor breach routes to priced-class cohort restitution plus the licensed hard ladder via the cohort_floor severity band (A18-I44), never to a payable line that licenses continuation.
- F6-I9 (no cross-axis buyback). No removal credit, abatement credit or spillover credit of any kind SHALL offset anti-value on a capability-floor or hard-limit axis, including depletion beyond a regeneration bound (C1-I4, MVAL-I10). Credits operate only inside the priceable residual on non-floor axes; a floor-axis breach routes to A18 regardless of any credit position.
- F6-I10 (removal is real, additional, durable, scaled). A removal credit SHALL book only for removal physically performed and verified post hoc at measured or modeled tier, never for projected removal; only where additional against an independently attested baseline under the C1-I7 and FVAL-I7 attestation discipline, never the claimant's self-declared baseline; and only against a durability horizon set as a mandate floor per substance and method in the flows-layer defaults annex (charter C54), never remover-declared. Credit and the matching subsidy scale to durability by tonne-year accounting or a published discount curve (an F6-I23 parameter), with full crediting only at the permanent class. The site stays monitored for the full credited horizon. Reversal liability is pre-funded by escrow in the latent-harm class per K8-I12 and FVAL-I9, surviving dissolution and sale of the site or the remover: an in-horizon re-release reverses the credit and books the re-release as new flux to the same owner, and a post-horizon release above the credited durability books as new flux to the original remover's beneficial owners per A18-I45.
- F6-I11 (no offset laundering). A removal books as negative flux ONLY in the inventory of the owner who physically performed it. No inventory SHALL absorb another owner's removal, and no instrument SHALL transfer a removal between inventories, at any price, on any rail. Purchasing removal as a $ service is lawful, but the buyer's own flux remains charged in full; the remover is rewarded through the ecology-axis Ae credit and the matching $ Pigovian subsidy (FVAL-I8, MVAL-I12). The offset market is replaced by the overlay: emitters pay, removers are paid, and no private instrument nets the two.
- F6-I12 (abatement is created value, rewarded once). A measured flux reduction against an independently attested counterfactual baseline is created value: credited on the ecology axis (D4) under the FVAL-I7 self-originated-credit cap and protected from confiscation with the same force that C1-I7 protects efficiency. Its $ reward is the avoided overlay charge itself; abatement draws no FVAL-I8 Pigovian subsidy, which is reserved for genuine positive spillovers and verified removals (F6-I10, F6-I11), so the effective marginal price of flux is the overlay price and nothing else. Baselines decay on a published schedule and SHALL re-attest (F6-I23 parameters); credit is denied where baseline-period flux exceeded the source's own trailing norm or the class benchmark. Baseline inflation, meaning the padding of historic flux to manufacture apparent reduction, is a concealment-class A18 offense with clawback of the credit.
- F6-I13 (import parity reads F6). The border parity charge of K8-I6, C2-I10 and MVAL-I3 SHALL read embodied flux from an F6-conformant attestation carried on the imported good; an import without one defaults high with the burden on the importing controller. Fungible carriers (grid electricity and its analogues) read their origin attestation under F1's fungible-carrier parity rule (charter C7): the exporting system's measured average or published marginal mix per interval, verified against the interconnector physical schedule, with contractual allocation below the physical mix barred and bare certificates taking the default-high rule. A credit for priced abroad attaches only against a verified, itemized equivalent origin charge net of origin-side rebates, exemptions and matched subsidies (K8-I6, charter C12), never a bare foreign attestation.
- F6-I14 (export of harm charged at source). Flux generated inside the polity is charged by the same uniform overlay regardless of where the harm lands: a plume, discharge or waste stream crossing the border outward SHALL be charged to its source owner at full measured or defaulted value, with no discount for the receptor being foreign. Transboundary inflow from abroad SHALL be measured, booked to the commons state with source attribution recorded, and never silently absorbed into any domestic owner's inventory; the attribution record is the polity's evidentiary basis under A15 and A16 and MAY ground a parity charge where an MOS-reachable responsible controller exists.
- F6-I15 (tamper is concealment with teeth). Instrument tampering, data falsification, monitor bypass, releases timed into monitoring gaps, and sampling manipulation are concealment-class A18 offenses: limitation tolls and the matter flips to the perpetual class (A18-I49), and clawback pierces to the natural-person beneficial owner (A18-I45). On a tamper finding the affected periods substitute the F6-I20 ratchet rate with the burden on the owner to re-establish any lower figure, and the escrow multiplier resets per K8-I12. Where a declaration attributes instrument compromise to an external attack, the RES victim-class carve-out (charter C35) governs instead: affected points demote to a neutral estimated tier, the tamper presumption is suspended for the attested victim class, and R5 fleet re-attestation governs recovery, with individual concealment inside the window remaining chargeable on specific evidence.
- F6-I16 (downtime substitutes the ratchet; maintenance windows are capped). Monitor downtime SHALL substitute the F6-I20 ratchet rate for the gap period, never zero and never favorable interpolation. Declared maintenance windows are capped in frequency and cumulative duration per instrument class on a published schedule (an F6-I23 parameter), and the activity-correlation tamper presumption applies to declared downtime exactly as to unexplained downtime: a gap, declared or not, that coincides with anomalous input, production or movement activity per the F6-I17 proxies raises the rebuttable presumption and routes through F6-P5. High-harm source classes carry a redundant-instrument duty so no single declared window opens an unmeasured period, and repeated coincidence of declared windows with activity at one source is itself a sealed flag.
- F6-I17 (statistical cross-checks under seal). Continuous cross-checks SHALL run over every declaring owner and region: source mass balance (inputs versus outputs plus declared flux, the physical side of K8-I4), input-proxy reconciliation (fuel and feedstock acquired versus flux declared), and ambient reconciliation (the sum of declared regional flux versus observed concentration change). The input-proxy reconciliation is the mandatory detector for sources sitting long on default tiers, and its adverse findings feed the F6-I20 ratchet as evidenced rates. The cross-checks run as a sealed stewarded duty emitting flagged referrals only, per the C2-I6 pattern; resolving a flag to a named owner beyond that owner's own inventory SHALL require an S2 judicial access contract through an A6 case.
- F6-I18 (owner-gated reads, ZK first; public duty split by grain). Inventories are owner-gated under the S cluster: default deny, reads only as contracted projections (S2, S3), every read logged (S4), state sensing at cohort grain (S5). Compliance predicates (flux under quota, floor distance, parity eligibility) SHOULD be served as zero-knowledge attestations (S6) so the fact verifies and the data never moves. Aggregate flux by region, substance and period is a mandated public duty split by cell grain (charter C47): k-satisfying cells publish as registered S5 releases; a single-source or sub-k cell is never force-published as an aggregate, and instead the owner carries a statutory facility-grain attestation duty on the NVAL-I13 and M3-I14 pattern, published as the owner's own attested figure. The k floor is never bent, and commons accountability never depends on per-owner disclosure beyond that statutory duty.
- F6-I19 (fuel-input join: one tonne, one charge, across the fuel chain). At any combustion or use act, the measured flux charge SHALL be reduced by the pre-charged use-phase component carried on the consumed fuel for the same substance and quantity, via an explicit running-ledger join keyed to the consumed fuel's residual_ledger; the difference between measured flux and the pre-charge reconciles to the fuel producer's K8-I14 escrow, an over-measurement drawing the escrow down and an under-measurement flowing back. Border parity paid on imported fuel is upstream credit at the domestic burner. F1-I2 and F3-I11 state the mirror rule that fuel-input embodied anti-value counts as already charged upstream (charter C1); this join is the single mechanism that nets the two, so no tonne is charged both at the fuel run and at the burner, and no fuel producer can under-fund the use-phase escrow expecting burner-side measurement to hide it.
- F6-I20 (adverse substitution ratchet). Wherever this section substitutes a rate for a void, downtime, tamper or otherwise unverifiable period, the substituted rate SHALL be the maximum of: the applicable default factor, the highest previously evidenced rate for that source, and the best adverse evidence from the F6-I17 cross-checks. The substituted rate MAY be lowered only by re-establishing a valid higher tier, prospectively per F6-I3. A source dirtier than its class default therefore never profits from losing, voiding or sabotaging its own measurement. The only exception is the attack-attributed victim class under the RES carve-out (charter C35), which substitutes a neutral estimated tier.
- F6-I21 (aggregate airshed escalation). While a cohort ambient floor breach persists beyond the published window (flows-layer defaults annex, charter C54): (a) the overlay strength_dial for the implicated (substance, region) escalates on a published uniform schedule under the MVAL-I6 parameter discipline, with no per-firm verdict required; (b) the K8-I8 same-class escalation threshold becomes computable at airshed grain over the dispersion-attributed source set, opening mandatory abatement review of the set's members with organization-level consequences only, the F6-I7 bar on natural-person hard rungs standing (A18-I9); (c) the escalation discharges only on restored ambient exposure measured per F6-I8, never on payment. A source population cannot collectively buy a floor: the price of the airshed rises for everyone in it until the cohort actually breathes at floor.
- F6-I22 (de-minimis upstream tier). Below a published per-owner annual threshold per substance (an F6-I23 parameter with a conservative sector-table activation default), flux is charged upstream at the point of sale of the fuel or input via the default factor riding the K8-I4 embodied carry: the owner keeps no inventory, and the residual books at cohort aggregate. Above the threshold the full F6-I1 and F6-I5 regime applies unchanged. The upstream charge and the F6-I19 join are one mechanism seen from two ends, so a small source is never uncharged and an above-threshold source is never double-charged.
- F6-I23 (the flows parameter steward). All measurement-layer parameters of the flows layer, including default factors, model versions, error bands, tamper and downtime substitution estimates, source-size thresholds, de-minimis thresholds, maintenance-window caps and reconciliation tolerances, SHALL be owned by a single A12-mandated flows parameter steward, structurally separated from every charging and revenue-receiving organ, bound by the full MVAL-I6 discipline (multi-source, staked, revaluable, published change provenance, capture flagging), with conservative activation defaults so no parameter is ever null. This invariant is hosted here and binds every section of the flows layer (charter C53).

- F6-P1 (flux capture and booking).
  1. On a source act closing (K8 run close, F1 dispatch interval, F3 leg or vehicle-period close per the F3 mobile-source rule of charter C11, K9/K10 operation period), enumerate the release points and substances defined for the source class. For an owner below the F6-I22 threshold, stop: the flux is already charged upstream at the point of sale and no inventory line is kept.

## Network infrastructure commons (NET)

The flows of this layer ride on shared physical networks: the electricity grid under F1, pipelines, rail, roads and ports under F3, water mains, and the collection networks whose flux F6 measures. These are U3-class physical infrastructure networks in the property doctrine's sense: one operator, many dependent users, duplication uneconomic at the served scale. That combination breaks the ordinary clearing story. The market doctrine says the state never sets a transaction price, but a natural monopoly has no competitive price for a market to discover: there is no rival book to clear against, so "the market clears and the state prices only the residual" appears to leave the tariff either to the monopolist or to the state, and both answers are forbidden. NET resolves this with the economy layer's own machinery, without ever ordering a price. The operator sets its own tariff. What the state does is what it does to every firm: it constructs the counterfactual competitive benchmark from independently attested cost (C1-I7, FVAL-I5, C7), adds the operator's benchmark-cleared declared capital return as protected created value, publishes the resulting tariff corridor, and charges everything the operator collects above the corridor as pricing-power rent under the standard C1 rent machinery, provisional, suspensive on contest, routed on finality first to the metered users who overpaid and only in the unattributable remainder to the commons. Inside the corridor nothing is charged and nothing is criminalized. The monopoly price is not capped; the monopoly rent is priced. The residual is all the state ever touches.

Scarcity on a network is real and its price signal is legitimate. When demand exceeds capacity the scarce capacity is allocated by a published, non-discriminatory mechanism, a sealed uniform-price batch auction cleared on an independent C2 venue per C2-I4 wherever feasible, with the operator itself barred from the book, and the congestion price is a scarcity signal the whole layer may rely on. What congestion revenue is never allowed to become is concealed rent: it is segregated from the tariff, routed to capacity expansion or the commons, funds expansion capital only at zero return, and sustained congestion converts into an enforceable expansion duty, so an operator cannot farm its own bottleneck. Structurally, the operator of an essential network is an essential-class systemic firm: it carries the COMP-I16 living-will and resolution discipline, and where its centrality is systemic and it also competes on the very network it operates, the dual-role rule applies (NET-I11): shown foreclosure shifts the burden to the operator, and absent an efficiency or genuine-integration justification with no less-foreclosing alternative, only divestiture or true structural separation cures the combination; no bond or charge buys it back.

Both flanks are catastrophic here, as everywhere in the economy layer. A regime that confiscates the network investor's return, reopens a declared return or an admitted prudence ruling retroactively, strikes mandated redundancy as gold-plating, or lets the corridor machinery stall starves the polity of capacity and makes every future network un-financeable: so investment recovery is protected inside the benchmark, mandated duties are per se prudent at commitment, prudence rulings are final for honest capital and stranded honest capital recovers on a published schedule, corridor defaults are operable from activation, and nothing in NET grants the incumbent a legal monopoly or bars a rival from building a bypass at its own risk. A regime that lets the bottleneck holder discriminate, foreclose, launder rent into fees and congestion, or feed its affiliate on network data holds every dependent user hostage: so access is mandatory and uniform, every term is public in R1, ancillary fees are pulled into the corridor test, analytic privilege is zero, and access disputes run with suspensive contest and a preserved status quo.

### Invariants

- NET-I1 (essential-network classification): A network SHALL be classified essential-facility class when it is a U3 physical infrastructure network (grid, pipeline, rail line, road corridor, port, water main, or a peer) whose duplication is uneconomic at the served scale and on which dependent users have no substitutable route. Classification is a public R1 register event with an A6 contest route and a published review cadence; it attaches to the factual bottleneck position, never to a granted franchise. Each classified network SHALL resolve to exactly one operator of record, a C1 firm whose beneficial-owner graph is positively verified per C1-I5 and R1-I39.
- NET-I2 (commons substrate, earned assets): The land corridor, watercourse, subsurface, or spectrum-like commons a network occupies SHALL be held under a rent-bearing, time-bounded, revocable stewardship grant per the P2-I7 discipline (finite term, revocation trigger, rent basis, funded restoration or decommissioning bond on the $ rail), never freehold. The constructed network assets above the commons are the operator's earned improvement per the U1-I2 split: the structure is private and protected, the location and commons value under it stays commons-owed.
- NET-I3 (mandatory non-discriminatory access): The operator SHALL serve every qualified user of a classified network on published, uniform, registered terms. Refusal, delay, degradation, or differential terms toward a dependent user is presumptively exclusionary with the COMP-I12 burden shift on the operator; access MAY be denied only on a published capacity, safety, or conformance ground that is attestable and contestable at A6. Self-preferencing of the operator's own affiliated traffic is a form of discrimination under this invariant.
- NET-I4 (tariff corridor, no state price): The state SHALL NOT set, cap, or order any network tariff. For each classified network a tariff corridor SHALL be computed and published: the corridor is the independently attested efficient cost of operating and renewing the network (C1-I7, FVAL-I5, C7 benchmark, never the operator's self-reported cost) plus the operator's benchmark-cleared declared capital return per NET-I5. The operator sets its tariff freely; all revenue above the corridor ceiling is pricing-power rent and SHALL be charged per the C1 rent machinery (C1-I7, C1-I10), booked as a provisional debit with suspensive contest per A18-I34 and A18-I6 subject to the NET-I14 repeat-contest rule. Because the harmed class on a classified network is metered and enumerable, confirmed rent routes on finality first to per-user tariff true-up or restitution of the affected user cohort; only the unattributable remainder routes via C6 to the commons rent fund. Revenue inside the corridor carries neither a charge nor an offense.
- NET-I5 (declared capital return protected): Investment recovery is created value. Depreciation of prudently committed attested capital, a risk-adjusted return declared prospectively at the moment of investment commitment and registered in R1, and innovation-driven returns inside the time-limited protection horizon of C1-I7 SHALL sit inside the corridor and SHALL NOT be charged as rent. The declared return SHALL clear an independently attested risk-adjusted benchmark (C7) at commitment, inside the published per-class band governed by the NET-I18 parameter discipline; a declaration above the band does not enter the corridor, and the protection of this invariant attaches only to a benchmark-cleared declaration. A benchmark-cleared declared return SHALL NOT be re-declared retroactively in either direction: the polity cannot confiscate it after commitment, and the operator cannot widen it after commitment.
- NET-I6 (cost-base discipline): The attested cost base SHALL admit only prudent, used-and-useful network capital and efficient operating cost, verified under the FVAL-I5 discipline and subject to the NET-I20 per-se-prudence rule and the NET-I21 finality rule: affiliate and acting-in-concert inputs re-priced at arm's length, above-benchmark input prices and related-party compensation stripped, rent capitalized as asset carry stripped. Shared and common costs between the regulated network and any competitive affiliate SHALL be allocated under a published common-cost allocation discipline with the burden on the operator per allocation line; persistent affiliate pricing below the affiliate's standalone attested cost raises a rebuttable cross-subsidy presumption. Unexplained cost inflation of the base is presumptive rent concealment (A18) with the burden on the operator per cost line; gold-plating the base to widen the corridor is the same offense.
- NET-I7 (substance over form in fees): Connection charges, standby and reservation fees, balancing and quality-tier charges, and any ancillary fee economically tied to use of the network SHALL be pulled into the tariff for corridor assessment, mirroring F8-I7, so rent cannot be laundered out of the corridor into fee income.
- NET-I8 (congestion pricing as scarcity, never rent): When demand for network capacity exceeds supply, capacity SHALL be allocated by a published non-discriminatory mechanism. NET defines the capacity product only (interval, direction, firmness); clearing runs as a sealed, uniform-price, frequent batch auction on an independent C2 venue operated by the venue steward per C2-I4, under C2-I6 sealed surveillance unchanged, never a mechanism the operator runs itself. The operator and its concert-aggregated affiliates (C1-I5) are barred from bidding for capacity on the operator's own network, and capacity declarations SHALL be attested against telemetry, never self-published alone. This invariant is the single owner of network-capacity scarcity pricing and congestion-revenue routing across the layer: F1 and F3 bind by citation, exactly one mechanism prices any given constraint per interval, a constraint already priced implicitly by zonal energy clearing SHALL NOT also be auctioned as capacity, and on unclassified corridors a published congestion-harm factor MAY apply only where declared mutually exclusive with NET pricing per corridor per interval. Congestion revenue is not operator revenue: it SHALL be segregated from the tariff account, excluded from the corridor computation, and routed via C6 to capacity expansion on the same network or to the commons; capital funded from congestion revenue or any user contribution enters the attested cost base at zero return. Presenting congestion revenue as tariff revenue, or engineering scarcity to raise it, is concealment (A18).
- NET-I9 (withholding offense, expansion duty): Withholding available capacity, slow-walking maintenance that reduces capacity, or scheduling outages to raise the congestion price corrupts the scarcity signal and is an offense of the C2-I5 manipulation class. Congestion revenue above a published threshold sustained over a published period is a rebuttable expansion trigger: the operator SHALL propose a costed expansion or open the expansion to third parties per NET-P4. An unrebutted trigger left unserved draws an escalating charge on the operator's own tariff revenue on a published schedule, routed per NET-I4 on finality, plus escalation on the A18 ladder while the trigger stays unserved.
- NET-I10 (interconnection duty): The operator SHALL interconnect with adjacent, upstream, and competing networks on cost-anchored, corridor-disciplined, registered terms; refusal or degraded interconnection is exclusionary under NET-I3. At a border interconnect the K8-I6 parity doctrine applies: flows entering the polity carry their embodied residual at import, and a foreign operator's access to the domestic network rides the same published terms as any domestic user, never a bilateral concession.
- NET-I11 (dual-role separation where centrality is systemic): Dual-role separation is a self-standing NET invariant with its own published systemic-centrality trigger, a NET-I18 parameter. An operator above the trigger SHALL NOT also compete in an upstream or downstream market that rides its own network where the dual role forecloses rivals. The rule is grounded in the COMP-I12 burden shift: once foreclosure is shown, the operator carries the burden of proving an efficiency or genuine-integration justification and the unavailability of a less-foreclosing alternative; absent that showing, the remedy is divestiture or a structurally separate unit with independent control, never a payable charge or a bond. COMP-I16 is cited in this section only for cascade-driven caps (NET-I12), never for this conduct trigger. Below the trigger, vertical integration is lawful subject to accounting and functional separation, the NET-I22 informational separation, non-discrimination attestation, and the NET-I3 burden shift; a below-cost affiliate price on the competitive segment cross-subsidized from the regulated network is a predation fact pattern under COMP-I5, whose recoupment-feasibility test is satisfied per se by recoupment through the regulated cost base.
- NET-I12 (systemic operator resolution): A classified-network operator is an essential-class systemic firm. It SHALL maintain a pre-committed, ring-fenced, transferable essential-function unit able to continue network operation through insolvency or resolution without the operator's cooperation (COMP-I16), carry an escrow bond per K8-I12 and A18-I45 sized to worst-case service-interruption and decommissioning harm, and suspend distributions and buybacks while resolution is under-funded. Under-sizing the bond is concealment.
- NET-I13 (capability-floor continuity): Floor ownership lives with the flows, not with NET: the household energy floor's definition, quantum, metered discharge, and funding are owned by F1 (F1-I9, funded per F1-I10), with the family ownership map in NVAL-I10, so exactly one COMP-I17 first-charge draw exists per household floor. NET keeps exactly one duty: the operator SHALL NOT disconnect, or degrade below the floor quantum, a below-floor cohort's service for arrears within the floor quantum, and the floor is financed per the cited F1 funding rule, never by distorting the tariff or corridor, so no hidden cross-subsidy corrupts the price signal.
- NET-I14 (access disputes, suspensive and status quo): Access, tariff, and interconnection disputes route to A6 adjudication on the A18 ladder. Every same-tick rent or offense booking against the operator is a reversible provisional debit with suspensive effect on contest per A18-I34 and A18-I6, except that after a first sustained corridor finding against the operator, materially identical repeat bookings lose suspensive effect and escalate on the A18 ladder, so serial contest cannot become a float business. Symmetrically, during a live dispute the operator SHALL preserve the status quo of existing service to the disputing user, and retaliatory disconnection or degradation during contest is an aggravated offense.
- NET-I15 (public registers): The classification, the corridor parameters and their derivation references, the attested cost base summary, the declared capital return, the published tariff, capacity and congestion events, access requests and their outcomes, and every interconnection agreement SHALL be registered in a public R1 tariff-and-access register. Terms, tariffs, capacity, and outcome statistics are public at term grain; the requesting user's identity is delayed-unmasked on the R2-I31 pattern, publishing only after the published delay window unless the requester opts into immediate publication or the request enters an A6 dispute, and this identity delay is a statutory register exception, stated as such. Per-user consumption stays owner-gated and is sensed only at S5 cohort grain, with per-user resolution only via an S2 judicial access contract. A tariff, fee, or access term not in the register is unenforceable against a user.
- NET-I16 (network externalities distinct from congestion): Network losses, emissions, leakage, and corridor ecological harm are the operator's own production residual, measured and priced through the K8 and MVAL overlay machinery on the running no-double-charge ledger (K8-I4): the operator is charged once for its own residual, and the embodied anti-value of the flows it carries stays booked to their run controllers, never re-charged in transit. Congestion is a scarcity price and SHALL NOT be booked, argued, or offset as an externality charge; the two quantities never mix.
- NET-I17 (operable defaults): The corridor machinery SHALL be operable from activation and never null: where the attested cost model for a network class is not yet calibrated, a published conservative sector-default cost benchmark and a published sector-default capital return apply, uniform with a review clause, mirroring the C1-I8 pattern, so no classified network operates without a corridor while calibration is pending. Activation defaults are conservatively low against the operator, paired with a suspensive true-up in the operator's favor when calibration lands, so a pending calibration can never widen the corridor and honest cost is never finally confiscated. Honestly attested legacy capital present at first classification, having no prospectively declared return to read, carries the published sector-default return from classification until recovery or a benchmark-cleared re-declaration at new commitment. Every published window, threshold, and cadence this section leaves parametric takes its activation default from the flows-layer defaults annex held in the RES register, each value revisable only with published change provenance.
- NET-I18 (parameter anti-capture): Corridor parameters (the benchmark cost model, the per-class capital-return band of NET-I5, the default capital return and default cost benchmark of NET-I17, congestion thresholds, the NET-I11 systemic-centrality trigger, classification criteria) are bound by the MVAL-I6 discipline: multi-source, staked, revaluable, no single organ, published change provenance, and the parameter-setting organ structurally separated from every organ receiving the resulting charges. An operator's share of influence over a parameter that materially widens its own corridor or delays its own expansion trigger is flagged and rebuttably presumed capture.
- NET-I19 (entry, bypass, and stand-down): Nothing in NET grants an incumbent a legal monopoly. Any party MAY build a competing or bypass network at its own risk subject to the same commons-grant, safety, and externality discipline; the incumbent SHALL NOT use corridor status, interconnection refusal, or commons-grant influence to block entry, and doing so is exclusionary. Where duplication or bypass makes the market contestable in fact, a classification review MAY stand the network down from essential-facility class to ordinary C1 and COMP treatment only on three showings: positively verified independence of the bypass network's beneficial owners from the incumbent and its concert parties (C1-I5, R1-I39), a sustained published observation window of actual user switching to the alternative, and an automatic re-classification tripwire registered at stand-down that restores the corridor parameters without a new proceeding if contestability lapses. So the corridor machinery never outlives the monopoly that justified it, and never stands down to a shell.
- NET-I20 (mandated duties per se prudent): Capital and operating cost committed under a NET-P4 expansion or interconnection order, a RES structural mandate, the living-will, ring-fencing, and drill duties of NET-I12 and the RES discipline, or COMP-I16 compliance is per se prudent and corridor-recoverable at commitment, contestable only for fraud or concealment; a used-and-useful audit SHALL NOT strike mandated redundancy as gold-plating. Cost passed through from the physically single-buyer system functions (balancing, system reserves, floor procurement) under the F1 single-buyer procurement rule with its C2-I9 and COMP-I13 substitute guards receives the same corridor treatment.
- NET-I21 (prudence finality and stranded capital): A commitment-time prudence ruling on honestly disclosed capital is contestable within its published window and then final, on the FVAL-I12 finality pattern: capital admitted as prudent in one corridor cycle SHALL NOT be stripped in a later cycle. Used-and-useful review applies prospectively, to new additions only. Where demand shifts strand honestly committed capital, the undepreciated base recovers over a published stranded-asset schedule inside the corridor; only fraud or concealment at commitment reopens an admitted base.
- NET-I22 (informational separation, zero analytic privilege): Zero analytic privilege on the R2-I27 pattern binds every classified-network operator: operating the network confers no analytic advantage. A competitive affiliate of the operator SHALL receive only the published projections available to all users on equal terms, never the operator's aggregate or derived network data (rivals' throughput, congestion patterns, locational demand growth). Network-scale aggregates past the M3-I14 threshold are stewarded as a data commons. A breach of this separation is exclusionary conduct under NET-I3 with the COMP-I12 burden shift on the operator.
- NET-I23 (congestion rent defined, trading band protected): Congestion rent is the auction clearing revenue on constrained capacity, and it accrues to the commons per NET-I8, never to the operator and never to a trader holding withheld capacity. Spreads earned above cleared capacity cost by a non-withholding trader who bought capacity at the cleared price and bore delivery risk sit in the protected free-competition band, the locational analogue of storage arbitrage, and SHALL NOT be confiscated. Withholding routes per C2-I5 and COMP-I11. F1 binds to this definition by citation for the energy-specific facts.

### Protocols

- NET-P1 (classification and registration): (1) any dependent user, steward, or the operator itself proposes classification; (2) test the NET-I1 facts: duplication uneconomic at served scale, no substitutable route for dependent users; (3) resolve the single operator of record and positively verify its beneficial-owner graph (C1-I5, R1-I39); (4) register the network, its asset inventory, and its commons grants (NET-I2) in the R1 register; (5) publish the classification with its A6 contest route; (6) review on the published cadence and run the NET-I19 stand-down test, including its independence screen, switching window, and re-classification tripwire, at each review.
- NET-P2 (corridor cycle): (1) run the independent C7 audit of the cost base under NET-I6, admitting only prudent used-and-useful capital and efficient operating cost, honoring NET-I20 per-se-prudent entries and the NET-I21 finality of previously admitted capital; (2) read the benchmark-cleared declared capital returns from the investment register (NET-I5), applying the NET-I17 sector-default return to honestly attested legacy capital; (3) compute and publish the corridor floor and ceiling with derivation references (NET-I15), applying NET-I17 conservative-low defaults with suspensive true-up where calibration is pending; (4) the operator files its tariff, with all NET-I7 ancillary fees consolidated; (5) each period, book revenue above the ceiling as pricing-power rent: provisional debit, suspensive contest per A18-I34 and A18-I6 subject to the NET-I14 repeat-contest rule, routing on finality per NET-I4, user restitution or tariff true-up first, unattributable remainder via C6 to the commons rent fund; (6) true up against metered volumes and re-run on the published cadence or on a material-change trigger, prospectively only per NET-I21.
- NET-P3 (capacity allocation and congestion): (1) the operator publishes available capacity per interval in the register, attested against telemetry per NET-I8, never self-published alone; (2) while capacity exceeds demand, allocate at the published tariff first-come or pro-rata per the registered access terms; (3) when demand exceeds capacity, NET hands the defined capacity product (interval, direction, firmness) to the independent C2 venue, which clears the sealed uniform-price batch auction per C2-I4 and reveals the clearing congestion price after each batch; the operator and its concert-aggregated affiliates do not bid (NET-I8); (4) the venue segregates congestion revenue and routes it via C6 to expansion or the commons (NET-I8), expansion-bound capital entering the cost base at zero return; (5) the venue steward surveils withholding and outage-timing signatures under the C2-I6 sealed duty emitting only flagged referrals, with per-participant resolution only through an S2 contract in an A6 case; (6) when the NET-I9 threshold is sustained, open NET-P4.
- NET-P4 (expansion and interconnection): (1) trigger on a sustained congestion signal (NET-I9), an unservable qualified access request, or an interconnection request (NET-I10); (2) the operator files a costed expansion or interconnection plan with independently attested costing within the published window; capital committed under the resulting order is per se prudent per NET-I20; (3) on decline, an inadequate filing, or slow-walking, tender the expansion to third-party investors, who take a registered access position and corridor treatment for the new capacity; (4) new capital enters the attested cost base at commitment with its benchmark-cleared declared return registered (NET-I5), except congestion-funded or user-contributed capital, which enters at zero return (NET-I8); (5) perfect interconnection terms cost-anchored and registered; unresolved terms route to NET-P5; (6) on completion, republish capacity and recompute the corridor.
- NET-P5 (access dispute): (1) a denied, degraded, or discriminated user files at A6; (2) the status quo of existing service is preserved for the duration (NET-I14); (3) the operator carries the burden of proving a published capacity, safety, or conformance ground and, where foreclosure is shown, the COMP-I12 justification burden; (4) any provisional debit or freeze runs suspensive per A18-I34 and A18-I6, subject to the NET-I14 repeat-contest rule; (5) a sustained finding yields an access order plus restitution to the harmed user first, with repeat or retaliatory conduct escalating on the A18 ladder to the operator's culpable natural persons per A18-I45; (6) the outcome is registered in the public access register (NET-I15).
- NET-P6 (systemic readiness): (1) maintain the ring-fenced essential-function unit and its operational living will (NET-I12), the cost per se prudent per NET-I20; (2) attest annually, by independent test, that the unit can operate the network without the operator's cooperation; (3) re-size the K8-I12 and A18-I45 escrow bond to the current worst-case interruption and decommissioning estimate, independently set; (4) bar distributions and buybacks while resolution is under-funded; (5) on operator failure, transfer the unit and continue service, operated where activated by the RES emergency sub-agent under its A12 mandate and declaration discipline, and run the COMP-I16 shortfall waterfall: bond, then beneficial-owner clawback, then the sector pool, with any commons backstop re-attaching as clawback.

## Flows in the value doctrine (NVAL)

Physical flows are where the value doctrine touches the ground. Every dispatched joule, every custody transfer of a pallet, every tonne of flux across a stack or an outfall is already a ledger event in the register of its owner, and that fact is the whole sensing architecture: metering IS sensing. The Value Flow Map of the state reads physical flows through the same append-only registers that settle them, projected to cohort grain under the S-cluster rules. There is no second metering channel, no state-side shadow feed, no surveillance instrument distinct from the settlement record itself. The parties of a flow see their own records in full; the state sees cohorts, corridors and imbalance signals, and reaches a named party only through consent or a judicial access contract. A polity that built a separate observation pipe would have built the thing this architecture exists to make impossible.

Flows book on the Ae vector per axis, never as a scalar. F6 environmental flux books anti-value on the environment axis; delivered energy and mobility book created value on the access axes; buffers, storage and restorable capacity book on the resilience axis per the RES rules. Per-axis booking is load-bearing: C1-I4 binds here in full, so no cross-axis netting ever reaches a floor. A clean-air breach on the environment axis is not offset by delivered kilowatt-hours on an access axis, and a resilience credit never buys back flux anti-value. On the money side the market clears flows on the $ rail and the state prices only the residual: unpriced flux runs through exactly one pipeline in which F6 measures at source, K8 attributes to the run controller on the running embodied-anti-value ledger, and the MVAL overlay charges in $. One measurement, one attribution, one charge capped at the measured total, no double-counting of the same tonne down a supply chain, and border parity so an offshored dirty flow re-enters priced.

Where a flow dimension is under-served, the state pulls and never commands: grid expansion, clean generation, storage, cold-chain coverage and clean-air restoration are bought as $ strategic demand clearing like any order, never paid in transferable Ae. Where the Charter guarantees a flow, the guarantee is a delivered floor: the energy floor, the essential-goods reach floor and the clean-air commons floor form one family with a single ownership map, each floor with exactly one owner and exactly one first-charge draw, all funded from standing reserves decoupled from same-period recovery, all discharged only on metered delivery, never on charging alone. And flow data is property before it is anything else: the metered party owns its meter record, the operator owns its network data, the emitter owns its flux record under an attestation duty, and ambient quality is a stewarded commons released statistically. Every read is owner-gated; every read is logged.

### Invariants

- NVAL-I1 (metering is sensing). Every physical flow record (an F1 dispatch or consumption interval, an F3 custody event, an F6 flux measurement) SHALL be appended exactly once to the owning register: an owner-held register carrying the R2 append-only pattern (witnessed checkpoints, corrections as new entries; R2-I1 and R2-I13 cited as pattern, not as membership, so the two-ledger rule stands), an R3 event register for occurrences or a dedicated register for inventories, reads gated by the S cluster. The state's Value Flow Map SHALL derive all state-sensing aggregates from those same registers through registered S5 cohort projections, and the S5-I12 aggregation pattern (operator-side aggregates or distributed local evaluation with secure aggregation) is the only balance channel: person-grain readings never leave the owner's register, and any per-point leg flows only under a registered standing S2 contract class bound to the balancing purpose at the coarsest sufficient grain, every read S4-logged (the F1-owned rule of charter C30). Standing up any parallel state metering, probing or collection channel outside the settlement registers is an unauthorized read under the S7 taxonomy, booked as anti-value against the operating organ.
- NVAL-I2 (no per-party reads without permission). Any per-party or per-household read of a flow record by a state organ SHALL require the owner's consent_record or a judicial access contract per S2-I8; state sensing SHALL occur only at cohort grain under the S5-I1 k floor with releases registered and budget-debited per S5-I6; and every read, including intra-organ reads, SHALL append an S4 access event. Absence of a grant is a denial, for every reader including the state.
- NVAL-I3 (meter granularity is a sensitivity class). Sub-interval household meter data can reveal B10-grade patterns (occupancy, medical devices, daily rhythm), so fine-grained residential flow data SHALL be treated at the high-sensitivity ceremony class of S2-I5: no auto-issued grants, full per-grant ceremony, and the default projection shape for any lawful purpose (billing, balancing) is the coarsest interval that serves the declared purpose per the S2-I3 minimality discipline. Raw sub-interval readings retain only for the published window of the F1-owned compaction rule (charter C52), then compact to billing-interval aggregates under a witnessed checkpoint hash preserving court-provability, so the register never accumulates a perpetual person-grain corpus.
- NVAL-I4 (per-axis booking, no netting into floors). Each flow class SHALL declare the Ae axes it books: F6 flux books anti-value on the environment axis, delivered energy and mobility book created value on the access axes, and buffer or redundancy capacity books on the resilience axis. Booking is per axis and C1-I4 binds without exception: anti-value on any floor axis SHALL NOT be offset by credits on any other axis (MVAL-I10, FVAL-I4), and a floor-axis breach routes to the A18 ladder regardless of a positive aggregate.
- NVAL-I5 (two rails, one flow). Flow settlement is $-only per C2-I3: tariffs, freight, capacity payments and balancing settlements clear in $, while the flow's Ae deltas are V3 annotations booked-not-transferred to the parties' own accounts. A flow contract that denominates consideration in Ae fails closed (Charter Art. 13), and no flows-layer organ converts an Ae quantity to a price.
- NVAL-I6 (one overlay pipeline). Unpriced flux is priced through exactly one pipeline: F6 measures the flux at source, K8 attributes it to the run controller on the running embodied-anti-value ledger with the no-double-charge rule (K8-I4), and the published uniform MVAL overlay computes the $ charge (MVAL-I2, MVAL-I5). No flows-layer organ SHALL set a second price, levy a per-firm flow verdict, or charge the same measured tonne twice across the supply chain. The deletion of the abatement subsidy leg (charter C6, F6-owned) confirms this invariant: abatement's reward is the avoided overlay charge plus the ecology-axis Ae credit under the FVAL-I7 cap, and a matching subsidy would have set exactly the second flux price (overlay minus subsidy) this invariant forbids; the FVAL-I8 Pigovian subsidy is reserved for genuine positive spillovers and verified removals, and negative-flux removal lines are exempt from the charge seam, compensated only through the FVAL-I8/MVAL-I12 route.
- NVAL-I7 (border parity on flows). Imported energy, imported goods in motion and transit flows carry embodied anti-value charged at import parity per K8-I6 and MVAL-I3: the same published overlay on attested or defaulted-high figures, with origin charges credited only against a verified equivalent charge net of origin-side rebates, exemptions and matched subsidies (the origin-credit test of charter C12). For fungible carriers, origin attestations SHALL use the exporting system's measured average or published marginal mix per interval, verified against the interconnector physical schedule; contractual allocation below the physical mix is barred, and bare certificates take the default-high rule (the F1-owned rule of charter C7). Transit is defined physically, never by nomination: a sealed consignment in transit books only the flux it emits inside the polity, and grid transit is the netted physical interchange per balancing interval, with nominated transit above net physical interchange booking as import at parity.
- NVAL-I8 (suspensive contest on flow charges). Every same-tick overlay debit against a flow party's standing or escrow is a reversible provisional freeze on the contest discipline of A18-I34, subject to the F1-owned split of charter C8: honest-error bookings (imbalance, disputed measured figures) keep full suspensive effect, accrual suspending on contest; tamper and unmetered-flow defaults accrue into escrow during contest, collection deferred and accrual never suspended. Either class restores with interest if the finding is not sustained, and no forfeiture accrues before an A6 finding or the close of an uncontested attestation window (A18-I6, K8-I15; window per the charter C54 defaults annex).
- NVAL-I9 (strategic demand on the $ rail). Demand for under-served flow dimensions (grid expansion, clean generation, storage, cold-chain coverage, clean-air restoration, mobility reach) SHALL be expressed only as $-denominated subsidies, procurement bids or A7 incentive payments clearing like any order per C2-I9 and MVAL-I11, never as transferable Ae. The demand-field concentration ceiling of COMP-I13 binds: any single mission's share of a flow sector's demand is capped and demand above the ceiling is split across independent buyers or tendered. Exactly two bounded carve-outs exist: physically single-buyer system functions (balancing, system reserves, floor procurement) carve out under the F1-owned rule of charter C21 with its substitute guards, and under a live declaration floor and reserve procurement MAY exceed the ceilings up to the published emergency multiplier of the RES-owned rule of charter C41, each exceedance registered and expiring with the declaration.
- NVAL-I10 (the delivered flow-floor family). The energy floor, the essential-goods reach floor and the clean-air commons floor are one family of Charter Art. 6 delivered floors, joined by the mobility reach floor where the mandate activates it, and the family is the delivery side of the published M3-I11 essential-class floor, never new machinery. Ownership is fixed once, here: F1-I9 owns the household energy floor (definition, quantum, metered discharge), funded per F1-I10; F3-I16 covers settlement-level logistics reach for goods classes only and cross-references F1 for energy carriers; NET holds only the no-disconnection duty, citing F1-I10 as the funding rule so no second reserve draw exists. Each floor SHALL be funded from a standing reserve that recovered charges replenish, with a minimum coverage ratio making the floor a first-charge obligation per COMP-I17, so delivery is decoupled from same-period recovery volume, and exactly one COMP-I17 first-charge draw exists per household energy floor. Each floor names its A12-mandated floor steward per the RES-owned rule of charter C40, and competing draws under simultaneous declarations follow the RES-owned inter-floor priority order and exhaustion path of charter C37.
- NVAL-I11 (delivered means metered). A flow floor counts as discharged only on measured delivery: metered receipt at the serviced party for energy and goods reach, or measured ambient quality at cohort grain for the clean-air commons floor. Routing money to the commons or to a program does not discharge the obligation (the K8-I11 and C1-P4 delivered-floor logic), and self-declared delivery is never sufficient attestation. When metering or venues are down under a declared hazard, the RES-owned degraded-evidence regime of charter C33 substitutes witnessed human attestation and conservative estimation as a temporary evidence tier, trued up as append-only corrections in the mandatory reconciliation window; floor verification falls back to physical spot-witnessing at cohort grain and never lapses.
- NVAL-I12 (flow data as property). Ownership of flow data follows the S1 graph by role: the metered party owns its consumption record, the network operator owns its network operational data, the emitter owns its flux record, and ambient quality data (air, water, radiation) is a commons under a steward with disclosure duties. Each class is read only under an S2 contract issued by its owner or steward, and statistical release of any class passes the S5 cohort floor. Operator ownership of network data never opens an affiliate feed: the NET informational-separation rule (charter C23) binds, the competitive affiliate receives only the published projections available to all users on equal terms, and network-scale aggregates are stewarded as a data commons past the M3-I14 threshold.
- NVAL-I13 (the emitter's attestation duty). The emitter's ownership of its flux record carries a bound duty: attested flux figures SHALL be disclosed into the NVAL-I6 pipeline, and a refused, missing or unverifiable figure defaults high with the burden on the emitter to attest a lower one (the K8-I3 pattern). The default factors and every other measurement-layer parameter this duty reads are owned by the single A12-mandated flows parameter steward (charter C53), structurally separated from every charging and revenue-receiving organ and bound by the full MVAL-I6 discipline, with conservative activation defaults so no parameter is ever null. Ownership gates who else may read the record; it never gates the overlay's right to a defaulted-high attribution.
- NVAL-I14 (the operator owns the wires, not the parties). An operator's control of meters, lines or depots (M8, U3) confers no ownership of the metered parties' records. Operator reads of per-party data SHALL bind to a declared purpose_set per S2-I3 (billing, balancing, safety, and curtailment execution and restoration for the priority-flag set held under the RES-owned rule of charter C32); purpose creep is an S7 offense; the operator holds zero analytic privilege over per-party data (charter C23); and operator-published network statistics SHALL be k-floored and release-registered per S5-I6.
- NVAL-I15 (flux concealment). Meter bypass, tampering, unmetered discharge, and flaring or venting outside the measurement boundary are concealment-class A18 offenses: attribution defaults high against the responsible controller, detection resets the escrow multiplier per K8-I12, and concealed flux is perpetually clawback-eligible per the FVAL-I12 finality split. The presumption assumes the controller is the beneficiary: when a declaration attributes meter or instrument compromise to an external attack, the RES-owned victim-class carve-out of charter C35 demotes affected points to a neutral estimated tier and suspends the tamper presumption for the attested victim class, while individual concealment inside the window remains chargeable on specific evidence.
- NVAL-I16 (emergency reads are narrow and loud). Life-safety flow reads (a grid emergency, a contamination trace-back, a cold-chain failure on a gated class) SHALL run only through the pre-declared emergency templates of the S2 emergency path: bounded scope, statutory maximum duration, judicial ratification within the fixed window, and the loudest audit events in the system, with wrongful invocation compensated.
- NVAL-I17 (the mastering table). Each physical quantity has exactly one mastering register and every other appearance is a projection: the metering-point register masters raw measured quantity; the F6 inventory masters flux and cites the meter facts; the K8 running ledger masters the charge and cites the inventory line. Everything else, including consignment embodied carry, is a projection by reference keyed to the good's ledger position, never a mergeable copy: an upstream correction lands on the mastering register and propagates by reference, and when a movement completes the reference closes rather than merging back. A register found holding a second master copy of a mastered quantity is a charging-layer defect.

### Protocols

## Resilience, disruption and emergency (RES)

The flows layer, as specified elsewhere, assumes the network is up. RES governs the polity when it is not: the grid islanded, the port closed, the aquifer contaminated, the operator insolvent or sabotaged. Its governing stance is that an emergency compresses the doctrine, it never suspends it. The Ae vector is still booked per axis, the $ rail still settles, the market still clears wherever it physically can, the state still prices only the residual and still never sets a transaction price (C2-I1, MVAL-I1), and every read is still owner-gated. What a declared emergency changes is narrow and enumerated: a pre-authored operating regime takes over quantity allocation for the declared flows, pre-funded reserves release on pre-declared triggers, and a bounded class of emergency powers becomes exercisable, each one evidence-pinned, independently registered, judicially ratified, and compensated when wrong. The declaration itself is no exception: it is ratified, contestable, and ratcheted on renewal exactly because it is the single biggest lever in the section.

Two failure modes are equally catastrophic on this surface and the section is engineered against both. An under-prepared polity lets one node's failure cascade through interdependent flow networks until life-critical service fails for below-floor cohorts, which is a Charter Art. 4 and Art. 6 event, not an operational inconvenience. An emergency-powers polity lets "crisis" become the permanent license for seizure, price administration and surveillance, which is how the doctrine dies. So the section's spine is a pair of rules: floors first, meaning every shedding, rationing and reroute plan is authored as a published tranche ladder that serves capability floors before anything else and cuts from the top of consumption downward, with the subsistence and life-safety bottom absolutely non-severable; and powers bounded, meaning no emergency authority exists that was not declared in advance as a class, invoked against pinned evidence, expiring with the declaration, and regularized through the A18 suspensive pattern (A18-I34, A18-I55). Both rules are engineered to survive the hard cases: relay-speed protective action, a metering substrate taken down by the incident itself, a mass meter compromise whose owners are victims, and simultaneous incidents competing for one stock.

RES is cross-cutting over the flows layer: it defines the criticality tiers, operating regimes, the single reserve machinery, systemic-risk machinery, restoration orders and disclosure duties that F1 (energy), F3 (goods movement), F6 (emission flux), NET (network topology and operators) and NVAL (flow valuation) invoke, and it hosts the flows-layer defaults annex (RES-I27) that every section of the layer cites for its load-bearing windows. It adds no new coercion vocabulary and no new price machinery: coercion draws on the canonical A18 ladder and calibration, funding draws on the COMP-I17 standing-reserve pattern, and systemic exposure draws on the COMP-I16 waterfall and hard structural cap, applied here to flow networks.

### Invariants

- RES-I1 (criticality tier register): every flow class SHALL carry a criticality tier from the closed ordered set {life_critical, essential, standard, deferrable}, recorded in a published R1 register under an A12 mandate, contestable at A6, and never assigned or re-assigned ad hoc during a live incident. life_critical SHALL include at minimum: energy to life-safety loads, potable water, staple food, medicines and care-delivery inputs (M4, M5, K12, B10), and the communications capacity needed to call for and coordinate help (F9). The same register (the RES register) carries the hazard-class catalog and each regime family, each with a named A12-mandated owner and a published review cadence, the declaration-authority roster mapping declaring organ to hazard class (RES-I2), the inter-floor priority order and arbiter organ (RES-I23), the floor-steward roster (RES-I25), and the coordinating organ for multi-network and multi-declaration incidents (RES-I10). Tier assignment is a fact on the register, not an operator's self-declaration; an operator's evasive under-tiering of its own flow to escape RES duties is an S7 audit_evasion and A18 offense.
- RES-I2 (disruption is a declared, bounded event): an emergency regime exists in law only under a declared X3 incident on the semantic timeline, carrying declared scope (flow classes, area, cohorts), the invocation evidence pinned immutably at declaration time, a named accountable declaring organ drawn from the published declaration-authority roster (RES-I1), and a stated expiry. An undeclared "emergency" confers no power of any kind, with the sole, bounded exception of the pre-authorized automatic protective actions of RES-I20, which force a declaration-or-stand-down decision; expiry lapses every power automatically; renewal requires fresh pinned evidence, never a rollover; and widening scope mid-incident is a new declaration event with its own evidence, not an edit. The declaration itself is judicially ratified, contestable and ratcheted on renewal per RES-I22.
- RES-I3 (regimes are pre-authored): for each criticality tier and each pre-declared hazard class, the emergency operating regime (shedding order, rationing rule, reroute plan, restoration order) SHALL be authored, published, and drilled before it is ever needed, by the regime family's named A12-mandated owner (RES-I1). Each regime SHALL include its pre-authorized automatic protective action classes (RES-I20), its degraded-evidence rules and offline allocation rules (RES-I19), and any severance rules for Art. 6 floor quanta above the subsistence bottom (RES-I4, RES-I14). A declaration selects a pre-authored regime; it SHALL NOT author one. This is the A18-I60 pre-approved-template pattern applied to flow operations: no organ improvises its own emergency powers under a broad heading. An unauthored regime for a registered hazard class is a preparedness breach on the RES-I16 pattern, booked against the named owner.
- RES-I4 (floors first, executable): every load-shedding, rationing and reroute plan SHALL be authored as a published tranche ladder, and the published ladder is the compliance standard; for energy the ladder is F1-I11, which binds to this rule. Floors-first is a design duty on the ladder: floor and life-safety tranches sit last, discretionary consumption classes are cut first, and the A9 subsistence quantum and attested life-safety loads are absolutely non-severable per RES-I14; any severance of Art. 6 floor quantum above the subsistence bottom exists only inside the pre-authored regime itself (RES-I3), never improvised at dispatch. The ladder carries a best-feasible-granularity duty: where per-service-point control exists, floor quanta are protected per point through S6 predicate flags; where only feeder-level or substation-level control exists, the plan SHALL document the granularity limit and its compensating tranche design. Conformance of a dispatch is judged against the published ladder per actuation tranche; the cross-household floors-first comparison applies at ladder design time and at post-incident audit at S5 cohort grain (RES-I17), never as a per-dispatch legality test. A floor miss found at cohort-grain audit is a measured Art. 6 breach routing per A18-I44. The priority-flag set behind per-point protection is held by the OPERATOR, not the state, under a purpose-bound S2 contract limited to curtailment execution and restoration, every read S4-logged, zero analytic privilege on the R2-I27 pattern, refreshed and pruned on a published cadence; compromise of the flag set is handled as a B10-grade breach.
- RES-I5 (no emergency price-setting): a declared emergency changes quantity allocation, never price authority. The state SHALL NOT set, cap or floor any transaction price under any declared regime (C2-I1, MVAL-I1); venues keep clearing $ orders per C2-I4 wherever physically possible. Where scarcity pricing would price a below-floor cohort out of a life_critical flow, the remedy is delivered floor quantity funded from the standing reserve (COMP-I17) plus rationed allocation under RES-I4, never an administered price. Symmetrically, seller conduct in a declared emergency remains fully inside C2-I5 and COMP discipline: cornering a life_critical class during an incident carries the essential-access aggravation (COMP-I11).
- RES-I6 (one reserve machinery, standing-funded, anchored sizing): RES owns the reserve lifecycle for every life_critical and essential flow class, energy included: sizing, funding, triggers, release, replenishment and rotation are single-owned here, and F1-I16 supplies only the energy-specific product grid and adequacy inputs, binding to this machinery. Sizing is anchored to the hazard-class catalog: for each pre-declared hazard class, a published minimum coverage (quantity and days) per tier for life_critical and essential classes, validated against RES-I16 drill results. Sizing parameters carry the full MVAL-I6 discipline: multi-source, staked, published change provenance, and a setting organ structurally separated from every organ whose budgets or charges the sizing affects. A dispersion invariant applies: reserve stock for a settlement or region SHALL be reachable under each pre-declared corridor-loss scenario, verified in the RES-P3 pass. Reserves are funded per the COMP-I17 standing-reserve pattern: a commons reserve that recovered charges replenish, carrying a minimum coverage ratio as a first-charge obligation ahead of discretionary commons routing, so floor delivery never depends on same-period recovery volume. Stock rotates through the ordinary market before expiry per the RES-I28 rotation calendar, with the K8-I7 and C2-I7 conformance gates fully intact.
- RES-I7 (release discipline): a reserve release executes only against its pre-declared trigger, only within the declared quantity band, and only into the declared regime's allocation under a live declaration or through the venue; all above-floor volume releases through the venue at the venue-cleared price. Reserve stock SHALL NOT be released, withheld, or timed as a standing instrument to manage market prices outside a declared X3 incident; on-calendar rotation sales per RES-I28 are per se lawful and are not releases, and any off-calendar sale outside a live declaration is per se an off-trigger release. An off-trigger or price-management release books anti-value against the releasing organ and routes to A18, and the release record (trigger, quantity, disposition) is a public timeline event.
- RES-I8 (interdependency map and the systemic cap on networks): every essential-class operator SHALL file and maintain a network interdependency declaration (the flow classes it requires, the classes and cohort-grain demand it feeds, its single points of failure) in an R1 register, at aggregate grain per the S cluster, so cascade paths are computable without per-customer reads. The COMP-I16 machinery applies to flow-network centrality exactly as to firms: cascade estimates are independently set; bonded exposure is sized to the estimate (the A18-I45 and K8-I12 escrow_bond pattern); and a node whose failure cascade no feasible bond plus reserve can absorb takes the HARD STRUCTURAL CAP (mandated redundancy, de-concentration, or ring-fencing until residual cascade is at most the feasible bond), never a payable charge.
- RES-I9 (operational living wills): every essential-class operator SHALL maintain a pre-committed, ring-fenced, transferable essential-function unit per COMP-I16, plus an operational living will: the staffing plan, control-handover procedure, black-start data, credentials-escrow and supplier list sufficient for a successor or the Emergency sub-agent (RES-I24) to continue the flow through insolvency, sanction, sabotage, or the operator's non-cooperation. The living will's executability is verified by drill (RES-I16), not by attestation; a living will that fails its drill is an unremediated preparedness breach under RES-I16.
- RES-I10 (restoration order declared in advance): for each network, the restoration order SHALL be pre-declared in the published regime: black start first where applicable, then capability floors and life_critical services, then essential, then standard, then deferrable, following the RES-I1 register. The restoration orders of interdependent networks SHALL compose without circular waits, verified and published per the RES-P3 coherence check, and the coordinating organ for multi-network and multi-declaration incidents is named in the RES-I1 register. Restoration priority SHALL NOT be sold, auctioned, or reordered by customer weight during an incident; any deviation from the declared order is logged at deviation time with reasons and is a mandatory item of the RES-I17 post-incident record.
- RES-I11 (attack and sabotage): an attack on a flow network is an offense classified and routed per A18-I2 on the ordinary taxonomy (an Art. 4 breach through the criminal non-priceable class, priced harm through the priced class), with S8 governing the cyber-integrity surface of control systems; defense against an external state-grade attacker is A17's mandate, and RES supplies A17 the criticality register, regimes and reserves as its civil-resilience substrate. Where a declaration attributes meter or instrument compromise to the attacker, the RES-I21 victim-class carve-out governs the affected points. A declared emergency grants NO exemption from A18 evidence discipline: attribution confidence remains individualized for any hard rung (A18-I9), exigent enforcement force remains quota-bound and independently registered (A18-I60), and the dignity floor remains absolute (A18-I57).
- RES-I12 (emergency powers bounded): every emergency power over persons, property or operations SHALL be drawn from a pre-declared, A6-pre-approved class (RES-I3); invoked only under a live, ratified-or-pending declaration (RES-I22) with the invocation evidence pinned at invocation; registered by an organ independent of the invoker (the A18-I60 pattern); executed at least intrusion; and submitted for judicial ratification within the fixed short window of the RES-I27 defaults annex, per the A18-I55 pattern. An unratified act lapses, books anti-value against the acting organ, and restores the affected party with interest per the A18-I34 suspensive pattern. No open-ended power exists: everything expires with the declaration (RES-I2), and a lapsed declaration leaves no residue of authority.
- RES-I13 (requisition and compensation): emergency use of private capacity (vehicles, premises, stock, generation, compute) is a bounded requisition: drawn from a pre-declared class, logged as K2 acts under the declaration, and compensated on the $ rail at C7 independent valuation, never through the Ae ledger (Charter Art. 13). Compensation is not deferred to regularization: a provisional payment at a published conservative fraction of the C7 valuation SHALL be made within a fixed short window of the requisition act (RES-I27), trued up at final settlement with interest on any shortfall per the A18-I34 restoration pattern. Capability-floor property is never requisitioned out of its floor use (the PROP-I7 floor carve-out pattern); a requisition that is wrongful, unratified, or held past the declaration is remedied per the A18-I51 wrongful-coercion rule, restoration on every damaged axis with the state as payer, no-limitation and discovery-based. Uncompensated seizure exists nowhere in the model, emergency or not. A regime-commanded curtailment of a cleared market position is not a requisition: it settles per the F1-I18 commanded-curtailment rule (shortfall at the interval imbalance price with the declaring organ as counterparty, funded per COMP-I17, each settlement bound one-to-one to a logged shedding act, and excluded from C2-I5 manipulation screening), which RES binds by citation.
- RES-I14 (one floor bottom, absolute): no regime, rationing rule, requisition, or restoration order SHALL breach the A9 dignity floor (A18-I57): subsistence quantities of life_critical flows and attested life-safety loads for every person physically inside the declared scope, citizen or not, are the invariant bottom layer of every plan, absolutely non-severable, and never traded against system optimization, cost, or triage of the network itself. This is the single floor bottom of the flows layer: no sibling section authors its own severance clause, and F1-I11 binds here by citation. Severance of Art. 6 floor quanta above the subsistence bottom exists only inside RES-I4 pre-authored regimes.
- RES-I15 (emergency reads narrow and loud): life-safety data access during a declared incident follows the S-cluster emergency rule: bounded to pre-declared access classes, producing the loudest S4 audit events in the system, reviewed after the fact, and compensated when wrongly used. A declaration never opens general per-person surveillance: the state continues to sense at S5 cohort grain, per-person reads still require consent or an S2 judicial access contract, and any emergency read outside its pre-declared class is an S7 breach booking anti-value regardless of outcome. Custody of the curtailment priority-flag set is governed by RES-I4: it sits with the operator, never the state.
- RES-I16 (drills and preparedness audits): every pre-authored regime, reserve, living will, black-start procedure, restoration order and composed cross-network sequence (RES-P3) SHALL be exercised on a published drill cadence, with drill results and preparedness-audit findings booked as events on the timeline; drill results feed the RES-I6 sizing validation. A failure above a published severity threshold opens a remediation obligation with a deadline; unremediated failure past deadline books V4 anti-value against the responsible operator or organ, and for an essential-class operator escalates through its escrow and, on persistence, the A18 ladder. An undrilled plan is treated as no plan for RES-I3 conformance.
- RES-I17 (failure disclosure): after any declared incident, the factual record (what failed and when, what was shed and in what order, every deviation from the declared restoration order, which floors were missed and for how long, restoration times, reserves released) SHALL be published to the public timeline within the fixed disclosure window of the RES-I27 defaults annex (activation default 30 days). The disclosure shape is governed by S5-I9 and S5-I10: floor-miss facts publish at the smallest k-satisfying cohort grain with duration bands; a miss touching a sub-k cohort surfaces through the pooled-or-blinded signal (S5-I10, B10-I7) plus a sealed record visible to A6, so accountability survives without membership disclosure. The existence of a floor miss, a restoration-order deviation, and the incident's declared scope are never suppressible as facts, though their published grain follows the S5 shape; commercial and personal detail is redacted through S2 projection shapes. Concealment or material understatement of a floor miss or of incident scope is an A18 concealment-class offense with the A18-I49 perpetual flip.
- RES-I18 (preparedness costs sit with the operator, the reserve serves floors): the cost of an essential-class operator's own resilience (redundancy, living will, drills, bonded cascade exposure) is that operator's duty, carried in its escrow sizing per K8-I12 and the FVAL-I9 systemic-class floor, and is never a claim on the standing reserve; for a classified-network operator these mandated resilience costs are corridor-recoverable at commitment under the NET per se prudence rule, which changes nothing about the standing-reserve bar. The standing reserve funds floor delivery to cohorts, not operator rescue: an operator's failure funds itself down the COMP-I16 waterfall (escrow bond, then beneficial-owner clawback, then the sector pool) before any commons backstop, while the essential function itself continues through the RES-I9 ring-fenced unit regardless of who is paying.
- RES-I19 (degraded-evidence regime): for each pre-declared hazard class whose scenario removes metering, telemetry or venue clearing, the pre-authored regime SHALL define a temporary degraded-evidence tier: witnessed human attestation and conservative estimation stand in for metered evidence for delivery and floor discharge while the substrate is down, and pre-authored offline allocation rules govern quantity allocation while C2 cannot clear. Floor verification falls back to physical spot-witnessing at cohort grain and never lapses. On restoration, a mandatory reconciliation window (RES-I27) trues up every estimated entry as an append-only correction against returned telemetry; degraded-tier entries are marked as such at booking and carry the suspensive-contest pattern (A18-I34) on true-up.
- RES-I20 (pre-authorized automatic protective actions): each pre-authored regime SHALL enumerate its automatic protective action classes: relay-speed and operator-speed actions (under-frequency load shedding, islanding, protection trips, black-start commitment) that execute against published technical triggers without a live declaration. Each execution is logged as a K2 act at execution time and forces a declaration-or-stand-down decision within the fixed short window of the RES-I27 defaults annex, on the A18-I55 ratification pattern applied to the incident itself. An automatic action outside its published trigger envelope books anti-value against the operator.
- RES-I21 (victim-class carve-out): when a declaration attributes meter or instrument compromise to an external attack (an A17 or S8 finding, provisional attribution sufficing), the affected points demote collectively to a neutral estimated evidence tier, class average or historical baseline, not the adversarial-high tamper default; the tamper presumption is suspended for the attested victim class; and a fleet re-attestation protocol governs recovery: R5 recalibration at a published cadence, with quarantined readings excluded from settlement until re-attestation. Individual concealment inside the incident window remains chargeable on specific evidence; the carve-out shields victims, never conduct.
- RES-I22 (declaration ratification, contest and renewal ratchet): the declaration itself SHALL be submitted for judicial ratification within the fixed short window of the RES-I27 defaults annex, on the A18-I55 pattern, and lapses on non-ratification. Any affected party or steward holds an A6 contest route against the declaration's scope and evidentiary sufficiency. A declaration not sustained books anti-value against the declaring organ and opens A18-I51 remedies for parties harmed by acts taken under it. Renewals ratchet: every Nth renewal (N published in the RES-I1 register) requires ratification by a higher or broader organ together with a published necessity finding, so a rolling emergency can never be sustained by the declarer and a registrar alone.
- RES-I23 (inter-floor priority and reserve exhaustion): a pre-declared inter-floor priority order SHALL be published in the RES-I1 register: A9 subsistence quantities of life_critical flows first, then the remaining Art. 6 floors by criticality tier. A named arbiter organ, recorded in the same register, allocates one physical stock across competing claims when two or more live declarations draw on it. On reserve exhaustion, a pre-authorized emergency funding path (borrowing against future overlay recovery) keeps floor delivery funded on the $ rail; price administration is never the exhaustion response (RES-I5).
- RES-I24 (Emergency sub-agent): the Emergency sub-agent is an A12-mandated organ whose activation class is operator-failure findings only (insolvency, sanction, sabotage, or non-cooperation of an essential-class operator). Its powers are limited to operating the ring-fenced essential-function unit of RES-I9 under the operational living will; its activation follows the RES-I12 registration and ratification discipline; a mandatory stand-down and handback trigger returns control when a qualified successor exists or the failure finding closes; every act is S4-logged and booked as a K2 act under the declaration. It holds no power over any solvent, cooperating operator and no power outside a live declaration.
- RES-I25 (floor steward and duty of means): each essential class and settlement cohort SHALL carry a named floor steward, an A12-mandated organ recorded in the RES-I1 register, who is the obligated deliverer for A18-I44 breach-routing purposes. During physical impossibility the steward carries a duty of means: activate pre-authored reroute regimes, draw reserves, tender alternate modes; impossibility periods are logged as timeline facts and suspend the breach clock only while the duty of means is demonstrably discharged. Carriers carry only their contracted leg liability per F3-I6, never the floor obligation itself.
- RES-I26 (emergency demand modulation): under a live declaration, floor and reserve procurement for the declared flow classes MAY exceed the C2-I9 and COMP-I13 demand-concentration ceilings up to a published emergency multiplier. Each exceedance is registered at the time it opens, expires with the declaration, and carries a mandatory published wind-down schedule executed at close-out (RES-P5), so emergency-scale state demand can never persist as standing demand share.
- RES-I27 (flows-layer defaults annex): RES hosts the flows-layer defaults annex: one published table of activation defaults for every load-bearing window and horizon in the layer (confirmation windows, reconciliation windows, disclosure windows, durability minimums, restoration and ratification windows), owned by the flows-layer mandate organ, each value revisable only with published change provenance. Sections cite the annex instead of leaving a window unset, and the annex carries a conservative activation default for every entry so no window is ever null; for example, the RES-I17 disclosure window activates at 30 days.
- RES-I28 (rotation calendar): the reserve steward SHALL publish an annual rotation calendar in R1, fixed quantities per C2 batch window. On-calendar sales are per se lawful rotation and are not releases; off-calendar sales outside a live declaration are per se off-trigger releases under RES-I7. Calendar amendments take effect only after a published notice period drawn from the RES-I27 defaults annex.

### Protocols

- RES-P1 (declare and operate an incident): (1) verify the triggering occurrence against a pre-declared hazard class; refuse declaration on any class not pre-authored (RES-I3); where automatic protective actions have already executed per RES-I20, their declaration-or-stand-down window governs this step. (2) Declare the X3 incident with scope, pinned evidence, the accountable organ from the declaration-authority roster, expiry, and the selected pre-authored regime; an organ independent of the declarer registers the declaration (RES-I2, RES-I12), and the declaration is submitted for judicial ratification within the RES-I27 window (RES-I22). (3) Activate the published tranche ladders floors-first per RES-I4, verifying floor delivery at S5 cohort grain each interval, and falling back to the RES-I19 degraded-evidence tier where the sensing substrate is down. (4) Release reserves only on declared triggers within the declared band (RES-I7). (5) Log every emergency act as a K2 act under the declaration, including each requisition with its C7 valuation basis, and pay interim requisition compensation within the RES-I27 window (RES-I13). (6) At expiry, renew only on fresh pinned evidence, honoring the RES-I22 renewal ratchet, or let every power lapse (RES-I2).
- RES-P2 (reserve lifecycle): (1) set per-tier, per-hazard-class coverage targets from the published sizing parameters under MVAL-I6 discipline (RES-I6). (2) Fund to the COMP-I17 first-charge coverage ratio from the standing reserve. (3) Procure at market as $-denominated strategic demand clearing through C2 batch auctions under the F1 single-buyer rule and its substitute guards (independent procurement agent or mandatory published tenders, product-level unbundling); outside a live declaration the C2-I9, COMP-I13 and MVAL-I11 ceilings apply unmodulated, and under a live declaration any exceedance runs only per RES-I26. (4) Rotate stock before expiry per the RES-I28 calendar, with the conformance gates intact (RES-I6). (5) On a declared trigger, release per RES-I7 and publish the release record. (6) Replenish to target and publish the post-release stock position on the timeline.
- RES-P3 (systemic-risk pass over a flow network): (1) collect interdependency declarations into the R1 register at aggregate grain (RES-I8). (2) Compute cascade estimates independently of the assessed operators (COMP-I16 discipline). (3) Where feasible bond plus reserve absorbs the estimated cascade, size the operator's bonded exposure per K8-I12 and A18-I45; where it cannot, apply the hard structural cap: mandated redundancy, de-concentration, or ring-fencing until residual cascade is at most the feasible bond (RES-I8). (4) Verify each living will and essential-function unit by drill (RES-I9, RES-I16).
