## 0. How the mobility and communications flows layer works

These are the two flows that carry persons and speech: the roads, rails and vehicles that move a body, and the networks and spectrum that move a message. Both are enabling flows, access to every other flow rides on them, and both are the sharpest rights and surveillance surfaces in the whole model. The layer's first commitment is that **freedom of movement and expression are non-priceable, the floors are delivered, and the flow never owns what it carries**: the state may not meter, gate, or shut down movement or communication outside the single A18-I4 coercion gate; a mobility floor and a communications floor are delivered upfront through the standing reserve, not declared and withheld; and a carrier carries bits without owning the content, a transit operator moves persons without owning the movement, and a spectrum steward holds a commons it can never enclose.

**Mobility (MOVE, TRANSIT, F7).** A passenger is not a consignment: they hold dignity, freedom of movement, and their own travel record. Freedom of internal movement and of exit is non-priceable, so a ban, checkpoint, internal passport, or exit block is coercion routing through A18-I4, and a public-health restriction reaches movement only through MEAS. The mobility floor is delivered through COMP-I17 (reachability of work, care, education and civic life, with accessible transit for disabled and elderly riders), a transit desert is a live person-grain breach, and denial of carriage never silently strands a floor-holder. The transit operator, a natural monopoly on its corridor, earns a C1-I7 benchmark return over a rent-bearing NET-I2 commons corridor, reaches floor-holding settlements on a bounded lead-time, and is backed by a named, pre-funded steward of last resort so an essential route is never stranded.

**Communications and spectrum (COMMS, SPECTRUM, F9).** The carrier carries lawful traffic without blocking, throttling, or paid-preferencing (net neutrality is the essential-facility rule for bits, decidable against a pre-published identity-blind policy), never determines content lawfulness (only an A6 order blocks a named item), and never claims ownership of the N-cluster content it transmits. The communications floor, including the RES-I1 life_critical capacity to call for help, is delivered with an accessible endpoint, and a communications shutdown routes only through A18-I4, never a public-health pretext. Spectrum is a RENT commons stewarded under the NET-I2 / P2-I7 grant discipline, licences are time-boxed rent-bearing grants against squatting, a protected unlicensed-commons floor stays open, and interference resolves against a registered protection ratio or a commons etiquette mask.

**Privacy and integrity (PRIV, FINT).** Communication content, communication metadata, and travel and location data are all subject-owned and default-deny, metadata never gated weaker than content; the state senses at S5 cohort grain only, per-person access needs consent or a targeted, time-bounded, loud-audited A6 judicial contract with mandatory delayed notice, and bulk collection is void. The right to encrypt is protected and a systemic backdoor is void; an anonymous fare instrument and an anonymous connectivity option are delivered at floor level so account-based use is never the only path. The integrity surface closes the panopticon of aggregated movement and metadata, the pretextual shutdown and internet kill-switch, the movement ban as coercion, the net-neutrality violation and carrier self-preferencing, spectrum hoarding, deep-packet inspection and content tampering, censorship, and the encryption-backdoor demand, on both flanks and with the money rail never netted against the anti-value rail.

Three commitments recur, matching the rest of the model:

1. **Movement and speech are non-priceable, and the floor is delivered.** No coercion of movement or communication happens outside the A18-I4 gate; the mobility and communications floors are delivered upfront through COMP-I17, and a public-health measure reaches movement only, never the communications medium.
2. **The flow never owns what it carries.** A carrier carries content it does not own or inspect, a transit operator moves persons it does not track, and spectrum and network-scale movement data are commons stewarded under NET-I2 / P2-I7 and M3-I14, never enclosed as freehold.
3. **Both flanks, everywhere.** The traveler, the speaker, the mobility-poor, the surveilled, and the dissident are protected against non-delivery, tracking, shutdown, and de-anonymization; and the honest operator and carrier and the genuine narrow emergency are protected against below-cost expropriation, rigid rules that bar real network management, sham interference and hardship claims, and a fare evader or a class-inflating flood.

The sections follow: MOVE passenger mobility and the freedom of movement; TRANSIT transit systems and operators; COMMS communications carriage and net neutrality; SPECTRUM spectrum and the transmission commons; PRIV communications and mobility privacy; FINT mobility and communications flow integrity and capture.

## MOVE passenger mobility and freedom of movement (F7)

### Purpose

F7 models the movement of persons across the U3 physical network as a MOS flow. It exists to do three things at once and to keep them from collapsing into each other: to let the market clear the carriage of passengers on the $ rail through honest operators who are paid for real service; to guarantee every natural person a delivered mobility floor (reachability of the essential destinations that everything else in life is reached through); and to hold freedom of movement, internal and exit, as a non-priceable right that the state may not weaponize, meter, or condition.

F7 rides on U3 (the roads, rail, and transit infrastructure are the pipe; F7 is the flow) and cites the F3 carriage pattern (route, leg, custody chain) for the mechanics of moving a body from origin to destination. But F7 refuses the F3 default in one decisive respect: the cargo is a person. A passenger is not a consignment. A person has dignity and freedom of movement, is never a commodity, and is never a tracked parcel. Every invariant below is written to protect both flanks: the traveler, the mobility-poor, the disabled and elderly rider, and the person whose movement is restricted on one side; the honest operator who SHALL NOT be ordered to run an unfundable route, and the genuine narrow safety intervention, on the other, while never opening a door to mass tracking or a rights breach.

MOVE owns the person, rights, and floor rules of F7: mobility-floor delivery, non-priceability of movement and of exit, coercion routing, the emissions and congestion residual, passenger-is-not-a-consignment, denial-of-carriage, and (with PRIV as the F7/F9 privacy home) travel-data sovereignty. TRANSIT owns the operator and network machinery. On any MOVE/TRANSIT conflict, MOVE governs the person and floor rule; the person-grain MOVE-I6 is the authoritative mobility-floor breach predicate that TRANSIT-I4 defers to.

This section defines only F7-specific person-side content. It does not re-model the F3 carriage machinery, the F3-I18 data-sovereignty pattern, the PRIV privacy invariants, the A18-I4 coercion gate, the C1-I7 attested-cost corridor, the essential-facility anti-abuse rules, the COMP-I17 standing reserve, or the PLAN/NET/CONN reach doctrines. It cites them.

### Object model

- **passenger**: a natural person in transit or intending transit. The passenger is the subject, not the object, of the trip. A passenger holds the mobility floor on their own-account basis (COMP-I17) and owns their own travel and location record (F3-I18, with PRIV the F7/F9 privacy home). A passenger is never reduced to a consignment record, a manifest line, or a persistent cross-context track.
- **trip**: a single intended movement from an origin to a destination, held by the passenger. A trip has an essentiality classification (MOVE-I5) determined by mapping the destination to a required-set essential-destination class, never by inferring the traveler's purpose.
- **journey**: the realized routing of a trip across one or more legs, possibly across operators and modes. The journey cites the F3 route object but its custody semantics are duty-of-care handoffs, not commodity custody (MOVE-I13, MOVE-I15).
- **leg**: one carriage segment on one operator/mode, citing the F3 leg. A leg has a fare that clears on the $ rail.
- **operator (O1 firm)**: the carrier running a route. Typically a natural monopoly and essential facility on its corridor; its returns are governed by C1-I7 and its access conduct by the essential-facility anti-abuse rules (TRANSIT machinery); its drivers hold the O3 delivered labor floor.
- **fare / tariff**: the price of a leg or journey, set by the operator, clearing on the $ rail, including any operator-applied class-uniform congestion price inside the C1-I7 corridor, subject only to a state-priced emissions or externality residual (MOVE-I9), never a state-set price.
- **fare instrument**: the means by which a fare is paid. An anonymous, non-identity-linked instrument (cash-equivalent or unlinkable token) SHALL be available at floor level (MOVE-I19).
- **mobility floor entitlement**: the per-person delivered reachability guarantee (MOVE-I5), whose required-set of essential-destination classes, magnitudes, and theta are owned by Distributive-Justice-and-Capability-Floors.md, delivered through COMP-I17.
- **restriction**: any state act that narrows a person's movement (a movement ban, an internal-passport requirement, a checkpoint, a removal, a curfew, a travel ban, or an exit block). A restriction is coercion and is an A18-I4 object, not an F7 administrative object.

### Invariants

**MOVE-I1 (a passenger is not a consignment).** F7 SHALL cite the F3 carriage pattern for route, leg, and custody mechanics, but the passenger object SHALL be modeled as a dignity-bearing subject and never as a commodity, manifest line, or freehold-tracked parcel. Even a consented persistent carriage identifier SHALL be unlinkable-by-default or rotated, SHALL exist only at the specific service grain and time-box for which it was chosen, and SHALL NEVER be a cross-context standing linkage key, nor a condition of the mobility floor, nor a condition of a fare. *Purpose:* prevents the F3 goods logic from silently importing owner-of-cargo, commodity-custody, or standing-track semantics onto persons. *Predicate:* any F7 record or protocol that (a) assigns a passenger a persistent unique carriage identifier readable across trips or contexts, (b) makes an identifier a condition of the floor or a fare, or (c) treats a person as inventory that a carrier owns while in custody, is void. *Hook:* schema conformance check at operator onboarding; a violating manifest field or cross-context key fails validation; FINT bars the cross-context linkage.

**MOVE-I2 (freedom of internal movement is non-priceable).** Freedom of movement within the polity SHALL be a non-priceable right. A movement restriction, an internal-passport or internal-permit-to-travel requirement, a checkpoint that gates ordinary movement, or a travel ban SHALL be treated as coercion and SHALL route through the A18-I4 coercion gate (see A18-I4 for the max_rung function and the A10-calibration fail-closed default); it SHALL NOT be issued as an F7 tariff, licence condition, or administrative rule. *Purpose:* denies the state any standing power to meter or gate ordinary movement outside the single coercion threshold. *Predicate:* a restriction lacking a valid A18-I4 authorization is unlawful and unenforceable. *Hook:* checkpoints and bans carry an A18 authorization reference or are void; absence is itself an A18 rights harm.

**MOVE-I3 (public-health movement restriction routes through MEAS).** A movement or gathering restriction justified on public-health grounds (quarantine, isolation, cordon, area closure) SHALL route through MEAS (Social-Provision-and-Public-Health.md), which itself cites A18-I4. It SHALL NOT be issued directly by a transit operator or an F7 authority, and public health SHALL NEVER reach communications. *Purpose:* keeps the health-emergency path narrow, loud, calibrated, and reviewable, and out of the hands of carriers. *Predicate:* the restriction carries a MEAS authorization; its scope, duration, and population are the minimum found necessary; it expires on a stated trigger. *Hook:* MEAS review and A18-I34 suspensive contest attach; an indefinite or untriggered restriction is void.

**MOVE-I4 (movement restriction to punish or suppress is a rights harm).** A movement ban, exit block, or travel restriction imposed to punish a person, to suppress dissent, association, or expression, or to coerce political behavior SHALL be an A18 non-priceable criminal-class rights harm, never a priceable measure and never a public-health or safety measure by relabeling. *Purpose:* closes the pretext gap between a genuine safety/health restriction and a suppression tool. *Predicate:* if the stated ground is safety/health but the effect selects on protected activity or political identity, the concealment class applies with the perpetual flip. *Hook:* A6 adjudication; loud audit; no priceable settlement discharges it.

**MOVE-I5 (the mobility floor is delivered, not declared).** Every natural person SHALL hold a delivered mobility floor: the reachability, within a bounded and accessible travel budget, of the enumerated required-set of essential-destination classes (essential services, work, care, education, and civic participation). The required-set of essential-destination classes and the per-class resolution rule are owned by Distributive-Justice-and-Capability-Floors.md; for example "work" resolves to reachability of the person's registered or nearest-adequate employment. Magnitudes and the participation condition theta are owned by the same section; delivery is through COMP-I17 on the person's own-account basis. The origin for a person with no fixed address SHALL be their current presence location or a nominated service anchor, so the floor resolves for the unhoused and transient. *Purpose:* makes mobility a delivered capability, not a paper right, because access to every other flow rides on it. *Predicate:* for each floor-holder, there exists a routing to each required-set essential-destination class within the floor's time/cost/accessibility budget from the resolved origin. *Hook:* COMP-I17 standing reserve; a shortfall is a live breach (MOVE-I6), not a waitlist.

**MOVE-I6 (transit desert and mobility poverty are live floor breaches).** A transit desert, a service gap, or a fare barrier that leaves a floor-holder unable to reach a required-set essential-destination class within the floor budget SHALL be a live mobility-floor breach, remediable in the present tense, not a planning aspiration. This person-grain predicate is the authoritative mobility-floor breach test to which TRANSIT-I4 defers. *Purpose:* forces present-tense delivery rather than declaration. *Predicate:* breach exists where reachable_essential_classes(person) is a strict subset of the required set under the floor budget, evaluated from the MOVE-I5 resolved origin, with essentiality set by required-set class mapping, not inferred purpose. *Hook:* the breach opens a COMP-I17 remedy (subsidized routing, on-demand accessible service, a topped-up fare per MOVE-I10) and a PLAN/NET coordination obligation (MOVE-I7); it does not by itself order an operator to run an unfundable route (MOVE-I11).

**MOVE-I7 (reach is coordinated across layers).** F7 mobility-floor delivery SHALL coordinate with PLAN settlement reach and NET/CONN universal reach: where the built environment or the network fails to place essential destinations within reach, the remedy is shared across PLAN, NET/CONN, and F7 and SHALL NOT be discharged by any one layer alone or dumped onto the operator. *Purpose:* prevents each layer from declaring the gap someone else's problem. *Predicate:* an open MOVE-I6 breach carries a coordination record naming the responsible layer(s). *Hook:* cross-layer remedy ledger; an unassigned breach escalates.

**MOVE-I8 (accessibility is part of the floor, not an option).** Accessible transit and accessible vehicles for disabled and elderly persons, and for persons with temporary or situational mobility limits, SHALL be delivered as a constitutive part of the mobility floor, not an add-on, a premium tier, or a best-effort. A service that is nominally reachable but not physically usable by a floor-holder with a disability SHALL count as unreached under MOVE-I6. *Purpose:* stops formal reachability from masking exclusion. *Predicate:* for a floor-holder with an accessibility need, an essential class is reached only if the routing is accessible end to end (vehicle, stop, transfer, information). *Hook:* accessibility is a validation dimension of MOVE-I6's reachability predicate; an inaccessible-only routing is a breach.

**MOVE-I9 (the fare clears on the $ rail; the state prices only the emissions or externality residual).** The fare and tariff SHALL be set by the operator and SHALL clear on the $ rail. Transit congestion pricing SHALL be an operator-applied, class-uniform money congestion price inside the C1-I7 corridor and SHALL NOT be a state charge (aligned with COMMS-I7). The state SHALL price only the genuine emissions or externality residual of the movement on a named Ae axis, and SHALL NOT set, cap as a price control, or dictate the fare itself, and SHALL NOT levy a state congestion charge that operates as demand-suppression. There is no taxation of the fare. *Basis test:* the state charge on a leg SHALL equal a measured externality on a named axis with a stated quantity basis; any charge whose magnitude tracks demand-suppression or the fare level rather than the measured externality fails the basis test and is void. *Purpose:* keeps the market clearing the price while the state books only the per-axis Ae residual, never netted against $ and never across axes. *Predicate:* the state's charge on a leg equals a priced residual externality with a stated axis and quantity basis; any state instrument that targets the price level itself, or tracks demand rather than a measured externality, is void. *Hook:* externality pricing is booked per axis (F6 emissions on its axis); a fare cap or congestion levy dressed as an externality charge fails the basis test.

**MOVE-I10 (a floor-pricing-out is topped up, not price-controlled).** Where a lawful market fare prices a floor-holder out of essential reachability, the remedy SHALL be a COMP-I17 top-up to the person (making the essential trip affordable on the person's own account), NOT an order to the operator to lower the fare below the C1-I7 corridor. *Purpose:* protects the floor-holder without confiscating the honest operator's return, and keeps the price signal intact. *Predicate:* affordability_gap(person, essential_trip) > 0 opens a person-side top-up; the operator is paid the cleared fare. *Hook:* COMP-I17 disbursement; the operator's C1-I7 corridor is untouched.

**MOVE-I11 (no unfundable-route conscription).** An honest operator SHALL NOT be compelled to run a route or service at a loss to satisfy a mobility-floor obligation without a funded path through the C1-I7 attested-cost corridor (benchmark-cleared return, user-first restitution) or an explicit funded public-service obligation on the $ rail. *Purpose:* protects the honest operator flank; the floor is society's obligation, not a private confiscation. *Predicate:* any service order below attested cost carries a corresponding funding instrument; absent it, the order is unenforceable. *Hook:* C1-I7 corridor attestation; the funding source is named (COMP-I17 / PLAN / NET), never left on the operator's balance sheet.

**MOVE-I12 (mobility is a floor and a right, not a patronage lever).** Mobility-floor delivery and freedom of movement SHALL NOT be conditioned on political loyalty, social-credit behavior, residence-registration compliance beyond the floor's own theta, identity linkage, or any patronage relationship. Movement SHALL NOT be granted or withdrawn as a reward or punishment. *Purpose:* forbids weaponizing access to movement as a governance lever. *Predicate:* any conditioning of floor delivery on a criterion outside the published theta is a floor-waiver-void event (O5) and, where it targets protected activity, a MOVE-I4 rights harm. *Hook:* theta is the sole gating condition of record; extra conditions are void and audited.

**MOVE-I13 (carrier duty of care to the passenger).** The operator SHALL owe the passenger a duty of care for safety and dignity across the journey, including safe carriage, protection while in the operator's charge, accessible handling, and safe completion or re-accommodation. Subcontracted carriage SHALL carry joint duty of care per the F3-I7 subcontracted-carriage joint-liability pattern. *Purpose:* the person-analogue of custody is care, not commodity possession. *Predicate:* an incident of harm during carriage attaches operator liability; a subcontracting handoff does not shed it. *Hook:* F3-I7 joint liability; O1 firm bears it with a natural-person bearer (no autonomous-agent escape).

**MOVE-I14 (travel and location data are subject-owned and default-deny).** A passenger's travel, location, and journey record SHALL be subject-owned and default-deny per F3-I18, with PRIV the F7/F9 privacy home. A per-person read of travel or location SHALL require the passenger's consent or an A6 judicial contract of the full PRIV-I7 shape (per-subject, necessity-found, finite-expiry and time-bounded, loud-audited, renewal only on a fresh necessity finding), so no standing or indefinitely-rolling contract can slip through. The state senses only at S5 cohort/corridor grain per PRIV; bulk or mass collection of passenger movement is barred per PRIV; metadata is protected no less than content. *Purpose:* the passenger-is-not-a-parcel principle in the data plane. *Predicate:* any per-vehicle or per-person travel read without consent or a PRIV-I7 contract is unlawful; any standing collection across passengers is barred. *Hook:* section-local S4 log at the read; the PRIV break-glass and delayed-notice discipline governs; over-broad or standing reads are void.

**MOVE-I15 (no standing surveillance record from custody).** The journey and leg custody handoffs SHALL exist for duty of care and service continuity only and SHALL NOT constitute or accrete a standing surveillance record of the passenger's movements. Retention SHALL be the minimum needed for the trip, billing on the $ rail, safety, and lawful audit, then extinguished, under the PRIV retention duty and expiry. *Purpose:* stops care-of-passenger logging from becoming a persistent track. *Predicate:* retention beyond the stated minimal purposes without consent or a PRIV-I7 contract is unlawful. *Hook:* retention schedule enforced; over-retention is an F3-I18 / PRIV violation.

**MOVE-I16 (a network-scale movement aggregate is a data commons).** An aggregate of passenger movement or corridor traffic crossing the scale threshold SHALL be stewarded as a data commons per M3-I14 and SHALL NEVER be held as carrier freehold. The state's use of it SHALL be at S5 cohort grain for planning and floor-delivery, not per-person, per PRIV. *Purpose:* the valuable network view is a commons, not a private asset or a surveillance store. *Predicate:* an aggregate above threshold held as freehold, or read at person grain, is void. *Hook:* M3-I14 stewardship; commons rent and access rules apply; the carrier is custodian, not owner.

**MOVE-I17 (denial of carriage and removal are bounded).** Denial of carriage or removal of a passenger from a vehicle SHALL be permitted only for a genuine, contemporaneous safety, capacity, or lawful-conduct ground, applied non-discriminatorily per the essential-facility anti-abuse rules; it SHALL NOT be used to punish, to suppress, or to select on protected identity or activity. The contemporaneous ground SHALL be recorded and disclosable to the passenger at the moment of denial. A18-I34 suspensive contest attaches to every denial or removal, not only those that function as a movement restriction. A denial or removal that leaves a floor-holder unable to complete an essential-destination-class trip within the floor budget SHALL itself be a live MOVE-I6 breach and SHALL open an immediate COMP-I17 accessible-routing remedy, so exclusion never silently denies reachability while the contest runs. A removal that functions as a movement restriction also routes through A18-I4. *Purpose:* protects both the passenger against pretextual exclusion and the operator/other passengers against a genuine safety threat. *Predicate:* a denial carries a recorded, disclosed, contemporaneous ground; a denial selecting on protected activity flips to the concealment class; systematic denial by a monopoly operator is an essential-facility abuse. *Hook:* anti-abuse review; A18-I34 contest on every denial; COMP-I17 accessible-routing remedy on a floor-holder denial; A6 adjudication on abuse.

**MOVE-I18 (stranded-passenger completion duty).** On service interruption, cancellation, or breakdown, the operator SHALL owe the passenger safe completion, re-accommodation, or return, prioritized by RES-I1 criticality of the trip (life_critical and essential trips first), and SHALL NOT abandon a passenger mid-journey. Where interruption is caused by a genuine RES emergency, the operator's obligation is best-effort re-accommodation with C1-I7 / RES cost recovery, not confiscatory. *Purpose:* the person-cargo cannot be written off like a delayed parcel, while the honest operator facing a real emergency is not bankrupted. *Predicate:* an abandoned passenger is a duty-of-care breach (MOVE-I13); a re-accommodation under a declared RES event carries a cost-recovery path. *Hook:* F3-I7 joint liability across carriers; RES-I1 tiering orders the response.

**MOVE-I19 (an anonymous fare instrument is delivered at floor level).** An anonymous, non-identity-linked fare instrument (a cash-equivalent or an unlinkable token) SHALL be available at floor level for every leg and journey, and SHALL NOT be degraded, surcharged, throttled, or given inferior service relative to identity-linked use. Its availability is a validation predicate, not an aspiration, tied to the no-floor-conditioning-on-identity rule (MOVE-I12) so account-based use can never become the sole practical path to the mobility floor or a fare. *Purpose:* anonymity delivered, not merely declared; the person need not surrender identity to move. *Predicate:* if no anonymous floor-level instrument is purchasable and usable end to end for an essential trip, or if such use is surcharged or degraded, the floor is not delivered (a MOVE-I6 breach). *Hook:* validation at operator onboarding and fare-system conformance; an identity-only fare gate fails validation; FINT bars cross-context linkage of the resulting fare identifiers.

**MOVE-I20 (fare integrity by the least-identifying means).** The operator MAY verify fare payment and deter evasion only through the least-identifying means that confirms a valid fare or floor entitlement, an unlinkable validity token or an S6 zero-knowledge predicate, without building or contributing to a MOVE-I14 track. Non-payment SHALL be a MOVE-I17 lawful-conduct denial ground only after the passenger's floor entitlement has been checked; a floor-holder's free-by-right trip SHALL NEVER be treated as evasion. *Purpose:* fare enforcement that does not become identification or a track, and does not criminalize the floor. *Predicate:* a verification pipeline that identifies the rider beyond a valid/invalid predicate, or a non-payment denial issued before the floor-entitlement check, is void. *Hook:* S6 zero-knowledge predicate; verification confirms validity, not identity.

**MOVE-I21 (exit from the polity is non-priceable and is coercion when restricted).** Any restriction on a natural person's EXIT from the polity SHALL be coercion and SHALL route through the A18-I4 coercion gate with A18-I34 suspensive contest. An exit block imposed to punish, to exclude, to suppress protected activity or association, or to coerce political behavior SHALL be a non-priceable criminal-class rights harm per MOVE-I4, regardless of the internal or external scope line and until a border doctrine is published. *Purpose:* closes the gap by which the exit case could be deferred to an unwritten border doctrine and used as a suppression tool; exit is held as coercion now, not later. *Predicate:* an exit restriction lacking a valid A18-I4 authorization is unlawful; one selecting on protected activity or political identity flips to the concealment class with the perpetual flip. *Hook:* A6 adjudication; A18-I34 contest; no priceable settlement discharges an exit-suppression harm.

### Protocols

**Trip planning and floor check.** On a trip request, the person's own-account agent (COMP-I17) classifies the trip's essentiality by mapping the destination to a required-set essential-destination class (MOVE-I5), and, for essential classes, evaluates the MOVE-I6 reachability predicate against the floor budget from the resolved origin. A pass routes to normal $-rail fare clearing. A fail opens a MOVE-I6 breach record with a MOVE-I7 coordination assignment and, where the cause is affordability, a MOVE-I10 top-up. A defined S4 audit event records the floor-check outcome, pass or fail, with the breach-record id and the MOVE-I7 coordination assignment, written at S5 cohort grain, so a breach finding is reviewable.

**Fare clearing.** The operator quotes the fare; the leg clears on the $ rail. Any congestion component is an operator-applied, class-uniform money price inside the C1-I7 corridor, not a state charge (MOVE-I9). The state's emissions or externality residual charge is computed per axis and booked per axis, never netted against the fare and never across axes (MOVE-I9). No fare is set, capped, or taxed by the state. An anonymous fare instrument is offered at floor level with no degradation or surcharge (MOVE-I19); fare validity is verified by the least-identifying means without building a track (MOVE-I20). A defined S4 audit event records the residual-externality booking with its axis, quantity, and basis, written at S5 cohort grain, so an externality charge is reviewable and a fare-tracking or demand-suppression charge is detectable.

**Restriction issuance.** No F7 actor SHALL issue a movement or exit restriction. A restriction is constructed only as an A18-I4 object (MOVE-I2, MOVE-I21), or, for public health, a MEAS object that cites A18-I4 (MOVE-I3). The issuing authority routes through the A18-I4 coercion gate (see A18-I4 for the max_rung function and the A10-calibration fail-closed default) and attaches scope, duration, and an expiry trigger; the subject holds A18-I34 suspensive contest.

**Data access.** A per-person travel/location read is served only on the passenger's consent or an A6 judicial contract of the full PRIV-I7 shape (per-subject, necessity-found, finite-expiry and time-bounded, loud-audited, renewal only on a fresh necessity finding), S4-logged (MOVE-I14). Planning and floor-delivery reads are served at S5 cohort/corridor grain from the M3-I14 commons (MOVE-I16). Bulk requests are refused at the interface.

### Lifecycle and edge cases

- **Multi-operator journey.** Legs clear independently on the $ rail; duty of care and completion duty (MOVE-I13, MOVE-I18) follow the F3-I7 joint-liability chain end to end; no operator sheds care at a handoff.
- **Disabled or elderly traveler with no accessible routing.** Scored as unreached (MOVE-I8), opening a MOVE-I6 breach and a COMP-I17 accessible on-demand remedy; formal existence of an inaccessible service does not close the breach.
- **Unhoused or transient floor-holder.** The origin resolves to the person's current presence location or a nominated service anchor (MOVE-I5); the breach predicate resolves for them like any other floor-holder.
- **Fare spike on an essential corridor.** The operator keeps its C1-I7 corridor; the floor-holder is topped up (MOVE-I10); the state does not price-control the fare. If the spike reflects essential-facility abuse rather than cost, the anti-abuse path (not a price cap) applies.
- **Rider who will not surrender identity.** An anonymous floor-level fare instrument is available with no surcharge or degradation (MOVE-I19); fare validity is confirmed by an unlinkable token or S6 zero-knowledge predicate (MOVE-I20); identity-only fare gating fails validation.
- **Alleged fare evasion by a floor-holder.** The floor entitlement is checked first; a free-by-right trip is never evasion (MOVE-I20); only after the check does non-payment become a MOVE-I17 lawful-conduct ground.
- **Genuine safety removal that looks like suppression.** Contemporaneous ground is recorded and disclosed to the passenger at the moment of denial (MOVE-I17); A18-I34 contest runs; if the removal strands a floor-holder short of an essential class, it is itself a live MOVE-I6 breach opening an immediate COMP-I17 accessible-routing remedy; if the pattern selects on protected activity, it flips to the concealment class (MOVE-I4) with the perpetual flip.
- **Emergency curfew or area closure.** Only via MEAS/A18-I4 (MOVE-I3), minimal and time-boxed, life_critical movement (calling for and coordinating help, reaching care) preserved per RES-I1; indefinite closure is void.
- **Exit block on a person.** Held as coercion now (MOVE-I21), routed through A18-I4 with A18-I34 contest; an exit block to punish or suppress is a non-priceable criminal-class harm per MOVE-I4, not deferred to a border doctrine.
- **Operator asked to serve a transit desert.** Served only with a named funding path (MOVE-I11); the floor obligation is funded by society through COMP-I17/PLAN/NET, never confiscated from the operator.
- **Passenger asks to erase their travel record.** Honored down to the minimal lawful retention (billing, safety, lawful audit) per MOVE-I15; the M3-I14 commons aggregate is unaffected because it is cohort-grain and non-identifying.

## TRANSIT transit systems, routes and mobility operators (F7)

### Purpose

F7 is the passenger-mobility flow: the movement of persons across the U3 physical network. TRANSIT owns the operator and network machinery of that flow: essential-facility returns, slot and capacity allocation, the corridor grant, the service tier, the steward-of-last-resort, MaaS conduct, and the operator's own operational-telemetry ownership. The person, rights, and floor invariants (mobility-floor delivery, non-priceability of movement, coercion routing, the emissions residual, denial-of-carriage, and travel-data sovereignty) are owned by MOVE. TRANSIT exists so that a genuine transit operator earns a benchmark return and is not expropriated, while the operator-facing machinery reliably discharges the MOVE floor as a delivered capability, not a declared aspiration.

F7 rides on U3 and does not own it. U3 (Buildings-Infrastructure-and-Settlement.md) owns the physical roads, rail, and stations; NET owns the essential-facility and natural-monopoly access rules; CONN owns metering. F7 owns the flow objects (route, schedule, service, ridership) and the conduct of the operator (O1) that runs them. F7 cites the F3 carriage pattern (Energy-Logistics-and-Emissions.md) for route and leg mechanics but a passenger is not a consignment: the cargo is a natural person with dignity and freedom of movement, never a commodity, and the person retains subject-ownership of their own travel record per MOVE and PRIV.

Precedence: on any MOVE/TRANSIT conflict over a person or floor rule, MOVE governs; TRANSIT governs only the operator and network machinery. This section protects both flanks on every contested point: the rider gets a reached, maintained, non-discriminatory, privacy-respecting, resilient network, and the honest operator gets cost recovery, a benchmark return, protection from expropriation, and a bounded, contestable set of obligations.

### Object model

- Route: a persistent origin-to-destination path over U3 right-of-way, with a stated set of served stops and interchanges.
- Schedule: the time-indexed binding of vehicles and crews to a route, publishing headway, span-of-service, and capacity.
- Service: an operating instance (a run) executing a schedule on a route, carrying a criticality tier assigned by an attested rule under the RES regime (RES-I1), never by operator discretion.
- Ridership record: the per-rider event stream (boarding, alighting, interchange, fare event) generated by fare collection. This is subject-owned travel-and-location data governed by MOVE-I14/PRIV.
- Reach obligation: the operator-facing binding that a route SHALL serve a floor-holding settlement at a service level sufficient to discharge the MOVE mobility floor.
- Transit operator: an O1 firm (Organizations-Contracts-and-Employment.md) holding a stewardship grant over corridor and right-of-way and running services. Typically a natural monopoly and essential facility.
- Corridor/right-of-way grant: the NET-I2/P2-I7 extraction grant under which the operator occupies right-of-way and shared control-signalling spectrum, held per NET-I2 and P2-I7, never as freehold.
- MaaS platform: a mobility-as-a-service intermediary that brokers, prices, or dispatches trips across one or more operators or gig drivers.
- Steward of last resort: the named standby actor (a designated public continuity operator or a pre-contracted standby operator registered in the A12 route register) that keeps an essential route running through operator insolvency.
- Continuity reserve: a standing, funded reserve on the $ rail, distinct from the P2-I7 wind-down bond, sized to run an essential route for a bounded takeover window.

### Invariants

**TRANSIT-I1 (operator is an essential facility; returns are corridor-clamped).** A transit operator that is a natural monopoly or controls an essential facility (a station, an interchange, a corridor, a ticketing rail with no timely substitute) SHALL recover cost and earn a return only within the C1-I7 attested-cost corridor with benchmark-cleared returns and user-first restitution, and SHALL be subject to the essential-facility anti-abuse rules. Purpose: the rider is not gouged by a monopoly and the honest operator is not denied a benchmark return. Predicate: essential_facility(operator) is true when no timely, adequate substitute route or facility exists for a served floor-holding settlement. Hook: returns above the corridor ceiling trigger C1-I7 restitution; do not re-model the corridor here.

**TRANSIT-I2 (non-discriminatory access to shared facilities and ticketing).** An operator controlling a shared station, interchange, platform, depot slot, or ticketing/settlement rail SHALL grant access to other operators and to MaaS platforms on published, cost-oriented, non-discriminatory terms, and SHALL NOT block, degrade, self-preference, or bundle to foreclose a rival. Purpose: shared bottlenecks stay open so riders get integrated journeys and honest competitors are not foreclosed, while the facility owner still recovers its attested cost of shared use. Predicate: access denial or price differential lacking a cost or capacity justification is a violation. Hook: essential-facility anti-abuse rules and C1-I16 harm-capacity escrow.

**TRANSIT-I3 (corridor and right-of-way are a stewardship grant, never freehold).** An operator's occupancy of corridor, right-of-way, and any control-signalling spectrum SHALL be held as a NET-I2/P2-I7 extraction grant; see P2-I7 for the required elements and the register conformance check, and SHALL NOT be enclosed as freehold. Every essential-or-higher route grant SHALL, as a condition of the grant, pre-name its standby steward and pre-fund the continuity reserve per TRANSIT-I16. Purpose: the public commons under the tracks stays commons-owed, the operator gets a defined, bankable, defensible tenure for its capital, and continuity capacity for essential routes exists before failure, not after. Hook: register the grant in the A12 register; on expiry or trigger, revocation runs through NET-I2, not through operator discretion.

**TRANSIT-I4 (settlement-reach delivery discharges the MOVE floor).** Transit SHALL reach every floor-holding settlement at a service level sufficient to deliver the MOVE mobility floor (MOVE-I5), coordinating with U3/NET universal reach and PLAN settlement reach. This is the operator-facing delivery obligation; the authoritative person-grain mobility-floor breach predicate is MOVE-I6 and the accessible-reach breach is MOVE-I8, which this obligation defers to and does not restate. Purpose: mobility is an enabling flow, so reach is delivered, not declared, and the operator carries a bounded, network-level duty rather than the person-grain adjudication. Hook: where the market clears the route on the $ rail the operator runs it under the C1-I7 corridor; where it does not, the residual is delivered through the COMP-I17 standing reserve on the person's own-account basis and, failing an operator, by the steward of last resort (TRANSIT-I16).

**TRANSIT-I5 (reach is delivered on a bounded lead-time, not a perpetual plan).** A reach obligation SHALL be met within a published, bounded lead-time, and a route SHALL NOT be held permanently in "planned" status to defer the floor. Purpose: prevents the reach floor from being evaded by indefinite planning. Predicate: reach_status == planned past the bounded lead-time without an accessibility-preserving interim service (for example a demand-responsive shuttle) is a breach. Hook: on expiry the obligation escalates to the steward of last resort (TRANSIT-I16); the person-grain breach itself is adjudicated under MOVE-I6/MOVE-I8.

**TRANSIT-I6 (accessible reach is owned by MOVE-I8).** Accessible reach for disabled and elderly persons is owned by MOVE-I8. The reach delivered under TRANSIT-I4 SHALL satisfy MOVE-I8 as delivered, and this section states no separate accessibility predicate. Hook: coordinates with the built-environment accessibility standard in U3; enforced as a delivered obligation, not a priced externality. See MOVE-I8.

**TRANSIT-I7 (operator contest never suspends physical delivery of the reach floor).** An operator MAY contest a reach obligation, a return determination, a slot allocation, or a grant condition, and the contest is suspensive of the financial and administrative determination per A18-I34, but it SHALL NOT suspend physical delivery of the MOVE reach floor to a floor-holding settlement. Purpose: protects both flanks: the operator gets genuine due process on money and obligation scope, and riders are never stranded pending an appeal. Hook: during contest the service runs; any over- or under-recovery is trued up on the $ rail once the contest resolves.

**TRANSIT-I8 (transparent, non-discriminatory capacity and slot allocation).** Scarce capacity (platform slots, depot access, peak paths, interchange dwell) SHALL be allocated by a published, non-discriminatory rule with an auditable basis, and SHALL NOT be allocated to self-preference the facility owner or to foreclose a rival. Purpose: scarce network capacity is a shared resource, not a lever for exclusion, while the operator retains a transparent way to prioritise safety and reliability. Predicate: an allocation lacking a published rule or an audit trail is void. Hook: allocation logs are S4-audited; disputes go to A6.

**TRANSIT-I9 (attested criticality tiering and continuity).** Every service SHALL carry a RES-I1 criticality tier (life_critical / essential / standard / deferrable) set by an attested, auditable rule against the RES regime and the served-population criticality, not by operator discretion, and an operator running any essential-or-higher service SHALL hold a continuity plan meeting the RES resilience regime for that tier, including graceful degradation and restoration order. BOTH under-classing (to shed the continuity-plan duty) and over-classing (to claim priority or protection) are misrepresentation under the A18 concealment class; class integrity is handled identically to the COMMS class-attestation invariant. Purpose: a transit-network failure does not strand the persons who most depend on it, the operator's obligations scale to the genuine tier, and the tier cannot be gamed in either direction. Predicate: a service whose tier is unattested, or attested contrary to the RES rule, or lacking a conforming continuity plan at essential-or-higher, is non-compliant. Hook: the tier assignment carries its attestation basis in the S4 log; RES regime governs; life_critical mobility (evacuation, access to emergency care) inherits the RES-I1 life_critical protections and coordinates with F9 life_critical communications.

**TRANSIT-I10 (the operator owns only its own operational telemetry, at the k-floor).** The operator owns only its own operational telemetry at operational grain and time-box (vehicle telemetry, occupancy, timetable performance). The operator SHALL NOT hold any network-scale movement or ridership aggregate, which is stewarded as an M3-I14 data commons per TRANSIT-I12/MOVE-I16. Operational or telemetry data held without a per-subject basis SHALL be maintained at, or aggregated to, a grain that cannot reconstruct an individual rider's trajectory (the S5 k-anonymity floor); any operational field that resolves below that grain is a per-rider record governed by MOVE-I14/PRIV, not the operational carve-out. Purpose: the operator keeps the operational data it needs to run and be regulated, but fare collection is a location-surveillance surface and the rider does not surrender their location history by buying a ticket. Hook: mirrors F3-I18 through MOVE-I14; cross-reads are S3 projections under S2 contracts, S4-logged. Do not re-model F3-I18.

**TRANSIT-I11 (per-rider travel-data sovereignty is owned by MOVE-I14/PRIV).** Per-rider travel-and-location data sovereignty is owned by MOVE-I14 (the F3-I18 pattern, PRIV as the F7/F9 home). The state and third parties SHALL sense ridership only at S5 cohort/corridor grain; a per-rider location read SHALL require the rider's consent or the PRIV-I7 A6 judicial contract in its full shape (per-subject, necessity-found, finite-expiry / time-bounded, loud-audited, renewal only on a fresh necessity finding), and bulk or standing collection of rider location data is void. This section restates no rule; see MOVE-I14 and PRIV-I7. Hook: A6 contract, S4 loud audit, X3 for narrow-and-loud emergencies only, compensated when wrongly used.

**TRANSIT-I12 (network-scale movement aggregate is owned by MOVE-I16).** A network-scale aggregate of movement or ridership data that crosses the M3-I14 scale threshold is an M3-I14 data commons owned by MOVE-I16; the operator SHALL NOT hold it as freehold or sell it as an enclosed asset. See MOVE-I16; do not re-model M3-I14.

**TRANSIT-I13 (movement restriction is coercion; owned by MOVE-I2).** A restriction on a person's movement (a travel ban, a boarding refusal on state order, a zone closure to persons, a service suspension imposed as a sanction) is coercion; the routing of a movement restriction through the A18-I4 coercion gate is owned by MOVE-I2 (see A18-I4 for the max_rung function and the A10-calibration fail-closed default). The operator and the state have no standing power to bar a person's movement that bypasses MOVE-I2/A18-I4. Hook: a public-health movement restriction reaches movement or gathering only via MEAS (Social-Provision-and-Public-Health.md), which cites A18-I4, and NEVER reaches communications. This does not reach an operator's ordinary, non-discriminatory fare-and-conduct rules applied without state coercion (denial-of-carriage is owned by MOVE-I17).

**TRANSIT-I14 (transit and gig-driver labor floor).** A transit worker or a gig/MaaS driver moving passengers SHALL hold the O3 delivered labor floor, and a MaaS platform SHALL NOT structure dispatch, rating, or contracting to void it; O5 floor-waiver-void applies, and no autonomous-agent dispatch path leaves a floor breach without a natural-person bearer. Purpose: the persons who deliver mobility are not floored below the labor floor by platform structuring, and the honest platform that meets the floor is not undercut by one that does not. Hook: O3, O5; the acting-in-concert graph and the canonical veil-piercing rule reach a platform that fragments employment to evade the floor.

**TRANSIT-I15 (MaaS platform is a broker, not an owner of the trip or the rider).** A MaaS platform SHALL carry and broker trips on published, non-discriminatory terms and SHALL NOT, by virtue of brokering, acquire ownership of the rider's travel record or foreclose competing operators or direct booking. Purpose: intermediation must not become enclosure of either the rider's data or the mobility market, while a genuine platform still earns its brokerage within the C1-I7 corridor where it is itself an essential facility. Hook: MOVE-I14 (rider owns the record), TRANSIT-I2 (non-discriminatory access), C1-I7 where the platform is a natural monopoly.

**TRANSIT-I16 (steward of last resort is named, funded, and pre-committed).** If an operator running an essential-or-higher route becomes insolvent, abandons the route, or has its grant revoked, physical service on that route SHALL continue under a named steward of last resort until an orderly successor is in place; continuity of the reach floor takes priority over the operator's estate. The steward of last resort SHALL be a specific actor: a designated public continuity operator or a pre-contracted standby operator, registered in the A12 route register. Continuity SHALL be funded by a standing, funded continuity reserve on the $ rail, distinct from the P2-I7 wind-down bond and sized to run the essential route for a bounded takeover window. Every essential-or-higher route grant under TRANSIT-I3 SHALL pre-name its standby steward and pre-fund the reserve as a condition of the grant, so continuity capacity exists before failure. Purpose: operator failure does not strand the settlements that depend on the route, and the honest operator's creditors still get an orderly, non-confiscatory wind-down rather than an expropriation. Predicate: an essential-or-higher route grant without a pre-named standby steward and a pre-funded continuity reserve is non-conforming and SHALL NOT be issued; cessation of an essential route without a successor or steward in place is barred. Hook: coordinates with TRANSIT-I7 (delivery never suspended); the P2-I7 wind-down bond and any residual value are settled on the $ rail per the insolvency estate, separately from the continuity reserve.

**TRANSIT-I17 (emissions ride on F6, owned by MOVE-I9).** The environmental externality of a transit service (emissions, noise) is priced and booked per MOVE-I9 on the F6 emissions flow, per axis on the Ae vector, never netted against fare revenue on the $ rail. See MOVE-I9; the state prices only the residual externality, not the fare.

**TRANSIT-I18 (published performance and honest information).** An operator SHALL publish its schedule, real-time service state, capacity, accessibility status, RES-I1 service tier and its attestation basis, and reach-standard performance, and SHALL NOT misrepresent service, mis-declare its service tier, or otherwise mislead a rider to defeat a reach or continuity obligation. Purpose: riders and planners can rely on accurate information and the reach floor is measurable, while the operator is judged on published, auditable metrics rather than shifting expectations. Hook: performance data feeds the RES tier assessment and the TRANSIT-I4 reach determination; a mis-declared tier under TRANSIT-I9 and any service misrepresentation are concealment-class matters under A18.

**TRANSIT-I19 (ridership retention is minimized and time-boxed).** The per-rider ridership record SHALL be deleted, or irreversibly aggregated to cohort grain (the S5 k-anonymity floor), on a bounded, published schedule: retained only for carriage, $-rail fare settlement, safety, and a specifically-bounded lawful-audit window, and then extinguished. Retention beyond that without the subject's consent or a PRIV-I7 A6 contract is void and is a PRIV-I8 bulk event. No purpose tag SHALL defer deletion indefinitely: in no case is a per-rider record retained beyond the PRIV-I16 maximum-retention ceiling. Purpose: a location-surveillance track cannot be built by accretion under an open-ended retention ground. Hook: PRIV-I16 owns the retention duty, the expiry, and the concrete ceiling; this invariant is the F7 operational instance of that duty.

**TRANSIT-I20 (fare integrity by least-identifying verification).** The operator MAY verify fare payment and deter evasion only through the least-identifying means that confirms a valid fare or floor entitlement, an unlinkable validity token or an S6 zero-knowledge predicate, without building a MOVE-I14 track; verification SHALL NOT become identification or a persistent linkage key. Non-payment is a MOVE-I17 lawful-conduct denial ground only after floor entitlement has been checked, and a floor-holder's free-by-right trip SHALL NOT be treated as evasion. Purpose: the operator protects fare revenue on the $ rail and the honest rider is not surveilled to ride, and the floor-holder is never criminalized for a trip they are entitled to. Hook: S6 zero-knowledge predicate; the fare-integrity right and the denial ground are owned by MOVE (fare integrity, MOVE-I17 denial-of-carriage); this invariant is the operator-side verification machinery.

### Protocols

**Reach determination and delivery.** PLAN and NET publish the settlement-reach standard; the reach obligation binds each floor-holding settlement to a route and a service level with a bounded lead-time (TRANSIT-I4, TRANSIT-I5). Where the market clears the route on the $ rail, the operator runs it under the C1-I7 corridor. Where it does not, the residual is delivered through the COMP-I17 standing reserve on the person's own-account basis and, failing an operator, by the steward of last resort (TRANSIT-I16). The person-grain floor breach and the accessible-reach breach are adjudicated under MOVE-I6/MOVE-I8, not restated here.

**Capacity and slot allocation.** Scarce slots at shared facilities are allocated by the published rule (TRANSIT-I8); requests, awards, and denials are S4-logged; a denied operator or platform contests to A6 without service to riders being suspended (TRANSIT-I7).

**Tier attestation.** Each service's RES-I1 tier is set by the attested rule (TRANSIT-I9) and published with its attestation basis (TRANSIT-I18); an under- or over-classed tier is a concealment-class matter under A18, handled identically to the COMMS class-attestation invariant.

**Ridership data access.** Default-deny on per-rider reads. A planner or researcher receives an S5 cohort/corridor projection (TRANSIT-I11). A per-rider read runs only on rider consent or the PRIV-I7 A6 judicial contract in full shape (per-subject, necessity-found, time-bounded, loud-audited). A network-scale aggregate is an M3-I14 data commons owned by MOVE-I16 (TRANSIT-I12). The operator holds only its own operational telemetry at the S5 k-floor (TRANSIT-I10). This mirrors the F3-I18 pattern through MOVE-I14/PRIV; F7 carries the person and never becomes the owner or inspector of the person's record.

**Retention and deletion.** The per-rider ridership record is extinguished or aggregated to cohort grain on the published schedule and never beyond the PRIV-I16 ceiling (TRANSIT-I19).

**Fare verification.** Fare validity is confirmed by an unlinkable token or S6 predicate; floor entitlement is checked before any evasion finding (TRANSIT-I20).

**Movement restriction.** Any state-imposed movement restriction, boarding bar, or travel ban is coercion owned by MOVE-I2 and gated by A18-I4 (TRANSIT-I13); a public-health restriction routes through MEAS. The operator executes only a lawfully gated, adjudicated restriction and logs it S4.

**Operator failure.** On insolvency, abandonment, or grant revocation of an essential-or-higher route, the pre-named steward of last resort assumes physical operation funded by the standing continuity reserve (TRANSIT-I16); the P2-I7 wind-down bond and the estate are settled separately on the $ rail without confiscation.

### Lifecycle and edge cases

- Route opening: a new route enters as planned, binds a NET-I2/P2-I7 corridor grant (TRANSIT-I3), and SHALL reach revenue-or-reserve service within the bounded lead-time (TRANSIT-I5); interim demand-responsive service preserves the floor meanwhile. An essential-or-higher grant SHALL pre-name its standby steward and pre-fund the continuity reserve (TRANSIT-I16) as a condition of issue.
- Route curtailment: an operator MAY seek to curtail a standard-or-deferrable route through contest (TRANSIT-I7), but an essential route SHALL NOT be curtailed unless a successor or steward is in place (TRANSIT-I16).
- Contested return: an operator disputing its C1-I7 determination keeps running (TRANSIT-I7); over/under-recovery is trued up on resolution.
- Emergency degradation: under a RES event, services degrade in reverse-criticality order (deferrable first, life_critical last) per the attested tiers (TRANSIT-I9); life_critical mobility coordinates with F9 life_critical communications.
- Tier gaming: an operator that under-classes to shed the continuity-plan duty, or over-classes to claim priority or protection, commits a concealment-class misrepresentation under A18 (TRANSIT-I9, TRANSIT-I18).
- Rider-data breach-glass: a narrow, loud X3 emergency read of a per-rider record is reviewed after the fact and compensated if wrongly used (TRANSIT-I11); it never becomes standing access.
- MaaS multi-homing: a rider MAY hold and use multiple platforms and direct bookings; no platform SHALL lock the rider's record or foreclose the others (TRANSIT-I15).

## COMMS communications, carriage and net neutrality (F9)

### Purpose

F9 is the communications and data-transmission flow: the movement of bits between endpoints across a network of networks. This section models F9 as carriage. A network_service accepts traffic at an address and delivers it to another address without becoming the owner, author, or inspector of what is carried. The carried thing, documents, datasets, software, messages, is N-cluster content (Documents-Data-and-Software.md) and its rights live in PRIV and the N-cluster, not here.

F9 is an enabling flow: access to markets, provision, care, participation, and the capacity to call for help all ride on it. So a communications floor is delivered, not declared, reaches an accessible endpoint, and carriage is non-discriminatory and content-blind. This section protects both flanks on every contested point: the speaker, the subscriber, the dependent public service, and the genuine emergency get delivered connectivity, private carriage, and non-discriminatory access; the honest carrier gets a benchmark return, the right to run real, pre-published network management, and protection from being conscripted as a censor or an unpaid common carrier.

F9 rides on the U3 physical communications plant (fiber, towers, exchanges, spectrum). U3, the NET essential-facility and natural-monopoly access rules, the CONN connection and metering, and the physical resilience are owned by the built-environment layer (Buildings-Infrastructure-and-Settlement.md) and are cited here, not re-modeled. U3 is the pipe; F9 is the flow. The spectrum a wireless carrier occupies is held under the NET-I2 rent-bearing, time-bounded, revocable stewardship grant per the P2-I7 extraction-grant discipline, never freehold; this section cites that grant and adds only F9-specific carriage content.

### Object model

- **network_service**: an F9 carriage service operated by an O1 firm (Organizations-Contracts-and-Employment.md). Attributes: service_id, operator (O1 ref), footprint (U3 segments and address_blocks served), essential_facility_flag, natural_monopoly_flag, return_basis (C1-I7 corridor ref), floor_carrier_flag (whether it delivers the connectivity floor under CONN coordination), management_policy_ref (the A12-registered network-management policy per COMMS-I21).
- **session**: an established carriage relationship for a stream or exchange of traffic between endpoints. Attributes: session_id, endpoints (address refs), class (RES-I1 tier: life_critical / essential / standard / deferrable, set by an attested rule per COMMS-I19, never the originator's assertion or carrier discretion), class_attestation_basis (S4 log ref), management_state (normal / congestion-managed), subject_owner (the natural or legal person whose communication it is). A session is subject-owned and default-deny to third parties.
- **endpoint**: the terminal or device at an address through which a floor-holder originates and receives carriage, including the COMMS-I3 life_critical call for help. Attributes: endpoint_id, address ref, life_critical_capable_flag, accessibility_flag (usable by disabled users). Floor delivery requires an accessible, life_critical-capable endpoint per COMMS-I22.
- **channel**: a provisioned carriage capacity between points on the U3 plant that sessions ride over. Attributes: channel_id, U3_segment ref, spectrum_grant ref (NET-I2 / P2-I7 where wireless), capacity, congestion_state.
- **address_block**: a range of routing identifiers, numbers, or namespace entries (network addresses, telephone numbers, routing identifiers). Stewarded namespace, A12-registered, portable by the subscriber, never carrier freehold.
- **traffic_aggregate**: a derived, cohort-grain measure of flow across channels or corridors (volumes, congestion, reachability). Sensed at S5 grain; at network scale it is a data commons under M3-I14, never carrier freehold.

### Invariants

**COMMS-I1 (F9 is flow, U3 is plant).** F9 communications SHALL be modeled as a flow riding on the U3 physical communications network and, where wireless, on spectrum held under the NET-I2 grant per P2-I7. F9 SHALL NOT claim, re-model, or enclose the U3 plant or the spectrum commons it occupies. Predicate: any F9 object asserting freehold over a U3 segment or spectrum band fails validation. Hook: register cross-check against the A12 spectrum register and the U3 asset register.

**COMMS-I2 (communications floor delivered not declared).** Every natural person SHALL hold the communications floor, basic connectivity sufficient for essential participation, delivered upfront on the person's own-account basis via the COMP-I17 standing reserve, coordinating with the CONN essential-communications carrier and the CONN connection floor, and reaching an accessible endpoint per COMMS-I22. The floor magnitude and the participation condition theta are owned by Distributive-Justice-and-Capability-Floors.md and cited, not set here. Purpose: access to everything else rides on F9. Hook: floor non-delivery is a floor breach, routed to the distributive-justice enforcement path, and is O5 floor-waiver-void (Organizations-Contracts-and-Employment.md) if a contract purports to waive it.

**COMMS-I3 (life-critical call-for-help capacity).** The connectivity floor SHALL include the RES-I1 life_critical capacity to originate and coordinate a call for help, and this capacity SHALL remain available even when the subscriber relationship is degraded, unpaid, or suspended. Predicate: a session with class = life_critical (emergency origination) SHALL NOT be blocked, throttled, or deprioritized by any carrier, including a non-serving carrier reachable on the U3 plant. Hook: RES resilience regime; failure to carry a life_critical origination is a criminal-class harm bearer question under A18 with a natural-person bearer (no autonomous-agent path escapes it).

**COMMS-I4 (carrier as natural monopoly and essential facility).** Where a network_service is a natural monopoly or essential facility (essential_facility_flag or natural_monopoly_flag set), its returns SHALL be set on the C1-I7 attested-cost corridor with benchmark-cleared returns and user-first restitution, and access to it SHALL be governed by the essential-facility anti-abuse rules (Economy-and-Markets.md). This section cites those rules and does not restate them. Both flanks: the honest carrier earns a benchmark return and is not expropriated; the dependent public cannot be gouged or foreclosed.

**COMMS-I5 (carriage non-discrimination, the net-neutrality rule).** A carrier SHALL carry traffic without blocking, throttling, or paid-preferencing by source, destination, application, protocol, or content, subject only to COMMS-I6 technical-attack mitigation and an A6-ordered block. The non-discrimination obligation attaches to ALL traffic; content-lawfulness is NEVER a carrier determination, and a content block issues only on a specific A6 order naming the item (COMMS-I13, I15). Net neutrality is the F9 analogue of the essential-facility non-discrimination rule and cites it. Predicate: for any two sessions alike in RES-I1 class and network conditions, differing treatment attributable to source, destination, application, protocol, or content identity is a violation; any undocumented or asymmetric treatment not explained by a pre-published, identity-blind, capacity-driven COMMS-I21 policy is presumed a COMMS-I5 violation. Hook: append-only carriage-conduct log auditable at S4; a proven discrimination triggers user-first restitution under C1-I7 and the anti-abuse escalation.

**COMMS-I6 (technical-attack mitigation is the only carrier-side carve).** A carrier MAY mitigate a technical attack only where a DECIDABLE technical signature is present: a volumetric anomaly, a protocol malformation, or a published attack signature, applied application- and source-agnostically, with the burden of showing the signature on the carrier. Ordinary congestion control and RES-I1 class prioritization are network management governed by the COMMS-I21 conformance procedure, not a content or commercial preference. Predicate: mitigation is permitted only if (a) a decidable technical signature is shown, (b) it is the narrowest effective measure scored against the COMMS-I21 published policy, and (c) it does not single out a source, destination, application, or protocol by identity. Purpose: protect the honest operator's right to repel attacks while barring pretextual throttling. Hook: mitigation actions logged at S4; burden on the carrier.

**COMMS-I7 (congestion price inside the corridor, not discriminatory preferencing).** A carrier MAY apply a genuine, transparent congestion price that is uniform within an RES-I1 class and sits inside the C1-I7 corridor. It SHALL NOT sell priority that displaces a like session of equal class for payment, nor create a fast lane conditioned on a commercial relationship. Predicate: a price is a genuine congestion price only if it is offered on equal terms to all traffic of the same class and clears within the C1-I7 corridor; a price that buys precedence over equal-class traffic is paid-preferencing and is barred by COMMS-I5.

**COMMS-I8 (zero-rating that advantages an affiliate is caught).** Differential pricing or zero-rating that advantages a carrier affiliate, an acting-in-concert party (O1 graph), or a paying content source over a competitor SHALL be treated as paid-preferencing and is barred. Neutral, application-agnostic zero-rating tied to the RES-I1 class (for example zero-rated life_critical or essential-provision access offered to all comparable providers on equal terms) is permitted. Hook: the acting-in-concert graph and the veil-piercing rule (Organizations-Contracts-and-Employment.md) determine affiliation; the concentration ceiling (Property-Rent-and-Commons.md) applies across acting-in-concert structures.

**COMMS-I9 (transmission not content).** The carrier carries bits and SHALL NOT inspect, alter, re-order for commercial reasons, degrade, or claim ownership or authorship of the content carried. The content is N-cluster; its rights and obligations are owned by PRIV and Documents-Data-and-Software.md and are cited, not re-modeled here. Predicate: any carrier act that reads, rewrites, or asserts a right over payload beyond what carriage mechanically requires is a violation. Purpose: keep the carrier a conduit, not a publisher, editor, or censor.

**COMMS-I10 (no deep-packet inspection beyond carriage).** Inspection of traffic SHALL be limited to the header and signaling information carriage mechanically requires (routing, addressing, class, integrity, attack mitigation). Deep-packet inspection of payload beyond that need is barred. Per-session content or metadata access by the state needs consent or an A6 judicial contract per COMMS-I13. Predicate: any payload inspection lacking a mechanical carriage necessity or an A6 contract fails. Hook: S4 audit of inspection scope; metadata is protected on par with content (COMMS-I14).

**COMMS-I11 (addressing and numbering are a stewarded, portable namespace).** Address_blocks, numbers, and routing identifiers SHALL be a stewarded namespace held in the A12-mandated address and numbering register (an append-only R1/R2 holder of record), assigned under time-bounded, revocable grants, and portable by the subscriber on demand. They SHALL NOT be captured, resold as freehold, or used to lock a subscriber to a carrier. Predicate: a carrier that refuses, delays, or degrades number or address portability violates this invariant. Hook: A12 register mandate; portability latency benchmark inside the C1-I7 service corridor; the underlying namespace commons yields commons-owed rent, never carrier freehold.

**COMMS-I12 (mandatory non-discriminatory interconnection).** Carriers SHALL interconnect and peer with one another on non-discriminatory terms so the network of networks stays whole; a carrier SHALL NOT refuse, degrade, or paid-prefer interconnection to foreclose a rival or partition the network. Interconnection compensation, where owed, sits inside the C1-I7 corridor. Predicate: refusal or degradation of interconnection traceable to a competitive or content motive, rather than a stated technical or C1-I7 cost ground, is a violation. Both flanks: no carrier is forced to interconnect for free where genuine cost is shown, and no carrier may weaponize interconnection to break the commons of reachability.

**COMMS-I13 (interception, blocks, and shutdowns are per-subject, judicial, non-bulk, never on a public-health ground).** A communications shutdown, a lawful-intercept, a channel block, or per-subject traffic-data access is coercion and SHALL route through the A18-I4 coercion gate. A communications shutdown, block, or throttle SHALL route ONLY directly through A18-I4 with a named target and SHALL NOT be reached on a public-health ground: only a public-health MOVEMENT or gathering restriction routes through MEAS (Social-Provision-and-Public-Health.md), which itself cites A18-I4, and MEAS never reaches communications. A blanket or area-wide communications shutdown is barred outright per PRIV-I8, PRIV-I14, and FINT-I5, regardless of the stated ground. Per-subject access SHALL require the subject's consent or an A6 judicial contract of the full PRIV-I7 shape (per-subject, necessity-found, finite-expiry / time-bounded, loud-audited, renewal only on a fresh necessity finding), execute per-subject only, and be barred as bulk or mass collection. The COMMS-I3 life_critical call-for-help capacity SHALL persist through any such action; a genuine health or disaster priority uses only the narrow, loud, reviewed COMMS-I18 X3 path, which cannot cut the life_critical floor or suppress others' traffic. There is no standing surveillance or shutdown police power that bypasses A18-I4. Predicate: any interception, block, or shutdown lacking (a) A18-I4 clearance and (b) for per-subject access, consent or a PRIV-I7 contract, fails closed; any communications action asserted on a public-health ground fails at intake. Hook: A18-I34 suspensive contest; wrongful use is compensated.

**COMMS-I14 (metadata parity, owned by PRIV).** Session metadata (who, when, where, how long, to whom), location, and routing data are subject-owned, default-deny, and protected on par with content, because metadata reconstructs the communication (the reconstruction attack). This is the PRIV metadata-parity rule (PRIV is the F7/F9 home); F9 cites it and does not re-model it, and metadata is never gated on a lower bar than content. State access follows COMMS-I13. Section-local hook: DPI is limited by COMMS-I10; any metadata access is logged loud at S4.

**COMMS-I15 (carrier is not a censor and not an unpaid common carrier).** The carrier SHALL NOT be compelled to block, filter, or surveil traffic outside an A18-I4-cleared, A6-contracted order, and SHALL NOT be conscripted to carry traffic below the C1-I7 corridor return. A block of specified content SHALL rest on a specific A6 order naming the item and cleared through A18-I4, never a standing delegation to the carrier to police content. Both flanks: the public is protected from privatized censorship and the carrier is protected from being made an unpaid or liability-bearing content police.

**COMMS-I16 (carrier records follow the F3-I18 data-sovereignty pattern).** Records the carrier holds about carriage (subscriber records, session logs, routing records) SHALL follow the F3-I18 data-sovereignty pattern, with PRIV as the F7/F9 home; F9 cites F3-I18 and the PRIV data-sovereignty invariant and does not re-model them. Section-local: a per-subscriber read needs COMMS-I13 clearance (consent or a PRIV-I7 A6 contract); a network-scale traffic aggregate is an M3-I14 data commons per COMMS-I17, never carrier freehold; retention is bounded by COMMS-I20.

**COMMS-I17 (traffic aggregates are cohort-grain and, at scale, a commons).** Network management and state sensing on traffic SHALL operate on traffic_aggregate objects at S5 cohort or corridor grain (the state sees cohorts, not persons) per the PRIV cohort-only sensing rule. A network-scale traffic_aggregate crossing the M3-I14 scale threshold SHALL be stewarded as a data commons and SHALL NOT be held as carrier freehold or sold as a private asset. Predicate: any per-subscriber sensing without COMMS-I13 clearance, or any private enclosure of a threshold-crossing aggregate, fails. Hook: S5 k-anonymity check (PRIV sparse-cohort fail-closed); M3-I14 commons stewardship.

**COMMS-I18 (emergency carriage powers are narrow, loud, and reviewed).** An X3 emergency carriage action (priority pre-emption for disaster response, a narrowly-scoped restoration measure) SHALL be narrow and loud, reviewed after the fact, and compensated when wrongly used (Security-Ownership-and-Access.md). It SHALL NOT become a standing shutdown or surveillance power, SHALL NOT suspend the COMMS-I3 life_critical floor, and SHALL NOT suppress others' traffic. Predicate: an X3 action outliving its stated trigger, or broadening beyond its scope, converts to a COMMS-I13 coercion question under A18-I4.

**COMMS-I19 (session class is attested, not self-asserted).** A session's RES-I1 class SHALL be set by an attested, auditable rule against the RES-I1 signatures, not by the traffic originator's unverified assertion and not by carrier discretion. The life_critical class is reserved to genuine emergency-origination and RES-I1 life_critical signatures. A mis-declared class, a sender inflating class to win the COMMS-I5 no-throttle guarantee or jump the COMMS-I7 congestion queue, or a carrier classing to preference an affiliate or degrade a rival, is V4 anti-value booked on the V4 axis (never netted against any $ recovery on the money rail) and, for the carrier, an essential-facility abuse under COMMS-I4. A flood or spam sender's inflated class is NOT sheltered by the COMMS-I5 no-throttle guarantee. Predicate: every class assignment carries an attestation basis recorded in the S4 carriage-conduct log; a class lacking an attested basis is treated as standard, and an inflated class books as V4 anti-value. Hook: S4 carriage-conduct log; RES regime signatures; anti-abuse escalation for a carrier's discretionary classing.

**COMMS-I20 (carriage records are retention-bounded, never a standing track by accretion).** Session, signaling, and routing records SHALL exist only for carriage, $-rail billing, safety, interconnection, and lawful audit, retained at the minimum those purposes require and then extinguished or irreversibly aggregated to cohort grain on a bounded, published schedule; no purpose tag SHALL defer deletion past the published maximum-retention ceiling. Retention beyond that without the subject's consent or an A6 contract is void and is a PRIV-I8 bulk event. This invariant cites the PRIV-I16 retention duty and expiry and parallels MOVE-I15. Predicate: any record held past its purpose window or the ceiling without consent or an A6 contract fails and books as a PRIV-I8 event. Hook: S4 retention audit; PRIV-I16 expiry schedule.

**COMMS-I21 (network-management conformance procedure).** A carrier SHALL pre-publish its network-management policy, its triggers, RES-I1 classes, and actions, to the A12 register before applying it. A management action is "reasonable" only if it matches the published policy and applies identity-blind within an RES-I1 class; "narrowest effective" is scored against the published policy and the availability of a less-restrictive published alternative, with the burden on the carrier. Predicate: this test is fail-closed until A10 publishes the scored conformance test, so any undocumented or asymmetric treatment not explained by a pre-published, identity-blind, capacity-driven policy is presumed a COMMS-I5 violation. Hook: policy versions and management actions logged append-only at S4; A10 calibration of the scored conformance test.

**COMMS-I22 (communications floor requires an accessible endpoint).** The COMMS-I2 communications floor is delivered only if an accessible endpoint or terminal capable of the COMMS-I3 life_critical origination is available to the floor-holder, mirroring the MOVE-I8 accessible-reach rule. A connection with no usable device for a floor-holder counts as non-delivery and opens a COMP-I17 accessible-provision remedy on the person's own-account basis. Predicate: floor delivery validates only when both connectivity and a usable, life_critical-capable endpoint are present; endpoint accessibility for disabled users is a validation dimension of floor delivery. Hook: COMP-I17 standing reserve; floor-breach path (COMMS-I2).

### Protocols

- **Floor provisioning protocol.** COMP-I17 standing reserve provisions the COMMS-I2 connectivity floor on the person's own-account basis; CONN delivers the physical connection; floor delivery validates only against an accessible, life_critical-capable endpoint (COMMS-I22); the floor_carrier network_service reports delivery to the distributive-justice ledger. Non-delivery of connectivity or endpoint raises a floor-breach event and opens a COMP-I17 remedy.
- **Carriage-conduct audit protocol.** Carriers keep an append-only carriage-conduct log (session class treatment and its attestation basis, management actions against the published COMMS-I21 policy, congestion-price applications, interconnection decisions). Auditable at S4; discrimination findings (COMMS-I5, I7, I8, I12), a mis-declared class (COMMS-I19), or an off-policy management action (COMMS-I21) trigger C1-I7 user-first restitution, V4 anti-value booking, and anti-abuse escalation.
- **Network-management conformance protocol.** Before applying network management, the carrier registers its policy (triggers, RES-I1 classes, actions) to the A12 register; each action is scored against the published policy and the least-restrictive published alternative, burden on the carrier, fail-closed until A10 publishes the scored conformance test (COMMS-I21). Technical-attack mitigation must show a decidable signature (COMMS-I6).
- **Lawful-access protocol.** A per-subject interception or traffic-data request enters at A18-I4, requires an A6 contract of the full PRIV-I7 shape (per-subject, necessity-found, time-bounded, loud-audited, renewal only on a fresh finding) or subject consent, executes per-subject only, is loud-audited at S4, and is contestable under A18-I34. A communications shutdown, block, or throttle enters only directly through A18-I4 with a named target, never on a public-health ground; blanket or area-wide requests and bulk requests are rejected at intake (COMMS-I13, I14).
- **Retention protocol.** Each carriage record carries a purpose tag and a bounded expiry under the PRIV-I16 schedule and the published maximum-retention ceiling; on expiry it is extinguished or irreversibly aggregated to cohort grain; over-retention without consent or an A6 contract books as a PRIV-I8 bulk event (COMMS-I20).
- **Portability protocol.** A subscriber portability request updates the A12 numbering/address register (append-only), and both the losing and gaining carriers execute within the C1-I7 latency benchmark (COMMS-I11).
- **Interconnection dispute protocol.** An interconnection or peering refusal is testable against COMMS-I12: the refusing carrier bears the burden of a stated technical or C1-I7 cost ground; absent it, mandatory interconnection is ordered.

### Lifecycle and edge cases

- **Non-payment.** A subscriber in arrears MAY have standard and deferrable class service suspended per contract, but the COMMS-I3 life_critical call-for-help capacity and the COMMS-I2 delivered floor (including its accessible endpoint per COMMS-I22) SHALL persist. Contracts purporting to waive them are O5 floor-waiver-void.
- **Congestion.** Under congestion, prioritization SHALL follow RES-I1 class (life_critical first) set by the attested rule (COMMS-I19), match the published COMMS-I21 policy, be identity-blind within a class (COMMS-I6), and any price SHALL be a uniform congestion price inside C1-I7 (COMMS-I7), never a paid fast lane (COMMS-I5).
- **Class inflation.** A sender declaring a class it cannot attest, to escape throttling or jump the queue, is treated as standard and, if inflated, books as V4 anti-value; a spam or flood sender's inflated class is not sheltered by the no-throttle guarantee; a carrier classing to preference an affiliate or degrade a rival is both V4 anti-value and an essential-facility abuse (COMMS-I19, I4).
- **Attack traffic.** Mitigating hostile or malformed traffic is permitted only on a decidable technical signature (volumetric anomaly, protocol malformation, published attack signature), application- and source-agnostic, burden on the carrier (COMMS-I6); it does not breach COMMS-I5.
- **Public-health emergency.** A public-health measure may restrict MOVEMENT or gatherings through MEAS (which cites A18-I4), but SHALL NOT shut down, block, or throttle communications; a genuine disaster priority uses only the narrow, loud, reviewed COMMS-I18 X3 path, which cannot cut the life_critical floor or suppress others' traffic. A blanket or area-wide shutdown is barred outright (COMMS-I13; PRIV-I8, PRIV-I14, FINT-I5).
- **No usable endpoint.** A floor-holder with a live connection but no usable, life_critical-capable device, or a device inaccessible to a disabled user, is a COMMS-I22 non-delivery that opens an immediate COMP-I17 accessible-provision remedy.
- **Carrier of last resort / withdrawal.** An essential-facility carrier proposing to withdraw from a footprint triggers the CONN essential-communications-carrier continuity obligation and the C1-I16 harm-capacity escrow (Economy-and-Markets.md); the connectivity floor SHALL NOT lapse on withdrawal.
- **Autonomous operation.** An automated routing or management agent SHALL NOT leave a floor breach (COMMS-I2, I3, I22) or a criminal-class carriage harm without a natural-person bearer (Organizations-Contracts-and-Employment.md).
- **Content unlawfulness.** A claim that carried content is unlawful is an N-cluster / PRIV / A6 question, not a carrier determination; a block issues only on a specific A6 order naming the item, cleared through A18-I4 (COMMS-I5, I13, I15).

## SPECTRUM spectrum and the transmission commons (F9)

### Purpose

Radio spectrum is the shared physical medium over which the F9 communications flow rides. It is finite, non-manufacturable, and rivalrous only through interference, which makes it a textbook commons and a textbook target for enclosure. This section governs how a scarce band is granted to an operator for investable exclusive or shared use, how hoarding is prevented, how an open unlicensed floor is protected, and how interference is decided and remedied.

This section adds only the F9-specific spectrum content. The commons-and-rent doctrine, the "new land" doctrine, and the concentration ceiling are owned by Property-Rent-and-Commons.md (the RENT doctrine), computed across the O1 acting-in-concert graph and the one canonical veil-piercing rule, and are cited here, not restated. The commons-grant discipline (rent-bearing, time-bounded, revocable, never freehold, with a stated rent_basis and revocation trigger and a funded restoration or decommissioning bond on the $ rail) is owned by NET-I2 and P2-I7 and is cited, not restated. F9 is transmission, not content: it carries bits and never becomes the owner or inspector of the N-cluster content that rides on the band, and holding a band grant never confers freehold in the band. Both flanks are protected throughout: the public keeps an open commons and rent returns to it, and the honest licensed operator gets protected, investable use inside its grant, while a genuine narrow emergency is served and mass surveillance or a rights breach is never enabled.

### Object model

- `band`: a defined frequency range over a defined geography, with a service class (for example exclusive-licensed, shared-coordinated, unlicensed-commons), a technical mask (power, out-of-band emission limits, protection ratios), and a coordination reference (national plan entry, ITU footnote). A band is stewarded under the RENT commons doctrine, never owned in freehold. S1 attaches only to the per-band grant register record, never to the medium itself and never to a single controller of the medium.
- `spectrum_grant` (P2-I7 extraction-grant instance): a NET-I2/P2-I7 extraction grant authorizing a holder to transmit within a `band` and geography under stated conditions. Carries the P2-I7 required elements plus buildout and utilization obligations and the acting-in-concert linkage used for the concentration test. A grant is a use right, not title.
- `interference` (event object): an assertion or measurement that emissions from one emitter degrade a protected reception beyond the band's applicable registered reference (protection ratio in licensed bands, SPECTRUM-I17 etiquette mask in commons bands). Carries: complainant, alleged interferer, band, measured metric, protected service, and disposition (harmful / not harmful / shared-use-adjustment).
- `commons_floor_band`: a `band` in service class unlicensed-commons, reserved as an open floor, not eligible for exclusive auction to exhaustion, and carrying a registered SPECTRUM-I17 etiquette or coexistence mask as its interference baseline.

The `spectrum register` is the append-only holder of record for bands, grants, etiquette masks, and interference dispositions (A12-mandated, R1/R2).

### Invariants

SPECTRUM-I1 (spectrum is stewarded under the RENT commons doctrine and NET-I2). Radio spectrum SHALL be held and stewarded as a commons per the RENT doctrine and NET-I2 (cited, not restated here), yielding commons-owed rent and never enclosed into freehold. S1 SHALL attach only to the per-band grant register record; it SHALL NOT be read as a re-definition of the commons, as title in the medium, or as a single controller of the medium. The steward holds no control of transmission that bypasses the SPECTRUM-I16 shutdown bar and A18-I4. Purpose: keep the medium a public commons so that access to F9, and through it access to everything F9 enables, is never privately owned at the root and never a back door to silence transmission. Predicate: any instrument purporting to convey a `band` as freehold or perpetual private right, or to vest control of the medium in a single controller, is void on its face. Enforcement hook: the register refuses to record a freehold, perpetual, or single-controller instrument; A6 voids on presentation.

SPECTRUM-I2 (licence is a NET-I2/P2-I7 extraction grant). A spectrum licence SHALL be issued only as a NET-I2/P2-I7 extraction grant; see P2-I7 for the required elements and the register conformance check. Purpose: give the honest operator a definite, investable, defensible use right while guaranteeing the band returns to the commons. Enforcement hook: register conformance check at issuance and renewal per P2-I7.

SPECTRUM-I3 (use-it-or-lose-it against squatting). A `spectrum_grant` SHALL carry a buildout and utilization obligation, and a holder that fails to meet it within the stated milestone SHALL have the unused portion (by geography or sub-band) reclaimed to the commons, without the reclamation being treated as a taking. Purpose: prevent a holder from acquiring a scarce band to warehouse it or to foreclose rivals rather than to serve. Both flanks: the milestone SHALL be objective and pre-stated, and a holder prevented from buildout by a documented external cause (permitting on the U3 physical network, force majeure) SHALL be granted a recorded, time-limited cure period, so that an honest slow build is not confused with squatting. Predicate: utilization below the milestone threshold at the milestone date, absent a recorded cure, triggers partial reclamation. Enforcement hook: register milestone check; reclaimed capacity re-enters the commons pool.

SPECTRUM-I4 (rent basis reflects scarcity and returns to the commons). The `rent_basis` of a `spectrum_grant` SHALL reflect the scarcity and opportunity value of the band and geography, and the collected rent is commons-owed and SHALL return to the commons per the RENT doctrine, not to a carrier freehold. Purpose: price the exclusive use of a public medium so the public is the residual owner of its value. Note: this is rent for stewarded use, not a tax (MOS has no taxation) and not a price the state sets on the F9 service itself; the market clears the service on the $ rail and the rent prices the scarce-medium use. Enforcement hook: rent flows booked on the $ rail to the commons account; concentration and returns cross-checked against C1-I7 for essential-facility operators.

SPECTRUM-I5 (no permanent enclosure; band returns on expiry). On expiry, revocation, or non-renewal of a `spectrum_grant`, the band SHALL return to the commons for re-stewardship (re-grant, re-plan, or reassignment to a commons floor), and no incumbency SHALL harden into a de facto perpetual private hold. Purpose: keep every band periodically contestable. Both flanks: a good-faith incumbent SHALL have a fair, non-discriminatory renewal path and reasonable transition notice so that investment is not stranded, but renewal SHALL NOT be automatic where it would defeat contestability or the concentration ceiling. Enforcement hook: register expiry event; renewal decision recorded with reasons and tested against SPECTRUM-I6.

SPECTRUM-I6 (concentration ceiling). Spectrum holdings SHALL be tested against the RENT concentration ceiling, computed across the O1 acting-in-concert graph and the one canonical veil-piercing rule (cited, not restated here). Purpose: stop a hoarder from cornering the medium to foreclose rivals. Enforcement hook: pre-acquisition concentration test at the register; the GOV level-capture guard applies to prevent a captured allocator from waiving the ceiling.

SPECTRUM-I7 (hoarding and foreclosure established by objective triggers). A hoarding or foreclosure finding SHALL be established by an objective trigger, never by inferred motive alone: (a) failure of a SPECTRUM-I3 utilization milestone absent a recorded cure, (b) crossing the SPECTRUM-I6 concentration ceiling, or (c) refusal of reasonable shared or wholesale access by a holder in an essential-facility position. On a trigger, the holder SHALL book the foreclosure harm as V4 anti-value on the affected axis and SHALL be subject to the essential-facility anti-abuse rules and C1-I7 returns discipline. Motive aggravates the finding but is never the trigger. Both flanks: an operator legitimately using or building out its full grant under SPECTRUM-I3, and a holder below the ceiling that grants reasonable access, SHALL NOT be deemed a hoarder regardless of commercial intent. Enforcement hook: V4 booked per axis, never netted; the trigger is read from the register (milestone record, concentration test, access-refusal record); access remedy ordered under the essential-facility rule.

SPECTRUM-I8 (unlicensed commons-bands floor). A protected set of `commons_floor_band` SHALL be maintained as an open, permissionless, unlicensed spectrum floor, and this floor SHALL NOT be auctioned to exhaustion or reallocated below its floor magnitude to fund exclusive grants. Purpose: guarantee a permissionless-innovation and community-network substrate that no auction can close. Predicate: a reallocation that would take the commons floor below its stated magnitude is refused. Enforcement hook: the floor magnitude is registered and the allocator cannot record a reallocation breaching it; coordinates with Distributive-Justice-and-Capability-Floors.md for the floor magnitude discipline.

SPECTRUM-I9 (community-network and communications-floor carriage on the commons). The unlicensed commons floor SHALL be usable to deliver the COMP-I17 communications floor and community-operated networks, coordinating with the CONN essential-communications carrier and the CONN connection floor. Purpose: let the delivered-not-declared communications floor ride on open bands where the built-environment carrier is absent or insufficient, so the floor is a capability, not a promise. Enforcement hook: commons-floor bands carry no per-use licensing gate for floor-delivering community networks; F9 remains transmission and never inspects the content carried.

SPECTRUM-I17 (commons etiquette mask is the registered interference baseline). Every `commons_floor_band` and every unregistered or unlicensed band SHALL carry a registered default technical etiquette or coexistence mask (power ceiling, duty-cycle limit, listen-before-talk requirement, out-of-band emission limit) that defines lawful coexistence on the open floor. Purpose: give the permissionless commons a decidable interference baseline so shared use is neither a free-for-all nor a pretext for exclusion. Predicate: in a commons, unlicensed, or unregistered band the etiquette mask is the protection reference; in a licensed band the registered protection ratio is the reference. Enforcement hook: the etiquette mask is a registered field on the `commons_floor_band`; SPECTRUM-I10, I11, and I12 read it as the coexistence baseline.

SPECTRUM-I10 (decidable harmful-interference rule). "Harmful interference" SHALL be defined by a decidable predicate keyed to the band's registered reference: in a licensed band, emissions that push a protected reception below the band's registered protection ratio (or that breach the band's technical mask); in a commons, unlicensed, or unregistered band, emissions that breach the SPECTRUM-I17 registered etiquette or coexistence mask, measured at the protected receiver's location. Purpose: replace a vague nuisance standard with a measurable, adjudicable line so neither side wins by assertion. Predicate: measured metric worse than the applicable registered reference (protection ratio in licensed bands, etiquette mask in commons bands) equals harmful; otherwise not harmful. Enforcement hook: the band's protection ratio, technical mask, and etiquette mask are registered fields; an `interference` disposition cites the measurement and the reference applied.

SPECTRUM-I11 (the interferer books and remedies). An emitter found to cause harmful interference under SPECTRUM-I10 SHALL cease or correct the offending emission and SHALL book the interference harm as V4 on the affected axis and remedy it (retune, power-down, filter, or coordinate). In a commons band, the emitter that breaches the SPECTRUM-I17 etiquette mask is the interferer and bears the correction. Purpose: put the cost of interference on the party that causes it, protecting both the licensed and the unlicensed protected user. Enforcement hook: V4 booked per axis; A6 orders correction; the restoration bond (P2-I7) backs correction where the interferer defaults.

SPECTRUM-I12 (a spurious interference claim cannot foreclose shared use). An interference complaint SHALL carry the complainant's burden to demonstrate a breach of the applicable registered reference: the registered protection ratio in a licensed band, or the SPECTRUM-I17 etiquette mask in a commons or unregistered band. A claim that cannot show such a breach fails the SPECTRUM-I10 predicate and SHALL NOT restrict the accused emitter. A knowingly spurious or strategic interference claim used to foreclose legitimate shared or unlicensed use SHALL itself book V4 against the claimant. Purpose: protect legitimate shared and permissionless use from being shut down by unfounded assertions of harm. Both flanks: this never weakens a genuine complaint that meets the predicate. Enforcement hook: complaint disposition records burden met or not and the reference applied; repeated failed strategic claims flagged to A6.

SPECTRUM-I13 (dynamic and shared spectrum access is coordinated, not enclosed). Where a band supports dynamic or shared access (coordination database, sensing, opportunistic secondary use, or coordinated primary-secondary sharing), the coordination mechanism SHALL be operated as a shared-use commons facility and SHALL NOT be used to re-enclose a shared band into de facto exclusivity for the coordinator or a favored holder. Purpose: capture the efficiency of sharing without letting the sharing infrastructure become a new chokepoint. Both flanks: a primary licensed incumbent retains its registered protection, and secondary users get non-discriminatory access to the coordination facility. Enforcement hook: the coordination facility is an essential-facility instance (non-discrimination and C1-I7 apply); its access records are S4-audited; movement or usage aggregates it produces at network scale are a data commons under M3-I14, never coordinator freehold.

SPECTRUM-I14 (spectrum stewarded as "new land"). Spectrum SHALL be tested against the RENT "new land" doctrine on the same footing as land, subsurface, compute, and data (cited, not restated here): commons-owed rent, time-bounded revocable grants, and the concentration ceiling, with no enclosure into freehold. Purpose: keep spectrum inside the single commons regime rather than carving a privileged exception for radio. Enforcement hook: any spectrum instrument is tested against the same RENT predicates as an extraction grant; F9 carrying transmission over the band never acquires the band nor the content it carries.

SPECTRUM-I15 (register of record). The spectrum register (bands, grants, etiquette masks, interference dispositions, commons-floor magnitudes) SHALL be an append-only holder of record per A12, with R1/R2 discipline: corrections are new entries, not overwrites, and the acting-in-concert linkage needed for SPECTRUM-I6 is a recorded field. Purpose: make allocation, concentration, and interference history auditable and non-repudiable. Enforcement hook: writes are append-only; concentration and floor checks read the register as the single source.

SPECTRUM-I16 (no transmission-suppression power hides in spectrum authority). Authority over the spectrum commons SHALL NOT be used as a back door to silence transmission. Any denial, suspension, or shutdown of a holder's or a person's transmission that functions as a movement, expression, or communications restriction is coercion and SHALL route through the A18-I4 coercion gate with A18-I34 suspensive contest, not through routine spectrum administration. Purpose: prevent spectrum stewardship from becoming a standing shutdown or censorship police power. Both flanks: a genuine, decidable interference correction under SPECTRUM-I10 and I11, and a narrow loud-and-reviewed emergency, remain available and are not a rights restriction when confined to correcting measured harm; anything broader is A18-I4-gated. A public-health restriction reaches movement or gathering only via MEAS and never reaches communications. Enforcement hook: a suppression order lacking an A18-I4 record or a SPECTRUM-I10 measurement is unenforceable and contestable.

### Protocols

Grant issuance. Steward defines or updates a `band` with service class, protection ratio or SPECTRUM-I17 etiquette mask, and technical mask, records it (SPECTRUM-I15). For an exclusive or shared grant, the allocator runs the concentration pre-test (SPECTRUM-I6), sets the NET-I2/P2-I7 grant (SPECTRUM-I2), attaches the buildout milestone (SPECTRUM-I3), records the grant. Rent flows to the commons account on the $ rail (SPECTRUM-I4).

Interference handling. A complainant files an `interference` event with a measurement against the band's applicable registered reference: the protection ratio in a licensed band, or the SPECTRUM-I17 etiquette mask in a commons or unregistered band (SPECTRUM-I10). If the predicate is met, the interferer corrects and books V4 (SPECTRUM-I11); if not met, no restriction attaches and a strategic false claim books V4 against the complainant (SPECTRUM-I12). Disposition recorded in the register.

Reclamation and expiry. At each buildout milestone the register tests utilization (SPECTRUM-I3); unused capacity absent a recorded cure re-enters the commons. At term end the band returns to the commons (SPECTRUM-I5) and any renewal is re-tested against the concentration ceiling (SPECTRUM-I6).

Hoarding finding. A hoarding or foreclosure finding is opened only on an objective SPECTRUM-I7 trigger read from the register (milestone failure absent cure, ceiling crossing, or essential-facility access refusal); on a trigger the holder books V4 per axis and the access remedy issues under the essential-facility rule.

### Lifecycle and edge cases

- Contiguous-band aggregation for a legitimate wideband service can be pro-competitive yet also foreclosing. Resolve by SPECTRUM-I6 (concentration test over the concert graph) plus SPECTRUM-I3 (must be built and used), with SPECTRUM-I7 opening a foreclosure finding only on an objective trigger, not by inferred intent alone.
- A commons-floor band under congestion pressure may tempt reallocation to exclusive use. SPECTRUM-I8 floors this: relieve congestion by technical means, by tightening the SPECTRUM-I17 etiquette mask, or by additional commons allocation, not by taking the floor below its magnitude.
- A commons emitter overpowering the shared floor is adjudicated against the registered SPECTRUM-I17 etiquette mask: an emitter breaching the mask is the interferer under SPECTRUM-I11, and a complaint that cannot show a mask breach fails under SPECTRUM-I12, so neither a free-for-all nor a pretextual shutdown wins.
- Emergency use of spectrum (disaster response, RES-I1 life_critical F9 to call for and coordinate help) is served by priority and coordinated sharing (SPECTRUM-I13), and any suppression of others' transmission to make room is a narrow, loud, reviewed emergency measure that still answers to SPECTRUM-I16 and A18-I4, never a standing power.
- A shutdown demanded during unrest or on public-health grounds is not a spectrum-administration act: it routes through A18-I4, and a public-health restriction reaches movement or gathering only via MEAS (which itself cites A18-I4) and never reaches communications (SPECTRUM-I16).
- Cross-border interference and harmonization conflicts are handled through the ITU interface below, with the domestic register remaining the holder of record.

## PRIV communications and mobility privacy (F7, F9)

### Purpose

This section models the privacy of communications (F9) and of movement (F7): the sharpest rights surface of the mobility and communications layer. Access to everything else rides on these enabling flows, so the freedom to communicate and to move, and the confidentiality of both, are non-priceable rights, not services the carrier or the state grants at discretion. This section is the single normative owner of the F7/F9 privacy rules: it fixes who owns communication content, communication metadata, and travel and location data; the parity between metadata and content; the cohort-only sensing discipline and its sparse-cohort fail-closed guard; the single per-person read gate; the canonical shape of the targeted judicial contract and its delayed-notice regime; the bar on bulk collection; the right to encrypt and the bar on systemic backdoors; the delivered anonymous and pseudonymous instruments for movement and speech; the narrow-and-loud emergency path; and the retention duty and ceiling. MOVE, TRANSIT, COMMS, and FINT cite the PRIV IDs and add only their section-local operational hook; each PRIV invariant is stated once here and is cleanly citable.

Both flanks are protected on every point. The speaker, the traveler, the dissident, the whistleblower, and the journalist SHALL be shielded from surveillance and de-anonymization; and a genuine serious crime SHALL remain reachable through a targeted, judicial, audited, time-bounded contract. Neither flank is served by collapsing into the other: mass collection does not make anyone safer, and unbreakable anonymity for a named suspect under judicial finding is not what these invariants defend.

This section does not re-model the data-sovereignty pattern (F3-I18), the S-cluster access and sensing discipline (S2/S3/S4/S5/S6/X3), the coercion gate (A18-I4), the spectrum commons grant (NET-I2, P2-I7), the essential-facility non-discrimination rule, or the movement-data-commons threshold (M3-I14). It cites them and adds only the F7/F9 privacy content.

### Object model

- **Communication record.** A transmission event on F9. Decomposes into **content** (the bits carried, which are N-cluster objects and never F9-owned) and **communication metadata** (who communicated with whom, when, for how long, from which endpoint, over which path, at what volume).
- **Movement record.** A trip or presence event on F7. Decomposes into travel data (origin, destination, route, leg, time) and **location data** (a subject's position or trajectory over time). A passenger is not a consignment; the movement record is about a person with dignity and freedom of movement, never a commodity.
- **Subject.** The natural person whom a communication or movement record is about. The subject is the S1 controller of the record under F3-I18.
- **Operator or carrier.** The F7 transit operator or F9 telecom carrier. Holds only its operational minimum: the data strictly required to deliver the flow, bill the metered service, and meet a safety or interconnection duty.
- **Access request.** A demand to read content, metadata, or location at per-person grain. Typed as: subject consent, targeted A6 judicial contract, or X3 emergency.
- **Anonymous access instrument.** A floor-level, non-identity-linked means of using the flow: an unlinkable fare instrument for movement and an anonymous connectivity or subscription option for communication, available without a standing identity demand.
- **Access ledger.** The append-only S4 record of every read of a communication or movement record, at any grain, and of every deferral of subject notice.

### Invariants

**PRIV-I1 (subject ownership of the three record classes).** Communication content, communication metadata, and travel and location data SHALL each be subject-owned records governed by the F3-I18 data-sovereignty pattern (cited, not restated), with PRIV as the F7/F9 home of the three record classes. Purpose: fix ownership at the subject, not the pipe. Predicate: for any read R of a record class in {content, metadata, location}, controller(record) == subject AND R carries an S2 basis in {consent, A6-contract, X3} AND R is entered in the access ledger. Hook: a read lacking an S2 basis or a ledger entry is void and is an enforcement event.

**PRIV-I2 (default-deny).** All three record classes SHALL default to deny for every reader other than the subject. Purpose: no ambient readability. Predicate: absent an explicit S2 basis naming the reader, the record is unreadable to that reader. Hook: default-open configuration is a floor breach at F9/F7 provisioning and blocks the operating grant.

**PRIV-I3 (operator operational minimum, and no purpose creep).** The carrier or operator SHALL hold only the data strictly necessary to deliver, meter, secure, and interconnect the flow, and SHALL NOT read, mine, sell, profile, or paid-preference on the content, metadata, or location it handles beyond that minimum. Purpose: the flow carries, it does not own or exploit. Predicate: for each field the operator retains, there exists a named delivery, billing, safety, or interconnection duty it serves, and the field is held at or aggregated to the S5 k-anonymity floor unless it carries a per-subject basis; fields without such a duty are barred. Hook: secondary use without a fresh S2 subject contract is void and is an essential-facility abuse event.

**PRIV-I4 (metadata is not less protected than content).** Communication metadata and location data SHALL receive the same protection gate as content, never a weaker one. Purpose: defeat the reconstruction attack, that who communicated with whom, when, from where, and how often reveals more than any single message, so a cheaper gate on metadata is a bulk-surveillance backdoor. Predicate: gate(metadata) == gate(content) == gate(location); no rule may grant metadata or location on a weaker basis than content. Hook: any programme or contract that reads metadata or location on a gate below the content gate is void.

**PRIV-I5 (state senses at cohort grain only, sparse-cohort fail-closed).** The state SHALL sense communications and movement only at S5 cohort grain, seeing corridors, cohorts, and flows, never persons, for all standing and administrative purposes. Purpose: the state plans reach and load without a population panopticon, and a sparse corridor cannot become a de-anonymization side door. Predicate: a state-facing F7/F9 sensing output is releasable iff realized cohort size >= k AND residual re-identification risk is below the published S5-calibration bound; any output failing either test SHALL be suppressed or coarsened before release and is never emitted, and until the k-threshold and the sparse-corridor bound are calibrated the output is withheld (fail-closed, mirroring the A18-I4 fail-closed posture). Hook: a sensing output that in fact resolves to a person is not a sensing output but a per-person read, void as a sensing feed and permitted only as an access request under PRIV-I6 (consent or an A6 contract meeting PRIV-I7); taken without that basis it is a coercion event under A18-I4.

**PRIV-I6 (per-person read gate: consent or targeted A6 contract).** A per-person read of content, metadata, or location SHALL require either the subject's consent or a targeted A6 judicial contract. Purpose: single, non-bypassable gate for reading a named person. Predicate: read at person grain is permitted iff basis in {subject_consent, A6_contract meeting PRIV-I7}. Hook: a per-person read on any other basis is void; obtaining or acting on it routes through A18-I4 and the non-priceable criminal class.

**PRIV-I7 (canonical targeted judicial contract shape: per-subject, necessity-found, loud-audit, time-bounded, never bulk).** An A6 interception or location-access contract SHALL name the specific subject or device, record a necessity and proportionality finding, be loud-audited in the access ledger, and carry a finite expiry with an enumerated scope; renewal SHALL require a fresh necessity finding. Purpose: one canonical break-glass shape for the mobility and communications surface, cited by MOVE-I14, TRANSIT-I11, and COMMS-I13 so no operational section is self-sufficient without the time bound and a standing or indefinitely-rolling contract cannot slip through. Predicate: contract is valid iff subject_or_device is named AND necessity_finding present AND expiry is finite AND scope enumerated AND ledger entry loud. Hook: an expired, unrenewed, or scope-exceeding read is void and reviewable under A18-I34 suspensive contest.

**PRIV-I8 (no bulk collection, no retain-everything).** A mass-collection, standing-interception, dragnet, or retain-everything programme over communications or movement SHALL be void, whether run by the state, the carrier, or a third party. Purpose: bulk is not a scaled-up targeted read, it is a different and prohibited thing. Predicate: any access whose subject set is not a finite enumerated list bound to necessity findings is bulk and is barred. Hook: standing collection cannot be authorized by any contract, budget line, or emergency; standing it up is a coercion event under A18-I4 and, if concealed, flips perpetually under the concealment class.

**PRIV-I9 (right to encrypt).** A person MAY encrypt their communications and their stored data end to end, and encryption itself SHALL NOT be a ground for suspicion, degraded service, or a movement or communications restriction. Purpose: confidentiality is a capability of the floor, not a privilege. Predicate: presence of strong encryption is never an input to a coercion, throttling, or access decision. Hook: penalizing or de-prioritizing encrypted traffic is a carriage non-discrimination breach and a floor breach.

**PRIV-I10 (no systemic backdoor, no key escrow, no client-side scanning).** A mandated backdoor, key-escrow scheme, or client-side-scanning demand that breaks or weakens encryption for everyone SHALL be a floor breach and a rights harm, and void. Purpose: a break that touches all subjects to reach one is the bulk-collection prohibition (PRIV-I8) in another form. Predicate: any measure whose effect degrades the cryptographic guarantee for subjects beyond the named target of a PRIV-I7 contract is barred. Hook: mandating it is a non-priceable criminal-class act; the honest carrier that refuses such a mandate is protected and incurs no liability for the refusal.

**PRIV-I11 (targeted compulsion reaches available plaintext, never a systemic break).** A PRIV-I7 contract MAY compel a named subject or the holder of a named device to produce available plaintext, a specific stored item, or lawful assistance within existing capability, and SHALL NOT compel the manufacture of a systemic decryption capability or a break affecting other subjects. Purpose: distinguish reaching one person's available data from breaking everyone's cryptography. Predicate: compelled scope is a subset of {available_plaintext, named_device_contents, assistance within current capability}; anything requiring a new systemic break is out of scope and void under PRIV-I10. Hook: over-broad compulsion is void and contestable under A18-I34.

**PRIV-I12 (anonymity and pseudonymity in movement and speech).** A natural person MAY move and MAY speak without presenting a standing identity, and MAY use a pseudonym, and no operator or state actor SHALL impose a general real-identity precondition for ordinary travel or ordinary communication. Purpose: the dissident, the whistleblower, and the ordinary traveler are protected, while a genuine crime is pursued by a named PRIV-I7 contract, not by abolishing anonymity for all. Predicate: identity MAY be requested only where a specific, published, proportionate duty requires it (for example a safety-critical carriage duty), never as a blanket precondition; the default is no identity demand. Hook: a standing real-identity mandate over a communications or transit surface is a floor breach and void; a targeted de-anonymization proceeds only under PRIV-I7.

**PRIV-I13 (de-anonymization is a per-person read).** Linking a pseudonymous or anonymous actor to a natural identity SHALL be treated as a per-person read under PRIV-I6 and gated by PRIV-I7. Purpose: close the side door where identity resolution is treated as cheaper than content. Predicate: an identity-resolution request is gated at content grain, never below. Hook: resolving an identity without consent or an A6 contract is void and a coercion event under A18-I4.

**PRIV-I14 (movement restriction, shutdown, and interception are coercion).** A travel ban, a movement restriction, a communications shutdown, and an interception SHALL route through the single coercion gate A18-I4, and the state SHALL hold no standing surveillance, shutdown, or travel-restriction police power that bypasses it. Purpose: no ambient power to stop a person moving or speaking. Predicate: each such act routes through the A18-I4 coercion gate (see A18-I4 for the max_rung function and the A10-calibration fail-closed default) and is contestable under A18-I34; a public-health restriction reaches movement or gathering only via MEAS, which cites A18-I4, and never reaches communications. Hook: an unrouted restriction or shutdown is void; a blanket or area-wide communications shutdown is a mass measure barred under PRIV-I8 and A18-I4.

**PRIV-I15 (emergency access is narrow, loud, reviewed, and compensated).** In a genuine life-critical emergency, an X3 access to a specific subject's location or communications MAY precede the A6 contract, and SHALL be narrow in scope, loud in the access ledger, reviewed after the fact, and compensated to the subject when wrongly used. Purpose: reach the person who called for help, or the person in imminent danger, without a standing power. Predicate: an X3 read is valid iff scoped to the named subject AND a life-critical trigger is recorded (consistent with F9's RES-I1 life_critical standing) AND post-hoc A6 review occurs within a bounded window. Hook: a failed review voids the access, triggers restitution, and, if the emergency was pretextual, routes through A18-I4; the honest responder acting in a real emergency is protected.

**PRIV-I16 (data minimization, retention duty, and maximum-retention ceiling).** Traffic, metadata, and location data SHALL be minimized at collection and time-limited in retention, held only as long as a named delivery, billing, safety, interconnection, or bounded lawful-audit duty requires, then deleted or irreversibly aggregated to cohort grain. This invariant owns the retention duty and expiry that MOVE-I15, COMMS, and TRANSIT cite. Purpose: unretained data cannot be dragneted; a short, bounded life is the structural defense. Predicate: each retained field carries a named duty and a finite published expiry, and no field is retained past a concrete maximum-retention ceiling regardless of purpose tag; on expiry the field is deleted or reduced below the k-anonymity threshold, so no accreting purpose tag defers deletion indefinitely. Hook: indefinite retention, retention absent a named duty, or retention past the ceiling is void and is a PRIV-I8 bulk event; a network-scale movement or traffic aggregate crossing the scale threshold is stewarded as a data commons under M3-I14, never held as carrier freehold.

**PRIV-I17 (subject access, correction, and portability).** A subject SHALL be able to read, correct, export, and revoke consent over their own communication and movement records held by an operator, without penalty to the flow. Purpose: ownership is exercisable, not nominal. Predicate: the operator answers a subject S1 request within a bounded window; revocation of a consent-based S2 read halts that read prospectively. Hook: obstructing subject access is an essential-facility abuse event; an A6-contract read is not haltable by subject revocation but remains bounded by its own expiry under PRIV-I7.

**PRIV-I18 (no floor-conditioning on surveillance consent or identity).** The delivered communications floor and mobility floor (COMP-I17 standing reserve, coordinating with CONN, PLAN settlement reach, NET universal reach, and O3) SHALL NOT be conditioned on the subject waiving content, metadata, or location privacy, or accepting a standing identity demand. Purpose: the floor cannot be turned into a lever that buys surveillance consent or forces identification. Predicate: floor delivery is independent of any privacy waiver and of any identity linkage; a waiver or identity demand bundled into floor access is void under O5 floor-waiver-void. Hook: conditioning the floor on a waiver or a standing identity is a floor breach and void.

**PRIV-I19 (loud-audit integrity and the reconstruction guard).** The access ledger SHALL be append-only, tamper-evident, and itself governed so that reading the ledger does not become a covert route to the metadata it protects. Purpose: the audit that protects privacy must not leak it. Predicate: ledger entries record basis, scope, reader, and any notice-deferral finding for every read; access to the ledger is itself an S2-gated, S4-logged read at content grain. Hook: a silent or editable ledger voids the reads it should have recorded and is a concealment-class event.

**PRIV-I20 (both-flanks non-degradation).** No invariant in this section SHALL be read to bar a properly targeted, necessity-found, time-bounded A6 contract against a named serious offender, and no invariant SHALL be read to license bulk collection, a systemic encryption break, a standing identity demand, or a blanket shutdown. Purpose: fix the construction rule so neither flank is sacrificed to the other. Predicate: for any proposed measure, it is valid only if it is simultaneously (a) reachable to a genuine crime through PRIV-I7 and (b) non-bulk, non-systemic, and non-blanket under PRIV-I8, PRIV-I10, PRIV-I12, and PRIV-I14. Hook: a measure that can only be justified by weakening one flank fails construction and is void.

**PRIV-I21 (delivered anonymous and pseudonymous access instruments).** An anonymous, non-identity-linked fare instrument (a cash-equivalent or unlinkable token) and an anonymous connectivity or subscription option SHALL be available at floor level and SHALL NOT be degraded, surcharged, or throttled relative to identity-linked use. Purpose: the right of PRIV-I12 is delivered as a usable instrument, not merely declared, so account-based use never becomes the sole practical path to move or communicate. Predicate: availability is a validation predicate on floor delivery, tied to PRIV-I18: an anonymous instrument exists at parity of price, priority, and service with identity-linked use, and the floor is deliverable through it alone. Hook: the absence of an anonymous instrument at parity, or any surcharge, throttle, or degradation of it, is a floor breach and void; forcing identity-linked use as the only practical path is a PRIV-I18 breach.

**PRIV-I22 (delayed-subject-notice regime).** A targeted read under PRIV-I7 SHALL be loud-audited to the access ledger and to the independent reviewer at execution, and subject notice MAY be deferred only by an explicit A6 finding, for a finite window renewable only on a fresh finding, after which subject notice is mandatory and A18-I34 contest opens. Purpose: reconcile immediate independent oversight with a bounded operational secrecy that cannot become permanent concealment. Predicate: at execution the read is discoverable to independent review immediately; subject notice is discoverable to the subject on expiry of the bounded deferral, never merely contemporaneously waived; each deferral and renewal carries a recorded A6 finding in the ledger. Hook: an interception whose subject notice is deferred past the maximum without a recorded renewal is void and a concealment-class event that flips perpetually while concealed.

### Protocols

**Per-person access protocol.** (1) Requester states target subject or device, record class, and purpose. (2) Basis check: subject consent present, else an A6 contract is sought. (3) A6 tests necessity and proportionality, names subject/device, sets finite expiry and enumerated scope (PRIV-I7). (4) Operator serves only the in-scope fields at operational minimum. (5) The read is written loud to the access ledger and disclosed to the independent reviewer at execution (PRIV-I19, PRIV-I22). (6) Subject notice issues at execution unless deferred by an explicit A6 finding for a finite renewable-only-on-fresh-finding window (PRIV-I22). (7) On expiry the authorization lapses and, where notice was deferred, subject notice becomes mandatory and A18-I34 contest opens; renewal requires a fresh finding.

**Emergency (X3) protocol.** (1) Life-critical trigger recorded. (2) Narrow scope to the named subject's location or communications. (3) Loud ledger entry at the moment of access. (4) Post-hoc A6 review within the bounded window. (5) On failed review: void, restitution, and A18-I4 routing if pretextual (PRIV-I15).

**Sparse-cohort release protocol.** A state-facing S5 sensing output is checked against realized cohort size >= k and residual re-identification risk below the published S5 bound; a failing output is suppressed or coarsened before release, and until the bounds are calibrated the output is withheld (PRIV-I5). An output that resolves to a person is treated as a per-person read requiring PRIV-I7.

**De-anonymization protocol.** Treated identically to a per-person content read (PRIV-I13): consent or an A6 contract, loud-audited, time-bounded, never a blanket lift of anonymity.

**Retention protocol.** Each field is tagged at collection with a named duty and a finite expiry bounded by the maximum-retention ceiling; a scheduled process deletes or aggregates-to-cohort expired fields; network-scale aggregates route to data-commons stewardship under M3-I14 (PRIV-I16).

### Lifecycle and edge cases

- **Subject deceased or incapacitated.** Controllership passes per the S-cluster succession rules; an emergency reach to an incapacitated person in danger runs under PRIV-I15, not as a standing power.
- **Cross-border carriage.** A leg leaving the domain does not strip subject ownership; the F3-I18 pattern is cited for the leg and custody chain, but the record remains subject-owned and default-deny (PRIV-I1, PRIV-I2). Foreign access demands are treated as third-party reads requiring a PRIV-I6 basis.
- **Shared or group communication.** Each participant is a subject over their own participation record; a read touching non-target participants exceeds scope and is barred under PRIV-I7 and PRIV-I4.
- **Anonymous instrument at a checkpoint.** An anonymous fare or connectivity instrument SHALL clear ordinary use without an identity demand (PRIV-I21); any move to link it to a natural identity is a per-person read under PRIV-I13 requiring PRIV-I7.
- **Operator breach or leak.** A leak of subject records is an essential-facility harm event routing to the C1-I16 harm-capacity escrow and user-first restitution; it does not extinguish subject ownership.
- **Automated or agent-run access.** No autonomous-agent path may execute a per-person read, a de-anonymization, or an emergency access without a natural-person bearer of the floor-breach or criminal-class risk; agent-run bulk analytics that re-identifies is a PRIV-I8 event.
- **Encryption the operator cannot read.** Where the operator holds only ciphertext, PRIV-I11 compels the named subject, not the manufacture of a break; the operator's inability to decrypt is not a liability (PRIV-I10).

## FINT mobility and communications flow integrity and capture surface

### Purpose

This section enumerates the attack surface specific to the F7 passenger-mobility and F9 communications flows and states the invariant that closes each attack, protecting both flanks: the traveler, speaker, and public on one side, and the honest transit operator, honest carrier, and the genuine narrow life-safety emergency on the other. Mobility and communications are enabling flows: a capture here forecloses access to everything downstream, so integrity here is load-bearing for the whole value substrate. FINT is the single owner of the integrity and capture framing for F7/F9: for each attack it states the detection signal plus the enforcement hook, and where the underlying rule is a privacy rule it cites the PRIV invariant it enforces rather than restating the privacy SHALL. PRIV is the single normative owner of the F7/F9 privacy rules (subject-owned content, metadata, and location per F3-I18; metadata parity; cohort-only sensing; the no-bulk void; the targeted A6-contract shape; the right to encrypt; retention limits). FINT does not re-model the coercion gate (A18-I4), the spectrum-commons grant (NET-I2, P2-I7), the essential-facility corridor (C1-I7), the F3-I18 logistics data-sovereignty pattern, the S-cluster sensing and break-glass discipline (S2, S5, X3), or the M3-I14 data-commons rule. It cites them and specifies the F7/F9 hooks. Every harm booked here is anti-value (V4) booked per axis, never netted across axes and never netted against the money ($) rail: a carrier's profit does not buy down a rights breach, and a shutdown's claimed public benefit does not net against the movement or expression harm it inflicts.

### Object model

- `restriction_event`: any state or operator act that reduces reachability or transmission: a movement restriction, checkpoint, travel ban, comms shutdown, throttle, block, takedown, interception, or access demand. Every one carries `{basis, scope, target_class, time_box, audit_ref, natural_person_bearer}`.
- `access_request`: a request to read per-subject travel/location data or communications content/metadata. Carries `{subject_ref, basis in {consent, A6_contract}, necessity_finding, audit_ref}` per the PRIV-I7 contract shape. Bulk or class-wide `subject_ref` is ill-formed and void per PRIV-I8.
- `aggregate`: a movement or traffic dataset. Below the M3-I14 scale threshold it is a subject-owned record under F3-I18 (home: PRIV); at or above it, it is a data commons, stewarded, never carrier or state freehold.
- `carriage_decision`: a per-flow routing/queuing/delivery decision by a carrier, with an attested non-discrimination attribute.
- `spectrum_grant`: a band licence held under the NET-I2 / P2-I7 stewardship grant (finite expiry, revocation trigger, rent_basis, restoration bond).
- `anti_value_booking`: a V4 entry, axis-tagged, tied to a `restriction_event` or `carriage_decision`, never netted against $ and never netted across axes.

### Invariants

#### Mass-surveillance creep

**FINT-I1 (collection-and-retention capture surface).** The purpose-binding, minimization, retention-limit, and cohort-grain sensing duties for transit-location data and communications metadata are owned by PRIV (cite PRIV-I1 for subject-ownership and metadata parity, PRIV-I5 for the cohort / k-anonymity sensing grain, PRIV-I16 for retention expiry); this invariant states only the F7/F9 capture surface that enforces them. Detection signal: retention beyond the declared purpose window, join keys that raise grain below the PRIV-I5 floor, or a collected field with no live purpose. Enforcement: excess collection or retention books V4 anti-value per axis on the privacy and freedom-of-movement/expression axes; the money saved or earned by holding the data does not net it down; a collection that in fact resolves to a person is a PRIV-I8 event.

**FINT-I2 (function-creep / repurposing capture surface).** The bar on reusing data collected for one declared purpose (fare settlement, congestion routing, network operation, billing) for a second purpose (policing, profiling, targeting, adjudication of a group) without a fresh, independently sufficient basis is enforced against the PRIV per-subject access rule (cite PRIV-I7). Aggregation of separately lawful purposes into a standing panopticon is the F7/F9 capture harm this invariant names and is not cured by the lawfulness of each part. Detection signal: a query or export whose consumer or purpose differs from the collection purpose tag. Enforcement: repurposing books V4 on the affected axis and, where it targets a protected class, escalates to the A18 non-priceable rights class with a natural-person bearer; a bulk repurposing is a PRIV-I8 event.

**FINT-I3 (per-subject access capture surface).** Per-subject travel, location, communications content, and communications metadata are subject-owned and default-deny, accessible only on the subject's consent or a targeted, time-bounded, loud-audited A6 judicial contract, with metadata gated no weaker than content and bulk / mass / class-wide collection void: this rule is owned by PRIV (cite PRIV-I1, PRIV-I7, PRIV-I8) and FINT states only the enforcing capture surface. Detection signal: an `access_request` with a non-singular or open subject set, or absent A6 `audit_ref`. Enforcement: bulk or class-wide access is a PRIV-I8 non-priceable A18 rights harm; a concealed bulk program carries the concealment-class perpetual flip with a natural-person bearer.

**FINT-I4 (network-scale aggregate is a data commons, never freehold).** A movement or traffic aggregate at or above the M3-I14 scale threshold SHALL be stewarded as a data commons: no carrier and no state organ holds it as freehold, its rent is commons-owed, and access to it is at the PRIV-I5 cohort grain for all readers including the state. A carrier's operational need for its own network telemetry is honored at operational grain and time-box and at the S5 k-anonymity floor, and does not convert the aggregate into carrier property. Detection signal: an aggregate asserted as a carrier asset, sold, or read at sub-cohort grain by the state without an A6 contract. Enforcement: enclosure of the commons books V4 and voids the freehold claim; cite M3-I14.

#### Communications shutdown / internet kill-switch

**FINT-I5 (a communications shutdown or throttle is coercion and a rights harm).** Shutting down, blocking, or throttling communications SHALL route only directly through the A18-I4 coercion gate with a named target and a named natural-person bearer for the order. There is no public-health, health-emergency, or disaster ground for a communications shutdown: a public-health restriction reaches movement or gathering only, never communications (see FINT-I8 and MEAS). A blanket or area-wide communications shutdown is barred outright regardless of the stated ground (cite PRIV-I8, PRIV-I14; this invariant). Suppressing dissent, an election, a protest, or a group is a non-priceable A18 rights harm; a pretextual or fabricated justification (claimed security or safety with no genuine, contemporaneous, life-safety basis) triggers the concealment-class perpetual flip. The RES-I1 life_critical call-for-help capacity is never within what any shutdown, block, or throttle may cut. Detection signal: any area-wide or blanket F9 restriction; a `restriction_event` on F9 whose time-box coincides with a civic event, whose target_class maps to a political or demographic group, or whose stated basis is not evidenced at the time of the act; any comms restriction asserting a public-health ground. Enforcement: booked V4 on the freedom-of-expression axis, never netted against any claimed public-order or public-health benefit on another axis or against the $ rail.

**FINT-I6 (genuine narrow X3 comms action, both flanks).** A carrier or authority MAY take a narrow, life-safety, time-boxed communications action (for example isolating a compromised segment during an active attack) only as an S-cluster X3 emergency: narrow in scope, loud in audit, reviewed after the fact, and compensated when wrongly used. A genuine health or disaster priority uses this X3 path and nothing broader; it cannot cut the life_critical floor or suppress others' lawful traffic. The RES-I1 life_critical call-for-help capacity SHALL remain available throughout: it is never part of what an X3 action or any shutdown may cut. Detection signal: an X3 comms action without a post-hoc review record, exceeding its time-box, or that severed life_critical capacity. Enforcement: an X3 action that fails narrowness, loudness, or preservation of life_critical capacity loses its X3 shelter and is adjudicated as FINT-I5; a genuine, logged, time-boxed, life-safety action is protected from being recharacterized as suppression.

**FINT-I7 (throttling and degradation count as shutdown).** Deliberate degradation, rate-limiting, or selective slowing that functionally suppresses a flow SHALL be treated as a partial shutdown under FINT-I5, not as an operational choice, when it is targeted by content, sender, group, or civic timing. Detection signal: asymmetric degradation not explained by a neutral, published, capacity-driven policy. Enforcement: as FINT-I5. The honest flank: genuine, neutral, published congestion management that applies without regard to content or identity (FINT-I10) is not a shutdown.

#### Movement restriction as coercion

**FINT-I8 (movement restriction routes through the A18-I4 coercion gate).** An internal passport, travel ban, cordon, or checkpoint is coercion and SHALL route through the A18-I4 coercion gate (see A18-I4 for the max_rung function and the A10-calibration fail-closed default). Where the restriction targets a protected group or is used to punish, exclude, or suppress, it is an A18 non-priceable rights harm with a natural-person bearer. A public-health restriction reaches movement or gathering only and routes through MEAS, which itself cites A18-I4; it never reaches communications (FINT-I5). There is no standing movement-police power that bypasses A18-I4. Detection signal: a `restriction_event` on F7 whose target_class is a protected group, or absent an A18-I4 disposition. Enforcement: booked V4 on the freedom-of-movement axis per axis; the mobility floor's reachability of essential services, work, care, and accessible transit is never the thing restricted.

**FINT-I9 (honest operational restriction preserved).** A genuine, neutral operational or safety restriction (maintenance closure, capacity limit, a real and present hazard, a lawful accident cordon) is not coercion when it is non-targeting, published, minimized in scope and time, and contestable under A18-I34 suspensive contest. Detection signal: a restriction that is content- or identity-neutral, evidenced, and time-boxed. Enforcement: such a restriction is protected from over-broad suspicion; it converts to a FINT-I8 harm only if evidence shows targeting or pretext. This protects the honest operator from having every operational decision treated as a rights breach.

#### Carrier capture and net-neutrality

**FINT-I10 (carriage non-discrimination).** A carrier SHALL carry bits without blocking, throttling, or paid-preferencing on the basis of content, sender, receiver, application, or affiliation. This carriage non-discrimination is the F9 analogue of the essential-facility non-discrimination rule and cites it; returns to the carrier are governed by the C1-I7 attested-cost corridor. Detection signal: a `carriage_decision` correlated with a payment, an affiliate, or a rival's identity rather than a neutral, published traffic policy. Enforcement: a neutrality breach is essential-facility abuse, booked V4; C1-I7 corridor and user-first restitution apply. Both flanks: reasonable, published, identity-neutral network management (security filtering of attack traffic, congestion handling) is permitted and is protected from being mislabeled discrimination.

**FINT-I11 (no self-preferencing or rival degradation).** A carrier or transit operator that also supplies a competing downstream service SHALL NOT self-preference its affiliate or degrade a rival's flow. Detection signal: measurable quality-of-service delta between an affiliate and an equivalent rival not explained by a neutral policy. Enforcement: essential-facility abuse under C1-I7 and the anti-abuse rules; booked V4, never netted against the affiliate's $ gain.

#### Spectrum capture

**FINT-I12 (use-it-or-lose-it, no warehousing).** A spectrum grant is a time-boxed, rent-bearing, revocable stewardship grant per NET-I2 and the P2-I7 extraction-grant discipline, never freehold. Hoarding, warehousing, or squatting on an unused or under-used band to foreclose rivals is a revocation trigger under that grant, and the concentration ceiling applies across acting-in-concert structures per the RENT doctrine. The unlicensed-commons floor (bands held open for permissionless use) SHALL be preserved and not enclosed. Detection signal: a granted band below a utilization threshold across its measurement window, or acquisitions that cross the concentration ceiling through a concert graph. Enforcement: revocation per the grant's own trigger plus V4 booking for the foreclosure; this section adds only the F9 use-it-or-lose-it and unlicensed-floor content and does not re-model the grant. Honest flank: a grantee genuinely building out on a published, evidenced rollout schedule is protected from premature revocation.

#### Deep-packet inspection and content-tampering

**FINT-I13 (carriage is transmission, not inspection or authorship).** A carrier SHALL NOT inspect, read, alter, inject into, or block the content it carries beyond what neutral routing and delivery require. F9 carries bits and never becomes the owner, author, or inspector of the content, which is N-cluster and subject-owned. Deep-packet inspection for surveillance, ad injection, content rewriting, or selective blocking is both an A18 rights harm and a net-neutrality breach. Detection signal: payload modification, injected content, TLS interception, or per-content routing not required by delivery. Enforcement: booked V4 on the privacy and expression axes (surveillance-grade inspection is a PRIV-I8 event); a concealed inspection program carries the concealment-class flip. Honest flank: metadata strictly necessary for routing, and neutral security filtering of malicious payloads under a published policy, are permitted.

#### Censorship and takedown

**FINT-I14 (lawful speech is not blockable; illegal content only via A6 order).** Blocking lawful speech is a rights breach. Genuinely illegal content SHALL be removed only through an A6 order with due process, narrow in scope, appealable, and transparently logged, never at carrier or state discretion and never by standing filter. A takedown SHALL name the specific item, the legal basis, and a natural-person bearer, and SHALL be contestable under A18-I34. Detection signal: a removal without an A6 `audit_ref`, a class-wide or keyword-based block, or a takedown with no appeal path. Enforcement: unlawful blocking books V4 on the expression axis; both flanks: a narrow, evidenced, appealable A6-ordered removal of genuinely illegal content is protected from being called censorship, and a lawful speaker is protected from discretionary blocking.

#### Encryption backdoor and client-side scanning

**FINT-I15 (encryption-backdoor capture surface).** The right to encrypt and the voidness of any systemic encryption break, key-escrow mandate, or population-scale client-side scanning are owned by PRIV (cite the PRIV right-to-encrypt invariant and PRIV-I8); per-subject access reaches only the subject's own endpoint under a PRIV-I7 / A6 targeted contract, never by compromising the cryptosystem for everyone. FINT states the F9 capture surface. Detection signal: any mandate whose effect is population-scale decryptability, or a scanning client shipped to all endpoints. Enforcement: void on its face as a PRIV-I8 mass-surveillance capability; this shields both the public's private communications and the honest carrier from conscription into building one.

#### Tracking and de-anonymization

**FINT-I16 (bar on cross-context identifier linkage).** Cross-context linkage of movement or speech identifiers (persistent identifiers, device fingerprinting, ticket-to-identity or session-to-identity keys) SHALL NOT occur absent per-context consent or a targeted A6 contract: this is a bar, not a default preference. Rotation or salting of such identifiers is the required technical floor. Any pipeline capable of cross-context re-identification is a PRIV-I8 event regardless of whether the linkage is realized or merely "default." The delivered anonymous or pseudonymous fare and connectivity instruments that make unlinked use practical are owned by PRIV and delivered by the MOVE/TRANSIT/COMMS floors (cite PRIV); FINT bars the linkage. Detection signal: a durable identifier joined across trips, sessions, or contexts to reconstruct a person's pattern; a fingerprinting pipeline; an identifier reused as a cross-context key. Enforcement: cross-context linkage books V4 on the privacy axis, is a PRIV-I8 event, and where used against a protected class escalates to the A18 rights class with a natural-person bearer. Honest flank: identity linkage strictly necessary and consented for a single named service the user chose is permitted only at that service's grain and time-box and never as a cross-context key.

#### Cross-cutting enforcement and honest-flank protection

**FINT-I17 (anti-value booking discipline).** Every harm in this section is booked as V4 anti-value per axis, never netted across axes and never netted against the $ rail. A carrier's or authority's revenue, cost saving, or claimed public benefit on one axis SHALL NOT buy down a mobility, expression, or privacy harm on another. The market clears on the $ rail; the state prices only the residual externality here and never the content, the speech, or the movement itself, which are non-priceable rights. Detection signal: any accounting that offsets a rights harm against a monetary or cross-axis gain. Enforcement: the offset is void; each axis is booked on its own.

**FINT-I18 (loud-audit register for every restriction).** Every `restriction_event`, `access_request`, takedown, interception, and X3 comms action SHALL be written to an append-only, A12-mandated register with its basis, scope, time-box, target_class, natural-person bearer, and `audit_ref`. The register entry SHALL be discoverable to independent review immediately at execution. Subject notice follows the PRIV delayed-notice regime (cite PRIV-I7 and the PRIV delayed-subject-notice rule): the subject SHALL be notified on expiry of the bounded, A6-found deferral window, not necessarily contemporaneously, after which A18-I34 contest opens. An interception whose subject notice is deferred past the maximum without a recorded fresh-finding renewal is void and a concealment-class event. Silent action with a downstream effect but no register entry is itself a concealment-class harm with the perpetual flip. Detection signal: an act with downstream effect but no register entry, an entry lacking a natural-person bearer, or a deferral past its window without a recorded renewal. Enforcement: the missing or falsified entry books V4 and flips concealment; a genuine, logged, narrow act with review-immediate discoverability and a lawful bounded deferral is thereby documented and defensible.

**FINT-I19 (no standing suspicion of the honest operator or carrier).** An honest operator, carrier, or grantee SHALL NOT be subjected to standing suspicion, blanket audit, or pre-emptive restriction absent an evidenced, particularized trigger meeting the A18-I4 threshold. Enforcement action requires a confidence class and evidence, is scoped to the finding, and is contestable under A18-I34. Detection signal: a restriction or audit with no particularized basis, or applied class-wide to all operators. Enforcement: over-broad suspicion is itself an abuse; this invariant shields the good-faith actor while leaving the evidenced case fully actionable.

**FINT-I20 (false-claim and extortion guard).** A fabricated safety, security, or illegality claim used to justify a shutdown, throttle, takedown, or movement restriction, or used to extort a carrier or operator (for example a threatened blackout unless a payment or preference is granted), is itself an anti-value booking on the affected axis and carries the concealment-class perpetual flip against the party that fabricated it. Detection signal: a claimed basis that is not evidenced at the time of the act, or a restriction demand coupled to a payment or preference ask. Enforcement: the fabricator bears the V4 booking and the concealment flip with a named natural-person bearer; the honestly-acting carrier or authority that declines to comply with an unlawful demand is protected. This closes the loop on both flanks: neither a pretextual state blackout nor a private extortion of a carrier is available, and neither the genuine narrow emergency nor the honest operator is exposed to a false accusation used to seize control of the flow.

### Protocols

- **Restriction disposition.** Any F7/F9 `restriction_event` runs: classify (movement, shutdown, throttle, interception, takedown, access); route through A18-I4 (or MEAS for a public-health movement or gathering restriction only, never communications); assign a natural-person bearer; set scope and time-box; verify RES-I1 life_critical capacity is preserved (F9); write the FINT-I18 register entry with immediate review-discoverability; open the A18-I34 contest window.
- **Per-subject access.** An `access_request` resolves only on consent or a validated A6 contract in the PRIV-I7 shape (necessity finding, per-subject scope, finite time bound, loud audit); anything class-wide is rejected as ill-formed and void under PRIV-I8; the read is logged loud with subject notice per the PRIV delayed-notice regime (FINT-I18).
- **Neutrality audit.** `carriage_decision` streams are tested for content/identity/affiliate correlation against the carrier's published, neutral policy; a delta not explained by that policy is escalated under FINT-I10/I11 to the C1-I7 corridor and anti-abuse process.
- **Spectrum utilization review.** Each `spectrum_grant` is measured against its utilization threshold and concert-graph concentration ceiling per the window in the NET-I2 / P2-I7 grant; a warehousing finding fires the grant's own revocation trigger plus a FINT-I12 booking.
- **Aggregate grain gate.** Any read of a movement/traffic `aggregate` at or above the M3-I14 threshold is served only at the PRIV-I5 cohort grain (all readers, state included); sub-cohort reads require a per-subject A6 contract.

### Lifecycle and edge cases

- **Genuine emergency vs pretext.** A narrow, contemporaneous, life-safety F9 action taken as X3 (FINT-I6) is sheltered while logged, time-boxed, and preserving life_critical capacity; the same act without those properties collapses into FINT-I5 with the concealment flip. The test is the contemporaneous evidence and the preserved call-for-help capacity, not the after-the-fact narrative.
- **Public-health scope.** A public-health priority reaches movement or gathering only via MEAS (FINT-I8); it is never a ground for a communications shutdown, block, or throttle (FINT-I5). A blanket or area-wide comms shutdown is void on its face regardless of the stated health, security, or disaster ground.
- **Congestion vs suppression.** Neutral, published, identity-blind congestion management (FINT-I10) is permitted; the same slowing targeted by content, group, or civic timing is a partial shutdown (FINT-I7).
- **Operational closure vs cordon-as-punishment.** A published, evidenced, time-boxed maintenance or hazard closure (FINT-I9) is protected; a checkpoint mapped to a protected group is FINT-I8 rights harm.
- **Necessary account linkage vs cross-context key.** Consented, single-named-service identity linkage at its own grain and time-box (FINT-I16 honest flank) is permitted; reusing any identifier as a cross-context key, or a pipeline capable of cross-context re-identification, is barred and is a PRIV-I8 event.
- **Own-network telemetry vs commons enclosure.** A carrier reading its own operational telemetry at operational grain, time-box, and the S5 k-anonymity floor (FINT-I4 flank) is fine; asserting the network-scale aggregate as property is enclosure.
- **Immediate review vs subject notice.** An interception is discoverable to independent review at execution; subject notice may be deferred only for the bounded, A6-found window and becomes mandatory on its expiry (FINT-I18). A deferral past the maximum without a recorded renewal is void and a concealment-class event.
- **Break-glass access wrongly used.** An X3 or emergency access later found unjustified is compensated per the S-cluster X3 discipline and booked V4; the wrongful-use finding does not retroactively legalize the data already held, which is purged under the PRIV-I16 retention duty enforced through FINT-I1.
