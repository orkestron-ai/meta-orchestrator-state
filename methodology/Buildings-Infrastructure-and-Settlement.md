## 0. How the built-environment layer works

This is the physical substrate every value flow runs through: the buildings people sleep in, the networks that carry water, heat, power and movement, and the settlements that arrange them. The layer's first commitment is that **the physical fact governs, and the floor is delivered not declared**: a structure that can collapse or burn is unsafe whatever its certificate says, an essential service is delivered only when it actually flows and not when it is merely reachable, and a person displaced by a condemnation or a rezoning is rehoused through the standing reserve before the order bites, never left with a floor that exists only on paper. The layer is deliberately the PHYSICAL and PLACE view: title, control, the housing floor, the monopoly-returns corridor, the harm-capacity escrow and the flows through the pipes are each owned by another spec and cited here, never re-modelled.

**Buildings and premises (BLD, U1 physical, U2).** The structure is a physical object distinct from its U1 title: it carries a structural, fire, egress and envelope life-safety floor that binds by the physical fact, pinned to the standard version in force at its last lawful acceptance so an honest owner is not condemned overnight by a number that changed under them, while a genuinely below-floor structure is condemned on an attested defect. Condition is an append-only record; the maintenance duty splits cleanly between the owner and the occupier; a decidable class schedule (not an undecidable label) sets which structures pre-fund the harm-capacity escrow; a disaster is handled by a transparent rapid-triage tier rather than by suspending the floor; and an ownerless in-service building is auto-stewarded by the state rather than left to rot.

**Infrastructure networks (NET, U3).** Roads, rail, pipes and grids are physical graphs run by an operator organization that is usually a natural monopoly and an essential facility: the layer supplies only the physical predicate and routes the returns to the C1-I7 attested-cost corridor and the access and anti-abuse tests to the economy and energy specs, never re-inventing them. Universal reach to a floor-holding settlement is delivered on a bounded lead-time, not satisfied by a perpetual plan; the operator's contest right never suspends physical delivery of the reach floor; and an orphaned in-service segment is auto-stewarded so the network never lands nowhere.

**Utility service points and connection (CONN, U5).** The connection is the point where a network meets a dwelling. The connection floor requires the essential minimum to actually flow, not merely to be connectable; the lifeline quanta are owned by named non-operator stewards and can never be set to zero; the disconnection floor binds from actual lawful occupation, not a registration artefact; the physical lifeline is never cut for a genuine-hardship household while a solvent won't-pay is not conscripted onto the commons; metering is the energy spec's sensing chain, cited not re-modelled; and a fabricated hardship claim is caught without ever cutting a real household mid-dispute.

**Construction, settlement and integrity (WORKS, PLAN, UINT).** A structure or network is born only through an independently certified acceptance, with the certifier rotated out of the developer's capture and the developer, builder and certifier standing as natural-person bearers for any defect. Planning is value-balancing and never a revenue tool: it weighs the housing floor, congestion, ecology and heritage per axis, is bound by the level-capture guard and the concert graph against insider rezoning, delivers the displaced occupant's floor in kind while routing owner value-compensation to the property and rent instruments, and screens irreversible heritage erasure. The integrity surface closes phantom structures, falsified safety certificates, essential-facility capture, disconnection-as-coercion, planning capture, and run-to-failure deferral, on both flanks and with the money rail and the anti-value rail kept strictly apart.

Three commitments recur, matching the rest of the model:

1. **The physical fact governs and the floor is delivered.** Safety binds by the state of the structure, service by what actually flows, reach by a connection actually built; every occupant-protective floor is delivered upfront through the COMP-I17 standing reserve, never declared and withheld.
2. **Reference, do not re-model.** Title and the housing floor are Property's, the monopoly-returns corridor and the harm-capacity escrow are the economy's, metering and the flows are the energy and flows spec's, the register mandate is A12's; each is cited by ID and the money rail is never netted against the anti-value rail.
3. **Both flanks, everywhere.** The occupant, the connected household, the displaced and the commons are protected against unsafe structures, disconnection, displacement and run-to-failure; and the honest builder, the operator earning a benchmark return, and the owner are protected against retroactive standards, arbitrary condemnation, below-cost expropriation, and sham safety, heritage or hardship claims.

The sections follow: BLD buildings, structures and premises; NET physical infrastructure networks; CONN utility service points, connection and metering; WORKS construction, permits and acceptance; PLAN settlement, urban form, planning and addressing; UINT built-environment integrity and capture.

## BLD buildings, structures and premises: the physical object and its safety (U1 physical aspect, U2)

### Purpose

This layer models the building, structure and its premises as PHYSICAL objects: mass, footprint, use-class, condition, and the life-safety it either delivers or denies to the people inside and next door. It is deliberately separated from the U1 PROPERTY object, which Property-and-Ownership.md owns at U1-I1..U1-I9 (single controller of a co-held structure, structure-earned / land-commons split, footprint exclusivity, encumbrance authority, capability-floor-never-collateral, enforcement discipline, concentration ceiling, housing/occupancy floor, co-owner floor override) together with P2 land and cadastre, the RENT overlay, and PROP-I5 succession. Those invariants are CITED here by ID and never restated.

The core commitment: a structure that can collapse, burn, or otherwise endanger its occupants or neighbours is a life-safety harm surface under A18 (Offense-and-Enforcement.md). The safety floor on a physical object binds by operation of the physical fact, not by declaration, is non-waivable, and is DELIVERED not merely written down. The controller who lets a structure decay below that floor books V4 anti-value and cannot offload the resulting collapse cost onto occupants, neighbours, or the commons.

Both flanks are load-bearing. The layer protects the occupant and the neighbour from an unsafe structure, and it protects the honest owner from arbitrary condemnation and from a fabricated safety-defect claim wielded to seize, extort, or evict. A regime that only caught the negligent builder, or only shielded the incumbent owner, would fail the polity. The floor is delivered upfront through the COMP-I17 standing reserve, never merely declared reachable: a displaced occupant is rehoused before a condemnation bites, an in-service structure whose controller vanishes is auto-stewarded by the state rather than left to rot, and a disaster window is handled by a transparent rapid-triage protocol rather than by suspending the floor.

### Object model

- structure: {structure_id; parcel_ref (P2, by reference, never embedded); footprint_geometry (single-sequenced, deferring to U1-I3 for cross-register exclusivity); use_class; controller_ref (S1, resolved via the U1 property object, not re-declared here); commission_marker; version_at_acceptance (the safety-standard version in force at last lawful WORKS acceptance or last mandated retrofit, per BLD-I3); lifecycle_state}.
- premises / spatial_unit (U2): {unit_id; parent_structure_ref; bounded_volume; use_class; occupancy_limit; egress_spec; embedded in the structure object when one controller holds both, else a referenced object under its own controller (architecture Rule 2)}.
- condition_record: an append-only series of X1 condition events (inspection, defect, decay, damage, repair, rapid-triage tag); the mutable current_condition is a non-authoritative FOLD over the appended entries (R1/R2), never itself the record of truth.
- safety_floor_state: {floor_class (structural, fire, egress, envelope); threshold_ref (published standard, at version_at_acceptance per BLD-I3); current_delivery (met / breached / contested); binds_by_fact = true}.
- harm_capacity_escrow_ref: for a structure in a BLD-I8 sizing-schedule class, the ex-ante escrow held under C1-I16 (by reference); a segregated, ring-fenced instrument standing outside the controller's insolvent estate.
- lifecycle_event: an X1 event appended by a K2 act for each transition (commissioned, occupied, altered, damaged, condemned, demolished), each also emitting an S4 audit event on the gate protocols.
- rapid_triage_tag: a witnessed rapid-assessment tag standing in for a full attested inspection during a declared incident (BLD-P6), later trued up append-only.
- condemnation_marker; safety_breach_marker; occupancy_marker (the U1-I8 occupancy_marker, surfaced by reference, never re-minted here).
- address_ref: the U7 address under the A12 address registrar, minted at WORKS acceptance and consumed here by reference (BLD-P1), never re-minted by BLD.

### Invariants

- BLD-I1 (structure-as-object is distinct from title-as-object): a structure SHALL be modeled as a physical object with its own identity, footprint, use-class and condition, distinct from the U1 property object that carries its title, control, encumbrance, concentration and housing floor. Control of the physical object resolves through the U1 single-controller rule (U1-I1); this layer SHALL NOT re-model control, transfer, encumbrance, or the housing floor. Purpose: safety, condition and lifecycle attach to the physical thing and persist across changes of title, so a transfer of ownership never resets the structure's safety history or clears its breach markers. Predicate: every structure_id resolves to exactly one current controller via U1-I1 and to at least one parcel_ref via P2. Enforcement: an orphan structure (no resolvable controller or no parcel_ref) fails closed at register append, except that an in-service structure whose controller has genuinely failed to resolve is auto-stewarded under BLD-I19 rather than failing its occupants.

- BLD-I2 (footprint and place by reference, exclusivity deferred): a structure's footprint SHALL bind to its parcel(s) by P2 reference and its address by U7 reference; footprint exclusivity across the parcel and building registers SHALL be resolved under U1-I3, not re-adjudicated here. Purpose: one physical space, one authoritative footprint sequence, so two structures cannot both claim the same volume and an honest builder's registered footprint is not silently overwritten. Enforcement: a footprint claim overlapping an already-sequenced footprint fails closed per U1-I3's cross-register single-sequence.

- BLD-I3 (structural-integrity and life-safety floor binds by physical fact, at the version in force): every occupied or occupiable structure SHALL deliver a life-safety floor across the structural, fire, egress and envelope classes, tested against the published threshold for its use-class AT THE STANDARD VERSION IN FORCE at its last lawful WORKS acceptance or last mandated retrofit (its version_at_acceptance), not the latest published number. The floor binds by operation of the physical fact of the structure and its occupation, is non-waivable by owner, occupant, contract, or agent, and is delivered not merely declared: a certificate, attestation, or waiver asserting safety that the physical object does not actually deliver is worth nothing against the fact. Immediate condemnation by physical fact (BLD-I10) is reserved for a structure below its version-at-acceptance floor; a standard that tightens AFTER acceptance reaches an existing compliant structure only through the bounded prospective retrofit of BLD-I20, never by instant condemnation. Purpose: the person asleep on the third floor cannot bargain away, and cannot be made to bargain away, the floor beneath them, and equally the honest owner who built to the then-current code is not condemned overnight by a number that changed under them. Predicate: safety_floor_state.current_delivery is measured against threshold_ref at version_at_acceptance by attested inspection, not inferred from paperwork. Enforcement: a breach of the version-at-acceptance floor is a life-safety harm surface under A18 (BLD-I4); a waiver naming the floor is void (aligned with O5 floor-waiver-void).

- BLD-I4 (unsafe structure is an A18 harm surface, concealment flips): a structural or fire-safety breach that can foreseeably kill or maim SHALL be booked as harm under A18; where the controller knowingly created it, knowingly occupied below it, or concealed it, it enters the A18 concealment class with the perpetual flip (concealing the defect is itself a violation and the clock never runs out on the concealed defect). Purpose: distinguish the honest owner surprised by a latent defect from the actor who papers over a known death trap. Predicate: knowledge or recklessness plus a foreseeable life-safety consequence. Enforcement: A18 booking with V4 anti-value per axis (never netted across axes, never netted against the $ rail); the suspensive-contest guarantee A18-I34 remains available to the accused controller.

- BLD-I5 (a natural person always bears the life-safety breach, thin bearer): a collapse or life-safety breach SHALL always resolve to a natural-person bearer. This invariant adds only the domain-specific harm (structural collapse, fire spread, floor breach) and CITES the O1 canonical veil-piercing and acting-in-concert rule together with the A18 no-bearerless-harm guarantee for the bearer-resolution mechanics, which it does not restate. Pending resolution the U1-I1 single controller of record holds the duty. Purpose: a collapse cannot be laundered through a shell, an agent, or an acting-in-concert structure into no one. Enforcement: per O1's guarantee a bearer always resolves; there is no steward-of-record fallback bearer category and none is created here.

- BLD-I6 (condition is an append-only record, current-state is a fold): the condition of a structure SHALL be held as an append-only series of X1 condition events; the current_condition SHALL be a non-authoritative FOLD over those entries and corrections SHALL be new events, never edits (R1/R2 semantics). Purpose: a decayed structure's history cannot be quietly rewritten to erase a known-defect trail, and an honest owner's completed repair is a permanent appended fact, not an owner's unverifiable assertion. Enforcement: any attempt to mutate a prior condition entry in place fails closed; a "clean" current-state contradicted by an appended open defect does not bind; a disaster-window rapid-triage tag (BLD-P6) is itself an appended fact that later full inspection trues up over, never overwrites.

- BLD-I7 (structural maintenance duty on the controller, decay books V4, cost cannot be offloaded): the U1-I1 controller of a structure SHALL maintain its STRUCTURAL, ENVELOPE and SHARED-ELEMENT condition to the safety threshold (BLD-I3). Allowing that condition to decay below threshold SHALL book V4 anti-value against the controller per affected axis (never netted across axes, never netted against the $ rail), and the controller SHALL NOT offload the resulting failure cost (collapse, fire spread, forced rehousing, restoration) onto occupants, neighbours, or the commons. Operational safety (rated occupancy, egress obstruction, occupier-induced hazard) is NOT this duty: it falls on the actual occupier or operator under BLD-I13. Each duty resolves to its own named natural-person bearer under the thin bearer rule (BLD-I5), so an owner/occupier split never leaves a breach unbooked or misattributed to the wrong flank. Purpose: deferred structural maintenance is a slow harm, not a private economy; the saving is not a real saving if the risk is dumped downstream. Cross-reference: which structures carry a harm-capacity escrow and the draw trigger are at BLD-I8; the escrow mechanics are C1-I16; the WORKS (construction) and UINT (upkeep and inspection) layers own the cadence and the works themselves. Enforcement: sustained decay below threshold triggers the UINT inspection escalation and, for an in-scope structure (BLD-I8), draws the C1-I16 escrow on a booked BLD safety breach.

- BLD-I8 (in-scope structures carry an ex-ante escrow, decidable class-based sizing schedule): a structure SHALL carry an ex-ante harm-capacity escrow under C1-I16 where it falls in a published, appealable sizing-schedule class: (a) rated occupancy above a published headcount, (b) structural adjacency such that a failure reaches a neighbouring occupied parcel, or (c) a listed stored-hazard inventory. The escrow is default-required for any in-scope structure, with the burden on the controller to attest OUT of scope; the sizing determination is contestable under A18-I34 BEFORE it binds the BLD-P1 occupancy gate. An escrow demand exceeding the schedule for the class is over-reach and void; arguing a genuinely multi-occupant or spread failure mode out of scope is itself a booked misrepresentation with a natural-person bearer (BLD-I5). This layer does not re-model the escrow: sizing, revaluation over life, solvency-independence and draw priority are owned by C1-I16 (cited); the escrow is a segregated, ring-fenced instrument standing OUTSIDE the controller's insolvent estate, and any residual lien or creditor ordering is U1-I4 (cited), not asserted here. A booked BLD safety breach is the draw trigger. Purpose: the money to make occupants and neighbours whole exists BEFORE the failure, keyed to a decidable and appealable class test rather than an undecidable "catastrophic" label; an honest controller who has funded the escrow and maintained the floor is shielded from personal ruin on a genuinely latent failure. Enforcement: an in-scope structure without a funded escrow fails the occupancy gate (BLD-P1). This resolves BLD-Q3.

- BLD-I9 (lifecycle transitions are K2 acts appending X1 events): the physical lifecycle commissioned -> occupied -> altered -> damaged -> condemned -> demolished SHALL be modeled as state transitions, each performed by a K2 act appending an X1 lifecycle_event with actor, time and evidence. Purpose: the physical history of the object is reconstructible and every consequential change has a named actor and a timestamp. Predicate: lifecycle_state only advances through an appended event; no state is inferred silently. Enforcement: an occupancy that is not backed by a commission-and-occupancy event (BLD-P1) surfaces a safety_breach_marker; whether that occupancy carries a housing floor is decided by the U1-I8 lawful-occupation test (BLD-I11, BLD-I21), not by the paperwork status of the occupancy path.

- BLD-I10 (condemnation discipline, both flanks): condemnation or a safety-driven occupancy prohibition SHALL issue only on an attested defect measured against the published threshold (at version_at_acceptance, BLD-I3) for the use-class, SHALL be appealable, and SHALL carry the A18-I34 suspensive-contest guarantee. Symmetrically, a false or reckless safety-defect claim used to seize, condemn, extort, evict, or depress the value of a structure is itself an A18 offense with a natural-person bearer. Purpose: protect the occupant and neighbour from a real death trap AND protect the honest owner from a fabricated defect wielded as a taking or a shakedown. Predicate: condemnation requires threshold_ref + attested measurement + named inspector; a claim failing attestation does not bind and, if knowingly false, books against the claimant. Enforcement: an unattested condemnation is void and reversible; a fabricated one is prosecuted under A18 and the wrongly-condemned owner is restituted (aligned with the user-first restitution posture of C1-I7).

- BLD-I11 (condemnation cannot displace below the housing floor, rehousing delivered): a condemnation or safety-driven occupancy prohibition that would displace a lawful occupant SHALL NOT extinguish the U1-I8 housing/occupancy floor; the occupancy_marker persists and the displaced occupant's alternative shelter SHALL be DELIVERED through the COMP-I17 standing reserve on the occupant's own-account basis (compute-and-route), citing U1-I8 for the entitlement. The condemnation SHALL NOT take physical effect until that delivery is booked. The condemning authority is the named actor triggering the reserve draw. Where the condemnation traces to the controller's decay (BLD-I7), the controller bears the rehousing cost and COMP-I17 is subrogated against it; where it traces to a genuinely latent or external cause, COMP-I17 delivers on the occupant's own account. The sole exception to delivery-before-effect is an immediate life-safety danger requiring emergency evacuation under the S-cluster X3 path, which carries the COMP-I17 delivery forward as a live first-charge obligation, never a discharge. Purpose: "the building is unsafe" SHALL NOT become a lever to strip the shelter floor, and the honest occupant is never turned onto the street by a condemnation. This layer cites U1-I8 and COMP-I17 and re-models neither; it binds the condemnation act to them. Enforcement: a condemnation that displaces without a booked rehousing delivery, absent X3 emergency, fails closed (aligned with PLAN-I9).

- BLD-I12 (premises and spatial units are U2 containers, embedded or referenced by Rule 2, duties follow control): premises within a structure SHALL be modeled as U2 spatial units, embedded in the structure object when one controller holds both and referenced as distinct objects under their own controller when ownership diverges (architecture Rule 2). Purpose: a single-owner building and a strata / multi-unit building are the same physical model at different embedding depths, and unit-level safety obligations attach to whoever actually controls the unit. Enforcement: a U2 unit with a controller distinct from its parent structure that is nonetheless embedded rather than referenced fails validation; unit-level OPERATIONAL safety duties (egress, occupancy limit, occupier-induced hazard per BLD-I13) resolve to the ACTUAL occupier or operator of the unit with its own named natural-person bearer (BLD-I5), while shared STRUCTURAL and ENVELOPE duties resolve to the structure's U1-I1 controller (BLD-I7), or to the U1-I1 owners-association controller for co-held structures.

- BLD-I13 (occupancy limits and egress are life-safety constraints, not administration): the occupancy_limit and egress_spec of a structure or unit SHALL be treated as binding life-safety constraints, not administrative formalities. Exceeding rated occupancy, or obstructing or removing rated egress, is a delivered breach of the safety floor (BLD-I3) and a harm surface under A18. This is the operational-safety duty and it falls on the actual occupier or operator (BLD-I12), not the structural controller. Purpose: the fire exit and the crowd limit are the difference between an event and a mass-casualty event; they are not paperwork to be waived for a busy night. Predicate: measured occupancy > occupancy_limit, or egress capacity < rated, at any moment of occupation. Enforcement: A18 booking with a natural-person bearer (BLD-I5); an operator who knowingly overfills or blocks egress enters the concealment class if it is hidden from inspection.

- BLD-I14 (change of use re-tests the safety floor before occupancy): a change of use-class SHALL re-test the structure against the safety, egress and occupancy thresholds of the NEW use-class before occupation under that class, as a K2 alteration event. Purpose: an office safely occupied by forty desk workers is not thereby safe as a two-hundred-person assembly hall or a residential floor; the floor is use-class-relative. Predicate: a use_class change with no appended new-class safety attestation blocks the occupancy transition. Enforcement: occupation under the new class without the re-test surfaces a safety_breach_marker (BLD-I9); conversely, a completed and attested re-test protects the honest converter from a later claim that the change itself was the defect.

- BLD-I15 (habitability floor for a dwelling, delivered not merely connectable): a structure or unit occupied as a dwelling SHALL deliver a habitability floor: structurally safe, weatherproof (envelope integrity against water, cold and heat to the habitability threshold), and with the essential CONN networks actually DELIVERED and flowing, not merely connectable. The authoritative carrier set is CONN-I4 (cross-referenced, not restated here) and BLD-I15 tracks it exactly: water, sanitation, energy, and basic communications. Purpose: shelter that is nominally "housing" but unsafe, uninhabitable, or nominally connectable while cold and dark does not satisfy the person's need; habitability is part of what the housing floor U1-I8 protects, and this layer supplies its physical content while citing U1-I8 for the entitlement. Delivery: a dwelling left in a connectable-but-not-connected state past the CONN/COMP-I17 delivery cap is a live floor breach; the interim essential minimum SHALL be delivered through the COMP-I17 standing reserve on the occupant's own-account basis, and an occupied floor-holding dwelling left below the delivered minimum past the cap routes to A18 with a natural-person bearer (BLD-I5). Coordination: the entitlement, non-collateralization and enforcement caps live in U1-I8 and are cited; the carrier list and delivery semantics live in CONN-I4/CONN-I6 and are cited, not re-modeled. Enforcement: a dwelling below the habitability floor books V4 against its controller (BLD-I7) and triggers the UINT remediation path; it does not, by itself, authorize eviction of the occupant (BLD-I11, BLD-I16).

- BLD-I16 (habitability both flanks: no decay, no fabricated-defect eviction): the habitability floor protects both sides. A controller who lets a dwelling fall below it books V4 and owes remediation and SHALL NOT convert the resulting uninhabitability into a lever to end a lawful occupancy. Equally, an occupant, agent, or rival SHALL NOT fabricate or manufacture a habitability defect to break a lawful tenancy, extract concessions, or seize the object. Purpose: neither slumlord decay nor tenant-side or raider-side fraud is allowed to weaponize habitability. Predicate: an asserted habitability defect SHALL be attested against the threshold (symmetry with BLD-I10); a knowingly false assertion books against its maker. Enforcement: genuine controller-side decay flows to V4 plus UINT remediation with the occupancy floor intact; a fabricated defect is an A18 offense with a natural-person bearer and restitution to the wronged party.

- BLD-I17 (neighbour and shared-structure externality): a structure that endangers a neighbouring parcel or occupant (collapse risk onto adjacent land, fire spread, a shared or party wall let to fail) SHALL book the harm across the parcel boundary to the neighbour, with footprint and boundary resolved via U1-I3 and P2 by reference. Purpose: the safety floor is owed outward, not only to one's own occupants; the honest neighbour who maintained their side is protected from the decayed structure next door, and the honest owner is protected from a neighbour who fabricates a spillover claim (symmetry with BLD-I10 / BLD-I16). Enforcement: cross-parcel V4 booking against the endangering structure's controller (BLD-I5 bearer); shared-element duties on a co-held structure resolve to the U1-I1 single controller of record (owners-association).

- BLD-I18 (mandated physical registers, surfaced to public faith by request): the building/improvement register of structures and the U7 address register are A12-mandated (cited, not declared here). BLD REQUESTS that a structure's subsisting safety_breach and condemnation markers be surfaced through the R1 public-faith minimum (R1-I28) so a good-faith acquirer, occupant, lender, or insurer takes with notice; the public-faith minimum and its scope are owned by R1-I28 and A12, which this layer cites rather than declaring surfacing on BLD authority. Judicial discovery of a structure's condition history for enforcement is post-judgment only (R1-I39). Purpose: the physical safety state of a building is discoverable by those who will rely on it, and an honest buyer is not ambushed by a concealed condemnation while an owner's private condition data is not exposed pre-judgment. Enforcement: a transfer or occupancy that would rely on a register lacking a subsisting safety_breach_marker does not defeat the underlying physical fact (the floor binds by fact, BLD-I3); an acquirer who relied in good faith on a clean public-faith projection is protected per R1-I28.

- BLD-I19 (steward of last resort for an ownerless in-service structure): the moment control of an IN-SERVICE structure fails to resolve to a natural person (the owner dies with no PROP-I5 successor, or abandons the structure), the state via the A12 registrar SHALL auto-assume stewardship, bearing the BLD-I7 maintenance duty, the delivered habitability floor of any occupants, and any V4 until control is re-assigned; the A12 registrar is the named actor that resolves succession or re-assignment. Where no market successor exists, the duty remains with the state steward rather than lapsing, so it never lands nowhere. Purpose: an occupied or occupiable structure never falls into a maintenance and safety vacuum because its bearer disappeared; the floor owed to its occupants and neighbours does not go unowned even in the no-buyer case. Enforcement: an in-service structure with no resolvable controller does not fail its occupants at register append (qualifying BLD-I1's fail-closed); it is auto-stewarded and its occupancy path stays open under the state steward, while an ownerless AND out-of-service structure routes instead to BLD-P5 end-of-life.

- BLD-I20 (tightened standards reach existing structures only by bounded prospective retrofit): where a published safety standard tightens after a structure's version_at_acceptance, an existing structure compliant at that version SHALL be brought to the new threshold only through a bounded, published, PROSPECTIVE retrofit process carrying a stated compliance horizon; where the retrofit destroys value, a COMP-I17/PLAN-style delivery to the owner SHALL accompany it (cite U1-I8 and PLAN-I9 by ID, not re-modeled). A uniform relaxation of the floor in incumbents' favour is barred (GOV level-capture guard). Purpose: occupant safety advances over time without ambushing the honest owner who built to the then-current code, and neither flank captures the versioning: incumbents cannot freeze an unsafe floor, and the state cannot weaponize a code revision as an instant taking. Predicate: a structure within its published compliance horizon is not in breach for the delta between its version_at_acceptance and the tightened standard; a structure below its own version_at_acceptance floor is in immediate breach (BLD-I3). Enforcement: condemnation for a tightened-standard delta before the compliance horizon expires is void (BLD-I10); condemnation for a below-version-at-acceptance floor is immediate and by physical fact. This resolves BLD-Q2.

- BLD-I21 (housing floor keys to the U1-I8 lawful-occupation test, not to the occupancy path): whether a given occupancy carries a housing floor SHALL be decided by Property's U1-I8 lawful-occupation test (cited), not by a home-grown lawful-versus-unlawful-occupation-path distinction in this layer. Occupation seized, staged, or collusively manufactured to fabricate a floor claim, and bad-faith occupation of a phantom structure or of another controller's structure, is an A18 harm with a natural-person bearer (BLD-I5) and carries NO housing floor. The genuine-resident SAFETY floor, owed to any person actually living there by physical fact (BLD-I3), is preserved regardless of the lawfulness of the occupation. Purpose: the shelter entitlement cannot be minted by a raider staging occupation, yet the person who is really asleep inside is never left in a burning building because their tenure is contested. Enforcement: the housing-floor determination routes to U1-I8; the safety floor and the BLD-I11 no-displacement-below-floor protection track actual residence, not tenure status.

### Protocols

- BLD-P1 (commissioning and standing-occupancy gate): (1) CONSUME the WORKS acceptance record as a precondition (WORKS-I3/I17); BLD does NOT independently mint the U7 address and does not re-perform birth acceptance, it tests standing and ongoing condition. (2) test the safety_floor_state against the version-in-force threshold_ref for the declared use-class (BLD-I3); an in-scope structure (BLD-I8) SHALL show a funded C1-I16 escrow whose sizing has cleared or exhausted its A18-I34 contest window. (3) verify DELIVERED CONN essential minimum for a dwelling (BLD-I15), not mere connectability. (4) on pass, append the occupancy event and consume by reference the U7 address minted at WORKS acceptance; on fail, occupancy is barred and a safety_breach_marker stands. (5) append an S4 audit event (actor, input evidence set, output decision, timestamp), distinct from the R1 append. The gate tests delivered safety, not paperwork (BLD-I3).

- BLD-P2 (condition assessment and maintenance cadence): (1) on the UINT inspection cadence, or on a damage trigger, append a condition event with attested measurement. (2) recompute the current_condition fold (BLD-I6). (3) where condition is below threshold, book V4 against the responsible bearer (structural to the U1-I1 controller per BLD-I7, operational to the occupier/operator per BLD-I13), open a UINT remediation obligation, and, for an in-scope structure (BLD-I8), arm the C1-I16 escrow draw on the booked breach. (4) a completed and attested repair is a new appended event that clears the open defect; it never edits the history.

- BLD-P3 (change-of-use gate): (1) append a use-class change intent as a K2 alteration event. (2) re-test the structure against the new class's safety, egress and occupancy thresholds (BLD-I14). (3) on pass, update use_class and occupancy_limit and re-run the CONN / habitability delivery checks if the new class is residential (BLD-I15); on fail, occupation under the new class is barred.

- BLD-P4 (condemnation with suspensive contest): (1) an inspector appends an attested defect measured against threshold_ref at version_at_acceptance (BLD-I10, BLD-I3). (2) issue the condemnation or occupancy prohibition with the A18-I34 suspensive-contest guarantee; the marker is surfaced to the R1 public-faith minimum by request (BLD-I18). (3) if the act would displace a lawful occupant, DELIVER the U1-I8 rehousing through the COMP-I17 reserve and book that delivery before the condemnation takes physical effect, except under an X3 emergency which carries the delivery forward as a live first-charge obligation (BLD-I11); where the defect traces to the controller's decay, COMP-I17 is subrogated against the controller (BLD-I7). (4) on a successful contest, reverse the condemnation and restitute the owner; if the original claim was knowingly false, book it against the claimant (BLD-I10). (5) append an S4 audit event (actor, input evidence set, output decision, timestamp), distinct from the R1 append.

- BLD-P5 (demolition and end-of-life): (1) append a condemned or end-of-life event with the controlling authority or owner as actor. (2) discharge or transfer any occupancy floor (BLD-I11) and clear or carry forward encumbrances via the U1 / PROP path (by reference, not re-modeled). (3) execute demolition as a K2 act; construction and demolition emissions book to F6 in Energy (by reference). (4) append the demolished event; retire the structure_id, preserving its append-only condition and lifecycle history and releasing the parcel_ref back to P2. (5) append an S4 audit event (actor, input evidence set, output decision, timestamp), distinct from the R1 append.

- BLD-P6 (disaster-scale rapid-triage protocol): (1) during a declared incident (S-cluster X3), a triaged rapid-assessment tag SHALL substitute for a full attested inspection where inspection capacity is overwhelmed. (2) occupancy and rehousing decisions run on the tag; a tag barring occupancy routes displaced occupants through the BLD-I11 rehousing delivery, which is preserved throughout the incident. (3) when inspection capacity returns, full attested condition and lifecycle records TRUE UP append-only over the tag per BLD-I6, never overwriting it; the tag remains in the record as the appended fact it was. (4) append an S4 audit event for the tag and again for the true-up. Purpose: safety triage does not stall for want of a full inspection in a mass-casualty or disaster window, and the shortcut is transparent and correctable rather than a hole in the record.

### Lifecycle and edge cases

- Latent defect on an honest owner: a genuinely latent structural defect surfacing after a maintained, attested history books to the failure mechanism and, for an in-scope structure, draws the C1-I16 escrow, but does not, absent knowledge or recklessness, enter the A18 concealment class (BLD-I4). The honest owner is shielded; the escrow is why the money exists.
- Occupied but never commissioned: a structure occupied without a BLD-P1 occupancy event surfaces a standing safety_breach_marker (BLD-I9); whether the occupant carries a housing floor is decided by the U1-I8 lawful-occupation test (BLD-I21), and the SAFETY floor owed to any person actually living there is real regardless of the illegality of the occupation path.
- Sham or raider occupation: occupation seized, staged, or collusively manufactured, and bad-faith occupation of a phantom or another controller's structure, is an A18 harm with a natural-person bearer and carries no housing floor (BLD-I21); the genuine-resident safety floor is still owed to anyone actually inside.
- Squatter-safety tension: adverse or informal occupation does not lower the safety floor owed to the occupant (the floor binds by physical fact, BLD-I3), and does not license the controller to render the structure dangerous to drive occupants out; a deliberately induced hazard is an A18 offense (BLD-I5, BLD-I13).
- Ownerless in-service structure: an occupied structure whose controller dies with no PROP-I5 successor or abandons it is auto-stewarded by the state via the A12 registrar (BLD-I19), which bears maintenance and V4 until re-assignment; the no-market-successor case leaves the duty with the state steward, never nowhere.
- Threshold versioning: when a published standard tightens, existing compliant structures are brought up only through the bounded prospective retrofit with a compliance horizon and, where value is destroyed, a COMP-I17/PLAN-style delivery (BLD-I20); only a structure below its own version_at_acceptance floor is condemned immediately.
- Heritage / immovable constraint: where a structure cannot be brought to threshold without destroying a protected physical fabric, the resolution routes through the PLAN layer's balancing, never by silently occupying below the life-safety floor; if the floor cannot be delivered, occupation is barred, not waived.
- Partial condemnation: a single unsafe U2 unit is condemned at unit granularity (BLD-I12) without condemning the whole structure, provided shared structural and egress elements still deliver the floor for the remaining occupied units; displaced unit occupants are rehoused per BLD-I11 before the unit prohibition bites.
- Disaster damage: a damage event (BLD-I9) that drops the structure below threshold triggers BLD-P4 assessment, or the BLD-P6 rapid-triage tag where inspection capacity is overwhelmed; emergency evacuation runs the S-cluster X3 path with the COMP-I17 rehousing delivery carried forward as a live first-charge obligation (BLD-I11).

## NET physical infrastructure networks: roads, rail, pipes, grids (U3)

### Purpose

This section models U3, the physical infrastructure network, as a durable engineered GRAPH laid over the land: nodes, segments, capacity, and condition. Roads, rail, pipelines, water and sewer mains, district heat, electrical distribution and transmission, and the physical communications plant (conduit, fiber, mast sites) are all instances of one object: a physical carrier whose value is SLOW value, booked as infrastructure-as-economic-capacity under kernel C4. U3 is the physical pipe. The flows that ride it are owned elsewhere: energy (F1), logistics (F3), passenger mobility (F7), communications and spectrum (F9). This layer governs the pipe, its operator, its capacity, its condition, and the terms on which dependent parties reach and use it. It does not re-model title, rent, the housing floor, the flows, the monopoly returns math, or the harm-capacity escrow; it cites those by ID.

Two flanks are protected throughout and neither yields to the other. The public and dependent operators are owed non-discriminatory access, transparent capacity, mandatory interconnection, a maintained and resilient network, and universal reach to floor-holding settlements. The honest operator is owed a benchmark-cleared return on prudent capacity, protection from expropriation, and a defensible, appealable process before any access or return order binds. A network that cannot be reached is a floor failure; a network that cannot earn is a network that will not be built or maintained. Both are failure modes and this section refuses to trade one for the other.

### Object model

- Network object U3. A physical carrier graph identified as an infrastructure class within the A12 building/improvement register (R1), with a single accountable steward-operator (see NET-I2), a network class (road, rail, pipe, water, sewer, heat, electric-distribution, electric-transmission, comms-physical), and a service territory expressed as a footprint over P2 cadastre.
- Node. A physical junction, terminal, interchange, substation, pumping or compressor station, exchange, or access point. Each node carries a location (P2), a class, a rated throughput, and a condition record.
- Segment. A physical edge between two nodes: a carriageway, track section, pipe run, main, cable, or duct. Each segment carries length, a rated capacity, a design standard, an as-built and as-maintained condition grade, and a criticality rank (how many downstream nodes lose service on its failure).
- Capacity. Per node and per segment: the rated sustainable throughput, the current committed allocation, the current metered utilization, and the reserved harm-capacity margin (see NET-I11). Capacity is a physical property of the asset, distinct from any commercial right to use it.
- Condition record. An append-only maintenance and inspection history per node and segment: inspections, defects, remediations, and a current condition grade as a non-authoritative FOLD over the appended entries (R1/R2 model).
- Connection point. The physical interface where a U1 structure, a settlement, or a peer network attaches to U3. A connection point is where U3 meets CONN (the connection layer) and where universal reach is measured.
- Interconnection. A physical and operational interface between two U3 networks of compatible or bridged class (a grid intertie, a gauge break with transhipment, a peering point, a road junction across operators).
- Access right. A commercial-technical entitlement of a dependent party (a shipper, a retailer, a downstream operator, a member of the public) to inject into or draw from the network on defined terms. Distinct from capacity: capacity is physical, an access right is contractual and governed here.
- Operator organization. The O1 firm that stewards the network, cited from Organizations-Contracts-and-Employment.md, never re-modeled.

### Invariants

NET-I1 Network as attested physical graph.
A U3 network SHALL be recorded as nodes and segments with attested capacity and condition, as an infrastructure class within the A12 building/improvement register (R1), cited not re-modeled. Purpose: capacity, criticality, and condition SHALL be legible before allocation, pricing of the residual externality, or resilience judgments are made. Predicate: every in-service segment resolves to two registered nodes, a rated capacity, and a current condition grade folded from appended condition entries; a segment carrying live flow with no registered capacity or condition record is a register defect. Enforcement hook: A12 owns the register mandate and flags unregistered live plant; an operator SHALL NOT book infrastructure-as-capacity value (C4) for plant it has not attested into the register.

NET-I2 Single accountable steward with a natural-person bearer.
Every in-service U3 network SHALL have exactly one accountable operator organization (O1) as its steward, and SHALL resolve, through the O1 acting-in-concert and veil-piercing graph (cited) under the A18 no-bearerless-harm rule (cited), to at least one natural-person bearer for any criminal-class harm or floor breach the network causes. The domain-specific harm this section attaches is network run-to-failure: the collapse, flood, rupture, electrocution, contamination, outage, or stranding a life-safety physical network can inflict (A18). Predicate: the steward field is non-null and single-valued and the bearer resolves under the cited O1 rule; a network operated by an entity with no resolvable person-bearer is non-conforming. Enforcement hook: A18 attaches the criminal-class or concealment-class harm to the resolved bearer; O5 floor-waiver-void voids any contract term purporting to strip the bearer.

NET-I3 Steward, not pure-exclusion owner.
The operator of a shared essential U3 network holds it as a STEWARD with duties, not as an owner with unconditional exclusion rights. Purpose: an essential physical network is a commons-facing facility; the right to exclude is bounded by the access, interconnection, condition, and reach duties in this section. Predicate: any exclusion of a party from access, interconnection, or reach SHALL cite a permitted ground in NET-I5 through NET-I15; a bare "because it is mine" refusal is void. Enforcement hook: S1 one-controller stewardship governs who acts for the network; a refusal without a cited permitted ground is an essential-facility abuse referral. Both flanks: this does not dilute the operator's title, control, or return, all of which remain owned by Property (U1-I1 single controller, U1-I4 encumbrance authority) and by the returns regime (NET-I7); it constrains only the exercise of exclusion against dependents.

NET-I4 Physical-versus-flow seam.
U3 governs the physical carrier and its capacity and condition; it SHALL NOT re-model, re-price, or re-allocate the flows that ride it. Purpose: keep one authoritative owner per concern. Energy flow F1, logistics F3, passenger mobility F7, and communications and spectrum F9 are owned by the flows spec; U3 owns the pipe. Predicate: a U3 rule that sets a flow's tariff, schedule, or product terms beyond physical capacity, condition, and non-discriminatory access is out of scope and defers to the F-owner. Enforcement hook: cross-references to F1/F3/F7/F9 resolve to the flows spec; the metering-IS-sensing and mastering chain (F1-I9, F1-I10) remains the flow-owner's, not U3's. Congestion governance seam: pricing of scarce physical pipe capacity is a U3 pipe-capacity-access matter under NET-I15; a congestion charge expressed as a flow-level tariff is an F-spec matter; one congestion event is priced under exactly one of the two, never both. Both flanks: dependent flow-parties get their flow rights adjudicated by the correct spec; the operator is not double-regulated for the same act under both layers.

NET-I5 Essential-facility non-discriminatory access.
Where a U3 network is an essential facility (a dependent party cannot economically replicate it and needs it to reach the public or a market), the operator SHALL grant access on non-discriminatory, transparent, cost-reflective terms. Purpose: the pipe's monopoly position SHALL NOT be levered into the competitive flow markets riding on it. Predicate: two similarly situated access-seekers receive materially equal published terms; any difference SHALL rest on an attested cost or capacity ground, not on the identity or rivalry of the seeker. Enforcement hook: the essential-facility anti-abuse rules of Economy-and-Markets.md and Energy-Logistics-and-Emissions.md apply; degradation or denial of access is caught there and this section supplies the physical predicate. Both flanks: access is owed to dependents AND the seeker pays cost-reflective terms so the honest operator is not forced to subsidize a rival off its own plant.

NET-I6 Self-preferencing barred.
A vertically integrated operator that both stewards U3 and competes in a flow that rides it SHALL NOT preference its own flow affiliate over a third party in access, capacity, scheduling, connection sequence, congestion treatment, or information timing. Purpose: prevent the essential-facility holder from foreclosing rivals through the pipe it controls. Predicate: affiliate and non-affiliate requests of equal priority class receive equal treatment on a documented queue; earlier or better terms to the affiliate without an attested cost or capacity basis is self-preferencing. Enforcement hook: functional separation of the access-granting function from the competing flow affiliate, with an S4 audit trail of allocation decisions; a proven self-preference is an anti-abuse violation and, where it denies a floor-reaching connection, a floor breach. Both flanks: integration is not prohibited and the affiliate may compete; only the leveraging of control over the pipe is barred.

NET-I7 Regulated returns by attested-cost corridor, no expropriation.
The operator's return on a natural-monopoly U3 network SHALL be set by the C1-I7 attested-cost corridor with benchmark-cleared returns and user-first restitution, not re-modeled here. Purpose: bind both flanks at once, no monopoly rent extracted from captive users, no expropriation of the honest builder. Predicate: allowed return references the published benchmark corridor on prudently incurred, attested capacity and condition spend; a return above the corridor without cost basis is over-recovery owed back to users first, and a forced return below prudent cost is expropriation and impermissible. Enforcement hook: the C1-I7 machinery governs; U3 supplies the attested capacity and condition cost base from the NET-I1 register. Zero analytic privilege: the operator's own cost model earns no deference beyond its attestation.

NET-I8 Transparent capacity and non-discriminatory allocation.
Rated capacity, committed allocation, and available headroom per node and segment SHALL be published to access-seekers on equal terms, and available capacity SHALL be allocated by a transparent, non-discriminatory, pre-published rule. Purpose: allocation is where discrimination hides; sunlight and a fixed rule are the remedy. Predicate: the allocation rule (priority classes, queue order, auction or pro-rata method) is published before allocation and applied as written; a discretionary override SHALL be logged with an attested ground. Enforcement hook: S4 audit of allocation events; an unexplained deviation from the published rule is an anti-abuse referral. Both flanks: seekers see real headroom AND the operator may run a defensible priority scheme (firm versus interruptible, safety-critical first) so long as it is published and applied evenly.

NET-I9 Unused committed capacity is a metered physical fact.
Committed capacity that a holder is not physically utilizing SHALL be metered and published as unused-but-committed against the NET-I8 transparent record. Purpose: the physical predicate for any foreclosure judgment is a measured fact, not a commercial inference. Predicate: firm capacity allocated to a holder and metered as unused across a defined window is recorded as unused-but-committed with its measured utilization; the record is the physical input, nothing more. Enforcement hook: whether unused-but-committed capacity is held to foreclose a rival, and any release remedy, is TESTED and REMEDIED under the essential-facility capacity-foreclosure rule of Economy-and-Markets.md and Energy-Logistics-and-Emissions.md, exactly as NET-I5 routes access abuse; this section supplies only the metered physical predicate and states no freestanding release remedy of its own. Both flanks: rivals get an honest capacity picture AND a genuine reserve or growth booking is not condemned by this section, its treatment being decided by the anti-abuse rule on the attested record.

NET-I10 Condition and maintenance duty.
The operator SHALL maintain nodes and segments to their design safety standard and keep the condition record current. Purpose: a physical carrier degrades; unmaintained plant becomes a life-safety harm surface (collapse, rupture, derailment, outage, contamination). Predicate: each in-service asset has an inspection within its class interval and no open safety-critical defect past its remediation deadline; a lapsed inspection or an overdue safety-critical defect is a condition breach. Enforcement hook: the physical-condition and maintenance obligation is enforceable against the NET-I2 bearer; an occupancy- or safety-relevant defect on plant serving a housing-floor connection escalates under A18. Cross-reference: the habitat/condition floor D4 (Missions-Science-and-Ecology.md) binds where the network touches a protected habitat.

NET-I11 Resilience regime, reserved harm-capacity, and operational-integration admission.
An essential U3 network SHALL be planned and operated to the Energy RES resilience regime, holding a reserved margin against foreseeable single-point and correlated failures proportional to the criticality of the affected nodes. Purpose: an essential network run without margin externalizes the cost of its own failure onto captive dependents. Predicate: for each safety-critical or floor-serving node, a documented contingency (redundant path, reserve capacity, or restoration plan) exists and is tested on its class interval; a critical node with no tested contingency is a resilience breach. Reserved harm-capacity: the financial counterpart is the C1-I16 harm-capacity escrow, sized by the decidable, published, class-based, appealable schedule (rated throughput or served headcount above a published band, structural or service adjacency such that a segment failure reaches an occupied parcel or floor-serving node, or a listed stored-hazard inventory), default-required for any in-scope segment with the burden on the controller to attest OUT of scope, the determination contestable under A18-I34 before it binds; C1-I16 owns sizing, revaluation, solvency-independence, and draw priority (cited, not re-modeled), and the operator SHALL fund it ex ante and SHALL NOT raid it to inflate near-term return. Operational-integration admission (seam with WORKS): WORKS certifies each segment's own design-standard and as-built condition (WORKS-I3), but a certified segment SHALL NOT join the live network until NET clears an operational-integration check under the RES regime that the addition does not degrade system resilience below standard; system-resilience admission is owned by NET, not by a WORKS birth-acceptance, so neither layer double-gates nor leaves resilience uncertified. Enforcement hook: RES governs the standard; the escrow is enforced under C1-I16; a segment admitted without the integration check, or a critical node left without a tested contingency, is a resilience breach against the NET-I2 bearer. Both flanks: dependents get a resilient network AND prudent resilience spend enters the C1-I7 attested cost base and earns its benchmark return.

NET-I12 Running-to-failure books anti-value and cannot externalize collapse cost.
Deferring maintenance or resilience below the standard to harvest short-term return SHALL book anti-value per axis (V4, booked with teeth, never netted against the money rail), and the operator SHALL NOT externalize the resulting collapse, outage, or contamination cost onto users, neighbors, or the commons. Purpose: close the loop where a monopoly monetizes decay and hands the public the wreck. Predicate: a condition or resilience breach (NET-I10, NET-I11) coincident with a return at or above corridor is a run-to-failure signal; the harm cost is charged to the operator, funded first from the C1-I16 escrow. Enforcement hook: V4 anti-value booked per axis; user-first restitution under C1-I7; where the failure was foreseeable and the decay concealed, A18 concealment-class attaches with its perpetual flip and the clock never runs out on the concealed defect. Both flanks: the public is made whole AND an operator that maintained to standard and still suffered a genuine act-of-nature failure books no anti-value for prudent operation.

NET-I13 Mandatory non-discriminatory interconnection.
Operators of compatible or bridgeable essential U3 networks SHALL interconnect on request at technically feasible points, on non-discriminatory and cost-reflective terms. Purpose: a fragmented set of monopoly islands strands dependents at the seams; the value of a network is in its reach and its joins. Predicate: an interconnection request at a technically feasible point is granted, or refused only on an attested technical-infeasibility or safety ground; refusal to protect the incumbent's flow affiliate is barred. Enforcement hook: the requesting operator pays cost-reflective interconnection cost into the incumbent's NET-I7 cost base; unreasonable refusal is an essential-facility abuse referral. Both flanks: peers and their dependents get through-service AND the incumbent recovers the attested cost of the join and is not forced into an unsafe or infeasible connection.

NET-I14 Universal reach to floor-holding settlements, delivered not declared.
An essential U3 network SHALL extend to reach every floor-holding settlement within its service territory, and the reach SHALL be delivered upfront through the COMP-I17 standing reserve on the settlement's own-account basis, not merely declared as a right. Purpose: a settlement that lawfully holds the housing/occupancy floor (U1-I8) but cannot be reached by essential water, power, road, or communications is a floor failure in physical form, and floors are delivered not declared.
Planned versus delivered: a merely planned connection satisfies reach ONLY within a bounded, published maximum lead-time measured from the settlement's registered occupancy_marker (U1-I8) or from its admission to the service territory, whichever is later; the placeholder maximum lead-time is 24 months pending per-class calibration with CONN and PLAN. Past that lead-time an unreached floor-holding settlement is a standing reach breach that books V4 anti-value per axis against the operator with a natural-person bearer (NET-I2), and COMP-I17 delivers interim essential service (for example tankered water or a temporary supply) until the permanent connection is built.
Composed obligation (seam with CONN): the network-extension reach up to the connection point (this section) and the premise connection from the connection point to the meter (CONN) are two distinct legs of ONE reach-and-connection obligation; each leg is delivered by exactly one COMP-I17 draw, neither leg duplicated nor gapped, and the total reach floor is the composed NET leg plus CONN leg. Funding follows NET-I19: the reach-extension asset COMP-I17 funds SHALL NOT also be recovered from the general ratebase.
Predicate: each settlement with a registered occupancy_marker has a delivered connection point, or a planned one within the lead-time, or interim COMP-I17 service where the lead-time has passed; a floor-holding settlement with none of these is a reach breach coordinated to CONN and PLAN for remedy. Enforcement hook: reach is delivered through the COMP-I17 standing reserve, never by commanding or suppressing a market price and never as MOS taxation; a lapse past lead-time books V4 and triggers interim delivery. Both flanks: floor-holders are reached and delivered upfront AND the operator is funded for the reach it delivers, through the single COMP-I17 draw at benchmark return, never bearing an uncompensated mandate.

NET-I15 Congestion state is a metered physical fact; congestion pricing is not freestanding.
Where a segment or node is congested, the operator SHALL publish the congestion state, and the published state SHALL match the metered physical utilization within tolerance. Purpose: congestion is a real physical fact and a legitimate allocation signal; the published state is a measured input, not a lever. Predicate: the published congestion state matches metered physical utilization within tolerance; a congestion signal not backed by metered utilization, or a maintenance withdrawal timed to create scarcity, is the physical predicate for a manufactured-scarcity judgment. Enforcement hook: whether a congestion signal is manufactured scarcity, and its remedy, is TESTED and REMEDIED under the manufactured-scarcity limb of the Economy-and-Markets.md and Energy essential-facility anti-abuse rule, exactly as NET-I5 routes access abuse; this section supplies only the metered predicate and states no freestanding scarcity remedy of its own. Congestion pricing: any congestion price the operator charges SHALL be booked inside the C1-I7 attested-cost corridor with over-recovery restituted user-first, OR routed to the flows spec as a flow tariff (per the NET-I4 seam); it SHALL NOT stand freestanding above the corridor. The operator's $-rail congestion price is separate from the state's residual-externality (Pigou) price; the state prices only the residual externality and SHALL NOT price the operator's congestion price itself. Both flanks: users get honest congestion signals and are not double-charged AND the operator may run genuine congestion pricing or curtailment where the physical state warrants, recovering it through exactly one correct channel.

NET-I16 Access and return orders are appealable before they bind; floor delivery is not suspendable.
No access grant, capacity release, interconnection order, condition directive, or return determination against an operator SHALL bind before the operator has had a suspensive contest, and no denial of access, interconnection, or reach against a dependent SHALL stand without an appealable, reasoned ground. Purpose: protect both flanks procedurally so that neither expropriation nor foreclosure can be imposed by fiat. Predicate: every binding order under NET-I5 through NET-I15 carries a reasoned basis and a contest window; the A18-I34 suspensive-contest guarantee attaches to enforcement that would strip capacity, return, or reach from the operator. Floor carve-out (mirroring CONN-I6): the operator's suspensive contest attaches to the money and to any capacity or return stripping against the operator, but SHALL NOT suspend physical delivery of a NET-I14 reach floor or a CONN-I15 reconnection to a floor-holding settlement or household; the operator litigates cost recovery inside the C1-I7 corridor after delivery, never by withholding delivery. Enforcement hook: A18-I34 governs the suspensive contest; R1-I39 confines any post-order asset discovery to the post-judgment stage. Both flanks: an operator cannot be summarily expropriated AND a dependent cannot be summarily foreclosed or left without a floor while the operator litigates.

NET-I17 Level-capture guard on network thresholds.
A single steward or regulator SHALL NOT uniformly relax the access, interconnection, capacity-release, condition, resilience, or reach thresholds of this section in incumbents' favor across the board. Purpose: prevent regulatory capture from hollowing out every duty at once while each relaxation looks individually reasonable. Predicate: a proposed simultaneous loosening of multiple NET thresholds that all benefit incumbents triggers the GOV level-capture guard (Organizations-Contracts-and-Employment.md). Enforcement hook: the GOV guard blocks the uniform relaxation and forces per-threshold, reasoned justification. Both flanks: incumbents may still win a specific, justified threshold change AND dependents are protected from a wholesale quiet rollback.

NET-I18 Construction and demolition emissions book to F6.
The lifecycle emissions of building, extending, maintaining, and decommissioning a U3 network SHALL be booked to the Energy F6 emissions lifecycle, not re-modeled here. Purpose: one authoritative owner for emissions accounting across the built environment. Predicate: a network capacity project carries an F6 lifecycle emissions entry for its construction and, at end of life, its demolition; U3 supplies the physical scope, F6 owns the booking. Enforcement hook: F6 governs; where an irreversible ecological effect is in prospect, the irreversibility precaution (Missions-Science-and-Ecology.md) binds before build. Both flanks: the commons gets full lifecycle accounting AND the operator books it once, in the correct spec, without duplicate charge.

NET-I19 One reach asset, one payer: no double recovery.
A reach-extension or connection asset that the COMP-I17 standing reserve funds to deliver a floor SHALL have exactly one payer and SHALL NOT be recovered a second time. Purpose: the floor is delivered upfront by COMP-I17 and the operator is made whole once; charging the same capex again to the general ratebase would double-recover a monopoly asset from captive users. Predicate: for each reach asset, either COMP-I17 funds it (floor delivery) or the general ratebase funds it, not both; the operator earns its C1-I7 corridor return on that asset on the single funded basis and SHALL NOT book a second recovery of the same capex from the general ratebase. Enforcement hook: over-recovery of a COMP-I17-funded reach asset from the ratebase is corridor over-recovery restituted user-first under C1-I7; the funding split is cross-referenced in NET-I14 and CONN-I5. Both flanks: the honest operator recovers its prudent reach cost once at benchmark return AND captive users are not charged twice for the same reach asset.

NET-I20 Steward of last resort for an orphaned in-service segment.
When control over an in-service U3 segment fails to resolve (the operator dissolves with no PROP-I5 successor, or abandons the plant), the state via the A12 registrar SHALL auto-assume stewardship of the segment the moment control fails to resolve, so the duty never lands nowhere. Purpose: an orphaned live segment still carries flow and still endangers dependents; the steward duty (NET-I2, NET-I3) does not lapse with the firm. Predicate: an in-service segment with no resolvable steward is auto-assigned to the state steward of last resort, which bears the maintenance and condition duty (NET-I10) and any V4 arising until the segment is re-assigned; the no-market-successor case is covered because the state holds the segment until, or in the continued absence of, a successor. Enforcement hook: the A12 registrar names the actor that resolves succession and holds the interim duty; re-assignment discharges the state only when a conforming successor steward with a resolvable NET-I2 bearer is in place. Both flanks: dependents are never stranded on an unstewarded live segment AND a solvent third party is not conscripted, the state carrying the duty until a market or assigned successor takes it.

NET-I21 Cross-border plant resolves to one register and one steward per segment.
Each physical segment and each U7 address SHALL resolve to exactly one register of record and exactly one accountable steward-operator, even where the network spans a jurisdictional boundary. Purpose: a segment or address that answers to two registers or two stewards at a seam is a bearerless gap in physical form. Predicate: no in-service segment and no U7 address is recorded in more than one register of record or assigned more than one accountable steward; a seam is split at the boundary into per-side segments, each with its own single register of record and steward, joined by an interconnection (NET-I13). Coordination rule at the seam: the C1-I16 harm-capacity escrow for the shared interconnection is funded and held by the steward of the side on which the reserved margin physically sits, apportioned to matched physical exposure; each side's C1-I7 corridor recovers only the cost of the plant it stewards, and neither side double-recovers the joint asset (NET-I19). Enforcement hook: A12 owns the register of record on each side and reconciles the seam; an unassigned or double-assigned seam segment or address is a register defect. Both flanks: dependents across the boundary get a single accountable steward per segment AND neither operator bears or recovers the other's plant.

### Protocols

- Network registration and attestation. On commissioning plant, the operator appends nodes, segments, rated capacity, and initial condition to the A12 building/improvement register as an infrastructure class (NET-I1), resolves the single steward and its natural-person bearer (NET-I2), and only then books infrastructure-as-capacity value (C4).
- Segment admission. A new segment carries the WORKS design-standard and as-built certification (WORKS-I3) as a precondition, and NET runs the operational-integration check under the RES regime before the certified segment joins the live network (NET-I11); a segment that fails the integration check is not admitted, though its own certification stands.
- Access request. A dependent submits an access request against a published connection point. The operator returns published, cost-reflective terms (NET-I5), checks capacity headroom against the transparent record (NET-I8), and either grants or refuses with a cited permitted ground (NET-I3). Refusals are appealable (NET-I16).
- Capacity allocation and release. Available capacity is allocated by the pre-published rule (NET-I8). Firm capacity metered as unused-but-committed is published against the transparent record; whether it must be released, and on what terms, is decided by the essential-facility anti-abuse rule on that metered predicate (NET-I9). Congestion states are published as they arise and matched to metered utilization (NET-I15).
- Interconnection. A peer operator requests interconnection at a feasible point; the incumbent grants on non-discriminatory cost-reflective terms or refuses only on attested technical or safety grounds (NET-I13), appealable under NET-I16.
- Maintenance and inspection cycle. Each asset is inspected on its class interval; defects are appended to the condition record with remediation deadlines; safety-critical defects escalate (NET-I10). Resilience contingencies are tested on their class interval (NET-I11).
- Universal reach delivery. Floor-holding settlements with occupancy_markers are matched to connection points. A planned connection satisfies reach only within the published maximum lead-time (NET-I14); past the lead-time, V4 books against the operator's bearer and COMP-I17 delivers interim essential service until permanent connection. Each of the NET reach leg and the CONN premise leg is delivered by exactly one COMP-I17 draw, and the reach-extension asset COMP-I17 funds is not double-recovered from the ratebase (NET-I19).
- Steward-of-last-resort assignment. Where control over an in-service segment fails to resolve, the A12 registrar auto-assigns the state steward of last resort, which bears maintenance and any V4 until a conforming successor is in place (NET-I20).
- Return determination. Prudent attested capacity and condition spend from the register feeds the C1-I7 corridor; over-recovery is restituted user-first; the C1-I16 escrow is funded ex ante and audited against raids (NET-I7, NET-I11, NET-I12); COMP-I17-funded reach assets are not double-recovered (NET-I19).

### Lifecycle and edge cases

- Greenfield build. New plant enters the register on commissioning; WORKS certifies the segment and NET clears the operational-integration check before admission (NET-I11); F6 books construction emissions; the irreversibility precaution binds where an ecological effect is irreversible (NET-I18).
- Capacity expansion versus reach extension. Commercial expansion of an existing corridor enters the NET-I7 corridor as prudent capacity in the ordinary way; a reach extension to an unreached floor-holding settlement is delivered by a single COMP-I17 draw (NET-I14) and that asset is not additionally recovered from the general ratebase (NET-I19).
- Reach lead-time lapse. A planned connection not delivered within the NET-I14 published lead-time flips to a standing reach breach: V4 books against the operator's bearer per axis and COMP-I17 delivers interim essential service until the permanent connection is built (NET-I14).
- Congestion at a floor-serving node. Congestion pricing or curtailment SHALL NOT curtail a floor-serving connection below the delivered floor; the floor is not congestion-tradeable (NET-I14 with COMP-I17 machinery), and any congestion price is booked through the single correct channel (NET-I15).
- Operator failure or insolvency. The steward duty (NET-I2, NET-I3) does not lapse with the firm; where control fails to resolve, the state steward of last resort auto-assumes the segment (NET-I20), and the natural-person bearer for pre-existing criminal-class or concealment-class harm persists (A18 perpetual flip).
- Cross-border plant. A segment or U7 address spanning a jurisdictional boundary is split at the seam so each side resolves to exactly one register of record and one accountable steward, with the shared interconnection escrow and corridor apportioned by the NET-I21 coordination rule; no seam segment is left unassigned or double-assigned.
- Decommissioning. End-of-life removal books demolition emissions to F6 (NET-I18); reach and interconnection duties transfer or are formally retired only where no floor-holder or peer still depends on the plant.
- Correlated failure event. A resilience breach (NET-I11) coincident with above-corridor return is a run-to-failure signal (NET-I12); a prudent operator suffering a genuine exceedance of the tested standard books no anti-value for the prudent portion.
- Concealed defect discovered late. A concealed condition or resilience defect triggers the A18 concealment class with its perpetual flip; the discovery clock does not start until disclosure, and the concealment is itself a violation (NET-I12).
- Contested access denial. Any denial of access, interconnection, or reach is appealable with a reasoned ground before it stands; any capacity, return, or reach stripping against an operator gets the A18-I34 suspensive contest, but that contest never suspends physical delivery of a NET-I14 reach floor or a CONN-I15 reconnection (NET-I16).

## CONN utility service points, connection and metering (U5)

### Purpose

U5 is the physical seam where a U3 network meets a U1 building or a U2 premise: the point at which a durable, essential flow (potable water, sanitation, energy for heat and power, basic communications) becomes deliverable to an occupied place. This section governs that seam as a PHYSICAL and PLACE object: the connection, the meter, the service point of record, and the tariff reference that hangs off it. It does three things and only three things. It defines the connection floor, so a place a person lawfully lives in is not merely reachable but actually reached, connected with the essential minimum flowing. It fixes the disconnection limit, so an essential service cannot be pulled out from under a floor-holding household as a lever of coercion or as collateral. And it binds the connection into the sensing and access-control substrate, so consumption is measured once, mastered once, charged once, and read only through the S-cluster gate.

This section does NOT re-model title, control, encumbrance, the housing floor, the tariff itself, the flow, the lifeline quanta, or metering mechanics. Title and control of the connected structure are Property's (U1-I1 through U1-I9). The housing and occupancy floor that establishes who is a floor-holding household is Property's (U1-I8, U1-I5, U1-I9, P2). The metering-is-sensing rule and the mastering chain that turn a meter reading into a charge are Energy's (F1-I9, F1-I10, F1-I4, F1-I5). The substantive lifeline quanta are owned by their named stewards (the Energy floor steward for energy and heat-and-power, a named water and sanitation steward for the wet carriers, the F9 owner for communications reachability); this section cites the owning steward and states no number itself. The regulated-returns corridor and essential-facility anti-abuse rules that govern what the network operator may earn and may not deny are the economy and energy specs' (C1-I7, C1-I16, and the anti-abuse rule). The capability-floor delivery machinery and theta are the distributive-justice spec's (COMP-I17). The flow that moves through the connection is the flows spec's (F1, F3, F7, F9). U5 is the pipe's mouth and the meter on it, nothing more and nothing less.

Both flanks are load-bearing throughout. The household gets a physically delivered, non-disconnectable essential minimum and a meter it can audit. The honest network operator and supplier get paid on the $ rail for actual consumption, are protected from meter fraud and theft of service and from false hardship or occupancy claims, and keep the C1-I7 corridor return on the physical assets they honestly commit to reach a premise.

### Object model

- service_point (U5): the registered physical point at which one U3 network is bound to one U1 building or U2 premise for one carrier class. It carries: a stable service_point identifier; the bound U3 network and carrier class {potable_water, sanitation, electricity, gas, heat, communications}; the bound U1 improvement or U2 premise and its U7 address; the responsible network operator of record; the current connection state {connectable, connected, suspended, disconnected, safety-isolated}; a floor_flag indicating whether the served place is a floor-holding dwelling; and a tariff_ref. A service point is per carrier class: a dwelling has as many service points as it has essential carriers. For a floor-holding dwelling the floor is satisfied only in state connected with the essential minimum actually flowing, never by connectable alone (CONN-I4).
- connection: the authorized physical link (pipe, cable, lateral, riser, meter loop) realizing a service_point. It has a single controller resolved per U1-I1 for the structure side and the operator of record for the network side, a footprint that SHALL clear U1-I3 exclusivity across registers, and an attestation chain for the physical works (BLD condition and safety attestations).
- meter: the F1-I5 attested measuring device sited at a service_point, sensing quantity for that point per F1-I4 and F1-I5. A prepay meter is a meter whose F1-I5 device carries an embedded credit-control function; that function is an attribute of the meter object, never a separate authority to sever the floor (CONN-I9).
- tariff_ref: a reference to the published, machine-readable tariff schedule under which consumption at this service point settles on the $ rail. It is a pointer, not a price set here.
- floor_flag: a flag indicating the served place is a floor-holding dwelling. It binds from asserted or physically-evidenced actual lawful occupation, not from a registered occupancy_marker alone: a live metered load evidences occupation and auto-opens the marker from the physical fact (CONN-I6, CONN-I14).
- hardship_marker: an S6-predicate-backed flag on the served household indicating active CONN-I13 hardship status and the CONN-I6 disconnection-limit protection, revealing eligibility only, never the underlying means facts.

### Invariants

- CONN-I1: A service point is the single authoritative object for one carrier's connection to one place. Every essential flow that enters or leaves a U1 building or U2 premise SHALL do so through exactly one registered service_point per carrier class, bound to one U3 network, one meter (or an attested unmetered exception per CONN-I11), one responsible operator of record, and one tariff_ref. Purpose: no essential flow reaches an occupied place through an unregistered, unsensed or ownerless mouth. Predicate: a flow leg whose injection or withdrawal point is not a registered service_point is a defect. Enforcement: the leg is refused as a market leg (charter F1 refusal rule), floor deliveries excepted per CONN-I6.
- CONN-I2: The connection has one structure-side controller and one network-side operator, and its footprint is exclusive. The structure-side controller of a connection SHALL be the single controller of the co-held structure per U1-I1, and the connection footprint (lateral, easement, riser path) SHALL clear U1-I3 footprint exclusivity across the cadastre, the improvement register and the address register. Purpose: a connection cannot be commanded by two rival controllers, and two premises cannot silently draw one lawful connection. This section cites U1-I1 and U1-I3 and does not re-model title or control. Enforcement: a footprint collision is an R1 register conflict routed to the address registrar (A12) and, where contested, to an A6 case.
- CONN-I3: Metering is sensing, mastered once. A meter reading at a service point is an append-only ledger entry owned by the metered party; its raw quantity is sensed and mastered exactly once through the F1-I9 metering-is-sensing rule and the F1-I10 mastering chain, one quantity to one master and one charge. This section cites F1-I4, F1-I5, F1-I9 and F1-I10 and SHALL NOT re-model metering, inventory or charging. Purpose: the connection is not a parallel surveillance or a parallel charging channel. Enforcement: any second master for the same quantity, or any charge not tracing to a mastered meter line, is void.
- CONN-I4: A floor-holding dwelling SHALL be connected, with the essential minimum actually flowing, not merely connectable. For every dwelling that carries the housing and occupancy floor per U1-I8, each essential carrier (potable water, sanitation, energy sufficient for heat and power, and basic communications) SHALL reach state connected with the CONN-I6 essential minimum physically delivered. Where a service point is only connectable, the state SHALL open a bounded, time-capped COMP-I17 first-charge obligation to physically deliver the interim minimum by interim or alternative delivery while the permanent connection is built; an occupied floor-holding dwelling left connectable past the cap is a live floor breach routed to A18 with a natural-person bearer per CONN-I7. Connectability is the physical precondition of habitability, and delivery is the floor: a dwelling that cannot be connected to the essential carriers is not habitable and SHALL be treated as such by BLD (BLD-I15). Purpose: the occupancy floor is not delivered by a roof alone, nor by a promise of reach; a place a person is entitled to live in is actually reached by the essentials. This carrier set is the authoritative habitability carrier list cited by BLD-I15. Predicate: floor_flag true and any essential carrier not in state connected with the essential minimum flowing, absent an active CONN-I18 safety isolation, a documented CONN-I17 access defect, or an in-cap CONN-I4 interim-delivery obligation. Enforcement: the gap is a habitability defect routed to BLD and a floor gap delivered through the COMP-I17 standing reserve per CONN-I5.
- CONN-I5: The connection floor is DELIVERED via COMP-I17, by compute-and-route, never by commanding a price. Where the market would not on its own reach a floor-holding dwelling with an essential connection and the flowing essential minimum at a bearable cost, the delivery gap SHALL be closed on the household's own-account basis through the COMP-I17 standing reserve, which buys the connection and the essential minimum at the price the operator lawfully charges, like any buyer. The state SHALL NOT deliver the connection floor by capping the operator's tariff, by ordering the operator to extend or serve below its attested cost, or by netting the cost against any Ae quantity. The premise connection, from the connection point to the meter, is the CONN leg of one reach-and-connection obligation reconciled with NET-I14, and it composes with the NET reach leg into exactly one COMP-I17 draw per household per carrier, neither duplicated nor gapped (CONN-I19). Purpose: both flanks. The household is reached and served; the honest operator is paid the C1-I7 corridor return on the physical assets it commits, and is not conscripted into a below-cost extension. This section cites COMP-I17, C1-I7 and NET-I14 and does not re-model the reserve or the corridor. Enforcement: the floor draw is a first-charge COMP-I17 obligation; exactly one composed draw exists per household per carrier, and CONN-I6 and CONN-I18 open no second draw.
- CONN-I6: The essential minimum to a floor-holding household in genuine hardship SHALL NOT be cut. The disconnection floor binds from asserted or physically-evidenced actual lawful occupation, not from the registered occupancy_marker alone: a live metered load evidences occupation (metering-is-sensing, F1-I9), which auto-opens the occupancy_marker from the physical fact via A12 and bars disconnection pending A6 or registrar resolution, and only a place with an affirmatively resolved non-occupation status may be commercially disconnected. An operator SHALL NOT reduce a service point serving a floor-holding dwelling below the published essential minimum on account of arrears, dispute, contract termination, or credit control where the household holds active CONN-I13 hardship status; that essential minimum is delivered in kind through COMP-I17 while the arrears run on the $ rail per CONN-I8. Each lifeline quantum is owned by a single named non-operator steward: energy and heat-and-power by the Energy floor steward, potable water and sanitation by the named water and sanitation steward, communications reachability by the F9 owner; this section cites the owning steward and states no number itself, a conservative interim quantum published per carrier binds until the steward value publishes and SHALL NOT default to zero, and no single steward may uniformly lower the quanta in operators' favor (GOV level-capture guard). ## WORKS construction, permits and acceptance (U6)

### Purpose

This section models U6, the works view of the built environment: how a structure or a physical network comes into being, is altered, is handed over, and is retired. It governs the pipeline from permit through construction to an independent safety acceptance, and it fixes who bears liability when that pipeline produces something that can collapse, burn, or fail on its occupants and neighbours.

U6 does not own title, control, encumbrance, the location rent, or the housing floor: those are Property (U1-I1..U1-I9, the RENT overlay, PROP-I5). U6 does not own the standing structural-safety condition of a finished building (BLD) or the standing resilience regime of a live network (NET, RES). For a structure, U6 is the gate that first delivers the BLD structural-safety floor at birth and re-delivers it after material change. For a network segment, U6 certifies only the segment's own published design-standard and as-built condition at birth; admission of that segment to the live system's resilience regime is a NET operational-integration matter under RES, not a U6 birth-acceptance (WORKS-I3). U6 does not price anything: acceptance is a life-safety floor, not a value instrument, and the state prices only residual externality on the Ae rail, never the works themselves.

Both flanks are load-bearing throughout. Occupants, neighbours, and the commons are entitled to an independently certified safe structure and a natural-person bearer for any defect. The honest developer, builder, and designer are entitled to a decidable, published acceptance standard, a bounded liability window, and protection from an unknowable retroactive rule. A protocol that satisfies only one flank is out of conformance.

### Object model

- Works project (U6 object): the append-only record of a construction, alteration, or demolition undertaking against a specific U1 structure or U3 network segment on a specific P2 cadastral footprint. Holds: the permit reference, the responsible-persons roster, the stage ledger, the harm-capacity escrow reference, the acceptance record, and the handover events.
- Permit: an A-cluster governed authorization to build, alter, or demolish within a stated envelope (use, footprint, load, network class). U6 consumes the permit as data; the A-cluster law and its register own its issuance, amendment, and revocation.
- Responsible-persons roster: the natural persons and O1 operator firms in the roles of developer, designer, builder, and certifier, each mapped to at least one natural-person bearer.
- Stage: a bounded phase of the works (for example excavation, structure, envelope, network energization) with its own start, close, and inspection points. Stages are events, not a mutable status; the current phase is a non-authoritative fold over the stage ledger.
- Acceptance record: the certifier's attested decision that the completed works deliver, for a structure, the BLD structural-safety floor as of the acceptance instant, or, for a network segment, the segment's own published design-standard and as-built condition as of the acceptance instant. It does not attest system-level resilience, which is admitted separately under NET/RES (WORKS-I3). It carries the evidence set that supports the decision.
- Harm-capacity escrow reference: the C1-I16 ex-ante-peak escrow instrument standing behind an in-scope site, where in-scope is set by the class-based schedule cited in WORKS-I9.
- Latent-defect window: the bounded post-acceptance interval during which a newly manifest defect is presumed a works defect and routes to the roster, with the interim default horizon per broad element class stated in WORKS-I14.

### Invariants

**WORKS-I1 (works project as append-only object).** Every construction, alteration, or demolition of a U1 structure or U3 network segment SHALL be recorded as a works project keyed to its P2 footprint, holding permit reference, responsible-persons roster, stage ledger, and acceptance record as appended events. Purpose: create one addressable object that carries the safety pipeline and the liability roster. Predicate: no lawful acceptance record may attach to a footprint with no antecedent works project. Enforcement: the building/improvement register (A12) SHALL reject an acceptance filing that lacks a project key.

**WORKS-I2 (permit is a cited A-cluster instrument, not re-modeled here).** A works project SHALL reference a valid A-cluster permit whose envelope (use, footprint, load class, network class) contains the works actually undertaken. Purpose: keep lawmaking and permitting in the A-cluster and its register, and bind U6 to their output. U6 SHALL NOT re-model permit issuance, amendment, or revocation; it reads permit state and books divergence between the permitted envelope and the as-built works as a finding against the roster. Both flanks: the developer gets a permit envelope fixed ex ante and is judged against it, not against a later reinterpretation; occupants get assurance that the built thing sits inside an authorized envelope.

**WORKS-I3 (independent acceptance gate before occupancy or energization; WORKS owns the birth gate; the network seam).** No structure SHALL be lawfully occupied until an acceptance record delivers the BLD structural-safety floor as of acceptance. No network segment SHALL be lawfully energized or connected to load until an acceptance record certifies the segment's own published design-standard and as-built condition as of acceptance. WORKS owns the birth acceptance gate and SHALL be the sole trigger for occupancy-capable U7 addressing at commissioning. Network seam: a segment acceptance certifies the segment in isolation (its design-standard and as-built condition, per-segment decidable) and SHALL NOT attest system-level resilience; admission of the energized segment to the live system's resilience regime routes to a NET operational-integration check under the RES regime (cited, not re-modeled here), so neither layer double-gates and resilience is not left uncertified. Purpose: floors are delivered at birth, not declared, and each layer gates only what it can decide. Predicate: lawful-occupiable(structure) requires a non-revoked WORKS acceptance record; lawful-energizable(segment) requires the same for the segment's design-standard and as-built condition, and lawful-system-admission(segment) additionally requires the NET/RES integration check. Enforcement: the address register (U7) SHALL NOT issue an occupancy-capable address absent the WORKS acceptance record; BLD-P1 commissioning SHALL consume the WORKS acceptance record as a precondition and SHALL NOT independently mint the address (WORKS-I17); F1/F3 connection SHALL NOT provision service absent acceptance. Both flanks: occupants never inherit an uncertified structure or segment; the honest developer gets a single, decidable birth gate at U6 rather than open-ended second-guessing, and the network operator faces one segment-acceptance test plus one system-integration test, not a resilience verdict smuggled into birth-acceptance.

**WORKS-I4 (certifier independence from the concert graph, with a decidable economic-independence limb).** The certifier of an acceptance SHALL have no acting-in-concert edge (O1 concert graph) to the developer, builder, or designer of the same works. The certifier's economic independence SHALL be structural and decidable, not an appearance test: the certifier SHALL be assigned from a pool by rotation or blind assignment and SHALL NOT be chosen by the developer; the same certifier SHALL NOT clear the same developer more often than a published frequency; and the certifier's fee SHALL NOT vary with the pass or fail outcome. The rotation, blind-assignment, and frequency-cap mechanism lives in the O1 anti-abuse regime (cited); WORKS consumes its output at the gate and books a violation of it as a WORKS-I10 concealment-class exposure. This limb is the built-environment mirror of the BLD inspector-independence concern (cross-reference); both a bribed clear and a captured condemnation are made structurally infeasible by the same mechanism. Purpose: an acceptance is only worth the independence behind it, and independence must be checkable ex ante, not litigated ex post. Predicate: concert_edge(certifier, {developer, builder, designer}) = false at acceptance and throughout the latent-defect window; and assignment, frequency, and fee satisfy the O1 anti-abuse mechanism. Enforcement: an acceptance recorded across a live concert edge or against a violated assignment/frequency/fee rule is void ab initio and flips to WORKS-I10. Both flanks: occupants get a genuinely arm's-length attestation drawn from a pool the developer cannot steer; an honest certifier gets a bright-line, mechanized independence rule instead of a vague appearance test, and is protected from a captured condemnation as much as the occupant is protected from a bribed clear.

**WORKS-I5 (acceptance delivers the birth floors, and states the standard it applied).** An acceptance record SHALL name the published standard version it verified against (the BLD floor standard for a structure, the network design-standard for a segment) and SHALL carry the evidence set sufficient to re-derive the decision. Purpose: acceptance is decidable and reviewable, not a signature. Predicate: acceptance is well-formed only if standard_version and evidence_set are present and the evidence supports each criterion. Enforcement: a bare acceptance with no cited standard or evidence is not a valid WORKS-I3 gate. Both flanks: the developer is measured against the standard in force at acceptance; occupants and reviewers can audit the basis.

**WORKS-I6 (a single thin bearer application for defective works).** Liability for a defective-works harm, meaning a collapse, a fire-spread, a network-run-to-failure, or a floor breach traceable to the works, SHALL resolve to at least one natural-person bearer for each culpable role on the WORKS-I1 roster (developer, designer, builder, certifier). This section adds only the defective-works and collapse harm surface; the bearer is resolved by the O1 veil-piercing rule and the A18 no-bearerless-harm rule (cited, not re-modeled here), which together guarantee that a bearer always resolves and that no entity death, dissolution, or insolvency extinguishes it. This invariant consolidates the former separate bearer and no-shield invariants. Predicate: for every confirmed works defect there exists a named natural-person bearer for each culpable role; a role with no resolvable bearer blocks acceptance under WORKS-I8. Enforcement: the O1 and A18 machinery attributes and preserves the bearer; the O5 floor-waiver-void and total-life-capture-void rules apply so the bearer survives the entity. Both flanks: victims and neighbours get a reachable bearer that no shell-and-dissolve escape can empty; an honestly-acting individual answers only for its own role's duty, not for strict liability across the roster.

**WORKS-I8 (a role with no bearer or no live escrow cannot pass the gate).** Acceptance SHALL be refused where any responsible role on the roster lacks a resolvable natural-person bearer, or where an in-scope site lacks a live harm-capacity escrow (WORKS-I9). Purpose: prevent bearerless or under-secured structures from reaching occupancy. Predicate: acceptance_permitted requires bearer(role) present for all roles and, for an in-scope site, escrow(project) live. Enforcement: the certifier SHALL withhold the acceptance record; the register SHALL reject a filing that names an unbacked role or an in-scope site with no live escrow.

**WORKS-I9 (site escrow: which sites carry it, and what draws it; C1-I16 owns the mechanics).** An active construction, alteration, or demolition site that is in scope SHALL carry a C1-I16 ex-ante-peak harm-capacity escrow. In-scope SHALL be determined by a decidable, published, class-based, appealable schedule (cited from C11): a site is in scope where it has rated occupancy above a published headcount, or structural adjacency such that a credible failure reaches a neighbouring occupied parcel, or a listed stored-hazard inventory. The escrow is default-required for any such site, with the burden on the controller to attest OUT of scope; the scope and sizing determination is contestable under A18-I34 before it binds the WORKS-I8 gate; an escrow demand exceeding the schedule for the site's class is over-reach and void; and arguing a genuinely multi-occupant or spread failure mode out of scope is itself a booked misrepresentation. The draw trigger is a booked WORKS, BLD, or NET safety breach at the site. Sizing, revaluation, solvency-independence, and draw priority are owned by C1-I16 and are cited, not re-modeled here. The escrow SHALL be a segregated, ring-fenced instrument standing OUTSIDE the controller's insolvent estate, asserting no creditor-priority reorder; any residual lien or creditor ordering follows U1-I4 (cited). Purpose: the point of maximum latent harm is during the works, before any floor is verified, so a funded, ring-fenced backstop stands there for exactly the sites whose failure mode warrants it. Predicate: escrow(project) live for every in-scope site; escrow amount within the C1-I16 sizing for the class. Enforcement: the escrow reference is a WORKS-I1 project field and a WORKS-I8 precondition. Both flanks: neighbours and the commons hold a funded, insolvency-remote backstop for site harm; the controller of an out-of-scope or lower-class site is not made to post an open-ended or oversized escrow, and can contest scope and sizing before the demand binds. This resolves WORKS-Q3.

**WORKS-I10 (false acceptance and the bribed certifier are a concealment-class harm with the perpetual flip).** Issuing, procuring, or accepting a false or corrupted acceptance, and concealing a known works defect behind a pass, SHALL be a concealment-class A18 harm to which the perpetual flip applies: the clock never runs out on the concealed defect, and concealing the violation is itself a violation. Purpose: a bribed certifier defeats every other floor at once, so it carries the harshest surface. Predicate: on discovery that an acceptance was false or that a defect was concealed, the latent-defect window (WORKS-I14) does not run and never expired for that defect. Enforcement: A18 concealment class, the A18-I34 suspensive-contest guarantee for the accused, and the WORKS-I6 bearer graph. Both flanks: occupants are protected against a defect buried by a paid pass for as long as it stays hidden; an honest certifier who discloses a marginal call in good faith is inside the ordinary window, not the perpetual one.

**WORKS-I11 (construction and demolition emissions and waste book on the F6 lifecycle ledger).** The embodied emissions of construction and the emissions and material waste of demolition SHALL book on the F6 emissions lifecycle ledger against the works project, and demolition SHALL record the disposition of removed materials. Purpose: works are a lifecycle emissions and waste event, kept where the flows spec owns them. U6 SHALL NOT re-model F6; it emits the booking event. Both flanks: the commons gets the externality booked on the Ae rail; the developer books a defined ledger event, not an ad hoc charge.

**WORKS-I12 (worker safety on site is the O3 labor floor delivered).** Every natural person working a U6 site SHALL be covered by the O3 labor floor as delivered, and a site that operates below it SHALL be an active violation independent of any structural outcome. Purpose: the site is a labor-floor surface in its own right, not only a future-occupant surface. Predicate: site_operating requires O3 floor coverage for all on-site persons. Enforcement: O3 machinery and the A18 harm surface for floor breach; U6 cites, does not re-model, the labor floor. Both flanks: workers get the floor at the point of maximum on-site risk; a compliant builder gets a defined coverage standard.

**WORKS-I13 (staged handover records who holds each stage).** Handover of a stage or of the completed works SHALL be an appended event naming the transferring and receiving roles and the floor status at transfer. Purpose: fix responsibility across the seams where defects hide. Predicate: no stage is deemed handed over without a handover event; responsibility for a stage rests with its last recorded holder until a handover event moves it. Enforcement: the stage ledger; a gap in handover holds the prior role. Both flanks: occupants and downstream trades can trace the responsible holder; an honest trade is released at its recorded handover, not indefinitely.

**WORKS-I14 (bounded latent-defect window, with the interim default horizon and the concealment carve-out).** Each works project SHALL carry a published, bounded latent-defect window per element class, during which a newly manifest defect is presumed a works defect and routes to the WORKS-I6 roster. Until the calibrated per-element schedule publishes, an interim default window per broad element class SHALL bind, published now, so the presumption and the concealment carve-out both have a defined horizon from acceptance: as interim placeholders, a longer horizon for structural elements (for example ten years), an intermediate horizon for envelope elements (for example six years), and a shorter horizon for services elements (for example two years), each subject to replacement by the calibrated schedule. Purpose: give occupants a real post-acceptance remedy and give honest builders a knowable end to ordinary exposure, with no gap while calibration is pending. Predicate: within the applicable window, defect_presumed_works = true (rebuttable); outside it, the claimant carries the burden. Carve-out: WORKS-I10 concealment suspends and never expires the window for a concealed defect. Both flanks: occupants get a presumption in their favour while the window runs, from the day acceptance is booked; the honest builder gets a certain horizon for defects that were not concealed, even before the fine-grained schedule exists.

**WORKS-I15 (material alteration re-triggers proportionate re-acceptance).** A works project that changes load path, egress, fire compartmentation, structural element, or network class SHALL require a re-acceptance of the affected scope before the altered structure is re-occupied or the altered segment re-energized; a non-material change SHALL NOT. Purpose: keep the birth floors current without gating trivial work. Predicate: reacceptance_required iff the change touches a floor-relevant element class, published in the standard. Both flanks: occupants are protected when the safety envelope actually moves; the honest owner-improver is not forced through full re-acceptance for cosmetic or non-structural work.

**WORKS-I16 (unpermitted or rogue works: presumptively unsafe, blocked from lawful occupancy, retro-certifiable, and floor-delivering).** Works built without a valid permit or without acceptance SHALL be presumptively unsafe and SHALL NOT confer lawful occupancy or energization until they pass a retro-certification against the current WORKS-I5 standard for the as-found condition. Purpose: protect occupants and neighbours from uncertified structures without punishing the fact of self-building. Predicate: rogue works are blocked from the WORKS-I3 gate until an independent certifier issues a retro-acceptance; retro-acceptance judges the as-found structure against the published standard, not against a stricter unknowable rule. Floor delivery (aligned to PLAN-I9): where a natural person is in actual lawful occupation, the U1-I8 housing/occupancy floor binds, and any rehousing or alternative shelter it requires SHALL be DELIVERED through the COMP-I17 standing reserve on the occupant's own-account basis (compute-and-route), citing U1-I8 for the entitlement, never merely declared as reachable. The retro-certification, condemnation, or any displacement it entails SHALL take no physical effect until the COMP-I17 delivery is booked, so the response is retro-certification, remediation, or, at the limit, booked rehousing under U1-I8, and never summary eviction into breach of the floor. Where the unsafety traces to a distinct controller's fault rather than the occupant self-builder, that controller bears the rehousing cost and COMP-I17 is subrogated against it (cited). Enforcement: the register flags the footprint as unaccepted; U7 withholds occupancy status; retro-acceptance clears the flag; the COMP-I17 delivery booking gates any physical displacement. Both flanks: occupants and neighbours are protected from a genuinely unsafe rogue structure, and the honest self-builder is offered a decidable route to certification and keeps the housing floor as a delivered floor rather than being erased.

**WORKS-I17 (acceptance and its revocation are register events under public faith).** The acceptance record, its evidence citation, and any revocation SHALL be appended to the building/improvement register (A12), and good-faith reliance on a current-state acceptance projection SHALL be protected by R1-I28. Purpose: make the safety state of a structure a matter of record that third parties can rely on. Predicate: a purchaser, lender, or connection provider relying in good faith on the projected acceptance state is protected even if the underlying record is later corrected by a new event. Carve-out: R1-I28 protection does not launder a WORKS-I10 concealed defect against the culpable bearers. Both flanks: an honest reliant party is protected; a concealing bearer cannot hide behind the public-faith projection.

### Protocols

- Acceptance protocol. On stage completion the developer requests acceptance; an independent certifier (WORKS-I4, assigned from the O1 anti-abuse pool) verifies the works against the named BLD or network design-standard version (WORKS-I5), confirms a live escrow for an in-scope site (WORKS-I9) and a complete bearer roster (WORKS-I8), and either appends an acceptance record or a reasoned refusal to the A12 register (WORKS-I17). At the moment of decision the certifier SHALL also append an explicit S4 audit event (actor, input evidence set, output decision, timestamp), distinct from the R1/A12 record append, so the acceptance or refusal is audit-traceable under the S cluster. Occupancy or occupancy-capable addressing (U7) is unlocked only on a recorded acceptance and its S4 audit event (WORKS-I3); for a network segment, system admission additionally requires the NET/RES operational-integration check.
- Defect-routing protocol. A defect manifest within the latent-defect window (WORKS-I14) routes to the roster with a rebuttable works presumption; a defect found to be concealed flips to the WORKS-I10 concealment class with the perpetual clock; the accused holds the A18-I34 suspensive-contest guarantee throughout.
- Retro-certification protocol. Rogue or unpermitted works (WORKS-I16) enter a retro-acceptance against the current standard for the as-found condition; where a person is in lawful occupation the U1-I8 floor governs the human outcome, the retro-certification takes no physical effect until the COMP-I17 rehousing delivery is booked, and the structure is brought to standard or the occupant is rehoused on the own-account COMP-I17 basis.
- Change protocol. A proposed alteration is classified material or non-material against the published element-class list (WORKS-I15); material changes re-enter the acceptance protocol for the affected scope.

### Lifecycle and edge cases

- Birth: permit, build, accept, occupy or energize. The BLD floor (for a structure) or the segment design-standard and as-built condition (for a segment) is delivered at the acceptance instant, not by the developer's declaration; system-level network resilience is admitted separately under NET/RES (WORKS-I3).
- Alteration: proportionate re-acceptance for floor-relevant changes only (WORKS-I15).
- Handover seams: responsibility follows the last recorded holder until a handover event (WORKS-I13); gaps hold the prior role.
- Certifier capture: a concert edge, a developer-steered assignment, an over-frequency clear, or contingent pay voids the acceptance and flips to concealment (WORKS-I4, WORKS-I10).
- Insolvency or dissolution mid-defect: the entity's death does not clear the bearer graph (WORKS-I6, O5 rules); the WORKS-I9 escrow stands outside the insolvent estate.
- Rogue self-build with an occupant: retro-certify and deliver the housing floor via COMP-I17; the retro-certification takes no physical effect until the delivery is booked, so no eviction into breach (WORKS-I16, U1-I8, PLAN-I9).
- Demolition: books emissions and waste on F6 (WORKS-I11) and closes the works project; an in-scope site's escrow (WORKS-I9) covers demolition peak harm.
- Emergency works: X3 emergency access and works proceed under the S-cluster emergency regime; acceptance is completed post hoc against the same standard, and the escrow and bearer rules still bind. An X3 emergency evacuation carries the COMP-I17 rehousing delivery forward as a live first-charge obligation, never a discharge (WORKS-I16, C4).

## PLAN settlement, urban form, planning and addressing (U4, U7)

### Purpose

This section models the PLACE view of the built environment above the individual structure: U4 settlement and urban form (the settlement, district, block and density objects) and U7 address and location referencing. It governs land-use PLANNING and zoning as a value-balancing act of the state, and it governs the address registrar as an A12-mandated public-faith register.

The governing frame is that MOS has no taxation. Planning and infrastructure charges are value-balancing instruments, not revenue tools. A planning decision prices no market. It books the residual externality across axes (a multidimensional Pigou overlay per the value substrate), balancing the housing floor, congestion, ecology, heritage and infrastructure capacity as separate booked axes that are never netted against one another and never netted against the money rail.

This section does not re-model title, control, encumbrance, rent or the housing floor. Those are property objects owned by Property-and-Ownership.md (U1-I1 through U1-I9, the RENT overlay, P2 cadastre, PROP-I5 succession) and are cited by ID. This section does not re-model what flows through a network: U3 is the physical pipe and F1/F3/F7/F9 are the flows, owned by the flows spec. It takes the settlement, form, planning and addressing surface only.

Both flanks are protected throughout. The occupant, the displaced, the commons and future cohorts are protected against capture, exclusion and irreversible erasure. The honest owner and honest builder are protected against arbitrary, confiscatory or pretextual planning, and a genuine heritage claim is separated from a sham one used to block housing.

### Object model

- **U4.settlement**: a named populated place with a boundary, a governing planning steward, and a booked infrastructure-capacity envelope (cite kernel C4 infrastructure-as-economic-capacity). Holds a settlement-scale housing-floor account computed at the settlement-of-record boundary fixed by PLAN-I3.
- **U4.district**: a sub-settlement zone carrying a land-use designation (the zoning class), a density parameter band, and per-axis value corridors.
- **U4.block**: the smallest urban-form aggregate above the parcel; the unit at which frontage, serviced-land connectivity and footprint aggregation are assessed. Parcels and their footprints are P2/U1 objects, cited not restated.
- **U4.density_parameter**: a capacity-and-liveability parameter attached to a district or block (dwellings or floor-area per unit land, height, coverage, connectable-service load). It is a capacity variable, never a membership screen.
- **U4.plan_decision**: an A-cluster governed act that creates, amends or refuses a designation, density band or corridor. Carries the deciding steward, the per-axis value book, the affected-cohort set, the location-value-uplift book (PLAN-I21), a suspensive-contest window (cite A18-I34), and an S4 audit event.
- **U7.address**: a location reference bound to a parcel, structure, unit or access point. A public-faith projection (R1-I28) held append-only under an address registrar (A12-mandated), resolving to exactly one register of record and one accountable steward (PLAN-I22).
- **U7.location_reference**: any non-address geospatial referent (coordinate, block-lot key, access-point id) used to resolve an address without being one.
- **U4.heritage_marker / U4.non_priceable_place**: a designation asserting a cultural or dignity value on a place not reducible to price, screened for irreversibility.
- **U4.displacement_event**: a record generated whenever a plan_decision or redevelopment lawfully removes an actual occupation, linked to the affected occupancy_marker and to the COMP-I17 delivery path.

### Invariants

**PLAN-I1 (planning is value-balancing, never revenue).** A plan_decision SHALL be booked as a per-axis value balance across at least the housing-floor, congestion, ecology, heritage and infrastructure-capacity axes, and SHALL NOT be justified by, priced against, or conditioned on revenue to any steward or the state. Purpose: forecloses zoning-as-tax and pay-to-rezone. Predicate: a decision whose recorded rationale reduces to a money transfer to the deciding authority, with no per-axis externality book, is void. Enforcement: the decision fails registration at U7/A12 and books anti-value (V4) against the steward with a natural-person bearer.

**PLAN-I2 (per-axis, never netted).** The axes in a plan_decision SHALL be booked separately and SHALL NOT be netted against each other or against the money rail. Purpose: a congestion gain does not erase a housing-floor loss or an ecological loss by arithmetic. Predicate: any decision record showing a single scalar net-benefit that cancels a floor-axis or ecology-axis debit against a credit on another axis is malformed and refused.

**PLAN-I3 (housing floor is a hard axis, breach is void; settlement boundary fixed).** A plan_decision SHALL NOT reduce the settlement-scale delivered-housing-floor account below the level required to keep every floor-holder in lawful occupation (cite the Property housing floor U1-I8 and its delivery via COMP-I17). The account SHALL be computed at the settlement-of-record boundary: the contiguous serviced daily-life settlement (the U4.settlement whose service, connection and labor catchment the floor-holder actually lives within) that contains the affected occupation, and it extends to an adjacent settlement only where the replacement floor there meets the comparable-reach standard of PLAN-I3a. Purpose: the floor is delivered, not declared, planning cannot quietly strip it, and the boundary at which it is measured is fixed rather than gerrymandered. Predicate: a decision whose booked housing-floor axis goes negative without an equal-or-greater floor delivered on connectable serviced land within the settlement-of-record (or a PLAN-I3a-qualified adjacent settlement) is void under the O5 floor-waiver-void rule. Enforcement: void ab initio, no good-faith cure.

**PLAN-I3a (replacement floor is genuinely reachable, and off-line relocation is caught).** A replacement housing floor credited under PLAN-I3 SHALL be genuinely connectable and comparably reachable: it is counted only where it meets a comparable-reach standard owned jointly with the connection and network layers (cite CONN-I4 carrier set, CONN-I6 essential minimum, NET-I14 reach floor), so the substitute floor is connected in kind, not merely inside a settlement line. A relocation that satisfies the settlement boundary on paper but concentrates a housing-floor axis debit on protected cohorts is caught under the PLAN-I8 exclusionary-effect book and refused regardless of formal compliance. Purpose: forecloses discharging the floor by drawing the settlement line around unreachable land and forecloses compliant-on-paper concentration of the debit. Predicate: replacement credit from land failing the comparable-reach standard, or a relocation whose booked effect is a PLAN-I8 exclusionary concentration, is disallowed and the floor axis recomputed.

**PLAN-I4 (connectable serviced land, not paper zoning).** Where a plan_decision claims to satisfy the housing floor by up-zoning, the credited supply SHALL be counted only as land that is serviced or has a booked, funded path to service (water, energy, access, per C4 capacity), never as bare designation. Purpose: bars discharging the floor with unbuildable paper. Predicate: floor credit from land lacking a connectable-service commitment is disallowed and the floor axis recomputed.

**PLAN-I5 (level-capture guard on rezoning).** A single steward SHALL NOT rezone, up-zone, down-zone or vary density uniformly in favor of an incumbent, an insider or the steward's own account (cite Organizations GOV level-capture guard). Purpose: the one steward able to relax every threshold is prevented from relaxing them all in one direction. Predicate: a pattern of decisions by one steward whose net effect uniformly advantages a single interest across thresholds is caught. Enforcement: decisions suspended pending GOV review; anti-value booked with a natural-person bearer.

**PLAN-I6 (concert-party spot-zoning is caught).** A parcel-specific or narrowly-drawn designation that benefits a party found to be acting in concert (cite the O1 acting-in-concert graph and the canonical veil-piercing rule) SHALL be treated as a single act of that concert party, not as independent decisions. Purpose: defeats spot-zoning laundered through separated nominal applicants. Predicate: designations whose beneficiaries collapse to one concert node under O1 are aggregated and tested against PLAN-I5 and PLAN-I7 as one act.

**PLAN-I7 (no arbitrary or confiscatory planning: the honest-owner flank).** A plan_decision affecting a lawful owner SHALL rest on a stated, published, per-axis rationale and SHALL NOT be arbitrary, retaliatory, or confiscatory in effect. Purpose: predictability for the honest builder and owner. The confiscation trigger is decidable: the decision books a residual-lawful-use value for the parcel, expressed as a fraction of the parcel's prior lawful-use value, and where that booked residual fraction falls below a published band the decision flips to the confiscatory branch. Both branches inherit this one threshold. Branch selection: a decision below the band without a booked, proportionate externality on a named axis is confiscatory and void; a decision below the band that IS justified by a booked, proportionate externality on a named axis is a lawful confiscatory planning act and routes owner value-compensation to the Property/RENT value-balancing instruments (cited, per PLAN-I9), never to the capability-floor reserve. The proportionality of the booked externality against the residual-use band is contestable on an A6 route against a stated proportionality standard. Enforcement: the suspensive-contest window (A18-I34) runs before the decision bites; a decision that cannot state its axis book does not take effect.

**PLAN-I8 (exclusionary density is caught).** A density_parameter or designation SHALL be set as a capacity-and-liveability parameter and SHALL NOT operate as a membership screen that pushes floor-holders or protected cohorts out of a settlement. Purpose: separates real capacity limits from exclusion. Predicate: a minimum-lot, minimum-unit-size, single-use or amenity requirement whose booked effect is a housing-floor axis debit concentrated on floor-holder cohorts, without a proportionate infrastructure-capacity or ecology justification, is exclusionary and refused; this exclusionary-effect book is the same one PLAN-I3a routes a compliant-on-paper relocation into. Both flanks: a genuine capacity ceiling (booked congestion or C4 capacity or D4 habitat limit) is upheld; a screen dressed as a ceiling is struck.

**PLAN-I9 (displacement delivers the floor in kind; owner value routes to the property rail).** When a plan_decision or redevelopment lawfully removes an actual lawful occupation, the displaced occupant's HOUSING FLOOR (shelter, connection, essential minimum) SHALL be DELIVERED in kind through the COMP-I17 standing reserve on the occupant's own-account basis, and SHALL NOT be extracted from the occupant or made a condition of the redevelopment's value credit. Owner value-compensation for a confiscatory planning act (PLAN-I7) and any above-floor displacement value SHALL route to the Property/RENT value-balancing instruments (cite the RENT overlay and Property U1-I4 encumbrance ordering), and the capability-floor reserve (COMP-I17) is NEVER charged with property-value compensation. Purpose: displacement is a capture surface; the person's floor moves with the person and is delivered upfront, while owner value is a money-rail matter kept off the floor reserve. Predicate: a redevelopment booking a value credit while its linked displacement_event has an undelivered floor is incomplete and does not register; a compensation claim that draws property value from the capability-floor reserve is malformed and refused. This in-kind floor delivery is the reference model that BLD-I11 and WORKS-I16 align to (per charter C4). Cite U1-I8, U1-I5 (floor never collateral), COMP-I17.

**PLAN-I10 (occupancy survives the plan).** A plan_decision SHALL NOT of itself extinguish an occupancy_marker (the R1 public-faith minimum, U1-I8). Occupation ends only by the property and enforcement disciplines (U1-I6, A18), not by a stroke of zoning. Purpose: zoning cannot be used to evict. Predicate: any plan_decision whose direct effect is deletion of an occupancy_marker is void.

**PLAN-I11 (heritage and non-priceable places: irreversibility screen).** A designation asserting cultural or dignity value on a place SHALL, where the value is irreversible on erasure, be screened like an ecological irreversibility (cite the D4 habitat/condition floor and the irreversibility precaution). Purpose: some places carry value not reducible to price, and loss is permanent. Predicate: a demolition or alteration of a screened non-priceable place proceeds only after the irreversibility precaution is discharged on the record.

**PLAN-I12 (demolish-then-apologize is barred).** Irreversible erasure of a screened heritage or non-priceable place SHALL NOT proceed ahead of its contest and precaution steps, and a completed erasure SHALL NOT be cured by after-the-fact compensation or apology. Purpose: closes the fait-accompli move. Predicate: erasure executed before the A18-I34 suspensive window closes is a concealment-class-adjacent life-of-place harm booked as anti-value with teeth and a natural-person bearer; money cannot unbook it. Cite A18 non-priceable class and the perpetual concealment flip.

**PLAN-I13 (sham heritage is caught: the housing flank).** A heritage or non-priceable claim SHALL be evidenced against stated cultural-value criteria and SHALL NOT be used pretextually to block housing-floor delivery or to freeze an incumbent's advantage. Purpose: protects the honest builder and the floor from heritage-as-blocker. Predicate: a claim asserted first in response to a floor-delivering proposal, lacking independent cultural-value evidence, and whose booked effect is a housing-floor debit favoring an incumbent (test under PLAN-I5/PLAN-I6), is refused as a sham and does not stay the proposal. Both flanks: PLAN-I11 protects the genuine place; PLAN-I13 protects the floor from the sham.

**PLAN-I14 (address is a public-faith projection).** A U7.address SHALL be a public-faith projection (cite R1-I28) held under an A12-mandated address registrar. Good-faith reliance on the registered address is protected. Purpose: addresses are relied on for service, notice and floor delivery, and SHALL be trustworthy.

**PLAN-I15 (addresses are append-only, corrected by new event).** The address register SHALL be append-only. A change of address, split, merge, renumber or correction SHALL be a new appended event, never an in-place mutation, and the current address is a non-authoritative FOLD over the appended events (cite R1/R2). Purpose: preserves the record chain and reliance. Predicate: any in-place overwrite of a prior address entry is rejected.

**PLAN-I16 (addressing is non-discretionary and non-exclusionary).** Assignment or withholding of an address SHALL follow published, uniform rules and SHALL NOT be used to deny a floor-holder service, notice or capability-floor delivery, nor to advantage an incumbent. Purpose: an unaddressed dwelling is a stripped floor by another means. Predicate: a lawful occupied structure without a registered address, or a selectively withheld address, is a registrar failure booking anti-value with a natural-person bearer; the occupant's floor delivery (COMP-I17) does not depend on the missing address.

**PLAN-I17 (life-safety and network access are not planning bargaining chips).** A plan_decision SHALL NOT trade away a life-safety condition or lawful access to an essential-facility network as a planning concession. Purpose: keeps the harm surface and the essential-facility rules intact. Predicate: a designation conditioned on waiving a building life-safety requirement (A18 life-safety harm surface) or on an essential-facility holder degrading or denying access (cite C1-I7 returns and the anti-abuse access rules) is void; U3 remains the pipe and F the flow, cited not re-modeled.

**PLAN-I18 (planning-capture has a natural-person bearer).** The domain harm of planning-capture (a plan_decision, address action or heritage screening that captures value, breaches a floor, or excludes a protected cohort) SHALL resolve to the accountable natural person(s) exercising the steward or registrar authority. Cite the O1 veil-piercing and acting-in-concert guarantee that a bearer always resolves, the A18 no-bearerless-harm rule, and the GOV level-capture guard; this invariant adds only the planning-capture harm and restates none of those mechanics. Purpose: no autonomous-agent or entity path leaves a planning-capture harm bearerless. Predicate: a decision or register event lacking a bound natural-person bearer does not take effect.

**PLAN-I19 (contest before it bites).** A plan_decision that debits any protected axis (housing floor, ecology, heritage, an owner's lawful use) SHALL carry a suspensive-contest guarantee (cite A18-I34): the debit does not take effect until the contest window closes or is waived by the affected party. Purpose: protects both flanks against fait-accompli, the occupant against silent displacement and the owner against an unappealable stroke.

**PLAN-I20 (construction and demolition externalities book elsewhere, once).** The emissions and material externalities of construction and demolition triggered by a plan_decision SHALL be booked once, in the emissions lifecycle (cite Energy F6), and referenced here, not re-booked. Purpose: prevents double-counting and keeps the axis book clean while ensuring the externality is not lost. Predicate: a plan_decision that books construction or demolition emissions locally instead of by reference to F6 is malformed.

**PLAN-I21 (location-value uplift is restored to the commons, not captured; two rails split).** Where a plan_decision creates location-value uplift on a parcel (an up-zoning, corridor or designation that raises lawful-use value by public act), the uplift SHALL be restored to the commons on the money rail under the RENT overlay (cite the RENT overlay, location-value uplift to the commons), as a value-balancing instrument and never as revenue to the deciding steward (PLAN-I1). Anti-value (V4) SHALL be booked only for the residual per-axis harm of a capture act (the trust-and-governance axis where publicly-created uplift is privately captured through an insider or concert route), never for the money magnitude of the uplift itself. Purpose: publicly created value returns to the commons and the capture harm is booked per axis, without equating money and anti-value. Predicate: a decision routing uplift to the steward as revenue is void (PLAN-I1); the money restoration books on the money rail and V4 books the residual per-axis harm, and the two are never equated or netted (cite PLAN-I2).

**PLAN-I22 (one register of record, one steward per address, across boundaries).** Each U7.address SHALL resolve to exactly one register of record and exactly one accountable registrar steward, even where the parcel, structure or network access point it references sits on or across a jurisdictional boundary. Purpose: a floor-holder's notice, service and floor delivery never fall between two registers or two stewards. Predicate: an address resolvable to two registers of record, or to none, is a registrar failure booking anti-value with a natural-person bearer (PLAN-I18); the seam is resolved by an explicit coordination rule naming the single register of record and steward before the address binds.

### Protocols

- **Plan decision protocol.** (1) Steward opens a U4.plan_decision, naming the natural-person bearer (PLAN-I18). (2) Affected-cohort set and affected occupancy_markers resolved. (3) Per-axis externality booked separately (PLAN-I1, PLAN-I2), including housing-floor at the settlement-of-record boundary (PLAN-I3, PLAN-I3a, PLAN-I4), ecology/D4, heritage screen (PLAN-I11), congestion, C4 capacity, and the location-value-uplift book (PLAN-I21). (4) Concert-graph aggregation and level-capture test (PLAN-I5, PLAN-I6). (5) Confiscation (residual-use band) and exclusion tests (PLAN-I7, PLAN-I8). (6) Suspensive-contest window opens (PLAN-I19). (7) On close, decision registers at U7/A12 with its axis book, or is refused/void. (8) The decision records an S4 audit event (actor, input evidence set, output decision, timestamp), distinct from the R1/A12 append. Construction and demolition externalities referenced to F6 (PLAN-I20).
- **Displacement delivery protocol.** A lawful occupation removal emits a U4.displacement_event linked to the occupancy_marker; the occupant's housing floor is delivered in kind via COMP-I17 on own-account basis (PLAN-I9), and any owner value-compensation routes to the Property/RENT value-balancing instruments, never to the capability-floor reserve (PLAN-I9); the redevelopment's value credit does not register until floor delivery is booked (PLAN-I9 predicate). Occupancy is never extinguished by the plan itself (PLAN-I10). The displacement decision and each delivery record an S4 audit event (actor, input evidence set, output decision, timestamp), distinct from the R1 append.
- **Heritage screening protocol.** A place asserted as heritage or non-priceable is tested against published cultural-value criteria; if irreversible, the D4 irreversibility precaution is applied (PLAN-I11); erasure is barred ahead of contest (PLAN-I12); a claim failing evidence and timed as a blocker is refused as sham (PLAN-I13).
- **Address lifecycle protocol.** Assignment, split, merge, renumber and correction are each new appended events under the address registrar (PLAN-I15); current address is a FOLD; reliance is protected (PLAN-I14); each address resolves to one register of record and one steward across boundaries (PLAN-I22); withholding is a booked failure (PLAN-I16). Each appended address action records an S4 audit event (actor, input evidence set, output decision, timestamp), distinct from the R1 append.

### Lifecycle and edge cases

- **New settlement / greenfield.** Housing floor is a supply-and-serviced-land obligation from the outset (PLAN-I4); ecology and D4 habitat floor screened before designation; addresses assigned non-discretionarily as structures become lawful (PLAN-I16, PLAN-I22).
- **Redevelopment of occupied land.** Displacement floor delivered in kind (PLAN-I9) and occupancy survival (PLAN-I10) bind; the honest developer gets a predictable, non-confiscatory decision (PLAN-I7) once floor delivery is booked.
- **Down-zoning an owner's parcel.** Confiscation test on the residual-lawful-use band (PLAN-I7); below the band without a proportionate axis debit the decision is void; below the band with a booked proportionate axis debit it is a lawful confiscatory act and owner value-compensation routes to the Property/RENT instruments, never to the capability-floor reserve (PLAN-I9). Proportionality is contestable on an A6 route.
- **Relocation across the settlement line.** A replacement floor in an adjacent settlement counts only if it meets the comparable-reach standard (PLAN-I3a); a paper-compliant relocation concentrating the housing-floor debit on protected cohorts is still caught in the PLAN-I8 exclusionary book.
- **Cross-border address at a jurisdictional seam.** A parcel, structure or access point straddling a boundary resolves to exactly one register of record and one accountable steward under PLAN-I22; the coordination rule names them before the address binds, so notice, service and floor delivery never fall into the gap.
- **Renumbering a whole block.** Append-only, event-per-change; prior addresses remain resolvable for reliance and notice (PLAN-I14, PLAN-I15).
- **Emergency planning act.** An X3-class emergency may compress the contest window but SHALL NOT waive the housing-floor void rule (PLAN-I3) or the demolish-then-apologize bar (PLAN-I12); the natural-person bearer, the S4 audit event and post-hoc contest survive.
- **Informal / unaddressed occupation.** Lack of an address never defeats floor delivery (PLAN-I16); the registrar failure is the state's, not the occupant's.
- **Heritage claim racing a housing proposal.** PLAN-I13 sham test versus PLAN-I11 genuine screen resolves which flank prevails, on evidence and timing, not on assertion.

## UINT built-environment integrity and capture surface

### Purpose

This section is the integrity and anti-capture layer of the built-environment world-model. The prior sections (BLD building object and register, NET physical networks, CONN connection and disconnection floor, WORKS construction and lifecycle, PLAN planning and land-use) define the physical and place view of the world. This section enumerates the attack surface against those constructs and states, for each attack, the detection signal, the invariant that closes it, the enforcement hook, and the symmetric protection of the honest builder, operator and homeowner.

Two design commitments govern every invariant below. First, both flanks are protected on every contested point: the invariant that catches a phantom building, a falsified certificate, a captured facility, a coercive shutoff, a rigged rezoning, a run-to-failure asset, or a heritage erasure SHALL NOT become a weapon against an honest developer, a diligent operator, or a resident, and SHALL NOT let a false safety, heritage, or hardship claim be used to extort money or to block legitimate development. Second, this layer never re-models what neighbouring specs own. Title, control, encumbrance, the concentration ceiling and the housing/occupancy floor are Property (U1-I1 through U1-I9, U1-I8, P2, RENT, PROP-I5) and are cited by ID. The harm surface, the criminal and concealment classes and the perpetual concealment flip are A18. Regulated returns and essential-facility anti-abuse are C1-I7, C1-I16 and Energy F1/RES. The register mechanics are R1/R2. The capability floor machinery and theta are Distributive-Justice-and-Capability-Floors and COMP-I17. This section binds the physical facts of the built world to those owners; it does not restate their rules.

A booking discipline runs through the whole section. Where an integrity harm both misdirects money and inflicts a residual harm, the two rails are kept strictly apart: the misdirected or captured dollar value is recovered on the money rail through its own channel, and V4 anti-value books only the residual per-axis harm of the wrongful act. The dollar recovery and the V4 booking are never equated and never netted against each other, and V4 is never netted across axes.

### Object model

The integrity layer operates over objects defined elsewhere in this world-model and cites them:

- `built_object` (BLD): a structure or improvement with a register entry, a footprint, a controller by U1-I1, a condition record and a safety-certificate record.
- `network_asset` (NET): a physical pipe, cable, conduit, track, road, main or node (U3 in the flows spec), frequently an essential facility and a natural monopoly.
- `connection` (CONN): the live physical link between a `network_asset` and a `built_object` or occupant, and the disconnection event over it.
- `works_record` (WORKS): the construction, alteration, inspection and demolition lifecycle of a `built_object`.
- `plan_act` (PLAN): a zoning, rezoning, permit, variance or land-use decision by a planning steward.
- `condition_attestation`: a signed statement of structural condition or fitness, by a natural-person attester with a role and a stake.
- `safety_certificate`: a signed statement that a life-safety duty (structural, fire, gas, electrical, egress, water) is met, with an issuer, a scope and an expiry.
- `heritage_marker`: a register flag that a `built_object` carries protected heritage value.

Every integrity object resolves, at the point of enforcement, to at least one natural-person bearer. No autonomous agent, operator firm (O1), shell or concert party may hold a criminal-class harm, a floor breach or a concealed defect without a named natural person carrying it; the veil-piercing rule and the no-bearerless-harm guarantee are the single canonical O1 rules and are cited, not re-modelled.

### Invariants

#### Phantom and shell buildings and networks

**UINT-I1 (existence-attestation binding).** A `built_object` or `network_asset` register entry SHALL be backed by a physical-existence attestation that binds the register record to a located, inspectable physical fact (a surveyed footprint under P2, a sensed or witnessed structure, a metered live connection). Purpose: a register entry is an append-only claim about the world (R1), and a claim of a building or a main that does not physically exist is a fabrication. Decidable predicate: an entry whose most recent existence attestation is absent, expired, or contradicted by a footprint check (U1-I3) or an on-site sensing event is `existence_unverified`. An `existence_unverified` asset SHALL NOT be usable as the basis of a value booking, a collateral claim, a regulated-return base (C1-I7), or a harm-capacity escrow credit (C1-I16). Honest-flank: `existence_unverified` is a suspensive status that pauses value claims, not a finding of fraud; it clears on a passing attestation with no penalty, and a genuine asset temporarily unsensed (remote, newly built, sensor outage) SHALL be given a bounded cure window before any adverse booking.

**UINT-I2 (phantom-asset harm on two rails, with a natural-person bearer).** Registering, or maintaining on the register, a `built_object` or `network_asset` that is known not to exist, in order to draw a value booking, a subsidy, a regulated-return base, or collateral, is a criminal-class A18 harm. Purpose: a phantom asset is theft dressed as a register fact and cannot be cured by a payment alone. The two rails run separately. On the money rail, the wrongly-drawn value (the phantom subsidy, the phantom regulated-return base, the collateral advanced against nothing) SHALL be restituted as an ordinary money-rail claim with user-first restitution under C1-I7, routed back through the channel it was drawn from. On the anti-value rail, V4 anti-value SHALL be booked ONLY for the residual per-axis harm of the fraudulent act, on the trust-and-governance axis (and on the safety axis where the phantom masked a real hazard), never for the dollar magnitude drawn, never netted against the money rail, and never netted across axes. The dollar recovery books on the money rail, V4 books the residual harm per axis, and the two are never equated or netted. The A18 concealment flip applies: concealing the phantom is itself a violation and the clock on the concealed phantom never runs out. The harm resolves to the natural person who signed or directed the false entry, through the O1 veil-piercing rule if a shell or concert party interposed. Honest-flank: an honest register error, a demolished-but-not-yet-updated entry, or a good-faith double-count corrected by a new appended event (R1) is a correction, not a criminal-class harm, and SHALL NOT book V4 or trigger restitution beyond reversing the erroneous booking.

**UINT-I3 (built-but-unregistered duty capture).** A physical `built_object` occupied or in use SHALL carry the full safety, condition and connection duties of this layer whether or not it is registered, and a party that builds or operates unregistered to evade those duties SHALL be treated as bearing them in full plus the concealment flip for the evasion. Purpose: this closes the mirror of UINT-I1, the operator who stays off the register precisely to escape inspection and the housing-floor auto-registration (U1-I8). Detection signal: a sensed structure or a live metered connection (Energy F1-I9 metering-is-sensing) with no corresponding register entry. Enforcement: the state SHALL auto-open a register entry from the physical fact (A12 mandate), and the occupancy floor auto-registers as an occupancy_marker under U1-I8 regardless of the builder's omission, so that a resident is never stripped of the housing floor by the builder's evasion. Honest-flank: a resident of an unregistered structure SHALL NOT bear the builder's evasion penalty and SHALL retain the U1-I8 floor; the penalty falls on the party who chose non-registration to evade duty, not on the occupant.

#### Condition and safety-certificate falsification

**UINT-I4 (attester identity and stake).** Every `condition_attestation` and `safety_certificate` SHALL name a natural-person attester, the attester's role and competence basis, the scope attested, and the attester's stake, and SHALL be appended to the `built_object` record as an event (R1). Purpose: a safety certificate with no accountable signer is unfalsifiable and unenforceable. Decidable predicate: a certificate with no resolvable natural-person attester is void for reliance and SHALL NOT satisfy any occupancy, sale, or connection precondition. Honest-flank: naming the attester SHALL NOT expose the attester to liability for a latent defect outside the attested scope or undetectable by the competent method used; the stake attaches to what was attested, not to omniscience.

**UINT-I5 (certificate falsification is a concealment-class harm with a perpetual flip).** Falsifying, backdating, forging, or knowingly issuing against the evidence a `condition_attestation` or `safety_certificate`, to avoid a maintenance or repair cost or to enable a sale, occupancy or connection, is a concealment-class A18 harm. Purpose: a falsified safety certificate is the concealment of a life-safety defect and is exactly the case A18 reserves the perpetual flip for. Enforcement hook: the concealed defect's clock never runs out; the A18 concealment flip runs from discovery, not from issuance, so a defect papered over decades ago is live on the day it is found. The harm books as V4 anti-value on the safety axis with teeth (V4), never priced away, and resolves to the attester and to any natural person who directed the falsification. Honest-flank: an attester who recorded a defect truthfully, or who issued a correct certificate later invalidated by new damage or new knowledge, bears nothing; and A18-I34 suspensive-contest applies, so an accused attester may contest before an adverse booking bites.

**UINT-I6 (self-report privilege and the honest-remediation path).** An owner or operator who discovers a condition or safety defect and appends it truthfully, and who enters a bounded remediation, SHALL be shielded from the concealment flip for that defect and SHALL bear only the underlying condition duty, not a concealment harm. Purpose: to make honest disclosure strictly better than concealment, so the perpetual flip drives disclosure rather than deeper burial. Decidable predicate: a defect appended before any external discovery, with a remediation commitment, is `self-reported` and is outside the flip. Honest-flank (occupant/commons side): the shield covers the concealment harm only, not the duty to actually remediate; an owner cannot self-report forever without acting, and a self-reported life-safety defect that endangers occupants still triggers the CONN and BLD safety duties immediately.

#### Essential-facility capture

**UINT-I7 (essential-facility access non-degradation).** An operator of a `network_asset` that is an essential facility SHALL NOT degrade, delay, deny, or self-preference access, nor manufacture artificial scarcity, to extract rent or to advantage an affiliate. Purpose: the physical monopoly pipe is where a network operator can tax everyone downstream. Enforcement: this is the essential-facility anti-abuse case owned by Economy-and-Markets and Energy; returns are the C1-I7 attested-cost corridor with benchmark-cleared returns, zero analytic privilege and user-first restitution, and this section cites those rules rather than re-pricing access. Detection signal: an access-quality or availability metric for affiliated versus unaffiliated users that diverges beyond the benchmark, or a scarcity that the capacity record (kernel C4 infrastructure-as-capacity) does not physically justify. A confirmed degrade-to-extract books as V4 anti-value on the trust-and-governance axis for the residual harm, while the over-recovered rent is restituted user-first on the money rail under C1-I7; where it endangers supply it engages the harm-capacity escrow (C1-I16). Honest-flank: a genuine physical constraint, a documented outage, a maintenance window, or a capacity limit truthfully recorded is not capture; an operator SHALL be able to ration real scarcity by a transparent, non-self-preferencing rule without penalty, and SHALL NOT be forced to serve access that would breach the safety or resilience regime (RES).

**UINT-I8 (physical-versus-flow seam at the facility boundary).** Enforcement against an essential-facility operator under UINT-I7 SHALL distinguish the physical pipe (U3, this layer) from what flows through it (F1 energy, F3 logistics, F7 mobility, F9 communications and spectrum, owned by the flows spec). Purpose: to keep the built-environment layer enforcing pipe access and pipe capacity, and to route flow-pricing, flow-metering and flow-mastering (Energy F1-I9, F1-I10) to their owner. Decidable seam: a complaint about the physical ability to connect, the capacity of the conduit, or the condition of the asset resolves here; a complaint about the price, quantity or quality of the flow resolves in the flows spec. Honest-flank: an operator SHALL NOT be charged twice for one act under both layers; the seam assigns each harm to exactly one owner.

#### Disconnection as coercion

**UINT-I9 (disconnection floor for the floor-holder).** A utility or network shutoff SHALL NOT be used to defeat a capability floor or the housing/occupancy floor. Purpose: cutting water, heat, power or communications to a floor-holder converts an essential service into a weapon and voids the floor by physical means. Enforcement: disconnection of a service that a floor depends on is bounded by the CONN disconnection floor cited here; the essential service to a lawful occupant under U1-I8 SHALL be maintained through the COMP-I17 standing reserve on the occupant's own-account basis, computed and routed, never by commanding or suppressing the market price. A shutoff executed to coerce a floor-holder books as V4 anti-value and, where directed at the person's participation or dissent, engages A18. Honest-flank: this is not a licence for non-payment; the operator retains its full money-rail claim for the unpaid service, pursued through ordinary channels, with claim priority and lien ordering determined by U1-I4 and U1-I6, cited not asserted here; and the operator SHALL retain the right to disconnect for genuine physical safety (a gas leak, an unsafe installation) under the safety duty, since a safety disconnection protects the occupant rather than coercing them.

**UINT-I10 (disconnection as targeted coercion of a dissident).** Weaponizing a shutoff, a connection denial, or a manufactured service degradation against a person for their dissent, participation, or identity is a criminal-class A18 harm and SHALL NOT be priced. Purpose: the built environment SHALL NOT become an instrument of political or personal coercion. Detection signal: a disconnection or denial whose pattern tracks a protected activity rather than a physical or billing fact, detectable only at the S5 cohort grain (see UINT-I20). The harm resolves to the natural person who ordered it, through O1 veil-piercing if routed through a firm. Honest-flank: an operator acting on a neutral, uniformly applied physical or billing rule SHALL NOT be inferred into coercion from correlation alone; a coercion finding requires the targeting to be shown, not merely a disparate outcome, and the operator may invoke A18-I34 suspensive-contest.

#### Planning capture

**UINT-I11 (insider-enrichment rezoning, on two rails).** A `plan_act` that rezones, upzones, or grants a variance in a way that enriches the planning steward, an affiliate, or a concert party SHALL be void for that beneficiary. Purpose: rezoning creates location value (the RENT overlay's base) at the stroke of a pen, and a steward who steers that uplift to an insider is capturing the commons' value. The two rails run separately. On the money rail, the captured location-value uplift belongs to the commons under the RENT overlay and SHALL be restored to the commons through that overlay, cited not re-modelled here. On the anti-value rail, V4 anti-value SHALL be booked ONLY for the residual per-axis harm of the capture act, on the trust-and-governance axis, never for the magnitude of the uplift, never netted against the money rail, and never netted across axes. The dollar recovery of the uplift books on the money rail under RENT, V4 books the residual governance harm per axis, and the two are never equated or netted. Detection signal: a `plan_act` beneficiary who intersects the acting-in-concert graph (O1) of the deciding steward, or a parcel assembled ahead of a rezoning it was not publicly foreseeable from. Enforcement: the O1 concert graph and the single veil-piercing rule identify the beneficiary. Honest-flank: a developer who bought at a public price and benefits from a rule-based, generally-applicable, publicly-reasoned plan change is not an insider; capture requires the private steer, and a legitimate rezoning that happens to benefit a landowner is not void merely because someone gained.

**UINT-I12 (spot-zoning and exclusionary-zoning guard).** A `plan_act` SHALL NOT single out one parcel for a benefit or burden inconsistent with its surroundings absent a stated public reason (spot-zoning), and SHALL NOT use land-use rules whose effect is to exclude floor-holders or a protected cohort from a place (exclusionary zoning). Purpose: both are planning used to redistribute value or exclude persons under cover of neutral land-use. Enforcement hook: this engages the GOV level-capture guard, since a steward who quietly relaxes or tightens thresholds parcel-by-parcel in incumbents' favor is doing at retail what the level-capture guard forbids at wholesale. Exclusion that defeats the housing floor engages U1-I8. Honest-flank: genuine, reasoned, generally-applicable land-use distinctions (a hazard buffer, a heritage core, a use-separation for real externality) are legitimate planning and SHALL NOT be voided as spot-zoning merely because they apply to a single parcel that uniquely presents the condition.

**UINT-I13 (displacement-through-planning).** A `plan_act` that has the effect of displacing lawful occupants SHALL account for the occupancy floor (U1-I8) as a binding constraint, not a cost to be bought out. Purpose: planning SHALL NOT be a lawful-looking route around the housing floor that title enforcement (U1-I6 discipline) could not take directly. Enforcement: displacement that would breach U1-I8 is blocked or must deliver the floor in kind; the anti-value of an uncompensated displacement books as V4 on the affected axis, while owner value-compensation and any above-floor displacement value route to the Property/RENT value-balancing instruments (cited), never to the capability-floor reserve. Honest-flank: this is not a veto on all development near occupants; a plan that relocates the floor without breaching it, or that occupants consent to on their own-account basis, proceeds, and an occupant SHALL NOT use the floor as a hold-out to extract value beyond the floor (see UINT-I18).

#### Deferred maintenance and run-to-failure

**UINT-I14 (deferred-maintenance externality with escrow).** An operator of a `built_object` or `network_asset` SHALL NOT run the asset toward a collapse, blackout, or catastrophic failure while offloading the failure cost onto occupants, the commons, or downstream users. Purpose: deferred maintenance converts a private saving now into a public catastrophe later, the classic externality the state prices as a residual Pigou overlay. Enforcement hook: the ex-ante harm-capacity escrow floor (C1-I16) SHALL be posted against the modelled failure exposure, so the operator, not the public, holds the collapse cost in advance; running the asset down draws the escrow, not the occupants. Detection signal: a condition-trajectory (from appended condition attestations) crossing toward failure while maintenance spend falls, or a resilience-margin (RES) eroding below the posted floor. A failure caused by concealed deferral engages the UINT-I5 concealment flip. Honest-flank: an operator who maintains truthfully within a funded plan, or who documents a constraint and posts the escrow, bears no anti-value for the ordinary aging of an asset; deferral is the harm, aging is not, and an operator SHALL NOT be penalized for a failure genuinely outside the modelled and escrowed exposure.

**UINT-I15 (life-safety failure has a named bearer).** A collapse, fire, explosion, flood, or blackout of a `built_object` or `network_asset` that injures occupants or the public, where the cause traces to a maintenance, condition, or safety duty breach carried in order to save a cost, SHALL resolve to a named natural-person bearer under the O1 no-bearerless-harm guarantee and the O1 veil-piercing rule, and SHALL NOT terminate at an autonomous agent, a shell, or an insolvent operator firm. Purpose: the built-environment application of the no-bearerless-harm rule, attaching to the domain-specific safety-versus-cost trade, the choice to carry a life-safety risk to save a maintenance or compliance cost. The harm books as V4 anti-value on the life-safety axis and, where a duty was concealed, carries the A18 concealment flip. Honest-flank: a genuine act-of-nature or third-party cause beyond the duty, truthfully recorded and within the posted resilience and escrow, is not a duty breach, and the bearer rule attaches to the person who made the safety-versus-cost trade, not to every person in the ownership chain by mere association.

#### Heritage erasure

**UINT-I16 (heritage precaution and the demolish-then-apologize block).** A `built_object` carrying a `heritage_marker`, or credibly proposed for one, SHALL NOT be demolished, gutted, or irreversibly altered ahead of adjudication, and a demolition that pre-empts a pending heritage determination is a violation regardless of any later apology or fine offered. Purpose: heritage value is often irreversible, and the demolish-then-apologize move exploits the gap between a cheap ex-post penalty and an irreplaceable loss. Enforcement hook: this engages the D4 habitat/condition floor and the irreversibility precaution (Missions-Science-and-Ecology), cited here; the demolition emissions and embodied-value loss book under Energy F6, and a pre-emptive erasure books as V4 anti-value that a fine cannot net away. Honest-flank: a heritage marker SHALL NOT be a costless perpetual freeze; an owner facing a genuine safety hazard, an unfundable maintenance burden, or a spurious marker SHALL have a bounded, decidable path to determination and, on a negative finding, to proceed, and a `heritage_marker` asserted in bad faith to block development is itself subject to UINT-I18.

**UINT-I17 (heritage decision on the record, at cohort grain).** A heritage listing, delisting, or demolition consent SHALL be an appended, reasoned event on the `built_object` record (R1), citing the value asserted and the evidence. Purpose: heritage is a value claim on the commons and SHALL be as auditable as any other value booking, and both a wrongful erasure and a wrongful freeze must be traceable. Honest-flank: the reasoning requirement binds the state as much as the owner; a listing imposed without a stated, contestable basis is not a valid constraint on the owner.

#### Extortion, over-broad suspicion and the honest-party shield

**UINT-I18 (false safety, heritage, or hardship claim used to extort or block).** Asserting a safety defect, a heritage value, or an occupancy/hardship claim that the asserter knows to be false or manufactured, in order to extort a payment from or to block a legitimate development by an honest party, is itself a violation and SHALL book as V4 anti-value against the asserter on the trust-and-governance axis, with any extracted payment restituted on the money rail. Purpose: the both-flanks discipline requires that the tools protecting occupants and the commons cannot be inverted into a shakedown of the honest builder. Detection signal: a claim contradicted by the on-record condition, heritage, or occupancy evidence, or a claim withdrawn on payment. Where the false claim conceals its own falsity, the A18 concealment flip runs against the asserter. Honest-flank (the other direction): a good-faith safety, heritage, or hardship claim that turns out mistaken is not extortion; only the knowingly-false or manufactured claim books anti-value, and a genuine occupant, neighbour, or objector SHALL be able to raise a concern without risk of being recharacterized as an extortionist.

**UINT-I19 (proportionate, curable enforcement, no collective punishment).** An adverse status under this section (`existence_unverified`, a suspended certificate, a capture finding, a deferral finding) SHALL be specific to the asset and the responsible natural person, curable where the underlying fact is curable, and SHALL NOT be extended by association to an honest party's other assets, an occupant of the offending asset, or a whole class of builders or operators. Purpose: to keep integrity enforcement surgical and to prevent an over-broad suspicion regime that chills legitimate development and punishes residents for their landlord's or operator's conduct. Enforcement discipline: mirrors U1-I6 enforcement discipline for title, applied to physical status. Honest-flank (both directions): the occupant is never punished for the owner's breach (their U1-I8 floor and CONN connection survive), and the honest operator's clean assets are never tainted by one bad asset; A18-I34 suspensive-contest applies before any adverse booking bites.

#### Sensing, register faith and the title seam

**UINT-I20 (state sensing at cohort grain).** State detection of the harms in this section (targeting patterns, degrade-to-extract, deferral trajectories, phantom entries) SHALL operate at the S5 cohort or k-anonymity grain: population-level anomalies surface at that cohort grain and SHALL NOT resolve to an individual occupant, meter, or address. Per-household resolution, confirmation, and remedy of a specific breach proceeds ONLY through the S2/S3 access-contract path with S4 audit, or through an S6 zero-knowledge predicate where a yes/no over a threshold suffices; there is no other route from a cohort anomaly to a named household. Purpose: the integrity regime must not become a pervasive surveillance of who lives where and consumes what, and this invariant is the canonical cohort-grain sensing rule that CONN-I6 reconciles to. Honest-flank: cohort-grain sensing protects residents and honest operators from a fishing-expedition, while the S2/S3/S4 path still lets a specific, audited, justified investigation reach a specific asset when a signal warrants it; neither the occupant's privacy nor the investigator's legitimate reach is sacrificed.

**UINT-I21 (public-faith reliance on the physical register).** A good-faith party who relies on the R1 public-faith projection of a `built_object` or `network_asset` record (its existence, its current certificate, its heritage status, its connection) SHALL be protected under R1-I28, and a correction of that record SHALL be a new appended event, never a silent retroactive edit. Purpose: the physical register is only useful if people can act on it; the phantom-asset and falsification harms above fall on the falsifier, not on the good-faith relying buyer, lender, or occupant. Post-judgment discipline: asset discovery against a party found liable under this section is post-judgment only, per R1-I39, so an integrity investigation does not become a pre-judgment ransack of an operator's holdings. Honest-flank: the falsifier cannot hide behind public-faith (their own bad entry does not protect them), and the good-faith relying party cannot be dispossessed to remedy a fraud they did not commit; the remedy runs against the wrongdoer's own value, not the innocent relier's.

**UINT-I22 (physical-versus-title seam).** A finding under this section about the physical fact of a `built_object` or `network_asset` (its existence, condition, safety, connection, heritage, capacity) SHALL NOT by itself alter title, control, encumbrance, the concentration ceiling, or the housing floor, which are owned by Property (U1-I1 through U1-I9). Purpose: the both-flanks and no-re-model discipline at the layer boundary; a safety or integrity finding informs but does not silently rewrite ownership. Decidable seam: a physical finding routes an occupancy-floor consequence to U1-I8, an enforcement-discipline question to U1-I6, an encumbrance or lien-ordering question to U1-I4, and a succession question to PROP-I5, and takes none of them into this layer. Honest-flank: an owner does not lose title to a defect (the defect is cured or the harm booked against the responsible person), and an occupant's floor is not weakened by a physical finding against the structure; each consequence lands with its correct owner.

### Protocols

**Existence-verification cycle.** For each registered `built_object` and `network_asset`, the state SHALL maintain a rolling existence attestation (UINT-I1). On a lapse, the asset enters `existence_unverified`, value claims pause, a bounded cure window opens, and the controller (U1-I1) is notified. A passing attestation clears the status with no penalty; a window that closes unverified escalates to an investigation at cohort grain (UINT-I20) before any UINT-I2 phantom finding. A confirmed phantom books on two rails per UINT-I2: money-rail restitution of the wrongly-drawn value under C1-I7 and V4 for the residual governance/safety harm, never netted together.

**Certificate lifecycle.** A `safety_certificate` is issued by a named attester (UINT-I4), appended to the record (R1), carries a scope and expiry, and blocks occupancy, sale, or connection preconditions when absent or void. Discovery of falsification runs the UINT-I5 concealment protocol from the discovery date, with A18-I34 suspensive-contest available to the accused attester before the V4 booking bites. A self-report (UINT-I6) reroutes the same defect out of the flip and into the ordinary remediation path.

**Capture and coercion complaint.** A complaint against an essential-facility operator is first sorted at the physical-versus-flow seam (UINT-I8): pipe, capacity and condition here; price, quantity and quality to the flows spec. A pipe-side complaint tests access non-degradation (UINT-I7) against the C1-I7 corridor and the capacity record. A disconnection complaint tests the CONN floor and UINT-I9/UINT-I10, with the coercion path resolving to a natural person via O1, the operator's money-rail claim preserved and priced/ordered per U1-I4 and U1-I6.

**Planning-integrity review.** A `plan_act` is screened against the O1 concert graph (UINT-I11), the spot/exclusionary tests (UINT-I12), and the displacement-floor test (UINT-I13), and every heritage decision is an appended reasoned event (UINT-I17). A confirmed insider-enrichment rezoning restores the location-value uplift to the commons on the money rail under RENT and books V4 only for the residual governance harm (UINT-I11), the two never netted. The GOV level-capture guard backstops any pattern of parcel-by-parcel threshold relaxation.

### Lifecycle and edge cases

- Demolished-but-registered: a genuine demolition (WORKS) is a correction event (R1), not a UINT-I2 phantom; the phantom harm requires knowledge and value-drawing intent.
- Newly built, not yet registered: the built-but-unregistered duty (UINT-I3) attaches from occupation or use, and the U1-I8 floor auto-registers for the occupant regardless of the builder's omission.
- Sensor outage or remote asset: `existence_unverified` gives a cure window (UINT-I1) rather than an immediate adverse booking.
- Latent defect outside attested scope: the attester's stake (UINT-I4) does not reach it; the UINT-I5 flip requires knowledge against the evidence.
- Act-of-nature failure: within the posted escrow and resilience margin (C1-I16, RES) it is not a UINT-I14 deferral nor a UINT-I15 duty breach.
- Spurious heritage or safety claim: UINT-I18 books anti-value against the false asserter and restitutes any extracted payment on the money rail, while UINT-I16/UINT-I17 still protect a genuine marker; the two are separated by knowledge and evidence, not by outcome.
- Two-rail booking: for every capture or fraud harm in this section (UINT-I2, UINT-I7, UINT-I11, UINT-I18) the dollar recovery runs on the money rail through its own channel and V4 books only the residual per-axis harm; the two are never equated or netted, and V4 is never netted across axes.
- Contested finding: A18-I34 suspensive-contest precedes every adverse V4 booking under UINT-I19.

### Open questions

- The cure-window length for `existence_unverified` (UINT-I1) should be calibrated per asset class (a remote main versus an occupied dwelling) rather than fixed globally; the calibration owner is left to the register mandate (A12).
- The threshold at which an access-quality divergence (UINT-I7) crosses from tolerable physical variance into a self-preferencing finding needs a benchmark owned jointly with C1-I7; this section states the rule, not the numeric corridor.
- Whether a heritage precaution freeze (UINT-I16) should carry a commons-funded holding cost during adjudication, to keep the honest owner whole while the irreversibility precaution runs, is deferred to the D4 owner.
- The exact evidentiary bar separating a knowingly-false extortive claim (UINT-I18) from a good-faith mistaken one is stated qualitatively here and should be sharpened with the courts layer.
