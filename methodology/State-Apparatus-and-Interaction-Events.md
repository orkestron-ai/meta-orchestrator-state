## 0. How the state-apparatus and events layer works

This is the layer where public power actually touches a person and a thing, and it is the sharpest capture surface in the whole model: the state weaponizing its own law, office, permit, register, or emergency against a citizen. The layer's first commitment is that **public power is always bearered, audited, and gated**: every exercise of authority is a K2 act that resolves to a natural-person bearer, books the loudest S4 audit, is bounded by the inviolable A9 rights floor, and routes any coercion through the single A18-I4 gate. No act of the state escapes a human who answers for it, and no coercion happens on a secret, retroactive, vague, or bearer-less basis. Both flanks throughout: the citizen is protected from arbitrary, secret, captured, or weaponized power, and the honest official and the genuine emergency are protected from paralysis and from a false accusation.

**Law in force (LAW, A5).** The enacted law is the output of the A10 process, held in an append-only statute book from which the law-as-of-a-past-time is a fold. A law binds only from lawful publication (a secret or back-dated law grounds no coercion), conforms up the norm hierarchy to the Charter (a rights-breaching clause is void ab initio and the A18-I4 gate refuses it on a facial showing), is never retroactive, and is precise enough to conform to. A mandatory contribution is value-balancing not taxation: its magnitude tracks a measured corridor deviation and cannot exceed a proportionate ceiling, and its enforcement against a non-payer routes through the coercion gate and never gates a floor. A coercive regulation carries a hard sunset and an independent review, and lapses if the review is not genuinely completed, so coercive power does not accrete unexamined.

**Public office and permits (OFFICE, AUTH, A11, A12, A14).** An office is a position held in trust, distinct from the natural person who holds it and acts as it on the dual-attributed record; conflict declarations are public at person resolution, abuse of office is reachable through the veil, a good-faith in-mandate act enjoys a bounded functional immunity, and a caretaker holding hard-lapses so a vacancy never becomes a standing power. A mandated register is kept by an independent registrar; a permit is a governed, timely, reasoned, contestable decision whose deemed outcome on a missed deadline is borne by the defaulting officeholder, never a bearer-less act, and where a permit gates a capability floor the activity is provisionally permitted so the floor is never cut before contest.

**Encounters, incidents and phenomena (XACT, INCIDENT, AINT, X2-X5).** An encounter is co-owned by its parties and default-private, and a state encounter cannot self-label consent (a stop or search is coerced by construction and routes through the gate). An incident's severity is a function of hazard facts, and the emergency power binds onto the RES regime, is bounded, time-boxed, individually contestable, and never touches an A9 right (a delivered capability floor may be only proportionately and compensably limited), while a manufactured emergency is a concealment-class harm. A phenomenon is an indicator not a truth, sensed at cohort grain, suppressed when a series thins below the k floor. The integrity surface closes the secret and retroactive law, abuse of office and state capture, register and regulatory capture, the manufactured emergency and the emergency ratchet, surveillance dressed as statistics, the weaponized permit, and the unaccountable automated act, all on both flanks and with the capture signal grounding an institutional remedy unless individualized attribution to a named person is proven.

Three commitments recur, matching the rest of the model:

1. **Public power is bearered, audited, and gated.** Every state act resolves to a natural-person bearer, books the S4 loud audit, and routes any coercion through the single A18-I4 gate; a bearer-less or secret act is void.
2. **The rights floor is absolute, the capability floor is delivered.** No law, office act, permit, or emergency ever touches an A9 inviolable right; a floor-gating act is blocked before breach rather than remedied after, and a delivered capability floor may be only proportionately, temporarily, and compensably limited.
3. **Both flanks, everywhere.** The citizen is protected from arbitrary, secret, captured, weaponized, or surveilling power, and the honest official, the honest registrar, and the genuine emergency are protected from paralysis, hindsight liability, and a false capture or abuse accusation.

The sections follow: LAW law and regulation in force; OFFICE public office and officials; AUTH registry mandates, permits and authorizations; XACT encounters, interactions and situations; INCIDENT incidents, emergencies and phenomena; AINT apparatus and interaction integrity and capture.

## LAW law and regulation in force (A5)

### Purpose

A5 is the enacted law, regulation, policy, and incentive as they stand IN FORCE: the output objects that the A10 lawmaking process (bill to enactment) produces, not the process itself (A10 is cited, never re-modelled here). This section models the law-in-force object and the statute book that holds it, the conditions under which an in-force norm actually binds a person and can ground coercion, and the standing instruments (mandatory contribution, incentive, delegation) that a norm may carry. It protects both flanks at once. The citizen is protected from secret, retroactive, vague, or rights-breaching law, from law weaponized to gate a right through a fiscal or incentive disguise, from an unbounded or punitive compelled contribution, from a stale coercive rule that outlives its warrant, and from a coercive machine encoding that no named person ever adopted. The polity, and the honest official who legislates inside the hierarchy, retain the full lawful power to legislate, regulate, incentivize, and compel a genuine rebalancing contribution, and are protected from a paralysing or unfounded voidness accusation. Enacting, amending, or repealing a norm is an exercise of public authority: it is a K2 act, it resolves to a NATURAL-PERSON bearer, and it books the loud S4 audit like every other state act; any coercion built on an A5 norm routes through the single A18-I4 gate.

### Object model

- law_in_force: an enacted norm currently in force, carrying an authoritative human-readable master text, a norm_version identity, an in-force interval, a rank in the norm hierarchy, a provenance reference to the completed A10 enactment, and a publication_event reference. Where coercive, it carries a review_clause and, per LAW-I9, a sunset. The law-in-force object is the legal characterization; the R1 event that recorded it is the holder of record.
- regulation: a subordinate norm made under a delegation from a higher law_in_force; same object shape, plus a delegation reference and, where coercive, a review_clause and a sunset (LAW-I9).
- policy: a non-coercive directive of a Ministry or sub-agent (A3) that steers value within a corridor but SHALL NOT of itself define an offense or ground coercion; a policy that purports to coerce is void as ultra vires and is re-characterized as an attempted regulation subject to the full A5 tests.
- incentive_instrument: a value-balancing signal (a bonus, a rebate, a corridor-nudge) attached to an A5 norm, bounded and non-priceable-floor-safe (LAW-I8, LAW-I15).
- mandatory_contribution_instrument: a compelled value transfer framed as corridor rebalancing, not taxation, carrying a named value dimension, a stated corridor target, a rebalancing rationale, a magnitude that tracks the measured corridor deviation, and an explicit proportionate ceiling (LAW-I7). MOS has no taxation and no general revenue levy.
- delegation: a bounded grant of norm-making or coercive authority from one norm or office to another, carrying an explicit scope, a ceiling, and a no-sub-delegation-of-breach limit (LAW-I10).
- norm_version: the time-indexed, versioned identity of a norm's text and effect, so that the norm-set binding at any past instant is reconstructible (the contemporaneous norm-set A6 adjudicates against).
- machine_encoding: an optional machine-readable rendering of a norm (LegalRuleML, ODRL, Akoma Ntoso) that MAY self-execute only a non-coercive, non-rights-affecting evaluation, subordinate to the human master on divergence (LAW-I6).
- publication_event: the appended R1 event that lawfully published a norm; it names the natural-person publication bearer, and its timestamp opens the binding interval (LAW-I3, LAW-I11).
- statute_book: the law-in-force register, an R1 instance under an A12 registry mandate, append-only, the single holder of record for all A5 objects. The current body of law is a non-authoritative fold over its appended events; the law-as-of-a-time query is that fold evaluated at an instant.

### Invariants

- LAW-I1 (the statute book is an R1 instance; the current law is a fold). The statute book SHALL be an append-only R1 register under an A12 registry mandate, the single holder of record for every A5 object. Every enactment, amendment, repeal, republication, or correction SHALL be a new appended X1 event; no in-force text is ever mutated or deleted in place. The current body of law is a non-authoritative fold, and the law-as-of-time-T query is that fold evaluated at T (the R1 mechanics and R1-I28 public-faith projection are cited, not re-modelled). Purpose: a norm that was once in force can always be reconstructed for contemporaneous adjudication, and no norm can be silently rewritten. Predicate: for every visible law_in_force object there exists an appended publication_event and an unbroken event chain to the current state. Enforcement hook: a register whose current state cannot be re-derived from its appended events fails the A12 mandate audit.

- LAW-I2 (norm-hierarchy conformance; a rights-breaching law is void). A law_in_force SHALL conform to the A1 constitution, which SHALL conform to the A0 Charter (the norm hierarchy and Charter are cited, not re-modelled). A norm, or any severable part of it, that breaches an A9 inviolable right or a non-priceable capability floor (COMP-I17) is void ab initio: it never entered force, binds no one, and grounds no coercion, regardless of its A10 provenance or its publication. Purpose: enactment cannot launder a rights breach into binding law. Predicate: rank(norm) is defined and conformance to every higher-ranked norm holds; no clause maps to an A9 breach or a floor gate. Enforcement hook: an A6 court declares the offending part void and refuses any coercion built on it (mirroring the A18-I7 refusal of a void offense definition), the A18-I4 gate refuses at once on a facial showing per LAW-I19, and the voidness attaches no jeopardy bar. Both flanks: a merely unpopular or contested law is NOT void, only a hierarchy or rights breach is; the polity keeps full power to legislate up to that limit.

- LAW-I3 (publication is a precondition of bindingness; fail-closed). A norm binds a person, and may ground coercion against that person, ONLY from the instant of its lawful publication_event in the statute book. A secret, unpublished, back-dated, or not-yet-published norm does not bind and SHALL NOT ground any coercion, exactly fail-closed as the A18-I4 unpublished-function cap bars a hard rung on an unpublished function. Purpose: no person is bound by a law they could not have known, and the state cannot manufacture a secret basis for coercion. Predicate: binds(norm, person, t) requires publication_event.timestamp <= t. Enforcement hook: an enforcement organ that computes a coercion rung on an unpublished norm is refused at the A18-I4 gate; the S4 audit records the attempt. Both flanks: publication binds the citizen going forward AND protects the honest official, whose lawfully published regulation is fully binding from its stamp.

- LAW-I4 (no retroactive criminalization or worsening). A norm SHALL NOT retroactively define conduct as an offense, increase a penalty, or worsen a person's position for a completed act (nulla poena sine lege praevia). Adjudication of any past act uses the CONTEMPORANEOUS norm-set in force at act-time, per A6 (cited, not re-modelled), reconstructed via the norm_version fold of LAW-I1. Purpose: a person is judged by the law that existed when they acted, never by later law. Predicate: for coercion on an act at time t_act, the governing norm_version.in-force interval contains t_act. Enforcement hook: an A6 court applies the act-time norm_version and refuses any coercion resting on a later-enacted worsening. Both flanks: this bars retroactive punishment of the citizen AND does not freeze lawmaking, since a purely prospective new norm and an evidence-based revaluation under contemporaneous norms both remain lawful.

- LAW-I5 (lex certa; cited to A18-I7). A rule that grounds coercion SHALL satisfy the definiteness precondition of A18-I7 (cited, not restated); A5 keeps no parallel void-for-vagueness test. Purpose: definiteness is owned in one place so a coercive A5 rule and an A18 offense definition are held to a single standard. Enforcement hook: the A6 court and the A18 classifier apply A18-I7, refusing classification and coercion on any coercive rule that fails it. Both flanks: the citizen can always challenge an indeterminate coercive edge under A18-I7, AND a precise-enough honest rule, and any genuinely open-textured non-coercive policy standard, are not touched.

- LAW-I6 (machine encoding self-executes only non-coercively; the human master governs). A norm MAY carry a machine_encoding (LegalRuleML, ODRL, Akoma Ntoso). The encoding MAY self-execute ONLY a non-coercive, non-rights-affecting evaluation. Any coercive or rights-affecting consequence SHALL require a named natural person to ADOPT the act per AINT-I2 (cited) and route through the A18-I4 gate before effect; self-execution never supplies a bearer, and a purported coercive or rights-affecting self-execution with no adopting natural person is void per LAW-I11. The authoritative human-readable master text SHALL govern on any divergence between master and encoding, mirroring O5-I11. Purpose: automation is enabled for routine non-coercive conformance without letting an encoding bug, or the mere fact of automation, ground coercion that no accountable person ever adopted. Predicate: every machine_encoding references exactly one master norm_version; every coercive or rights-affecting effect resolves to an adopting natural person and an A18-I4 gate pass; on a detected master-encoding divergence the master value is used. Enforcement hook: a coercion or rights-affecting effect computed from a self-executed encoding with no adopting natural person, or from an encoding that diverges from its master, is refused at the A18-I4 gate; the divergence is a correctable defect booked as a new event, never a fait accompli. Both flanks: citizens and agents may rely on the encoding for routine non-coercive conformance, AND no one is bound by a coercive encoding artifact that no named person adopted or that the master text does not support.

- LAW-I7 (mandatory contribution is bounded value-balancing, not taxation). A compelled value transfer under an A5 norm SHALL be a corridor-rebalancing instrument tied to a named value dimension owned by a Ministry (A3), with a stated corridor target and a public rebalancing rationale; MOS has no taxation and no general revenue levy exists. The instrument SHALL pass a decidable severability test that operationalizes the no-taxation rule: (a) its magnitude SHALL track the measured deviation of the named dimension from its corridor and SHALL NOT exceed that deviation, so a transfer whose amount is invariant to the corridor state is an ultra vires general levy and is void; and (b) it SHALL carry an explicit magnitude ceiling proportionate to the corridor gap and SHALL NOT be confiscatory or punitive, so a disguised punitive levy is re-characterized by substance and flips to the concealment class (mirroring the manufactured-emergency pattern). Purpose: the compulsion to contribute is disciplined by an explicit, measured value corridor and a proportionate ceiling, not by open-ended fiscal appetite or a hidden penalty. Predicate: every mandatory_contribution_instrument names its value dimension, its corridor, its A10 provenance, a magnitude function of the measured corridor deviation, and a proportionate ceiling; magnitude <= measured deviation <= ceiling. Enforcement hook: a compelled transfer with no named dimension or corridor, or invariant to the corridor state, or above its proportionate ceiling, is ultra vires and void; a punitive-in-substance transfer flips to the concealment class; its collection is an unlawful state act booked in the S4 audit. Both flanks: the polity keeps the full lawful power to compel a genuine, measured, ceilinged rebalancing contribution, AND the citizen is protected from a disguised, unbounded, or punitive exaction.

- LAW-I8 (incentive instruments are bounded and floor-safe). An incentive_instrument SHALL be bounded in magnitude and duration, tied to a named corridor, and SHALL NOT of itself define an offense or ground coercion. Purpose: incentives steer value without becoming a coercive or capture instrument. Predicate: the instrument has an explicit ceiling, an expiry or review point, and no coercion rung. Enforcement hook: an unbounded or perpetual incentive with no review fails the A5 audit and is treated as unreviewed regulation under LAW-I9. Both flanks: the state may reward and nudge freely within bounds, AND no incentive may become a permanent, un-sunsetted transfer that entrenches an interest.

- LAW-I9 (fail-closed sunset and independent mandatory review of every coercive norm). Every coercive norm, primary law_in_force and regulation alike, SHALL carry a mandatory review_clause with a defined review point, and the sunset of a coercive regulation is a SHALL with a fail-closed default horizon: a coercive regulation with no set sunset lapses at a conservative default horizon and grounds no further coercion until A10 sets the class-specific horizon, and continuation requires an affirmative A10 re-enactment, never a self-review. The mandatory review SHALL be conducted or ratified by an organ INDEPENDENT of the office that authored the norm, and SHALL reach a reasoned, published finding of continued warrant and proportionality; an un-completed or non-independent review at the review point suspends the norm by the LAW-I1 fold, grounding no further coercion until a compliant review completes. For primary coercive law, the sunset MAY be replaced by a reasoned, published A10 or A13 exemption plus a periodic legislative re-affirmation, but the independent review_clause still binds. Purpose: coercive rule keeps living only by renewed, independently verified democratic warrant, and no office marks its own homework. Predicate: coercive(norm) implies review_clause present with a set review_point and an independent reviewer; a coercive regulation additionally has a sunset or, for primary law, an exemption with a re-affirmation schedule. Enforcement hook: a coercive regulation past its sunset with no A10 re-enactment lapses by the fold; a coercive norm past its review_point with no completed independent review is suspended by the fold; either grounds no further coercion. Both flanks: sunset and independent review protect the citizen from stale, self-perpetuating coercive rules, AND the review-and-re-enact path protects the polity's genuine ongoing need to keep a working coercive norm in force.

- LAW-I10 (delegation is bounded; no sub-delegation of a breach). A delegation SHALL carry an explicit scope and ceiling and SHALL NOT confer more authority than the delegator itself lawfully holds. A delegate SHALL NOT make a regulation that breaches an A9 right or a non-priceable floor, and SHALL NOT further sub-delegate any authority the delegating norm did not expressly permit; no chain of delegation can authorize what the top of the chain could not (nemo dat quod non habet). Purpose: subordinate rule-making cannot exceed or launder the primary norm's limits. Predicate: for each delegation, scope(delegate) is a subset of scope(delegator) and contains no A9 breach and no unpermitted sub-delegation. Enforcement hook: a regulation outside its delegation scope is ultra vires and void (LAW-I2 voidness applies); an A6 court refuses coercion on it. Both flanks: delegation lets the state administer at scale AND cannot become an escape hatch around the hierarchy.

- LAW-I11 (the publication act resolves to a natural-person bearer; collective authorship is traced through A10). Enacting, amending, republishing, or repealing an A5 object is a K2 act, and the determinate NATURAL-PERSON bearer requirement attaches to the PUBLICATION / promulgation act: a determinate officeholder accountable for correct publication SHALL be resolved and the loud S4 audit event appended to the statute book for every such change. This publication bearer is DISTINCT from authorship of the norm's content, which for an ordinary statute is a collective A10 act traced through A10's own vote record (cited, not re-modelled); a statute enacted by collective vote is NOT malformed for lacking a single content-author. Purpose: no norm enters or leaves force with an anonymous publication, while collective lawmaking is accountable through its vote record rather than forced into a fictional single author. Predicate: every statute_book event carries a resolved natural-person publication bearer and an office reference; content authorship resolves to the A10 vote record where the norm was collectively enacted. Enforcement hook: a statute_book event with no resolved natural-person publication bearer is malformed and is not a valid in-force change (self-execution never supplies this bearer, LAW-I6). Both flanks: the citizen can trace who promulgated the law that binds them and, through the A10 record, who voted for it, AND the honest official's lawful publication is recorded as lawful, protecting them from a later false accusation.

- LAW-I12 (provenance to a completed A10 process; fail-closed). A law_in_force object is a valid, binding norm ONLY if it is traceable to a completed A10 enactment (the A10 process is cited, not re-modelled). A purported law with no valid A10 provenance does not bind and grounds no coercion, fail-closed. Purpose: only the democratic process, not administrative fiat or a forged register entry, produces binding law. Predicate: binds(norm) requires a resolvable, completed A10 enactment reference. Enforcement hook: a statute_book row lacking A10 provenance is treated as void on challenge, and the A18-I4 gate refuses at once on a facial provenance defect per LAW-I19; coercion built on it is refused. Both flanks: this blocks fabricated or bypass law AND affirms that every properly enacted A10 output is fully in force.

- LAW-I13 (amendment and repeal by new event; the prior text survives). An amendment or repeal SHALL be a new appended event that supersedes the prior norm_version prospectively; it SHALL NOT erase the superseded text, whose in-force interval remains queryable for contemporaneous adjudication (LAW-I1, LAW-I4). Purpose: the law can change while the past remains judgeable by the law that then held. Predicate: after a repeal at t_r, the repealed norm_version.in-force interval ends at t_r and is still reconstructible for acts before t_r. Enforcement hook: a repeal that removes the historical text from the fold fails the A12 mandate audit. Both flanks: the polity may freely amend and repeal AND no one loses the ability to be judged by the law in force when they acted.

- LAW-I14 (genuine accessibility of the published law). Lawful publication under LAW-I3 SHALL make the norm genuinely retrievable by the persons it binds, in both the human-readable master and, where present, its machine encoding, without a barrier that defeats access. Purpose: publication is a real precondition of bindingness, not a formality satisfied by an inaccessible or paywalled deposit that functions as a secret law. Predicate: the published norm is retrievable through the public statute-book projection at zero access barrier. Enforcement hook: a norm whose only publication is effectively inaccessible is treated as unpublished for LAW-I3, so it does not bind and grounds no coercion. Both flanks: the citizen can actually find the law that binds them, AND the state that publishes accessibly gets full binding effect.

- LAW-I15 (no floor gating by fiscal or incentive disguise). A mandatory_contribution_instrument, an incentive_instrument, a fee, or any A5 instrument that de facto conditions access to a non-priceable A9 right or a delivered capability floor (COMP-I17) on payment, forbearance, or compliance is caught as a floor breach and is void under LAW-I2, whatever fiscal or incentive form it wears. Purpose: the non-priceable floor cannot be gated indirectly through the contribution or incentive rails when it cannot be gated directly. Predicate: an instrument whose non-payment or non-compliance withdraws a floor good is a floor gate. Enforcement hook: an A6 court re-characterizes the instrument by substance, voids the gating effect, and, where the gating was designed to seize a coercive lever, routes it to the concealment class with the perpetual flip (mirroring the manufactured-emergency pattern). Both flanks: genuine bounded contributions and incentives remain fully lawful AND cannot be engineered into a toll on a right.

- LAW-I16 (conflict resolution: lex superior cited, equal-rank tie-break A5-owned, resolve() total). Lex superior is the Charter norm-hierarchy supremacy (A0 / A1 / A9, cited, not restated): no lower-ranked norm, however specific or late, ever overrides a higher-ranked one. Among norms of EQUAL rank, resolution SHALL apply, in order: lex specialis (the more specific governs the general), then lex posterior (the later-published governs the earlier). For norms fully tied on rank, specificity, and publication time, resolve() SHALL route to A6 for a narrowing construction, or where the conflicting enactment states its own ordering that stated ordering governs, so that resolve() is a total, deterministic function. Purpose: conflicting law yields a single decidable answer without letting an ordinary law override the constitution or the Charter, and no residue of ties is left undecided. Predicate: resolve(n1, n2) is a total function on the ordered tuple (rank via the cited hierarchy, specificity, publication time, then A6 narrowing or the enactment's stated ordering). Enforcement hook: an A6 court applies the ordering and refuses any resolution that would let a subordinate norm defeat a superior one. Both flanks: the citizen and the official both get a predictable answer AND the hierarchy's supremacy is never traded away by a specificity or recency argument.

- LAW-I17 (retained legislative power; presumption of validity rebuttable at the gate). Nothing in A5 SHALL be read to paralyse lawful lawmaking: the polity retains its full power to enact, amend, regulate, incentivize, and compel a genuine contribution within the norm hierarchy, and an official who legislates inside the hierarchy, publishes lawfully, and traces to a completed A10 process is protected from an unfounded voidness, indefiniteness, or retroactivity accusation. An enactment that satisfies LAW-I2, LAW-I3, LAW-I5, LAW-I12, and A18-I7 is presumptively valid and binding. That presumption is rebuttable AT THE A18-I4 GATE by a facial A9 or floor-breach showing, or a facial provenance or publication defect, per LAW-I19, and not only by a completed A6 judgment, so the non-priceable floor binds before litigation rather than after. Purpose: the disciplines above bind arbitrary and captured power, not honest governance, yet the floor is never left exposed while a challenge waits for judgment. Predicate: a challenge that cannot point to a specific hierarchy breach, indefiniteness, retroactivity, publication defect, or provenance defect fails, and the norm stands; a facial floor-breach showing at the gate suspends coercion pending an expedited A6 ruling. Enforcement hook: an unfounded challenge is dismissed and the norm stands; a facial floor-breach showing triggers LAW-I19 gate suspension. Both flanks: the citizen keeps every ground to strike a bad law, and to stop a facial floor breach at the gate, AND the honest polity keeps every power to make a good one.

- LAW-I18 (coercive collection of a mandatory contribution routes through the single gate and never breaches the floor). Any coercive enforcement of collection of a mandatory_contribution_instrument against a non-paying person SHALL route through the single A18-I4 gate (cited, fail-closed until A10 calibrates), resolve to a determinate natural-person bearer, and book the loud S4 audit; it SHALL be individually contestable under A18-I34. Such collection SHALL NOT breach or gate a COMP-I17 capability floor or an A9 inviolable right; where the contributor is floor-exposed, the collection SHALL be waived or deferred per AUTH-I12 (cited) before any breach, since a floor breach is never discharged by compensation. Purpose: compelled collection is gated coercion held to the same single threshold and floor limit as every other coercive act, not a self-executing seizure. Predicate: every coercive collection act has an A18-I4 gate pass, a resolved natural-person bearer, an S4 audit entry, an A18-I34 contest path, and no floor or A9 breach; a floor-exposed contributor triggers AUTH-I12 waiver or deferral. Enforcement hook: a collection act with no gate pass, no bearer, or that would cut a floor is refused at the gate and booked in the S4 audit; a floor-breaching collection is blocked before breach, not remedied after. Both flanks: the polity may lawfully compel a genuine contribution and enforce it through the gate, AND the non-payer keeps the single-gate, contestable, floor-protected treatment of any coerced person.

- LAW-I19 (facial voidness determined at the gate, not only after litigation). The A18-I4 gate SHALL fail closed and refuse coercion where the coercing organ or the target raises a FACIAL A9 or capability-floor (COMP-I17) breach, or a facial provenance or publication defect in the grounding norm, suspending the coercion under A18-I34 pending an expedited A6 voidness ruling (the A6 courts and the A18 gate are cited, not re-modelled). Purpose: the non-priceable floor and the publication and provenance preconditions bind at the moment of coercion, so a facially void basis cannot be enforced first and litigated later. Predicate: a facial A9 or floor breach, or a facial LAW-I3 publication or LAW-I12 provenance defect, raised at the gate suspends coercion pending A6; only a completed A6 ruling lifts or confirms the suspension. Enforcement hook: the gate refuses the rung, books the S4 audit, and routes to the expedited A6 voidness track; a bare presumption of validity does not override a facial floor-breach showing. Both flanks: the citizen and the honest coercing organ can both stop a facially void basis at the gate, AND a merely speculative or non-facial challenge does not suspend coercion, so genuine enforcement is not stalled by unfounded objection.

### Protocols

- LAW-P1 (enactment into force). Actor: an officeholder acting AS the responsible publication office. Input: a completed A10 enactment. The actor (a) constructs the law_in_force object with its human-readable master, norm_version, rank, and A10 provenance (LAW-I12); (b) runs the conformance check against the A1 constitution and A0 Charter and the floor check (LAW-I2), refusing to publish any part that breaches an A9 right or floor; (c) confirms A18-I7 definiteness for any coercive rule (LAW-I5) and sets the review_clause and sunset for any coercive norm (LAW-I9); (d) appends the publication_event to the statute book, resolving the natural-person publication bearer and booking the loud S4 audit event (LAW-I1, LAW-I11). Output: an in-force norm whose binding interval opens at the publication timestamp (LAW-I3).

- LAW-P2 (amendment or repeal). Actor: the responsible publication office. Input: an amending or repealing A10 output. The actor appends a new supersession event that closes the prior norm_version's in-force interval prospectively and, for an amendment, opens the new version's interval; the prior text remains queryable (LAW-I13); coercive amendments carry the review_clause and sunset of LAW-I9. Output: the superseding event, booking the loud S4 audit and resolving the natural-person publication bearer (LAW-I11).

- LAW-P3 (law-as-of-a-time query). Actor: a court, enforcement organ, or agent. Input: an instant T. The actor reconstructs the binding norm-set at T by evaluating the LAW-I1 fold at T, returning the norm_versions whose in-force intervals contain T. Output: the contemporaneous norm-set A6 adjudicates against (LAW-I4). This is a read projection and appends no state event.

- LAW-P4 (delegated rule-making). Actor: a delegate office. Input: a delegation and a proposed regulation. The actor constructs a regulation only within its delegation scope and ceiling (LAW-I10), runs LAW-P1 steps (b), (c), and (d) against the same hierarchy, floor, definiteness, and review-and-sunset tests, and records the delegation reference; a regulation outside scope is refused as ultra vires and never published. Output: an in-force regulation with a resolved natural-person publication bearer and an S4 audit event, or a refusal.

- LAW-P5 (voidness challenge). Actor: an A6 court. Input: a challenge to a norm. The court reconstructs the norm via LAW-P3, tests it against LAW-I2 (hierarchy and floor), A18-I7 via LAW-I5 (definiteness), LAW-I3 and LAW-I14 (publication and access), LAW-I7 (contribution bounds), LAW-I9 (review and sunset), LAW-I10 (delegation scope), and LAW-I12 (provenance), and declares the offending part void ab initio where any test fails, refusing coercion built on it while leaving severable valid parts in force. Output: a reasoned, published ruling booking the S4 audit; where the challenge was raised at the A18-I4 gate, the expedited voidness track of LAW-I19 governs.

- LAW-P6 (coercive collection of a mandatory contribution). Actor: the enforcement officeholder resolved as the natural-person bearer. Input: an unpaid mandatory_contribution_instrument obligation. The actor first tests floor exposure and, where the contributor is floor-exposed, applies AUTH-I12 waiver or deferral (LAW-I18); otherwise it routes the collection through the single A18-I4 gate (fail-closed until A10 calibrates), booking the loud S4 audit and opening the A18-I34 contest path. Output: a gated collection act with a resolved natural-person bearer and an S4 audit event, or a refusal where the gate fails or a floor would be breached.

### Lifecycle and edge cases

- Back-dated or secret law: a norm published today but stamped with a past date binds only from the actual publication event, and a norm never lawfully published binds no one and grounds no coercion (LAW-I3); an enforcement attempt on it is refused at the gate and booked in the S4 audit.
- Self-executing coercive encoding: a machine encoding that computes a coercive or rights-affecting effect with no adopting natural person is void; only a non-coercive evaluation self-executes, and any coercive consequence waits on a named person adopting it per AINT-I2 and a pass at the A18-I4 gate (LAW-I6). Encoding drift from the human master is a correctable defect fixed by a new appended event; the master governs.
- Repeal during a pending matter: repealing a norm does not erase it; a completed act before the repeal is still judged by the then-in-force version via LAW-P3 (LAW-I13, LAW-I4), while the repeal takes prospective effect.
- Fee or contribution that gates a floor: a fiscal-looking charge whose non-payment withdraws a floor good is re-characterized by substance and voided as a floor gate, and, if designed to seize a coercive lever, flips to the concealment class (LAW-I15); a floor-exposed non-payer of a genuine contribution is waived or deferred, never cut, per LAW-I18 and AUTH-I12.
- Contribution invariant to the corridor or above ceiling: a compelled transfer whose amount does not move with the measured corridor deviation is an ultra vires general levy and is void, and a transfer above its proportionate ceiling, or punitive in substance, is void or flips to the concealment class (LAW-I7).
- Sunset lapse and stale review: a coercive regulation past its sunset with no A10 re-enactment lapses by the fold and grounds no further coercion, and any coercive norm whose independent review is un-completed or non-independent at its review point is suspended by the fold; re-entry into force is a fresh LAW-P1 with an affirmative A10 act (LAW-I9).
- Facial floor breach raised at the gate: a coercing organ or target that raises a facial A9 or floor breach, or a facial publication or provenance defect, suspends the coercion at the A18-I4 gate under A18-I34 pending an expedited A6 voidness ruling (LAW-I19); the presumption of validity does not defeat the facial showing.
- Delegation overreach: a subordinate regulation that a delegate had no scope to make, or that would sub-delegate a rights breach, is ultra vires and void even if lawfully published, because publication cures form, not authority (LAW-I10, LAW-I12).
- Fully tied conflicting norms: two equal-rank norms tied on specificity and publication time are resolved by A6 narrowing construction, or by the enactment's own stated ordering, so resolve() is total (LAW-I16).
- Border exit and entry restriction: the F7 MOVE exit/entry restriction is deferred to a border doctrine (A16), which this section does not cover; an A5 norm purporting to restrict exit or entry is noted at that interface and not resolved here.

## OFFICE public office and officials (A11)

### Purpose

This section fixes the distinction on which all accountable state power rests: the OFFICE is a standing position that holds a mandate, and the OFFICEHOLDER is a natural person appointed to bear it for a bounded term. Powers attach to the office and are held in trust; the person merely wields them and answers for them. Because every exercise of public authority is the sharpest CAPTURE surface in the model, this section is written to protect both flanks at once. The citizen is protected from a captured, self-dealing, secretly-conflicted, or unaccountable official who treats a public mandate as a private instrument. The honest officeholder is protected from a fabricated conflict or abuse accusation, from removal without due process, from being maneuvered into forfeiting their own A18 protections through a relabeled inquiry, and from the fiction that taking office strips them of their own rights as a person; a good-faith act taken within the mandate and within corridor carries a functional-immunity shield (OFFICE-I20) so honest discretion is not personal exposure. This section does not re-model the A18 coercion gate, the A6 courts, the A10 lawmaking process, the A13 election machinery, the RES emergency regime, the R1 register mechanics, or the norm hierarchy; it composes on top of them and cites them by published ID.

### Object model

- **office**: an O2 position (per Organizations-Contracts-and-Employment O1/O2) situated in an A2 branch or A3 ministry organization, carrying an office_mandate, an office_powers set, an office_term spec, and an accountability_binding. The office is the durable object; it persists across holders and across vacancy.
- **office_mandate**: the A2/A3 constitutional mandate the position holds (the value dimension a ministry owns, or the branch competence), referenced from Ministries-Branches-and-Municipality, never re-enumerated here. The mandate is the source of the office's powers and the measure of their limit.
- **office_powers**: the closed, enumerated set of public authorities the office may exercise. Powers are held in trust (OFFICE-I5), bounded by the office_mandate, and never exceed what A5 law-in-force grants the position.
- **officeholder**: a B1 natural person (Persons-Life-Events-and-Property) appointed to the office. The holder is distinct from the office by construction; a holder change does not change the office.
- **holding**: the composed (office, officeholder, appointment_event, term_window) relation that binds a specific natural person to the office for a specific interval. An act of the office is authored through exactly one live holding or an OFFICE-I18 caretaker holding.
- **appointing_authority**: the office or body empowered to appoint and to remove this office's holder, itself an office resolving to a natural-person bearer (via A13 election or A11 appointment).
- **conflict_declaration**: a standing, public, append-only R1 record of an officeholder's interests that could bias the office's acts (OFFICE-I8). It concerns the holder's official capacity, not private third-party data, and is inspectable at person resolution by any citizen; only incidental private third-party data referenced within it is confined to S5 cohort grain. Its absence where a conflict exists is a concealment-class harm (OFFICE-I9).
- **recusal**: a logged withdrawal of a holder from a specific matter in which they carry an undischarged conflict (OFFICE-I10).
- **office_register**: the R1 instance (an A12 registry mandate) that is the holder of record for offices, holdings, appointments, removals, declarations, and recusals; a current roster is a non-authoritative fold (R1), corrections are new events.
- **caretaker_holding**: a bounded, time-capped, reduced-power holding that keeps a mandate borne while an office is vacant (OFFICE-I18). Its powers are a closed positive enumeration by office class (default-deny), and at the time cap absent a full appointment they hard-lapse exactly as a term expires.

### Invariants

- **OFFICE-I1 (office is a position, not a person)**. An office SHALL be modelled as an O2 position holding an A2/A3 office_mandate by pure composition (per O1/O2 and Ministries-Branches), distinct from any officeholder. The office's mandate, powers, term, and accountability attach to the position and persist across holders and across vacancy. Purpose: powers that inhere in the position rather than the person can be constrained, audited, and transferred without depending on any individual's goodwill. Predicate: an office record whose powers or mandate are defined by reference to a named natural person, rather than to an A2/A3 mandate, is malformed and SHALL be rejected by the office_register. Both flanks: this both denies a holder any personal ownership of state power and gives the honest holder a bright line between the office's acts and their private life.

- **OFFICE-I2 (a holding binds exactly one natural-person bearer)**. Every live office SHALL resolve to exactly one accountable natural-person bearer through a live holding, save during a vacancy governed by a caretaker_holding (OFFICE-I18). No office act SHALL issue from an anonymous actor, an unfilled position, or a purely-automated process with no resolved human bearer. Purpose: the K2 act must always trace to a person who answers. Enforcement hook: an office act appended to a register with no resolvable holding is void as an exercise of authority and books the loudest S4 audit against the organization.

- **OFFICE-I3 (the officeholder acts as the office; the audit records both)**. When a holder exercises office_powers, the act SHALL be attributed simultaneously to the office (the mandate wielded) and to the natural person (the bearer who wielded it), by the pure-composition rule of Organizations-Contracts-and-Employment (the officer acts AS the office). The S4 audit SHALL record BOTH bindings and neither alone. Purpose: attribution to the office alone hides the culpable human; attribution to the person alone hides the mandate and its limits. Predicate: an audit row for an office act missing either the office binding or the natural-person binding is malformed. Both flanks: the citizen can trace the human behind the mandate, and the honest holder is recorded as having acted within the office rather than personally.

- **OFFICE-I4 (powers are bounded by the mandate)**. An office SHALL exercise only office_powers enumerated within its A2/A3 office_mandate and granted by A5 law-in-force. An act outside the mandate is ultra vires: void as an exercise of authority, and, where it applies coercion, gated and refused by A18-I4 exactly as any other act. Purpose: the mandate is both the source and the ceiling of the office's reach. Enforcement hook: an office act whose claimed power is not in the enumerated set is rejected at append time, not merely reviewable after the fact.

- **OFFICE-I5 (powers held in trust, not owned)**. Office_powers are held in trust for the polity and the value dimension the mandate serves; they are never the personal property of the holder. A holder SHALL NOT alienate, delegate beyond the mandate's delegation rules, bequeath, sell, or privately encumber any office power. Purpose: trust, not ownership, is what makes answerability (OFFICE-I6) and clean succession (OFFICE-I17) possible. Predicate: any purported transfer of an office power by the holder's private act, rather than by the appointing authority under A5, is void ab initio.

- **OFFICE-I6 (answerability duty, bounded by use immunity)**. The holder SHALL answer for the office's acts during their holding: they SHALL respond to lawful inquiry from the A6 courts, the A8 budget-accountability organ, the legislature, and the affected citizen, and SHALL NOT refuse an account of an office act on the ground that it was official. The duty is administrative and civil. A compelled account produced under it SHALL NOT be used, directly or derivatively, to ground a hard A18 rung against the accounting holder (use immunity), and the duty yields to the A18 due-process spine and the no-adverse-weight-from-silence rule the instant the inquiry turns to the holder's own criminal exposure. Where the account is compelled it is a coercive encounter under XACT-I6 routing through the A18-I4 gate. Purpose: an unanswerable office is an unaccountable one, but answerability is an administrative light on official acts, not a lever to extract self-incrimination outside A18's spine. Both flanks: the duty to account is real and enforceable AGAINST the holder, and equally the honest holder cannot be maneuvered into forfeiting their A18 protections by relabeling a criminal inquiry as an administrative account.

- **OFFICE-I7 (appointment and removal are governed events)**. Entry to and exit from a holding SHALL occur only by an appointment_event or removal_event authored by the office's appointing_authority (via A13 election or A11 appointment) and appended to the office_register as an X1 event. A holding that is not opened by a valid appointment_event is not a holding, and its acts fall under OFFICE-I2 as unbeared. Purpose: no one takes or loses public power by self-assertion or by silence. Enforcement hook: the office_register rejects a holding open/close with no authorizing event from the competent authority.

- **OFFICE-I8 (conflict-of-interest declaration is a standing public duty)**. Every officeholder SHALL maintain a standing, public, append-only conflict_declaration in the office_register covering interests, holdings, relationships, and prospective engagements that could bias the office's acts, updated on any material change within a defined window. Because the declaration concerns the holder's official capacity and not private third-party data, it SHALL be inspectable at person resolution by any citizen, with NO S5 cohort-grain limit and NO A6-contract gate on citizen inspection; only incidental private third-party data referenced within a declaration is confined to S5 cohort grain. Purpose: sunlight on interests is the cheapest guard against capture, and the citizen who bears the office's power is entitled to see the conflicts it carries. Both flanks: the duty is affirmative and continuous, but a good-faith, timely, complete declaration is a full shield: a holder who has declared cannot later be accused of concealment on the declared facts.

- **OFFICE-I9 (an undeclared conflict is a concealment-class harm)**. A material conflict that a holder knew or should have known and failed to declare within the window is a concealment-class harm carrying the A18 perpetual flip (per the concealment class and its no-limitation rule): the limitation clock never starts while the conflict stays hidden. Purpose: concealment, not the conflict itself, is the culpable act; a declared conflict is managed, a hidden one is capture. Predicate for the honest-holder flank: the concealment finding SHALL independently clear the A18 hard-rung confidence standard (high-confidence, objective, corroborated) before any perpetual-flip attaches; a mere allegation, a de-minimis interest, or an interest the holder could not reasonably have known does not flip the clock, and a good-faith late correction is mitigating.

- **OFFICE-I10 (recusal on an undischarged conflict)**. Where a holder carries a live, undischarged conflict in a specific matter, they SHALL recuse from that matter, logging the recusal in the office_register, and the matter SHALL route to another holder, a deputy, or a caretaker_holding. Purpose: declaration surfaces the conflict; recusal neutralizes it in the specific act. Enforcement hook: an office act in a matter where the acting holder has a logged, undischarged conflict and no recusal is voidable on that ground and books an S4 audit; conversely, a properly recused holder bears no answerability for the recused matter's outcome.

- **OFFICE-I11 (abuse of office resolves to a natural-person bearer)**. Using the office as a private instrument, self-dealing, steering office acts to the holder's own benefit, nepotism, directing state power for private or political gain, extracting rents from the mandate, is an A18 harm classified on the office act's anti-value booking and carrying a resolved natural-person bearer per OFFICE-I3. Purpose: abuse of a public trust is among the loudest harms because it weaponizes the mandate itself. Enforcement hook: routed through the A18 taxonomy and, where it breaches a rights floor, the non-priceable criminal class; never priced away as an ordinary cost. An abuse-of-office act is a named carve-out from the functional-immunity shield (OFFICE-I20).

- **OFFICE-I12 (the veil never shields the culpable human)**. The O1 organization and the office abstraction SHALL NEVER shield the natural person who authored an abuse-of-office act from A18 liability. The acting-in-concert graph and veil rule of Organizations-Contracts-and-Employment traces through the office to the culpable human; "the office decided" is not a defence. Purpose: the whole point of recording both bindings (OFFICE-I3) is that the human is always reachable. Both flanks: liability attaches to the person who authored or knowingly enabled the abuse, and equally does NOT attach to a holder who acted within mandate on the record, so a lawful hard decision is not retroactively recast as abuse merely because it was unpopular or later revalued under D2.

- **OFFICE-I13 (term limits and succession)**. Every office SHALL carry a bounded office_term; a holding SHALL NOT persist beyond it. Term length, term limits, and the succession order are owned by Leadership-Selection-and-Succession and cited here, not re-modelled. A holding whose term_window has closed is expired: its holder's authority lapses and the office falls to succession or to a caretaker_holding (OFFICE-I18). Purpose: no indefinite incumbency, which is the slow form of capture. Enforcement hook: the office_register marks a holding expired at term close automatically; acts authored after expiry fall under OFFICE-I2 as unbeared.

- **OFFICE-I14 (revolving-door and level-capture guard)**. Movement of a natural person between an office and the parties it regulates, contracts with, or licenses SHALL be subject to the revolving-door detection of Organizations-Contracts-and-Employment and the GOV level-capture guard. A single steward SHALL NOT uniformly relax thresholds in incumbents' or their post-office employers' favor, and post-office engagements that would have biased in-office acts trigger the same concealment analysis as OFFICE-I9. Purpose: capture is often deferred, paid after office rather than during it. Both flanks: cooling-off and disclosure constrain the corrupt path, while an honest holder retains the right to earn a living after office, so the guard targets the specific conflicted engagement, not a blanket ban on future work.

- **OFFICE-I15 (the holder keeps their own rights as a person)**. Taking office SHALL NOT strip the holder of their own A9 inviolable rights or their COMP-I17 capability floors. In any removal, investigation, or abuse proceeding the holder retains the full A18 due-process spine (presumption of innocence, proportionate proof standard, the right to contest), and no removal, sanction, or public accounting SHALL breach any A9 inviolable right of the holder or gate a COMP-I17 capability floor delivered to them. Purpose: the office is a trust the person holds, not a forfeiture of their personhood. Both flanks: the citizen's right to remove and hold an official to account coexists with the official's right not to be scapegoated, defamed, or degraded, and a fabricated conflict or abuse charge is itself an A18 harm against the accused holder.

- **OFFICE-I16 (removal is due-process bounded and floor-respecting)**. A removal_event SHALL follow the removal grounds and procedure fixed by A5 for the office (for-cause, misconduct, incapacity, or the electoral/term mechanism), authored by the competent appointing_authority, with the holder's right to notice and to contest per OFFICE-I15. A removal SHALL NOT be a covert coercion route around A18: where removal is used to punish, the A18-I4 gate and A18-I34 suspensive contest apply, and a removal, sanction, or public accounting SHALL NOT breach any A9 inviolable right or gate a COMP-I17 capability floor. Purpose: removal protects the polity from a bad holder without becoming a tool to purge an honest one. Enforcement hook: a removal_event with no stated A5 ground and no contest window is void; a contested removal has suspensive effect where A18-I34 applies.

- **OFFICE-I16a (interim suspension of powers during contested removal)**. Where continued exercise of the office during a contested removal poses an ongoing risk of an A9 or COMP-I17 floor breach or a continuing capture harm, the appointing_authority or the A6 courts MAY order an interim suspension of the holder's powers, routing the mandate to a caretaker_holding (OFFICE-I18), WITHOUT prejudging the removal merits. The interim suspension SHALL itself be reasoned, time-capped, floor-respecting, and independently contestable under A18-I34, and SHALL resolve to the natural-person bearer who ordered it with the loud S4 audit. Purpose: a live floor-breach or capture risk during a contest must be stoppable without the contest's outcome being pre-decided, and without the suspension itself becoming an unchecked punishment. Both flanks: the polity can halt ongoing harm by a holder whose tenure is genuinely contested, and the honest holder is protected because a wrongful interim suspension of an honest holder is compensable and books V4 anti-value against the ordering office, so suspension is not a cost-free purge.

- **OFFICE-I17 (clean succession)**. On expiry, removal, resignation, or incapacity of a holder, the office_powers SHALL pass intact to the successor holding per the Leadership-Selection succession order, and SHALL NOT lapse to the outgoing person, vest personally in a deputy, or dissolve. Purpose: powers held in trust (OFFICE-I5) transfer with the office, never with the person. Predicate: any residual office power exercised by a former holder after their holding closed is an act by a non-bearer, void under OFFICE-I2 and, if coercive, an A18 harm.

- **OFFICE-I18 (caretaker continuity, closed reduced power set, hard lapse)**. A vacancy SHALL NEVER leave an office_mandate unbeared. On any gap between holdings, a caretaker_holding SHALL bear the mandate: a bounded, time-capped holding, held by a resolved natural person, exercising ONLY a CLOSED, POSITIVE enumeration of continuity and preservation acts fixed by office class (default-deny: any act not enumerated is outside the caretaker set), with no irreversible, precedent-setting, or discretion-expanding act, until a full holder is appointed. At the time cap, absent a full appointment, the caretaker's powers SHALL hard-lapse by default to a strictly non-discretionary continuity-preservation minimum, or wholly to the next roster authority per Leadership-Selection, exactly as a term expires under OFFICE-I13; any caretaker act beyond the time cap is unbeared and void under OFFICE-I2, not merely irregular. Purpose: continuity of the mandate without letting a caretaker entrench, expand their own remit, or become a full holder by outrunning the cap through inertia. Both flanks: the citizen is never left with a mandate no one bears and never left with a caretaker who quietly acquires full power by running past the cap, and the caretaker is protected by a narrow, positively-enumerated remit so they are not later accused of overreach for keeping the lights on. Enforcement hook: a caretaker act outside the enumerated set is ultra vires under OFFICE-I4 and void; a caretaker_holding that reaches its time cap without a full appointment hard-lapses as above, and the S4 audit escalation and governance signal are ADDITIONAL consequences, not the sole ones. Until OFFICE-OQ3 calibrates the per-class enumeration and cap, the caretaker set fails closed to the non-discretionary continuity-preservation minimum.

- **OFFICE-I19 (no self-authored expansion of one's own office)**. A holder SHALL NOT expand their own office's powers, extend their own term, or relax their own accountability_binding by their own act; such changes SHALL originate from A10 lawmaking or the competent A2 authority external to the office. Purpose: closes the self-dealing loophole where an office rewrites its own mandate. Predicate: an office_register event that widens an office's powers or term and is authored by the same holding it benefits is malformed and rejected; this both blocks the incumbent's grab and protects the honest holder from being asked to self-authorize an unlawful expansion.

- **OFFICE-I20 (functional-immunity shield)**. A good-faith act taken by a holder within the office_mandate (OFFICE-I4) and within its corridor SHALL be protected from PERSONAL liability, so an honest officeholder does not bear personal A18 consequence for a lawful in-mandate decision merely because it was hard, unpopular, or later revalued under D2. The shield SHALL NOT protect, and carves out: an act breaching an A9 inviolable right or a Charter Art. 4 right, a concealment-class act (OFFICE-I9), and an abuse-of-office or capture act (OFFICE-I11). Purpose: officials must be able to exercise a lawful mandate without fear of personal ruin for good-faith judgment, while the exact acts that betray the trust remain fully reachable. Reconciliation: the shield does not touch the answerability duty (the holder still accounts, OFFICE-I6), does not shield the culpable human behind an abuse (OFFICE-I11, OFFICE-I12), and does not diminish the holder's own procedural rights (OFFICE-I15); it bounds personal LIABILITY for good-faith in-corridor acts and nothing more. Both flanks: the honest holder is shielded from personal liability for lawful discretion, and the citizen loses nothing because a rights breach, concealment, or capture defeats the shield by its own terms. AINT-I18 cites this invariant.

### Protocols

- **OFFICE-P1 (appointment)**. The appointing_authority authors an appointment_event naming (office, natural person, term_window), verifies the appointee's eligibility and non-conflicting status against the conflict_declaration (OFFICE-I8), and appends the event to the office_register with the S4 audit, opening the holding. No holding exists before this event.

- **OFFICE-P2 (acting as the office)**. A holder exercising office_powers (a) confirms the power is within the office_mandate (OFFICE-I4), (b) confirms no undischarged conflict in the matter (OFFICE-I10, else recuse), (c) authors the K2 act with dual attribution to office and person (OFFICE-I3), and (d) appends the resulting X1 event to the owning register with the loud S4 audit. Any coercion routes through the A18-I4 gate.

- **OFFICE-P3 (declaration and recusal)**. On appointment and on any material change, the holder updates the standing conflict_declaration within the window, appending the update to the office_register with the S4 audit. Where a specific matter engages a live conflict, the holder logs a recusal and the matter re-routes (OFFICE-I10).

- **OFFICE-P4 (removal)**. The appointing_authority authors a removal_event stating the A5 ground, serves notice, affords the holder the OFFICE-I15 contest, and on final determination appends the removal to the office_register with the S4 audit, closing the holding. Where removal is punitive, the A18-I4 gate and A18-I34 suspensive contest apply. Where an ongoing floor or capture risk warrants it during the contest, an interim suspension may issue under OFFICE-I16a.

- **OFFICE-P5 (vacancy and caretaker)**. On a holding closing with no successor holding open, a caretaker appointment_event is authored by the appointing_authority's OWN live holding, opening a caretaker_holding with a resolved natural-person caretaker, the closed positive reduced power set, and a time cap (OFFICE-I18); it is appended to the office_register with the S4 audit; succession per Leadership-Selection is initiated; the caretaker_holding closes on the successor's appointment or hard-lapses at the time cap (OFFICE-I18). Where the appointing_authority is itself vacant, the caretaker appointment_event is authored by the named fallback chain per Leadership-Selection succession, terminating in a default constitutional organ that can never be simultaneously vacant; in the cascading-vacancy case the caretaker for the subordinate office is opened by the appointing_authority's own caretaker. Every step names a natural-person actor and books the S4 audit.

### Lifecycle and edge cases

- **Term expiry with no successor**: the holding is marked expired at term close (OFFICE-I13); a caretaker_holding opens under OFFICE-I18; the outgoing person exercises no residual power (OFFICE-I17).
- **Holder resigns mid-term**: resignation is a removal-class governed event; the holding closes, succession or caretaker continuity opens, and the resigning person's answerability for acts during their holding survives their departure (OFFICE-I6).
- **Undeclared conflict surfaced years later**: the concealment perpetual flip (OFFICE-I9) means the clock never ran while hidden; but the honest-holder guard requires the concealment finding itself to clear the A18 hard-rung standard before the flip attaches.
- **Automated decision with no human bearer**: an office act emitted by an automated pipeline with no resolvable holding is void under OFFICE-I2; a human holding must own the pipeline's outputs for them to be exercises of authority.
- **Removal used as punishment**: recharacterized as coercion, gated by A18-I4, suspensively contestable under A18-I34, and floor-bounded by OFFICE-I16; an honest holder removed on a fabricated ground has an A18 restoration claim and a claim for any A9 or COMP-I17 floor breach.
- **Contested removal poses ongoing harm**: the appointing_authority or the A6 courts may order a reasoned, time-capped, contestable interim suspension routing to a caretaker (OFFICE-I16a) without prejudging the merits; a wrongful interim suspension of an honest holder is compensable and books V4 anti-value against the ordering office.
- **Compelled account turns criminal**: an administrative account (OFFICE-I6) that begins to touch the holder's own criminal exposure yields to the A18 due-process spine and the no-adverse-weight-from-silence rule; the compelled account carries use immunity and SHALL NOT ground a hard A18 rung against the holder (OFFICE-I6).
- **Caretaker overreach**: a caretaker taking an act outside the closed positive enumeration acts ultra vires (OFFICE-I18, OFFICE-I4); the act is void and audited.
- **Caretaker outruns the time cap**: at the cap absent a full appointment the caretaker's powers hard-lapse to the non-discretionary continuity-preservation minimum or wholly to the next roster authority (OFFICE-I18); any act beyond the cap is unbeared and void under OFFICE-I2, and the S4 escalation and governance signal are added, not the sole, consequences.
- **Appointing authority itself vacant (cascading vacancy)**: the caretaker for the subordinate office is opened by the appointing_authority's own caretaker, the fallback chain terminating in a default constitutional organ that is never simultaneously vacant (OFFICE-P5, OFFICE-I18).
- **Incumbent self-entrenchment**: a holder authoring their own term extension or power widening is blocked at append time (OFFICE-I19) and, if pursued off-register, is abuse of office (OFFICE-I11) with the veil pierced (OFFICE-I12) and the functional-immunity shield defeated (OFFICE-I20).
- **Honest holder sued for a hard lawful decision**: a good-faith in-mandate in-corridor act carries the functional-immunity shield (OFFICE-I20); the holder still answers under OFFICE-I6 but bears no personal A18 liability absent a rights breach, concealment, or capture.
- **Genuine emergency by an office**: an emergency exercise of office power follows the RES declaration regime (narrow, loud, reviewed after the fact, compensated when wrong); the office's dual attribution and S4 audit still bind, and the acting person still bears it.

## AUTH registry mandates, permits and authorizations (A12, A14)

### Purpose

This section specifies two adjacent exercises of public authority. A12 is the constitutional shell around the R1 registers that the state SHALL keep: which registers must exist, who is the registrar of record, and which registers carry public faith. A14 is the permit, license, and authorization: the state's power to say whether a private actor MAY lawfully do a regulated thing, held as an R5 grant.

Both are among the sharpest CAPTURE surfaces in the model. A falsified register launders a lie into public record; a weaponized permit denies a citizen the floor of essential work or supply, or a captured regulator freezes out entrants to protect incumbents. Every rule below protects both flanks: the citizen and the public against arbitrary denial, secret falsification, and license capture, AND the honest registrar and the honest regulator against paralysis, and the genuine safety gate against a rubber stamp. Every act named here is a K2 act with a resolved natural-person bearer, appends an X1 event to the owning register, and books the loud S4 audit. This section does not re-model R1 register mechanics, the A10 lawmaking process that sets a standard, the A6 courts that hear a contest, the A18-I4 coercion gate, or the RES emergency regime; it cites them.

### Object model

- Registry mandate (A12): a constitutional obligation that a named R1 register instance SHALL exist, naming its subject matter, its registrar office, and its public-faith status. The mandated register set includes at minimum the law register (A5 law-in-force output), the office register (O1/O2 offices and their holders), the incident register (X3 incidents), and the permit register (R5 grants issued under A14).
- Registrar: an O2 position, held by a B1 natural person, carrying the A12 mandate to keep a named register. The officer acts AS the registrar office (pure O1/O2 composition) and the S4 audit records both the person and the office.
- Authorization instance (A14): an R5 grant with a grantee, a subject activity, a governing published standard, a decision (grant, conditional grant, or reasoned denial), zero or more conditions, a validity window, and a fee record on the $ rail. A permit and a license and an authorization are the same object at different names; this section treats them uniformly and says "permit" throughout.
- Hazard-class assignment: the K2 act that sorts an application into a published hazard class, which fixes whether a deadline lapse resolves to a deemed-grant or a deemed-denial under AUTH-I8. It is a bearered, separately contestable act, not a silent clerical tag (AUTH-I19).
- Application: the X5 encounter that opens a permit lifecycle, co-owned by applicant and regulator.
- Decision record: the reasoned K2 act that grants, conditions, or denies, appended as an X1 event to the permit register. A deemed outcome at deadline is a decision record too, borne by the officeholder who let the deadline lapse (AUTH-I8).

### Invariants

#### A12 registry mandates and registrar independence

**AUTH-I1 (mandated register set).** The state SHALL keep, as R1 register instances, every register a law in force declares mandatory, and at minimum the law register, the office register, the incident register, and the permit register. Purpose: the state's own law, offices, incidents, and grants are held as append-only record, not as mutable current-state a captor can quietly rewrite. Predicate: for each mandated subject in A5, an R1 instance exists with a named A12 mandate. Enforcement: absence of a mandated register is a governance defect surfaced to A2/A3 oversight; the missing-register condition SHALL be a monitored corridor signal, not a silent gap.

**AUTH-I2 (named registrar, resolved bearer).** Every mandated register SHALL have exactly one registrar office (S1 one-controller) whose current holder resolves to a natural person. Purpose: a register with no accountable keeper is a register no one can be held to; a register with two keepers is a register whose record can be forked. Enforcement: each append to the register carries the registrar bearer in the S4 audit; an append with no resolved bearer fails closed.

**AUTH-I3 (registrar independence, direct and material interest).** A registrar SHALL recuse from a specific record where it has a DIRECT and MATERIAL interest in that record, and SHALL route that record to an independent alternate. Predicate (decidable, mirroring the AINT-I10 material-conflict standard): a disqualifying interest exists where the registrar, its kin, or an acting-in-concert entity (per the O1 veil rule) is (a) a named party to that record, or (b) the direct, identified beneficiary or target whose position the content of that record decides. A remote, diffuse, or immaterial interest does NOT disqualify. Purpose: the register cannot be captured by the actor whose specific record it decides, yet the registrar is not paralyzed by every distant connection. Enforcement: a record entered in violation of this predicate loses its public-faith projection (AUTH-I5) for that record and flags for A6 revaluation; a knowing self-interested falsification is a concealment-class harm with the perpetual flip under A18. Both flanks: independence protects the recorded party and the public from a rigged register, AND the material-and-specific test protects the honest registrar from being forced to recuse, or blamed for the appearance, over a remote or immaterial tie.

**AUTH-I4 (ministerial duty to record, non-discretionary).** Where a submission meets the published formal conditions of a register, the registrar SHALL record it and SHALL NOT exercise substantive discretion to refuse, delay, or edit it. Purpose: a register is a holder of record, not a gate; discretionary refusal turns the clerk into an unaccountable censor. Predicate: refusal is lawful only on a named formal defect (wrong form, missing mandatory field, out of subject-matter scope), stated in writing. The resolved-natural-person-bearer and S4-audit fields required by AINT-I1 are FORMAL completeness conditions (a missing mandatory field) that the registrar checks under this ministerial duty, NOT substantive discretion; a submission lacking a resolved bearer or the audit field is refused for that formal defect, the refusal citing the missing field in writing and contestable at A6. A correction is a new event, never an erasure (cite R1). Both flanks: this stops a registrar suppressing an inconvenient true record, AND protects the registrar from liability for content it is merely obliged to hold, since public faith attaches to the fact of record, not to the registrar's endorsement of its truth.

**AUTH-I5 (public-faith designation).** The A12 mandate for each register SHALL state whether that register carries public faith, and only a register so designated projects reliance under R1-I28. Purpose: a third party must know which registers it may safely rely on and which are merely informational, so reliance is never mis-placed and never wrongly denied. Enforcement: reliance protection under R1-I28 is available if and only if the register is public-faith designated and the relying party acted in good faith; a non-designated register grounds no reliance defense. This section does not re-model R1-I28; it designates its scope.

**AUTH-I6 (register act is a loud state act).** Every registrar act (append, correction, recusal, refusal) is a K2 act that appends an X1 event to the register and books the S4 audit; the register's own history is itself the append-only record. Purpose: the keeper of the record is recorded, closing the "who edited the register" gap. Enforcement: an append that does not itself generate an audited X1 event is void.

#### A14 permit, license, and authorization

**AUTH-I7 (governed, decidable grant).** A permit SHALL be decided against a standard published in force before the application, and SHALL NOT be granted or denied on an unpublished, retroactive, or ad hoc criterion. Purpose: the applicant knows the test in advance; the regulator cannot invent a reason after the fact. Predicate: the decision cites the specific published standard clauses it applies. Enforcement: a decision resting on an uncited or unpublished criterion is void on contest under A18-I34. Both flanks: the applicant gets a knowable test AND the regulator has firm published ground to stand on when it refuses a dangerous activity.

**AUTH-I8 (bounded timeline, deemed outcome borne by the defaulting officeholder).** Every permit standard SHALL carry a bounded decision deadline, and at the deadline the outcome SHALL be either a reasoned decision or a deemed outcome fixed by the standard; indefinite non-decision is prohibited. Predicate: at deadline, exactly one of {grant, conditional grant, reasoned denial, standard-defined deemed outcome} is recorded. The deemed outcome SHALL be deemed-grant for classes the standard marks low-hazard, and a recorded, contestable deemed-denial (never silent) for classes the standard marks high-hazard, with a mandatory expedited review. Bearer: a deemed outcome is NOT a bearer-less act and is NOT void; it SHALL resolve to the responsible officeholder who let the published deadline lapse as its constructive natural-person bearer (cite AINT-I1, AINT-I2, which state that an operation-of-law deemed outcome is a bearered act borne by the defaulting office). The deemed grant or denial appends the X1 / R5 permit-register event naming that person (AUTH-I10), books the loud S4 audit against them personally (AUTH-I17), and books V4 anti-value on the duty axis for the missed-deadline breach. The high-hazard deemed-denial's mandatory expedited review SHALL itself carry a bounded deadline terminating in exactly one recorded, reasoned, contestable outcome, so the expedited review cannot become a fresh limbo. Enforcement: a missed deadline with no recorded outcome triggers the deemed outcome automatically, bearered to the defaulting office, with the S4 accountability flag against that officeholder. Both flanks: the applicant is freed from limbo and the deemed outcome still names an accountable decider, AND a genuinely dangerous class is not auto-granted by mere clock expiry.

**AUTH-I9 (reasoned decision).** Every grant condition and every denial SHALL state reasons sufficient to be tested against the published standard. Purpose: a reason that can be read is a reason that can be reviewed; an unreasoned decision is unreviewable and therefore arbitrary. Enforcement: an unreasoned denial is treated as no decision and falls to AUTH-I8's deemed outcome on contest. Both flanks: reasons expose a capricious denial AND document the honest regulator's genuine safety ground.

**AUTH-I10 (grant held in R5, recorded in the permit register).** A granted permit SHALL live as an R5 grant and its issuance, conditioning, and revocation SHALL each append an X1 event to the permit register. Purpose: the authorization is a first-class held record, traceable and revocable, not a loose letter. This section does not re-model R5; it places the object there.

**AUTH-I11 (floor-gating permit act blocked before breach).** Where a permit gates a delivered COMP-I17 capability floor or an A9 right, a denial, revocation, or delay SHALL have MANDATORY suspensive effect: the floor-gating activity continues, or interim floor delivery is provided, pending the contest, so the floor is never actually cut (mirroring XACT-I7). A floor breach SHALL NOT be discharged by compensation; compensation is reserved for priceable collateral loss only. Suspensive effect is NOT optional for any floor-gating permit act, unless the regulator shows a published, reasoned, imminent floor-endangering hazard that defeats the provisional grant. An act that gates an A9 inviolable right rather than a priceable interest is a rights violation, criminal and never priceable (cite A9, the norm hierarchy). Purpose: a permit is a regulatory tool, not a lever to starve a citizen of the non-priceable floor; the floor binds before litigation, not after. Enforcement: a floor-gating denial, revocation, or delay taken without suspensive effect and without a published imminent-hazard showing is void, and any coercion within it routes through the single A18-I4 gate to a resolved natural-person bearer. Both flanks: the citizen cannot be locked out of the floor by a permit while contesting it, AND the regulator retains full power to refuse a genuinely floor-endangering activity on a published, reasoned, imminent-hazard ground, which is not a wrongful denial.

**AUTH-I12 (fee bounded to attested cost).** A permit fee SHALL clear on the $ rail and SHALL be bounded to the attested administrative cost of review; it SHALL NOT be set at a revenue-motivated level that prices a lawful applicant out of a lawful activity. Purpose: the fee recovers cost, it does not ration a lawful right by wealth or fund the treasury off the backs of applicants. Predicate: fee is less than or equal to attested cost, with the cost basis pinned as D2 evidence (an indicator, honestly revaluable). Where the gated activity is a capability floor, an inability to pay SHALL NOT defeat the floor; a fee waiver or deferral applies. Enforcement: a fee exceeding attested cost is contestable under A18-I34 and the excess is recoverable. Both flanks: the applicant is not priced out AND the regulator recovers the genuine cost of honest review rather than being forced to review for free.

**AUTH-I13 (proportionate, related conditions).** Conditions attached to a grant SHALL be proportionate to, and related to, the specific harm the standard regulates, and SHALL NOT be a lever for unrelated coercion. Purpose: the permit power ends at the regulated harm; it is not a general hook to extract unrelated compliance. Predicate (decidable): each condition names the regulated-harm clause it mitigates; a condition with no such nexus is void. Enforcement: an unrelated or disproportionate condition is severable and struck on contest under A18-I34, the rest of the grant surviving. Both flanks: the grantee is not conscripted into unrelated demands AND the regulator may impose every condition genuinely tied to the regulated harm.

**AUTH-I14 (no rubber stamp).** A regulator SHALL NOT grant a permit for an activity that fails the published safety standard, and the granting officer is accountable for a grant that the standard required to be refused. Purpose: the safety gate is real; the flank that protects the applicant from arbitrary denial does not license waving through the dangerous. Predicate: a grant issued against a failed published criterion, with no reasoned finding meeting the standard, is a defective act. Enforcement: a knowing grant of a standard-failing activity, especially one that later breaches a floor or a right, is an accountable state act booked to the natural-person bearer under S4, and if concealed carries the concealment-class perpetual flip under A18. Both flanks: this is the mirror of AUTH-I9; the public gets a genuine gate, AND an officer who applied the standard honestly and reached a defensible grant is protected from second-guessing by outcome alone.

**AUTH-I15 (revocation with due process, no retroactive standard).** A revocation SHALL be reasoned against the standard, contestable under A18-I34, and SHALL NOT retroactively void a good-faith reliance protected under R1-I28. Predicate: revocation cites a ground of {breach of condition, changed facts, fraud in the application}. "Changed facts" means changed FACTUAL circumstances of the activity or its hazard, and expressly NOT a retroactively tightened standard. An existing grant SHALL be adjudicated against the standard contemporaneous with its issuance (cite LAW-I4), save a genuine breach of condition or fraud; a standard change applies only prospectively, at the AUTH-I18 renewal boundary. A revocation resting on a post-grant standard change is void as retroactive. A revocation for fraud in the application MAY reach back, since reliance procured by fraud is not good-faith. Enforcement: an unreasoned, uncontestable, or retroactive-standard revocation is void; a revocation that gates a floor is blocked before breach under AUTH-I11. Both flanks: the holder gets notice, reasons, a contest, and protection from retroactive re-standard-setting, AND the regulator retains the power to pull a grant whose factual basis has genuinely failed or that was procured by fraud.

**AUTH-I16 (regulatory and license capture guard).** A regulator SHALL NOT operate a pattern that systematically grants to incumbents and denies or delays entrants on grounds not in the published standard; such a pattern is caught by the GOV level-capture guard. Purpose: the classic capture, where the gate protects the gated rather than the public, is detected structurally, not case by case. Predicate: a grant-rate divergence between incumbents and comparable entrants that the published standard does not explain, read as a corridor signal on cohort data (S5, the state sees cohorts not persons). Enforcement: a flagged divergence triggers oversight review; a steward who tunes thresholds to favor incumbents uniformly is caught by the GOV level-capture guard exactly as an incumbent-favoring threshold relaxation is. Both flanks: entrants get a fair gate AND a regulator whose divergence is fully explained by the published hazard standard is cleared, since the guard targets unexplained divergence, not every difference.

**AUTH-I17 (non-arbitrary denial, resolved bearer, loud audit).** Every permit denial is a state act against a person: it SHALL resolve to a natural-person deciding bearer and book the loud S4 audit, and it SHALL NOT be issued in retaliation or on a protected characteristic. Purpose: a denial is not anonymous; someone decided it and is accountable for it. Enforcement: a denial with no resolved bearer is void; a retaliatory or discriminatory denial is a rights-floor breach, criminal and never priceable (cite A9, norm hierarchy). Both flanks: the applicant faces an accountable decider AND the honest decider, having applied the published standard, is shielded from a false accusation by the very reasons and audit the rule requires.

**AUTH-I18 (expired, lapsed, or transferred permit).** A permit's validity window SHALL be stated on the grant; on expiry or lapse the authorization ends and continuing the activity is unauthorized, and a transfer of a permit is valid only where the standard permits transfer and the transferee meets the same published standard. Purpose: an authorization is time-bounded and person-bounded; it does not silently persist or migrate to an unqualified holder. Predicate: activity after the window with no renewal is unauthorized; a transfer records a new R5 grant event naming the transferee tested against the standard, not a silent substitution. Renewal SHALL be decidable on the same bounded-timeline and reasoned-decision rules (AUTH-I7 through AUTH-I9) so that a lapse is never used as a back-door indefinite denial. A tightened standard takes effect at this renewal boundary and applies only prospectively (AUTH-I15). Both flanks: the public is not exposed to a stale or hand-me-down authorization AND the good-faith holder gets a fair, timely renewal path rather than losing a floor-gating permit to a clock.

**AUTH-I19 (hazard-class assignment, bearered and contestable).** The assignment of an application to a hazard class, which fixes whether a deadline lapse resolves to a deemed-grant or a deemed-denial under AUTH-I8, is itself a K2 state act: it SHALL be made against the published class predicate, cite the specific clause invoked, resolve to a natural-person bearer, book the S4 audit, and be independently contestable under A18-I34 apart from the merits decision. Purpose: the sort into high-hazard versus low-hazard decides whether silence grants or denies, so a mis-sort is a covert denial or a covert waiver and must be reviewable on its own. Enforcement: a hazard-class assignment used to trap an entrant, sorting a comparable entrant to high-hazard while incumbents are sorted low, is caught by the GOV level-capture guard (cite AUTH-I16); an uncited or unpublished class assignment is void on contest under A18-I34. Both flanks: the applicant can contest a pretextual up-classing AND the regulator's honest, clause-cited class assignment stands.

### Protocols

- **Application and decision.** Applicant opens an X5 application encounter (co-owned, per the bilateral-encounter co-control rule). The regulator assigns the hazard class against the published predicate, citing the clause and booking the bearer and S4 audit (AUTH-I19). It applies the published standard (AUTH-I7), and by the bounded deadline records grant, conditional grant, or reasoned denial (AUTH-I8, AUTH-I9), appending the X1 decision event to the permit register (AUTH-I10) and booking the S4 audit against the deciding bearer (AUTH-I17).
- **Deadline lapse.** Actor: the officeholder who let the deadline lapse. Input: the pending application and its assigned hazard class. Output: the standard-defined deemed outcome (deemed-grant for low-hazard, recorded deemed-denial plus a bounded-deadline expedited review for high-hazard), appended as the X1 / R5 permit-register event naming that officeholder, with the loud S4 audit against them and V4 anti-value booked on the duty axis (AUTH-I8).
- **Fee clearance.** The fee clears on the $ rail bounded to attested cost (AUTH-I12); a floor-gating activity with an unable-to-pay applicant proceeds under waiver or deferral.
- **Contest.** A denial, condition, revocation, hazard-class assignment, or fee is contested under A18-I34. A floor-gating denial, revocation, or delay carries mandatory suspensive effect and interim floor continuity under AUTH-I11, so the floor is never cut pending the contest, unless the regulator makes a published, reasoned, imminent-hazard showing. The A6 courts adjudicate against the contemporaneous standard (cite LAW-I4).
- **Register append.** Every registrar and regulator act appends its own X1 event; corrections are new events, never erasures (cite R1, AUTH-I4, AUTH-I6).

### Lifecycle and edge cases

- **Deemed outcomes.** Low-hazard class at deadline: deemed-grant. High-hazard class at deadline: recorded deemed-denial plus a mandatory expedited review that itself terminates in exactly one recorded, reasoned, contestable outcome by a bounded deadline, never silent. Either deemed outcome names the defaulting officeholder as its constructive natural-person bearer, books the loud S4 audit against them, and books V4 anti-value (AUTH-I8).
- **Contested hazard class.** An applicant may contest the up-classing that would sort a deadline lapse to deemed-denial, independently of the merits (AUTH-I19); a class assignment used to trap an entrant is caught by the license-capture guard (AUTH-I16).
- **Changed facts versus changed standard.** A revocation for changed facts rests on changed factual circumstances of the activity or its hazard, adjudicated against the standard contemporaneous with issuance (cite LAW-I4); a revocation resting on a post-grant standard change is void as retroactive, and the tightened standard bites only prospectively at the AUTH-I18 renewal boundary (AUTH-I15).
- **Fraud in the application.** Revocation may reach back because reliance procured by fraud is not good-faith (AUTH-I15); the concealment-class flip may attach.
- **Floor-gating denial, revocation, or delay.** The floor-gating activity continues or interim floor delivery is provided, pending contest, so the floor is never actually cut; compensation covers only priceable collateral loss, never the floor breach itself (AUTH-I11).
- **Emergency and expedited authorizations.** Where a permit must issue under a declared emergency, the RES declaration-authority roster and narrow-and-loud, reviewed-after-the-fact, compensated-when-wrong discipline governs the emergency posture; the permit act itself still books its own bearer and audit. A false emergency declared to force or seize a permit power is a concealment-class harm with the perpetual flip, mirroring the manufactured-public-health-emergency pattern.
- **Conflicted registrar.** Recuse and route to an independent alternate only where the interest is direct and material to the specific record (AUTH-I3); the conflicted act, if taken, loses public faith for that record.
- **Register vs permit boundary.** The registrar's duty is ministerial (AUTH-I4); the regulator's decision is substantive and reasoned (AUTH-I9). Recording a permit in the permit register is a ministerial append, and the AINT-I1 bearer and audit fields are formal completeness checks at that append; deciding the permit is a governed act.

## XACT encounters, interactions and situations (X5, X4)

### Purpose

This section models the two substrate objects on which the state apparatus and every other agent actually touch the world: X5 the interaction (a meeting, transaction, or encounter between agents) and X4 the situation (a state of affairs holding over an interval). These are the raw material that A5 law, A11 offices, A14 permits, A6 disputes, and RES emergencies read from and write to. The design protects both flanks at once. The parties to an interaction and the subject of a situation are protected from surveillance, unilateral disclosure, coerced encounters, and fabricated records, so that private life is default-private and no citizen is exposed to a captured or weaponized state read. At the same time a genuine dispute is fully reachable through A6, a genuine crime is reachable through a targeted A6 unmasking contract, and the honest official who conducts a lawful encounter and the genuine emergency responder are protected from paralysis and from a false accusation by the resolved-bearer and loud-audit discipline.

X5 and X4 are specializations of the X1 occurrence and are cited from Actions-and-Events.md, not re-modelled here. This section adds only the encounter-specific and situation-specific normative surface. A defining feature of a state encounter, carried through the invariants below, is that the state can never certify its own consent: where public authority is a party, coercion is the default posture and the citizen's own co-owned side is the sole source of a valid consent attestation.

### Object model

X5 interaction. A specialization of the X1 occurrence (K2/X1). An X5 record carries: `parties` (a set of two or more agent references, each a B1 person or an O1 organization acting through an O2 position), `channel` (the medium: physical presence, a transaction rail, a message channel, an office counter), `interval` (start, optional end), `outcome` (the recorded result: a transaction, a grant, a refusal, a citation, a nil outcome), and a `consent_state`. The `consent_state` is one of consensual or coerced; for any encounter in which one party exercises public authority it defaults to coerced and MAY be recorded consensual only by an affirmative citizen-side attestation per XACT-I6b. An interaction in which one party is the state resolves that party to a natural-person bearer through the acting-AS-office composition (O1/O2) and appends an X1 event to the owning register (the incident register, the permit register, the office register, or the law register as applicable) with the loud S4 audit.

X4 situation and condition. A specialization of the X1 occurrence describing a state of affairs, not an act. An X4 record carries: `subject` (the agent or asset the situation is about), `interval` (the span over which the condition holds), `status` (the asserted condition value), and a `provenance` chain naming the X1 events that produced it. A situation is a fold over those events, exactly as a register current-state is a non-authoritative fold over R1 events.

Both objects are assertions, not facts. Both may carry conflicting accounts from different provenances at the same time.

### Invariants

XACT-I1 (interaction is an occurrence). An X5 interaction SHALL be recorded as a specialization of the X1 occurrence, carrying parties, channel, interval, outcome, and consent_state. Purpose: one event spine, no shadow encounter log outside K2/X1. Enforcement hook: an X5 record with no backing X1 event id is malformed and SHALL be rejected at write.

XACT-I2 (state encounter resolves a bearer and books the loud audit). Every interaction in which one party exercises public authority SHALL resolve that party to a named natural-person bearer through the acting-AS-office composition and SHALL append the interaction as an X1 event to the owning register with the S4 loud audit. Purpose: no anonymous state power; the citizen sees who acted, and the honest official has a clean contemporaneous record that protects against a later false accusation. Predicate: `is_public_authority(party) implies bearer_natural_person != null AND s4_audit_id != null`. A state encounter with no resolved natural-person bearer is void.

XACT-I3 (bilateral co-ownership). Where an interaction has two or more distinct parties, each party SHALL own its own side of the record, and no party SHALL unilaterally disclose another party's side. Purpose: an encounter is a shared fact, not one party's property, mirroring B10-I5 shared-fact co-control. Enforcement hook: the record is co-owned under S1 one-controller-per-side; a disclosure of a counterparty's side without its S2 contract or a targeted A6 contract is an access violation.

XACT-I4 (cross-reads are contracted projections). A read by one party of another party's side of the encounter SHALL be an S3 projection served under an S2 access contract and SHALL be S4-logged. Purpose: cross-visibility is possible but always mediated, never ambient. Predicate: `cross_side_read implies (s2_contract_id != null AND s4_log_id != null)`.

XACT-I5 (state senses interactions at cohort grain; recurring-pattern reads fail closed). The state SHALL sense the population of interactions only at S5 cohort or k-anonymity grain, where the parties are not resolvable and the live cohort is at or above the S5 k floor. A per-encounter state read that resolves to identified parties SHALL require either the consent of a party disclosing its own side or a targeted A6 judicial contract naming the encounter. Sensing a recurring pattern between the same identified party set is a per-encounter and per-person read, not a cohort read, and SHALL fail closed absent that same consent or a targeted A6 contract naming the parties; only genuinely k-anonymous cohort aggregation, in which no party is resolvable and the cohort holds at or above the S5 floor across every sensed interval, is permitted without a gate. Purpose: the state sees patterns across persons, never a pattern about a person; a same-party recurrence read is exactly the dragnet that reconstructs an identified relationship from ostensibly aggregate data. Both flanks: cohort sensing keeps governance informed without a surveillance dragnet, and A6 keeps a genuine investigation reachable. Enforcement hook: a per-encounter or same-party-recurrence state query without a consent token or an A6 contract id SHALL fail closed; only the numeric value of k is left to S5 calibration.

XACT-I6 (consent gate on the encounter). An ordinary consensual interaction SHALL be default-private and SHALL NOT be exposed beyond its parties. A non-consensual or coercive encounter (an intrusion, a forced meeting, a stop, a search, a seizure, a compelled appearance) SHALL route the coercion through the single A18-I4 gate and SHALL record its `consent_state` as coerced. Purpose: consent is the line between a private interaction and an exercise of force. Predicate: `consent_state == coerced implies a18_i4_rung_id != null`. Both flanks: the citizen is never subjected to off-gate force, and the official who clears A18-I4 has an authorization on record.

XACT-I6b (the state cannot self-certify consent). For any encounter in which one party exercises public authority, `consent_state` SHALL default to coerced and the A18-I4 rung SHALL be mandatory, UNLESS the citizen's own co-owned side of the record, S1-controlled by the citizen per XACT-I3, affirmatively attests consent. A "consensual" label authored by the state party with no citizen-side consent attestation is malformed, and the encounter SHALL fail closed to coerced with the A18-I4 rung required. Any physical-contact class of encounter, a stop, a search, a seizure, or a compelled appearance, is coerced by construction and SHALL carry `consent_state == coerced` regardless of any recorded label or any purported citizen attestation. Purpose: coercion by the state cannot be laundered into consent by the state's own record-keeping; only the person on the receiving end can attest that a state touch was consented to, and a hands-on encounter is force by definition. Predicate: `is_public_authority(party) AND NOT citizen_side_consent_attested implies consent_state == coerced AND a18_i4_rung_id != null`; and `physical_contact_class implies consent_state == coerced`. Both flanks: the citizen is never bound by a consent they did not give, and the honest official who obtains and records a genuine citizen-side attestation for a non-contact encounter has a clean consensual record.

XACT-I7 (encounter floor: A9 absolute, capability floor gated). No encounter, however authorized, and no measure taken within one, SHALL ever breach, gate, or price an A9 inviolable right; this floor is absolute, non-priceable at the point of contact, and a breach under color of authority is a non-priceable harm under A18. A delivered COMP-I17 capability floor MAY be temporarily and proportionately limited within an encounter only under a measure that clears the A18-I4 gate, is the least-restrictive effective option, is time-boxed, and triggers the compensation duty for the affected person; a limitation that is not proportionate, time-boxed, or compensated is caught. Purpose: mirrors the emergency-floor split at INCIDENT-I11a/b so the two floors are governed by one rule across encounters and emergencies. Enforcement hook: a floor-breach predicate on the encounter blocks execution before the outcome is written; an A9 breach is blocked and never remediable by a price, and a capability-floor limitation that fails the proportionate, time-boxed, and compensated test is likewise blocked before the outcome is written and is never discharged by compensation after the fact.

XACT-I8 (interaction is assertion, not fact). An X5 record SHALL be treated as a PROV assertion carrying its provenance, never as settled truth, and conflicting accounts of the same encounter SHALL be preserved side by side rather than merged or overwritten. Purpose: mirrors the X1 assertion-not-fact discipline and D2 evidence discipline; an encounter is what someone recorded, an indicator, not a verdict. Enforcement hook: a write that would delete or overwrite a counterparty's account is rejected; correction is a new event.

XACT-I9 (anonymous and pseudonymous interaction; bounded identity exception). A person MAY transact, meet, or otherwise interact without presenting a standing identity, and the state SHALL NOT impose a general standing-identity demand as a condition of ordinary interaction. An A5 rule or an A14 permit MAY condition a specific interaction on standing identity ONLY where identity is necessary to the specific regulated harm and no less-identifying means, in particular an S6 zero-knowledge predicate that proves the required fact without disclosing who the party is, suffices; each such condition is reviewable at A6 against that necessity-and-least-identifying-means test. The set of standing-identity conditions in force SHALL be held under an aggregate guard, mirroring the GOV level-capture guard: the cumulative reach of identity-demanding rules SHALL NOT amount to a de facto general standing-identity regime even where each rule is individually justified, and a set that crosses that threshold is caught and the marginal condition SHALL NOT take effect. Purpose: default-private civic and economic life, with a narrow, reviewable, and cumulatively bounded exception rather than an open-ended one; identity is proven where a specific regulated harm genuinely needs it and no privacy-preserving proof will do, not everywhere by default and not by the slow accretion of a thousand small rules. Both flanks: the citizen keeps pseudonymous room and the aggregate guard against death by a thousand rules, and a specific rule that genuinely needs identity for a specific harm can still require it at that point.

XACT-I10 (unmasking needs a targeted court contract). Resolving a pseudonymous or anonymous party to a standing identity SHALL require a targeted A6 contract that names the encounter and states the genuine offense under investigation, and SHALL be S4-logged. Purpose: unmasking is a court act, not an administrative convenience, and a general fishing read is barred. Predicate: `unmask(party) implies (a6_contract_id != null AND named_offense != null)`. Both flanks: privacy holds against blanket de-anonymization, and a genuine crime is reachable through the named-offense gate.

XACT-I11 (situation is subject-owned). An X4 situation SHALL be owned by its subject under S1 one-controller. A read of a subject's situation by another agent SHALL be an S3 projection under an S2 contract, or S5 cohort sensing for the state, or a targeted A6 contract; there is no ambient third-party right to a person's condition. Purpose: a person's state of affairs is theirs to disclose. Enforcement hook: same fail-closed rule as XACT-I5 applied to X4 reads, including the same-subject recurring-pattern read.

XACT-I12 (situation is a fold over events). An X4 situation SHALL be a non-authoritative fold over the X1 events that produced it, and a correction SHALL be a new event, never an in-place edit of the fold. Purpose: mirrors the R1 register-fold and correction-by-new-event rule; honest revaluation under D2 rather than silent rewriting of history. Predicate: `situation.status derives_from provenance_events AND correction is_a new_event`.

XACT-I13 (contract-boundary snapshot is lawful and marked). A snapshot of a situation taken at a contract boundary or a governance-sensing boundary SHALL be lawful and SHALL be explicitly marked as a snapshot with its capture time and provenance. Purpose: a contract may lawfully freeze the state of affairs it is priced against, mirroring the architecture snapshot rule, but a snapshot must never masquerade as the live condition. Enforcement hook: a snapshot lacking a `snapshot_at` and provenance marker is malformed; a party that presents a stale snapshot as current commits a D2 evidence-discipline breach. Both flanks: the counterparty relying on the boundary state is protected, and the subject is protected from a frozen figure being read as an ongoing truth.

XACT-I14 (situation sensing at cohort grain). Governance sensing over situations (imbalance and corridor monitoring per Governance-Mechanics.md) SHALL consume situations at S5 cohort grain, and a per-subject situation read for governance SHALL require consent or a targeted A6 contract. Purpose: the value-balancing sensor sees the distribution, not the individual. Both flanks: governance stays informed while no steward gains a per-person condition feed, and a targeted lawful need remains reachable.

XACT-I15 (disputed encounter or situation routes to A6). A conflict between preserved accounts of an interaction, or between asserted statuses of a situation, SHALL be resolvable through A6, and the dispute SHALL be judged by the norms contemporaneous with the encounter or the interval in question. Purpose: disputes have a forum, and the citizen is judged by the rules that were in force when the event occurred, not by later rules applied backward. Enforcement hook: routing an XACT dispute pins the applicable norm version to the encounter or interval timestamp.

XACT-I16 (manufactured encounter or fabricated situation is concealment-class). Staging a coercive encounter under a false pretext, or fabricating or backdating a situation record, to seize or justify power SHALL be a concealment-class harm. Where the pretext is a manufactured or pretextual emergency, the concealment-class treatment, the perpetual flip, and the honest-declarer documented-basis carve-out are those defined at INCIDENT-I13, which this invariant CITES and does not restate; XACT-I16 adds only the encounter-and-situation-fabrication trigger, that a staged encounter or a fabricated or backdated X4 record is itself the fabricating act that INCIDENT-I13 governs. Purpose: closes the sharpest capture surface, the state inventing the very facts that authorize its force, under a single owning definition rather than a parallel one. Both flanks: the fabricator is exposed without a limitation shield under the INCIDENT-I13 regime, and the genuine emergency responder who declares under the RES narrow-and-loud regime and records honestly is protected precisely because the fabricated case is the one that is punished.

XACT-I17 (no unilateral destruction of a bilateral record). Neither party to a co-owned interaction record SHALL unilaterally destroy or expunge the shared record; erasure of a party's own side SHALL be a new event that preserves the counterparty's ability to trace and contest, subject to R1-I28 public-faith projection and R1-I39 post-judgment tracing. Purpose: a shared fact cannot be erased by one side to defeat the other's claim, while genuine erasure rights over one's own side are honored through the event log rather than through deletion.

XACT-I18 (public-faith reliance on a published situation). Where a situation projection is published under R1-I28 public-faith, a third party relying on it in good faith SHALL be protected, and the subject's remedy against an incorrect projection SHALL be a new correcting event and, if contested, A6, not a retroactive invalidation of the good-faith reliance. Purpose: registers can be trusted at face value, while the subject keeps a live, non-destructive path to correction. Both flanks: the relying party is safe and the subject is not silenced.

### Protocols

Encounter write. Actor: the party opening the encounter (for a state encounter, the natural-person bearer acting AS office). Input: the parties, channel, and proposed outcome. Output: a written X5 record and its backing X1 event. Steps: (1) create the backing X1 event; (2) resolve each state party to its natural-person bearer through O1/O2 and open the S4 loud audit (XACT-I2); (3) set `consent_state`, defaulting a public-authority encounter to coerced and reading it consensual only on an affirmative citizen-side attestation, and forcing coerced on any physical-contact class (XACT-I6, XACT-I6b); if coerced, obtain the A18-I4 rung authorization before writing the outcome; (4) run the A9 and COMP-I17 floor predicate and block on breach, blocking an A9 breach absolutely and a non-proportionate, non-time-boxed, or uncompensated capability-floor limitation before the outcome is written (XACT-I7); (5) write each party's side under its own S1 controller (XACT-I3). S4 audit event: the loud audit opened at step 2 records the completed write.

Cross-side read. Actor: a party or the state seeking a counterparty's side. Input: an S2 contract or a targeted A6 contract. Output: an S3 projection and an S4 log entry. Steps: present the contract, receive the S3 projection, and log the read to S4 (XACT-I4). For the state, default to an S5 cohort query at or above the k floor; escalate to a per-encounter read, including any same-party recurring-pattern read, only with consent or an A6 contract (XACT-I5). S4 audit event: the read log.

Unmasking. Actor: an investigating office. Input: an A6 contract naming the encounter and the offense. Output: the S3 identity projection and an S4 log entry. Steps: obtain the A6 contract, execute the S3 identity projection, and S4-log it (XACT-I9, XACT-I10). Absent the contract, the read fails closed. S4 audit event: the unmasking log.

Situation snapshot. Actor: the contracting or sensing party. Input: the live situation fold. Output: a marked non-live snapshot. Steps: capture the fold, stamp `snapshot_at` and provenance, and mark it non-live (XACT-I13). A governance sense over situations runs as an S5 cohort fold (XACT-I14). S4 audit event: the snapshot-capture record where the capturing party is a state office.

Dispute. Actor: a party or subject contesting an encounter or situation. Input: the preserved conflicting accounts. Output: an A6 judgment. Steps: route to A6, pin the contemporaneous norm version to the relevant timestamp, and let A6 adjudicate over the preserved conflicting accounts (XACT-I8, XACT-I15). Corrections land as new events (XACT-I12), never in-place edits. S4 audit event: the A6 routing and judgment records.

### Lifecycle and edge cases

Nil-outcome encounter. An encounter with no substantive result (a stop that yields nothing, a refused counter request) is still an X5 event with an outcome of nil and, if it involved any coercion, still carries the A18-I4 rung and the S4 audit. Purpose: a fruitless coercive encounter is exactly the one a captured actor would prefer to leave unrecorded.

Third party present. A bystander swept into an encounter is a party to the extent their own side is recorded; their side is theirs under XACT-I3 and is not disclosable through another party's contract.

Ongoing versus closed situation. A situation with an open interval is live and is read as such; once closed, later reads are of a historical condition. A stale open-interval situation presented as current when its provenance has lapsed is a D2 evidence breach (XACT-I13).

Consent withdrawn mid-encounter. If consent is withdrawn during an interaction, the `consent_state` transitions to coerced from that point and any continuation requires the A18-I4 gate (XACT-I6). The record preserves both phases.

State encounter labeled consensual. A state encounter recorded consensual with no citizen-side attestation, or one involving physical contact recorded consensual, is malformed and re-reads as coerced with the A18-I4 rung required (XACT-I6b); the missing or contradicted attestation does not merely lower evidentiary weight, it flips the encounter to the coerced posture at read time.

Emergency encounter. An encounter conducted under a RES declaration follows the narrow-and-loud, reviewed-after-the-fact, compensated-when-wrong discipline of RES; it is not exempt from XACT-I2, XACT-I6, XACT-I6b, or XACT-I7, and the declaration authority is on the roster. Its A9 floor is absolute and its capability-floor limits obey the XACT-I7 gated, time-boxed, compensated test. A false emergency pretext is caught by XACT-I16 through INCIDENT-I13.

Border interface. Exit and entry restriction encounters are deferred to the A16 border doctrine (per the F7 MOVE deferral) and are out of scope here; this section notes the interface but does not model A15/A16/A17. An in-territory encounter that a border rule attempts to reach still obeys XACT-I2, XACT-I6, and XACT-I6b.

2. The minimum cohort size k for S5 interaction sensing where interactions are sparse (rare channels), so that cohort sensing does not de facto identify a single pair; this numeric floor is delegated to S5 per XACT-I5 and is open only as an S5 calibration, not as a policy question in this section.
3. The evidentiary weight A6 assigns to a marked contract-boundary snapshot (XACT-I13) versus a live situation read when the two conflict at judgment time.
4. The precise threshold at which the XACT-I9 aggregate guard against a de facto general standing-identity regime trips, mirroring the calibration of the GOV level-capture guard, pending A10 and A6 practice.

## INCIDENT incidents, emergencies and phenomena (X3, X2)

### Purpose

This section models three objects the state apparatus acts on and through when the world goes wrong or moves under it: X3 the incident (a harm event requiring response), the emergency (a governed escalation of state power triggered by an incident class), and X2 the phenomenon (a recurring natural or social pattern the state senses to stay ahead of harm). It specifies how an incident is recorded, how its severity is decided, how an emergency is declared and bounded, how a manufactured emergency is caught, and how a phenomenon informs governance without ever becoming a per-person surveillance power.

Both flanks are load-bearing throughout. The public is entitled to a real, proportionate, time-boxed response to genuine harm and to honest early warning of building imbalance; no person is entitled to be subjected to a manufactured, indefinite, or rights-breaching emergency, and no phenomenon may be resolved into surveillance of a named person without a targeted judicial contract. The honest first responder and the genuine emergency are equally protected: from paralysis, from second-guessing of good-faith action taken under the published rules, and from a false accusation of pretext.

This section does not re-model the RES emergency-declaration regime (Missions-Science-and-Ecology.md), the A18-I4 coercion gate, the A18-I7 definiteness precondition, or the A18 concealment class (Offense-and-Enforcement.md), the R1 register mechanics or A12 registry mandate (Registry-and-Ledger.md), the A6 judicial path (Courts-and-Adjudication.md), the A10 lawmaking calibration (Civic-Health-and-Democracy.md), the MEAS public-health-measure regime (Social-Provision-and-Public-Health.md), the D2 evidence discipline, or the S5 cohort-sensing and k-anonymity rule (Security-Ownership-and-Access.md). It cites them and binds the state-apparatus incident and phenomenon objects onto them. It owns exactly one genuinely new emergency rule of its own, the single definition of a manufactured or pretextual emergency as a concealment-class harm (INCIDENT-I13), which the AINT and XACT surfaces cite rather than restate.

### Object model

- **X3 incident**: a specialization of the X1 occurrence (Actions-and-Events.md, cited), typed as a harm event requiring a state response. Fields: hazard_class, onset_time and detected_time, affected_scope (cohort or territory at S5 grain), severity_tier (INCIDENT-I3), response (the K2 acts taken in reply, each an X1 event), resolution (the terminating event and its verdict), and a status in {open, responding, contained, resolved, revalued}. An incident is a record of harm, not an act; the state's replies to it are K2 acts with resolved natural-person bearers.
- **Severity tier**: a value drawn from the published RES criticality-tier vocabulary (cited), assigned by a decidable predicate over incident facts, not by live discretion, and never a function of the state's response posture (INCIDENT-I2, INCIDENT-I3).
- **Emergency declaration**: a K2 act by an officeholder on the RES declaration-authority roster (cited) that opens a bounded set of emergency powers. Fields: declaring_officeholder (a B1 natural person acting AS an O2 office, per Organizations-Contracts-and-Employment.md), named hazard, named scope, enabled measure-classes, hard sunset_time, and the independent evidence basis. It is the loudest S4 audit event in the model.
- **Emergency measure**: a coercive or rights-affecting act taken under a live declaration. Each is an individual K2 act that routes through the A18-I4 coercion gate (cited), is individually addressed to its target, and is individually contestable under A18-I34.
- **X2 phenomenon**: a recurring natural or social pattern (a trend, a drift, a cluster) observed by the state at cohort grain. Fields: pattern_class, observation_series (INCIDENT-I16, INCIDENT-I17b), stated method, stated uncertainty, and a version chain. It is an indicator, never a truth and never a per-person fact.
- **Observation series**: the append-only, evidence-pinned sequence of cohort-grain measurements behind a phenomenon, carrying the D2 evidence discipline (cited), suppressed or coarsened at any interval where its live cohort falls below the S5 k floor (INCIDENT-I17b).
- **Incident register**: an R1 instance (cited), append-only, corrections by new event, wrapped by an A12 registry mandate that names the register, its registrar, and its public-faith rule.

### Invariants

- **INCIDENT-I1** (the incident is an X1 occurrence, not an act). An X3 incident SHALL be recorded as a specialization of the X1 occurrence (cited), carrying no bearer of its own; every state reply to it SHALL be a distinct K2 act that appends its own X1 event to the owning register and resolves to a named natural-person bearer. Purpose: a harm event and the state's response to it are separately auditable, so the public can see both that harm occurred and who acted on it. Predicate: an incident record with a bearer field, or a response with none, is malformed and rejected at append.

- **INCIDENT-I2** (mandatory response; a window lapse escalates response, not severity). An incident at or above the response threshold of its severity tier SHALL bind a named responsible office to act within the tier's published response window. A lapse of that window without a recorded justifying ground SHALL escalate the RESPONSE and accountability posture and SHALL NOT change the incident's severity_tier: it books V4 anti-value against the defaulting officeholder, a named superior office assumes the response duty, and oversight escalates by default. Severity stays strictly a function of hazard facts under INCIDENT-I3, and any higher response tier still requires a new evidence-pinned fact event under INCIDENT-I4. Symmetrically, an officeholder who acts in good faith within the published rules and windows SHALL NOT be faulted for an outcome the rules did not let them prevent (a duty-of-means standard on the RES-I25 pattern, cited). Purpose: the public is protected from silent inaction by escalating who must act, without letting a procedural lapse counterfeit a hazard fact, and the honest responder is protected from hindsight liability.

- **INCIDENT-I3** (severity is decided by a published, decidable tier). The severity_tier of an incident SHALL be assigned by a decidable predicate over recorded incident facts against the published RES criticality tiers (cited), version-pinned to the tier definition in force at assignment time. Ad-hoc live re-classification to unlock a power or evade a floor is prohibited. Purpose: severity governs which powers open, so it cannot be a discretionary dial. Predicate: a severity assignment whose facts do not satisfy the cited tier predicate, or that cites no tier version, is void.

- **INCIDENT-I4** (re-classification is a new evidence-pinned event, never an edit). A change of severity_tier SHALL be a new register event citing the changed facts and the D2 evidence for them (cited), never a mutation of the prior record; a downward re-classification that would close an open floor breach or terminate a compensation duty SHALL carry the same evidentiary standard as the original upward assignment and is contestable at A6. Purpose: severity cannot be quietly walked back to shed obligations, and honest revaluation on new evidence remains open.

- **INCIDENT-I5** (the incident register is an R1 instance under an A12 mandate). The incident register SHALL be an R1 instance (cited): append-only, current state a non-authoritative fold, every correction a new event. An A12 registry mandate (cited) SHALL name the register, assign its registrar, and fix its public-faith rule; the register's cohort-grain projection carries the R1-I28 public-faith standing (cited), and post-judgment tracing runs on R1-I39 (cited). Purpose: the record of state response to harm is tamper-evident and no entry can be silently deleted.

- **INCIDENT-I6** (the emergency declaration binds to the RES governed act). An emergency declaration SHALL be a K2 act by an officeholder on the RES declaration-authority roster and SHALL satisfy the RES narrow-and-loud pattern (both cited); this section adds only that the declaration appends to the incident register as the loudest S4 audit event and resolves to the declaring natural person acting AS the office (Organizations-Contracts-and-Employment.md O1/O2 composition, cited). A declaration that fails the cited RES pattern, or that lacks a named roster authority, is void and confers no power. Purpose: emergency power opens through the RES regime, not a second apparatus regime, while its register binding and personal ownership are fixed here.

- **INCIDENT-I7** (hard sunset, no silent extension). Every declaration SHALL carry the RES hard sunset and no-silent-extension rule (cited): all enabled powers lapse by default at sunset, and extension SHALL be a fresh declaration on the full RES standard with its own independent evidence, never a renewal by silence and never an amendment of the original. Purpose: the burden of continuing an emergency stays on the state at every step, and lapse to normal law is the default.

- **INCIDENT-I8** (mandatory after-the-fact review). Every declaration SHALL undergo the RES after-the-fact review by an organ independent of the declaring authority, against the norms and evidence contemporaneous with the declaration (cited); this section adds only that the review's finding appends to the incident register. Purpose: emergency power is audited even when it was used honestly, and the record supports both compensation for wrong use and exoneration of right use.

- **INCIDENT-I9** (every emergency measure is bounded coercion through A18-I4). A coercive or rights-affecting emergency measure SHALL route through the single A18-I4 coercion gate (cited), fail-closed until A10 calibrates; this section does not restate the max_rung formula. It adds only that the measure SHALL be the least-restrictive option effective against the named hazard, addressed no wider than the named scope, and time-boxed within the declaration's sunset. Purpose: a declaration opens a bounded envelope, not a blank coercive check; the emergency does not create a second, looser coercion path.

- **INCIDENT-I9a** (interim conservative emergency-rung calibration). Pending A10 calibration of the A18-I4 emergency max_rung, the RES regime SHALL supply an interim, published, conservative emergency-rung calibration per criticality tier, so that a genuine declared emergency is not left with a zero coercive envelope while calibration is outstanding. The interim calibration is narrow (bounded to the named hazard, scope, and enabled measure-classes), loud (each rung books the S4 audit), sunset-bounded (INCIDENT-I7), reviewed after the fact (INCIDENT-I8), individually contestable under A18-I34 (INCIDENT-I10), and floor-bounded by INCIDENT-I11a and INCIDENT-I11b. Purpose: the fail-closed default protects against overreach without paralyzing a real emergency, and the interim envelope is itself the narrowest that answers the hazard. Predicate: a rung exceeding the published interim calibration for the incident's tier, or applied absent a live declaration, is void.

- **INCIDENT-I10** (individual contestability survives the emergency). Every emergency measure SHALL be individually addressed and individually contestable under A18-I34 suspensive contest (cited); the emergency context SHALL NOT strip, defer past sunset, or collectivize a target's contest right. Purpose: urgency justifies speed of action, never the removal of the individual's day in court.

- **INCIDENT-I11a** (the A9 inviolable floor is never touched, even in emergency). No declaration and no emergency measure SHALL EVER breach, gate, or price any A9 inviolable right; the A9 set is absolute and non-priceable. A breach committed under color of emergency is a non-priceable criminal act (A9, A18, cited) and the declaration confers no cover for it. Purpose: the floor that bounds ordinary power bounds emergency power identically; emergency is a reason for speed, never a licence against the person. Predicate: a measure that on its face gates or prices an A9 right is void before effect and is not remedied after.

- **INCIDENT-I11b** (a delivered capability floor may be limited only under a gated, time-boxed, compensated measure). A delivered COMP-I17 capability floor (cited) MAY be temporarily and proportionately limited by an emergency measure only where the measure clears the A18-I4 gate (cited), is the least-restrictive effective option against the named hazard (INCIDENT-I9), is time-boxed within the sunset (INCIDENT-I7), and triggers the RES wrong-declaration and compensation duty for the affected persons (INCIDENT-I12). A limitation that is not proportionate, not time-boxed, or not compensated is caught: it is void as an ungated floor breach and the cost books against the responsible office. Purpose: a genuine emergency may briefly narrow a delivered floor to answer a real hazard, but only on the gated, bounded, compensated terms, never as a standing deprivation.

- **INCIDENT-I12** (compensation for wrongly used emergency power). Where the after-the-fact review (INCIDENT-I8) or an A6 ruling finds a declaration or measure unwarranted, disproportionate, or wrongly targeted, the affected persons SHALL be compensated on the RES wrong-declaration pattern (cited), and the cost books against the responsible office, never against the victim class (RES-I21 victim-class pattern, cited). Honest, within-rules action found warranted books no fault. Purpose: the state bears the cost of its own error, and the genuine emergency responder is not deterred by the risk of good-faith mistakes.

- **INCIDENT-I13** (manufactured or pretextual emergency is a concealment-class harm; single owner). This is the single definition in the model of a manufactured or pretextual emergency as a concealment-class harm; AINT-I11 and XACT-I16 cite it by ID rather than restate it. Declaring an emergency the declarant knew or should have known was false, exaggerated, or fabricated in order to seize coercive power SHALL be a concealment-class A18 harm carrying the perpetual flip (cited), with a resolved natural-person bearer (the declaring officeholder and any acting-in-concert principals under the O-cluster veil rule, cited), mirroring the MEAS manufactured-public-health-emergency pattern (cited). The honest-declarer carve-out is stated once here: a declaration made in good faith on a documented independent basis books no concealment fault even where later found unwarranted. Purpose: the worst abuse of this surface, weaponizing a false crisis, is placed in the most severe, non-time-barred harm class, while the honest crisis responder is protected. Predicate: a declaration whose named hazard is contradicted by the pinned evidence at declaration time, absent a good-faith documented basis, meets the concealment standard.

- **INCIDENT-I14** (independent evidence to declare; no ratchet by re-declaration). A declaration SHALL rest on evidence independent of the declaring authority's own assertion, pinned on the D2 discipline (cited); a chain of serial re-declarations over the same or a mutating hazard SHALL NOT ratchet into a standing power. The independent after-the-fact review escalates on each re-declaration, and repeated re-declaration without fresh independent evidence is itself evidence toward the INCIDENT-I13 concealment finding. The GOV level-capture guard (cited) bars any single steward from uniformly relaxing declaration thresholds in incumbents' favor. Purpose: an emergency cannot be self-certified into permanence, and the sunset cannot be defeated by repetition.

- **INCIDENT-I15** (emergency access is narrow, loud, and destroyed at sunset by default). Where a declaration authorizes access to otherwise contracted or sealed data, that access SHALL run on the S-cluster X3 emergency-access pattern (cited): scoped to the named hazard and scope, logged as loud S4, time-boxed within the sunset, and reviewed after the fact with mandatory backfill of the justifying record. Per-person data gathered under emergency access SHALL default to DESTRUCTION at sunset unless a fresh, independent, A6-gated lawful basis is recorded BEFORE sunset. Any quarantine retained on that fresh basis SHALL name a natural-person custodian bearer, be S4-logged, be sealed against query until the fresh basis clears, and terminate at a published hard cap on quarantine duration, after which destruction is mandatory. Emergency access SHALL NOT be used to assemble a standing per-person dataset that outlives the emergency. Purpose: crisis may justify a narrow, temporary look, never a permanent widening of the state's gaze; the default at sunset is that the data is gone, not merely paused.

- **INCIDENT-I16** (the phenomenon is an indicator, not a truth). An X2 phenomenon and its observation_series SHALL carry the full D2 evidence discipline (cited): pinned evidence, stated method, stated uncertainty, version-chained revaluation. No single phenomenon metric SHALL be a sufficient basis for coercion against any person or organization; a phenomenon informs sensing and priority, and any hard consequence routes through the ordinary evidentiary and A18-I4 paths on its own facts. Purpose: a trend line is a reason to look, never a verdict, and a moving indicator cannot be frozen into a charge.

- **INCIDENT-I17** (phenomena are sensed at cohort grain, never resolved to persons without a judicial contract). The state SHALL sense phenomena at cohort grain under S5 (cited) and feed them to the imbalance signal and corridor monitoring (Governance-Mechanics.md, Value-Axes-Corridors-Voting.md, cited) as early-warning and value-balancing inputs. Resolving a phenomenon into observation of a named person SHALL require consent or a targeted A6 judicial access contract (cited); building a per-person profile by disaggregating cohort phenomena is prohibited and, where done to evade the contract requirement, is a concealment-class harm. Purpose: honest early warning protects the public, and it does so without ever becoming a surveillance power over individuals.

- **INCIDENT-I17b** (a thinning series is suppressed at the k floor). An observation_series projection SHALL be suppressed or coarsened at any interval where its live cohort falls below the S5 k floor (cited); the append-only underlying record is retained, and the suppression is itself a new projection-control event on the register, not a deletion. Purpose: a series whose cohort thins toward a handful of persons must never become per-person identification by the back door, while the evidentiary record behind it stays intact. Predicate: a published series interval whose live cohort is below the S5 k parameter, absent an applied suppression or coarsening event, is malformed.

- **INCIDENT-I18** (honest early warning is protected from suppression). Suppressing, delaying, or altering a phenomenon's observation_series or an incident's severity assignment to avoid triggering a response, a floor obligation, or a corridor signal SHALL be a concealment-class A18 harm with a natural-person bearer; conversely, a good-faith honest revaluation that lowers an indicator on pinned new evidence (D2, cited) books no fault, and a k-floor suppression under INCIDENT-I17b is a protected projection control, not a concealment. Purpose: the sensing apparatus cannot be silenced to spare the state an obligation, and honest correction or lawful suppression of an indicator remains safe.

- **INCIDENT-I19** (a coercive act during a witnessing outage fails closed). Under a witnessing outage, only NON-coercive continuity and preservation acts MAY be admitted through the bounded degraded mode (X1-I17, cited, the only lawful degraded path); any new coercive rung SHALL fail closed until witnessing recovers and the A18-I4 gate and S4 audit actually resolve. A coercive act taken during a witnessing outage without a completed A18-I4 gate is void, not merely backfillable; a missing backfill after recovery voids the provisional inclusion and every act it covered. A manufactured, induced, or deliberately prolonged witnessing outage used to act unaudited SHALL be a concealment-class A18 harm with the perpetual flip and a resolved natural-person bearer (AINT-I13a, cited). Purpose: an outage may preserve the state's ability to keep people safe by non-coercive means, but it may never become a window to coerce off the record; the incentive to go dark is removed by voiding the acts and flipping the manufactured outage into the concealment class.

### Protocols

- **INCIDENT-P1** (incident intake and response). Actor: the responsible office and its named officeholder. Input: a detected hazard signal. (1) Detect and append the X3 record with hazard_class and affected_scope at S5 grain (INCIDENT-I1). (2) Assign severity_tier by the published RES predicate, version-pinned (INCIDENT-I3). (3) Bind the responsible office and open the tier response window (INCIDENT-I2). (4) Record each reply as a distinct K2 act with its bearer and X1 event. (5) On window lapse, escalate response and accountability without changing severity_tier (INCIDENT-I2); re-classify only by new evidence-pinned event (INCIDENT-I4). (6) Terminate with a resolution event and status; leave the record open to revaluation. Output and audit: the appended incident-register events, each booking the S4 audit.

- **INCIDENT-P2** (declaration and emergency power). Actor: the roster authority (a named natural person acting AS the office). Input: independent pinned evidence for the named hazard (INCIDENT-I14). (1) The roster authority declares on the RES narrow-and-loud pattern, naming hazard, scope, enabled measure-classes, and hard sunset, as the loudest S4 event resolving to the natural person (INCIDENT-I6). (2) Each measure routes through A18-I4 within the interim conservative calibration for the tier as the least-restrictive effective, scope-bounded, time-boxed option (INCIDENT-I9, INCIDENT-I9a), individually addressed and contestable under A18-I34 (INCIDENT-I10), never touching the A9 floor (INCIDENT-I11a) and limiting a delivered capability floor only on the gated, time-boxed, compensated terms (INCIDENT-I11b). (3) Any emergency data access runs the S-cluster X3 pattern; per-person data defaults to destruction at sunset absent a fresh A6-gated basis recorded before sunset (INCIDENT-I15). (4) Powers lapse at sunset by default; extension is a fresh declaration (INCIDENT-I7). (5) The independent after-the-fact review publishes warrant and proportionality findings against contemporaneous norms (INCIDENT-I8); wrong use compensates on the RES pattern (INCIDENT-I12). (6) A public-health emergency runs this protocol through MEAS (cited), not as a second regime. Output and audit: the declaration, each measure, the review finding, and any destruction or quarantine event, each booking the S4 audit.

- **INCIDENT-P3** (phenomenon sensing and routing). Actor: the sensing office and its named officeholder. Input: cohort-grain observations under S5. (1) Build the observation_series at cohort grain under S5 with pinned evidence, stated method and uncertainty (INCIDENT-I16, D2, cited), suppressing or coarsening any interval below the S5 k floor (INCIDENT-I17b). (2) Feed it to the imbalance signal and corridor monitoring as an indicator (INCIDENT-I16, INCIDENT-I17). (3) Revalue honestly on new evidence, version-chained (INCIDENT-I18). (4) Any per-person resolution routes through consent or a targeted A6 contract, never by disaggregation (INCIDENT-I17). (5) Any hard consequence proves out on its own facts through the ordinary evidentiary and coercion paths (INCIDENT-I16). Output and audit: the appended series and projection-control events, each booking the S4 audit.

### Lifecycle and edge cases

- **Incident under a witnessing outage.** Only non-coercive continuity and preservation acts are admitted through the bounded degraded mode (X1-I17, cited); a witnessing outage SHALL NOT freeze the state's non-coercive ability to respond to a genuine incident. Any new coercive rung fails closed until witnessing recovers and the A18-I4 gate and S4 audit resolve; a coercive act with no completed gate is void, not backfillable, and a missing backfill after recovery voids the provisional inclusion and the acts it covered (INCIDENT-I19). A manufactured or prolonged outage used to act unaudited is a concealment-class harm with a natural-person bearer (INCIDENT-I19, AINT-I13a, cited).
- **Incident that is also an offense.** Where an incident is caused by a chargeable act, the harm record (X3) and the enforcement action (A18) are separate objects on separate registers; the severity tier governs the response, the A18-I4 gate governs any coercion, and neither substitutes for the other.
- **Declaration during a leadership gap.** The declaration-authority roster (RES, cited) names the acting authority through succession (Leadership-Selection-and-Succession.md, cited); a declaration by a person not on the roster at declaration time is void (INCIDENT-I6), and the gap does not open an unauthorized emergency.
- **Sunset reached mid-measure.** At sunset, all enabled powers lapse; an in-flight measure completes only its non-coercive wind-down, and any continuing coercion requires a fresh declaration. Per-person data gathered under emergency access is destroyed at sunset by default unless a fresh, independent, A6-gated lawful basis was recorded before sunset (INCIDENT-I15).
- **Window lapse does not raise severity.** A missed response window escalates who must act and the accountability posture and books V4, but the severity_tier is unchanged; any higher response tier requires a new evidence-pinned fact event (INCIDENT-I2, INCIDENT-I4). A procedural lapse cannot counterfeit a hazard fact.
- **Phenomenon that crosses into an incident.** A phenomenon that materializes as concrete harm spawns an X3 incident on its own facts; the phenomenon's indicator status does not lower the incident's evidentiary burden, and the incident's response does not retroactively convert the indicator into a per-person truth.
- **Thinning observation series.** As a series' live cohort falls below the S5 k floor, its projection is suppressed or coarsened by a new projection-control event while the underlying append-only record is retained (INCIDENT-I17b); a thinning series never becomes per-person identification.
- **Serial small emergencies.** A run of narrow re-declarations that together approximate a standing power triggers escalating review and counts toward an INCIDENT-I13 concealment finding (INCIDENT-I14); the individual smallness of each does not launder the aggregate ratchet.
- **Honest false alarm.** A declaration made in good faith on a documented independent basis that the after-the-fact review finds unwarranted books no concealment fault against the declarant (INCIDENT-I2 duty-of-means, INCIDENT-I12 no-fault-on-honest-action, INCIDENT-I13 honest-declarer carve-out), while still compensating the affected persons for the wrong use; only knowledge or reckless disregard of falsity meets INCIDENT-I13.

- **INCIDENT-Q1** (cross-scope incident routing; interim rule stated). ## AINT apparatus and interaction integrity and capture surface

### Purpose

This section is the integrity and anti-capture surface for the state-apparatus and interaction-events layer, the pass in which the state acts on the world. It is the sharpest capture surface in the model because here the attacker is the state itself: a law, an office, a permit, a register, or an emergency power turned against a citizen. Every invariant below protects BOTH flanks. The citizen is protected from arbitrary, secret, captured, retroactive, or weaponized public power. The honest official, the genuine emergency, the honest regulator, and the lawful statistical program are protected from paralysis, from over-broad suspicion, and from a false accusation of capture or abuse.

This section does not re-model the governance kernel. ### Object model

- Public-authority act. An exercise of public authority is a K2 act with a resolved NATURAL-PERSON bearer, appended as an X1 event to the owning R1 register, booking the loud S4 audit. The officer acts AS the office (O2 position in an O1 organization holding an A-cluster mandate); the audit records both the person and the office by pure composition (OFFICE). An operation-of-law deemed outcome (a permit deemed granted or denied when a published deadline lapses, AUTH-I8) is itself a public-authority act with a resolved bearer, the defaulting officeholder, not a bearer-less act and not void.
- Capture signal. A decidable pattern that a state act may be serving a private, financial, or graph-aligned interest rather than the mandate's value axis: a beneficiary asymmetry, a bearer conflict, a missing evidentiary basis, a grain mismatch, or a purpose mismatch. A capture signal is an A6-ADJUDICATED signal that triggers routing, disclosure, and institutional remedy, NOT an append-time decidable gate and NOT, by itself, a person-level hard consequence; any person-level hard consequence is gated by AINT-I22 through AINT-I23. Each attack surface below defines its own signal.
- Aligned relation. The CLOSED, decidable set of relations that make a beneficiary "aligned" with a bearer for capture purposes: membership in the O1 acting-in-concert graph, or a recorded OFFICE conflict_declaration relationship, being kin, a financial interest, a prior or prospective employer, or a declared political affiliation on the record. This is a graph-membership test, not a political characterization. Where NO declared relation and NO graph edge exists, there is no "aligned" beneficiary and the only capture signal is the beneficiary or incumbent-entrant asymmetry standing alone.
- V4 capture-anti-value. The anti-value booked against the value axis a captured act harms. It is per-axis and is NEVER netted against the $ rail (a fiscal gain does not offset a democratic, dignity, institutional-integrity, or trust harm).
- Attack surface enumeration. Eight surfaces are in scope: (1) secret or retroactive law; (2) abuse of office and state capture; (3) regulatory and register capture; (4) manufactured emergency and the emergency ratchet; (5) surveillance-as-phenomenon and function creep; (6) the weaponized permit or license; (7) the unaccountable or automated state act; (8) the official-immunity abuse. The border exit/entry restriction deferred to A16 is noted as an interface only and is not modelled here.

### Invariants

Both flanks: the citizen faces no faceless power, and a properly borne, audited act is fully valid and is shielded from later retro-attack precisely because its provenance is on record.
- AINT-I2 (no automated bearer, human adoption required). An automated, algorithmic, or model-assisted decision system MAY prepare, score, or recommend a state act but SHALL NOT be its bearer. Both flanks: lawful automation that a named officer genuinely adopts and can override is protected and is not itself a capture signal.

#### Secret and retroactive law (surface 1, cite LAW)

- AINT-I3 (no coercion on an unpublished rule). A rule not published in the A5 law register and in force at the time of the conduct SHALL NOT ground any coercion, penalty, denial, or adverse state act against a person (this section applies A18-I7 to the apparatus, it does not re-model it). Detection signal: an adverse act citing a norm with no in-force A5 register event at the conduct date. Enforcement hook: the act is void; deliberate coercion on a rule kept unpublished is a concealment-class harm (A18) with the perpetual flip, booking V4 anti-value on the rule-of-law axis, never netted against any fiscal recovery. Both flanks: an honest officer applying the published law is fully protected, and a citizen is never bound by a rule they could not have known.
- AINT-I4 (backdated law is void, honest correction is not). A law whose stated effective date precedes its R1 register-append event, or whose text is altered after append other than by a NEW corrective event, is void ab initio to the extent of the backdating and SHALL NOT ground any coercion for conduct before the true append. The officer who booked the backdating bears a concealment-class harm with the perpetual flip. Both flanks are held by the append-only rule (R1): a genuine correction of an error, published as a new dated event that leaves the prior state legible, is lawful and is NOT backdating; only a silent rewrite or a pre-dated effect is caught.

- AINT-I7 (appointment packing and the level-capture guard). #### Manufactured emergency and the emergency ratchet (surface 4, cite INCIDENT and RES)

Enforcement hook: power beyond the un-renewed term is void; a knowing ratchet is the manufactured-emergency concealment class of INCIDENT-I13. Both flanks: a genuinely continuing emergency CAN be lawfully re-declared on fresh evidence and its powers are protected; only the evidence-free or pretextual re-declaration is caught.
- AINT-I13 (emergency never suspends accountability, and the two-tier floor). #### Surveillance-as-phenomenon and function creep (surface 5, cite XACT and INCIDENT)

- AINT-I14 (cohort-grain floor on state observation). Detection signal: a query, join, or output grain finer than the S5 floor, or a re-identification risk above the published S5 parameter from a nominally cohort-grain dataset. Enforcement hook: the access is unlawful; V4 anti-value on the privacy axis; the concealment-class flip where the per-person grain was disguised attaches on D2 evidence per AINT-I22. Both flanks: a bona fide cohort-grain statistical or public-health program is protected (see AINT-I16), and the honest analyst is not accused merely for observing aggregates. AINT-I14 and AINT-I16 are separated by the single calibrated S5 parameter: below or at it a program is protected, above it observation is surveillance.
- AINT-I15 (no function creep, purpose limitation on interaction records). An X5 encounter or X3 incident record collected for one purpose SHALL NOT be repurposed for control, profiling, or coercion of a person outside that purpose. A bilateral X5 encounter record is co-owned by its parties and neither the citizen's side nor the state's side is disclosed unilaterally, mirroring the B10-I5 shared-fact co-control (XACT). Detection signal: an interaction or incident record queried, joined, or actioned for a purpose other than the one under which it was collected, or a unilateral disclosure of a co-owned encounter. Enforcement hook: the repurposing act is void; V4 anti-value on the privacy and trust axes; the concealment flip where the repurposing was hidden attaches on D2 evidence per AINT-I22. - AINT-I16 (lawful statistical program protection). A genuine statistical, census, or public-health program is PROTECTED from a surveillance or capture accusation where it satisfies the S5-parameter predicate: grain at or above the S5 k-anonymity floor AND no per-person output AND re-identification risk at or below the published S5 parameter AND a published lawful purpose. This is the decidable predicate distinguishing the program from surveillance, stated against the calibrated S5 parameter rather than as an unsatisfiable "no re-identification path" absolute. Enforcement hook: a program meeting this predicate fails an accusation at screening; a surveillance signal against a program that does NOT meet it is A6-adjudicated per AINT-I23, grounding institutional remedy, with any person-level hard consequence gated by AINT-I22. Both flanks: this shields the honest statistical program from over-broad suspicion while AINT-I14 and AINT-I15 close the disguised-surveillance path; the three are separated purely by grain, purpose, and the one S5 re-identification-risk parameter.

#### Official-immunity abuse (surface 8, cite OFFICE)

#### General enforcement and both-flank guard

- AINT-I20 (V4 anti-value per axis, never netted against the $ rail). Every capture, abuse, or weaponization harm in this section books V4 anti-value on the SPECIFIC value axis it damages (democratic integrity, rule of law, privacy, trust, market fairness, institutional integrity, liberty, dignity) and this anti-value SHALL NOT be offset, netted, or discharged against any fiscal, security, or efficiency gain on the $ rail. - AINT-I21 (concealment perpetual flip as the general capture hook). Detection signal: a recorded ground for a state act that is contradicted by the pinned evidence of the act's true purpose. - AINT-I22 (both-flank false-accusation guard). Both flanks: the citizen keeps a real path to hold power to account on individualized evidence, and the honest official, honest regulator, and genuine emergency-declarer are shielded from conviction-by-pattern and from a manufactured accusation.
- AINT-I23 (institutional remedy on a signal, the person-consequence gate). A surface-specific capture signal (AINT-I5 through AINT-I8, AINT-I16, and OFFICE-I11) is an A6-ADJUDICATED signal that triggers routing and disclosure, NOT an append-time decidable gate. Detection signal: a capture signal disposed of as an append-time gate, or a person-level hard consequence resting on the signal alone without the AINT-I22 showing. ### Lifecycle and edge cases
