## Preamble

As intelligence automates labour, the industrial link *labour → value* breaks, and a state
built to tax labour and redistribute money loses its footing. This Charter constitutes a
state that governs not by taxation but by **coordinating multidimensional value flows** —
seeing society as a federation of **value transformers** that consume, transform, create
value, or generate anti-value. The state becomes an **orchestrator of civilizational value,
not its owner**. Money endures as one instrument among many; the steering variable is the
**balance of value**, measured well beyond GDP, and the ends are civilizational
sustainability and human meaning.

---

## Part I — Foundations

**Art. 1 — The Value-Balancing Mandate.** The state shall govern by **sensing and correcting
imbalance** in multidimensional value flows; it shall maximize no single axis (money
included) and shall not govern by taxation. *(A0 §1, §8)*

**Art. 2 — The Value Substrate.** Value is a **multidimensional vector (Æ)**, booked as
**flows** between transformers; **anti-value is an independent category**, not the absence of
value. *(V1–V4; `Value-Axes-Corridors-Voting.md`, `Anti-Value-Taxonomy.md`)*

**Art. 3 — Perspectival Value; Federation of Frames.** Value exists **only within a value
system**; the polity is a **federation of value systems**; the state is **one frame with a
bounded mandate — never the apex or owner of all value**. *(`Value-Frames-and-Perspectival-Value.md`; VMT A1/A2/T1/A10)*

---

## Part II — Inviolable Rights *(the floor removed from all governance)*

**Art. 4 — Inviolable Rights.** Life, bodily integrity, liberty, consent, and the right of
**exit** are inviolable: **not priceable, not votable, not tradeable.** Their breach is a
rights violation, not a value-transaction. *(`Charter-Norm-Hierarchy-and-Rights.md` §2)*

**Art. 5 — Protected Meaning.** The `meaning` axis carries a floor that may **not be traded
away** even when economically optimal. *(A0 §9)*

**Art. 6 — Per-Cohort Floors (Minority Protection).** Floors apply **per cohort**; the
majority may steer emphasis but **may not vote a cohort below floor**. The system **cannot
register "balanced" while any cohort is below floor.** *(A0 §10; `Value-Axes-Corridors-Voting.md`)*

---

## Part III — Governance Principles

**Art. 7 — Imbalance is the Only Signal.** The state acts only to correct **measured
imbalance**; no axis may be optimized below another's floor. *(A0 §1)*

**Art. 8 — Weights Democratic, Rules Not.** Citizens set the **weights** of value dimensions
— directly or via **revocable, topic-scoped delegation** to parties — but not the **rules of
measurement**. The state steers **within constitutional corridors**. *(A0 §4; `Governance-Mechanics.md` B0)*

**Art. 9 — Subsidiarity.** Value is balanced at the **lowest competent level**; only
cross-level imbalance escalates. *(A0 §6)*

**Art. 10 — Decay & Anti-Concentration.** Autonomy follows reputation and is revocable;
**concentration of value or power self-charges as anti-value — including for the state's own
organs.** *(A0 §7)*

**Art. 11 — Generosity over Accumulation.** Value produced and **donated** books additional
value; **recognition is peer-issued, not state-issued.** *(A0 §13)*

**Art. 12 — Participation-Condition Sustainability.** The state shall monitor the
**participation condition θ of every actor**; a governance that makes actors' θ fail is, by
that fact, **unsustainable and defective.** *(`Value-Frames-and-Perspectival-Value.md`; VMT A9/T7)*

## Part IV — Value, Money & Markets

**Art. 13 — Money is Instrument, not Sovereign.** **Æ is a non-transferable accounting
unit**; money is **one axis and a settlement rail**. Across frames and currencies the
invariant is **Realized Æ**, not price. *(`Consumption-and-Settlement.md`, `Relative-Value-and-Inter-Frame-Routing.md`)*

**Art. 14 — Markets Clear; the State Prices the Residual.** Markets clear allocation; the
state prices **only the externality residual** (multidimensional Pigou); **profit is
decoupled from civilizational value.** *(`Markets-and-Civilizational-Value.md`)*

**Art. 15 — Consequence Coupling.** Anti-value couples to consequence **instantly** (escrow,
continuous debit, concentration charge) and **retrospectively** (revaluation, clawback):
**finality for the honest, perpetual liability for the concealing.** *(`Value-Money-Coupling.md`)*

## Part V — Epistemic & Judicial Integrity

**Art. 16 — Transparency by Construction.** Every agent's logic, every vote, every
revaluation is **publicly reproducible**; a decision not reconstructable from public state is
**void**. *(A0 §5)*

**Art. 17 — Non-Purchasable Influence.** Reach is equalized; every source carries a
verifiable, falsifiable record; **the state annotates, it does not censor.** *(A0 §12)*

**Art. 18 — Rebuttable Interpretation.** Value and anti-value are interpretable; every
assessment is authoritative-by-default but **contestable in court**; there is **no final
unaccountable authority.** *(A0 §15; `Courts-and-Adjudication.md`)*

**Art. 19 — Non-Retroactivity.** Acts are judged by the **norms in force when committed**;
**evidence updates retroactively, norms do not.** *(A0 §14)*

---

## Part VI — Supremacy & Amendment

**Art. 20 — Supremacy (jus cogens).** This Charter is supreme. Any subordinate norm — a
polity's constitution, code, regulation, or ruling — that contradicts it is **void**.
**Constitutional review** against the Charter is a first-class function. *(`Charter-Norm-Hierarchy-and-Rights.md` §1)*

**Art. 21 — Eternity Clause.** The **unamendable core**: the Value-Balancing Mandate (Art. 1),
Inviolable Rights and Floors (Art. 4–6), Anti-Concentration (Art. 10), and Transparency &
Contestability (Art. 16, 18). **Axiacracy's own amendment process cannot abolish Axiacracy.**

**Art. 22 — Amendment.** Ordinary articles are amendable only by **deep supermajority and
slow (rate-limited) change**; the core (Art. 21) is unamendable; every amendment is itself
subject to Art. 20. *(hysteresis: `Value-Axes-Corridors-Voting.md` §5)*
