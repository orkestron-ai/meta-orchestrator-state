## 0. How the knowledge layer works

This is where the model governs the polity's evidence and its operational substrate at once. Documents, datasets and software are not files, they are governed first-class objects: a record persists while its carrier migrates, a dataset masters its bytes while its distributions are renditions, a software product masters its identity while its deployed systems are instances. The layer's first commitment is that **information is trustworthy by construction, not by assertion**: every governed object carries an immutable content digest under a live collision-resistant suite, every citation pins that digest, and integrity-class changes (withdrawal, supersession, tamper, resolution failure) propagate over the full transitive closure so silent evidence mutation is impossible. This is the concrete implementation of the pinning promise the science layer only stated.

**Documents (N1).** A record is distinct from the file that carries it and from its renditions, which are re-derivable projections carrying their own fidelity attestation; the version-versus-rendition line is mechanical (a lossless transform is a rendition, any semantic transform is a new authored version). Official records do not master the facts they record, the register does, under its declaratory, probative or constitutive effect class. Retention is genuinely bidirectional (a long minimum floor against shredding, a short maximum ceiling against the eternal dossier), and a lawful erasure destroys a per-subject key so microdata becomes unrecoverable while the integrity chain survives.

**Datasets (N3).** A dataset is the evidence substrate the whole model rests on, and it masters the content-pinned references the claim graph cites. Quality is graded against a named reference basis (no self-grading, accuracy caps at modeled absent a valid higher-tier reference). The privacy machinery composes disclosure loss across the linkage closure so two individually k-safe releases cannot difference to unmask, meters predicate answers against the same budget, and adds a governed public-interest-linkage enclave and an accredited-research tier so lawful statistics and research function between the extremes of per-subject consent and pre-shaped aggregates.

**Software (N4).** Software is a full-context product bound to AISMM by pinned reference (never re-modelled) and the operational substrate the polity runs on: the code running the registers, ledgers, venues and gates is itself governed software on the strictest change tier as a coverage rule over the transitive dependency closure, reproducibility independently verified by a rebuilder quorum, and the governing rule public while the source diff of a security fix waits for the S8 containment embargo. AI and agent software is a special class under A-layer authorization, with a consent-for-training basis, a measured memorization grade on synthetic outputs, and consent-withdrawal that cascades to the weights.

**Provenance, licensing, value (PROV, LIC, IVAL).** Content addressing, custody chains and instrument-bound measurement make the objects authentic; the actor in a provenance node is a data subject of it, not a permanent public activity ledger. Licenses never transfer a data subject's consent, holder-exhaustion and orphan routes never reach living subjects, and commons-funded knowledge lands open with a bounded enclosure-detection path. Information books value per axis with the two rails never netting; non-rival essential information meets the pricing-power rent and essential-facility discipline; portability reaches the trapped subject and contributor; and no download count, dataset size or line-of-code total is ever a funding or charging basis.

Three commitments recur, matching the rest of the model:

1. **One master, one digest, one propagation.** Every governed object has exactly one holder of record and one content digest; integrity events reach the full transitive closure with no hop bound; the code that computes and verifies those digests is itself strictest-tier.
2. **The subject is a party, not an audience.** Composed privacy budgets, per-subject-key erasure, consent-for-training and consent-on-transfer, actor-as-subject provenance, and a first-class rectification channel make the person the record is about a party with standing, while lawful research and statistics keep a governed path.
3. **Non-rival value, both flanks.** Essential-information rent and lock-in are charged, commons-funded knowledge lands open, portability and interoperability are floors; and the honest curator, the open-source contributor and the reference-data builder keep the free-competition band, suspensive contest, and value re-establishment above a minor input.

The sections follow: N1 documents and records; N3 datasets and data registers; N4 software products and systems; PROV provenance, evidence and authenticity; LIC licensing, rights and the knowledge commons; IVAL information in the value doctrine.

## Documents and records (N1 core)

A document is a governed first-class object, not the file that carries it. Its identity is stable and outlives any single rendition: the record persists while the format migrates, the carrier is re-encoded, or the paper decays, and exactly one S1 controller answers for it at every instant (S1-I1). The authoritative content of each version is referenced, never copied, and mastered in exactly one holder of record on the Meta-Universe ARCH-018 mastership pattern; every PDF, print, translation or accessibility transform is a declared mirror or an S3 projection of that master, carries its provenance and its own fidelity attestation, and never acquires authority of its own (R1-R1, R1-I33). This is the discipline that keeps a document honest as evidence. An official record (a birth certificate, a court judgment, a registered contract, a permit) does not master the fact it records: the register masters the authoritative record of the fact under its A12 effect class, which may be declaratory, probative or constitutive (R1-I25, R1-I26, R1-I27), and the document is the portable R1/R5 evidence of that entry, never a second source of truth competing with the register, which governs on conflict (R1-I26). The knowledge-layer mastering table, which fixes the one master for a document, a dataset, a software product and an official record alike, is stated once at N1-I22.

Issuance is the moment a draft becomes an instrument, and it is a governed act, not a save. A drafted document confers none of the effect of an issued record and attracts no reliance; effect attaches only when a party holding the A14 authority for the instrument class performs the issuance K2 act, recorded with a received_at that a witness independent of the holder timestamps before effect, plus any mandated pre-effect notice (R1-I8, R1-P1, R1-I14). Authorship (who composed the content) and issuance (who put it into effect under authority) are distinct attributions, both recorded in the provenance chain, neither editable. Signatures and seals are R5-class attestations that bind a specific content digest to a specific signer or organ at a specific append position: they attest the content as it stood then, verifiable against a valid, unrevoked, unexpired attestation whose issuer held the mandate at signing (S6-I4, R1-R3). Tamper voids the binding: any alteration after the attestation position makes the attested digest fail to resolve, voids the rendition as a signed instrument, and books against the altering party on the integrity-void pattern (F6-I4, R1-R5). The record lifecycle (drafted, issued, superseded, retracted, archived, resolution_unavailable) runs entirely on the append-only register pattern (R1-I2, D2-I2). No issued record is ever edited or deleted in place; a correction is a new version openly superseding the old, and a silent edit of an issued record is not an edit but concealment, routed to the A18 concealment class with its A18-I49 perpetual-limitation flip.

Reading a document is owner-gated like every other read in the Dimension. There is no research, official or emergency basis for raw access to a document store: every read passes an S2 access contract, leaves as an S3 projection, and appends an owner-enumerable access_event, while a read of a record whose subject is a natural person, including reads a state organ performs over holdings it itself controls, is additionally subject-enumerable (S2-I8, S3-I1, S4-I7, S4-I1, S2-I2). A non-disclosing operation over the store (a backup, a re-indexing, an integrity scrub, the re-anchoring pass), where no projection leaves the owner trust boundary, logs at segment or aggregate grain and is not a subject-enumerable read; only projection-producing reads are subject-enumerable. Classification and redaction live inside this machinery, not against it: a redacted release is a field_subset or summary projection of the intact master, produced by a registered projection function under a steward-mastered classification vocabulary and generated in the owner trust boundary, and it removes nothing from the record, it shapes what leaves (S3-I2).

Retention is symmetric protection against the two opposite failures, the shredded record and the eternal dossier, and it needs two directional defaults, not one: a minimum-retention floor conservatively LONG against premature destruction, and a maximum-retention ceiling conservatively SHORT for person-grain records against the eternal dossier, both named in the single D-layer defaults annex with their protective direction stated. Disposition is a governed, logged event barred while any legal hold, open A6 case, preservation_order or unexpired clawback window covers the record (S1-I7, S4-I12), and a hold is itself time-bounded and renews only on periodic A6 re-attestation, so it cannot become a permanent dossier. Records of enduring value sit under the HOR-I12 append-only preservation duty and their history is never physically deleted, while records past their lawful life have their public projection reduced to a tombstone rather than their history erased (R1-P2). A lawful subject erasure is different again: it destroys a per-subject key so the person-grain content becomes unrecoverable, while the content digest and provenance skeleton survive for integrity and clawback provability.

Finally, a document put to work as proof is pinned: a document a D2 claim cites or an A18 case admits is bound to an immutable version digest under a live, collision-resistant suite, resolves to that exact version, and drags its later supersession, retraction or resolution-unavailability across the full transitive closure of pins, with no hop bound, to every consumer (D2-I1, D2-I17), so the polity holds one claim-evidence graph rather than a thousand drifting reprints. Admission additionally resolves chain-of-custody continuity, and a master pinned only under a deprecated suite resolves to resolution_unavailable until it is re-anchored under a successor suite (PROV-I5, PROV-P5).

### Invariants

- **N1-I1 (identity outlives the carrier).** A document SHALL be a first-class N-layer object whose identity is distinct from any file, format, encoding or rendition that carries it, persists across format migration and re-encoding, and resolves to exactly one S1 controller at every instant (S1-I1). Loss, migration or corruption of a carrier SHALL NOT destroy the record; the record is recoverable by replica recovery of its sealed master, never reconstructed from a rendition (R1-I36).
- **N1-I2 (content referenced, one master, no competing truth).** The authoritative content of a document version SHALL be referenced, not copied, and mastered in exactly one holder of record per the ARCH-018 mastership rule (R1-R1). Every other rendition SHALL be a declared mirror or an S3 projection labelled with source, version, as-of AND the immutable master content digest; consumers SHALL pin that digest, never the mutable version name, and a mirror or projection labelled without a content digest confers no reliance and SHALL NOT acquire authority by any sequence of copying (R1-I33, D2-I1). An official record SHALL NOT master the fact it records: it is R1/R5 evidence of a register entry whose effect class the A12 mandate assigns as declaratory, probative or constitutive, never a source of truth competing with the register (R1-I25, R1-I26, R1-I27). The knowledge-layer mastering table is stated once at N1-I22.
- **N1-I3 (version versus rendition, mechanical, fidelity-attested).** A version SHALL be an authoritative content state of the document; a rendition (a named encoding, a format map, a lossless accessibility transform) SHALL be a re-derivable S3 projection of one named version, produced by a registered, versioned projection function and carrying its own R5 attestation binding {master version digest, projection-function id, rendition digest} plus the immutable master content digest, so a verifier can re-derive the rendition from its master and confirm fidelity or detect divergence as tamper (F6-I4, R1-R3). A rendition not re-derivable from its named master, or carrying no fidelity attestation, confers no reliance and SHALL NOT be a new version. The version-versus-rendition line is mechanical: a transform in the registered content-preserving transform class is a rendition; every semantic transform (a translation, an abridgement, a summarization) is a new authored version by construction, with its own provenance, removing any meaning-change judgment.
- **N1-I4 (append-only lifecycle).** A record's lifecycle state SHALL be drawn from the closed set {drafted, issued, superseded, retracted, archived, resolution_unavailable}, aligned with the N3 dataset_version set and D2-I2 so a pinned consumer demotes correctly when a master cannot be resolved; every transition SHALL be an append-only X1 event on the owning R3 register (R1-I2, D2-I2). No issued record SHALL be edited or deleted in place: a correction is a new version linked to the superseded one, and the superseded version is retained append-only and remains queryable, subject to the N1-I21 content-summarization discipline for non-enduring versions and to the N1-I19 integrity-preserving erasure path for person-grain fields (R1-P2, R1-I17).
- **N1-I5 (silent edit of an issued record is concealment).** After issuance, any modification of a record's content, issuance metadata or attestation links otherwise than by an appended superseding version SHALL be record falsification and route to the A18 concealment class, carrying the A18-I49 perpetual-limitation flip and the concealment clawback against the falsifier (D2-I14). An honest correction that openly supersedes the prior version SHALL NEVER be an offense and stays strictly prospective (D2-I15, D2-I20).
- **N1-I6 (draft versus issued instrument, independently witnessed received_at).** A drafted document SHALL confer none of the effect of an issued instrument and SHALL attract no reliance. Effect attaches only on issuance, a K2 act performed by a party holding the A14 authority for the instrument class, recorded with a received_at timestamped by a witness independent of the holder before the issuance carries effect, and any mandated pre-effect notice interval (R1-I8, R1-P1, R1-I14). The maximum unanchored window between received_at and its witnessed sealing is a named entry in the single D-layer defaults annex; an issuance whose received_at is not independently witnessed books contested, never authoritative, and append order SHALL equal attested received_at order (R1-I14, R1-I32). A purported instrument issued without the A14 authority is void ab initio and attracts no reliance protection at any tier (R1-I8, S1-I4).
- **N1-I7 (authorship and issuance both attributed, neither editable).** Every version SHALL carry a provenance chain naming its author(s), and every issued record SHALL additionally name its issuer and the A14 authority basis. Authorship and issuance are distinct attributions and are both recorded; a change to either is a new appended event, never an in-place edit (PROV bind).
- **N1-I8 (signatures and seals are R5-class attestations).** A signature or seal SHALL be an R5-class attestation binding a specific content digest to a specific signer or issuing organ at a specific append position, verifiable against a valid, unrevoked, unexpired R5 attestation whose issuer held the required mandate at signing time (S6-I4, R1-R3). It attests the content as it stood at that position, not the document's later state, and its binding to a filing is single-use so a once-valid signed artifact cannot be replayed onto a later version (R1-R4).
- **N1-I9 (tamper voids the binding, conservative compromise window).** Any alteration of signed or sealed content after the attestation append position SHALL void the binding: the attested digest no longer resolves, the rendition is void as a signed instrument, and the alteration books against the altering party on the integrity-void pattern (F6-I4, R1-R5 forged_evidence). Revocation of the underlying attestation follows the R5 lifecycle within the catalogued notification window (S6-I15); key compromise of a signing key follows the S8 role-typed cascade, suspending that signer's bindings pending re-attestation (S8-I4). The compromise window SHALL default conservatively to the last independently witnessed checkpoint at which the key's integrity was affirmatively verified, with the burden on the holder to prove a narrower window by external evidence; under-declaration of the window is concealment-class carrying the A18-I49 flip (R1-I31, R1-I32, S8-I4).
- **N1-I10 (official records draw effect from their register).** An official record (civil-status certificate, court judgment, registered contract, permit) SHALL draw its effect from its register, not from its carrier: the register entry is declaratory, probative or constitutive per its A12 mandate (R1-I25, R1-I26, R1-I27) and the document is the portable R1/R5 evidence of that entry. A declaratory entry (a birth, a death) records a fact existing independently of registration and creates a rebuttable presumption, so the register masters the authoritative record of the fact, not the fact itself (R1-I26). A document asserting an official fact the register does not hold warrants nothing, and the register governs on conflict (R1-I26).
- **N1-I11 (private records).** A privately held document (a personal letter, an internal memo, an unregistered agreement) SHALL be a first-class record under the same identity, versioning and append-only discipline, controlled by its author or holding organization under S1, and SHALL carry only the effect its content and any attestations independently support. It SHALL NOT acquire official effect without registration.
- **N1-I12 (every read owner-gated, owner- versus subject-enumerable).** Every read of a document SHALL be default-deny and occur only through an S2 access contract producing an S3 projection, and SHALL append an owner-enumerable access_event (S2-I8, S3-I1, S4-I7). A read of a record whose subject is a natural person, including a read by the S1 controller of its own holdings, SHALL additionally be subject-enumerable (S4-I1). A non-disclosing operation over the store (a backup, a re-indexing, an integrity scrub, the PROV-P5 re-anchoring pass), where no S3 projection leaves the owner trust boundary, logs at segment or aggregate grain under S4 and is not a subject-enumerable read; only projection-producing reads are subject-enumerable. No instrument in the Dimension confers raw access to a document store (S2-I2).
- **N1-I13 (classification and redaction as projection, not edit).** A document SHALL carry a classification level drawn from a classification vocabulary that, with its per-level projection and redaction policy, is a steward-mastered R1 parameter object in the single D-layer defaults annex (DVAL-I15), with a classifying authority per record class under an A12 mandate; an unclassified record defaults to the most restrictive projection until classified. A redacted release SHALL be an S3 field_subset or summary projection of the authoritative version, produced by a registered projection function carrying its own fidelity attestation (N1-I3), generated inside the owner trust boundary, never a content edit of the master (S3-I1, S3-I2). The unredacted version is retained intact and append-only; redaction removes nothing from the record, it shapes what leaves it.
- **N1-I14 (retention, bidirectional, holds and disposition).** Every record class SHALL carry, in the single D-layer defaults annex mastered by the civilization parameter steward (DVAL-I15), two directional retention windows with their protective direction stated: a minimum-retention floor whose conservative activation default is LONG (against premature destruction) and a maximum-retention / disposition-due ceiling whose conservative activation default is SHORT for person-grain records absent an enduring-value or legal-hold basis (against the eternal dossier). For every privacy-protective parameter the conservative activation default is the maximally protective value, lowered only by the steward re-establishing a calibrated value and only prospectively (the F6-I20 ratchet), so no privacy guarantee is suspended pending calibration. Disposition at end of schedule SHALL be a governed, logged event, never a silent deletion, and SHALL be barred while any legal hold, open A6 case, preservation_order or unexpired clawback window covers the record (S1-I7, S1-I12, S4-I12); a legal hold is time-bounded and requires periodic A6 re-attestation to renew, so a hold cannot become a permanent dossier. The archive of record for enduring-value documents is a delivered floor, standing-funded first-charge on the COMP-I17 pattern under the single published HOR-I20 inter-floor priority rule (D2-I8).
- **N1-I15 (disposition is projection reduction, not history erasure).** Where a retention schedule or a privacy right requires a record to stop being served, disposition SHALL reduce its public projection to a tombstone (existence, class, supersession or disposition reference) with the full content retained append-only and readable only under S2 contract, case standing or court order (R1-P2, R1-I17). Integrity commitments and clawback provability SHALL NOT be impaired, and physical destruction of enduring-value history is prohibited (HOR-I12). Tombstone reduction of a projection is distinct from the N1-I19 integrity-preserving erasure of a subject's person-grain fields, which destroys the content while preserving the digest and provenance skeleton.
- **N1-I16 (a document as pinned evidence).** A document referenced by a D2 research claim or entered into an A18 case SHALL be pinned to an immutable version identifier or content digest under a live, collision-resistant digest suite; the reference resolves to that exact version, and any later supersession, retraction or resolution-unavailability propagates over the FULL transitive closure of pins, with no hop bound, to every registered consumer, steward materiality deferring recompute urgency but never suppressing an integrity notification (D2-I1, D2-I17). A document offered as evidence whose master cannot be resolved by pinned reference, or is pinned only under a deprecated suite, SHALL resolve to resolution_unavailable and be inadmissible for any validated or adjudicative use (D2-I1, D2-I2, R1-R3); a re-anchoring event under a successor suite preserves and links the prior digest chain before the incumbent suite reaches its published break horizon (PROV-P5).
- **N1-I17 (integrity by witnessed anchoring).** A record's existence and content digest at each append position SHALL be provable without trusting its holder: version and attestation events seal into k-of-n replicated segments with witnessed segment roots mutually anchored into the registrar root-of-roots on a bounded cadence (R1-I31, R1-I32, S4-I4), so a holder can neither backdate an issuance nor equivocate on what a record said at a past position; the R2-I13 ledger checkpoint is the analogous pattern, not the governing authority for content objects. The record state at any past position is derivable from the latest witnessed checkpoint plus subsequent appended events (R1-I31).
- **N1-I18 (rights in a document never reach the record graph).** Copyright, licence terms and other N12 rights over a document's expression SHALL be time-bounded carve-outs on the C1-I7 innovation-protection pattern: they MAY govern reproduction and distribution of the expression but SHALL NEVER cover the identity, version graph, provenance chain, attestation links or lifecycle state of the record, and no licence term SHALL forbid the citation, contest or evidentiary use of an issued record (N12, D2-I9). Licence terms bind through the LIC sibling section.
- **N1-I19 (integrity-preserving erasure).** Person-grain fields of a document SHALL be stored under per-subject keys so that on lawful erasure (an S2 withdrawal absent an overriding, documented, time-bounded legal hold) the key is destroyed, rendering the content unrecoverable while the content digest and provenance skeleton survive for integrity and clawback provability. Where a legal hold or enduring-value duty genuinely overrides erasure, the override SHALL be explicit, logged, time-bounded, subject-notified and under A6 or court standing, never a blanket append-only default (S4-I1, S1-I7). Erasing a subject's microdata, which SHALL remain reachable for erasure, is separated from destroying the integrity chain, which is preserved by digest.
- **N1-I20 (data subject as a first-class party).** The data subject SHALL be a first-class party distinct from the S1 controller. The subject SHALL hold a logged rectification-and-objection channel, a subject-asserted correction or dispute annotation that travels with the record and its S3 projections without editing the master; a restriction flag narrowing projection policy pending resolution; and A6 escalation that does not make litigation the only path (S2-I8, S3-I2). The controller keeps its append-only integrity duty; the subject is a named party with standing over their own microdata, not merely an audience of the access log.
- **N1-I21 (content summarization of superseded non-enduring versions).** A superseded, non-enduring version whose externality, hold, clawback and citation windows have all closed clean MAY reduce its content bytes to a commitment plus witnessed checkpoint, preserving the content digest and clawback provability while dropping the payload, on the R2-I20 pattern; enduring-value records under HOR-I12 are exempt. The owner is the holder of record, under a steward-mastered summarization window in the single D-layer defaults annex (DVAL-I15, R1-I31).
- **N1-I22 (the knowledge-layer mastering table, stated once).** The document record masters the fact-record and its renditions are projections; the N3 dataset version masters its bytes and its distributions are renditions; the N4 software product masters identity and releases and a deployed system is an instance; an N3 dataset masters the evidence bytes a D2 claim graph cites by pinned digest, and the claim graph never masters the dataset; an N1 official record is R1/R5 evidence of a register entry and the register masters the authoritative entry. Every governed object names exactly one holder of record per ARCH-018, the accountable S1 controller of that entry rather than a second source of truth (R1-R1, R1-I33, R1-I36). Assignment is mechanical: a schema-bearing structured set is an N3 object, an executable or configuration artifact is an N4 object, otherwise the object is an N1 document, so no object is claimed by two sections.

### Protocols

- **N1-P1 (author, issue, attest).** 1. The author composes a version inside the owner trust boundary and registers it with its provenance chain and content digest (N1-I7, R1-R1). 2. To issue, a party holding the A14 authority for the instrument class performs the issuance K2 act; the event receives a received_at timestamped by a witness independent of the holder, and any mandated pre-effect notice interval runs (N1-I6, R1-P1, R1-I14). 3. Signers and the issuing organ append R5-class signature or seal attestations binding the content digest at that position (N1-I8). 4. For an official record the corresponding register entry is filed per R1; the document is emitted as R1/R5 evidence of the entry, never as its master (N1-I10, N1-I22).
- **N1-P2 (render and release).** 1. A rendition request presents a capability_token; the owner gate generates the rendition as a re-derivable S3 projection of the named version under a registered projection function, carrying its version identifier, content digest and fidelity attestation (N1-I3, S3-P2). 2. For a classified or redacted release the gate applies the field_subset or summary projection policy and logs exactly one access_event (N1-I13, S4-I1). 3. Any person-grain or aggregate-shaped release registers in the S5 release register and is tested against the composed privacy-loss budget over the linkage closure before delivery, failing closed where composition drops effective-k below floor (S3-I14, S5-I6); a release of person-grain content into a training corpus additionally requires an S2 consent scoped to the training purpose, an S5-registered aggregate, or an S6-attested projection, absent which the input is over_collection (S2-I8, S5-I6, S6-I1). 4. No release SHALL serve raw store access or an unshaped dump (S2-I2).
- **N1-P3 (correct, supersede, retract).** 1. A correction registers a new version linked to the superseded one, retained append-only (N1-I4, R1-P2). 2. A court-ordered or falsity-found correction reduces the superseded version's public projection to a tombstone, full content retained (N1-I15, R1-I17). 3. A retraction annotates the record and propagates over the full transitive closure to every registered consumer (D2-I17); an honest self-retraction is prospective and never an offense (D2-I15, D2-I20). 4. A silent edit of an issued record is booked to the A18 concealment class (N1-I5). 5. A subject's rectification-and-objection annotation or restriction flag is appended and travels with the record's projections without editing the master (N1-I20).
- **N1-P4 (retain, hold, dispose).** 1. Each record binds its two directional retention windows and holder of record at issuance (N1-I14). 2. A legal hold, open case, preservation_order or unexpired clawback window suspends all disposition, and a hold is time-bounded and renews only on periodic A6 re-attestation (S1-I7, S4-I12). 3. At the disposition ceiling, disposition executes as a logged projection-reduction event, never a deletion of enduring-value history (N1-I15, HOR-I12); a lawful subject erasure destroys the per-subject key while preserving the digest and provenance skeleton (N1-I19). 4. A superseded, non-enduring version whose windows have all closed clean MAY be summarized to a commitment plus witnessed checkpoint (N1-I21). 5. Enduring-value records deposit into the HOR-I12 preservation class with HOR-I13 dispersion, the holder of record mastering and any other party a verifier (D2-I26).
- **N1-P5 (produce a record as evidence).** 1. The offering party pins the document to its exact version identifier or content digest under a live suite (N1-I16, D2-I1). 2. The verifier resolves the pinned master and checks its R5 attestation chain: valid, unrevoked, unexpired, issuer-mandated at signing (N1-I8, S6-I4, R1-R3). 3. Chain-of-custody continuity SHALL resolve before any validated or adjudicative use; a tamper-voided, custody-broken or unresolvable master is rejected as inadmissible (N1-I9, N1-I16, PROV-I5). 4. ## Datasets and data registers (N3 core)

A dataset is the polity's unit of recorded observation held as an owned, catalogued, quality-graded asset, and it is the evidence substrate the whole model rests on. N3 gives it first-class machinery: a dataset is an N-layer object with exactly one S1 controller, carrying identity, a versioned schema reference, declared coverage (spatial, temporal, and population frame), subject grain, sensitivity class, and a provenance head. A dataset is versioned, and every version carries an immutable content digest that is the identity of its bytes. Distributions are re-derivable projections of one mastered version (the same version served as a CSV export, an API surface, a bulk dump, a mirror), never independent masters. This is the layer on which truth is pinned: D2 research claims, F6 measurements, C7 appraisals, and every operating parameter of the polity cite evidence, and every such citation binds an N3 dataset version by its content digest. N3 is where the D2 pinning promise (D2-I1) is actually implemented. The dataset masters the evidence bytes; the D2 claim graph only cites them by pinned digest and never masters the dataset, and every governed object names exactly one holder of record per ARCH-018 (C36).

The critical binding is that silent evidence mutation is impossible. Because a citation pins a content digest computed under a live collision-resistant suite over a registered canonicalization spec, re-cleaning, re-windowing, re-deriving, migrating a schema, correcting, or withdrawing data cannot move the cited bytes: any such change is a new dataset version and an X1 evidence-basis-changed event on the dataset object, propagated over the FULL transitive closure of pins with no hop bound for integrity-class events (C5) to every citing claim, measurement, appraisal, and parameter, with mandatory recompute and republication of affected grades (D2-I17). Withdrawal or loss of a pinned version drives its dependents to resolution_unavailable and demotes downstream parameters prospectively to their conservative default (D2-I2). Quality and fitness (completeness, accuracy, timeliness, provenance integrity) are graded metadata under the F6-style tier discipline and the DVAL parameter-steward separation, never self-declared where they price anything, because a grade governs funding, reliance, and admissibility. Honest re-cleaning and good-faith withdrawal are the system working and are never offenses; procured or foreseeable destruction of load-bearing data by an interested party is concealment-class.

Ownership, commons, and markets follow the MOS doctrine unchanged. The data holder owns the dataset per S1; reads are default-deny and served only as S2-contracted S3 projections, every read S4-logged; sensitive microdata escapes only through S5 cohort releases and S6 predicates, never as raw person-grain rows without the subject's consent or a judicial contract. Every aggregate and every predicate answer meters against a composed disclosure budget over the linkage closure (N3-I18), and a subject's consent-withdrawal propagates over the lineage graph like an evidence-basis change (N3-I19). Public-interest reference datasets land in the data commons under the property commons doctrine and, where they are floor infrastructure, are standing-funded on the COMP-I17 pattern as part of the D2-I8 knowledge-commons floor, under the single HOR-I20 / RES-I23 inter-floor priority rule (C23). Tradable datasets clear on the $ rail through C2 venues: the market sets the price and no organ sets or caps it, while the state prices the externality residual (privacy harm) and, on an essential non-substitutable asset held above the COMP threshold, the pricing-power rent margin above the attested benchmark (N3-I21). Any created value books on the knowledge axis in Ae (DVAL-I1; D2-I4 calls this the epistemic axis), never netted against the $ price. Synthetic and derived data are clearly typed and are never a laundering path around S5. The register of datasets is an R1 instance, append-only, with corrections as new entries.

## Software products and systems (N4 core)

A software product is an N-layer object that binds, by pinned reference, to the full-context AISMM whole-model that already describes it. Its requirements, components, APIs, data entities, decisions, risks and tests live in that model; N4 holds the world-model's handle on the product, its identity, its releases and its running instances, and never a second copy of the model. On the knowledge-layer mastering table the software product masters identity and releases and a deployed system is an instance of it, and every governed object names exactly one holder of record per ARCH-018; an executable or configuration object is an N4 object. Evidence is referenced, never copied (composition Rule 2, on the D2-I1 pattern), so the polity holds one product-model graph rather than a thousand drifting exports, and a reference that is not pinned to a content digest is unresolvable by construction. A release is an issued artifact: an append-only event carrying a content digest over the built binary, a version, its build provenance and a Software Bill of Materials whose dependency graph is first-class, where every dependency is itself a pinned reference to another N4 product release, not an inlined copy. Like every record in the Dimension the product is owner-gated (S1, S2): its source and internals are readable only with the controller's permission, and corrections are new releases, never edits (R1-I2, R2-I1).

The software that runs the polity's registers, ledgers and venues is itself governed software, and this is the load-bearing doctrine of the section. Strictest tier is a coverage rule, not a closed list: any N4 system whose output is relied on to digest, attest, anchor, verify, grade or gate a strictest-tier system is itself a strictest-tier, reproducibly built system, and the tier together with its reproducibility, dual-review and attestation duties propagates over the transitive SBOM closure, the composed grade being the minimum over the closure on the PROV-I8 weakest-link rule. The rule enrols the software running the R1 registers, the R2 ledgers, the C2 venues and the S-cluster gates (the owner-side gate, the S4 Audit Registrar, the S5 evaluator, the S6 verifier), and explicitly the digest and crypto-suite implementation, the build-and-attestation toolchain, the reproducibility verifier, the parameter-steward scoring evaluator and the grade-combination evaluator; a named trusted-base register closes the bootstrap so the toolchain that attests every change is itself attested. The integrity of each such system is the integrity of the record it keeps: a corrupted register binary corrupts the ledger as surely as a forged entry. Strictest-tier change control attaches to the product and reference implementation, not to every deployed instance: a change is attested, dual-reviewed by structurally independent reviewers and reproducible-build verified, and it is not self-attested by the deployer but independently rebuilt to the exact running digest by a quorum of structurally independent rebuilders that counter-sign before deploy, anchored with the release event (R1-I31, R1-I32, S4-I4); absent the quorum the change fails closed. A silent compromise of such a system is A18 concealment-or-worse against the ledger itself, carrying the A18-I49 perpetual flip. Because there is no secret law, there is no secret law in executable form: the polity's own governing code lands in an auditable, reproducible, forkable commons (S1-I13 public_record, D2-I7 commons landing), the one class of software where owner-gating yields to the transparency doctrine, so that anyone can rebuild the running digest from published source and confirm the state runs the code it says it runs. Transparency (the rule is public) is separated from synchronous source publication (the diff is published now): for a security-fix governing-code change the running digest and a commitment to the source anchor at deploy and the human-auditable source publishes once containment across all roots is attested, under the S8-I9 patch window and never before, so the exploit window is closed while auditability is preserved; and the governing behavior and its conformance predicates publish in full, while a narrow, enumerated, steward-attested class of security-load-bearing detection heuristics publishes on delay or as an S6-verifiable commitment auditable to a bonded reviewer, never covering the substantive access, pricing or eligibility rule.

A deployed running system is an instance distinct from the product it runs: it carries configuration, state and operators, its operation is K2 acts by those operators, its incidents are X1 occurrences and X3 emergencies, and it attests only that it runs a published release digest rather than carrying the product's per-change control. Proprietary software trades as an ordinary good on the $ rail through C2 venues under a bounded, time-limited N12 licence, and the state never sets or caps the market price: it prices only the residual, stated as two components, the externality residual (chiefly privacy harm) and, where the holder is above the COMP market-power threshold on an essential non-substitutable asset, the C1-I7 pricing-power rent margin above the attested benchmark; it rewards software it bought by the purchase price and attaches a Pigovian subsidy from the commons_rent_fund only to the demonstrably additional increment (FVAL-I8, D2-I6), never paying twice for one result. AI and agent software that acts in the polity is a special class of N4 system under A-layer authorization and S-cluster gating: an agent's model and training data are N3/N4 objects, person-grain data enters its training corpus only on a training-scoped consent, aggregate or predicate basis with the training read logged and withdrawal reaching the weights, and its decisions are K2 acts booking to the acting agent and piercing through the operator chain to the deploying natural person (K2-I23, K2-I24, A18-I45). Vulnerabilities are X1 events under the S8 coordinated-disclosure discipline; defect liability splits by cause on the layer-wide suspensive-contest pattern, the honest disclosed defect prospective and restitutionary, the concealed defect perpetual and clawed back; and security-critical software carries the externality-escrow discipline that tolls while any vulnerability indicator is open.

## Provenance, evidence and authenticity (PROV)

PROV is the connective tissue that makes N1, N3 and N4 trustworthy. Every governed information object, a document rendition per N1, a dataset version per N3, a software release per N4, and every evidence item any of them cites, carries an immutable content digest computed under the registered collision-resistant suite over a registered, versioned canonicalization spec, and is addressable by that digest. Identity is a stable name that resolves to digest-pinned renditions; content is pinned even as format migrates, so migration is a new rendition, never an edit, and each rendition is a re-derivable projection of its master under the N1-I3 rendition rule. Content addressing scales on the R1-I31 inclusion-promise and R1-I32 mutual-anchoring discipline at segment grain (S4-I4): governed objects seal into k-of-n witnessed segment roots on a bounded cadence, and verification checks the segment proof, not a full re-hash of the corpus. This one mechanism stands behind the D2-I1 pinned-reference promise (evidence referenced by content digest, never copied); the R2-I13 witnessed-checkpoint discipline is the analogous ledger pattern, not the governing authority for content objects. PROV governs whether an object is what it claims to be and where it came from; it never governs whether the object may be read. That question belongs to the S cluster: everything here is an owner-gated record, readable only with the owner's permission, and append-only throughout, with corrections and re-anchorings entered as new events rather than overwrites.

The provenance graph records who created an object, from what pinned inputs, by what K2 act, under what A-cluster authority, and at what time. It is a directed acyclic graph over information objects, shaped on the W3C PROV model of entity, activity and agent, and it is itself append-only on the R3 Semantic Timeline. Each natural-person actor named as author, signer or custodian is a data subject of the node that names them (PROV-I17), and the immutable digest-binding, which may be pseudonymous or keyed, is separated from human-identity resolution so integrity never requires a permanent person-keyed activity ledger. Authenticity binds content to signer through an R5 attestation, on the R1-R3 authenticity chain; a chain of custody carries measured and observed evidence from instrument to claim on the F6-I4 discipline, with each measured reading signed at capture to the instrument's own key so no fabricated chain can mint measured status; tamper detection is digest mismatch under a live suite; and a broken chain voids evidentiary weight, never the object itself, which survives as an honest record of unknown provenance. PROV adjudicates nothing. It computes verifiable facts, digest match, signature validity, graph reachability, custody continuity, and routes every merits and mens-rea question to A6 under suspensive contest, so an authenticity accusation is never an unadjudicated career or asset execution.

Two disciplines close the layer. First, transitive integrity: a derived object is only as trustworthy as its least-trustworthy resolved input, so provenance carries the weakest-link grade forward on the DVAL conservative-side and dominance rule, and no re-encoding or relabeling launders a low-tier input into a high-tier output. The cap governs integrity, admissibility and access restriction; a derivative's own value and funding grade is not permanently capped by a minor input but may be independently re-established by evidence on the N3-I8 prospective path (PROV-I8, reconciling PROV-I8 and N3-I8). Second, longevity: because cryptography ages, digest equality proves non-mutation only under a live collision-resistant suite, so a standing-funded re-anchoring sweep re-digests and re-signs every live load-bearing object under a successor suite before the incumbent reaches its published break horizon, keeping century-scale objects verifiable, bound to the HOR-I12 preservation duty and to the R1 succession rule that keys never transfer; an object still pinned under a deprecated suite resolves to resolution_unavailable, never verified. Synthetic content is a first-class provenance category inside both disciplines: training-data provenance, model provenance and generated-content provenance are declared and typed, and undeclared synthetic passed as authored or as measured evidence is concealment, not a labeling lapse.

### Invariants

- **PROV-I1 (content digest on every governed object).** Every governed information object (an N1 document rendition, an N3 dataset version, an N4 software release, and every evidence item they cite) SHALL carry an immutable content digest computed under the registered digest suite over the registered, versioned canonicalization spec, and SHALL be addressable by that digest. The registered digest suite SHALL be collision-resistant: two artifacts sharing a digest are the same content, and any byte difference is a different object with a different digest, ONLY while that suite is live. An object pinned or offered under a deprecated suite resolves to resolution_unavailable and is never verified on the stale match (PROV-I13). The canonicalization spec is a registered, versioned parameter object mastered by the civilization parameter steward in the single D-layer defaults annex (DVAL-I15) alongside the digest suite, and ignore-and-drop canonicalization is barred for accountability-critical registers. Content addressing scales on the R1-I31 inclusion-promise and R1-I32 mutual-anchoring discipline at segment grain (S4-I4): objects seal into k-of-n witnessed segment roots on a bounded cadence and verification checks the segment proof, not a full re-hash of the corpus. This is the single mechanism behind the D2-I1 pinned-reference promise; the R2-I13 witnessed-checkpoint discipline is the analogous ledger pattern, not the governing authority for content objects. N1, N3 and N4 renditions inherit this without restating it.

- **PROV-I2 (identity persists, content is pinned, migration mints a rendition).** A governed object SHALL carry a stable object identity distinct from the content digest of any single rendition. Identity persists across format migration, re-encoding and re-issue; content does not. Each rendition is separately digest-pinned, and a migration SHALL append a new rendition carrying its own digest and a provenance edge to the prior rendition, never mutate an existing one. Each rendition SHALL be a deterministic, re-derivable projection of its master under a registered versioned projection function carrying its own R5 attestation that binds {master version digest, projection-function id, rendition digest}, so a rendition not re-derivable from its master confers no reliance; this rendition-projection machinery is owned by N1-I3, where a content-preserving transform is a rendition and every semantic transform is a new authored version, and is cited, not restated, here. This is the append-only rule of R1-I2 and R2-I1 applied to information objects: corrections, redactions and format changes are new entries linking what they supersede, and the superseded rendition is retained and stays resolvable subject to the N1 and S-cluster projection rules.

- **PROV-I3 (the provenance graph is an append-only DAG).** Every governed object SHALL carry a provenance graph recording who created it, from what pinned inputs, by what K2 act, under what A-cluster authority, and at what time, shaped on the W3C PROV model and acyclic by construction. Every node and edge SHALL be an X1 event appended to the owning R3 register, carrying its actor and evidence references, and SHALL resolve to exactly one S1 controller. Each natural-person actor referenced in a node is a data subject of that node under PROV-I17, and the node's immutable digest-binding MAY be pseudonymous or keyed, separated from human-identity resolution so the graph survives forever without a permanent person-keyed activity ledger. No provenance assertion is edited in place (D2-I2): a correction is a new event, a retraction annotates the record, and the graph survives forever as its full history.

- **PROV-I4 (signatures bind content to signer, keys are role-typed).** Authenticity SHALL be a signature binding a content digest to a signer through an R5 attestation whose issuer mandate was Active at the signed as-of time and resolvable at verification (the R1-R3 authenticity chain). A signature SHALL verify only against a registered key whose role matches the asserted act; a signature from an unregistered or role-mismatched key authorizes nothing and attests nothing (S8-I2). Binding is to the digest, so a signature travels with the exact content it signed and covers no other content.

- **PROV-I5 (chain of custody and instrument-side capture for evidence).** An object offered as measured or observed evidence SHALL carry a chain of custody from instrument to claim: the measuring instrument identity (M8 reference), a current calibration attestation (R5), the operating party, and every custody transfer, each an appended event on the F6-I4 measured-fact discipline and the A18 custody discipline. Measured-tier evidence SHALL additionally carry an instrument-side capture attestation: the instrument is a registered N4 object on a tamper-evident strictest tier that signs each reading to its own key at capture, so a fabricated custody chain cannot mint measured status. A tier claim SHALL carry its PROV-I5 basis as an ingestion gate, instrument and capture attestation and custody for measured, model provenance for modeled, failing to the conservative substitute when unsupported (F6-I4, F6-I20). Custody continuity SHALL resolve before any validated or adjudicative use: the N1-P5 evidence-production path and the D2 admission path bind this gate. A gap or unresolved link voids the object's evidentiary weight, not the object: the artifact survives as a record of unknown or degraded provenance and MAY still carry non-evidentiary value, but SHALL NOT enter any validated status or parameter pipeline until its chain resolves.

- **PROV-I6 (tamper is digest mismatch, a broken chain is not itself an offense).** Tamper detection SHALL be digest comparison: a re-resolved rendition whose recomputed digest differs from its pinned digest is tampered or corrupt, and the mismatch SHALL raise a tamper event and freeze the object's evidentiary and parameter use pending adjudication. A digest match under a deprecated or collision-vulnerable suite is not proof of non-mutation: such an object resolves to resolution_unavailable and re-anchors before reuse (PROV-I13), never verified on the stale match. Where a pinned input can no longer be resolved to its digest, the citing object transitions to resolution_unavailable (D2-I2) and triggers PROV-I9 propagation. ## Licensing, rights and the knowledge commons (LIC)

LIC is the rights-and-use layer over the knowledge cluster: it governs who may use a governed information object (an N1 document or record, an N3 dataset, an N4 software product or system) and on what terms, and it is the interface between those objects and the N12 intellectual-property model. In the MOS frame every information object is an owner-gated record whose read is already governed by the S cluster (S1 control, S2 access contract, S3 projection, S4 audit); LIC governs the use that sits above the read. A license here is not a document of prose but an access-and-use contract: an S2 access_contract specialized for information objects, carrying scope, permitted uses, a redistribution rule, a derivation rule, a term and a price. It binds a declared S3 shape like any S2 contract, it is machine-checkable against a registered template vocabulary, and it travels with the object by reference (composition Rule 2, never copied), so authorization is resolvable wherever the object goes and default deny holds wherever it is not.

Rights over an information object compose in a fixed precedence, and the license sits under all of them. The order is: the data subject's privacy and consent (the S cluster, Charter Art. 4) first; then moral and attribution rights (bound to PROV); then the copyright and other N12 IP of the author or producer; then the compilation or database right of the N3 data holder over the assembled set, which is thin and never reaches the underlying facts; and only then the contractual terms of the license itself. Two consequences are load-bearing. A holder cannot license away what it does not own: a data subject's consent is never an asset of the holder and is never transferred by a license between holders, so every downstream use of person-subject content re-derives its authorization from the subject's live consent (S2-I4), not from the licensee's paper. And there is no property in a fact: copyright and database rights attach to a specific expression, compilation or embodiment, never to the fact, claim, measured quantity or idea it conveys (D2-I9), and no license term may forbid the citation, replication, contest or independent re-derivation of a published fact.

Over this rights stack sits the commons doctrine, imported whole from the property spec: ownership extends as far as creation and no further, and knowledge is built largely on the commons (public science, humanity's aggregated data, network effects), so its unearned portion is commons-owed. Publicly funded information therefore lands in the knowledge commons under an open license: research by majority commons funding (D2-I7), the state's own operational records (the N1 public-record classification, S1-I13), and the polity's own governing software (the N4 systems the state runs to govern, released source-open so the governed can read the code that governs them). Routing commons-owed knowledge into private, closed or exclusively licensed control to defeat that landing is enclosure, the offense the property and D2 specs name, prosecuted as concealment-shaped evasion. Where an information object is lawfully private and tradable its license clears as a price on the $ rail through C2 like any order; the state never sets the price of knowledge, pricing only the externality or rent residual through the uniform MVAL overlay, and an essential-information monopoly (a standard every implementer must adopt, a reference dataset that is a natural monopoly), once it carries a registered essential-information classification (LIC-I18), meets the essential-facility duty and the C1-I7 rent discipline with its access delivered as a floor ordered under the single published inter-floor priority rule (LIC-I12).

### Invariants

- **LIC-I1 (a license is a specialized S2 access contract).** Every authorization to use a governed information object beyond a read SHALL be an information_license, an S2 access_contract (S2-I1) specialized to carry scope, permitted_uses, redistribution_rule, derivation_rule, term, price and attribution_obligation. It SHALL bind a declared S3 shape, be machine-checkable against the registered license-template vocabulary (LIC-I16), and travel with the object by reference (composition Rule 2), never copied. Default deny holds: absent a resolvable license or an open license or statute that already grants the use, no use right exists. The read layer stays governed by S2 unchanged; LIC governs the use layer above the read.
- **LIC-I2 (the rights stack composes in fixed precedence).** Rights over an information object SHALL compose in this order, with the license under all of them: (a) data-subject privacy and consent per the S cluster and Charter Art. 4; (b) moral and attribution rights per PROV; (c) the author's or producer's copyright and other N12 IP; (d) the N3 data holder's compilation or database right over the assembled set, distinct from any right in the underlying facts; (e) the contractual license terms. A license term contradicting a higher tier is void to the extent of the conflict and books nothing against the licensee, and the remainder of the license survives (severability).
- **LIC-I3 (no property in a fact, binding D2-I9).** There SHALL be no ownable right in a fact, a measured quantity, an idea, a claim statement, a lifecycle state or a citation (D2-I9). Copyright and database rights attach only to a specific expression, compilation or embodiment; no license term SHALL forbid the citation, replication, contest, measurement or independent re-derivation of a published fact or claim. Rights over N4 software attach to the specific code and its expression, never to the algorithm-as-idea or to an interface necessary for interoperation; a term barring interoperation is void.
- **LIC-I4 (consent is a non-licensable floor, binding S).** A data subject's consent (S2-I4, Charter Art. 4) is never an asset of the data holder and SHALL NOT be transferred, sublicensed, assigned or inherited by any license between holders. A license over an N3 dataset or N1 record conveys only the holder's own rights; every downstream use of person-subject content re-derives its authorization from the subject's live S2 consent or a judicial contract, and a licensee acquires no read the subject has not granted. Purported licensing of subject consent is void ab initio (S2-I16) and routes to S7; structuring a transfer to launder subject data through a license is concealment-shaped and routes to A18.
- **LIC-I5 (privacy overrides the holder's license).** Where a license and a data subject's rights conflict, the subject's rights SHALL win: revocation of subject consent (S2-P4) propagates through the S8 cascade to every license and derivative resting on that content, which suspend within one capability-token TTL (S2-I10), and an N3 dataset lands or relicenses only as its owner-consented, S-cluster-conformant projection (D2-I18), never as raw subject rows.
- **LIC-I6 (attribution and integrity are enforceable rights, binding PROV).** An attribution term and a work-integrity term SHALL be first-class enforceable obligations, not courtesies. Every licensed use SHALL carry the PROV attribution chain of its inputs unbroken; stripping, falsifying or truncating a required attribution is a license breach booked in S7, and where the input is commons-funded or share-alike-licensed it is a concealment-class misattribution on the D2-I14 plagiarism pattern. Attribution obligations survive relicensing and derivation and bind every successor through the derivation intersection (LIC-I8).
- **LIC-I7 (open licenses are first-class).** The open-license classes (public_domain, attribution, share_alike, and bounded permissive and non-commercial variants) SHALL be first-class registered license templates in the license-template vocabulary (a named entry in the single D-layer defaults annex mastered by the civilization parameter steward, DVAL-I15, to which IVAL contributes under its A12 mandate), not private contracts of one holder. Their obligations are enforceable by any person with standing, not only by the licensor, and by the commons steward for commons-landed works. A public_domain dedication is irrevocable; an attribution or share-alike grant is irrevocable as to copies already released and binds all derivatives.
- **LIC-I8 (share-alike is an inherited redisclosure rule).** A share_alike (copyleft) obligation is a redisclosure_rule that SHALL propagate by the derivation intersection of S3-I10 and S2-I17: any derived_artifact over a share-alike input SHALL be offered under terms no more restrictive than the strongest share-alike input, SHALL name every input owner as co-grantor or issue under the input's pre-accepted derivative policy, and its attribution chain SHALL concatenate every input (PROV). Enclosing a share-alike-derived work under closed terms suspends the deriving agent's grant rights pending cure (the S3-I10 unregistered-derivation pattern) and, if concealed, routes to A18 per LIC-I10.
- **LIC-I9 (commons-landing of publicly funded information).** Publicly funded information SHALL land in the knowledge commons under an open license, per the property commons doctrine and D2-I7: (a) research outputs at majority commons funding land pro-rata per D2-I7, below-majority landing pro-rata with commons access proportional to the commons share; (b) state operational records land world-readable under a public_domain or attribution license per the N1 public-record classification (S1-I13), subject only to the closed, harm-tested, time-bounded exemption list; (c) the polity's own governing software (N4 systems the state runs to govern) lands source-open under an open license, so the governed can read the code that governs them. The commons license is a non-waivable term of the funding or procurement contract.
- **LIC-I10 (enclosure is the named offense).** Enclosure, routing a commons-owed information object into private, closed or exclusively licensed control to defeat its commons-landing obligation, is the offense the property and D2 specs name. It SHALL be prosecuted as concealment-shaped evasion: it routes to the A18 offense ladder, carries the concealment clawback of any gain and any credit obtained (D2-I14, D2-I20), and any contractual structure built to defeat the majority-commons threshold is an aggravating basis on the INT-I4 anti-structuring rule (D2-I7). Provisional bookings carry full suspensive contest (A18-I34); a materially incomplete self-report of an enclosure flips the entire matter into the perpetual, no-limitation class (A18-I49).
- **LIC-I11 (proprietary licenses clear on the $ rail; the state prices only the residual).** Where an information object is lawfully private and tradable (its rights are private and no commons-landing obligation attaches), its license SHALL clear as a price on the $ rail through C2 like any order; the Ae ledger never books the object itself (the M3 pattern). The state SHALL NOT set the price of knowledge: the MVAL overlay is automatic, uniform and carries no per-object verdict (MVAL-I5), and the public charge is the externality residual priced on the C7 counterfactual (M3-I10) with the data-good default commons share of M3-I14; where the same object is essential information under a standing classification (LIC-I18), the pricing-power rent margin of LIC-I12 is charged in addition (MVAL-I7). Neither is a set tariff on the work.
- **LIC-I12 (essential-information monopoly meets rent and essential-facility discipline).** A monopoly license over essential information (a standard every implementer must adopt, a reference dataset that is a natural monopoly, a format required for interoperation) SHALL, while a registered essential-information classification (LIC-I18) stands, be treated as an essential facility: it meets the essential-facility duty and the C1-I7 rent discipline. The holder's efficiency and genuine innovation are credited as created value and protected for the bounded innovation horizon (C1-I7); the pricing-power margin above the attested-cost benchmark is charged as captured rent (MVAL-I7), and that benchmark's amortization denominator SHALL be independently attested on the C1-I7 and C7 discipline from verifiable delivery or license counts, never holder self-report, with understatement of the base treated as concealment on the same footing as understated cost and the burden on the above-threshold holder. Access is a delivered floor funded first-charge from the COMP-I17 standing reserve that recovered charges replenish, ordered under the single HOR-I20 and RES-I23 published inter-floor priority rule and the HOR-I4 minimum coverage ratio so exactly one ordering governs every N-layer floor under reserve stress, non-severable at the point every implementer needs it, and the essential terms are non-discriminatory across licensees. Access-floor funding and non-discrimination apply only while the classification stands (LIC-I18).
- **LIC-I13 (IP carve-outs are bounded and expire, binding N12 and D2-I9).** Every proprietary or exclusive information license SHALL be a bounded, time-limited carve-out on the C1-I7 innovation-protection pattern, registered against the N12 ip_right and its IP registrar. It MAY cover a specific embodiment, application or expression for the published horizon; it SHALL NEVER cover the claim-evidence graph or an underlying fact (LIC-I3, D2-I9); and any embargo on commons landing is bounded by the embargo ceiling named in the single D-layer defaults annex mastered by the civilization parameter steward (DVAL-I15, D2-I19) and expires automatically. Perpetual or auto-renewing exclusivity over essential or commons-funded information is void.
- **LIC-I14 (orphan works and lapsed rights, bounded consent terminus).** Where an information object's rights-holder cannot be resolved after a registered diligent search, a bounded orphan_use_license MAY issue on the civilization-spec consent-terminus pattern (the HOR-owned bounded consent terminus of D2-I18), and only over tiers (b) through (e) of the LIC-I2 rights stack, never over a data subject's consent (LIC-I17): use is permitted under a published open-terms floor, any royalty owed is escrowed to a resolved-holder account, and the license converts or terminates on a returning claimant, who recovers the escrow but holds no veto over uses already made in good faith. A diligent search over person-subject content of a living or post-mortem-protected subject never unlocks that content (LIC-I17). The diligent-search standard, the escrow rate and the terminus window are named entries in the single D-layer defaults annex mastered by the civilization parameter steward (DVAL-I15); a false or perfunctory diligent-search attestation is concealment-class (A18).
- **LIC-I15 (the license register is append-only, corrections as new entries).** Every license, amendment, revocation and orphan or relicense event SHALL be an X1 act appended to the owning R3 register on the R2-I1 append-only discipline: a license is never edited in place, a correction is a new entry plus a superseded event, and a revoked license survives forever as the revoked record plus its history. License state SHALL be derivable and court-provable from the register's inclusion-promise and mutual-anchoring chain (R1-I31, R1-I32) sealed on the S4-I4 cadence, the R2-I13 ledger checkpoint being the analogous pattern and not the governing authority, and deposited commons artifacts carry a tamper-evident hash commitment on the F6 integrity pattern; succession of a licensor or of a license registrar follows R1-I11 without lapsing any live grant.
- **LIC-I16 (license terms are machine-checkable, necessary but not sufficient).** Every license SHALL be expressed against the registered license-template vocabulary, a named entry in the single D-layer defaults annex mastered by the civilization parameter steward (DVAL-I15) to which IVAL contributes under its A12 mandate, so permitted_uses, redistribution_rule, derivation_rule, term and attribution_obligation are checkable by the owner-side gate in constant time, and license-scope compliance MAY be discharged by an S6 predicate that proves conformance without moving the object. The vocabulary SHALL publish its bounded expressiveness, a named steward-mastered list of what it can and cannot represent; a license term outside that vocabulary routes to A6 adjudication and SHALL NOT by itself book unauthorized_use. The constant-time gate check is necessary but not sufficient: it can deny a use that contradicts a machine-checkable term, which is a per se breach, but a pass does not certify compliance with a non-expressible term. Default deny still governs where no license basis resolves at all: absent a resolvable license, an open license or a statute granting the use, a use asserted over a governed object is unauthorized_use, evidenced on the possession-without-access_event pattern (S3-I7). Ambient scanning for infringement is prohibited and forensic checks run only on an artifact entering a case; lawful discovery of enclosure and copyleft breach runs the bounded paths of LIC-I19.
- **LIC-I17 (holder-exhaustion never reaches the subject, binding PROV and S).** The consent-terminus and orphan_use_license routes (LIC-I14, and the HOR-owned consent terminus of D2-I18 that PROV binds for training data) SHALL resolve ONLY tiers (b) through (e) of the LIC-I2 rights stack and SHALL NEVER resolve tier (a), a data subject's privacy and consent. Person-subject content of a living or post-mortem-protected subject is never reachable through holder-exhaustion, attested succession-exhaustion or a diligent search: where the subject is living, use requires the subject's live S2 consent (S2-I4) or a judicial_access_contract (S2-I8) regardless of the holder's resolvability, and a holder vanishing says nothing about whether the subject consented. A vanished or exhausted S1 controller succeeds to a commons steward under R1-I11, which inherits the duty to re-derive each subject's live consent for itself as recipient, never a license or terminus that presumes it.
- **LIC-I18 (essential-information classification is a registered, contestable, expiring event).** Essential-facility treatment of an information object (LIC-I12) SHALL rest on a registered essential-information classification, a NET-I1 clone: an X1 classification event appended to the owning R3 register recording the two-part bottleneck test (no substitutable route to the information AND uneconomic to reproduce it at served scale), attached to the factual bottleneck, with an A6 contest route and a published review cadence. NET-I19 is imported wholesale: an implementer holds a bypass and entry right, the classification stands down on demonstrated contestability, and an automatic re-classification tripwire fires when a substitutable route or economic reproduction appears, so the corridor machinery never outlives the monopoly that justified it. The access-floor funding and non-discrimination of LIC-I12 apply only while the classification stands; on stand-down the holder returns to ordinary COMP treatment. The bottleneck test, the review cadence and the tripwire thresholds are named entries in the single D-layer defaults annex mastered by the civilization parameter steward (DVAL-I15).
- **LIC-I19 (enclosure and copyleft are discoverable without ambient surveillance).** Because ambient scanning of private holdings is prohibited (LIC-I16), share-alike breach (LIC-I8) and enclosure (LIC-I10) SHALL be made discoverable only by two bounded, privacy-preserving paths: (a) any person with standing (LIC-I7) or the commons steward MAY compel an S6 conformance predicate proving whether a released artifact derives from a share-alike or commons input, answered without moving the content (S6-I1, S6-I4); and (b) the commons steward holds an audit right over commons-landed objects for which it is holder of record (D2-I8, D2-I26). ## Information in the value doctrine (IVAL)

The knowledge and information layer books the polity's most peculiar value, because information is not like a tonne of steel: it is non-rival, near-costless to copy, and worth nothing until someone reads it. IVAL is the value-doctrine binding for the N layer, and it inherits the whole frame unchanged. Every N1 document, N3 dataset and N4 software system is an owner-gated record: it resolves to exactly one S1 controller, is readable only under an S2 contract on a default-deny footing, has its reads logged at S4, is sensed by the state only at S5 cohort grain, and answers with an S6 predicate wherever a predicate suffices. Authoring a document, curating a dataset, and building software are K2 acts realized over X1 occurrences that book created value per axis on the Ae ledgers, primarily on the knowledge axis, append-only and witnessed-checkpointed on R2, with corrections filed as new entries and never as edits. The $ price of the information good is whatever the market clears, and by the two-rail rule the Ae credit and the $ price never net, never sum, and never convert into one another.

On top of every cleared information flow the state applies the one uniform published overlay, and it prices only the residual the price signal cannot carry. Information generates four residuals the overlay must reach. Non-rivalry makes pricing-power rent on essential information the paradigm unearned rent: a reference dataset or a de-facto-standard format has a marginal copy cost near zero, so any price held above an attested fixed-cost-plus-innovation benchmark is rent, charged on the C1-I7 counterfactual against independently attested cost and an independently attested user base, with the free-competition band and the innovation horizon protected. Network effects make lock-in rent the second: a platform whose value is its user base or corpus can raise switching cost until exit is foreclosed, and the COMP concentration-on-power discipline binds it, with interoperability and portability reaching the data subject and each contributor as the anti-lock-in floor. Information externalities are the third: a leaked dataset, an insecure release, or a manipulated record imposes measured anti-value on the harmed, with privacy harm the paradigm, priced where it is a bounded residual and floored where it touches the cohort or dignity floor. Quality misrepresentation is the fourth: a dataset sold as fit-for-purpose that is not imposes anti-value on every downstream consumer, honest error revaluing prospectively while concealment claws back.

The commons side is delivered, not hoped for. Three information-commons floors, the knowledge floor, the data-commons floor, and the auditable-governing-software floor, are standing-funded from a reserve that recovered charges replenish and bind the single published inter-floor priority rule (HOR-I20 / RES-I23), so exactly one ordering governs all N-layer floors under reserve stress rather than each floor ranking ahead of discretionary routing in isolation. Where a value dimension of information is under-served, the state pulls it into being, reference datasets, open standards, secure public software, as strategic demand on the $ rail: it is a large customer with civilizational preferences, never a planner that commands producers or sets the price of knowledge. No proxy governs: download counts, dataset size, and lines of code are diagnostic signals only and never a value or funding base. Both flanks are guarded: the honest author, curator and producer keep the protected creation return, the free-competition band, provisional booking, honest revaluation without offense, and suspensive contest, while the rent-holder, the leaker, the tamperer and the fitness-fabricator meet the overlay, the concealment class, and the offense ladder.
