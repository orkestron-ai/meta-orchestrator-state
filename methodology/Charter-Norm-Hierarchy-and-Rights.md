## 1. The norm hierarchy — the Charter is jus cogens

```
Axiacracy Charter   (meta-constitution; universal to ANY adopting state; jus cogens)
   ↑ conform-or-VOID
State Constitution  (one polity's instantiation of the Charter)
   ↑ conform
Legal Codes         (civil / criminal / administrative / ...)
   ↑ conform
Regulations / policies / ControlPolicy dials
   ↑ conform
Individual rulings / charges
```

- **The Charter is methodology-level** — the same for every state that adopts Axiacracy. A
  polity's own constitution **instantiates** it and **may not contradict** it; a
  non-conforming norm is **void** (generalizing Principle 5: what can't be reconstructed
  from — or conforms to — public supreme norms is void). This maps onto the existing
  Meta-Universe **MUC anchor** (A1: "Universe = the State; its constitution = the MUC").
- **Constitutional review is first-class:** the judiciary / a guardian Sub-Agent checks any
  law against the Charter (does it breach a floor? enable concentration? break transparency
  or non-retroactivity?).
- **Eternity clause** (cf. German Basic Law Art. 79(3)): the Charter's **core is
  unamendable** — Axiacracy's own amendment process **cannot abolish Axiacracy**.
  Unamendable core: inviolable rights, anti-concentration, transparency, contestability, and
  the value-balancing mandate itself.

> **We have been drafting the Charter all along.** The 15 A0 principles
> (`Governance-Mechanics.md`) are effectively its **articles**; the 9 methodology docs are
> its **rationale + mechanics**. The Charter is the short supreme document; the rest
> elaborates. This is exactly the methodology/implementation split at the legal layer:
> **Charter = methodology (universal); State Constitution + codes = implementation (per-polity).**

## 2. Ultra-anti-value — split by KIND, not lumped

Adds an **intent/kind axis** to the anti-value taxonomy (on top of *scope* and *lag*).
**Not everything is priced** — some harms are *rights violations*, not value-transactions.

| Kind | Examples | Axiacracy's response |
|---|---|---|
| **Rights violation** (NOT priced) | murder, violence, coercion, slavery | **Not chargeable as anti-value** (pricing would imply it's payable/tradeable — grotesque). → **inviolable rights + criminal law.** The Charter sets the *rights floor*; local law supplies specifics |
| **Malicious / predatory** | theft, fraud, malicious hacking, economic crime, parasitism (T11, pure leakage) | **native**: large anti-value charges, clawback, escrow forfeit, standing/autonomy loss + criminal law at the extreme |
| **Self-directed / distress** | addiction, self-harm | **care, not punishment**: a signal that the person's θ and `meaning` axis are collapsing → reshape the demand field + needs restoration (Cognitive Health / B5). Criminalizing it is a category error |
| **Negligent** | externalities without intent | the standard Pigou overlay |

### Two principled points
- **Not everything is priced.** Rights-protected harms are **removed from the value market
  entirely** — protection against monetizing the impermissible.
- **Why anyone may be removed from the system (incapacitation/imprisonment)** is **not "the
  price of the crime"** but **A9**: their continued participation makes **others'
  participation conditions θ fail** (a murderer collapses everyone's safety-θ). Incapacitation
  is justified by **protecting the sustainability of others' value systems**, not by
  retribution. "**Ultra**"-anti-value = anti-value that threatens the *system's* sustainability.
- **Dual-use (hackers):** malicious attacker vs security researcher — distinguished by
  **realized effect + intent**, not by label; interpretable and contestable in court
  (`Courts-and-Adjudication.md`).

## 3. Subsidiarity of enforcement — what's native vs delegated

- **The Charter mandates and constrains** (universal): the inviolable-rights floor; that
  anti-value **must** be priced; that a judiciary **must** exist; and the invariants
  (transparency, non-retroactivity, contestability, anti-concentration, per-cohort floors,
  participation-condition sustainability).
- **Local law supplies the specifics** (per-polity): the actual criminal statutes,
  procedure, sentencing — **provided they conform to the Charter.** Non-conforming statutes
  are void.

So ultra-anti-value is **neither out of scope nor fully centralized**: Axiacracy is the
**meta-layer that requires, bounds, and reviews** the local criminal-law layer that does the
operational work.
