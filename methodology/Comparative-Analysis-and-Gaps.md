## 2. What each rival foregrounds — take / reject / gap it exposes

- **Liberal democracy** — legitimacy by consent, rule of law, separation of powers, **peaceful
  transfer of power**. *Take:* contestability, rights, checks. *Reject:* headcount-majoritarianism
  as the value signal. **Exposes gap:** succession/transfer of power (G2).
- **Capitalism** — private property, price discovery, capital allocation, profit.
  *Take:* market clears allocation (revealed value). *Reject:* single-axis value; concentration.
  **Exposes gap:** property/capital regime (G1).
- **Socialism / communism** — collective ownership, need-based distribution, class.
  *Take:* externalities/commons matter; distribution is a first-class concern. *Reject:* central
  planning (Hayek), single class lens. **Exposes gap:** explicit distributive principle (G6).
- **Georgism** — land & natural-resource **rent belongs to all**; tax unearned rent; citizen's
  dividend. *Take (strongly):* the **earned-vs-unearned-rent** distinction; **AI-compute/data as
  the new "land"** whose rent is commons-owed — a precise tool for the article's anti-BigTech
  thesis. **Exposes gap:** property/rent (G1).
- **Futarchy** (Hanson, *vote values, bet beliefs*) — democracy sets the welfare metric; markets
  pick policies to maximize it. *Take:* we already "vote on values (weights)"; we should add a
  **belief-aggregation layer** for *which interventions actually move the Æ-balance* (an
  "Æ-futarchy"). **Exposes gap:** policy efficacy (G5).
- **Doughnut economics** (Raworth) — social foundation (floor) + ecological ceiling; a safe &
  just space. *Take:* near-identical to our **floors + ceilings/corridors** — a validation and a
  ready **binding target** for floor/ceiling specification (SDG social foundation + planetary
  boundaries). *Transcend:* ours is *dynamic weighted balance*, not static thresholds.
- **Capability approach** (Sen/Nussbaum) — wellbeing = **capabilities** (real freedoms to
  achieve functionings), threshold secured by institutions. *Take (strongly):* grounds our
  **floors and needs (B5)** normatively — floors should protect a *threshold of central
  capabilities*, and **realized Æ ≈ achieved functionings** (vs nominal resources). **Fills gap:** G6.
- **Cybernetic governance / VSM** (Beer, Cybersyn) — the closest ancestor: recursive 5-subsystem
  **viability structure**, **requisite variety** (Ashby), autonomy+cohesion. *Take:* map
  Axiacracy onto VSM (citizens≈S1, Sub-Agents≈S3/4, Charter≈S5) as a **structural viability
  check**; adopt requisite-variety as a design constraint (does state sensing match societal
  complexity?). **Exposes gap:** structural viability (G8).
- **Realism / IR & Weber** — the state = **monopoly on legitimate violence**; external
  sovereignty, defense, diplomacy. **Exposes gap:** external relations & defense (G3).

## 4. Where Axiacracy already exceeds the rivals (positioning)

- **Value beyond GDP** — a *tensor*, not a scalar (vs capitalism/GDP).
- **Perspectival value + minority floors** — pluralism structural, not majoritarian (vs democracy).
- **Anti-value priced instantly + retrospectively** — externalities are first-class (vs capitalism's blind spot; vs the slow political chain).
- **Money demoted to instrument** — steering variable is balance, not revenue.
- **Transparency, contestability, non-retroactivity** — against technocracy's opacity.
- **Cybernetic + value-vector** — Beer's feedback *plus* a multidimensional value substrate he never had.

**Verdict:** the value-theoretic core is more complete than any single rival; the gaps are the
**classical "statecraft" dimensions** (property/rent, succession, external relations, membership,
emergency) plus two **method upgrades** (policy-efficacy/futarchy, capability-grounded floors) and
two **hygiene** items (VSM viability, self-diagnosed pathologies). None block P0; G1/G5/G6 lightly
touch P0 (what floors mean, what's ownable).
