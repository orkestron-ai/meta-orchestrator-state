## 0. Name & positioning

- **Axiacracy** = ἀξία (*axía*, **value**) + -κρατία (**rule**) = **governance by value**. The
  **-a-** anchors on *axía* (value), deliberately distinct from **αξιοκρατία** (*axiokratía* =
  meritocracy, "rule by the worthy **persons**") and from the market-only "axiocracy"
  (exchange-value only). Axiacracy = **rule by value (the multidimensional vector/flows)**, not
  by the worthy, not by price.
- **Two layers:** **Axianomy** = the *discipline/methodology* (how to govern by value);
  **Axiacracy** = the *form of government* it produces. (Cf. VMT/Æilus = methodology.)
- **One line:** *a state that governs by balancing multidimensional value flows instead of by
  taxation — money demoted to an instrument, civilizational value the steering variable.*

## 1. Premise & thesis

AI breaks the industrial link **labour → value**; the loop labour→income→consumption→tax→
redistribution collapses; value concentrates around AI-infrastructure owners. The state must
evolve **tax-collector → orchestrator of civilizational value**. Society is a **graph of value
transformers** (citizens, families, businesses, AI, cities, infrastructure, agents) that
consume/transform/create value or generate **anti-value**. The two central risks: **value
concentration** and **loss of meaning**. Governance = **balancing multidimensional value
flows**, measured far beyond GDP.

## 2. The value substrate (V1–V5) — the governing core

| | Meta-model | Essence |
|---|---|---|
| **V1** | Value Transformer | any entity, modelled as consuming/transforming/creating value or anti-value |
| **V2** | Multidimensional Value (Æ-vector) | value is a **10-axis vector**, a *shared vocabulary* (partial commensurability), with per-cohort/region **corridors** (floor/band/ceiling) |
| **V3** | Value Flow | edges; lifecycle **Planned → Realized → Retrospective**; **flow resistance** (Planned≠Realized) and **leakage** |
| **V4** | Anti-Value | **independent category** (not "negative value"); classified by **scope** (edge/systemic), **lag** (immediate/short/long/generational), **intent/kind** (rights-violation/malicious/distress/negligent); **responsibility share** |
| **V5** | Value System / Frame | VMT **{A,F,G,R,T}**: the frame that *interprets* value; each citizen/family/business/state is a frame; **participation condition θ** |

**The 10 axes:** economic · educational · scientific · demographic · cognitive ·
infrastructural · social_stability · ecological · meaning · **epistemic**. (*Equity* and
*generosity* are **not axes** — equity is a floor-protected constraint + guardian; generosity is
a flow type. Design rule: **axes are what you trade off via weights; what you must never trade
off is a constraint, not an axis.**)

**VMT grounding (perspectival value).** Value exists *only within a value system* (A1/A2/T1);
the polity is a **federation of value systems**; the state is **one frame with a bounded
mandate, never the apex/owner of all value** — a no-apex result *forced* by T1 (no
value-in-general) + A12 (no conservation) + A10 (systems meet only at external flows). Across
frames/currencies the **invariant is Realized Æ, not price**. When a state's goals make an
actor's **θ** fail, its own value system is unsustainable (A9/T7) — a first-class alarm.

## 3. The governing loop (one tick)

**sense → diagnose imbalance → generate strategic demand → incentivize → re-sense.**
The **imbalance signal**:
```
imbalance =  w_a · distance(current_Æ, target_band)   ← steering, weight-scaled
           + below_floor_penalty                       ← LARGE; NOT weight-scaled
           + variance_penalty(across cohorts)          ← equity; NOT weight-scaled
```
The majority steers *emphasis*; it **cannot vote a cohort's floor into irrelevance**. Steer on
**settled** value + trends; react cautiously to **provisional** value. Hysteresis limits swing.

## 4. The Charter (meta-constitution) + norm hierarchy

The **Charter** is the supreme document (**jus cogens**): 22 articles in 6 parts (Foundations ·
Inviolable Rights & Floors · Governance Principles · Value-Money-Markets · Epistemic & Judicial
Integrity · Supremacy & Amendment), with an **unamendable eternity core**. Norm hierarchy:
```
Charter → State Constitution → Legal Codes → Regulations → Rulings   (conform-or-VOID)
```
**Charter = methodology (universal); everything below = implementation (per-polity).**
Constitutional review is first-class.

**The 15 principles (A0, the Charter's source):** 1 imbalance-only signal · 2 declared/attested/
revisable value · 3 anti-value symmetric & delayed · 4 weights democratic, rules not · 5
transparency-or-void · 6 subsidiarity · 7 decay & anti-concentration (incl. state organs) · 8
generate demand, don't command supply · 9 protected meaning · 10 per-cohort floors (minority
protection) · 11 symmetry of harm · 12 non-purchasable influence · 13 generosity > accumulation
· 14 non-retroactivity · 15 rebuttable interpretation.

## 5. Governance mechanics

- **Civic loop (4 signed public events):** `NeedAnnounced` (request) · `ContributionMade`
  (→ value flow, *claimed* Æ) · `ValueAttested` (*realized* Æ by an independent beneficiary) ·
  `VoteCast` (priority budget over axes). The **claimed-vs-attested gap** = flow resistance;
  over-claiming costs standing.
- **Weights & corridors:** citizens set the **weights** (what to prioritize) and the **floor
  thresholds** (democratic); the **axes + the existence of floors are Charter-entrenched**. The
  state steers within corridors.
- **Liquid democracy:** revocable, **topic-scoped** delegation to **parties**; **quadratic**
  vote cost; reputation-weighted but **capped**; party delegated-weight **quadratically
  discounted** past a threshold (no dictator-by-accumulation).
- **Experts / anti-propaganda:** domain **calibrated expertise** (Brier), earned & on the
  verifiable log; **reach non-purchasable**; the state **annotates, never censors**;
  conflict-of-interest on-ledger.
- **Recognition & generosity:** medals are **peer-issued** (costly to the giver, weighted by
  giver reputation); **donated, un-consumed value books bonus Æ** — the gradient points to
  solidarity, not hoarding.
- **Minority protection:** floors are **per-cohort and non-votable**; an **Equity/Ombudsman
  guardian** watches cohort variance; **symmetry of harm** — every policy's net effect across
  *all* cohorts is measured and published (protecting a minority at net cost to the majority is
  as visible as the reverse).

## 6. Value, money & markets

- **Two layers:** a **sovereign Æ-ledger** (non-transferable accounting — Æ is **not a coin**) +
  a **thin `$` settlement rail** (fast, impersonal). Money is **one axis + an instrument**, not
  the sovereign.
- **Consumption:** ceremony proportional to stakes — **T0** ambient (take a yogurt, auto-settle,
  *silence = attestation*) · **T1** attested · **T2** reviewed. **Default-trust; reclamation by
  exception** (spoiled → anti-value charge to the business); statistical QC.
- **Markets:** the **market clears allocation** (revealed value); the **state prices only the
  externality residual** (multidimensional **Pigou**). **Profit ≠ civilizational value** (a
  profitable-but-harmful actor loses standing). The **vote-vs-spend gap** is a first-class
  diagnostic.
- **Value↔money coupling:** anti-value and money share one ledger, cleared same tick.
  **Instant:** externality-escrow, continuous anti-value debit, concentration charge,
  standing/access loss (**not** discretionary fines). **Retrospective:** revaluation + clawback.
  **Finality for the honest; perpetual liability for the concealing.** Recovered value is
  **earmarked to the harmed cohorts** (remediation routing).
- **Relative value:** no universal unit (T13/A12/A13); **Realized Æ is the cross-currency
  invariant**; intra-axis ratios = market discovery, inter-axis = voted weights; **fairness is a
  constraint (floors + θ), not the objective**.
- **Inter-frame routing:** knowing an actor's frame lets the state **pay them in their own
  currency** (positive-sum), **monitor θ**, and **route positive-sum flows money-markets miss**
  (state as value **router/matchmaker**, not owner). **Privacy-preserving frame disclosure:** a
  scoped access grant yields a **frame summary** (from top realized/given flows), never the raw
  ledger; cohort-aggregate by default, opt-in for individuals, ZK-attestable.

## 7. Property, rent & the commons (G1)

**Own the earned, not the unearned.** Ownership extends **as far as creation, no further**.
**Unearned rent = captured − created** (VMT T11 leakage/parasitism) — from nature, society,
position, or concentration — is **commons-owed**, and is priced by the **existing** overlay →
**commons fund + citizen dividend**. **AI-compute/data/network-effects = the new "land"** (value
from humanity's collective data + public science + network effects) → their rent to the commons
= *mechanizes the article's mandatory contributions to education/cognition/science*. **Renting a
produced asset** (car, tools, building **structure**) is **earned service income**; only the
**land/location/scarcity/monopoly** component is unearned. **Inheritance:** goods bequeathable,
but land-rent stays commons-owed, the concentration ceiling caps dynasties, and **Æ/standing is
non-transferable** (you inherit goods, never civic power). **Capital/credit** when money is
demoted: **returns to creation, not to ownership**; credit = resources advanced against **future
realized Æ**; interest-as-pure-time-rent constrained (flagged as the least-tested part).

## 8. Distributive justice (G6)

**Capability sufficientarianism + a bounded contribution-responsive band + an anti-concentration
ceiling + a generosity premium = the corridor, grounded.** Floors are in **capability-space
(Sen), not resource-space** — guarantee *achieved functioning*, which needs **unequal inputs
(equity, not equality)**; **Realized Æ ≈ achieved functioning**. **Nussbaum's 10 central
capabilities** crosswalk to axes+rights, fixing **which axes carry a floor**. Thresholds are
democratic; the existence of floors is entrenched. *Not* egalitarian, *not* pure meritocratic,
*not* utilitarian (floors lexically prior).

## 9. Law, rights & crime

Inviolable rights (life, bodily integrity, liberty, consent, exit) are **not priceable/votable/
tradeable**. **Ultra-anti-value split by KIND:** **rights-violation** (murder/violence → **not
priced**, criminal law) · **malicious/predatory** (fraud/theft/economic crime → native charges +
clawback) · **self-directed/distress** (addiction → **care, not punishment**) · **negligent**
(→ Pigou). **Incapacitation is justified by A9** (a person whose participation makes others' θ
fail), **not retribution**. **Courts:** an agent's assessment is **authoritative-by-default but
rebuttable**; contestable in court (independent reviewer). **Non-retroactivity:** judged by the
**norms in force at act-time** — *evidence updates retroactively, norms do not*; requires a
**versioned, time-indexed norm-set**. **Subsidiarity:** the Charter mandates & bounds; local law
supplies specifics.

## 10. Leadership & succession (G2)

**Separation of powers:** demos legislates *values* (weights/floors) · Sub-Agents *execute* ·
courts *review*. **Selection = "vote on values, appoint on competence"** (best-calibrated agent
assigned, performance-revocable); **sortition** for oversight juries. **Rotation without losing
institutional memory** (PositionMemory belongs to the office, BR8). **Continuous** performance
accountability + recall + capture→clawback (organs included). **Peaceful transfer structurally
defused:** power distributed (no throne), gradual (autonomy ramps), **transparency voids
seizure** (Art. 20), **loyalty to the Charter, not the person**, PositionMemory = no vacuum. The
**Meta-Orchestrator = a rotating, Charter-bound coordinator, not a sovereign.** **Succession =
secure hot-swap of an open, validated algorithmic officeholder:** candidates pass a **validation
gate** (vs system abuse / regime-change / hidden value-redirection); structural defense — **all
value moves as visible flows (A3), so hidden diversion surfaces as a flow + unearned-rent
anomaly**; plus reproducible-build/logic-hash, multi-vendor cross-check, staged autonomy,
continuous monitoring + retrospective clawback for sleepers.

## 11. Anti-value taxonomy

**Scope** (edge vs systemic = accumulated flow-resistance/leakage) · **lag** → enforcement
instrument (immediate→continuous debit, short→escrow, long→clawback, generational→trustee) ·
**intent/kind**. **Responsibility share** = Shapley-style marginal contribution + concealment
multiplier, provisional/revaluable under contemporaneous norms. Three worked profiles: **yogurt**
(edge/short), **content-dependency** (systemic/long — *no edge is harmful*), **BigTech
concentration** (systemic/continuous+long). The **simulation has ground truth** → scores the
state's anti-value **estimator accuracy** (reality can't).
