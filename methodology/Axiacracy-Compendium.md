## 0. Name & positioning
**Axiacracy** = ἀξία (*axía*, **value**) + -κρατία (**rule**) = **governance by value** (the
multidimensional vector/flows), not by the worthy (that is αξιοκρατία, meritocracy), not by
price (market-only "axiocracy"). **Axianomy** = the discipline; **Axiacracy** = the form of
government. One line: *a state that governs by seeing and balancing multidimensional value
flows instead of by taxation — money demoted to an instrument, the balance of value the thing
it steers toward.*

## 1. Premise & thesis
AI breaks **labour → value**; the tax-and-redistribute loop collapses; value concentrates
around AI-infrastructure owners. The state evolves **tax-collector → orchestrator of
civilizational value**. Society is a **graph of value transformers**. The two central risks:
**concentration** and **loss of meaning**. Governance = **balancing multidimensional value
flows**, measured far beyond GDP.

## 2. THE GOVERNING STANCE (the v2 core) — sensing vs. coercion

Three moves define Axiacracy:

### 2.1 Sensing is rich; coercion is bounded
- **Sensing** (seeing multidimensional value — the civilizational dashboard) is *observation*.
  It is **not tyranny and not an "apex metric"** — it is the founding revolution: *stop being
  blind to non-GDP value.* Sensing is **rich across dimensions**. *(But rich ≠ total
  surveillance: sensing is cohort-aggregate by default, privacy-budgeted, individual
  granularity only by consent — §7.)*
- **Coercion** (charges, floors enforced, resources compelled, liberty restricted) is where
  legitimacy, Goodhart and collusion bite. It is **strictly bounded** (§2.2).

**The founding idea lives in the sensing layer and survives whole.** Only coercion is disciplined.

### 2.2 Graduated coercion = f(measurement-confidence × inter-frame scope)
The state's **power to compel scales with how well it can measure and how far the harm crosses
between frames**:
- **High-confidence · objective · inter-frame harm or material-floor breach** → **coercive
  action permitted** (charge → restriction → loss of liberty, via escalation ladders, §10).
- **Low-confidence · subjective · intra-frame** → **soft power only**: publish the signal,
  offer incentives, illuminate; let markets/citizens/frames respond. **The state may never
  coerce on a metric it cannot defend.** It may *see* meaning; it may never *compel* on it.

This is a **constitutional dial tying power to epistemics** — it dissolves the
"measurement-tyranny" and "apex-metric" objections at the source. **The specific thresholds and
penalty ladders are set per-polity (implementation); the principle is universal (Charter).**

### 2.3 Measurement is adversarial security, not an oracle
No single self-reported signal is trusted. Measurement is **multi-source and cross-checked**:
objective side-effects · revealed behaviour (costly, hard-to-fake signals) · independent
third-party sensors · **prediction markets on outcomes** (belief-aggregation / futarchy-style)
· attestation only as a *cross-checked* input, **staked** (false attestation is costly), with
**collusion-graph anomaly detection**. **Retrospective revaluation is an anti-Goodhart device**
(you cannot durably game a metric that is re-judged with hindsight). Measurement is
**continuously red-teamed**. Markets keep doing allocation (Hayek respected); the state only
*adds* signals for what markets miss — it does not replace price discovery.

> **The simulation's job (and the game's):** map *where value-flow governance stays stable vs.
> gets gamed* under noisy, adversarial, contested measurement — **not** assume a truth oracle.
> Players *are* the adversarial pressure on the sensors. The keystone risk is the experiment's
> central variable, honestly.

**10 axes:** economic · educational · scientific · demographic · cognitive · infrastructural ·
social_stability · ecological · meaning · epistemic. (*Equity* and *generosity* are **not axes**.)
**Weights `w(t)` are the state frame's democratically-set priorities** — an open political
choice, revisable, contestable — **not a metaphysical claim that the axes are interchangeable.**
Other frames keep their own weights; the state does not translate or overwrite internal frame
value — it acts only at inter-frame externalities + material floors (§2.2).

## 4. The governing loop (one tick)
**sense (richly) → diagnose imbalance → generate demand → RESPOND (graduated).**
```
imbalance =  w_a · distance(current_Æ, target_band)   ← steering signal, weight-scaled
           + below_floor_penalty                       ← LARGE; NOT weight-scaled
           + variance_penalty(across cohorts)          ← equity; NOT weight-scaled
```
The **response is graduated (§2.2)**: coerce only where confidence×inter-frame permits;
otherwise illuminate + incentivize. Steer on **settled** value + trends; act cautiously on
**provisional** value; hysteresis limits swing. *(Formal math, event schemas, cohort definition,
target-band setting, and a sample tick are the pending formal spec — §15.)*

## 5. The Charter (meta-constitution) & norm hierarchy

graduated-coercion principle**: *coercive power is a function of measurement confidence and
inter-frame scope; the state may compel only on defensible, objective, inter-frame or
floor-relevant measurement.*
```
Charter (principle) → State Constitution → Legal Codes (specific thresholds/penalties) → Regulations → Rulings   (conform-or-VOID)
```
**Charter = methodology (universal); the specific coercion ladders = implementation (per-polity).**
**The 15 principles** (imbalance-only signal · declared/attested/revisable value · anti-value
symmetric & delayed · weights democratic, rules not · transparency-or-void · subsidiarity ·
decay & anti-concentration incl. state organs · generate demand not command supply · protected
meaning · per-cohort floors · symmetry of harm · non-purchasable influence · generosity >
accumulation · non-retroactivity · rebuttable interpretation) — **+ graduated coercion (new).**

## 6. Governance mechanics
Civic loop (`NeedAnnounced` · `ContributionMade` claimed-Æ · `ValueAttested` realized-Æ,
cross-checked & staked · `VoteCast`); claimed-vs-attested gap = flow resistance. Weights &
floor-thresholds are voted; axes + existence-of-floors are entrenched. **Liquid democracy**
(revocable topic-scoped delegation, quadratic cost, capped, party-weight quadratically
discounted). **Experts** = Brier-calibrated, reach non-purchasable, annotate-not-censor.
**Recognition** = peer-issued, costly, **network-centrality-dampened + decayed** (no scaling into
macro power). **Minority protection** = per-cohort non-votable floors + Equity/Ombudsman guardian
+ symmetry-of-harm.

## 7. Value, money & markets
Two layers: sovereign non-transferable **Æ-ledger** + thin `$` settlement rail (money = one axis
+ instrument, never sovereign — separates civic power from wealth, anti-plutocracy). **Markets
clear allocation** (Hayek); the state **prices only the externality residual** (multidimensional
Pigou); **profit ≠ civilizational value**. Consumption: ceremony proportional to stakes; but
**no silent-positive attestation for contested/high-stakes goods** — silence=attestation applies
only to T0 low-stakes, with random audits + asymmetric consumer protection. **Realized Æ is the
cross-currency invariant**; intra-axis ratios = market discovery, inter-axis = voted weights;
fairness is a constraint, not the objective. **Inter-frame routing** pays actors in their own
frame, monitors θ, matches positive-sum flows — over a **privacy-preserving frame disclosure**
(scoped summary, cohort-aggregate default, opt-in individual, forbidden-inference limits, ZK-attestable).

## 8. Property, rent & the commons
**Own the earned, not the unearned.** Rent is priced from **observable signatures — not an
uncomputable counterfactual**: returns durably above the competitive/risk-adjusted norm,
monopoly pricing power, location value by comparables, resources/spectrum with clear title, and
network-effect capture. Narrow, auditable categories replace "captured − created" as the
operational test. **AI-compute/data/network-effects = the new "land"** → their rent to a commons
fund + citizen dividend. Renting a **produced asset** = earned service income; only the
land/location/scarcity/monopoly component is unearned. Inheritance: goods bequeathable; land-rent
stays commons-owed; ceiling caps dynasties; Æ/standing non-transferable. Capital/credit: returns
to creation, not ownership (module still least-tested — §15).

## 9. Distributive justice
**Capability sufficientarianism + contribution-band + anti-concentration ceiling + generosity
premium = the corridor.** Floors are **capability *availability* — material/infrastructural
inputs — NOT measured internal functioning** (the state guarantees the *means* to function; it
does not audit whether you *feel* fulfilled — that resolves the mind-monitoring objection). The
ascetic who chooses "low functioning" is free; only availability is guaranteed. Nussbaum's 10
crosswalk to axes+rights fixes which axes carry a (material) floor. Not egalitarian, not pure
meritocratic, not utilitarian (floors lexically prior).

## 10. Law, rights & crime — the coercion ladders
Coercion enters here as **graduated escalation across lower-bound thresholds** (the owner's
model): crossing a value-flow lower bound triggers, in rising order, **annotation → charge/fine
→ access/standing restriction → loss of liberty**, each threshold defined per-polity and only on
defensible, inter-frame/floor-relevant measurement (§2.2). Inviolable rights (life, bodily
integrity, liberty, consent, exit) are **not priceable/votable/tradeable**. **Ultra-anti-value by
KIND:** rights-violation (not priced → criminal law) · malicious (charges + bounded clawback) ·
self-directed/distress (**care, not punishment**) · negligent (Pigou). **Incapacitation is
justified by A9** (participation that makes others' θ fail), not retribution. Courts: rebuttable
interpretation, contestable, independent reviewer; **non-retroactivity** (judged by
contemporaneous norms; evidence updates, norms do not); versioned norm-set. **Distinguish
criminal sanction / civil liability / administrative restriction / reputational annotation** —
they are different ladders, not one automated debt.

## 11. Leadership & succession
Separation of powers (demos legislates values · Sub-Agents execute · courts review). **Vote on
values, appoint on competence** (calibrated, revocable); sortition for oversight juries.
**Rotation without losing PositionMemory.** Continuous accountability + recall + capture→clawback
(organs included). **Peaceful transfer structurally defused** (power distributed, transparency
voids seizure, loyalty to the Charter not the person). **Meta-Orchestrator = rotating,
Charter-bound coordinator, not sovereign.** **Succession = validated hot-swap of an open
algorithmic officeholder** (reproducible build/logic-hash, multi-vendor cross-check, staged
autonomy, monitoring + clawback for sleepers) — with a **named human liability chain** and
suspension/emergency-injunction powers (§15 work).

## 12. Anti-value taxonomy
Scope (edge/systemic) · lag → instrument · intent/kind. **Responsibility share bounded** —
proportional-exposure by default; Shapley only on small, well-defined participant sets (societal
Shapley is infeasible). **Clawback bounded**: statute of limitations for non-concealed harm;
**fixed-rate Pigouvian premiums** instead of continuous recomputation; **systemic-risk
throttles** (transition bands, insurance, bankruptcy, no synchronized clawback); perpetual
liability **only for concealment/fraud**. Three worked profiles (yogurt / content-dependency /
BigTech). The **simulation tests estimator robustness under gaming** (§2.3), not against a truth
oracle.

**Still genuinely hard / open (the productive targets for the next critique):**
1. **Formal executable spec** — imbalance math, event JSON-schemas, state machine, cohort
   definition & anti-fragmentation, target-band setting, hysteresis, a sample tick. *(the P0 spec)*
2. **The inter-frame / intra-frame boundary** — the line that gates coercion is itself
   contestable and expansible; needs a bright-line test + judicial policing.
3. **Governance of the measurement/accounting-rule layer** — who writes/audits the value-
   attribution schemas (else the un-voted coder is the real sovereign): independent standards
   body + sortition-jury ratification + court review. *(being designed)*
4. **Confidence estimation & its governance** — who sets the confidence threshold that unlocks
   coercion, and how it resists capture.
5. **Missing constitutional pillars — promoted to now:** external relations & **defense**,
   **emergency / state-of-exception** (a homeostatic loop needs a hull), **citizenship + identity
   / AI-agent personhood + anti-Sybil**, and a **bounded-rationality model of the citizen**.
6. **Human liability chain** for algorithmic-officeholder decisions; **constituent power**
   (a human body may replace the Charter by deep supermajority — reconciling "no final authority"
   with the eternity core).
7. **Capital/credit module** and **prior-art failure→response matrix**.
8. **Sensing's own limits** — even without coercion, pervasive measurement chills behaviour;
   privacy/aggregation/consent limits on the sensing layer itself.
