# Adam Smith vs. Axiacracy — Critique & Required Augmentations

*A close reading of* **The Wealth of Nations** *(Books I–V, ~384k words), read line by line by five
parallel readers of the actual text, turned into a survivability critique of Axiacracy.*

---

## 0. The verdict in one paragraph

Smith is **both the ancestor and the sharpest critic** of Axiacracy. He *vindicates* its deepest
intuitions — that value is multidimensional and the price system is blind to much of it (the
water-diamond paradox), that **unearned rent** is real, separable and the best thing to tax, that a
commercial economy **degrades the worker's mind** and the state must protect that non-monetary value,
and that taxing **earned labour** violates "the most sacred and inviolable" property. But he also
supplies, ~170 years before Hayek, the **knowledge problem** that is aimed precisely at Axiacracy's
core mechanism: a state that senses and steers value across ten axes *is* the "statesman who should
attempt to direct private people in what manner they ought to employ their capitals," assuming "an
authority which could safely be trusted... to no council or senate whatever." **Axiacracy survives
Smith's stress-test only if it renounces the self-appointed "fourth duty" of value-steering /
profit-decoupling and confines itself to correcting genuine externalities and providing genuine
public goods — and if it fixes measurement, restores a capital/credit engine, un-defers public debt,
protects its settlement unit, ring-fences defence, and hardens every mechanism against capture.**

---

## 1. What Smith VINDICATES (planks that survive — or grow stronger)

- **Value is multidimensional; price misses use-value.** The water-diamond paradox — "Nothing is
  more useful than water; but it will purchase scarce any thing" — is Axiacracy's founding text.
  Governing by the "water side" is the legitimate ambition. *(Book I)*
- **Unearned rent is real, separable, and the best tax base.** Rent is what landlords "reap where
  they never sowed"; ground-rent "is altogether owing to the good government of the sovereign...
  nothing can be more reasonable than that [it] should be taxed peculiarly." Taxing rent gives "no
  discouragement... to any sort of industry." This is Axiacracy's **most Smith-defensible pillar** —
  on rent it is *more orthodox* than income-tax states. *(Books I, V)*
- **Do not tax earned labour.** "The property which every man has in his own labour... is the most
  sacred and inviolable." Axiacracy's "fund the state from rent, not earned income" has a direct
  Smithian charter. *(Book I)*
- **The division of labour mutilates the mind — a non-monetary value the state must defend.** The
  worker "generally becomes as stupid and ignorant as it is possible for a human creature to become...
  mental mutilation." Axiacracy's cognitive/meaning axis + capability floors are the exact
  institutional answer Smith calls for. *(Book V)*
- **Money-as-circulating-wheel is a costly instrument worth economizing.** Money "makes no part of the
  revenue of the society... the great but expensive instrument of commerce." Demoting *circulating*
  money is genuinely Smithian. *(Book II)*
- **Credit mobilizes idle "dead stock," it does not create capital** — "resources advanced against
  future realized value" is almost verbatim Smith. Axiacracy already speaks this language. *(Book II)*

These are load-bearing: a Smith-grounded Axiacracy leads with **rent taxation + the cognitive axis**,
its two strongest credentials.

## 2. The eight convergent critiques (Smith → threat → augmentation)

### C1 — The knowledge problem: value-steering is the statesman's folly *(Book IV core; I, III echo)*
**Smith:** "The statesman who should attempt to direct private people in what manner they ought to
employ their capitals... would assume an authority which could safely be trusted, not only to no
single person, but to no council or senate whatever, and which would nowhere be so dangerous as in
the hands of a man who had folly and presumption enough to fancy himself fit to exercise it." Prices
are a decentralized computation: profit above the natural rate is *itself* the signal that
reallocates capital, "without any intervention of law." **Decoupling profit from value jams the one
self-correcting signal the economy has.**
**Threat:** Axiacracy's per-axis ministries that "sense value and steer via strategic demand" *are*
the directing statesman; an AI Sub-Agent *sharpens* the "folly and presumption." Overweighting axis
*k* does not create value on *k* — it *diverts* finite capital from a more valuable use
("every derangement of the natural distribution of stock is necessarily hurtful").
**Augmentation:** **Correct-and-release, don't decouple.** The Æ-price is added *on top of* market
prices as a **capped externality correction**, then profit-seeking re-clears allocation. Add a
**subsidiarity-of-steering rule + local-knowledge burden of proof**: a ministry may steer a named
actor's capital only by proving it holds genuinely *non-local* knowledge the actors lack (a real
externality invisible at the point of decision); otherwise steering is presumed folly and disallowed.
**Confine ministries to the externality residual and public goods; renounce promotional missions.**
*(Revises: Markets-and-Civilizational-Value, the whole "orchestration" framing.)*

### C2 — Measurement is the load-bearing weakness; certainty outranks flexibility *(I #1, IV #5, V #8)*
**Smith:** He spends ~150 pages *failing* to measure the real value of **corn** across centuries — "I
do not pretend that any very certain conclusion can be drawn." The balance-of-trade has "no certain
criterion... national prejudice... prompted always by the private interest of particular traders...
generally direct our judgment." And the second maxim of taxation: certainty is so important that "a
very considerable degree of inequality... is not near so great an evil as a very small degree of
uncertainty."
**Threat:** A 10-axis Æ-metric is *ten* simultaneous unsolved measurement problems, most *less*
observable than Smith's single labour axis and with **no "higgling of the market" to force a number
into existence**. A guardian-defined, annually-reweighted obligation is structurally the arbitrary
French **taille** Smith condemns; pervasive multi-axis measurement is the "odious inquisition" of the
fourth maxim, digitized.
**Augmentation:** Make **"coerce only in proportion to measurement-confidence × inter-frame scope"
the supreme, enforced constitutional rule.** Per axis: a **falsifiable, real (not nominal) definition
+ published error bar + a market-like settling mechanism** (markets / prediction markets / contested
sortition attestation) + **out-of-sample validation** against an independent welfare outcome +
**sensor ≠ beneficiary** separation. Axes without a settling mechanism carry **zero coercive weight**.
Buy **certainty at the cost of some equality**: publish measurement rules as slow-to-change
fundamental law; strip guardian discretion from anything affecting an *individual's* liability;
**confine the full 10-axis vector to collective steering**, and assess individual *contributions* on
a few simple, certain, hard-to-conceal bases (rent, luxury consumption). *(Revises: the sensing-vs-
coercion doc, measurement-layer governance, Value-Axes.)*

### C3 — Capture is the default, not the edge case *(I #5/#10, IV #2, V #4)*
**Smith:** "People of the same trade seldom meet together... but the conversation ends in a conspiracy
against the public." Any law proposed by them "ought never to be adopted till after having been long
and carefully examined... with the most suspicious attention." The three orders are **not
symmetrically aligned** with the public good: the profit-order's interest "has not the same
connexion with the general interest of the society," yet it is the best-organized and most
articulate; the labourer's "voice is little heard, and less regarded." Traders make "very bad
sovereigns."
**Threat:** Ten axes = **ten independent lobbies**, each with a legitimizing vocabulary, and the
weight-vote is a higher-value capture surface than any tariff. Whoever can dress private interest as
"civilizational value on axis *k*" captures the weight, the mission budget, and the externality
price. One-citizen-one-attestation yields "the organized order's interest wearing the public's name."
**Augmentation:** A **presumption-of-capture regime**: proposer ≠ scorer (no ministry proposes *and*
scores its own missions); **hard sunsets with fresh public-interest re-proof, no auto-renewal**;
**published beneficiary registers** for every priced externality and mission; **separate
demand-generation from disbursement**; **sortition-based adversarial attestation** (open attestation
is trivially astroturfed); **alignment-weighted input, not one-vote**, with the profit-order's wealth
firewalled from weight-setting via the non-transferable Æ ledger, and the aligned-but-voiceless
structurally amplified. *(Revises: Governance-Mechanics B0, Leadership/validation, the guardians.)*

### C4 — The missing savings / capital / credit engine (the fatal gap) *(Book II entire; III tenure)*
**Smith:** "Parsimony, and not industry, is the immediate cause of the increase of capital"; saving
*is* investment ("what is annually saved is as regularly consumed... but by a different set of
people"), driven by the womb-to-grave "desire of bettering our condition," expressed as "augmentation
of fortune." **Accumulation must precede production** ("the accumulation of stock must, in the nature
of things, be previous to the division of labour"). Long/fixed capital returns "after a period of many
years... too distant to suit the conveniency of a bank" — it needs *patient* funders paid "the
interest of their money" for years. Capping interest **backfires**: it drives lending underground at
higher cost, and, mis-set, funnels capital to "prodigals and projectors."
**Threat:** Non-transferable Æ + a *thin* money rail appear to **sever the abstention→accumulation
motive** (consumption keeps its motive; saving loses it) → prodigality bias, capital decay. Zeroing
time-rent + no patient-rentier class + banks structurally unable to fund long assets = **no financing
channel for anything with a multi-year payback** (infrastructure, R&D, energy) = civilizational
stagnation. And re-priceable value-weights make the **return on any sunk improvement politically
variable** — Smith's short-lease/metayer/taille disincentive reborn.
**Augmentation (the survival kit for the weakest module):**
- **Split money's functions:** thin rail for *circulation*, but restore a **stockable, compounding
  store-of-value** for savings/investment, firewalled from the civic Æ ledger (civic power ≠ wealth
  is preserved; the saving *motive* is restored).
- **License patient, duration-tolerant capital:** an equity-like "share in future realized value"
  with at-risk return over years — rate-*constrained* if you must, never *zeroed*.
- **Don't price credit by decree; select borrowers by proven creation** — let the rate float near the
  profit-of-stock and use the **Æ track record as the borrower-quality signal** (ration by
  demonstrated creation, not administered price).
- **Adopt Smith's dead-stock / real-bills credit doctrine** as the official theory + **maturity-
  matching + a redemption ("water-pond") test** so the state never repeats the Ayr-Bank detonation of
  funding slow assets with callable rails.
- **A "yeoman guarantee" (value-weight lease):** lock the weights/prices applying to a *specific sunk
  improvement* for a long, constitutionally-fixed horizon — steer *new* activity, never retroactively
  reprice committed capital.
- **A productive/reproductive axis + track the capital/revenue ratio** ("wherever capital predominates,
  industry prevails; wherever revenue, idleness") so a value-state doesn't legislate a Versailles.
*(Revises/repairs: Property-Rent-and-Commons §7 capital/credit — Axiacracy's admitted weakest part.)*

### C5 — Money-as-numéraire must survive the demotion; protect the settlement unit *(I #2, V #13)*
**Smith:** Money defeated barter because metals alone "be divided into any number of parts" — money is
the **commensuration and settlement layer**, not a commodity-among-commodities. And states escape debt
by **debasing the unit** — "raising the denomination of the coin" — "an injustice of treacherous
fraud," enriching "the idle and profuse debtor at the expense of the industrious and frugal creditor."
**Threat:** A thin rail carrying only one axis re-creates the **double-coincidence problem *between
axes*** (how does an educational gain settle against an ecological loss?). And **money as a
guardian-steered "policy dial" pre-builds the debasement lever** — the temptation to inflate away
obligations becomes structural.
**Augmentation:** Keep money **demoted in civic power but supreme as the cross-axis numéraire and
clearing layer** — price and settle all Pigou charges and the dividend in the money rail. Make the
**settlement unit inviolable**: redenomination / inflating-away of obligations = a **supermajority
constitutional act**, closing Smith's fraud lever. *(Revises: Consumption-and-Settlement, Value-Money-
Coupling.)*

### C6 — Fiscal survivability: debt, defence, the Commons Fund, incidence *(Book V core)*
**Smith:** Defence is "the first duty," grows *more* costly with prosperity, is non-excludable → must
be "defrayed by the general contribution of the whole society," never user-fees or donation, and needs
"three or four times" surge capacity in war → states **must** borrow. Unmanaged debt "has gradually
enfeebled every state which has adopted it" and escape comes "always... by a bankruptcy; sometimes by
an avowed one, though frequently by a pretended payment." **State-owned productive funds are a poor
base** — crown lands yield "not the fourth part" of private-management potential and "cost more to the
society than perhaps any other equal revenue." **Stock is "a citizen of the world"** and flees a
"vexatious inquisition." The **four maxims**: proportion-to-revenue, certainty, convenience, economy.
Never tax **necessaries**.
**Threat:** Axiacracy **defers debt & credit** — i.e., defers the exact mechanism every surviving
state uses to fund war (loses its first war, or invents debt badly under wartime duress). Its **votable
contribution can vote defence down**; its rent base can't surge 3–4×. Its **Commons Fund is the
"state's own funds" model Smith rejected** — insufficient and negligently managed — and its
**compute/data rents are the most mobile "world-citizen" capital**, prone to flight. A guardian-defined
multidimensional obligation **fails the certainty maxim**; charges on necessaries + unmodeled
**incidence** may land on the very citizens the dividend protects.
**Augmentation:**
- **Un-defer public debt: constitutionalize debt discipline *before* launch** — a rules-based, bounded
  borrowing framework + explicit **war-contingency facility** + **un-raidable sinking discipline** + a
  **Berne-style peacetime reserve** in the Commons Fund.
- **Ring-fence defence & core-justice as non-votable existential floors** on the broadest inelastic
  base (votes may *raise*, never *lower* below survival).
- **Convert the Commons Fund from asset-*operator* to rent-*taxer*** — leave land/compute/data in
  decentralized productive hands and capture only the *unearned* rent (Smith's favorite tax), weighting
  toward **immobile bases** (land, resources, spectrum, local network position); treat mobile
  compute/data rents as *moderate and internationally-coordinated*.
- **Model tax incidence** (forward/backward shifting) before claiming the dividend is progressive; site
  charges on Smith's *sticky* bases (rent, ground-rent, luxuries, monopoly gains); **zero-rate
  necessaries**.
- **Give justice a stable, constitutionally-fixed endowment** with metric-setting removed from guardian
  discretion, and irremovable judges (attestation-contingent judicial pay recreates executive
  dependence Smith forbids).
*(Revises: State-Apparatus-Budget-and-Accountability, the whole fiscal reframe.)*

### C7 — Rent & floors are vindicated — but must be operationalized *precisely* *(I #3/#7, III, V #5/#9)*
**Smith:** Rent is detectable by a **cause-vs-effect asymmetry**: "high or low wages and profit are the
*causes* of high or low price; high or low rent is the *effect* of it." But the categories **confound**
in practice — the gardener "unites... landlord, farmer, and labourer"; a produce-share levy (the tithe)
"is found to be a very great hindrance to improvement." And fully-salaried providers rot: endowed Oxford
professors "have given up altogether even the pretence of teaching."
**Threat:** "Unearned rent by observable signatures" will **over-tax the improving owner and under-tax
the rentier who cloaks rent as improvement**. Fully-funded capability floors / AI ministries reproduce
the **endowed-university decay** (no "necessity of application"). Floors delivered as **dependence on a
central ministry** recreate the servile retainer, not the independent yeoman.
**Augmentation:** Replace "signatures" with **Smith's cause-vs-effect test**: rent = the part of value
that is an *effect* of scarcity (its withdrawal would not reduce supply), with explicit **deductions
before the commons claim** — interest on genuine improvement, wages of superintendence, a risk premium,
own labour — and an **improvement-exclusion** (capture *unimproved locational rent only*, non-
retroactively, 100% of marginal improvement return left to the improver). **Keep competition + skin-in-
the-game inside the floors** (co-payment, provider choice, emulation, badges) and design floors for
**dispersed independence** ("maintained without him"), not central dependency. *(Revises: Property-Rent-
and-Commons, Distributive-Justice-and-Capability-Floors.)*

### C8 — Epistemic humility: the "least observed" and institutional ossification *(III #4/#8, IV #6)*
**Smith:** Commerce's most important effect — order, liberty and good government — was "**by far the
most important of all their effects**" and yet "**the least observed**," produced by people "who had not
the least intention to serve the public." And "laws frequently continue in force long after the
circumstances which first gave occasion to them... are no more." Over-steered concentration makes "an
unwholesome body... in which some of the vital parts are overgrown."
**Threat:** A scoring state optimizes the axes citizens can *currently name and measure* and is
**structurally blind to the "least observed" second-order goods** that turn out to matter most; its
axes and (AI) ministries **ossify and grow constituencies** past their usefulness; "strategic demand"
concentration creates the **fragile over-swollen sectors** it must then protect.
**Augmentation:** An **unobserved-effects reserve** — a permanently unallocated weight-band ("value we
cannot yet name"), a deliberately **unsteered zone**, and **ex-post positive-externality auditing** that
back-credits value the scoring missed. **Mandatory sunset-and-rejustify** for every axis, weight and
ministry (burden of proof on continuation). **Concentration/fragility caps** + market *variety* as an
explicit competing objective. *(Revises: Comparative-Analysis gap backlog, Governance.)*

## 3. The survival kit — ranked master list of augmentations

1. **Renounce the "fourth duty"; adopt correct-and-release.** Confine the state to correcting genuine
   externalities + providing genuine public goods; price *on top of* market prices with a ceiling, then
   let profit re-clear. (C1) — *the deepest, most viability-critical.*
2. **Un-defer public debt + inviolable settlement unit.** Rules-based bounded borrowing, war-contingency
   facility, un-raidable sinking fund, peacetime reserve; redenomination = supermajority. (C6, C5)
3. **Restore the savings/capital engine.** Split money's functions (thin rail + compounding savings
   vehicle); license patient capital; don't zero time-rent; select borrowers by Æ track record; dead-
   stock/real-bills + maturity-matching; yeoman value-weight lease; productive/reproductive axis. (C4)
4. **Measurement-confidence constitution + certainty over flexibility.** Higgling-analogue or zero
   coercive weight per axis; real definitions + error bars + out-of-sample validation; sensor≠beneficiary;
   individual liability on simple certain unshiftable bases; 10-axis vector for *collective* steering
   only. (C2)
5. **Ring-fence defence & core-justice** as non-votable floors on the broadest stable base. (C6)
6. **Presumption-of-capture regime.** Proposer≠scorer, hard sunsets, beneficiary registers, separate
   demand-generation from disbursement, sortition adversarial attestation, alignment-weighted input. (C3)
7. **Commons Fund = rent-taxer, not asset-operator**, weighted to immobile bases; model incidence;
   zero-rate necessaries; lean into rent + luxury (Smith's favorite, most-defensible taxes). (C6, C7)
8. **Operationalize rent by cause-vs-effect with deductions + improvement-exclusion**; keep competition
   inside floors; design floors for dispersed independence. (C7)
9. **Epistemic-humility mechanisms**: unobserved-effects reserve, sunset-and-rejustify, concentration
   caps. (C8)

## 4. The single deepest reframe

Smith's Book IV *discharges the sovereign* from "the duty of superintending the industry of private
people, and of directing it towards the employments most suitable to the interest of the society" —
"no human wisdom or knowledge could ever be sufficient" for it — and reduces the state to three plain
duties: **defence, justice, and the public works/institutions no individual would provide.** Axiacracy's
**externality-pricing + public-goods + rent-taxation + cognitive-floor** core maps cleanly onto duties
two and three and is **legitimate and Smith-vindicated**. Its **value-steering, strategic-demand,
profit-decoupling, multidimensional-personal-assessment** ambition is the **self-appointed fourth
duty** Smith proves is folly, capturable, and beyond any council's knowledge. **The doctrine becomes a
survivable, balanced mechanism precisely to the degree it moves weight from the fourth duty to the
first three — measuring richly, but steering humbly, correcting rather than directing, and taxing the
unearned rather than commanding the earned.**

  effect; capture & the three orders' asymmetric alignment; self-interest not benevolence.

  dead-stock credit; productive/reproductive axis; security of the return-to-waiting.

  the four maxims + certainty; incidence; never tax necessaries; competition inside floors.
